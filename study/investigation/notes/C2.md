## 1. Summary of each source as it bears on investigation

### 1.1 `BIO_Case_Making_v0_1.md` (CM) — the case-making design pass, 2026-08-01 to 2026-09-29

**Bob's frame is already an investigation frame.** CM §The frame records Bob's purpose for the whole system: the substrate "collects the record, extracts meaning rigorously, understands the legal/regulatory/policy framework, makes connections, and figures out how {information, money, responsibility, relationships, decisions, power} is SUPPOSED to flow, how it ACTUALLY flows" (`:94-101`). The path is "Questioning → exploring → discovering (understanding) → documenting → impacting", plus sharing (`:103-104`). The Grandview question is this path from its first step: a member's question about a promise (one school year) against an observed flow (two years and counting). *(inference)* The supposed-vs-actual flow is D-128's "declared-versus-actual flow" (`:265-266`, `:422-425`), and a school rebuild's promised schedule against its status reports is exactly that shape.

**The four record constructs (post-collapse), and where an investigation lives among them.**

| construct | what it is (CM) | role in an investigation *(inference where marked)* | status per CM |
|---|---|---|---|
| `information` | "a captured document with provenance and custody. Evidence, not a claim about evidence" (`:559-561`) | every RFP, bid, award, status report, invoice the engine pulls | built (in the plane before the pass) |
| `inquiry` | one recursive object: "a question, which may gather evidence and other inquiries, which may reach a conclusion, which may be published" (`:523-525`); member-facing names inquiry → finding (`:600-612`) | the member's question ("what's going on at Grandview?") and each sub-question ("was the contract term one school year?", "what change orders were approved?") *(inference)* | built: "`inquiry`, `conclude`, `citeinquiry`, `inquiryground` and `inquirystrength` all exist in the plane" (`:79-80`) |
| `action` | "outward-facing engagement with a `counterparty` and correspondence. An inquiry is a thought; an action is a thing done to the world" (`:562-563`); states `planned → active → awaiting_response → resolved / abandoned` (`:151`) | records requests to the school district (CPRA), letters to the board | built, with many §2 extensions (below) |
| `project` | "a CONTAINER WITH MEMBERSHIP AND ACCESS CONTROL; an inquiry is a claim structure" (`:564-568`); states `forming → investigating → matured → closed`, carries an `objective` (`:373`) | the workspace a group investigates in; the nearest existing thing to "the investigation's home" *(inference)* | built |
| `case` | after DEC-72: "a PRODUCTION OF A PROJECT — a set of finding-versions plus the publishing project, published by a project owner against the project's own bar" (`:590-598`) | the signed, published answer at the end, if any | built per RESUME.md's note on the word; CM itself points to `docs/archive/CASE-AS-PRODUCTION.md` |

So "case file" in Bob's 2026-10-06 words collides with `case` here; RESUME.md already flags it. *(inference)* The investigation's working holding is closest to a **project** (container, membership, objective, dead ends kept: "The dead ends stay in the project ... D-81 already argues that a recorded dead end is what makes the next member's work cheaper", `:141-144`) holding **inquiries** (the questions and sub-questions), **information** (the documents) and **actions** (requests). What none of them is: a per-investigation planning state, a document's *purpose in the file*, or a reading log. CM never designed an "engine"; it designed the record an engine would write into.

**Intake and clarifying.** CM has no intake design. The nearest: "questioning" is the inquiry early (`:600-612`); the focus/inquiry is kept separate from the project because "hundreds of open questions is survivable, hundreds of open investigations is not" (D-79, `:383-387`); a machine-surfaced question and a machine-derived conclusion are one type, so "aggregation, ageing, and D-82's must-look-derived rule are written once" (`:539-541`). *(inference)* An engine's clarifying questions to the member are not record objects in CM's model; the member's question becomes an inquiry, and the objective lives on the project.

**Planning toward objectives.** Two strands:
- *Objectives engine (DEC-22).* "An UNSUPPORTED CLAIM is a standing objective the system can pursue (DEC-22)" (`:1003-1004`). The backward question — "what additional findings or claims would ALSO need to be true for action X to be a credible option" — "is the objectives engine pointed at a new target. Its output is not prose — it is a WORK LIST ... each of which becomes an objective under DEC-22, pursued by the member and by the machine together" (`:1022-1030`). Deferred as D-165 (`:1100-1104`; Incomplete-sections bullet `:13`: trigger "S11 exists and members are answering the backward question by hand"). This is the closest canon to Bob's "planning function ... able to stay focused on its objective(s)". *(inference)*
- *The action plan (S11).* A PLAN holds STEPS; a taken step produces an `action`; a declined step keeps its reason (`:933-934`). Steps borrow `progression_stages` vocabulary (`stage_no`, `after_stage`, `within_interval`, `required`) plus outcome-keyed branching, a free-form resources list, and a disposition `chosen · declined-with-reason · done · blocked` (`:938-948`, `:1080-1082`). It must NOT share an object with progressions: "A progression models what SOMEBODY ELSE is supposed to do ... A plan models what WE intend to do. If the two shared machinery, our own missed deadline would surface as a finding about the record" (`:950-955`). Plan is working material, never published (DEC-25 provisional, `:1121-1126`). Status: mapped, "not yet in the review document" (`:13`); S11 **specified/planned, not built** per CM. Note this plan is an *action* plan (what to do about a finding), not an *investigation* plan (what to read and ask next); the investigation plan Bob describes has no canon object in CM. *(inference)*
- *Planning from what is not established (6a).* "the gate belongs at the ACT, not at the reasoning. A plan may rest on premises not yet established. An act that reaches outside the group may not be taken on them" (`:1014-1016`). Every plan element carries a support status established / short of the standard / hypothetical, displayed everywhere "exactly as `undetermined` is" (`:1034-1047`). A hypothetical premise is HUNCH DEBT, blocking publication (`:1050-1053`). An outward act's pre-flight refuses when its step is not established (`:1054-1055`).

**Reading a document with purpose.** Absent from CM. The only reading-adjacent doctrine is capture grade vs connection grade (R2, `:1218-1280`): capture grade "is a property of an INFORMATION object — how the bytes were read in"; connection grade "is a property of an EDGE" (`:1259-1260`). An engine extracting "the term of the contract" from an award document creates, in CM's terms, a leg (an edge with a connection grade) to an information object (with a capture grade). *(inference)*

**Holding an investigation over time.** Recursion makes the record cumulative: "A published case is an INPUT, not a terminus ... a later inquiry cites it exactly as it cites a document" (`:655-660`). Strength changes are events: "DEC-16 already makes that an EVENT propagating to every ancestor, and DEC-12 already permits REOPENING as an act with a name and a date. Nothing reverts by itself" (`:738-743`). Division (`:623-667`): sub-questions are free by recursion; supersession of a malformed question needs an edge that, as of 2026-08-01, was not built ("`supersedes` ... ZERO occurrences in `store.mjs`", `:634`); apportioning basis is an authored act (`:636-639`); R4 makes division as costly as severance (`:1298-1312`). Merging is "a parent that cites both" (`:666-667`).

**Competing explanations.** CM's nearest constructs:
- *Contradiction* (§CONTRADICTION, `:808-888`): three cases — in the WORLD (a finding: "the department said X in March and Y in October"), in the RECORD (a defect with a duty), NEITHER (imprecision). "contradiction is not an edge case the model must tolerate, it is an OUTPUT the system exists to find" (`:819-820`). The first case "is ONE inquiry whose CONCLUSION IS the discrepancy, supported by legs on both sides" (`:834-838`). Mechanisms IDENTIFY / PRESENT / RESOLVE, "NONE is built" (`:14`); IDENTIFY designed at level 2 (`CONTRADICTION-IDENTIFY-DESIGN.md`) with over-strictness as its acceptance test (`:843-854`). DEC-76: a contradiction is conditional ("cannot both hold IN THE SAME RESPECT"), a taken-up candidate becomes an inquiry whose conclusion records its kind (DISSOLVED, CORRECTED, GENUINE) (`:866-868`). DEC-77: met inline; six forms including a "competing-hypotheses matrix" and "the auditor's Criteria / Condition / Cause / Effect finding" (`:870`).
- *Plurality of grounds* (DEC-32, `:791-806`): one finding, `1..n` independently sufficient grounds, composing disjunctively; "COUNT THE FALSIFIERS. One proposition, one falsifier" (`:802-806`). CM records DEC-32 as raised; the Incomplete-sections bullet says "DEC-32 remains open" is stale, decided 2026-08-04 (`:12`); `inquiryground` is named as built (`:80`).
- *Competing explanations as such* (several hypotheses for why Grandview is late: design changes, contractor default, funding, permits, unforeseen conditions) have **no dedicated construct** in CM. *(inference)* Under CM each hypothesis is a separate inquiry with its own falsifier (different propositions → different falsifiers → separate inquiries, `:803-806`), composed under a parent; DEC-77's competing-hypotheses matrix is a presentation form for contradictions, not a hypothesis-tracking object.

**Evidence and grading.** Basis legs rest "on information or on another inquiry, nothing else" (C-2.8, `:1130-1131`). Strength is derived, weakest-link; the falsifier is accounted for (stated, or its absence stated and attributed — REC-117, `:696-737`); DEC-15 lets a project declare its required strength, "A declaration, not a gate" (`:697-699`). R1/DEC-18: an ungraded leg is INERT, every ungraded leg named, UNRATED only when every leg is ungraded (`:1184-1216`). R2/DEC-21: capture and connection grades are two measurements over two populations, never composed (`:1218-1280`). R3: basis graph is a DAG enforced at write, depth bound exhaustion reported as `undetermined` (`:1294-1296`). D-181: an action is never a basis leg; a non-response is an OUTCOME carried by `references[]` (`:1128-1147`).

**Time and promises.** The *nonconformity* finding has legs to "BOTH the standard and the conduct" (`:906-909`) — Bob's "doesn't conform to the law, policies, regulations, stated intentions/promises" (`:892-895`). Grandview's "promised one school year" is a stated promise; the finding is the departure. *(inference)* `progression_stages` (`stage_no`, `after_stage`, `within_interval`, `required`) model "what SOMEBODY ELSE is supposed to do, and its entire purpose is to make a gap DETECTABLE and raise it as a finding about the world" (`:938`, `:950-952`) — the canon's existing mechanism for a promised schedule and its slip *(inference: whether progressions are built is outside this source)*. The records-request lifecycle (D-147) holds our own request's clock: stages, outcomes, member-stated `due_by` with `due_cite`; "The clock is never encoded (D-149: the plane encodes no law's rules)"; the plane derives only elapsed time and passed due dates (`:233-254`) — built in the plane 2026-09-25 per CM; member surface and cross-action index not built (`:253-254`).

**Money.** Only the fee quote (D-148): "A FEE QUOTE IS EVIDENCE" about the body that quoted it; amount, currency, basis verbatim; projected to `action_quotes`; "the machine may set quotes side by side and never judges one" (`:193-203`). Built per CM. No canon for contract values, budgets, change orders or payments in this source.

**People and organisations.** The subject registry (D-83) "must carry no adversarial attribute, no suspicion flag, no category-level prior. An entity is an entity; what is claimed about it is claimed with evidence attached" (`:326-329`). Bad actors "are identified BY EVIDENCE, never assumed by role" (`:323`). Directly binds contractor-history research (Bob's "contractor info/history"). *(inference)*

**Procurement.** Absent as a domain. The adjacent pieces: `counterparty` on actions; the action repertoire `cpra_request`, `grand_jury`, `controller_referral`, `public_comment`, `media`, `litigation_support`, `other` (`:962-964`); governing laws by citation per agency (D-149, `:205-231`), with the law-neutral `records_request` kind adopted as direction but not built (`:227-231`). "The plane encodes no law's rules" (`:221-223`) — would equally bind any encoding of procurement law (public contract code deadlines, change-order limits). *(inference)*

**Member-in-the-loop acceptance.** Pervasive: governing laws — the machine proposes (`op=actionlawspropose`), the list "moves only through the member's own act", and there is deliberately NO adopt control because "a list a member did not author is not the member's" (`:3`, `:213-220`); risk tier — only a member's authored act sets it, a machine may only propose a reconsideration (REC-215, not built) (`:161-191`); plans — "a suggested plan is a set of CANDIDATES; the member adopts, edits or declines each, with a reason. The machine never adopts a step" (`:974-976`); "nothing that drafts framing FOR a member — the same rule as never prefilling a justification, extended from a field to a whole argument" (`:345-347`); DEC-77 "judgements are recommended with a reason and accepted in one attributed act the record remembers as acceptance ... the acceptance rate is measured as the guard against steering" (`:870`). Note the tension between UI-102's "no adopt control" and DEC-77's "accepted in one attributed act" — see §4.

**AI runs and their bounds.** CM refers to DEC-24's machine boundary: rule 1 (machine candidates, member adopts) and rule 4 ("a checker RAISES into a queue, never edits the plan") (`:972-981`). PURSUE and CHECK are named as machine roles for plans (`:974-979`). Machine credentials are fenced at write for risk tier (`MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_RISK_TIER`) and governing laws (`:3`, `:161-163`). D-82's "must-look-derived" rule for machine-derived content (`:541`, `:856`).

**Cost.** CM's only cost content is the ergonomics question: "stating one finding may cost more than a volunteer's evening ... should be measured, not predicted" (`:763-765`), and the resources list on plan steps (`:1064-1082`). No AI run cost.

**Open questions still on CM's list that touch this study** (`:1314-1332`): Q6 "What closes the loop from action to consequence, and how does a consequence become evidence about the system's own responsiveness?" — CM §2 says "The loop from evidence to action to consequence is the thing that is unbuilt" (`:260-266`). Q8 "What does the ladder look like once case-making is at the top".


### 1.2 `BIO_Interaction_Constructs_v0_1.md` (IC) — how a member meets the work and acts on it

IC is the member-surface canon for M8 (`:5`): two constructs (QUEUE, ACT), a published rung ladder, the UNDETERMINED primitive, and later rulings (DEC-82 measures map, DEC-86/98 undetermined and its neighbours, DEC-87/88 friction, DEC-99 access, DEC-106 working/published, DEC-120/121 wizards). Status line: partially complete; its own falsifier ("build T and one act, then re-read this") has no recorded re-read (`:3`, `:281-287`).

**QUEUE — how investigation work would reach a member.** "*things that want me,* grouped by the case they belong to. Items are typed ... an obligation I must dispatch (do / forward / resolve), or something the system noticed and nobody has judged yet (adopt / defer / dismiss)" (`:106`). Three DOMAINS: FINDING (case-making), OBLIGATION ("the civic system's own declared flow, whose delta against the observed flow is the analytic product (D-128)"), CONDITION (a signal: recorded / noticed / actionable) (`:108-113`). Generators are catalogued in `NOTIFICATIONS.md`, "about thirty", with stable `N-` ids (`:135-139`). DEC-10: grouped by Focus/Project — "one live item per (member, case) while it has unhandled events", standing and accumulating, not a stream; group mute scoped to kinds present (`:141-163`). DEC-16: once inquiries nest, "an event reaches EVERY ancestor, and one member's resolution settles it for all of them"; "The unit of state is the EVENT, not the notification" (`:169-198`); a machine credential may not resolve (D-151, `:193-195`); ancestor walk inherits R3's depth bound (`:196-198`). DEC-36: an invisible ancestor is stated only as incompleteness (`:200-208`).
*(inference)* An engine that runs for weeks would surface its results — a newly read document, a proposed sub-question, a passed due date — as queue items under the investigation's project/inquiry, aggregated, not streamed. "one check across 58 contracts is ONE proposal with 58 instances, never 58 tasks" (`:392-394`) is the relevant guard for an engine that reads many documents.

**ACT and the rung ladder.** One motion: "choose, see what it will refuse and why BEFORE it runs, author the reason, get a receipt" (`:210`). Rungs: reversible · reasoned (justification required, never prefilled) · terminal · attested · IRREVERSIBLE (publishing), published by the plane via `op=affordances` (`:211`, `:123-133`). Built per IC: rung ladder published and asserted total by `bio-plane/test/rung-ladder.test.mjs` (`:127-131`); DEC-88 assigned 57 unranked acts (`:643-644`). DEC-8: "a surface may RENDER a refusal it received from the plane. It may never COMPUTE one" — via publication (`op=affordances`) or dry run (`op=publishpreflight`, deferred behind DEC-33) (`:227-270`, `:8`). D-311: affordances withhold from a machine credential every act the store refuses its class (`MACHINE_REFUSALS`) (`:272-279`).

**TASK (attention layer).** Plane half built (D-98: `tasks`, `taskforward`, `taskresolve`, routing via `member_expertise`) (`:325-329`). Carries, among others, "a temporal expectation that has come due" and "a review of an assistant-surfaced focus" (`:331-334`). D-86: bias debt and temporal expectations are one shape, "an obligation with a clock, attached to an object" (`:336-348`); the temporal half still blocks, the bias half surfaces (DEC-20 correction). Accountability: never silently dropped; ages with a reason (D-79); addressed or honestly `unassigned` (`:350-353`). *(inference)* This is the canon home for "the promised completion date passed" and for an investigation's own follow-up obligations, though CM §3 keeps our own plan deadlines off the progression machinery.

**PROPOSAL — the machine-proposes shape.** Carries "an assistant-surfaced focus · a connection the system inferred, with its grade · a missing predecessor in a progression · the gap list derived from an objective's satisfaction condition · a decayed bias measure" (`:379-381`). Charter D-90 invariant 8: "*derived things inform and authored acts bind.* A proposal reports; it never decides, never blocks, and never edits the thing it is about" (`:383-386`). D-82: a proposal must LOOK derived — "what a member needs to know is that nobody has yet judged it worth asking" (`:388-391`). "Three affordances, and only three: adopt (into an objective, a focus, a problem), defer with a recorded reason, dismiss with a recorded reason. Nothing is adopted automatically and nothing disappears silently" (`:396-398`). Build order: P "when the framework produces findings to propose (M4)" (`:741-742`); IC's status line says D-82's marker is built for an agent-surfaced question (`:3`). *(inference)* This is the exact canon shape for Bob's "the machine proposes and a member accepts": proposed sub-questions, proposed documents to add to the file, proposed readings/extractions, proposed explanations. Note "the gap list derived from an objective's satisfaction condition" — the canon already imagines objectives with satisfaction conditions whose gaps become proposals.

**JUSTIFIED TRANSITION and the prefill rule.** "the system must never put words in a member's mouth ... No templates, no 'suggested reason', no LLM-drafted default" (`:423-427`); the boundary: "assembling what a member already wrote is not a fabricated attribution; drafting a justification for them is" (`:417-421`). Later softened for wizard fields by DEC-120: "A step may place a **labelled draft** in a field for the member to edit and adopt (as DEC-101 and K1019 allow)" (`:557-559`). See §4 tension.

**ATTESTATION.** DEC-19: reversible but never silently; publishing is the IRREVERSIBLE rung with forward correction (`:439-473`). "What a published hash asserts is bytes, address, date and route — not that the document is an authentic municipal record" (`:481-484`). *(inference)* Binds how an engine's captured RFP/award documents may be described to the member.

**SELECTION-SCOPED.** Bundles via server-side lease; enumerated sets for non-drifting items, answered per item (D-291, D-126 built) (`:486-509`). "Auto-updating a selection is rejected doctrine (D-35)" (`:506-509`). *(inference)* Relevant if a member accepts or rejects an engine's batch of proposals in one act: per-item answers, nothing preselected (DEC-69).

**THE ASSISTANT (DEC-27) — the nearest canon to an investigation engine's member interface.** "free natural-language input ... on every surface" (`:513-514`); three request kinds FIND (read, no ceremony), CREATE (proposes objects, needs confirmation), ACT (changes the record) (`:519-520`). "the assistant may INITIATE an act, and the act still runs its four beats" (`:522-525`). Accountability: "it never commits, only proposes and shows what it understood; it structures only what was SAID, and asks rather than asserts when it thinks something further is implied; and it may propose project defaults from a self-identification but never set them silently" (`:527-533`) — this is the canon basis for **clarifying questions at intake**: the assistant *asks* rather than asserts. "**The line is AUTHORSHIP, not writing.** It may do what the record attributes mechanically: search, gather, extract content, run a check, capture a document ... It may not do what requires a person to vouch: a conclusion, an authored reason, a completeness statement, an attestation, a grade asserted rather than earned" (`:537-541`) — this licenses most of an engine's research (fetch the RFP, the bids, the award, status reports; extract content) and fences its outputs. Attribution: "a NAMED NON-MEMBER actor ... *the assistant did this, at Anna's request*" (`:543-546`). The WIZARD: "The assistant CONDUCTS; the member ACTS" (`:548-552`); DEC-120 wizard scripts, and "where a group has set a key, the assistant may also plan a wizard on the fly" (`:554-559`); DEC-121 governs script libraries; "Checks refuse a script ... telling a member what to conclude" (`:565-566`). "THE ASSISTANT HOLDS NO COPY OF THE RULES -- it asks the plane" (`:572-577`); "Only OBJECTIVE checks are advisory -- *you said three claims and there are two* -- because the objective belongs to the member" (`:579-581`); "the assistant introduces NO NEW TRUST BOUNDARY" (`:583-585`).
*(inference)* A planning function that keeps an investigation "focused on its objective(s)" is, in IC's terms, an advisory objective check owned by the member, plus an on-the-fly wizard plan; IC gives it no persistent state of its own beyond the queue.

**UNDETERMINED and its neighbours (DEC-86, DEC-98).** One component with a mandatory "because…"; neighbours "Withheld", "Unrated", "Nobody looked" (distinct from looked-and-absent), "Refused" (`:603-610`). Waiting states: "Every wait carries one line naming what is awaited, from whom, and the expected or legal date where one exists ('Waiting on the City Clerk's reply, due 14 October'), or that no date is set" (`:612-619`). *(inference)* An engine's "we have not found the change orders" must be "Nobody looked" or looked-and-absent, never silence; a pending records request reads as a wait with its due date.

**MEASURES MAP (DEC-82).** "letters grade evidence, bars show progress, weights mark acts"; "*An answer is only as strong as the weakest thing it depends on* ... side by side, never merged"; "*A grade tells you how easily someone else could check it for themselves; it never tells you whether it is true*"; project workspace shows each question's capture and connection badges against the project's bar (`:646-672`). *(inference)* An investigation's progress (how far along the plan is) is a bar; its findings' support is letters; the two must not be borrowed across.

**FRICTION (DEC-87/88).** Friction escalates with rung; "Friction also follows consequence in the world ... a step toward something leaving the group ... opens the full dialog" (`:630-644`). *(inference)* Sending a records request an engine drafted is such a step.

**ACCESS AND LANGUAGE (DEC-99, DEC-127, DEC-122).** WCAG 2.2 AA; machine translation labelled "Machine translation · original in English" and never replaces the original (`:690-700`); "signing, publishing and sending happen on a larger screen for now" (`:703-705`); no outside fonts, analytics or trackers (`:706-707`).

**WORKING AND PUBLISHED (DEC-106).** Two spaces; in-between items banded; path-to-publication marker; "Publishing remains the only crossing, through the ceremony" (`:718-726`). *(inference)* An investigation's file is working material by construction.

**Intake, reading, money, people, procurement, cost:** IC has nothing specific beyond the assistant's FIND/CREATE/ACT and "asks rather than asserts". No AI cost content.


### 1.3 `BIO_Assistant_and_AI_Roles_v0_1.md` (AR) — the AI construct: four roles, ten rules, one built run

AR is level 1 for construct 11, "RULES NOTHING: every rule below is restated from the ledger it was ruled in" (`:3`); amended through K1502 (2026-10-05). Its front matter is mostly build history of refusal-code translation (rule 10), not investigation-relevant.

**The boundary (DEC-24).** "*THE MACHINE MAY DO THE LOOKING; THE MEMBER DOES THE CONCLUDING.*" (`:32`). The assistant helps "at every point on the path — questioning, exploring, discovering, documenting, impacting" (`:32`). It is not a trust boundary and not an author (`:34`). This is the direct canon answer to Bob's engine: an engine that researches the RFP, bids, award, status reports and contractor history is "the looking"; what's going on is the member's conclusion.

**The four roles (DEC-24)** (`:38-45`), mapped to Bob's engine *(mapping is inference)*:

| role | canon definition | status (AR §2, §8) | engine function it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| FIND | search across content and claims at four levels, "naming the level it searched and the level at which it found nothing" | built inside the investigative session's loop; standalone FIND absent | locating the RFP, bids, award, reports |
| PURSUE | "an unsupported claim is a standing OBJECTIVE — find material bearing on it and return candidates, supporting AND cutting against" | built as the investigative session (DEC-62 merges FIND and PURSUE) | "digging into what's going on" toward an objective |
| EXTRACT | "document → CONTENT: propose passages, readings, structure; resolve what a document names to the registry — 'the role that makes everything else addressable'" | 1 of 5 productions built (proposed reading, SK-8); 3 unbuilt; no fleet mode deployed (`extract: { deployed: false }`) | Bob's "reading a document with purpose": an invoice's sections, an RFP's TOC, extract and connect |
| CHECK | "read the record ADVERSARIALLY and raise what it finds — 'the role most worth building' ... 'Sequence the CHECK role first' (DEC-55)" | CHECK-first mode of the investigative session deployed (D-199); standalone/standing CHECK not built/not designed | testing explanations against the record; flagging a falsifier with evidence |

Beside the roles: **request capture** and **initiate an act** (`:47`).

**The ten rules (AR §3)** — all binding; quoted in §2 below. Key for an engine: rule 2 "The machine may not choose the question" (`:54`); rule 3 machine work labelled and graded, "the machine never mints D", A/B only when the source's own link or a shared identifier grounds it (`:55`); rule 4 "A checker raises; it never resolves. No machine credential performs the attested act" and "*A CAPTURE IS NOT EVIDENCE*" until a member stands behind it (`:56`); rule 5 the `ai` credential class, "THE ENDPOINT SURFACE IS THE FENCE ... 'A SKILL MAY NEVER HOLD A GATE'" (`:66`); rule 6 transcripts device-local, never in the record; "The observation log is the structured account of where a search WENT, and it is never a transcript" (`:67`); rule 7 structure only what the member said, unless the member switches on suggestions (off by default, K1479), superseded for the investigative session by DEC-60 ("lets the session formulate claims and legs proactively as suggestions") (`:68`); rule 8 derived informs, authored binds; "a plan proposal is derived, LOOKING derived (D-90), and never itself an acquisition" (`:69`); rule 9 no generated justification, "no single confidence score; no connection-density ranking" (`:70`).

### 4.2 Tensions inside or between the sources

1. **Prefill rule vs labelled drafts and suggestions.** IC §J: "the system must never put words in a member's mouth ... No templates, no 'suggested reason', no LLM-drafted default" (`:423-427`); AR rule 9 "no generated justification anywhere" (`:70`). Against: DEC-120 "A step may place a **labelled draft** in a field for the member to edit and adopt (as DEC-101 and K1019 allow)" (IC `:557-559`); K1479 opt-in suggestions (AR `:68`); DEC-60 lets the investigative session "formulate claims and legs proactively as suggestions" (AR `:68`); DEC-127's machine-drafted translations (IC `:695-699`). The rule has become "never unlabelled, never adopted without the member's act" rather than "never drafted" — but IC §J's absolute wording was not amended. An engine that drafts a proposed finding or a records-request letter sits exactly on this line; which reasons/fields remain draft-free (justifications, completeness statements, conclusions per DEC-27's list) needs a single statement.
2. **No adopt control (UI-102) vs one-act acceptance (DEC-77).** For governing laws, "adopting is not an act the record has" and the form opens EMPTY even with a proposal standing (CM `:3`, `:217`); for contradiction context, "judgements are recommended with a reason and accepted in one attributed act the record remembers as acceptance" (CM `:870`); IC §P lists "adopt" as one of three proposal affordances (`:396`). Three patterns for machine-proposes/member-accepts coexist: (a) no adoption, re-author; (b) adopt as an attributed act recorded *as acceptance*; (c) labelled draft edited then adopted. An engine with many proposals needs to know which applies to which kind. *(inference: the line seems to be whether the adopted content is a legal or vouching claim; not stated as such.)*
3. **"The machine may not choose the question" vs PURSUE and CHECK proposing sub-questions and K1491.** DEC-24 r2 (AR `:54`) against IC's "an assistant-surfaced focus" as a proposal kind (`:379`), DEC-60's proactive claims, and K1491's narrowing to "the machine may point, members decide what to pursue". A planning engine that proposes sub-questions is legitimate only as proposals that look derived (D-82) and age (D-79); the question becomes a member's only on adoption. Consistent once read that way, but AR's rule 2 wording predates K1491 and is unqualified.
4. **Action plan (S11) vs investigation plan.** CM §6a's support-status labels (established / short / hypothetical) and backward question (work list of objectives) are the most investigation-like planning canon, yet attached to *action* plans. *(inference)* Bob's investigation plan would want the same support labelling and the same "gate at the act, not the reasoning" rule, but the canon never extends them to investigating itself. D-165 (backward question) is deferred by Bob with its own trigger (K1500, `build/rulings.md:1502`).
5. **Plan as our deadlines vs progression as theirs** (CM `:950-955`). An investigation engine tracks both the agency's promised schedule (a progression: a gap there is a finding) and our own investigation's schedule (records-request due dates, next steps: never a finding about the world). Any shared "timeline" component must keep the subjects separate.
6. **Run (episode) vs investigation (standing).** AR's run carries conditions, bounds, log and resumability (`:77-80`), but is bounded and per-credential; the investigation outlives many runs and many members. The observation log is the cross-run memory (it "folds" into the general log), but the *planner's* state (objectives, open leads, what to read next and why) has no canon object. Risk of over-building a second run-like object vs under-building by stuffing plan state into run conditions.
7. **"Case" naming.** Bob's "case file" (RESUME.md) vs `case` = a production of a project, signed and published (DEC-72, CM `:590-598`), plus IC's QUEUE "grouped by the case they belong to" (`:106`) where "case" means Focus/Project (DEC-10, `:141-142`). IC itself already uses "case" loosely; the study should name the investigation's home with a new word.
8. **Bounds vs "never decides when to stop".** AR's investigative session stops by code/bounds (`:81`), but Bob's investigation "keeps digging". *(inference)* Iteration must therefore be a sequence of member-started runs (K1481) with the plan carried between them, not one long autonomous run — unless a member-authored standing question (read-only, re-runs a saved search, AI only on new material) is the vehicle.
9. **Stale canon.** CM is non-authoritative and visibly stale on S11, contradiction PRESENT/RESOLVE, consequences, and DEC-32 (§3); IC's build order (`:728-745`) is superseded by MILESTONES M8 (`:14`); AR's frontier "will lag the queue" (`:11`). Any analysis unit must take built status from `build/` and requirements, not from these three files.
10. **Contradiction vs "contact" on the goal axis.** CM warns that Content Framework's "contradicting aspirations" is a different construct ("we do not judge conflict") (`:880-888`); an engine must not import that non-judgement into conflicting *claims* about Grandview (e.g. district says "on schedule" in March, "delayed" in October — a world contradiction, a finding).
