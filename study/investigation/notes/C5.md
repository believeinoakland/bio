## 4. Gaps and tensions found

1. **No investigation construct in CL.** CL covers nine constructs and CONNECTIONS; none is "the investigation" as a whole (objective, plan, case file, reading purpose). Investigation appears only as consumer ("where investigation runs", l.706; "investigation can read them", l.47). The closest are QUESTIONS L4 (question → bounded run) and the `inquiry` in layer 6. Per CL §1 l.24–27 a new need is added to an existing register; but §10 l.1114 forbids "drift into … case management" (DEC-48, D224, D231). An engine with a "case file" construct must show it "takes a place in an existing construct" (inference: the inquiry/project) or go to Bob as a decision. **Tension**, needs a ruling or a careful fit.
2. **Q4 is the engine's rung, and it is the latest and least designed.** QUESTIONS L4 is stage 4, "Size: not studied", prerequisites Q1–Q3 plus every construct's first services plus VF-4 plus measured run cost (l.1056–1059). Bob's engine would need Q4 well before stage 4 *(inference)*; reordering is Bob's (stages open by tranche, K1425).
3. **"Machine proposes" vs "no standing AI run".** Bob's engine "starts digging"; CL allows AI only at a member's act or a member's standing question (read-only, no outside fan-out, l.1064, l.1067). A multi-step engine that plans and fetches over weeks must be modelled as a sequence of member-started runs, each bounded, or a new ruling is needed *(inference)*. Standing questions explicitly exclude "fan-out to outside sources" (l.1064) and fee-bearing sources (l.1067).
4. **Targeted extraction (K1468) vs "read deeper and deeper".** Bob's reader studies a document by its purpose and explores deeper. CL permits machine extraction "only at a member's request for a basis or claim, or by a member's act scoped to a body and period; never a sweep" (l.1109). A purpose-driven reader is compatible if each document's reading is tied to a member-accepted plan step *(inference)*; whether an accepted plan counts as "a member's request" is a doctrine question for Bob.
5. **Purpose-aware reading has no home.** CL's reading capabilities are per-construct EXTRACT proposals (dated facts, events, money facts, duties, people), each at stage 3, measured on gold sets (e.g. contract obligations ≤70.56%, l.348; LLM event extraction ~42 F1 behind strict, l.566; temporal 70–78%, l.227).
