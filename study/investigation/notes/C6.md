# C6 · The investigative session design and the constructs study synthesis

Unit C6, phase 1 (PROTOCOL §R). Scope: `docs/development/INVESTIGATIVE-SESSION.md` (whole) and `study/investigation/prior/constructs-study-synthesis.md` (whole), checked against `build/rulings.md` for every ruling they cite.


## 1a · `docs/development/INVESTIGATIVE-SESSION.md` — what it says, as it bears on investigation

**What it is.** The level-2 design (v3, 2026-08-07, by Bob with session BOB; status line at `INVESTIGATIVE-SESSION.md:3`) of the "investigative session": a skilled AI run that reads an inquiry, searches evidence across four levels (meaning, content, documents, open internet) and writes SUGGESTED *basis versions* a member accepts or rejects. It carries the reasoning for DEC-60 (proactive claim formulation), DEC-61 (device-local transcripts) and DEC-62 (pursue and investigate are one session) (`:76-80`, `:103-108`). Its status line says it is "complete as design and superseded by the build": `IS-BUILD-PLAN.md` decomposed §18 into 43 rows, recorded COMPLETE 2026-09-13 (`:3`; `:28`). Since then it has been amended in place by many BOB rulings (BOB #15–#33) and their builds (REC-, D-, UI- rows), recorded in its front matter and "Built" paragraphs. Its §17 "what is NOT settled" list is declared history (`:21`).

**The unit of investigation it assumes.** Not a case file. The subject is ONE **inquiry** (a question with a claim field, a basis of legs and a falsifier), inside a **project** that sets the evidence standard. A *claim is a field of an inquiry*, not an object (§3, `:253-265`). The session's output attaches to that inquiry's `basis[]`. It has no construct for an investigation spanning many inquiries, documents and leads over months *(inference: nothing in §3–§11 names such a container; leads go to notifications, §17 item 1, `:1695-1702`)*.

## 3 · Where the two agree, and where later decisions moved on

### 3.1 Where they agree

The two were written two months apart for different questions — the IS design for *how an AI run forms an inquiry's basis*, the synthesis for *what world facts the record can hold and walk* — and they meet cleanly at five seams:

| seam | IS design | synthesis | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Machine proposes, member accepts | §4: no op that accepts; outputs `suggested` | §4.5: proposals labelled, at a member's request; machine writes only identifier-backed rows; "never … a conclusion, an attestation" | Same line, drawn at two altitudes: basis versions (IS) and world facts (synthesis). Both are what K1627 restates. |
| Grades earned, one family, weakest governs | §5: grades from resolutions, never minted; DEC-18 inert-and-named | §4.2 (c): one A–D family on two axes for every new fact; derived graded by weakest input | The synthesis extends the IS rule to events, lines, money and people facts without a new scale. |
| Independence of legs | §12 (a)–(c): D-195 shared upstream origin derived and shown | INT C-18: independence also fails on a shared person, event origin, ledger table, `acts_for` or `within` tie | Agreement, widened: an engine composing OR-branches must use the wider test *(inference: whether `#independenceOf` reads these new owners was not checked here; for a module unit)*. |

## 4 · Gaps and tensions found

Measured against Bob's K1627 goal (the interview; a planner that stays on the objectives and knows why each document is held; research runs; competing explanations; the answer; member in the loop; procurement documents; purpose-driven reading; the project as home with one view and a timeline) and the school-rebuild scenario in `RESUME.md`.

**G1 · No construct holds an investigation.** The IS design's subject is one inquiry; its durable object is the *run*, which is scratch with an expiry (IS §11, `:801-805`). The synthesis's subject is the record of world facts; its only investigation-shaped home is "the working inquiry" for hypotheses (`:194`). Neither has an object that holds *objectives, the questions under them, the documents gathered and why each is held, the leads, the plan and its state* across weeks. K1627 names the project as that home; the IS design's §7/§7.1 project-relationship machinery (CURRENT, conclusions per project) is the nearest built substrate. *(inference)*

**G2 · No intake or interview.** The IS session starts from an inquiry that already exists ("a member with an inquiry presses a button", `:101`). §8's *sharpen the question* is the only clarifying act, and it operates on a written question. The interactive mode "may ask any applicable question" (§10) but is designed as review of a completed run, not intake. Bob's "maybe asks a few follow-on questions" has no home. *(absent in both)*

**G3 · No planning object, and the doctrine constrains one.** IS §14b.4 puts control flow in code and keeps "when the loop stops" from the model (TREC 2011 evidence). A planner that "stays focused on its objective(s)" is therefore judgement *inside* a deterministic loop: it may propose the next searches and sub-questions, but bounds, stopping and gates stay code. The run's objective today is fixed ("claims and legs supported by evidence"); a member-authored objective list per investigation is not specified anywhere in these sources. The synthesis's plans are the group's *action* plans (K1466), a different layer (9).

**G4 · Reading with purpose is undesigned.** Both sources assume readers by known document type (docprofile, doctypes, budget-doctypes, office readers) plus targeted extraction (K1468) and the after-read hook. Neither says how a document of an unfamiliar kind is read: told *why it is held*, structure first (TOC, sections), then deeper. IS §14b.1's progressive disclosure ("per-format knowledge load[s] when the run reaches work that needs them") and report-returning sub-sessions are the nearest mechanism; K1627's "a recurring kind earns an approved reading profile" connects to docprofile's profile pattern *(inference)*.

**G5 · Procurement is pieces, not a construct.** Present: award and payment events; change orders amending an award; `committedAgainstPaid`; amount-free junction checks (award without solicitation, one response, payments past term) in `progressions`; Open Contracting red flags as C8 detectors. Absent: RFP/IFB, addenda, bid submissions, bid tabulation, award recommendation, contract terms (completion date, liquidated damages, change-order limits), performance bonds, contractor licence and prior-performance records as recognised document kinds; a schedule-of-record (baseline vs current completion) as dated facts. Oakland publishes no contract register or payment ledger (K1506), so the school-rebuild case would rest on records requests *(inference from K1506; schools may be a school district, not the City — the profile question is open)*.

**G6 · "Competing explanations" sits at two altitudes that are not joined.** IS versions are alternative *compositions of one claim's support*; the synthesis's hypotheses (K1467) are alternative *causes, identities, relations, flows*. Bob's "what's going on?" is a set of competing causal explanations (contractor default, change orders, funding gap, design error, permit delay), each needing its own evidence. Under DEC-32's falsifier test each would be its own inquiry (IS §3, §6.3b, §8), held under one project. Nothing in either source shows the set of explanations side by side with their evidence state. *(inference)*

**G7 · Project run over several questions has no target (D-572).** Still open (K83, K86; IS `:27`). If the project is the home, multi-question runs are normal, and *this level is empty* candidates are refused `SUGGEST_NO_TARGET` meanwhile.

**G8 · Runs are bounded and one-shot; an investigation iterates.** IS §14b.6 bounds each run; §11 rule 1 makes an interactive session after a background run open a new run. A months-long investigation is therefore a series of runs, each with its own lens-at-open, standard pair and observation log. Nothing aggregates the series' observation logs into "what we have looked at, ever" for the investigation. The `observation-log` module (moved within layer 5 by the synthesis, K1470) holds the group's looks; whether run logs feed it was not checked here *(for a module unit)*.

**G9 · Time and promises are well served by the synthesis but unused by the IS design.** Bob's scenario turns on a promise ("no longer than 1 school year") against a schedule slip. The synthesis provides dated facts with precision, events (award, notice to proceed, completion), duties with recorded occurrences, timelines and three-valued sequence. The IS design predates all of it and never mentions time. A promise made in a board presentation is a dated statement (an event with a `stated_cause`-style attribution?) or a commitment-basis duty (K1431: "a due date's basis may be … a commitment") — which one is undecided *(inference)*.

**G10 · Budget and live spend.** IS §19 F11: the bound is visible only after the fact; no member-facing budget at launch, no live spend. Under K1502 the member pays from their own subscription, so a visible budget per investigation matters more *(inference)*.

**G11 · The no-AI version.** K1547 requires a product without AI whose capabilities are a subset. Both sources assume the AI for proposing; the synthesis's member acts (declare a trail, adopt an event, record an identity claim) are the no-AI path. An investigation engine's planner and reader have no stated no-AI fallback *(absent)*.

**G12 · Stale text in the IS design that a reader could act on.** Its own front matter lists these (`:12-29`): §14b.2 still frames D-222 as a precondition (landed); "UNBUILDABLE TODAY" for the bias object (built, PL-12); D-218 "to MEASURE" (measured); §14a's `[BOB-4]` provisional (superseded by DEC-52 final); §17 is history; and, not listed there, the §14a cascade (superseded by K1502). Section numbers in the IS doc are stable; its BOB/REC/IC/D- numbering belongs to the retired process (CLAUDE.md), so citations into it should name the section, not the row *(inference)*.

**G13 · Label collision "C12".** See §3.2: K1470 withdraws the synthesis's C12 (hunches), K1473 records a ruling on "C12" about machine signals. Cite by K-number.

**G14 · The word "case".** The IS design and the canon use *case* for the signed, published container (DEC-72; IS §13); RESUME.md warns of this. The synthesis uses "case" for worked examples ("R-3 case 2") and "the founding case". An investigation design must not call its home a case file *(per RESUME.md)*.
