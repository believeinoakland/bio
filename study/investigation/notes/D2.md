## 2. How a member starts and follows a pursuit today, in the design

The design (not the built product, which has no member screens for this: HANDOFF §4 "no member-facing panel, prompt box or wizard exists") describes it as follows.

**Starting.**
1. The member arrives at the group's home, which asks **"What brought you here?"**; every empty project offers **"Start from…"** (journeys §3). Front doors are a problem they live with (first) or something they read: a code, court case, news story, budget, meeting, contract, regulatory proceeding, dataset, tip, another group's case (journeys §3 table).
2. For a lived problem (journey 4): a private note in their own words (gap: not built, J11 open), what they saw, then "Finds the standard the city set itself, with the assistant's help if wanted", turns it into "a question the record can answer", finds or requests records (journey 5), then **joins a project already working on it, or starts one** (journey 4 step 6).
3. The "Your first question" wizard (journeys §5) walks: what drew you in → say it as a question (assistant suggests some) → find or capture a document → point at the passage → say whether it supports or cuts against → see strength against the project's bar "and what would raise it".
4. Clarifying the claim: journey 6 step 2 pins down terms ("what counts as reported, as filled, and over what period"), recording "Undetermined, because the city does not define it" where the city is silent. ASSISTANT-PILOT §2 (via oq-research Q10) has the assistant ask "at most one clarifying question".

**Following.**
5. Evidence work is journey 7: capture → vouch (release) → open a question (or the assistant suggests one) → cite for and against → conclude with what would change the conclusion → owner checks strength against the bar and clears hunches.
6. The assistant (journey 9) is opened in a docked panel beside the work, asked "to find, pursue, extract or check"; it requests captures ("they arrive over time"), suggests documents, versions and questions labelled as machine work; the member accepts or rejects each. In the inventory it works inside an **assistant run** — "A bounded, attributed working session a member opens for the assistant over a question or project" with modes check / investigate / extract / plan, "only check deployed" (`ux-substrate-v2.json` construct "Assistant run (AI run)").
7. Leads: a lead is "a place to look, never evidence", with looks recorded (Never looked / Looked) and a queue path to "Take it up into a question / set it aside" (`ux-substrate-v2.json` construct "Lead"; observation-log R14–R21; queue R18 "not yet met", K533). The journeys page does not give leads a journey of their own *(inference from reading the seventeen)*.
8. Over time: the daily queue (journey 10) carries To do, Noticed, Signal, grouped by case; due dates from records requests; "Overdue" once; newer versions of cited documents to adopt or keep; "pick up where you left off" with unfinished work listed (wide-path rule 6). Source changes reach members via monitoring (journey 14).
9. Expertise is routed by asking for a check (journey 8; gap: no finding of members by expertise).
10. When something is found wrong, the pursuit continues as a **plan** opened "while the inquiry is still young" (journey 13): matters (plan subjects) with support status (established / short / hypothetical), options suggested by the assistant five at a time and chosen or declined with reasons, scenarios with checkpoints, determinations against standards, escalation stages proposed by the system and decided by a member, actions started and sent with outward warnings, clocks tracked, outcome recorded, plan closed (views/plan-page, matter-page, start-and-send, surfaces).
11. The answer is a published **case** (journey 7 end; journey 16 for readers) or an action (journey 13).

**What the design does not yet describe** *(from §6 Gaps and J12)*: following a person, building a timeline, a money trail, exploring connections (J12, proposed for after C3); calculation over a dataset; explaining a charge from its ordinance or rate schedule; code structure; following a court case; meetings and recurring time; offices over time. There is no journey whose unit is "an investigation" spanning several questions, documents, leads and runs toward one objective, other than the project itself *(inference)*.

## 3. What the design names the working container

- **Project** is the working container. Glossary: "A shared workspace where some members build a case together; hidden unless made discoverable", with the warning "a project holds questions, it is not one" (`ux-substrate-v2.json` glossary "Project"). Construct: "A working group inside the copy with owners and participants, an objective, and the questions and cases it works on" (construct "Project"), key fields: title, **objective (required)**, owners, participants, visibility, strength bar, CURRENT versions, conclusions, computed **stage** (Forming, Investigating, Matured, Closed with reason; State Rules §4.3, publication R44–R47, built T12 N300), case readiness, **kind of work** (reporting, fixing, legal, oversight, other; action-plans R21), its plans. The journeys use it the same way ("Joins a project already working on it, or starts one"; "every empty project offers 'Start from…'"; "a project page" is where "Your first question" starts). An imported case lands in "its own read-only project" (principle 5.4).
- Inside it, the unit of pursuit is the **question** (internal "inquiry"; "Something the group is trying to answer, and the evidence it rests on"), which becomes a **finding** when concluded; the project's **objective** has a checkable condition, progress and gaps, and the assistant can "work the objective" (construct "Objective, progress and gaps"; intent R1–R7, R15–R18).
- Action-side containers: the **plan** ("A project's working notes for deciding what to do about one or more matters ... never published") and its **matters** (plan subjects; the members' word "Matter" vs "Subject" is Bob's to decide per the glossary; HANDOFF §4 step 5 calls them "Matters").
- The word **"case"** is reserved for what a project publishes ("a set of findings and evidence, signed, that anyone can check"); before DEC-72 "a case was a phase of a question" (glossary "Case"). **"Investigation"** is not a construct name anywhere in the scope; the word appears only in HANDOFF §4 ("this investigation began from a tip"), in the principle-4 outward warning ("anyone you are investigating"), and as the project stage "Investigating" and the run mode "investigate". "Experienced investigator" is an audience (journeys §1). Principle 2.4 asks the screens to say "my questions", not "inquiries".

## 5. Gaps and tensions found

1. **No named investigation container beyond the project.** The design's pursuit lives in a project (objective, questions, plans) and in assistant runs scoped "over a question or project". Bob's "case file" (RESUME) has no counterpart by that name; "case" is the signed publication, so the study's naming caution in RESUME is consistent with this stream. Whether a project is the investigation's home, or one investigation spans projects, is not addressed in scope *(inference)*.
2. **The assistant's pursuit is specified, not built at the member surface.** Journey 9 and principle family 6 describe it; HANDOFF §4 says only check mode is deployed, no panel exists, planning mode "looks ready to switch on but is off"; the question-to-search flow (ASSISTANT-PILOT §2) "is designed, not built" (HANDOFF §4; journeys §6 "Explaining a charge").
3. **Reading with purpose is not in the design's vocabulary.** The design has "Point at the passage that matters" (wizard "Your first question") and machine "Propose readings of a document" (extract mode, run-productions R10–R11), but no journey describes the assistant reading a document by its role in the pursuit (an RFP's TOC, an invoice's sections) *(inference)*.
4. **Leads lack a journey**, though the construct is built with gaps and the lead→question queue path is "not yet met" (K533).
5. **Capability gaps the school-rebuild scenario would hit** (journeys §6, HANDOFF §4): no calculation over datasets; no org relations ("contracts with") or role holders over time; no recurring meetings, UTC days; no court-case tracking; no document effective dates; chronology only in the counsel packet; contract obligations are declared as standards (journeys §3 "A contract, franchise or agreement"), and a contract's schedule promise ("no longer than one school year") would be a standard with a deadline *(inference)*. All ruled to reach their full ladders (K1432), none opened.
6. **Private notes** (J11) open: journey 4 starts from a note only its author sees; today only a shared observation exists.
7. **Planning appears twice**: the assistant can "plan" a wizard on the fly (principle 6.6) and the plan mode proposes action-plan options (glossary "Five at a time"; agent-worker R50–R53); neither plans an investigation's research steps toward an objective. The "work the objective" act (intent R18) is the nearest, with "surface: none" (construct "Objective").
8. **Clock tension**: wide-path rule 6 lists unfinished work; DEC-69 forbids nagging; Operational Principle 3 "Deadlines are deadlines" (oq-research Q14). A long pursuit with many pending captures and requests must live within "told once" *(inference)*.
9. **Design is paused and step 3 unrecorded.** Any investigation journey would be new step-3 material (cf. J12) through the design stream (README rule 3: BOB never edits this folder).
10. **README/HANDOFF drift**: README says "fourteen journeys and nine wizard outlines"; the page has seventeen and eleven (noted, not judged).
