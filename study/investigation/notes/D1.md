# D1 · Bob's rulings (docs/development/DECISIONS.md) as they bind an investigation engine

Unit D1, phase 1 (§R). Source: `docs/development/DECISIONS.md` at the pinned tree (`SOURCES-PIN` c30c6ca). Read whole, lines 1–2056.


## 0 · The file itself (lines 1–98)

`DECISIONS.md` is the register of Bob's rulings ("the return channel", est. 2026-07-31). Its process framing (CONDUCT, QUEUE, `plancheck`) belongs to the retired process (CLAUDE.md), but the rulings themselves are live doctrine. Two framing rules carry over as doctrine:
- **What is Bob's** (§"Two rules", l.39–46): *"doctrine (what the record means and may claim), risk carrying his name (legal, the City), effects on people outside the project, and the gated mechanical acts."* Mechanism, sequencing, scoping are not his. An investigation-engine design must sort its open questions on this line.
- **An open decision never blocks work** (l.29–37): run a provisional only when it is cheap to reverse; otherwise run the cheap alternative.
- Answered entries from 2026-08-04..08-10 were rolled to `docs/archive/ledgers/DECISIONS-2026-08.md` (l.90–97) and are **not in this unit's scope**; `node tools/decided.mjs` (retired tooling) searched them. Gap noted in §4.

## 1 · Rulings, in file order, as they bear on investigation

### DEC-69 (l.99–148) · no nagging, no second-guessing — **binds notifications, prompts, acceptance**
- *"THE WORKFLOW MUST NOT NAG OR SECOND-GUESS MEMBERS. RESPECT FOR MEMBERS AND THEIR JUDGMENT IS A REQUIREMENT, AND ANYTHING SHORT OF IT IS A FLAW."*
- Boundary: *"INFORMING AT THE ACT, ONCE, IS RESPECT"*; *"REPEATING, RE-CONFIRMING, OR MEASURING is the flaw"* — incl. "are you sure" on reversible acts, *"responsibility reminders detached from any act"*, *"grading or counting a member's attention"*.
- Rung-ladder ceremonies for terminal/attested/irreversible acts are not second-guessing.
- Amendment (bulk): *"wherever a set of decisions arises, the member is ENABLED to act singly or in bulk and is FORCED into neither."*
- *"The failure asymmetry ('when uncertain, be noisy') is a rule about the RECORD'S OWN CLAIMS, and licenses no noise directed at members."*
- Engine consequence: an engine that proposes many leads/documents/next steps must offer per-item and bulk acceptance; may tell a member a thing once at the act; may not chase, re-ask, or remind detached from an act.

### DEC-68 (l.150–201) · no surveillance of reading; the approval IS the act — **binds acceptance, measurement**
- Bob: *"If the user approves it, then it's approved."* Read-event logging refused: *"logging what a member LOOKED AT is a doctrine question about surveilling members"*.
- *"a machine act stands attributed AS machine; a member act binds AS the member's"*; *"Authored acts bind; the approval IS the act."*
- Engine consequence: no telemetry of which proposed document text a member read before accepting; no "diligence" score on acceptance. If a property matters, *"the honest instrument is asking members, not logging them."*

### DEC-72 (l.203–249) · case is a production of a project; the bar is the project's — **binds projects, grading, the answer**
- *"The bar — that is, the standard of evidence — is a property of a project, not an inquiry or claim."* Nothing composes across projects.
- *"Only findings that are part of a project can be published"*; publication pins versions.
- *"All load-bearing findings of a case being published must meet the necessary bar. Other findings/claims that don't meet the bar can be a part of the published work, though they aren't presented as load-bearing."*
- Publisher must be a project manager (= existing owner role); a finding has *"lasting value"* and may serve many cases across projects.
- Engine consequence: an investigation lives in (or is) a project, inherits its standard of evidence; its findings outlive it and are reusable; the study must not collide "case" with the investigation's home (RESUME.md note).

### DEC-71 (l.251–316) · superseded by DEC-72; Bob's clarification of the bar — **binds grading**
- Bob: the standard *"doesn't require that every piece of evidence must meet that standard. Rather, it means that the overall findings in that project must meet the standard — even if some evidence cited is below the necessary grade."* Draft-agenda example: weak evidence *"can still be included … The citation doesn't have to be severed."*
- Axes stay separate: a document can be *"STRONG on capture and weak on connection"*.
- Engine consequence: the engine must not discard or hide weak documents to protect a grade; it keeps them, graded honestly.

### DEC-70 (l.318–378) · severance discharges support, never connection — **binds holding over time, notifications**
- Bob: *"keeping evidence connected has value over the lifetime."* *"SEVERANCE DISCHARGES SUPPORT, NEVER CONNECTION."*
- *"The connection INFORMS, never binds"*; if a re-evaluation feed is ever pushed *"it tells ONCE, is dispositionable, and ages (D-79, D-266's machinery) — never a recurring nag."*
- Engine consequence: the template for any engine notification (a document changed, a status report arrived, a deadline passed): told once, dispositionable, ages.

### DEC-2 (l.380–416) · root of trust, deferred
Not engine-binding; trigger = a multi-member instance. Mentioned only because any engine credential (e.g. records-request mail, API keys) sits under the same unresolved custody model *(inference)*.

### DEC-25 (l.418–451) · action plans are never published (deferred, constraint attached) — **binds the investigation's working material**
- Provisional in force: *"THE PLAN IS WORKING MATERIAL AND IS NEVER PUBLISHED"*; if ever reversed, *"it can only apply PROSPECTIVELY … Retroactively publishing deliberation that was recorded under a privacy assumption would be a betrayal of the members who wrote it."*
- Engine consequence *(inference)*: an investigation plan (hypotheses, next steps, the engine's planning state) is the same kind of strategic deliberation; the safe reading is that it stays working material, private, unless Bob rules otherwise — and the rule must be fixed before members write candidly in it.

### DEC-31 (l.454–533) · addressed non-public delivery → "review/advance copy" — **binds the answer's delivery**
- In-band rule (2026-08-03, in force): *"any rendering that leaves the instance addressed to someone carries its hash, date, author and both threshold floors inside itself."*
- 2026-09-17 Bob: a review copy *"is mutable. Only a real publish is not."*; *"a review copy never leaves the instance"*; recipient reaches it by *"a scoped, revocable, read-and-comment grant"* (mechanism, BOB's). Completeness: a review copy *"states its own gaps in the same words a published case does"* (`searched` section, undetermined-is-first-class), and a gate that pressured a member to fill gaps *"would be a bug in the gate (DEC-69)"*. Naming (advance/review copy) put back to Bob (DEC-8: member-facing vocabulary is Bob's).
- Engine consequence: an interim investigative answer shared with e.g. a school-board member is a review copy inside the fence, carrying its gaps; any export carries hash/date/author/floors.

### DEC-32 (l.535–734) · parallel claims, AND/OR strength, elicitation — **binds grading, hypotheses, questions to members**
- Arithmetic: *"strength is the MINIMUM over AND-related legs and the MAXIMUM over OR-related branches"*; *"a flat implicit-AND basis is now known to be wrong."*
- Object identity by citability: *"does anything need to cite this part ALONE?"*; distinct propositions with own falsifiers are separate inquiries (recursion).
- Elicitation, binding: *"NEVER show AND / OR / disjunction / grounds — not even as tooltips"*; *"ASK ONE CONSEQUENCE QUESTION PER LEG … 'If this turned out to be wrong, would your answer still hold?'"* (surface spelling: *"Would refuting this alone change your conclusion?"*, amendment l.727–734); *"SHOW THE DERIVED FALSIFIER BACK, in plain words"*; *"THE DEFAULT IS AND, AND THAT IS THE ANTI-GAMING KEYSTONE"*; *"THE STRUCTURE IS AUTHORED BEFORE THE STRENGTH IS SHOWN"*; restructuring after seeing strength *"LEGAL, RECORDED AND ATTRIBUTED — never blocked"*; *"a machine may not refuse the act and must not hide it"*; the reader is the final check.
- Bob's build constraint: *"the average CivicOS [member] doesn't have a philosophy degree … the system needs to support the user … we don't want a user to be able to game the system"*.
- Research finding (D-195): OR-max is sound only if branches are independent; the system can derive shared upstream provenance and surface it (*"derived informs, authored binds"*).
- Engine consequence: competing explanations (hypotheses) are separate inquiries when they are distinct propositions; the engine never asks members for logical structure, only consequences; the engine may derive and surface shared-origin (circular reporting) between legs, never decide independence itself.
- Contradiction (Q14: two conclusions disagreeing) is *"SEPARATE and stays undesigned"* — a gap for competing explanations.


### DEC-33 (l.736–780) · publication ceremony deferred; substrate first
- Bob: *"The publication process is very involved. Defer anything related to the process, though create a placeholder surface."* Confirmed 2026-09-18: *"we need a solid substrate before building on top of it."*
- Bears on the engine as method: Bob's sequencing doctrine is substrate before surfaces; an engine stage that ends in publication cannot assume the member ceremony exists (re-entry: a group publishing without its operator).
- Honest narration of an absent capability (the "Q12 rule") is the placeholder pattern — an engine that cannot do a step says so on the surface.

### DEC-39 (l.782–846) · co-attestation fence states the question it answers — **binds capture and documents, grading wording**
- Bob: *"Yes, it must report the question it answers."* The plane owns fence wording and publishes it with the act.
- Drafted wording (CONDUCT's, under the ruling): *"What co-attestation answers: when did these bytes exist?"*; *"What it does not answer: whether the document is TRUE, whether its source is authoritative, or how close it stands to the fact you are citing it for. A secondhand report that is co-attested is still a secondhand report."*; it *"never reaches Grade A"*.
- D-184 (left open): *"a member's FIRSTHAND observation has no home as a basis leg"* — directly relevant to Bob's school example (a parent who saw the site idle).
- Engine consequence: any machine statement about what a document is worth must come from the plane's published wording, not be authored by the engine or surface; capture strength (when did bytes exist) is distinct from truth and from directness.

### DEC-43 (l.847–892) · ADMIN_TOKEN fallback retirement — not engine-binding
Mechanism/fleet. Method carried over: *"MEASURE, DO NOT ASSUME"*; an absent count must not read as clean.

### DEC-53 (l.894–953) · how far the machine may propose toward an ESTABLISHED record — **binds proposals and acceptance, AI runs**
- Question: whether *"a member is now one click from an established resolution off a machine-composed list."*
- Answered by DEC-52 (Bob 2026-08-07, rolled-off entry): *"allowing the machine to rule doesn't go against doctrine. So it can rule"* — a machine credential may declare, resolve and thread directly into the record, **machine-attributed**.
- Guardrails: *"the record names the machine principal, a machine statement is visibly machine-attributed (D-82), grades stay EARNED (`op=resolve` is the only grader, `grade_if_resolved` is conditional and honest)"*; *"Bulk acceptance is the same act over a set."*
- The watch item (accepts-without-reading) was later **withdrawn** by DEC-68.
- Engine consequence: this is the widest licence in the register — an engine may both *propose* graded candidates (with what acceptance would mint shown honestly) and, under a machine credential, *act* directly, provided the act is machine-attributed and grades are earned by the record's own grader, never asserted by the engine. Note the tension with Bob's study framing "the machine proposes and a member accepts" (§4).

### DEC-51 (l.955–1013) · grade note rendered whole at the moment of capture — **binds capture**
- *"RENDER IT — WHOLE — AT THE MOMENT OF CAPTURE"*; *"a surface that receives the record's own account and discards it is withholding at exactly the moment the member forms the belief, which the failure asymmetry forbids (when uncertain, be noisy)."*
- Verbatim from the plane's export (DEC-49 translation discipline); no surface-computed grade letter.
- Engine consequence: when the engine captures a document on a member's behalf (RFP, bid tabulation, status report), the record's own capture note travels with the capture to the member, once, whole (consistent with DEC-69's "inform at the act, once").

### DEC-50 (l.1015–1057) · a grouped question refuses a new leg — **binds proposals onto structured findings**
- *"THE SHIPPED REFUSAL STANDS"*: `op=cite` against a GROUPED question is refused; route is *"ungroup with a reason → cite → regroup"*; *"the friction lands exactly where the accounting belongs: on the person changing a structured case's shape."*
- Engine consequence: an engine that finds a new document for an already-structured finding cannot silently attach it; it must propose, and a member (or attributed act) states where it belongs.

### DEC-48 (l.1059–1097) · no single-document container until a group asks
- *"a capability that does not serve the path is not obviously worth building"* (Bob, 2026-08-01, via CLAUDE.md). Bytes remain answerable by hash.
- Engine consequence: the build-only-what-the-path-needs doctrine applies to the study's architecture (no speculative exports of investigation material).

### DEC-73 (l.1100–1139) · git author identity — not engine-binding.

### DEC-74 (l.1141–1183) · external OCR not funded — **binds reading and cost**
- *"NOT FUNDED"*; image-only pages have a tier-3 path (tesseract-wasm `ocr-worker`, cap C). External tier reconsidered *"when an image-only document is LOAD-BEARING in a real case and C is below that project's bar; that is a funding request with a document and a bar attached, brought to Bob then — DEC-35's word still governs spending."*

### DEC-75 (l.1185–1235) · a conversion is a derivation step, cap UNDETERMINED until calibrated — **binds capture and reading**
- *"CAPTURE GRADE IS ABOUT THE FETCH PATH; A CONVERSION IS A DERIVATION STEP IN THE CHAIN"*; *"every derivation step weakens, and a step whose fidelity was never measured has cap UNDETERMINED, stated, never a letter."*
- The direction rule: claim less now and raise by calibration later; *"the reverse (a letter now, lowered later under authored legs) is the move Bob's 5.8 forbids."*
- Engine consequence: every transformation an engine applies while reading (OCR, PDF-to-text, table extraction, an AI summary or extraction) is a chain step that weakens and, until calibrated, caps at UNDETERMINED *(inference: AI extraction is a derivation step under this rule; DEC-75 rules on Google conversion, Part I chain rules generalise it)*.

### DEC-76 (l.1237–1253) · contradiction is conditional; resolution kinds — **binds competing explanations, hypotheses, machine proposals**
- *"A contradiction is CONDITIONAL: two assertions that cannot both hold IN THE SAME RESPECT"*; *"A coordinate offered to dissolve a conflict is itself a claim that needs evidence; until it has it, the conflict is hypothetically dissolved, not resolved. The machine may PROPOSE which coordinates differ, labelled as machine work; it never picks one (DEC-24)."*
- *"A candidate a member takes up becomes an INQUIRY (the one recursive object)"*; each assumption explored is a sub-inquiry.
- Families DISSOLVED / CORRECTED / GENUINE / IRRECONCILABLE; *"OBLIGATION AGAINST ACT routes into `conformance`"* (a promise vs. what the government did — Bob's school case: promised one school year, two years elapsed); WORLD candidates are *"leads to take up or dismiss with a reason"*; RECORD candidates carry a duty.
- Publication discloses, never blocks, an unresolved RECORD contradiction.
- Engine consequence: the inquiry is the engine's unit of hypothesis; a broken promise is a conformance determination (obligation against act), not a new mechanism; engine-found conflicts in the wider world are leads, dismissible with a reason.

### DEC-77 (l.1255–1270) · inline presentation; ACCEPTING A PROPOSAL; context supports, never silently decides — **binds proposals and acceptance, UX of machine work**
- Six forms incl. a timeline for a reversal, the Key Assumptions Check, a *"competing-hypotheses matrix"*, and Criteria/Condition/Cause/Effect/Recommendation (*"the government auditing standard's elements of a finding"*) for obligation-against-act.
- *"CONTEXT SUPPORTS, NEVER SILENTLY DECIDES. (a) A FACT the record holds is filled in with its source … (b) A JUDGEMENT is RECOMMENDED with its reason, labelled machine work, shown first, and accepted in one act; the record keeps whether the member chose unaided or accepted a recommendation, and which one. This is a new act, ACCEPTING A PROPOSAL … introduced for contradictions first … (c) Nothing is silently preselected: a value a member never saw does not enter the record under their name."*
- *"The guard against steering is measured: the acceptance rate of recommendations, per kind, is reported, and a rate near all is the signal that recommendations have become decisions"*.
- Engine consequence: this is the act the study's "machine proposes, member accepts" is built on — it exists in doctrine, scoped to contradictions *first*; extending it to investigation proposals (next steps, documents to pull, hypotheses) is a reconsideration Bob anticipated ("governing laws and risk tiers are reconsidered under it separately"). Note: an aggregate per-kind acceptance rate is ruled here; DEC-68 forbade counting a member's attention — the two are reconciled as an aggregate, per-kind measure of the *recommender*, not of a member *(inference)*.

### DEC-78 (l.1272–1294) · a knock (tip) enters the record; source identity evolves — **binds intake of tips and member-brought documents, people**
- *"Pulling ADMITS the knock's material as a capture at `collected` … and commits the member to nothing more. Vouching stays with release."*
- *"A whistleblower is still a source, though unnamed."* The bringing member is the capturing actor, *"never recorded as its source"*.
- Knocker continuity by a secret the knocker keeps; *"continuity as possession of the same secret, never as identity"*.
- Note *"labelled as the knocker's and never as evidence of its truth"*; contact never in the record.
- Source identity as dated, attributed disclosure history; (a) *"The group is never the first to make a source more public"*; (e) when identity firms up, *"the change in their material's weight reaches every finding resting on it as a re-evaluation notice, never a silent regrade."*
- Engine consequence: a parent's account and documents they bring (e.g. a school-district email forwarded to them) enter with the parent as source and the member as capturer; an inside tipster at the contractor is an unnamed source with possible continuity; nothing the engine does may expose a source.

### DEC-79 (l.1296–1308) · project stage display — **binds projects**
- Four stages: `forming`, `investigating`, `matured` computed; `closed` the owner's reasoned act. *"what is still needed is stated from the same rule that computes the stage, so the display can never promise a stage the computation would not give"*; *"an abandoned project is not a completed one"*.
- Engine consequence: an investigation's progress display must be computed from the record, not from the engine's own optimism; "investigating" is already a project stage name.

### DEC-80 (l.1310–1326) · publication ceremony built in the redesign; browser signing
- Five steps re-derived for DEC-72 (what becomes permanent; what this rests on; what you are leaving out; the edition; sign); pre-flight runs *"the real refusals, without writing"*, incl. *"the uncleared-hunch refusal (case-authoring R12)"*.
- Engine consequence: the end of an investigation that publishes passes the ceremony; an engine's uncleared hunches block publication (R12) — hypotheses must be cleared or excluded.

### DEC-81 (l.1328–1344) · co-attested B suffices to publish; Grade A deferred — **binds capture, cost**
- *"A CO-ATTESTED Grade B … is sufficient to publish a work product resting on the document."* *"Grade A stays the ceiling for adversarial or legal use."*
- A failed co-attestation may still publish, *"deliberately, visibly and repairably"*, marked *"self-attested only"*; *"never refused outright, because some sources can never be co-archived and a refusal would push groups to drop evidence rather than disclose its limits."* oaklandca.gov refuses the Internet Archive crawler.
- Grade A deferred (Bob: *"I don't see the need for this support as being high enough a priority"*), options in `GRADE-A-CAPTURE.md`.
- Engine consequence: the engine's captures of city documents earn at best co-attested B; legal-grade (A) evidence for e.g. a lawsuit over the school contract is not available.

### DEC-82 (l.1346–1359) · the measures map; workspace shows strength — **binds grading display, honesty about grades**
- Frame: *"letters grade evidence, bars show progress, weights mark acts."* *"one unifying idea (an answer is only as strong as the weakest thing it depends on)"*; *"state what grades never measure (a grade tells you how easily someone else could check it, never whether it is true)"*.
- Workspace shows two strength badges (capture, connection) per question and a phrase against the project's bar; *"never shows one combined badge or score (DEC-44)"*; "Undetermined" always with its reason.
- Engine consequence: the engine never reports a single confidence score for its answer; it reports per-question capture and connection strength against the project's bar.

### DEC-83 (l.1361–1370) · refusal wording: shared sentence + act-specific remedy
- Engine consequence: an engine refusal (e.g. a step the member's role cannot take) shows the standard translation then a specific next step. Minor.

### DEC-84 (l.1372–1379) · PRESENT and RESOLVE detail — **binds machine recommendations, hypotheses, notices**
- *"(1) an undetermined candidate is a lead, never a duty"*; (2) a RECORD duty is an *"unmutable OBLIGATION"* for joined members of every project drawing on either side; *"(5) the machine recommends only which respects may differ, never which side is wrong nor a GENUINE kind"*; (7) a stale mark moves nothing by itself: holders get a re-evaluation notice.
- (10) *"Cause is member-authored and published only when evidenced, a hypothesized cause stays in the working inquiry and never contributes to the determination (a hypothesis is a placeholder), published as 'cause not established'"*; Recommendation *"limited to a proposed action, never a policy position"*.
- *"(14) accepting records the proposal accepted, the machine's reason stays the machine's, the member's own words optional and never filled from it"*; (16) a kept decision is always reasoned; (17) fixed dismissal reasons.
- Engine consequence: the answer to "what's going on?" at Grandview is a *cause*; doctrine says a cause is member-authored and published only when evidenced; the engine's hypothesized causes stay working material — **the single most direct constraint on the engine's "answer"**.

### DEC-85 (l.1381–1389) · conflicts across sight boundaries — **binds notification, member involvement across projects**
- Members of each conflicting project are notified *"without being shown the other project"*; both opt in before communicating; *"there is no discussion thread and no general messaging"*; publication highlights *"a side in conflict with a record not shown, never naming the hidden project"*.
- Engine consequence: an engine must never leak another project's investigation through a proposal (e.g. "another group is also looking at this contractor") beyond this notice pattern.

### DEC-86 (l.1391–1403) · Undetermined and its neighbours — **binds the engine's honest-gap vocabulary**
- *"'Undetermined' is one component with a fixed mark and a mandatory 'because…' line. 'Withheld', 'Unrated', 'Nobody looked' and 'Refused' each have their own visibly distinct treatment"*; none dressed as an error.
- Engine consequence: a records request refused, a document not yet sought, an extraction not calibrated — each maps to one of these named states, not a blank or a spinner.

### DEC-87 (l.1405–1417) · rung ladder feel — **binds acceptance UX**
- *"reversible acts inline; reasoned acts a reason field in place; terminal and attested acts a full dialog …; irreversible acts only through the ceremony"*; friction *"as low as possible so the tool fades and the work stays in focus"*.
- Engine consequence: accepting an engine proposal is placed on the ladder by what it does; most acceptances are reversible/inline.


### DEC-88 (l.1419–1435) · rungs for 57 acts — **binds AI runs, proposals, leads, objectives**
- REVERSIBLE (bind nothing): *"`suggest`, `extractpropose`, `contradictionpropose`, `themepropose`, `standardpropose`, `comparisonpropose`, `theorypropose`, `actionriskpropose`, `actionlawspropose`, … `airunopen`, `airunclose`, `projectfork`"* — machine-proposal acts and opening/closing an AI run are reversible.

### DEC-89 (l.1437–1449) · significance is prose, never a score — **binds the engine's prioritisation output**

### DEC-90 (l.1451–1463) · the assistant panel — **binds assistant behaviour**

### DEC-94 (l.1509–1525) · reminders are the member's own request; no outside channel — **binds notification, waits, deadlines**

### DEC-95 (l.1527–1542) · grade note for unattended captures; relevance suggestions under six guards — **binds capture by the assistant, proposals**

- Engine consequence: this is the ready template for every engine proposal (a document that may bear on a question, a lead to follow): in place, labelled, reasoned, dismissal remembered, switchable off, acceptance-rate measured.

### DEC-96 (l.1544–1561) · accepting and flagging other groups' work

## 4 · Gaps and tensions found

1. **Half the register is not in this file.** Entries answered 2026-08-04..08-10 were rolled to `docs/archive/ledgers/DECISIONS-2026-08.md` (l.90–97), and in this tree that file is only a COORD-POINTER to the retired `coord` branch. The rulings most central to the engine — **DEC-24** (*"THE MACHINE MAY DO THE LOOKING; THE MEMBER DOES THE CONCLUDING"*), **DEC-27** (the assistant may only structure what the member said), **DEC-52** (the machine may rule, machine-attributed), **DEC-55, DEC-60** (the investigative session may formulate claims and legs as suggestions; *"the machine may not accept the object"*), **DEC-61** (transcript purge suspendable), **DEC-62** (the extraction run), DEC-35 (no funding without Bob), DEC-10, DEC-13, DEC-14, DEC-15, DEC-20, DEC-44 — are cited here only second-hand. They are restated in `docs/architecture/BIO_Assistant_and_AI_Roles_v0_1.md` (status line and §3, checked by grep; that document "RULES NOTHING" and restates them). The A units should take those rulings from that document (or the C/M unit that read it), and BOB should consider recovering the rolled ledger into the study's sources *(recommendation)*.
2. **"Machine proposes, member accepts" vs "the machine may rule."** Bob's study framing (RESUME.md) is propose/accept. DEC-53 (resting on DEC-52) licenses a machine credential to declare, resolve and thread *directly* into the record, machine-attributed; the Roles document (§3 rule 4, via DEC-60) says the AI *"holds no op that ACCEPTS"*. These are reconcilable (machine may write machine-attributed statements; may never accept, vouch, ratify or attest), but the engine design must state which of its outputs are machine-attributed record writes and which are proposals awaiting a member's act. A Bob decision may be needed if the engine is to write leads, entities or hypotheses directly.
3. **The ACCEPTING A PROPOSAL act is scoped to contradictions first** (DEC-77 (b): *"introduced for contradictions first; governing laws and risk tiers are reconsidered under it separately"*). Its extension to investigation proposals (next steps, documents to seek, hypotheses, plans) is not ruled.
4. **Measuring acceptance rates vs not counting attention.** DEC-77 and DEC-95 (f) require the per-kind acceptance rate of recommendations to be measured; DEC-68 and DEC-69 forbid counting or grading a member's attention. Reconcilable only as an aggregate measure of the recommender, never per-member *(inference)*; the engine design should say so explicitly.
5. **The "answer" collides with cause doctrine.** Bob's question ("What's going on?") asks for a cause. DEC-84 (10): Cause is member-authored, published only when evidenced; a hypothesized cause *"never contributes to the determination"* and publishes as *"cause not established"*. The engine can assemble the Criteria/Condition (promise vs reality) as facts and propose causes as hypotheses, but cannot itself supply the answer.
6. **No outside channel vs months-long investigations.** DEC-94 forbids email/push; an engine waiting on records requests or board meetings can only surface work when the member opens the product. Member-requested reminders are in-product only. Whether a member-requested reminder may ever leave the product is unruled (DEC-94 item 3 is absolute as written).
7. **Assistant transcripts are ephemeral** (DEC-113: device-held, deleted at a time limit and at publication). An engine whose "planning function … has context about all the various things going on in the investigation" cannot keep that context in transcripts; it must live in the record (projects, inquiries, hunches, objectives, plans). The record's home for an engine's *plan* is unclear: DEC-25 (action plan never published) covers action plans; an investigation plan is not named anywhere in this file.
8. **Firsthand observation has no basis-leg home** (D-184, noted under DEC-39); the parent in Bob's scenario saw the site. DEC-102 then sets testimony's weight by credit level; the corroboration standard ("journalistic and legal standards") is owed, not specified.
9. **Contradiction between conclusions is half-designed.** DEC-32 left "Q14's contradiction case" undesigned; DEC-76/77/84 later design PRESENT/RESOLVE (owed, not built). Competing explanations (hypotheses that are not yet conflicts between held assertions) have no ruled object other than inquiries and hunches; the ACH matrix is ruled only as a presentation form for contradictions.
10. **Prioritisation without a score.** DEC-89 bans any stored significance/priority/rank. An engine planner naturally ranks leads; the ranking must be transient and explained, never stored as a value *(inference)*; a ruling may be needed if leads carry an engine-assigned order.
11. **Cost bounds exist only as a word.** DEC-88 shows `workobjective` with "the run's budget and scope"; DEC-74 makes external spending Bob's per decision. No ruling in this file sets who sets an AI budget, how spending is shown to members, or what a run does when its budget ends.
12. **AI is optional per group** (DEC-120: a group sets a key). An investigation engine must have a non-AI floor (wizard scripts) or be explicitly a keyed-only capability; the file does not say which.
13. **Vocabulary collisions to avoid:** "case" (published case), "case file" (DEC-112: the published structured archive), "obligation" (a public body's duty, DEC-107), "subject" (an entity, DEC-114), "matter" (what a plan addresses), "investigating" (a project stage, DEC-79), "lead" (an existing reasoned act, DEC-88; also a WORLD contradiction candidate, DEC-76). The engine's home and objects need names that do not collide.
14. **Recreatability vs third-party data.** DEC-112 (4) refuses publication where a relied-on finding rests on material that cannot travel whole. Contractor-history and licensing databases with restrictive terms may be usable only as leads *(inference)*.
