### 1.1 entities (layer 5) — `build/requirements/entities.md`

**Purpose.** The registry of subjects ("the things a case is about, which outlive any document naming them"), with aliases and declared constitutive relations; the recogniser that resolves a reading's references to entities with a grade A–D; "which documents concern / name this entity" (entities §Purpose).

**Kinds (closed).** `source, institution, office, movement, person, body, ordinance, parcel, contract, fund, program, place, proceeding` (entities §Terms; `program`, `place`, `proceeding` added T33, K1441). Organisation kinds (`institution`, `body`, `movement`) carry `sector` incl. `government`, `company`, `nonprofit` (R42, K1453). **`contract` is a registered entity kind**; there is no `project`, `solicitation`/`rfp`, `bid`, `award`, `change_order` or `school`/`facility` kind (a school campus would be `place` or `institution`) *(inference from the closed list)*.

**For an investigation.** The school district, the contractor (`institution`, sector `company`), the architect, the board (`body`), the bond `fund`, the `contract`, and the campus (`place`) are all registrable, and every captured document naming them can be resolved and listed (`concerns`). A contractor's licence number (a "licence number" identifier scheme, if a profile lists it, entities §Terms; `jurisdictions` R52) would resolve at grade A. Relations are only `proxy_for`, `member_of`, `overlaps`: there is no "awarded to", "party to", "subcontractor of", "parent company of" relation in this module (those belong to `lines`, see 1.10).

**Limits.** Kinds closed; an RFP, bid or change order is not an entity kind (they are documents/events); a "project" (the rebuild as a thing) has no kind other than `program` or `place` *(inference)*. Declared relations never carry evidence (R26). `testify` is grade D only.

### 1.6 duties (layer 5) — `build/requirements/duties.md`

**Purpose.** "The one obligation object: who owes what, to whom, by when, under which authority" — duty, prohibition or power; occurrences derived on read; transitions recorded append-only so "overdue as known on that day" stays readable; "'Overdue' raises a question, never a violation" (duties §Purpose). "All met at T33-35 (K1585)" — **built** on the tranche branch.

**Key terms.** Obligor may be a non-government organisation "acting for a public body under a public law, contract, franchise or grant (K1440)" — **a contractor can be an obligor**, but only if its source or a held `acts_for`/`contracts_with` line names the public body, and the duty names an enforcing office (`NOT_ACTING_FOR_PUBLIC`, `NO_ENFORCER`, R1). Source kinds: `standard`, `court`, `practice`, `dependency`. Trigger: `event`, `recurrence`, `source`, `date`. **Time basis kind: `rule`, `commitment`, `dependency`, `window` (K1431); "Only `rule` is answered as a deadline the law sets."** Occurrence states `met, met_late, overdue, pending, discharged, undetermined`. `reported_status` quotes a cited extent.

|---|---|---|

| `arising_in` a proceeding or report, `reported_status` quoted | R4 | an audit or grand-jury recommendation and the district's response |
| `revise`, `withdraw` (versions kept) | R6 | a change order extending the contract time is a revision *(inference)* |

| `matchEvent` (member), `registerOccurrenceEvidence` | R12 | the completion event meets the duty |
| `recordTransitions`, `transitionsOf` | R13–R14 | the slip, recorded as known on each day |
| `powersOf` | R15 | who could approve change orders |
| `setAgainst` (prohibition/threshold vs money facts, "never 'breach'") | R17 | spending against a bond restriction |
| connection owner "owes", "met by" | R18 | — |

**This is the closest built construct to "a promise and its slip"**: a duty with basis kind `commitment` (a promise), a trigger, a computed due date, derived occurrence state, and append-only transitions. A public official's spoken promise ("no longer than one school year") is a `commitment`; whether the official's office can be an obligor for a spoken promise depends on R1's source kinds — `practice` or `dependency` are the non-law options, and a press statement is neither a standard nor a court source *(inference: a gap; see §4)*.

**For an investigation, this is the procurement-lifecycle skeleton.** A group can declare a flow "public-works procurement": `needs_assessment → design → solicitation (RFP/IFB) → bids (cardinality 1..n) → award → contract → notice_to_proceed → change_orders (0..n, sometimes) → progress_reports (recurring) → substantial_completion → acceptance/closeout`, thread the documents of one `contract` entity (or `program`), and the record answers which stage's document is missing, which successor is overdue by its `within`, and which is out of order — with no new code *(inference: the shape fits Framework §8.2 "junction checks", e.g. "payment before award")*. The flow's basis may cite a held standard (the public contract code) (R39). Limits: one entity per instance (a rebuild spanning a design contract and a construction contract is two instances or one `program` entity); `within` is a fixed interval from the predecessor, not a contract-stated date (a contract's own completion date is a `duties` occurrence, not a progression interval) *(inference)*; findings are documents-present/absent, not quantities.

### 1.18 action-plans (layer 9) — `build/requirements/action-plans.md`

**Purpose.** "A project's working material for deciding what to do about one or more matters: an inquiry still open (a suspected matter) or a determination's outcomes (determined)"; options, member choice, ≤3 scenarios with checkpoints, link to started Actions; "It is not a project-management system … It is never published" (§Purpose). R1–R29 specified at K608; R30–R34 (K660) and R36–R38 later; Status lists them "not yet met" at each fold — **specified** (state on the tranche branch not verified).

**What it offers an investigation engine (as pattern and as service).**
- **Subjects may be suspected**: `{kind: "inquiry", inquiry}` with support `hypothetical` (§Terms, R1) — a plan can open while the investigation is still open.
- **The planning run** is the substrate's existing "machine proposes, member accepts" loop for a *plan*: an AI run in mode `plan` proposes options through `optionPropose` (R31), each stored apart with run id, skill version and `sources` (R31), shown in a tray five at a time in "the assistant's order of strength", with a fixed disclosure sentence "Suggested by the assistant (machine work) in run RUN-… It is not the group's decision; it becomes an option only when a member adopts it" (R32, R34); "No score, rank figure or strength is recorded or answered: the order is the only sign of it" (R34). `optionAdopt` by a member (R11). "A machine proposes (R11) and nothing else" (R24); R33.
- **Conditioning a step on a duty's state**: `starts: {when_duty: {duty, occurrence?}, state}` — "so an overdue response activates the next step" (R38, T33). This is how "if the district does not answer the records request in 10 days, file the follow-up" is expressed.
- Checkpoints are the group's own and "never … a finding" (R23); R19's derived checks (an outward option resting only on hypothetical subjects; no branch for a hostile response).

**Limits.** An action plan plans *outward action* (mitigation, legal, awareness, journalistic, grassroots), not the *investigative* work (what to read, what to request, what to ask next). Its options are not research steps; a records request is an `action`, so "request the change orders" can be an option, but "read the bid tabulation and compare to the award" has no home *(inference)*. No costs, assignees, hours (R26).

### 1.19 actions (layer 9) — `build/requirements/actions.md`

**Purpose.** "The Action object …: what the group sends or does outside the system, to whom, why, under which laws, at what legal exposure, by when, and what came back" — kind, risk tier, counterparty (addressee), clock, basis legs, lifecycle, correspondence ledger, governing laws, fee quotes, the records-request lifecycle, the action's own outcome (DEC-14); "A member decides every move; a machine prepares and never advances, testifies, sets a tier or states a law" (§Purpose). **For an investigation this is the records-request engine.** Product kinds `records_request`, `request_for_comment` (DEC-13: a non-response is a fact), `other`, plus profile kinds (R10). Correspondence ledger: `sent`, `received`, `no_response`, each captured bytes or a member's account, never both (R14–R16, R34); a `received` reply's capture gains a `responds_to` edge to the action (R17); fee quotes (R27); the records-request lifecycle with `due_cite`, exemptions, `passed_unanswered` (R22, R25); `pressure` marks for retaliation or legal threat (R48) and litigation holds (R52–R60). The group's sent request becomes a `duties` trigger (R67, T33), so the district's response duty and its overdue state are derived.

**Doctrine.** Machine fences R1, R5, R13, R15, R18, R23, R33; append-only ledger R34; "No action is refused for the capture grade of what it rests on" (R49); `breach: true` needs a live noncompliant determination or an open, stamped override (R8).

**Net.** The world model already carries the procurement backbone: contract entity, award and change-order events with `amends`, committed-against-paid, OCDS party roles, duties with `commitment` basis and recorded transitions, declared flows with overdue and out-of-order findings, and public commitments as standards. What it lacks is procurement-*specific* typing (solicitation, bid, change order, milestone, completion, forecast), a contract clause as a duty source, a schedule/forecast series, and — most consequential for an AI investigator — a *proposal path* by which a machine reading of a bid tabulation or status report could propose events, lines and money facts for a member to adopt.

## 5 · Gaps and tensions

1. **No machine-proposes path for events, lines or money facts.** Duties (R2), standards (R9), money sets (R13), themes (connections R41), action-plan options (R11) and action laws/tiers (actions R19, R28) have propose → adopt. Events, lines and money facts accept a machine write only when both ends are source-identified (events R22, lines R4, money R4) and otherwise only a member's direct act; lines leaves "whether a member line may be adopted from a machine proposal" open, "EXTRACT is stage 3, outside T33" (lines §Suggestions open (4)). An AI reading an award resolution or a bid tabulation (names, not ids) can write none of it and has no tray to put it in. **Gap** for "the researcher … extract[s] content and develop[s] meaning and connections" (RESUME.md, Bob's words).
2. **Procurement typing is generic.** Event kinds lack solicitation, bid opening, change order, notice to proceed, substantial completion; entity kinds lack solicitation/project; money kinds/phases lack bid/offer; dated-fact kinds lack forecast. Members can use `other`/`issuance`/`amends`, but queries, checks and progressions cannot tell the difference without a typing layer (events Terms; entities Terms; money Terms). Whether to add kinds or to rely on progressions' declared stages is a design decision.
3. **Contract clause as a duty's source.** Duties' source kinds exclude a contract; standards' six kinds exclude a contract (standards R1). K1440 allows a contractor obligor "under a … contract" (duties Terms). A contract clause must be shoe-horned in as a `commitment`/`policy` standard. **Tension.**
4. **Promises as durations and the school calendar.** civil-time's `commitment` is a stated date (R18); "no longer than one school year" from an event needs a duration count from a trigger and a school-calendar fact the profile does not hold (jurisdictions R46–R54 list no academic calendar). **Gap.**
5. **Forecast/slip series.** No home for the succession of projected completion dates stated in status reports except superseding standards (standards R6) one at a time; no read gives the series. **Gap.**
6. **Investigation planning is absent from this layer.** Action-plans plan outward action and forbid assignees and costs (R26); progressions declare the *world's* expected flow; nothing here holds the investigator's own leads, open questions or next reading steps. Themes (lens, member/hunch) and `hunch` connections scoped to a working inquiry (connection-grammar R8) are the nearest. (Inquiry, hypotheses and leads are outside M4's scope.)
7. **Jurisdiction coverage.** The first profile covers city and county (jurisdictions R21); a school district's systems, its board's Legistar-like portal, its fiscal year, its bond program codes and a state school-construction agency would need sourced profile entries before identifiers from them count (R44: "A fact without a source is not written"). **Gap** for Bob's scenario specifically.
8. **Hubs.** The school district will be named in most documents; co-mention through an entity in more than 32 documents is stepped around as a hub (connections R65). Walks must go through typed lines/events, not co-mention *(inference)*.
9. **Machine sight.** "A machine credential … sees every bundle" (membership R43); an AI investigator run's reach across projects is bounded elsewhere (`ai-runs`, `run-rules`, outside scope). To be checked by the AI-runs unit.
10. **Status mismatches.** `connection-grammar`, `civil-time` and `calc-grammar` files still read "DRAFT … Every id is not yet met", while their dependants are marked met (events K1574, duties K1585, money K1580) and their code is present (§1.21). `progressions`' Status says R37–R42 not yet met but `out_of_order` is in code. `action-plans` R38 and `actions` R66–R67 are specified and not in code. A design should treat T33-dated claims as "built on the tranche, not yet on `main`" until `build/modules.json` on `main` and the job records confirm *(inference)*.
11. **Overlap noted in the files.** money-checks R1 vs progressions R32 on amount-free junction checks (money-checks open (1); progressions §Suggestions T33). Resolved by K1505 (8) per progressions R32's text, but money-checks R1's wording still lists them.
12. **No cross-contract performance read.** Contractor history needs "this contractor's duties across contracts, met late or overdue" — `duties.dutiesOf({entity, as: obligor})` (R7) gives the list; the lateness pattern is a gated machine pattern (calculations R22). A member-run, ungated calculation over a frozen set (calculations R21) can produce "N of M contracts late" with a denominator — usable today.
