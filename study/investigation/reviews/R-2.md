# R-2 · Review of studies/PLANNING.md and studies/HOME.md

Unit R-2, phase 3 (`PROTOCOL.md` §V). Both studies were read whole. Claims that matter were checked against the notes they cite, and the other four studies were searched for overlaps. Severity: **blocking** (the synthesis cannot proceed until it is resolved), **should fix**, **minor**.

**Overall.** Both studies are sound and well cited. Their doctrine tables match the notes. Each rejects the agent-memory option for the right reasons. Each keeps the machine to proposing, the plan unpublished, and orders free of any score. Spot-checked citations held (intent R18/R20, action-plans R32/R34, contradiction R39, DEC-25, DEC-77, DEC-121, K1481, inquiry R55, capture R77, membership R43, D-572). The problems are not inside either study. They are at the seam between the two: each study builds its own home for the same facts, and they disagree about who may write them. That seam has to be closed before synthesis.

## 4 · Risks neither study addresses

1. **Concurrency between members on one plan.** Two members may adopt and decline the same proposal, or one may retire a step another's question needs. PLANNING passes this to LOOP (§9). HOME's journal records it but does not resolve it. LOOP's K1618 disagreement work should be cited by both. *(should fix, in the synthesis)*
2. **Scale of the derived reads.** `journalOf`, `replanDue` and `planOf` are all derived on read from many owners. For S2 or S11 (years, hundreds of documents) neither study bounds the cost of the read or says which reads are paged. HOME bounds each section but not the join. *(minor; state page bounds and a test at S11 scale)*
3. **Eval ownership.** PLANNING's P2 eval set is left to COST for measures, and HOME has no acceptance measure for machine-stated purposes. If X2 lets runs state purposes, the rate of purposes members later revise should be measured. *(minor)*
