# B5 · Procurement and public works: best practice (phase 1b)

Unit B5 of the investigation study. Scope: Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS) and its red-flag guides; US public construction delivery, procurement, contract administration and oversight; California school construction (Prop 39 oversight, DSA, OPSC); contractor licensing and history (CSLB). What documents exist at each stage, who publishes them, what they hold, and what typically explains a delayed or over-budget project.
