## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- jurisdictions: requirements for the layer-1 profile module (APPROVED K102; widened K608, K921–K925). Holds every local fact as data with a basis (measurement/ruling/UNMEASURED/TEST): identifier spaces, publishing systems, crosswalks, vocabulary (bodies, member titles, codes), practice, and the action sections (standard sources, counterparties by role, action kinds, deadlines, holidays, hours, time zone, legal organisations). list/get/validate/combine; combine never chooses between disagreeing profiles.
- id-spaces: requirements for the layer-1 judge of shared identifiers (enactment, project, fund, parcel): recognise/normalise, coverage reach, parcel standing, systemOf, judgePair (form → value → system independence → fund name → referent). Pure; every "no" says which kind of no.
- docprofile: requirements for the layer-1 content-type recogniser (split T19, K617: stack axis, digests, event catalogue moved to `site-profiles`). `doctypeFor` (minutes, agenda, regulation, staff report, staff directory, meeting calendar), `assess` (layered change verdict between two captures of one address, with `events[]` and `connections[]` referential/temporal), `readText`/`flattenText`/`makeLocator` (position discipline). All local vocabulary from the jurisdiction view.
- office-readers: requirements for the layer-1 DOCX/PPTX/XLSX/CSV readers (I7 entries): detection, I2 structure (links, IC-1 element references) and the DEC-5 evidentiary envelope (tracked changes with author/date, comments, core properties, speaker notes, hidden slides/rows/cols/sheets, formulas with cached values), I2 text, size guard, CSV dialect, and the reference builders reused by odf-reader. Pure; asserts nothing about meaning (R24).
- odf-reader: requirements for the layer-1 OpenDocument reader (.odt/.ods/.odp; Google Drive export format per CAP-8/COFF-10), projecting into the same I2/DEC-5 shape as the OOXML siblings, plus `odfEvidentiaryDigest` (D-351) for substance stability. Repeat-expansion bound (R45, N30). Pure, no local fact.
- extraction: requirements for the layer-4 module that makes and records readings (APPROVED K102; pipeline split to `reading-pipeline` N513/K1193): writes `readings`, `reading_refs` (raw `kind:key` references with positions/occurrences), `reading_ref_terms` (name-term fold), text index, text-source chain, reading history; re-read (`op=pdfstructure&ocr=1`); agenda item/file membership by containment (R52); drift obligations; EXTRACT role vocabulary (R41–R43); testimony indexing (R61–R65). Resolves no reference (R46).
- content: requirements for the layer-4 module of the citable unit (APPROVED K102): the extent grammar (document, pdf-page, sheet-cell, slide-shape, doc-para, sheet-range, doc-table, image, + envelope R33), the content address `hash(capture, extent, chain)`, mint-or-find rows, member transcriptions and attestations (DEC-88 note), stale marking on re-read, the passage notice against newer captures (graded A/B/C/NOT_FOUND/UNDETERMINED), and the citation resolver for inquiry, basis versions, cases and actions. Never moves a reference (R34).
- entities: requirements for the layer-5 entity axis and subject registry, one construct (Framework §13; APPROVED K102; DEC-88 note K1025). Closed kinds (`source, institution, office, movement, person, body, ordinance, parcel, contract, fund`), first-class aliases, member-declared constitutive relations (`proxy_for, member_of, overlaps`), resolution of reading references to entities graded A/B/C (recogniser) and D (testimony), `concerns`, `namingDocuments`, `idMatch`, defect reports (DEC-76 item 3), alias/relation withdrawal (K106). Derives no connection.
- connections: requirements for the layer-5 module of the record's connections (APPROVED K102; R20 amended K935): derived connections (two captures resolving to one entity, graded by the weaker end, with determining pair and member on-point choice), portion grades for cited passages, edges between bundles (`refs`: cites, links_to, supersedes; backlinks, dangling), source-link connections (A, with timing), member-asserted connections (D), agenda item–file containment (C), the dirty-set sweep, and themes (Framework §8.4, a lens never evidence).
- progressions: requirements for the layer-5 module of declared flows (APPROVED K102; Framework §8.2): a group declares a versioned progression (ordered stages with `after_stage`, cardinality, `within` interval, requiredness `always|usually|sometimes|never|unless_exception`); an instance threads captured documents for one entity through the stages; on every read it derives grade (weakest link), `missing_predecessor`, `discharged_skip`, `overdue_successor`, `cardinality_exceeded` findings, aggregated proposals; member dispositions age findings. Junction checks over amounts (R32) deferred.
- bias: requirements for the layer-5 declared-bias module (APPROVED K102; DEC-88 K1025): bias sets (statements of kind scrutiny/inference/pattern, each with a registry subject `ENT-YYYY-NNNN` and justification, never a verdict on a source, never a bar), adoption by a member for instance or project pinned to a revision, the effective lens manifest with `statements_sha`, policy inhale into a proposal, and the bias debt over work products (AI runs, project findings) when a lens moves. Disclosed, never blocks (DEC-20).

### connections

- [GAP] connections — no person-to-person connection; the only connection is document↔document through one entity (R1, R34: "a derivation never forms a connection through a declared relation"): "who knows who" is not derivable.
- [DESIGN] connections R2 (src 22) — pair bound default 500 (max 5,000) → document bound 32 (100 at 5,000): a widely-mentioned person (e.g. a mayor in hundreds of documents) is truncated "first documents by capture digest".

- [DESIGN] connections R53 (src 91) — a member may assert a connection between two documents with a basis (≤4,000), grade D, never established.

## Cross-construct observations
- jurisdictions is where any jurisdiction-specific rule for PEOPLE (official titles, directories), EVENTS (meeting/minutes practice, deadlines' start events) and MONEY (fiscal year, thresholds, fund forms) would have to live as data; today only titles, fund forms, minutes-due and deadlines exist.
- docprofile's `events[]` (significance-graded document changes) and `connections[]` temporal (`expected_by`) are the nearest built shapes to EVENTS; the word "event" is already used for document-change significance (site-profiles R13), so the EVENTS construct needs a distinct name or explicit disambiguation.
- office-readers' envelope joins PEOPLE (authors, initials, lastModifiedBy), EVENTS (dates of edits/comments, created/modified) and MONEY (formulas with cached values) at one evidentiary layer; none is indexed (`envelope` extent kind designed, not built), so none reaches entities or connections today.
- extraction's R52 is the one built machine "part of" relation between events (agenda item) and documents, already carrying the grade/standing/established vocabulary (grade C, inferred, not established) that any EVENTS relation would need; the containment rule is profile-driven (links patterns).
- content is the citation substrate all nine constructs share: a PEOPLE fact (a name in a roster), an EVENTS fact (a vote in minutes) and a MONEY fact (a cell in a budget) are each a content row with two grades; the passage notice is the built mechanism for "the source changed after we relied on it", which EVENTS (amendments) and MONEY (revised budgets) both need.
- entities is the natural seat of PEOPLE identity (person kind, aliases, ambiguity kept, D testimony, defect reports) but its relations are constitutive and untraversed (R26); posts, careers, family ties and who-knows-who are evidentiary, dated relations that would belong to `lines` (K1439/K1442) rather than to entity relations — this registers a design fork: widen `entities` (attributes, as-of) or add a people module over it.
- The `isEstablished = A|B (captured identifier at both ends)` rule (R34) suits identifiers (funds, parcels, enactments) but not persons, who rarely have one; a PEOPLE design must say how a person identity is established (e.g. member testimony D plus corroboration) without changing R27.
- connections' derived connection is quadratic co-mention through one entity; it cannot carry person–person, event–event or money flows. EVENTS and MONEY relations (dated, typed, walkable) fit the ruled `lines` (evidentiary, walkable bounded as of a date) rather than connections or entity relations; the existing containment (item→file, grade C, judged by members) is the precedent for a machine-proposed, member-judged relation.
- progressions is the built half of EVENTS-as-process (declared flow vs observed) and the declared home of MONEY-in-process checks (R32, deferred until amounts and funds are values). It threads by one ENTITY and places DOCUMENTS; an EVENTS construct would let a stage be satisfied by an event (with its own date, participants) rather than a document's reading/registration date, and MONEY would supply the amounts the junction checks need. It would also need jurisdictions' business-day calendar (R33) that progressions' `within` does not use.
- The registry (entities) is shared by PEOPLE subjects and bias subjects (Framework §13; bias R3, R25): a PEOPLE construct that widens person entities widens what a bias statement can be about; the brief's "group's own members' ties to those it examines" belongs nearer bias/membership disclosure than to the evidentiary record, and neither module carries it today.
