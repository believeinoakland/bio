## What these documents are
- local-facts: requirements (K921, approved 2026-10-01) for a layer-9 module recording members' confirm/correct/dispute of a jurisdiction profile's holidays, office hours and time zone; horizons for lapse; facts due list. All ids not yet met at drafting (T21).
- standards: requirements (K102) for a layer-9 module holding standards (statute, regulation, ordinance, court, policy, commitment) as record objects with citation, kind, issuer, source match against profile `standard_sources`, period in force, supersession, machine proposals stored apart.
- conformance: requirements (K102, K171) for the layer-9 module recording a member's determination that a named government act is compliant/noncompliant/unclear against named standards, per standard, resting on published findings; machine comparisons stored apart; cause (DEC-77) member-authored and evidenced.
- consequences: requirements (K102, K12, K172) for the layer-9 module recording what a breach did and to whom: parts (affected, measure, period) computed/assessed/undetermined, causation established/unproven/not_applicable, and addressed state that escalation reads. The only money-measuring module in the action layer.
- action-grammar: requirements for a pure layer-9 module (split from actions, K617, K653 BOB-2; met by ACTION-GRAMMAR #1, K835; C-117.23–.25 rows not yet met T27) holding the action vocabularies, the records-law, quote and lifecycle grammars, the request-for-comment basis rule, the audit arm, readers and catalogue rows `actions` uses.
- actions: requirements (K102; many folds through K1253) for the Action object (State Rules §4.4): the group's own act outward — kind, risk tier, counterparty/addressee, clock, basis legs, lifecycle, correspondence ledger with fee quotes and records-request lifecycle, governing laws, own outcome (DEC-14), pressure marks and litigation holds (DEC-108, DEC-113). Not the EVENTS construct (taken word).
- action-clocks: requirements (split from actions, K617; reminders DEC-94, K613–K615, K624 (3); local facts K921) for the cross-action reads of the group's action deadlines (pending, overdue), clock entries proposed from profile deadlines (calendar/business days on the confirmed holiday calendar), and members' own reminders. Several ids marked not yet met (R3–R6, R8, R9; R10, R11 T21; R12 T22) at drafting.
- filing-templates: requirements (K921, K924, approved 2026-10-01) for the group's governed library of filing templates: versioned wording with closed `FILING_BLANKS`, attribution, member and professional reviews (revocable grants), approval by a non-sole-author, tier rules, retirement. Every id not yet met T21 at drafting; R20 `project` T22.
- filings: requirements (K102, K13; folds through K1038) for what the group sends, prepared from the record: Tier 1/2 filing drafts filled from templates or member's words with every blank naming its source; counsel packets at any tier (facts, chronology, exhibits, standards, candidate theories/remedies, deadlines; never fileable, never published); communications; approval and recording as sent; the evidence package's available-actions block.
- escalation: requirements (K102, K14, K172; DEC-89, DEC-88 folds) for the seven-stage escalation protocol (DR 7 as amended) from a live noncompliant determination to its end (compliance restored AND consequences addressed); triggers derived from the record and proposed with their age; member advances/declines; decline-to-escalate and pre-assembled reason (R27–R29).

## Cross-construct observations (in progress)
- standards' period-in-force and supersession are the only as-of/event-relation machinery among the first two modules; a money rule (fee limit, threshold) as a standard has only text and a period, no structured value.
- conformance's act (`ACT-`) is an embryonic EVENT object: id-identity, actor office, date/period, evidence; it is owned by conformance and has no bundle. An EVENTS construct would either absorb it (acts as events with determinations pointing at them) or need a shared identity rule ("the same act is equality of id") at a lower layer.
- consequences is the only home of money amounts and arithmetic with grades (weakest link) and totals within state/unit/currency: a pattern a MONEY construct's figures could reuse, but it sits in layer 9 and reads figures only from passages; consequences explicitly defers a budget reader to extraction/docprofile.
- PEOPLE: two layer-9 rules (conformance actor = office; consequences R10 no individual) embody the set-aside "offices, never private individuals" rule; they need re-reading under K1452/K1455 + DR6 (record vs publication).
- The correspondence ledger + lifecycle (stage/follows/due_by/outcome) is a working, built event-sequence grammar with should-vs-did statuses, but scoped to the group's own action (taken word); an EVENTS construct for the world would face the same needs (dated entries, follows, due vs answered, capture or testimony) and could generalise it without colliding with "action".
- The only stored money values (fee quotes, D-148) are in actions, with currency, parsed value, revisions and waiver-as-zero: a small precedent for MONEY's amounts-with-stages-and-revisions; consequences reads figures from passages instead. Two different money representations already exist in layer 9.
- actions R9's `entity_id` ties an addressee to `entities`; a person entity refused as addressee: PEOPLE in entities would meet actions here.
- action-clocks R2's "start from the event the rule names in the ledger" + duration + calendar is a rule relating an event to a later due date: the generic shape EVENTS needs for "what should have happened against what did" (statutory deadlines on government acts), today only for the group's own action and only proposals.
- filing-templates' professional grant (recipient, organisation, credential as stated, never verified; names held by value at the act) is a small precedent for PEOPLE outside the group: identity by stated name + organisation, credentials as unverified statements, dated snapshot names.
- UTC-day doctrine in actions R12/action-clocks Terms vs profile time_zone in local-facts: TIME meets EVENTS (event dates and deadlines) unresolved in layer 9.
- filings R9's chronology is the de facto EVENTS timeline today: it merges the world event (the determined act) with group events (publication, action states, correspondence, clock) by date. An EVENTS construct would supply a shared, reusable chronology read (with date precision and relations) that the packet would consume, rather than each consumer assembling one; also keeps "action" (group's) distinct from world events while still interleaving them in one timeline.
- escalation's exit requires "the same act" (ACT- id) across determinations: act identity is load-bearing; if EVENTS owns acts, escalation/conformance identity must move with it.
- PEOPLE meets escalation at stage 7 (elected offices, oversight) and filings (counsel by name + organisation): offices carry `elected`/`oversight` in profile data, but no office→holder→person link exists; a PEOPLE construct (posts held over time) would let these reads name who held the office at the act's date without changing the addressee doctrine.
- MONEY reaches filings and escalation only via consequences parts; no audit/finance-specific stage logic.
