## Cross-construct observations (connections between the nine constructs these documents make or imply)
- The bond worked example (ACTION-PLAN l.65–68) ties EVENTS (certification act, deposit act), MONEY (restricted proceeds, general fund), LAW (two-thirds requirement, purpose restriction), COURTS (election contest, grand jury) and TIME (short regulated end date); and one event's reversal (voiding) removes the money's legal basis — an event-relation that changes a money question.
- The Action layer's whole unit is a government "act" measured against a standard (conformance); an EVENTS construct would supply/subsume these acts.
- Person-exclusion appears in three built/designed places (addressee, consequences' harmed party, actor kinds, D1/D4/DEC-54); these concern outward acts and published products (Requirement 6), and must be distinguished from K1452's full record of people.
- Conformance's `ACT-` id (sources_build-state l.81–82) is the built seed of an EVENTS object: an act with description, actor office, date/period, evidence, and identity across determinations; any EVENTS design must absorb or link it rather than duplicate.
- Consequences already implement a narrow MONEY calculus (unit/currency/value|range, computed ops on content ids, weakest-grade, totals per state/unit/currency, undetermined ≠ zero); relates to the ruled `calc-grammar`/`calculations` modules and to the person-exclusion (no person kind) that K1452 may reopen for harm to named individuals.
- Profile deadlines start on events (received, filed, act, known): TIME and EVENTS meet in the clock; "known" implies a who-knew-when fact.
- Progressions (declared flow, D-128, "what SOMEBODY ELSE is supposed to do") and the records-request lifecycle's declared-versus-observed derivation (D-147) are existing EVENTS-like machinery: expected sequences vs observed entries, "follows/answers" links, elapsed time. An EVENTS construct should be designed with these, and with the A10/OQ-25 rule that our own deadlines never become findings about the world.
- The subject registry (D-83, "no adversarial attribute") and the review/subject right-of-reply bear on PEOPLE: a person-index of cases "may read as adversarial" (OQ-17) — tension with K1452's full people tracking, to be reconciled at publication (Design Requirement 6), not at the record.
- Edge vocabulary is closed (SR §5.1) yet already breached by `responds_to`, `references[]`, `action_basis`; EVENTS relations (authorises, answers, amends, reverses) will need the spec revision SR demands.
- Req 6's disclosure of the producing group's and members' relationships to "government entities, officials, contractors, or other stakeholders" (sources_canon-mission l.38) is a canon NEED for PEOPLE×ORGS ties of the group's own members — the brief's "the group's own members' ties to those it examines".
- DEC-77's Criteria/Condition/Cause/Effect form maps across constructs: criteria = LAW (standards), condition = EVENTS (what happened), cause = ANALYSIS (DEC-77 (10)), effect = consequences/MONEY.
- The government "act" is built as an id with no bundle (sources_code l.57): EVENTS has a built identity seed but no home object; giving acts a home (with date/period, actor, place, participants, relations) is a natural EVENTS rung and would let conformance, consequences, filings' `act`/`act_date` blanks and escalation's exit share one event record.
- Correspondence stages (fee_estimate, production, denial, appeal_decision…) are a typed event log of the group's own records requests with money (quotes) attached; a MONEY design should decide whether fee quotes stay a correspondence attribute or become money records.
