## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **OBSERVATION-LOG-DESIGN** (v0.1 DRAFT, BOB #11, 2026-09-14, folded in up to 2026-09-25). This is the design of `observation_log`, the one append-only table of "events about looking" across four levels: internet, document, content and meaning. It sets the four-state absence vocabulary (D-129) and §5.1's three causes of a missing row. It also covers the frontier view, the completeness statement's `searched` section (D-196), withholding and tally rulings (REC-110, D-486, BOB #32/#33 on the watermark), purge and growth. All four §8 items are BUILT. It bears on the three constructs mainly at the meaning level (resolution attempts and entity subjects), through member-search privacy (§4.6), and through TIME (whole-second `at`, interval reading).
- **BIO_Technical_Architecture_Decisions_v10** (July 2026, banner "THIS IS THE ARGUMENT, NOT THE SYSTEM"). These are the old technology decisions: the six-object model (Information, Focus, Project, Action, Annotation, Work Product; since superseded by the inquiry collapse), bundles, work products and source-grounding, two evaluation functions, no transitive trust (since revised), sessions, extraction, change detection, the gathering contract, constrained endpoints, secret tiers, the interruption model, the manifest contract, the promotion gate, a sewer-fund prototype sequence, declared bias and the Focus rename. Its rules are live; its mechanisms are retired. Little in it addresses people, events or money directly. What bears on them: identity rules, the honest-null rule, the money exemplars (sewer fund, ACFR, OpenGov transfers, Prop 218), and "clocks belong to Actions".
- **BIO_Distribution_v0_1** (v0.1 DRAFT, BOB #11, 2026-09-14, folded in to 2026-09-25). This is the level-1 home of construct 15: the sovereign instance in the group's own Cloudflare account, the signed release, the fleet, the installer and the deploy-to-serve ladder. Multi-instance isolation is planned and not built. It touches PEOPLE only through where personal data lives (only in the group's account) and through member identity tables. It has nothing on events or civic money.
- **PRACTICE-SURVEY** (BOB, 2026-08-01, never re-run). This is an outside-in survey of comparable tools: DocumentCloud, Zotero, Obsidian, NVivo, i2 Analyst's Notebook, OCCRP Aleph, Everlaw Storybuilder, Relativity, MuckRock, Linear, GitHub, Wikipedia, Wikidata, Bellingcat and Perma.cc. It ends in ten ADOPT conventions and ten DELIBERATELY VIOLATE ones; four of the violate items are BUILT as SK-3 prohibitions. It is the main source here for how link-analysis and people-matching tools shape member expectations: the entity–link–property model, centrality, Aleph's name matching, Everlaw's fact timelines, and Wikidata's somevalue/novalue.
- **CONSTRUCTS** (2026-07-30 inventory, as of 2026-09-23). This is the inventory of document-profile and content constructs, with eight overlaps, the connection-table brief and a ten-step plan (FW series). It is directly relevant to EVENTS (event types, temporal connections, `expected_by`, progressions, ageing), to PEOPLE (the entity axis as the subject registry, aliases, `person serves_on body`, membership monitoring) and to MONEY (shared identifiers such as fund code and contract/PO; the budget-or-dataset content type is owed).

## Cross-construct observations (connections between the nine constructs these documents make or imply)
- **Progressions are the existing seed of EVENTS.** CONSTRUCTS' connection table and progression rows (meeting→agenda→minutes, need→signed contract) already model processes, expected timings (`expected_by`, "within N days"), exception documents and ageing (REC-8). They join EVENTS (steps), TIME (intervals, due dates), ORGANISATIONS (`held_by` body), LAW (`proposes_amendment_to` regulation) and MONEY (need-to-signed-contract). An EVENTS design should say whether it absorbs progressions or sits beside them, so the result is not "the eighth vocabulary" (CONSTRUCTS L182–184).
- **The three-valued `asserted_by`** (source / system / member; CONSTRUCTS L191–195) applies equally to a person's tie, an event relation (authorises, answers) and a money flow. Who asserts a relation sets its weight.
- **Shared identifiers join MONEY, EVENTS and ORGANISATIONS.** Fund code, project number, contract/PO and C.M.S. number shared across budget, Legistar and the Auditor (M-119) move a progression from grade C to grade B. A money flow and the votes that authorise it are linked by these identifiers. The identifier-space construct is ABSENT, and contract/PO is UNDETERMINED.
- **The PEOPLE identity model is the subject registry.** The entity axis must also be the bias subject registry (D-83), with aliases first-class and identity graded by resolution method (identifier > exact > name correspondence). Person identity, same-name ambiguity (Aleph's "name collision until a person judges it") and declared bias meet in this one registry. The observation log's `reference` subject kind records failed resolutions, which is how "we looked for this name and could not resolve it" becomes stateable.
- **The meaning-level observation evidence is one-sided** for references and entities (OBS §5.1). Any PEOPLE "never looked for" statement over pre-log data degrades to undetermined. The signed `searched` section cannot enumerate entity subjects (OBS Incomplete §6 L33), so the ANALYSIS completeness claim for a person-centred case has a structural hole.
- **The entity frontier is unfenced, and that is open** (OBS L47). Adding sensitive personal facts (K1455) to an instance-wide registry served to any reader by `op=concerns` raises how visibility interacts with projects. The D-486 ruling (project's thinking: attribution withheld, bytes shared) is the nearest precedent.
- **Member searches are never logged** (OBS §4.6), on legal-process grounds. This is the same exposure concern that applies to the group's own members' ties to those it examines. Both are about what a subpoena of the record would reveal. Distribution keeps all of it in the group's account (no operator access), but TAD's "the store is designed to mirror, fork and distribute" premise has not been reconciled with holding full personal facts.
- **The interval reading of a coarse timestamp** (BOB #33's three answers: AFTER / BEFORE / WITHIN BAND, stating undetermined inside the band) is a ruled TIME mechanism that EVENTS with approximate dates could reuse in place of a new uncertainty model.
- **Taken or conflicting words to avoid.** "observation" (the look log), "event" (already used for document-change events: `SIGNIFICANCE` event/notice/routine, `delisted`, `minutes_replaced`, and "graded events" from `assess`), "budget" (an AI run's budget), "clock" (an Action's clock), "Focus" (renamed), and "membership"/"members" (the group's own members, and the `membership` monitoring contract). A world-EVENTS construct must be distinguished from the document event catalogue that already exists.
- **Every one of these documents keeps "proposed versus decided" apart.** Focus graph connections (TAD §7.5), Aleph matches (PS), assistant-surfaced findings (CONSTRUCTS 8b) and model-judged PRESENT (OBS §4.4) all say the machine proposes and a member decides. That applies to person matches, event relations and money-flow links alike, and the grade travels with every edge (PS VIOLATE 4).

- **How PEOPLE, EVENTS and MONEY must fit with all nine (from these sources, K1460).**
  (1) *Identity and resolution:* one registry, the entity axis that is also the bias subject registry (CONSTRUCTS Step 4). People, organisations, bodies, funds and contracts all resolve there, graded by method (identifier > exact match > name correspondence), with aliases first-class. Shared identifiers (fund code, project number, contract/PO, C.M.S. number, APN) are what join MONEY to EVENTS (Legistar votes) and ORGANISATIONS (M-119). Failed resolutions are recorded as `reference` looks (OBS). Whole ids are identity and legible parts are hints (TAD §10.8). Canonical ids, never source locators (TAD §3).
  (2) *As-of and time:* every event or money fact needs an instant or interval at stated precision. BOB #33's interval reading is the ruled mechanism. The record's write instant (real UTC, TAD §10.10) is distinct from the world date. Due dates come from rules ("within N days"), aged by REC-8.
  (3) *Grades and provenance:* every edge carries a grade (PS VIOLATE 4); progression instances take the weakest grade along their chain (CONSTRUCTS Step 5); `asserted_by` is three-valued; machine proposals look proposed (D-82).
  (4) *Processes:* EVENTS meets LAW (a statute or order is the source of a due date, TAD §8.2; `proposes_amendment_to`), ORGANISATIONS (`held_by` body), PEOPLE (`serves_on`, attendees) and MONEY (need-to-signed-contract) in the one progression table. That table is the existing load-bearing seam, and a new EVENTS construct should extend it rather than duplicate it (overlap 5: a shared catalogue, not per-type strings).
  (5) *ANALYSIS and QUESTIONS:* absence statements about a person, event or amount follow the four-level vocabulary and §5.1's three causes. A case's signed `searched` section cannot yet cover entity subjects (OBS Incomplete §6 L33), which is a gap that matters most for PEOPLE. The assistant's claims need referents (OBS §4.4), and unattended discovery beyond store-named sources is out of scope (TAD §10.4, DEC-47).
  (6) *Courts:* these sources say little. TAD §4 names "a court record" as a primary source, TAD §8.2 names a civil suit's multi-stage schedule as an Action clock, and PS §3 covers e-discovery chain of custody and privilege logs.
  (7) *Requirements across all nine:* edge-triggered growth and bounded, non-amplifying reads (OBS); the subrequest ceiling and one alarm (DIST); row-whole fencing, and counts treated as disclosures (OBS); everything inside the sovereign instance (DIST); the Mechanical Verification Law (TAD). These bind every one of the three as much as the six.
