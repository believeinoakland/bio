## Cross-construct observations (connections between the nine constructs these documents make or imply)
- The contradiction coordinates (subject, time/occasion, scope, meaning, observer/method, precision) are a ready taxonomy for reconciling conflicting statements about PEOPLE (identity: "not the same subject" → entities defect), EVENTS (time/occasion; reversal timeline) and MONEY (precision, scope — e.g. two amounts at different stages or rounding; inferred, not stated).
- The `reportResolutionDefect` act in `entities` is directly a PEOPLE identity/same-name-ambiguity mechanism: a wrong merge of two persons surfaces through contradiction K4.
- The timeline form is an EVENTS read over contradiction sides only; an EVENTS construct (chronology, ruled L5) would generalise it — the timeline's rule "unstated date placed nowhere and counted, never guessed" is the as-of/undetermined doctrine to carry over.
- Obligation-against-act (5Cs: Criteria/Condition/Cause/Effect/Recommendation) ties LAW (criteria), EVENTS (condition = what happened), cause doctrine (member-authored, "not stated"), and consequences (effect) — a template for MONEY rule violations too (e.g. a fee limit vs fees charged), inferred.
- Double-speak/reversal by a subject is the PEOPLE×EVENTS "statements over time" finding, kept a civic verdict (never machine-proposed).
- BSC's Action clock ("clock entries with basis", silently-past-due prohibited; CPRA statutory clock "honestly overdue") is the existing pattern for EVENTS' "what should have happened against what did" with TIME and LAW: a deadline derived from a statute, measured against a dated act. It is the group's own action, though; a world-event deadline (an agency's response duty) would need the same shape outside the `action` taken word.
- BSC's sewer-fund exemplar and its dollar-figure hash catch show MONEY figures were held, from the first design, as attested values in a hashed dataset with gaps recorded, not as a money model (no stages, flows or rules).
- BSC's additive-schema and "new type is a file drop plus a matrix row" doctrine, and CPR's placement-by-module-order (§12), together frame how PEOPLE/EVENTS/MONEY objects would enter: grammar first in the earliest module that holds the vocabulary, checks added (never rewritten), later modules reading earlier ones.
- Neither document mentions people as subjects with privacy concerns, events as world objects, or money flows; their relevance is through identity defects (CPR §9), timelines and 5Cs (CPR §5), clocks and record integrity (BSC).
