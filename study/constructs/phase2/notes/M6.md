## What these documents are
- **contradiction** (L6): IDENTIFY/PRESENT/RESOLVE of conflicts between held assertions. Five deterministic pairing keys (K1 legs of one inquiry, K2 same subject entity, K3 same referent, K4 one entity in two sources, K5 two projects' conclusions); an AI run labels pairs (`world`, `record`, `precision`, `unrelated`, `undetermined`) as append-only candidates; members dismiss, clarify, take up or resolve; DEC-85's cross-project notice/opt-in/reveal/relay. The machine never judges a side.
- **case-authoring** (L8): `publishCase` (op=publish), the preparation of an unsigned case document `bio-case-document/7` from concluded findings, roles, bar, statement, exclusions, searched section, bias/lens, "What changed"; statement acknowledgements; the publishing ceremony's pre-flight. Disclosures delegated to case-disclosures.
- **promotion** (L2): the one write path into the record: CAS, append-only manifest/history, document as its own word, gate runs of the check catalogue, reopen, project fork, registerStep/registerFact/onCommitted hooks for later modules, CATALOG_VERSION and ROW_CENSUS.
- **docket** (L8): a published case's public response log (DEC-116, DEC-100): record entries filed by members, signed public entries placed by the manager on `listed`/`reactions`, standing grants, receipts for replies naming a private person, withdrawal, take-back; public read and Atom feed.
- **case-grammar** (L8): pure grammar of the case document and the case file (`bio-case-file/1`): formats, attribution levels, materials and attestations, method, grading facts and passages, complete edition (one HTML, no network), standing line.
- **case-import** (L8): importing another group's case file into a read-only project, recreation by case-checker, acceptance/withdrawal/flags by reasoned member acts, and watching the publisher's docket (verified hash chain, signatures).
- **case-disclosures** (L8): what a case must disclose about what it rests on (tensions, capture grades and co-attestation, sources stated or "Withheld", materials held whole, method, accepted work and flags, grading facts, hunch debt); disclose, never block.
- **queue-producers** (L11): derive-on-read producers of every member queue item (FINDINGs, CONDITIONs ("signals"), OBLIGATIONs ("to dos")): bias debts, progressions anomalies, contradiction items, monitoring, action clocks, docket core, imports, wizard scripts.

## Cross-construct observations
- **PEOPLE ↔ ANALYSIS (identity feedback).** The only place in these modules where a member's judgement corrects identity is contradiction R32: a `differs` on coordinate `subject` over a K4 pair reports a resolution defect to `entities` (R38). Same-name ambiguity thus surfaces as a contradiction between two documents "about one entity". A PEOPLE construct's resolution model should keep this door (or widen it) rather than invent a second one.
- **PEOPLE ↔ PUBLICATION.** Four separate person-exposure rules live in four modules with no shared model: docket R8 (a reply naming a private person → receipt only), case-disclosures R4 (a source "Withheld" unless consented or publicly named), case-grammar R2/R12 and case-disclosures R10 (members at attribution levels group/project/cover/name), contradiction R53/R55 and docket R20 (members never named across projects or in public). Design Requirement 6 (an individual only in official capacity) is applied by none of the case modules read here: case-authoring R22 and case-disclosures R6 publish findings and every relied-on material whole, and R34 leaves republishing judgement to the group. K1452/K1455 widen what the record holds; nothing in these modules screens what a case publishes about people in the record. A PEOPLE design needs one publication rule for persons named in findings and materials, homed once.
- **PEOPLE ↔ ORGS.** The docket's named subject is a single entity (`inquiry_subject_entity`, one column), and a standing holder is a free name; `from: other` is a free 200-character name with no resolution. A docket is therefore a source of person/organisation statements that never meets `entities`.
- **EVENTS ↔ TIME.** Every event-like date here is a single string: a document's reader-stated `date` (contradiction R9), a docket entry's posting day (UTC; code), a clock's `YYYY-MM-DD` (queue-producers R25). None holds approximate, ranged or uncertain dates, and contradiction compares dates as raw strings. The ruled `civil-time` and `chronology` modules (K1438–K1439) would be the home that contradiction K4, R25's entity ordering and the docket's `date` should read.
- **EVENTS ↔ ANALYSIS.** Contradiction's resolution kinds already name event relations: `double_speak_or_reversal` (a statement reverses another), `superseded_version`, `obligation_against_act` (what should have happened vs what did, read by conformance R21). Progressions (via queue-producers R2) is the only existing "missing predecessor / overdue successor" sequence detector. An EVENTS construct should treat these as its first consumers and avoid a second sequence-anomaly mechanism.
- **EVENTS ↔ the group's own acts.** The docket shows the boundary the taken word "action" requires: a subject's `statement`, `response` and `outcome` are world events captured as docket entries that are "never evidence" (R17); the group's actions (plan checkpoints) are offered but never performed by the docket (R3). An EVENTS construct must decide whether a docket `outcome` (e.g. a body's later vote) becomes an event in the record, since today it cannot be cited.
- **MONEY.** None of the eight modules holds amounts, funds or flows. The nearest are contradiction's `precision` label/`same_fact_different_precision` dismissal (two figures differing in rounding would be dismissed as a lead, not compared) and T32's left-out A37 "no amounts or funds as values" for progressions. A MONEY construct would need its own key in contradiction (two amounts for one commitment), a disclosure row in case-disclosures/case-grammar (flat rows, K549), and queue producers for money anomalies.
