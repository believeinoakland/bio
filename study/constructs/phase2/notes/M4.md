## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **retrieval** (L5): runs the query language over the record; keeps each bundle's projection and full-text index; answers searches at bundle and meaning grain, each with the four-level statement of which absence is true; holds selections (leases of 300 s); reads the frontier (what was looked for at each level, what came of it, what nobody has looked at); since T19 it also holds the gated bundle roster (`op=list`, `index`, `image`, `file`). It mints nothing: "a hit is an address" (DEC-24).
- **query-language** (L5): the one grammar members type and the one pure compiler to SQL; fields (closed `FIELDS` registry) and five meaning arms (`leg`, `resolves`, `concerns`, `content`, `passage`), the viewer gate the only gate, relevance ordering without a published score, `NEAR` proximity (K105).
- **observation-log** (L5): append-only log of *looking* at four levels (internet, document, content, meaning), with vocabulary of states (LOOKED_ABSENT, LOOKED_INDETERMINATE, partial, PRESENT; NEVER_LOOKED never stored), missing-row causes, the row-whole visibility fence, and the member's **lead** ("somewhere to look and never evidence").
- **intent** (L7): aspirations, goals, objectives with a satisfaction condition over a progression and an entity; progress derived on read, gaps as proposals; the discovery loop (adopt/question/defer/dismiss, machine may only `question`); ageing of machine-surfaced questions; `servesOf` for scheduler's rank.
- **reevaluation** (L7): says which findings are affected when something they rest on changes, deriving the obligation on read from causes (supersession, edition, deferred, reopened, dismissed, corrected, source, attribution, withdrawal, contested, acceptance, cited_case_moved); pushed version notices with adopt/keep; `onBasisChanged` listeners; changes nothing itself.
- **monitoring** (L10): the daemon's watch over monitored addresses (cadence, archive fallback, Drive shells, gathering grammar, named requests, docket watch of other groups' editions), action-clock `deadline-recheck`; detects change mechanically, never decides its meaning.
- **scheduler** (L10): one reconciling Durable Object alarm with a registry of consumers (due/wake/tick); ranks batch work by `intent.servesOf`.
- **acquisition** (L3): the fetch act (direct, Drive export, archive via Memento, rendered), content-addressed storage, receipts, profile, supporting files, co-attestation; returns a provenance document and writes no bundle.
- **sources** (L3): the **person behind a capture given to the group** (the knocker/doorbell), held as an append-only, dated, attributed, evidenced history of disclosures (pseudonym link, attribute occupation/employer/role, name), with sight lists, read log, consent per entry per audience, a rung ladder, and `publishableAt`. The only built person-history construct in this set.

## Cross-construct observations (connections between the nine constructs these documents make or imply)
- **Sources is a seed for PEOPLE, but on a different footing.** It already has the shape a person record needs (append-only dated history, evidence, `how` the fact became known, a later entry superseding, identity claims that never merge, rungs of knownness, as-of publishability), yet it covers only the knocker, has a closed three-word attribute list, no validity period ("employer from–to"), and no link to `entities`. A PEOPLE design should reuse its pattern (claims about a person as evidenced, dated entries; linking without merging) and must decide how a source and an examined person relate when they are the same individual, keeping source protection (sight, consent, never first to publish) apart from the open record K1452/K1455 allows.
- **Identity resolution is already a logged, graded act** (observation-log R8 per resolution attempt; query-language `resolves:` grade with C = flagged for a member; `concerns:` by entity_id; frontier `reference` and `entity` kinds). Same-name ambiguity for people would extend this, not start anew; the one-sided-evidence rule for references and entities (observation-log R11) applies to "nobody looked for this person".
- **Search is the bottleneck for all three.** `FIELDS` is closed and registration only re-homes existing fields (verified), the projection has no person, event-date or amount columns, and range queries exist only for registered time/number fields. People (post, period), events (event date, kind) and money (amount, fund, stage) each need fields and probably meaning arms in `query-language` (layer 5), with `as_of` marks (R17) and the four-level absence statement (retrieval R13) at the new grain ("no payment of this kind in any ledger read" vs "ledger not read").
- **Events: the record already holds two kinds of events, neither of which is world events.** The group's looking (observation-log) and the record's own changes (reevaluation causes, all derived on read with `since`) are event logs; world events (meetings, votes, payments) have only progressions' stages (intent R4) and action clocks. `per_meeting` monitoring explicitly waits on a meeting schedule the plane does not hold: an EVENTS construct with scheduled meetings would let monitoring compute that cadence (TIME × EVENTS × ORGANISATIONS).
- **Change propagation is the shared spine.** reevaluation's `onBasisChanged` and cause arms are how any construct's revision reaches findings; a person's credibility already rides it (R29, R32, R28) without regrading. A corrected event date, a rescinded vote, a restated budget amount would each need a cause arm (derived on read, never stored, R18) or would reach findings only as a newer capture (R14).
- **Time model.** Everything in these modules is UTC instants to the second (observation-log, acquisition, reevaluation C-10.1) or UTC days (monitoring R50). Events with approximate or civil dates and money by fiscal period will need the ruled `civil-time` layer; the as-of reads that exist (`publishableAt({at})`, Memento `Accept-Datetime`, `as_of` on cached columns) are the precedents.
- **Intent and the scheduler can carry the three's work.** Objectives over entities (intent R4) could target people (every official in a post filed a disclosure) or money (spending per program), but conditions measure progression instances and filter only `entity_kind`; money-valued conditions wait on progressions R32 (T32 "Left out" A37). Anomaly detectors over people/events/money would enter as proposal sources (intent R15) and be ranked by scheduler R10.
- **Reachability gap.** Intent, sources, leads, the frontier and monitoring's member reads are built and declared as ops yet called by no screen of `civicos-ui/app.html` (UX deferred to Bob, K633, K899 (2)). Any people/events/money capability will face the same: built is not reached until the new interface lands.
- **Monitoring watches addresses, not subjects.** Watching a person, a program or a fund would require a subject kind beyond the address (R15) and a fetch authorisation from store state (R36): e.g. a named request (R28) generated from a person or fund of interest, ratified by a member.
