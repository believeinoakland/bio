## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **record-core** (L2, APPROVED 2026-09-26): the record's storage: id allocation (sequential `allocId`; opaque `mintOpaqueId` for gated PROJ CASE DRAFT RVG TASK), leases, the append-only `history` and `manifest`, `commit`/`transact`, purge with per-module table declarations, settings (incl. `jurisdiction_profiles`), the audit pass and its registration seams, stats/counts, `stampInstant`/`instantOrder` (UTC), the `perItem` set form (max 100), censuses, and a stated SQL read contract (R37) other modules join. Holds no member or fence.
- **provenance** (L3, APPROVED): the trust root: the register (one capture, one home), the plane's acquisition receipts, version chains of an address, the capture grade by route (direct B, archive C, unrecorded/doorbell authored, testimony D), a member's firsthand observation (`testify`), a member's declared origin of a document. Chain/route went to `provenance-routes`, attestation/instance key to `attestation` (N512, T25).
- **membership** (L2, APPROVED): the group's own members (founder, administrators, invitations, enrolment, capabilities, declared/confirmed expertise, cover and handle) and projects as working groups (participation, ownership votes, visibility, requests to join), and the one sight rule (`viewerPredicate` R43, `sight` R44, `hiddenBundles` R88) every module uses. Credentials went to `credentials` (K637).
- **instance-setup** (L11, APPROVED K102): the producing group's slug (once), display name and verified domain, active jurisdiction profiles (with a `view` of deadlines, venues, legal organisations), the root page (claim, sign-in, enrolment; a placeholder record browser), the instance's reports (bootstrap, selftest, livefire), and the runtime's measured limits (`op=runtime`, `op=cpuprobe`).
- **text-chain** (L1, all met T2): a pure library: the transcription provenance chain (derivation only weakens, DEC-4), confidence honesty (DEC-35), image anchors, member attestation and its extent, the reading-position and content-extent geometry (pdf-page, doc-para, sheet-cell, slide-shape, sheet-range, doc-table, image), tier-2 merge. No store, network or clock.
- **reading-pipeline** (L4, split from extraction N513, T25): produces a capture's reading from stored bytes through the tier ladder (plane format entry, pdf-worker, ocr-worker), composes the chain, runs the document type's reader (docprofile) to read entities and facts, states reading provenance, counts and text units (wire ≤512 KiB). Pure; no table.
- **ratification** (L8, APPROVED K102): the two publication ceremonies (owner signs the case document; each finding and its evidence crosses by its own signature), the case-conclusion comparison, the case-document catalogue (C-41), attribution and anonymous-testimony gates (DEC-102, DEC-119), bulk release (collected→verified) and bulk retirement (verified→retired). Commits through `publication`.
- **review** (L8, APPROVED K102): the review copy (DEC-31): a draft case shown inside the instance to members with standing and to named outside recipients (a label, never a member) through a revocable read-and-comment grant; never publishes, signs or leaves the instance.
- **project-stage** (L8, DRAFT for BOB, split K617/K651): a project's stage (forming, investigating, matured, closed), what each earned and still needs, with `since` instants, and its cases' readiness ladder, all derived at the read and never stored.

## Cross-construct observations (connections between the nine constructs these documents make or imply)
- PEOPLE × membership: the system's only person model is the member (cover, handle, expertise, never deleted, publication of the pairing by the member's choice). A people construct would need a second, distinct person kind for people in the world, plus a link from member to examined person for conflicts of interest, which no module holds today. Membership's privacy patterns (cover administrator-only, per-person publication choice, never-deleted rows with recorded actors) are reusable precedents.
- PEOPLE × publication: ratification's attribution ladder (name / cover / project / group, anonymous tip needs a corroborating leg) and SUBJECT_POSITIONS (sought for response) are the existing publication-side rules for persons; Design Requirement 6 (official capacity only) is not stated in any of these nine modules, so its home is elsewhere (publication / case-authoring).
- PEOPLE × EVENTS: testimony is the only person-authored dated statement (observedAt); officials' statements and acts live only inside captured documents with no speaker or event object. A "statement" event (who said what when) has no home.
- EVENTS × TIME: every timestamp in these modules is a UTC instant (record-core R47) or a calendar date (testimony); no approximate or uncertain date, no local time. Provenance's two-clock rule (document dates never order captures) and review's `undetermined_within` show the doctrine an event timeline must keep: never compare two clocks in one column; state ties as undetermined.
- EVENTS × derived state: project-stage's "computed at the read, never stored, a move is a move of its inputs", with `since` moving after a withdrawal, is the pattern a derived timeline or "what should have happened" status could reuse; its bounded read (pages of 500, ≤2,000, else undetermined with `at_least`) shows how to stay on the everyday path.
- MONEY × content: text-chain and reading-pipeline give cell-level positions in spreadsheets (sheet-cell, sheet-range, doc-table) and read CSV faithfully, but hold no number, unit or currency; a money construct starts from these extents (cite an amount to its cell) and must add value parsing and the 512 KiB wire / CSV bound limits.
- ORGANISATIONS × PEOPLE: reading-pipeline's `staff_directory` reader already yields staff entries keyed by e-mail address from a body's directory (staffing of an organisation as of a document's date); provenance's `declareOrigin` (office a document came from) is built but unreachable. Both are footholds for "staffing over time".
- ALL × sight and purge: any new people/events/money table must declare itself to record-core purge (R21), subtract membership's `hiddenBundles` (R88), never widen at EXISTENCE (R44), and register counts (R63) — and personal data has no expunge path (I-19 not built), which bears on privacy, legal exposure and the B8a ruling that any stated personal fact may be held.
