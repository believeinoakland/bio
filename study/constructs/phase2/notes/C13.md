## Cross-construct observations

### Synthesis (C13's reading of these nine documents)
- PEOPLE × action addressees: D1 (2026-09-30) "never a private individual" as addressee predates K1452/K1455; the record may now hold individuals, but the group's own action is still addressed to an office by role and body, a reporter by role and outlet, an organisation/group by role and organisation, or an audience (actions R9 amended; action-plans R10; tests negative control "a named person with no role and organisation refuses"). A PEOPLE construct must keep person records distinct from addressees; an office addressee already carries `entity_id?` (deltas L13), so an addressee could link to an office held by a person (a post) without addressing the person.
- PEOPLE × AI runs: the planning run "reads no person's attributes" (planning-skill R51; §3 Q4 "never a person's attributes", BIO_Action_v0_1.md §4 rules 8, 10). Any PEOPLE design that holds career, credentials, family ties (K1452, K1455) must say which runs may read them; today's Action doctrine bars the planning mode from them.
- PEOPLE × the group's own people: three identity kinds already appear: members (contact R44, authorship, declared expertise in `membership`), non-member professionals named by grant with stated credential never verified (filing-templates R8–R9), and outside parties named by role. The "held by value at the time of the act plus stable id" rule (filing-templates R16) is a ready pattern for names/aliases-as-of. The stated-not-verified credential (bar number) is a ready pattern for PEOPLE credentials (licences, degrees) carrying basis and grade. D6 (stake disclosure, DR 6) is the existing hook for the group's own members' ties to those examined.
- PEOPLE/EVENTS × safety: actions R47 pressure entries (threats, retaliation, discrediting, legal harassment against the group or its supporters) are events done by people to people, evidence that may start an inquiry; the litigation-hold OBLIGATION (DEC-61) follows from them.
- EVENTS × TIME: the Action design's dated things are all "date with basis" (regulated dates `{date, basis}`; clock entries; deadlines that name their basis, layer-9 contract), and all business-day counts depend on per-office calendars that are researched, confirmed, lapsing facts (local-facts R1–R5; action-clocks R10). An EVENTS construct's world dates (a meeting, a filing received after close, an e-filing outage) need the same per-office calendar, time zone and hours (jurisdictions R41–R43), and the same three-way status (confirmed/unconfirmed → computed with a statement; disputed/absent → undetermined).
- EVENTS × the group's own acts: the Action design separates the group's own intentions (checkpoints; missed = never a finding, R23) and the group's acts (actions, sending recorded, append-only history) from the counterparty's deadlines (a CONDITION on the action) and from world facts. "action" is taken for the group's own act; scenarios/phases/checkpoints are the group's process, not world events. An EVENTS construct must not mint findings or conditions from the group's own timeline, and an impact claim needs "a leg outside the group's own actions" (actions R7 amended).
- EVENTS × ORGANISATIONS: which calendar governs an office depends on its organisational affiliation (Grand Jury convened by the court, housed by the county; City offices under employee MOUs), itself uncertain — events dated against offices need the organisation construct's as-of affiliation.
- EVENTS × processes: the template lifecycle (draft → in review → reviewed → approved → updated/retired, each act dated and attributed, reviews pinned to a SHA) and plan scenarios (phases, branches, cycle refusal, cross-subject waits derived on read) are internal process models that could inform "processes and their steps" and "what should have happened against what did", but they are the group's own records, not world events.
- MONEY × Action: the Action layer bans cost/budget/amount-to-spend/assignee/hours/score keys (action-plans R26; ruling 3) — about the group's own resources. A MONEY construct about the government's money must not be confused with this ban; plan options touching world money would reference it through subjects (inquiries/determinations), not plan keys. The worked case is a bond measure; finance offices (Controller, Treasury, Auditor, State Controller) are profile counterparties; labour MOUs and payday calendars appear as sources but nothing models money.
- Architecture patterns relevant across nine: derived-on-read liveness and checks (R8, R15, R19), append-only + purge declaration (R27), registration hooks for upward reads under the total order (ai-runs R47), proposals stored apart with run/skill/sources (R11, R31), one-answer refusals for absent/invisible (R22), profile facts as data with basis and conflict withholding (jurisdictions R39 "withheld and reported in `conflicts`"), and a generic member-confirmation store keyed by fact path (`local-facts`, "generic by key so any `UNMEASURED` fact ... can later be confirmed the same way") which could serve confirmations of facts about offices, posts or money rules.
- Fit with all nine (K1460): LAW — regulated dates and deadlines name a statute/order basis; lobbying must name the standard enforced (R12). COURTS — venues, tiers, counsel at Tier 3, the venue's evidence standard as profile data (jurisdictions R39), venue hours and holidays (M-NEW-3, M-NEW-7). ANALYSIS — subjects carry support from grades/strength (`projectBar`); breach gated on a live noncompliant determination; refused reads → UNDETERMINED in a proposal's why. QUESTIONS/assistant — proposals apart, labelled, sourced, never ranked, member adopts. ORGANISATIONS — addressees by role and body/outlet/organisation with `entity_id?`; offices' calendars by affiliation. TIME — date+basis, per-office business days, time zone, confirmation horizons. A PEOPLE/EVENTS/MONEY design should reuse these seams (addressee shape, date+basis, local-facts confirmation, proposal pattern, attribution by value) rather than add parallel ones.
