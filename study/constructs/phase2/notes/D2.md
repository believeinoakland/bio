## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- design-principles.txt: "Civicsmith Design Principles", design step 1 of 5, approved by Bob 3 Oct 2026 (8.5 amended 4 Oct, DEC-127). Nine families, 57 principles (Respect, Depth, Truth, Weight, Where you are, The assistant, Alive, For everyone, One product) each with statement, practice example and source rulings (DEC-24…DEC-127, K1). Gaps G1–G5 ruled. The yardstick for every later screen. Kept at docs/development/ux-substrate/design-principles.html.
- design-brand.txt: "Civicsmith Brand and Voice", design step 2 of 5, approved 4 Oct 2026 (DEC-124 to DEC-127). Who speaks (group leads, Civicsmith credited quietly, Believe in Cities the network), name spelling, seven voice traits, tone by moment, word list (use/avoid), addressing people, V1–V4 answered (plumb-bob mark, group logo, assistant says "I", translation).

- design-measures.txt: "Civicsmith Measures Map", for Bob's review, revised 2026-09-29, marked "Draft · not yet canon" in text (but HANDOFF l.89 lists it as approved, DEC-82). Every scale a member meets: four letter scales (Capture, Connection, Testimony, Subject match), how strength combines (weakest, side by side, against the project's bar), progress bars (project stage, work-product readiness, source identity, closed project), act weights, and marks for what is not known.
- design-HANDOFF.txt: the UX design session's handoff (rewritten 2026-10-03, kept to 2026-10-05). Who the session is, channel with BOB, ruling checklist (DEC entries, next free DEC-128), where the design phase stands (steps 1–2 done, step 3 journeys draft, 4 visual language, 5 layouts), what is owed both ways, and U41: Bob's capability questions (time, organisations, law, courts, arithmetic, high-level questions) with findings from requirements.
- design-view-matter-page.txt: "Matter Page" design view (ACTION_DESIGN #1, sample data, nothing saved): one matter (the certification of a school bond measure as passed) on a plan, its determination table (standard / requires / the act did / outcome), consequences table (who or what / measure / how known / caused by the act), escalation stage proposal, actions, and pressure recorded.
- design-view-plan-page.txt: "Action Plan Page" design view (ACTION_DESIGN #1): a plan "Bond measure: certification and proceeds" with checks, two subjects (certification; deposit of bond proceeds into general fund), options (assistant-suggested), scenarios with started/planned actions.
- design-view-start-and-send.txt: "Starting and Sending" design view: step 1 the ACT preview of starting an option (kind, addressee office, rests-on subjects, clock, contact cover names, asserts-a-breach refusal), step 2 the assistant-drafted complaint text with filled blanks citing sources, approve/record sending.
- design-view-surfaces.txt: "Action Surfaces" design view: Standards table (citation, kind, issuer, source, in force on date, text), assistant-proposed standard, Tier 2 filing draft with filled blanks, Tier 3 counsel packet (includes chronology), and what reaches the queue (condition / obligation kinds).
- design-ux-surfaceRules.txt: a JSON array of surface rule sheets (the UX substrate's per-surface rules): for each surface (Queue, ACT, Question workspace, ...) its purpose, mustShow, mustNeverShowOrDo, statesToDesign (undetermined, withheld, refused, truncated, empty, loading, stale, conflict...), primaryActs (with rung and weight) and accessibilityAndLanguage; every line marked basis fixed/open with src (requirement R ids, DEC, K, Interaction Constructs sections).
