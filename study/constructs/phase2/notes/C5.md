## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **INVESTIGATIVE-SESSION** (design, Bob with session BOB, v3 2026-08-07, status block through 2026-10-01): the design for the investigative session, a skilled AI run that reads an inquiry, searches four levels (meaning, content, documents, open internet) and writes SUGGESTED basis versions a member accepts. Carries DEC-60/61/62, the version mechanism (§6), CURRENT and CONCLUSION as per-project relationships (§7, §7.1), the run object (§11), strength pair (§12), published cases (§13), bias fence (§14), integration (§14a), run architecture (§14b), D-222 retrieval options (§14c), withdrawn positions (§16) and the IS-1..9 decomposition (§18, all built per QUEUE 2026-09-13). It is about the assistant and the inquiry machinery; it says almost nothing directly about people, events or money as constructs, but sets the rules any such construct's AI work and evidence must follow.
- **ASSISTANT-PILOT** (design, session BOB 2026-08-04, amended to 2026-10-03; status as of 2026-09-14): the pilot design for the assistant (prompt → INTERPRET → FIND/HELP/CREATE/ACT → wizard). Training is self-description layered by drift rate (doctrine, vocabulary, capabilities, surfaces, wizard scripts); the pilot credential is read-only. Largely unbuilt: §2 flow and §3 wizard DESIGNED-not-built; surface registry, recipe format and `ai` credential class built; the investigative session shipped first. Bears on the three constructs only through how an assistant names people, bodies and absence, and how its vocabulary must come from the plane.
- **RETRIEVAL-SUBSTRATE** (probe 2, measurement study 2026-07-25, status as of 2026-09-25): what search, filter, list, sort and select require of the plane, measured at 20,000 synthetic rows; Bob's settled design of 2026-07-25 (searchable locator/authority, results with full provenance, relevance default, server-side selection, bare string = AND); the public-class fence fix (0.14.1); the D-15 viewer predicate; selection leases. Everything it recommends is BUILT (`query.mjs`), but the substrate is document-grain and content-grain search is absent. Bears on the three constructs as the storage/search precedent (WIDE typed columns over EAV) and the runtime limits any new table must respect.
- **CONTRADICTION-IDENTIFY-DESIGN** (v0.1 draft, BOB #16, 2026-09-19; status as of 2026-09-25): level-2 design for IDENTIFY, mechanism 1 of 3 under Case Making §CONTRADICTION (IDENTIFY → PRESENT → RESOLVE). Pairing is deterministic in the plane (four keys K1–K4); judgement is the machine's labelled proposal (`world`/`record`/`precision`/`unrelated`/`undetermined`); acceptance test is the false-conflict rate (threshold 0, provisional). Items 1–3 built (`op=contradictionpairs`, the 26-pair synthetic fixture, `op=contradictionpropose`, `contradiction_candidates`); PRESENT and RESOLVE not designed. Bob's own examples are about statements over time, rules vs acts and money figures, so it bears directly on PEOPLE (statements), EVENTS (dated acts) and MONEY (figures at differing precision).
- **FINDINGS-WORKPLAN** (ledger, session BOB 2026-08-07, status as of 2026-09-14): the catalogue of the 2026-08-07 investigative-session sweep — seven families (A register repairs, B debt/measurements, C milestones, D the design rewrite, E code work, F Bob's seven, G final-comparison gaps F9–F11), three execution waves (all executed) and a status log. F9 (stale BOB-4 text) still open. Process history; almost nothing on people, events or money; one relevant record of how DEC-52 (machine-written constitutive acts) changed.
- **RETRIEVAL-PROBE** (probe 1 measurement, 2026-07-24; status as of 2026-09-14): FTS5 vs table scan vs exported index on real workerd SQLite, held to one matching rule with three-way agreement proved; recommendation (FTS5 inside the DO) ADOPTED; its five design questions superseded by RETRIEVAL-SUBSTRATE. Bears on the three constructs only through runtime limits, scale curves and the fence; its real-corpus queries show the live record is money-heavy (sewer fund, transfers, fund statements).
