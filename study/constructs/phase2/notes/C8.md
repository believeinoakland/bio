## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **MEMBER-KNOWLEDGE-DESIGN** (MK; D-184/D-194; BOB #14, amended by #15, #19, #34-carried provisionals, DEC-102 of 2026-10-01). It covers a member's firsthand observation as authored content, graded on a third axis `testimony` = D. It also covers attribution in a published case, chosen by the author per (case edition, observation) among group · project · cover · name, where name means the handle. Other topics: the LEAD (an authored tip that is never evidence, with existence-private visibility and the counter/size disclosure rules), and the opinion as a case element that is never a leg. Built: MK-1, 2, 4, 6, 7. MK-5 (opinion) is not built. Surfaces belong to Program B.
- **EXTRACTION-BREADTH-DESIGN** (EB; Part II §18 piece 4; BOB #11, 2026-09-14, with many later amendments). It covers the order in which content-type readers are written, set by a census: minutes, staff report, ordinance/resolution, staff directory, budget-or-dataset, financial report. Also covered: tables and images as content (the `sheet-range`, `doc-table` and `image` refs, with `cited_as: text|bytes`), the AI EXTRACT role's productions, opt-in tier-3 re-extraction (D-319), the per-page tier-2 rule (D-283), and the unfunded external OCR tier (DEC-74).
- **DOCUMENT-PROFILES** (DP; the design of record, 2026-07-30, with two Bob rulings). The `docprofile/` package: stack handlers, three digests (identity / rendition / evidentiary), five verdicts, a six-layer change pipeline, monitoring contract per kind (substance / membership / unmonitorable), and content type as a separate axis whose types do detect / parse / assess / connections. It includes the calendar's event vocabulary and the split between referential and temporal connections, including "absence with a due date". Seven content types are registered.
- **OFFICE-FORMATS** (OF; research of 2026-07-31, now built as the FORMAT axis). It covers the OOXML/ODF container reader, the DOCX/XLSX/PPTX/ODF entries and the CSV entry (FW-23). Also: the DEC-5 evidentiary envelope (formulas beside cached values, tracked changes with author and date, comments, speaker notes, hidden sheets, and core properties such as creator and lastModifiedBy), the "risk" section on personal data the publisher left in files (superseded by DEC-5), the D-124 restricted-material limitation, and the designed but unbuilt `envelope` extent kind.
- **CONTENT-SEARCH-DESIGN** (CS; Part II §18 piece 2; BOB #11 with many REC/UI amendments). It covers content-grain search: `capture_text` with FTS5 units per element reference, the `passage:` and `content:` arms with `rows=passage`/`rows=content`/`rows=leg`, unit and byte bounds, and the four-level absence envelope with its five-bucket tally and presentation rules (named denominator, SAMPLE, no proportions). A hit is an address and never a minted row. D-241/UI-95 state when a connection derivation was cut.
- **SCHEDULER** (SCH; REC-1, 2026-07-31, superseded in place by N225/K528). It covers the plane's one reconciling Durable Object alarm and its consumer registry, now the `scheduler` module with 15 consumers (`SCHEDULER_ORDER`). Topics include no cron and no second alarm, idle self-termination, `calibration-reprobe` (a 30-day cadence that marks a subject owed and runs no probe) and `bias-debt` (which raises one obligation per run whose lens moved). It is the mechanism any periodic clock for events or money (due dates, ageing, cadence) would join.

## Cross-construct observations (connections between the nine constructs these documents make or imply)
- **EVENTS × TIME × ORGANISATIONS**: DP's calendar type is a working prototype of the EVENTS construct for one domain. It has typed events with significance (event / notice / routine), stable keys (`MeetingDetail.aspx?ID=`), time-window-aware absence, the referential/temporal split, and "absence with a due date". But it covers only events about meetings and documents, and none of it reaches the plane (`compare()` has no caller; the contract per kind is not reached). An EVENTS design could generalise this vocabulary, but its taken word "event" (the monitoring significance level) would need distinguishing from world events.
- **EVENTS × the taken word**: DP and SCH already use "event" in a monitoring sense ("Any change is an event; furniture moving is a notice", DP l.197) and "obligation" for the bias-debt queue item (SCH l.175). Naming for the EVENTS construct must avoid these clashes, as the brief says for "action".
- **EVENTS × QUESTIONS (expected against actual)**: "minutes three weeks after a meeting" is a due-date expectation whose threshold is local (profile data) and raises questions only. It would run as a scheduler consumer (SCH), and the ruled `duties`/`chronology` modules would host it.
- **MONEY × LAW**: the plan/actuals type split (BOB #32) is the first MONEY stage distinction in the canon. Budget = plan (budgeted/appropriated); financial report = actuals for a closed period. Neither has a reader. Formula-beside-value (OF) and the `sheet-range` citation grain are the built substrate for MONEY's derivations ("how a total was reached"). Workbook search has no unit arm (CS) and PDF tables are NO-GO (EB), so money figures are reachable mainly as cell citations in xlsx/ods/csv.
- **PEOPLE × publication × members**: the sources hold two opposite identity regimes. Members get opaque per-observation references, no legal name, handle-only, and structural anonymity of sources. Officials and staff named in documents are surfaced in full (DEC-5: lastModifiedBy, tracked-change authors). K1452/K1455 extend full tracking to every person a document names. A PEOPLE design must keep the member regime (MK) and the structural non-recording of off-record sources while tracking subjects fully. It also needs to answer how a member who is also a subject (DEC-102's "vested interest") is linked, given that member identities are deliberately unlinkable across observations.
- **PEOPLE × ANALYSIS (grades)**: AI-extracted persons named only by name grade C, and an identifier earns B (EB l.117). Testimony is D, and its weight depends on the member's identity level (DEC-102). Person-identity confidence (same-name ambiguity) has no axis here. Whether "who is this person" is a connection-grade question or a new axis is open; DEC-21's axis separation argues against folding it into an existing one.
- **PEOPLE × ORGANISATIONS (staffing)**: the staff_directory type, recognised by email domain, plus unrecognised rosters and org charts, are the staffing sources. Directory census ~400–600 documents. Staffing over time needs dated directory captures, and monitoring's membership contract (added / removed / altered entries of an index) is a natural "who joined or left" signal. It is designed but not reached.
- **EVENTS × PEOPLE (drafting events)**: tracked changes and core properties give who-edited-what-when inside government documents (DEC-5 says this IS evidence). They are extracted but not projected (envelope extent NOT BUILT). This is a ready source of person-act-date triples once `envelope` lands.
- **All three × QUESTIONS**: the four-level absence statement and REC-36 whole-row withholding are mandatory for any "who / what happened / where did the money go" search. A people-name search across the fence must withhold whole rows, because even a nameless hit discloses that a project mentions the subject.
- **All three × SCHEDULER**: any periodic work the three need (due-date ageing, re-derivation of person or money links, disclosure-deadline clocks) joins the one alarm registry with zero idle cost, and a clock only marks something owed or raises a question.
