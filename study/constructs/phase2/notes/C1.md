## Cross-construct observations (connections between the nine constructs these documents make or imply)
- Roadmap §1 shows MONEY (fund transfers), LAW (Prop 218 restriction, OMC, missing franchise agreement), ORGANISATIONS (Sewer Service Fund, General Purpose Fund, Council, Controller), EVENTS (audit, deadlines, non-response) and TIME (fiscal years, approximate label-change window) entangled in the single originating case: money flows are judged against law via event histories.
- Roadmap App. A ties PEOPLE to ORGANISATIONS (posts in City Administration, Finance, Auditor, Attorney, Public Works, Mayor) and to TIME (approximate tenures) and EVENTS (the transfer period FY2012–2021, the non-implementation) and MONEY (the diverted funds): who held office while the money moved is the people question the founding case asked.
- Roadmap §9 Skill 6 frames ANALYSIS as "what the standard requires, what the city did": EVENTS (what did happen) measured against LAW (what should), the base of any should-vs-did event model; App. B shows MONEY rules (Prop 218, Prop 26) as LAW.
- DR6 joins PEOPLE, ORGANISATIONS, EVENTS and TIME: published text names a role ("the Finance Director") doing a documented act ("certified the ACFR"); "predecessor occupants of a role" means the record must resolve which person held which post when an event occurred, even though publication speaks of the role. DR6's disclosure clause adds a member→official/contractor tie that publication must state.
