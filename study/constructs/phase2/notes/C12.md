## Cross-construct observations (connections between the nine constructs these documents make or imply)
- Digit-fidelity of OCR (DEC-35/42) is the floor under MONEY amounts and EVENT dates; a minted digit is a minted fact; `confidence: none` and the fidelity cap feed grades of any extracted figure.
- Case = container over findings (DEC-44) is the publication shape for multi-finding money or people investigations; no case-level strength composition.
- DEC-52 is the key ruling for PEOPLE identity and EVENTS sequencing: alias registration (`entityalias`), resolution (`resolve` derived / `resolvetestify` testimony), relation declaration and progression threading may all be machine-performed and machine-attributed. A person-identity design inherits the derived/constitutive split and the sidebar bulk-review surface.
- DEC-45/46's regrade-with-causal-chain is the existing mechanism by which a changed lens shows which conclusions moved — the same diff shape a changed identity resolution (two same-name people merged/split) or a corrected money figure would need.
- DEC-54's split (a bar gates, bias discloses; scrutiny statements about sources) bears on PEOPLE as sources/witnesses: "in a position to have direct knowledge", "track record, position and motive" are person-facts (posts held, relationships) that scrutiny statements would consume — a PEOPLE construct supplies the facts bias kind 1 reasons over, yet DEC-54 warns such properties are uncountable and not mechanisable.
- DEC-54's pinned, versioned external policy (source, retrieval date, hash) is the as-of pattern any external moving rule needs (MONEY rules such as fee schedules, thresholds; LAW).
- DEC-56's "record adds, never un-says" governs corrections to identity merges, event dates and money figures alike: a marker stating doubt, not a retraction.
