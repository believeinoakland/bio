## What these documents are
- **affordances** (layer 11): the plane-sourced act pre-flight (DEC-8): for one object and one caller, which acts exist, their capability, mode, weight, rung on the ladder (reversible…irreversible), prompts, and all vocabularies, published once so no surface holds a copy. Pure reads; computes no refusal of its own. Publishes `entities`' kind and relation vocabularies by reference, and the consequence statements of the six high-friction acts (incl. `entitycreate`).
- **op-declarations** (layer 11): frozen tables of every op: classes, session sets, `NEEDS` capability, stamps, `UNATTENDED_BY_DECISION`. Declares; judges and routes nothing. Every new op of any construct must be declared here.
- **wizard-scripts** (layer 11, first): the library of wizard scripts (authored step lists walked on real screens; DEC-120, DEC-121): governance like filing templates, mechanical checks, unattributed daily tallies; a step never acts or submits; drafts labelled. No construct content.
- **tasks** (layer 11): the "obligation inbox" (a member's to-do sense of "obligation"): tasks from captures whose authority is undetermined, drained, routed among members, forwarded/resolved. No construct content.
- **queue** (layer 11): the member's one feed of items typed OBLIGATION ("To do") / FINDING ("Noticed") / CONDITION ("Signal"), with homes, mutes, snoozes, dispositions; catalogue of every item kind. Holds the only sequence-related findings in my set (progressions' `missing_predecessor`, `overdue_successor`, `cardinality_exceeded`, `temporal-expectation-due`).
- **control-plane** (layer 11): the two doors (Worker HTTP entry and store dispatch): routing, stamps from credentials, envelope, the doorbell pull, purge gates (litigation hold), no-outside-loads policy. Security/system requirements.
- **publication** (layer 8): the published record: case documents, the published projection (append-only, signed, verifiable without the instance), case relation and flags, member attribution levels, source consent at commit, tensions after publication, held materials (DEC-112). The DR6/naming-individuals rule is not in it.
- **corpus-export** (layer 8): verified export of the working corpus (bundles, files, promotions, snapshots, refs, register) with an append-only log, and the verifying import that trusts nothing (Membership v2 §8). Exit/sovereignty guarantee.

## Cross-construct observations (connections between the nine constructs these documents make or imply)
- **One vocabulary home for all nine.** affordances R4 publishes every vocabulary "exactly" and by reference to its owner (R21: no surface composes text). Any PEOPLE (roles, posts, credential kinds, tie kinds), EVENTS (event kinds, event relations) or MONEY (stages, flow kinds) vocabulary must be owned by its construct module and re-exported here, and it then reaches the assistant through the pack (control-plane R41, skills, agent-worker R48). Today the only people/money vocabulary is `entities`' flat `ENTITY_KINDS` (person, body, office, contract, fund…) and three declared relations; "introducing a kind is a doctrine change, not a write" (entities/index.mjs:25–27).
- **Every new op passes four tables.** A construct op needs an `op-declarations` spec (R6 totality), a `control-plane` route via the owner's map (R26) with stamps never from the body (R29), an affordances rung or `RUNG_ABSENT` ground plus a `NON_ACTS` sentence (R3, R12 totality), and, if it raises member work, a catalogued queue kind (queue R1, R11 mint refuses `NO_SUCH_KIND`). This is the integration cost each of PEOPLE/EVENTS/MONEY adds at layer 11.
- **Entity identity is the shared substrate for PEOPLE, ORGANISATIONS and (via `contract`/`fund` kinds) MONEY**, and resolution is a reasoned act backed by `basis` and `method` (affordances R19; entities R9–R11) — identity already carries a basis, matching "every derived thing carries a basis".
- **Sequence today = progressions.** EVENTS' "what should have happened against what did" is served only by progression stage findings in the queue (`missing_predecessor`, `overdue_successor`, `cardinality_exceeded`); the generic `temporal-expectation-due` is catalogued but unproduced. EVENTS meets TIME (deadlines, `due`, civil-time: the UTC-day tally limit) and ORGANISATIONS/LAW (progressions declared per profile) here.
- **Publication separates three people-sets.** Members (attribution levels, R17, R60), sources (consent re-read at commit, R51/R52; "Withheld" identity), and people in the record — for whom this module states no rule; DR6 (official capacity, documented act) must be held elsewhere (case-authoring / case-disclosures / ratification / case-checker). K1452/K1455 widen what the record holds; publication is where DR6 would bite, so the analysts should locate DR6's enforcement outside this module.
- **Exit/sovereignty vs module tables.** corpus-export's "verified" export covers bundles only; facts kept in module SQL tables (entities, relations, resolutions, connections, progressions; and any future `lines`, ledgers, chronology tables) are not exported. A load-bearing design for PEOPLE/EVENTS/MONEY must either keep its facts as bundles or widen corpus-export R1/R3 to module tables (each with its re-derivation rule), or DEC-112's recreatability and Membership v2 §8.1 fail for those constructs. Single-answer export also bears on scale (no paging).
- **Append-only, forward correction everywhere** (affordances `IRREVERSIBLE_CORRECTION_PATH`, publication R24, wizard-scripts R18, `entitycreate` "never erased"): the record's transaction-time history is uniform, which supports bitemporal designs for careers (PEOPLE), event dating (EVENTS) and money stages (MONEY) without new correction machinery.
- **Privacy is enforced as "absent = invisible" and "counts never reveal"** (affordances R16, tasks R9, queue R33, publication R29, wizard-scripts R20): any people graph, event timeline or money flow read must answer an invisible node as absent and never count it — a constraint on bounded walks of `lines` and on aggregates (e.g. totals across funds hiding a project's rows).
- **Machine never concludes** is enforced per act (affordances R7 `MACHINE_REFUSALS`; R17 catalog never `machine` mode; wizard-scripts R19; tasks C-32.10/11): identity resolution, event relations (e.g. "causes") and money classifications proposed by AI must enter as labelled proposals and be adopted by a member's reasoned act.
- **Taken words seen in these modules:** "obligation" (member to-do code vs DEC-107 public duty; queue R48), "event" (capture queue events, wizard tallies, queue items), "action" (the group's acts), "case", "docket". New construct object names should avoid these.
