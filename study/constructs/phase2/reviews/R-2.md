## Verdict

**MONEY: sound with the corrections listed.** The three that matter most:
1. **P4 and one home.** Checks and detectors over ledger tables cannot sit in `money` before `calculations`: they become recipes in `calculations` or go by registration. A total stays a `CALC-` and is never a money fact's source. The duty–amount link lives only on the duty. Amount-free junction checks stay in `progressions`.
2. **The standards misread for the founding fund.** The Sewer Service Fund is an enterprise fund (accrual basis; net position, not GASB 54's classes), so `balance_class` and the reconciliation example must branch by fund type. FA's $2.1M is FY2023-24.
3. **Bob's new rulings.**
   - K1463 removes M-B1 and every "group's own money stays out" passage: followed impartially, unmarked; R26 kept.
   - K1468 makes extraction into money facts targeted at a member's request (whole datasets captured, facts extracted on demand); K1464 adds zone to periods; K1466 makes discharge recorded.
   - Also add Oakland's behested-payment and lobbying data, and correct the Oakland availability claims.

**INTEGRATION: sound with the corrections listed.** Its citations largely hold despite the lost context: 27 of 31 checked, and every load-bearing code and module citation. The three that matter most:
1. **The order still has upward reads, and the stages contradict it.** The timeline reads the money facts' payees (`events` before `money`). The junction checks read `calculations` tables. `lines`, `standards` and `money` at stage 1 read `events`, which is built at stage 2. Add the registrations, and move a minimal `events` to stage 1a or reserve the event-bound form.
2. **K1466 and K1468 change two settled mechanisms.** Duty occurrence states are recorded (D199 amended for them), not derived on read. Dated facts, Legistar events and money facts are written at a member's targeted request (K1443 says who; K1468 says when), not by an after-read sweep and a re-read job.
3. **Decisions for Bob after K1463 and K1465.** Withdraw I-B8. Reduce I-B4 to addressees and publication. Turn I-B5 and §3.2's A-rows into BOB's K1465 list with proposed wording. Add K1464's zone to the shared validity value. Carry MONEY's corrections, and fix the unsourced normalisation quote and the Gov. Code 7928.215 term (elected officials, profile data).

On R-1's eight points (INTEGRATION §3.4):
- **Settled:** (2) payer/payee, but see Error 1; (3) id width, verified, with the tail-alphabet caveat; (4) the `when` cache, plus zone; (6) the walk bound, but the measurement is dropped; (7) the DR6 filter's reach, now inside K1465's refinement; (8) Gov.
