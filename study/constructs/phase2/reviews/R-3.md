**Cycles and missing provisions (each a correction, with its fix).**
- **I-1. A bound that names an event has no indexable date.** C-6 makes a validity bound `{event, edge}`. `holderAt({office, at})`, overlaps' set sizes (PEOPLE §5.3), `inForceAt` and every "as of D" read are interval look-ups; SQLite can index a column, not a reference whose value lives in another module's table, and each module owns its tables and reads others only through services (`layers.md`, Bob's ruling 3). INT's `civil-time` "the caller passes the resolved bound" therefore costs one `events` call per candidate row and can never seek. **Fix:** each owner of event-bounded rows (`lines`, `standards`, `duties`) keeps a `bound_cache` (resolved instants, precision, zone; K1464) beside its `valid` value, re-derivable and never authoritative, like `when_cache`; `events` offers `onWhenChanged(module, fn)` (the `registerStep` pattern), run inside the transaction that moves a `when`, so the caches move with it; one rebuild test per owner byte-compares. Without this the founding case's 60 `holderAt` reads stay small, but overlaps over 10,000–60,000 lines (PEOPLE §5.9) become scans.
- **I-2. Absence levels are read from modules placed later.** `observation-log` uses only record-grammar, text-chain, record-core, membership, capture, provenance, extraction, content, entities, connections (`modules.json` l.45), so it **can move directly after `connections`** with no edge broken; then `events`, `standards`, `progressions`, `money`, `duties` and `people` may state the level searched from the group's looks. `retrieval` (which needs `query-language`, whose new fields read `lines` and `people`) stays where it is, and level-of-search across documents is composed in L6 (`answers`, `inquiry`), which is where INT's case 1 question is asked anyway. The first study's order had the same latent break (duties before observation-log, synthesis §3.2).
- **I-3. A body's response duty is triggered by the group's own request, held in layer 9.** INT case 1 derives "the City's response duty's occurrence" in `duties` from a CPRA request in the `actions` ledger, and **K1466** now makes this load-bearing ("an overdue response activates the next step"). `registerEventSource` feeds the timeline's "ours" lane only. **Fix:** a registration `registerTriggerSource` (or `events` exposing its ours-lane reads to `duties`, stated) that `actions` fills with sent, received and request-stage entries; `duties` derives occurrences from it; `action-plans` (L9) reads the recorded transitions (H-8) down the order; the scheduler (L10) evaluates due occurrences on the one alarm and records transitions.
- **I-4. A calculation as the evidence of an occurrence.** `calculations` is last in layer 5; `duties` cannot read a `CALC-` output. **Fix:** `registerOccurrenceEvidence` filled by `calculations` (as INT does for rosters), or the leg is composed in L6 (inquiry, K1447) and the occurrence stays "undetermined at this level" in `duties`. State which; the consent decree (case 3) needs it.
- **I-5. Who writes machine-written money facts.** MONEY §5.7 has "money facts from … a bound portal or checkbook table … once a member has adopted the table's binding", with the binding in `calculations` (MONEY §5.3). `money` cannot read it. **Fix (with H-7):** the writer is the later module (`calculations`' ingest consumer, or `sheet-worker`'s report through it), calling `money`'s write service down the order; sources are content extents. Same for INT case 2's "38 payment rows become money facts because a junction check cites them": the check in `money` cannot see rows that are not yet facts, so the promotion of rows is a member's act (or the ingest consumer's, by identifier) **before** the check runs.
- **I-6. Two `events` reads reach up.** A payment's payee in `timeline` (C-2's own test, "a payment's payee appears once in a timeline") needs `money`, later than `events`; `timeline({scope: inquiry | action})` needs L6 and L9. **Fix:** the timeline that shows payees and inquiry scopes is composed above `money` (in `answers`, or a `timeline` read in `people`/`case-authoring`), with `events.timeline` taking an explicit set of entities or event ids; the "ours" lane stays a registration.
- **I-7. The dated-fact writer's hook does not exist yet.** `reading-pipeline` has no registration; C-12 is a new requirement on it (and on `extraction`, which already has `joinPromotion`). Under **K1468** the main writer is targeted: dated facts are written for the captures a member's basis or claim needs, and the after-read step is opt-in per capture class, not every capture.
- **Group's own money (K1463).** `actions` (L9) writes the amount the group paid into `money` (down) and cites it (H-6); `action-plans` R26 stays a ban on budget keys in the group's plans.

No other pair breaks P4. The registrations INT names (`registerEventSource`, the after-read step, `registerRosterSource`) are sound; I-1 to I-5 add four more (`onWhenChanged`, `registerTriggerSource`, `registerOccurrenceEvidence`, and the ingest writer), and I-2 moves one module.

### Case 1: the founding case's sewer-fund transfers over nine fiscal years

(The brief says nine fiscal years; INT says "FY2012–FY2026 … ten ACFR years"; the auditor's figure is "FY 2012-2021". The difference is cosmetic, but the case's period should be stated once.)

1. **Capture.** ACFR PDFs, the auditor's February 2022 report and the OpenGov transfer export are captures (layer 3). Under **K1468** the export's figures are content (layer 4); nothing is bulk-extracted.
2. **Funds.** Sewer Service Fund and General Purpose Fund are `entities` with a scheme identifier (fund code with name, id-spaces R1). ✓
3. **Amounts.** For each fiscal year a member asks for the transfer line: a money fact (`kind: transfer`, `phase: actual`, `stage: paid`, `basis` as stated or undetermined) **sourced from a content extent** (H-7). The OpenGov rows may be written by the ingest consumer only when the member binds the columns and the rows carry fund code + name (I-5). ACFR figures are attested transcriptions (content R24–R25). ✓ after H-7 and I-5.
4. **The join of the two readings.** INT mints **one `transfer` event per fiscal year** for both readings to `concerns`. A year's total transfers out is an aggregate over a period, not a happening; minting an event per aggregate gives `events` objects with no participants and a fiscal-year `when`, and makes "the same transfer" an artefact of the join. **Correction:** reconcile the two facts by their dimensions (`reconcile` over kind, funds, period, basis; MONEY §5.4) and by series membership; mint a transfer event only where an act is attested (a council resolution, a journal entry, a budget adoption that authorises it). This also removes an `events`↔`money` dependency the case does not need.
5. **Relabelling.** The `MSR-` series in `money`, each inclusion the member's act with a reason; machine proposals labelled. ✓
6. **The rule.** Prop 218 Art. XIII D §6(b)(2) and OMC 13.04.080 as `standards` at version; the restriction a `duties` prohibition with obligor the City. A prohibition has no due date: its "occurrences" are the series' flows read against it. `duties` reads `money` (↓) ✓.
7. **"Was the $2.1m transfer authorised?"** The derivation needs an authorising `adoption` event (events ↓ ✓) and the **level searched** ("Legistar searched"): `observation-log` is placed after `duties` (**I-2**). With observation-log moved after `connections`, ✓.
8. **Holders per fiscal year.** Finance Director, Controller, City Administrator, Auditor: `holds` lines, bounds EDTF (`~2011/2014`) or appointment events: 4 posts × 9–10 years ≈ 40 `holderAt` reads; at this volume per-row resolution of event bounds is tolerable, but the same read under overlaps is not (**I-1**).
9. **The CPRA request** (sent 19 March 2026, due 30 March, rolled) is the group's action (`actions`, L9). The City's response duty in `duties` is triggered by it: no registration provides this (**I-3**). Under **K1466** the plan step "if no response by 30 March, file the petition" is conditioned on the occurrence's state: needs **H-8**'s recorded transition, evaluated by the scheduler's consumer, read by `action-plans` (↓) ✓ once I-3 and H-8 exist. Under **K1464** "by 30 March" is the local day in the office's zone (`civil-time`, synthesis B9 (iii)). ✓
10. **Totals.** "$52.6M over FY2012–21, about 10% of sewer charge revenue" is a `CALC-` over the series with its denominator (revenue facts, stage collected), recomputed by `case-checker`. ✓ (and under H-7 never re-entered as a money fact).
11. **Hunch.** "The transfers covered the general-fund deficit" is a source's `stated_cause` only where the audit says so; the member's own conjecture is a hypothesis row (**H-9**, K1467), never in a walk or a total. Today no module holds it.

*Volumes:* a few hundred money facts, ~10 events, 4 duties, 2 standards, ~40 lines: well under 1 MB. Nothing here strains the store. **Rework needed to reach L5:** H-7, I-2, I-3, H-8, H-9, and the event-minting correction (step 4). With those, L5 (a watched OpenGov query diffed by key, TAD §7.4's "keyed field-level diff with numeric tolerances") adds facts without changing any object's shape.

### Case 2: an official's career meets a contract's award, change orders and payments, with a family tie

1. **The official.** A `person` entity with scheme id `legistar:PersonId` (machine-written, K1443 extended, C-21). Her seat: a `holds` line (capacity elected), start bound to her swearing-in event (I-1's cache needed for every as-of read on it).
2. **Career.** "Operations manager, Bay Hauling Holdings, ~2014/2018": `holds` to the organisation (capacity employee), from a résumé passage, grade C at the name end. ✓
3. **Interests.** Form 700 filed with the City's filing officer (R-1 PEOPLE E3): Schedule C income, a money fact (`kind: income`, `precision: range`, `to` her); Schedule A-2 stake, an `owns_interest_in` line; the fact `concerns` the line (C-9). ✓
4. **The family tie (missing from INT's case).** A grand jury report states that her brother is a director of the hauler's parent: a `related_to` line her→brother (K1455) and a `holds` line brother→parent (capacity officer or director). INT's interest check (`CHK-`: "a voting participant with a line or income fact naming a party of the item within three years") is **one hop**; the family path is two (her → brother → parent). **Correction:** a check's condition names the line kinds and a hop bound (≤ 2, through `related_to`/`associate_of` only), evaluated by the scheduler into the check's stored result table (X108), never on the queue read; the "Noticed" item cites both lines and the vote. `pathBetween` (stage 3) is not needed for this; a declared two-hop check is (stage 2). Under DR6 the brother is a private individual: the published case says "a relative of the councilmember" unless Bob's refined DR6 (K1465) says otherwise; the filter in `case-disclosures` lists him (C-19). The record holds him fully (K1455).
5. **The award.** Meeting event (EventId) → vote event `within` it (EventItemId) → eight `voted` participants (hers `aye`) → `award` event. Under **K1465** the city manager who signed the contract is a `signatory`/`decider` participant (H-10). The awarded amount: a money fact `actual/encumbered` concerning the award event and the contract entity. ✓
6. **Change orders.** INT and MONEY give them phase `adjusted`; MONEY §5.3 reserves `adjusted` for budget amendments and its summation rule refuses a total across phases, so **"$2.6M committed" is refused by the design's own rule**. **Correction:** a change order is `actual/encumbered` (signed, positive or negative), concerning a change-order event that `amends` the award (event relation); `adjusted` stays a budget phase.
7. **Payments.** The city checkbook: 100,000–450,000 rows a year in comparable cities (MONEY §5.9). Under **K1468** it is a captured spreadsheet whose figures are content; nothing is extracted until a member asks. The member asks "what was paid on this contract?": rows are found by **vendor id**, but contract and PO numbers are "UNPUBLISHED AT SOURCE" in Oakland (CF §8.3, MONEY §6 stage 2), so a vendor with several contracts cannot be attributed to *this* contract by identifier. **Correction:** `committedAgainstPaid` reads a member-declared inclusion set (the `MSR-` mechanism applied to a contract) whenever the payment→contract join is not identifier-backed, and says "attributed by member, reason …"; only then does "payments past commitment" mean anything. The 38 rows become money facts by that act (I-5), not "because a junction check cites them".
8. **Finding them at volume.** Searching a vendor id across a decade means up to 120 monthly partitions of ≤ 20 MiB. In one Durable Object that is single-threaded with synchronous SQLite ("each request runs to completion before the next begins"), a scan of this size on the alarm still **blocks members' requests** while it runs. **Correction (system-wide):** heavy work runs either off the object (`sheet-worker`, a fleet Worker, returning a hashed report) or on the alarm in **slices with a stated budget** (e.g. tens of ms, then yield and re-arm), measured; INT's "background on the one alarm" is necessary but not sufficient. The opt-in payee index (~80 B/row, ~120 MB per decade per ledger, MONEY §5.9) is the everyday-path alternative and must be counted against D142's curve.
9. **Everyday path.** Her person page: one entity read, ≤ 500 lines by index on `from`, ≤ 500 events by participant (a councilmember accumulates thousands of `voted` participations a year if votes are loaded: paged, fine), money read on `(entity, period)`. ✓ But X110 warns that `connections`' co-mention reads are capped at 5,000 and pairwise-quadratic once an entity is concerned by > 32 documents: a sitting official is that entity. Co-mention for her will read `truncated` (D-224's trigger reopens): say so on the person page rather than imply completeness.
10. **Publication.** DR6 filter over claims, timeline participants and money parties (C-19); under K1465 the signer and decider are named as participants in the record; the published wording waits on Bob's refined DR6.

*Volumes:* targeted (K1468): a few hundred facts, ~50 events, ~20 lines; with Legistar votes loaded for one body, ~32,000 rows a year (EVENTS §5.7), ~130 MB a decade. **Breaks at:** EVT- or LIN- counters at 10,000 a year without C-15; ledger scans on the object's thread (step 8); co-mention for hub persons (step 9). **Rework to reach L5:** the two-hop check, the change-order phase, the contract inclusion set, the slicing rule, C-15 extended to the bundle grammar.

### Case 3: a court order's paragraphs tracked as duties through events and payments

1. **Proceeding.** *Delphine Allen v. City of Oakland* (N.D. Cal.): an `entities` proceeding (forum, number as alias at grade A); parties by `party_to` lines; judge and monitor are persons (K1456). ✓
2. **The order's paragraphs.** The Negotiated Settlement Agreement (2003) and later orders modifying it are `court` standards at version and portion (`standards`); each task a `duties` obligation `arising_in` the proceeding, source the paragraph in force. Amending orders are law relations (`amends`) between versions **and** `order` events (two homes kept apart by kind set, C-10). ✓
3. **Register.** CourtListener/RECAP entries with entry ids become machine-written `filing`/`order` events (C-16); other rows proposed. Under K1468 the register is read when the member follows the case. ✓
4. **Monitor reports.** ~60 `issuance` events (2010–2025, roughly quarterly); per-task `reported_status` rows quoted on the duty, never graded. ✓
5. **Occurrences.** A task's state comes from events that meet it (an adopted General Order, a training completed) and, for measured tasks (complaint investigations within a period), from **calculations** over the department's data: `duties` cannot read `CALC-` (**I-4**). Until I-4 is fixed, the measured half of the decree reads "undetermined" in the record.
6. **Who acted.** Under **K1465** the chief who signed a General Order and the officer who implemented it are participants with roles (H-10); DR6 governs publication (to be refined).
7. **Payments.** Monitor fees and settlements in related suits: money facts (`kind: settlement`, or `payment` to the monitor) concerning the proceeding; a decree's payment term is a `pay` duty citing them (C-7). ✓
8. **Plans.** "If the monitor reports Task 5 out of compliance, request a hearing" is a plan step conditioned on a reported status or occurrence transition (K1466, H-8). The *reported* status is a quote, not the group's state; the step should be conditioned on the group's recorded transition, which may cite the quote. State it.

*Volumes:* tens of tasks, ~60 reports, ~3,000 reported statuses, ~1,500 docket events: small. **Rework:** I-4 only, plus H-8's transition record.
