## 1. The answer in one page

Oakland's money data is OpenGov exports and PDFs; no vendor-payment ledger is published (R-2 M-E7). *Needs:* stated amounts with kind, phase or stage, basis, period, parties and one source; member-declared money trails across relabelling and fiscal years (the founding case); reconciliation; rules and commitments set against flows; ledgers kept as captured tables and read into facts only at a member's request (K1468).

ORGANISATIONS: one `holds` line kind with a `capacity` field carries every career; a duty may bind a person where a law does; a duty's occurrence state is now *recorded* (met, met late, overdue, undetermined) so a plan step can wait on it (K1466). **Architecture.**

A reading's stated dates already sit in the reading; a dated fact is materialised when a capture is cited or requested, through a new after-read hook on `reading-pipeline` (none exists today, R-3 I-7) or the member's act, after commit, its cost measured before it may join the promote transaction (R-1 V-E4; D177).
- *The AI.* Proposes events, participants and relations from prose, labelled, measured on a gold set that scores extents strictly and roles softly: guideline-driven LLM extraction trails supervised systems by about 42 F1 on strict argument matching, but o3-mini reaches 70.8 F1 with soft matching and its main error is boundary detection (R-1 V-E5). Never infers cause, links by likeness, says "knew", or ranks events.

### 2.3 MONEY

**Anticipated work** (MONEY §1; 26 needs). *Core:* A1 hold an amount as its source states it ("FY2023-24: $2.1M transferred from the Sewer Service Fund to the General Purpose Fund"; FA's figure is FY2023-24 and the page number illustrative, R-2 M-E2); A2 never confuse planned, committed and spent, or one basis with another ("That's the adopted budget, not what was spent."); A3 a scanned or machine-read figure marked and never raising a grade; B1 one flow across fiscal years and labels ("Do the post-FY21 transfers continue the franchise fee under new labels?"); B2 where money comes from; B3 where it goes; B4 a payment's chain of authority; C1 a restriction set against flows ("Sewer fee revenue may only be spent on the sewer system."); C2 transfer authority ("Was the $2.1m transfer authorised?"); D1 commitments against payments ("The contract was $2M; we've found $3.4M paid."); E1 what it bought; E2 unit cost ("Cost per pothole filled, 2019 to 2025."); E3 budget against actuals; F1 two sources of one figure (ACFR against OpenGov, PROB-2026-0007); G1 reading budget books, ACFRs and portal exports. *Regular:* precision (A4), revision history (A5), balances (B5), money given and received by people and organisations (B6), including behested payments made at an official's request (Oakland's Form 803 datasets; R-2 M-O), thresholds (C3), restricted proceeds (C4), other commitments (D2, D3), one-time against recurring (F2), patterns (F3), whole ledgers captured (G2), watched sources (G3), plain questions (G4). And, under K1463, the group's own money, followed like anyone else's.

**Ladder.**
- L0 Figures cited: cite the passage, cell, range or table that holds an amount; formulas and hidden sheets as evidence.
- L1 Money facts held: an amount as stated, with kind, phase or stage, basis, period, parties and fund, one source and a grade; totals only within one kind, phase or stage, basis, currency and period.
- L2 Flows and trails: money followed from sources through funds to payees as of a period; member-declared money trails across years and labels; chains of authority; balances in the class family of their fund type; reconciliation naming the dimension that differs.

### 4.4 Connections: one model (K1469)

**What it is.** Every relationship keeps its one owner, and every owner presents it in one shape, defined in `connection-grammar`: `{id, from, to, kind, owner, valid (precision, zone), evidence (cited extents, fact ids, or a derivation), grade {assertion, ends}, derived}`. This is FollowTheMoney's interstitial edge (a `Directorship`, `Ownership` or `Payment` is a node with dates and properties drawn as an edge) applied across owners, and a property-graph read over owners' indexed rows, not a graph database (R-3 best practice). The owners and their kinds:

| owner | kinds (members' words in C3) |
|---|---|
| `lines` | posts and careers (`holds`), memberships, education, credentials, interests held, ties (`related_to`, `associate_of`), agency, contracts, funding, structure, proceeding families |
| `events` | took part (participant with role), concerns, within, and event links (`authorises`, `answers`, `amends`, `reverses`, `stated_cause`) |
| `money` | a money fact as one edge from payer to payee, with fund, period and stage (contribution, payment, transfer, gift, income, behested…) |
| `duties` | obligor and obligee; occurrence met by an event (derived) |
| `standards` | law relations; in force at an event's date (derived) |
| `progressions` | placed on a declared flow |
| `people` | identity claims, as explicit hops |
| `connections` | mentioned together in documents (derived; the built co-mention becomes one kind) |
| `contradiction` (layer 6) | two statements in tension, by `registerConnectionOwner` |

**The read.** Each owner implements `neighbours({node, kinds, at, page})`; `explore({from, to?, kinds?, at, depth})` in layer 5 after `people` calls them down the order. Every hop is cited and graded, the weakest hop governs, the result is as of a date, ordered by hop count then date as traversal order and never by a score; it writes nothing and logs no one's exploring (D68, D91, D53). A derived connection gets a deterministic id `sha(kind, from, to, as_of, method)` so a leg can cite it and the checker can re-derive it (W3C PROV's qualified derivation; K1447). Hypotheses are never hops. Rosters, checkbooks and Form 460 schedules are not connections until a member's request reads rows into facts; the read says so by level ("ledger held as a table, not read"). `lines.chain`, `events.relationsOf`, `money.flowsFrom` and `people.pathBetween` become presets of `explore`: four walkers become one, with one bound set, one test battery and one hub rule (R-3 S-1).

**Indexes.** Both ends of every owner, with validity: `lines (from, kind, bound_cache)` and `(to, kind, bound_cache)`; `event_participants (entity)`; `event_relations (from)`, `(to)`; `money (party, period)`, `(fund, period)`, `(concerns)`; `duties (obligor)`. Derived kinds are computed per hop within the hop's fan-out.

**Bounds, settled (BOB's).** Bob's own chain is: donor −(contribution, a money fact from a Form 460)→ the councilmember's controlled committee −(`acts_for`)→ councilmember −(participant `voted: aye`)→ vote −(`authorises`)→ award −(`concerns`)→ contract −(payments, a member's attribution set)→ vendor, with the payments' payer fund (Fund 1010) the seventh hop: **seven hops across four owners**, beyond K1442's depth 6 once `within` is counted (R-3). So: **depth 8 by default, 10 at most on request; fan-out 1,000 per hop; a total of 5,000 visited nodes, after which the answer is `truncated` and `undetermined`, never a partial path shown as complete** (INT C-14); a hub (a 5,000-employee employer, a city-wide fund, a sitting official concerned by more than 32 documents, X110) is named "too common to walk" by its measured set size (M-P4). Each hop is a synchronous query on the instance's one thread, so the 5,000-node budget is held to a measured time budget and sliced if needed; Bob's chain is measured on real data before the stage that ships it (M-X1). Without the shared read a member would chain four walks by hand; with it, the chain is one exploration whose weakest link (the member's attribution of payments to the contract, grade D) is shown as governing. The family path (her → brother → the hauler's parent) is two more hops through `lines`. What the read may pass through, beyond these owners, is doctrine (C4).

**C1 · Design Requirement 6, refined (requirement meaning; K1465).**
- **The question:** Design Requirement 6 says a published work product names individuals "only in their official capacity in connection with specific documented actions" and that "accountability belongs to the role and the institution". Its examples name the *role* ("the Finance Director certified the ACFR"), not the person (R-1). Since K1452 and K1465 the record holds who decided, signed or carried out each act. So what may a published case say about them: the person's name, or only the title? And about people outside any public role (a relative, a settlement payee, a sole-proprietor vendor)? At stake on one side: accountability that survives staff turnover, and the fact Bob singled out, that someone signed an order against policy. On the other: defamation by misidentification (TransUnion: name-only matches misflagged 8,185 people), and the safety of people who hold no public role.
- **Options.** (a) Adopt BOB's draft: *"Work products document institutional actions and compliance, and the people who carried them out. A person is named, by name and role, in connection with a specific documented act they performed or took part in: deciding, authoring, signing, approving, implementing (e.g., "Finance Director Jane Doe certified the ACFR"; "the order was signed by Deputy Director John Roe, contrary to policy §4.2"). Accountability belongs to the role and the institution, and also to the person who acted; staff turnover does not extinguish either. If the institution cannot answer for actions taken by predecessor occupants of a role, that failure of institutional recordkeeping is itself a compliance issue. A person outside any official, professional or public role is identified in a published work product only where a documented act of theirs bears on the finding."* (b) Title by default; the name only for elected and appointed officials and department heads, or where the source itself names the person in the act (INTEGRATION I-B5). (c) Keep Design Requirement 6 unchanged.
- **Recommend (a), with two points confirmed.** (i) A person in an official, professional or public role may also be named for a documented *position or tie* that bears on the finding, not only an act (the councilmember's brother who directs the hauler's parent; a registered lobbyist for a client); otherwise such a person reads as "a relative of the councilmember". (ii) The rest of the requirement stands: metadata, and disclosure of relationships between the group or its members and those analysed (met by C7, with vendors named in money facts among the triggers). *Why:* it states what accountability means once the record holds who acted; the predecessor sentence matches the requirement's own predecessor clause; (b) would hide the signer Bob named as the important fact; (c) leaves the record and publication rules reading against each other.
- **What follows (BOB's):** one filter in `case-disclosures` lists every person a case names (authored statements, subjects, the published bias lens, docket entries, timeline participants, money parties) and asks for a recorded basis: a documented act or position (the `holds` line valid at the act's date and the event, both cited), consent or prior publication, or "a private party"; an unrecorded basis refuses pre-flight, naming the person to the publisher only. Carried materials travel whole and unredacted (DEC-100 (2), DEC-5): the requirement governs the group's own words and fields. This settles MONEY M-B4 (a private payee appears as "a private party" with the amount) and the register's conflict A10.
