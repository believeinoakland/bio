## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **OLD**: a v0.1 DRAFT level-2 design by BOB #11 (2026-09-14), not yet reviewed by Bob. It covers the `observation_log`, an append-only log of events about looking (fetched, changed, gone, could not tell, extracted half, found nothing) across the four search levels (internet, document, content, meaning).
