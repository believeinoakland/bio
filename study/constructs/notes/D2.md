## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
Abbreviations used in "where": SR = design-ux-surfaceRules.txt; PR = design-principles.txt; BR = design-brand.txt; MS = design-measures.txt; HO = design-HANDOFF.txt; VM = design-view-matter-page.txt; VP = design-view-plan-page.txt; VS = design-view-start-and-send.txt; VF = design-view-surfaces.txt. "L" = src line.
- **design-principles.txt** (PR): "Civicsmith Design Principles", design phase step 1 of 5, APPROVED by Bob 3 Oct 2026 (DEC-123), 8.5 amended 4 Oct (DEC-127). 57 principles in nine families (Respect, Depth, Truth, Weight, Where you are, The assistant, Alive, For everyone, One product) plus five gaps G1–G5 ruled. Each principle: statement, example, source rulings. The yardstick every later screen is checked against. Bears on constructs mostly as DOCTRINE (undetermined, five gap names, never one score, no city in product, assistant proposes never concludes, reminders) and a few TIME/ORG examples.
- **design-brand.txt** (BR): "Civicsmith Brand and Voice", step 2 of 5, APPROVED 4 Oct 2026 (DEC-124 to DEC-127). Who speaks (group leads, Civicsmith credited quietly, Believe in Cities as links), how names are written, seven voice traits, tone by moment, word list (use/avoid), addressing rules, and four answered questions V1–V4 (mark = plumb bob; group logo optional; the assistant says "I"; translation). Relevant: assistant's voice and labelling, neutral-on-policy wording of determinations, offices addressed by role, dates written out with time zones.
- **design-measures.txt** (MS): "Civicsmith Measures Map", draft dated 2026-09-29 ("Draft · not yet canon" in the body; HO L88 lists it as "approved, DEC-82"). Defines three kinds of measure: letters grade evidence (capture, connection, testimony, subject match; A–D), bars show progress (project stage, work-product readiness, source identity), weights mark acts (reversible, reasoned, terminal, signed, irreversible), plus marks for gaps (Undetermined, Nobody looked, Machine work, Newer version, Withheld, In tension). Relevant to ANALYSIS (grading, weakest-link strength, no derived-number scale), ORGS (subject match), LAW (ordinance number as identifier).
- **design-HANDOFF.txt** (HO): the UX design session's handoff, rewritten 2026-10-03, kept current to 2026-10-05. Who the session is, how it works with Bob, the ruling checklist, where things stand (steps 1–2 done, step 3 journeys in draft with open J-questions), and — most relevant — §4's summary of U41 (Bob's capability questions on time, organisations, law, courts, arithmetic, high-level questions) as researched from the requirements, plus the state of the assistant (server side built and fenced, only "check" mode deployed, no member panel).
- **design-view-matter-page.txt** (VM): design view "Matter Page", sample data, ACTION_DESIGN #1, nothing saved; per SR L1598–L1600 "design view, not stated as approved". One matter ("The certification of the bond measure as passed"): the act, a determination table (standard / requires / the act did / outcome), consequences table (who or what / measure / how known / caused by the act), escalation with proposed stage, actions with response due dates, legal tools from the profile, pressure recorded. Dense in TIME, LAW, COURTS, ORGS, ANALYSIS examples.
- **design-view-plan-page.txt** (VP): design view "Action Plan Page", same sample; per SR L3017 the plan page is "approved by Bob, K608 (4)". A plan with checks (court filing window vs phase week), subjects (matters) with determinations and escalation stages and a dependency between them, options suggested by the assistant (sortable by "regulated end date"), scenarios with sent/planned demands and due dates.
- **design-view-start-and-send.txt** (VS): design view "Starting and Sending" (per SR L3340, L3472 "design view, not stated as approved"). Step 1 the ACT preview for a "Complaint to the county civil grand jury" (kind, addressee office marked oversight, rests on matters, clock from profile, would-be refusal for an unestablished breach basis); step 2 the assistant-drafted complaint from the profile's template with each blank naming its source, exhibits graded against the venue's standard, approve/record-the-sending.
- **design-view-surfaces.txt** (VF): design view "Action Surfaces" (per SR L75, L85 "design view, not stated as approved"): the Standards register ("In force on" date, citation, kind · issuer, source match against profile, in-force status, captured text with version note, an assistant-proposed standard), a Tier 2 filing draft (petitioner/respondent, UNFILLED blanks, venue standard, profile advisory about filing windows), a Tier 3 counsel packet (chronology, deadlines that bind a claim), and what reaches the queue (condition/obligation items with whose deadline it is).
- **design-ux-surfaceRules.txt** (SR): a JSON array (the UX substrate's "surface rules", from the design branch) of 21 member surfaces: Queue; ACT; Question workspace; Tension mark, clarifier and contradiction inquiry; Document page; Project home; Case editor; Publication ceremony; Review copy; Standards, determinations, consequences, actions and escalation; Filing and counsel packet; Monitoring; Setup and install; Members, keys and credentials; Assistant; Published case; Doorbell; Search and frontier; Action plan; Assistant tray; Start and send. Each surface lists purpose, mustShow, mustNeverShowOrDo, statesToDesign (undetermined, withheld, refused, truncated, empty, loading, stale, conflict …), primaryActs (rung and weight) and accessibilityAndLanguage, every item with basis (fixed/open) and its source (requirement R ids, DEC/K rulings, PATH.md, canon §). It is the most direct statement of how time, law, courts (filings), obligations and the assistant are meant to appear to members; its accessibility and language items predate DEC-99 and DEC-127 and still read "open".

## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
- **Every deadline is a TIME–LAW–ORGANISATIONS joint.** The design never shows a bare date: a clock names "its statute" (SR L1620), an overdue item names "the entry's date, basis and text" (SR L93), options show "regulated dates with their basis" (SR L3030), the response window names "the group's stated window; basis: DEC-13 precedent" (VM L52, VP L54), the claim deadline has "basis from the profile" (VF L50). Four deadline origins appear: regulated (statute/profile), the group's stated window (with precedent), the group's own checkpoint ("ours"), and the member's reminder. Each also carries a direction of obligation ("the counterparty's deadline, not ours", SR L73, VF L57). So time support cannot be built apart from law (the basis) and organisations (who owes it).
- **As-of is one pattern across four objects.** Law in force on a date (VF "In force on" selector L10; SR L1605), captures superseded by newer captures (SR L58, L904; VF L16), question versions with CURRENT and conclusion history (SR L443), case editions with supersession (SR L2605), the lens "in force" (SR L978). Analysts may treat "as of" as a shared construct rather than per-module features.
- **Layer-order evidence (tests the brief's hypothesis).** In SR, standards, determinations, clocks, consequences (calculation) and venues appear only on action-stage surfaces ("Standards…", "Filing…", "Action plan", "Start and send": SR L1584–L2002, L3004–L3473). The Question workspace (SR L422–L640) lists legs, grades, versions and tensions but no standard, deadline, figure or calculation. VS L38 makes the order explicit: "a breach claim rests on a published finding". But three items pull law and analysis earlier: (a) the published case shows "For a finding against an office: Criteria, Condition, Cause … Effect, Recommendation" (SR L2636, DEC-84 (10)): the criteria (a standard) are needed at publication (layer 8), before layer 9's standards register; (b) the contradiction inquiry (layer 6) must handle "a conflict of norms" with a competing-hypotheses matrix (SR L667); (c) the connection scale grades a link B when "the same identifier (an ordinance number, say)" is in both captures (MS L41), so legal identifiers matter at the content and connection layers. The journeys (HO L61) add a pre-publication need: checking a claim about city performance needs "in-product calculation over a dataset", which is a stated gap.
- **The matter page is where all six constructs meet, and it is not approved.** One page (VM) holds an act by an office (ORGS), standards "in force" with captured text (LAW), a 30-day rule met "on day 21" and a two-thirds threshold against "61.8%" (TIME, ANALYSIS), a consequence "computed from the resolution and the bond schedule · grade B" (ANALYSIS), escalation clocks and triggers (TIME), grand-jury and court-petition tiers from the profile (COURTS), and assistant drafts (QUESTIONS). SR L1598–L1600 records it as basis "open", a "design view, not stated as approved"; only the plan page is approved (K608 (4)).
- **Audit-finding form links LAW, ORGANISATIONS and ANALYSIS.** "Criteria, Condition, Cause (only when evidenced, else 'cause not established'), Effect, Recommendation" (SR L1640, L2636; DEC-77 item 2; DEC-84 (10)) is the auditor's elements-of-a-finding form. It needs a standard (criteria), an office (against whom), a measured condition and a quantified effect (analysis), and audit sources (COURTS' quasi-judicial side). That matches PR L20's members (auditors, accountants, lawyers).
- **A derived number has no scale of its own.** MS has four evidence scales (capture, connection, testimony, subject match) and the rule "no form is borrowed across them" (PR L62, MS L17–L66). The one computed figure in the designs inherits a capture grade ("grade B, co-attested", VM L29), consistent with consequences R2's "weakest-input grade" (HO L72). If analysis grows (datasets, spreadsheets, budget against actuals), the grade of a derived figure must either fit the capture/connection pair or become a new scale. Either way it must follow DEC-82's measures doctrine and "totals only within a state" (SR L1615).
- **Role, never person, yet holders matter.** The doctrine is consistent: offices by role (BR L136), addressee "by role and organisation" (SR L1660), individuals only "in official role" (SR L1712), never a private addressee (SR L1727, L3390; actions R9), consequences by class (VM L33). But the designs also need office holders over time: "a public statement by an office holder" recorded as pressure (VM L61), and a plan check for "a lobbying option whose target is superseded" (SR L3020). HO L69 says there is "no holder-of-role over time" (second-hand).
- **Recurrence and meetings are nearly absent.** The only recurrence is monitoring cadence (SR L2007–L2016). Meetings appear only in examples: a special meeting that may trigger open-meeting notice law (VF L22), and a council-meeting video (VM L61). HO L68 confirms "no recurring-meeting model". Notice rules would join TIME (notice periods) to LAW (open-meeting law) to ORGS (the body that meets).
- **Fiscal and fund material appears in examples, with no surface for it.** Examples include "FY22 sewer fund transfers authorized?" (MS L78), proceeds deposited "into the general fund, against the voter-approved purpose" (VP L32), a "restricted account" (VP L58), and "$100,000,000 of bond debt authorised" (VM L29). No surface rule covers budgets, funds or fiscal periods, and HO L72 says "no budget reader". Note: SR L3077's "Never a cost, budget … on plans" is about planning the group's own work, not about government budgets.
- **Courts end at "record the sending".** The design carries a matter to a Tier 1 complaint or a Tier 2 filing draft, and to a Tier 3 counsel packet with "chronology" and "deadlines that bind a claim" (VF L45–L50). Then the member sends by the venue's own means (SR L3368; VS L62). Nothing follows a proceeding after filing: no court docket, orders, rulings or appeals. Bob's reference point is regulatory proceedings ("the CPUC especially", HO L18), and those quasi-judicial proceedings have no surface at all. The only timelines are the counsel packet's chronology and the contradiction inquiry's "timeline for a reversal" (SR L667).
- **The assistant is designed across every construct but deployed for none.** In the designs it proposes standards (VF L20), plan options from "reasoning over the matter and the same project's own earlier plans" (SR L3256, agent-worker R51), filing text from templates (VS L50), clarifier recommendations (SR L662) and translations (BR V4). Natural-language FIND / CREATE / ACT is the question model (SR L2412). But "Modes other than CHECK" are "refused as not deployed" (SR L2540), and there is "no member-facing panel" (HO L77). Every role is bounded the same way: it proposes, its work is labelled, it never concludes or advances (PR §6; SR L2471–L2498; SR L1717; SR L3251). A question-answering assistant for law, time or analysis would inherit those bounds, plus "Never drop qualifiers when compressing" (SR L2663) and the look-state vocabulary for absence (SR L2912).
- **Profile-as-data carries much of LAW, COURTS and TIME.** Holidays (PR L157; BR L44), state-code matching of standards (VF L14), action-kind clocks ("the profile states none", VS L26), venues and legal organisations (SR L1911, L3045), venue evidentiary standards (SR L3358; VS L58), advisories on filing windows (VF L31) and claim deadlines (VF L50) all come from the jurisdiction profile (K1). Richer construct support implies a much richer profile schema, or profile-like shared data.
- **Taken words in these documents (per the brief's phase-1 corrections).** "Obligation" in SR's queue is a member's queue item: "Class of each item: obligation (do / forward / resolve), queue finding …, condition" (SR L13, queue R1, R12). VF's queue labels ("obligation", "obligation · ours", VF L61–L69) use the same sense. Only "obligation-against-act finding" (SR L1640) and "counterparty's deadline" (SR L73) mean a public body's duty. "Standard" carries three senses here: a legal standard (VF L9–L18; SR L1605); the group's own rule ("The group's standard is that a breach claim rests on a published finding", VS L38); and a venue's evidentiary bar ("The venue's standard: accepts B co-attested", VS L58; SR L3358). There is also the project's evidence "bar" (MS L65). "Docket" in PR L66 and BR L28 is the case's public response log, not a court docket. "Matter" (BR L100, VM, VP) is the plan-level name for a government act under examination (DEC-114, per BR L236), and "subject" is used for the same thing on the plan page (VP L19–L36).
