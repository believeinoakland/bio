## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **retrieval** (layer 5, APPROVED K102). Runs the query language over the record and "says what it could not see". It keeps each bundle's metadata projection and FTS index current with promotion, answers search at bundle grain and at meaning grain with a four-level statement of absence, holds selections (server-side sets with drift detection), answers a capture's content-axis state, and reads the frontier: per level, what was looked for and what came of it. It also serves the gated bundle roster (`op=list/index/image/file`). Higher layers' facts (action clock columns, leg grades, decorations, fields) reach it only by registration.
- **query-language** (layer 5, APPROVED K102). "The one grammar a member types to search the record" and its compiler to SQL. It is pure (no store, clock or network), and the viewer's predicate is its only gate. Field registry (`FIELDS`), meaning arms (`leg`, `resolves`, `concerns`, `content`, `passage`), comparisons and ranges on time and number fields, `NEAR`, relevance without a published score, `asOf` notes on cached columns. Nothing in it is unbuilt.
- **observation-log** (layer 5, APPROVED K102). An append-only record of looking: each look's level (internet, document, content or meaning), authority, subject and state (`LOOKED_ABSENT`, `LOOKED_INDETERMINATE`, `partial`, `PRESENT`; `NEVER_LOOKED` is never stored), plus the rules for reading a missing row (`pre_log`, `purged`, `watermark_band`, `never_looked`), the row-whole visibility fence, and the member's lead ("somewhere to look and never evidence"). It is the substrate of the four-level absence doctrine.
- **intent** (layer 7, APPROVED K102). Holds what the group is trying to achieve: aspirations, goals, and objectives with a satisfaction condition (progression × entity × relation × required grade or stages × share). Progress is derived on read and never reported. Gaps become proposals (`objective-gap`). It runs the discovery loop (a member adopts, questions, defers or dismisses), ages questions an assistant surfaced (30 days by default), ranks work for the scheduler (`servesOf`), and lets a member set an assistant to work an objective (`workObjective`).
- **reevaluation** (layer 7, APPROVED K102). "When something a finding rests on changes, this module says which findings are affected and how, and changes nothing itself." It derives the re-evaluation obligation on read from dated causes (supersession, edition, deferred, reopened, dismissed, corrected, source, attribution, withdrawal, contested, acceptance, cited_case_moved, plus `wp_retraction` and `weakened`). It pushes version notices and lets a member adopt or keep a version. It audits "changed from" sentences and tells later modules (layer-9 `conformance` and `consequences`) through `onBasisChanged`.
- **monitoring** (layer 10, APPROVED K102). "The daemon's watch over what the group's standing intent names." It checks monitored documents at their cadence (hourly, daily, weekly, monthly; `per_meeting` has no interval), records looks, never decides what a change means, and runs the archive fallback, Drive shells, named requests and the docket watch of other groups' editions. It marks action clock entries `overdue` (`deadline-recheck`, a UTC day) and asks `escalation` whether a stage's trigger is met. The link sweep has moved to `link-sweep` (N506).
- **scheduler** (layer 10, APPROVED K102). One reconciling Durable Object alarm, "never on a cron", over a registry of consumers ({due, wake, tick}), each consumer's cadence its owner's. It ranks batch-bounded work by intent (objective gap, then aspiration, then longest-waiting, with anti-starvation). It is the clock substrate for deadline-recheck, overdue-scan, intent-age, notice-sweep, network-notices' weekly and monthly attestations, and AI run reap and wake.
- **acquisition** (layer 3, DRAFT split from `capture`, K617/K649 (1); "the split is BOB's"). The fetch act: direct, Drive export, web archive (CDX or Memento), or rendered. It hashes and stores, writes the receipt and provenance chain, profiles the document (doctype through jurisdiction profiles), handles supporting files and co-attestation, and owns the user agent. It writes no bundle and does not read the document.
- **sources** (layer 3, APPROVED K509 (1), new module, met T16). Holds a source (the knocker or person who handed material over) "as a dated, attributed history of disclosures, never as one overwritten field" (DEC-78 item 5): pseudonym links, attributes (occupation, employer, role), names, consent per audience, sight lists and a read log, `publishableAt(at)`, the rung ladder, and `onDisclosure` for `reevaluation`.

## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
- **Higher-layer facts reach lower layers only by registration, never by use (P4).** Layer-9 action clock facts (`due`, `overdue`, `addressee`) reach layer-5 search through `retrieval.registerActionFacts` (R53). Layer-6 grades and fields arrive through `registerLegGrades` and `registerField` (R55, R62). Layer-9 `conformance` and `consequences` hear layer-7 basis changes through `reevaluation.onBasisChanged` (R8). Query-language's Suggestions (query-language.txt:76) name this as the way to keep "the grammar unchanged and the order intact". It is the established pattern for exposing LAW, TIME and ANALYSIS facts to investigation (layers 5–7) without moving modules, but it carries data, not services: an inquiry cannot ask layer-9 `standards` or `consequences` a question.
- **TIME: the clock runs in layer 10 on UTC days, while the jurisdiction's time facts sit in layer 9 unused by it.** Monitoring marks action deadlines overdue at the UTC midnight after the date (`monitoring/index.mjs`:2747–2756, 2791–2813). `local-facts` (layer 9) holds `holidays`, `hours` and `time_zone` per profile, but monitoring does not use it. Acquisition renders in UTC (`render.mjs`:60–61). Query ranges compare strings. Ageing and policy ages are 30 calendar days. `per_meeting` cadence is unschedulable because "This plane does not hold" a body's meeting schedule. Bob's "richer time" (business days, holidays, local day boundaries, meeting calendars) has a home for facts (local-facts) but no consumer in these modules.
- **TIME×ORGANISATIONS: sources is a template for "holders over time".** A dated, evidenced, append-only disclosure history; an attribute (`occupation, employer, role`); claims that never merge; an as-of read (`publishableAt({at})`); and a source-as-it-stood-at-capture beside the current history (sources R1–R9). It is exactly the shape a position-and-holder model would need, but it is scoped to protecting private sources. Entities offer only `proxy_for`, `member_of` and `overlaps`.
- **TIME×LAW: as-of retrieval exists in mechanism but not in use.** Memento (acquisition R32) can ask an archive for a document at a date; the code always asks "now" and only for unreachable sources. Reevaluation's version reasoning (edition in force, frozen pair, `since` after `last_updated`) applies to evidence and case editions, not to law in force.
- **QUESTIONS×DOCTRINE: the absence doctrine is fully built at the substrate but barely surfaced.** Observation-log holds levels, states and causes. Retrieval states the four levels on every meaning read, and skills teach it. The assistant's sub-sessions search only through `op=meaningrows` with a model-written query string (agent-worker `model.mjs`:215–226). The frontier, content axis, leads, and the whole of intent and sources have no UI calls. So "what did we look for and what came of it" and "what are we trying to achieve" are answerable by the plane but not askable by a member through `civicos-ui/app.html` (the design stream's new interface may change this; not checked here).
- **QUESTIONS: there is no natural-language layer in the plane.** The member types the query grammar (structured; warnings; widening), and the assistant's model translates natural language into that grammar inside agent-worker. Answers cite addresses, never mint, and state the level of absence. Any NL question about time, organisations, law or numbers can only be as rich as the query grammar's fields (bundle metadata dates, `authority`, `addressee`, meaning arms), which have no organisation, law or arithmetic vocabulary.
- **ANALYSIS: counting yes, calculating no.** Retrieval and query-language give totals, facets, tallies and reproducible sets (selections with digests, R18–R20, R59). Intent computes one proportion (`meeting/matched` against `share`) three-valued. Nothing here sums or compares values; spreadsheets are searchable at sheet grain (R25), with named tables and ranges deferred (K102). Layer-9 `consequences` is reached only as a listener of reevaluation.
- **COURTS: the only "docket" in these modules is the publication docket of cases** (the group's own and other groups': reevaluation R30 and R33, monitoring R67). Its move stream (seq, date, kind, verified, never evidence) is the nearest existing shape for court dockets, which no module models.
- **ORGANISATIONS×QUESTIONS×TIME: the scheduler's rank ties work order to intent** (objective gap → aspiration → longest-waiting, never starving: scheduler R10, intent R28). Monitoring R33 ties watched sources to live objectives and published findings. But a legal deadline does not raise a source's watch frequency on its own: a member must type `legal_deadline_approaching` (monitoring R52).
- **Doctrine common to all nine:** no place named; hidden as absent; the machine proposes and never adopts, enables, regrades or advances. Undetermined is stated by name (query-language R5, observation-log R11–R12, intent R3–R4, reevaluation R21, monitoring R5 and R14, acquisition R11 and R15).
