## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **jurisdictions.txt** (`build/requirements/jurisdictions.md`, APPROVED K102, layer 1). Requirements for the jurisdiction-profile library: the only place local facts live, as data, each with a basis (measurement, ruling, `UNMEASURED`, `TEST`). Sections: identifier spaces, publishing systems, crosswalks, vocabulary (bodies, member titles, codes of law, file numbers, staff-report template), practice (minutes due), records laws, and the layer-9 action sections (standard sources, counterparties, action kinds with tier/venue/template/evidence, deadlines, legal organisations, holidays, time zone, office hours). Services: list, get, validate, combine (union; never chooses between disagreeing profiles). R40–R45 (templates, time zone, hours, per-office holidays, status) were added for T21 "not yet met" per the status line.
- **id-spaces.txt** (`build/requirements/id-spaces.md`, DRAFT by BOB #37 T6 sample, layer 1). Requirements for the pure judge of shared identifiers (enactment, project, fund, parcel) across two captures: recognise a value against profile forms, its coverage `reach` against floors, a parcel's standing (current/retired/undetermined), the publishing system of addresses, and `judgePair` (form → value → system independence → fund name → referent). Every "no" names its kind; absence never non-existence. Status line still says code is `bio-plane/src/idspaces.mjs` naming Oakland (N2), i.e. pre-refactor wording.
- **docprofile.txt** (`build/requirements/docprofile.md`, DRAFT by BOB #37 T6, split T19 K617, layer 1). Requirements for content-type recognition (`doctypeFor`: meeting minutes, agenda, regulation, staff report, staff directory, meeting calendar, generic), the layered change assessment of two captures of one address (`assess`, verdicts identical…changed/routine, events, referential and temporal connections), and the one text entry point for extraction (`readText`, `flattenText`, `makeLocator`). Every local vocabulary fact comes from the jurisdiction view; with no view it combines every non-test profile (K39, K880, permanent). Host-stack identification, digests and the event catalogue moved to `site-profiles`.
- **office-readers.txt** (`build/requirements/office-readers.md`, DRAFT by BOB #37 T6, layer 1). Requirements for the DOCX, PPTX, XLSX and CSV readers into `format-registry`'s I7 entry contract: detection, the I2 structure shape (links, IC-1 element references, DEC-5 evidentiary envelope: tracked changes, comments, formulas with cached values, hidden rows/cols/sheets/slides, speaker notes, core properties) and I2 text shape; a 20 MiB size guard; CSV dialect (encoding, delimiter); the shared reference builders (`docParaRef`, `docTableRef`, `sheetCellRef`, `usedSheetRange`, `slideShapeRef`, `a1Corner`, `rangeUnitFor`) reused by odf-reader. Pure; asserts nothing about meaning.
- **odf-reader.txt** (`build/requirements/odf-reader.md`, DRAFT by BOB #37 T6, N30 folded T13 K408, layer 1). Requirements for the OpenDocument (.odt/.ods/.odp) reader over one container walk, projecting into the same I2/DEC-5 shape as docx/xlsx/pptx (reusing office-readers' reference builders), plus `odfEvidentiaryDigest` (a stable substance digest for Google Drive exports, D-351). ODF is the harvest format of Google Drive exports (CAP-8/COFF-10, 2026-09-14). Adds named/database ranges as range units (R44) and a repeat-expansion bound (R45).
- **extraction.txt** (`build/requirements/extraction.md`, APPROVED K102, layer 4). Requirements for making and recording readings of captured bytes: `read` hands `reading-pipeline` the store, the jurisdiction view and the live calibration (the tier ladder moved out by N513, T25); `writeReading` (readings, references as they appear with positions/occurrences, name terms, text-source chain, text index, reading history); the reading-record reads (`op=reading`, `op=readingref`, `op=textprovenance`); the OCR re-read (`op=pdfstructure&ocr=1`); drift obligations from calibration; the EXTRACT AI role's vocabulary (`propose-reading`, computed grades); testimony indexing; migrations N26/N439; and a stated SQL read contract on its tables for later modules. Size about 4,500 lines (over P6's 4,000).
- **content.txt** (`build/requirements/content.md`, APPROVED K102, layer 4). Requirements for "the unit the record points at": the extent grammar (document, pdf-page, sheet-cell, sheet-range, doc-para, doc-table, slide-shape, image, envelope), content addresses `hash(capture, extent, chain)`, mint-or-find of content rows with mint labels (member/plane/machine), member transcriptions with second-member attestation (with a note, DEC-88), citation checks and resolution for every citing module (inquiry, basis versions, cases, actions), stale marking when a reading is replaced, the graded version notice of a cited passage against newer captures, `passageText`, crops, and an SQL read contract on `content`. Status line: R48–R51 "not yet met (T19)", R25/R43 note "not yet met (T22)".
- **entities.txt** (`build/requirements/entities.md`, APPROVED K102, layer 5). Requirements for the entity axis and Declared Bias's subject registry (one construct, Framework §13): entities of ten closed kinds (source, institution, office, movement, person, body, ordinance, parcel, contract, fund) with aliases and three closed constitutive relation kinds (proxy_for, member_of, overlaps); the recogniser resolving reading references to entities graded A/B/C (D only by member testimony); `concerns` and `namingDocuments`; `idMatch` over id-spaces; withdrawal without erasure; resolution-defect reports; the grade order (`gradeRank`, `isEstablished`); an SQL read contract. Status: R40–R41 "not yet met (T19)", R1's note (DEC-88) "not yet met (T22)".
- **connections.txt** (`build/requirements/connections.md`, APPROVED K102, layer 5). Requirements for the record's connections: derived connections (two captures resolving to the same entity, graded the weaker end, with a determining pair and member on-point choices), the portion grade a cited passage earns, the dirty-set sweep, edges between bundles (`references[]` projected: cites, links_to, supersedes; backlinks, dangling, severed), `projectLinks`, member- and source-asserted connections, agenda item-to-file containment (stored grade C, member judged), and themes (a lens, never evidence; AI may propose hunches). Status: R60 "not yet met (T19)"; R20's withheld citer "not yet met: T21".
- **progressions.txt** (`build/requirements/progressions.md`, APPROVED K102, layer 5). Requirements for declared flows of a kind of happening (stages with order, presupposition, cardinality, `within` interval, requiredness), versioned with basis and citation; instances threading captured documents through stages by following one entity; findings derived on every read (weakest-link grade, `missing_predecessor`, `discharged_skip` by an exception document, `overdue_successor`, `cardinality_exceeded`); `overdueScan` and `next_deadline` for the scheduler; the machine proposals feed aggregated per (progression, stage) and member dispositions that age without hiding. Junction checks over amounts (R32) are deferred until amounts are values.
- **bias.txt** (`build/requirements/bias.md`, APPROVED K102, layer 5). Requirements for Declared Bias: bias sets of scrutiny/inference/pattern statements about registry subjects, structure checks (no verdicts on a source, no thresholds/bars), the bias state machine at promotion, member adoption per instance or project pinned to a revision, the effective lens and its hash (`biasManifest`), reading an outside organisation's policy into a proposal that is never installed (`biasInhale`), and the bias debt raised on work products (AI runs, inquiry findings) when the lens they were made under moves. Disclosed, never blocking. R24–R26 (interactions, strictest-applies) not built/deferred.

## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
- jurisdictions carries TIME (deadlines, holidays, hours, time zone), LAW (standard sources, records laws, code-citation patterns) and ORGANISATIONS (counterparties) facts in layer 1, but its R23–R45 sections are named "the action sections ... for layer 9" (l.32): the data are low in the order, while their only stated consumers are layer 9 (actions, filings, action-clocks, standards). Layer-5/6 modules could read them as far as the layer order goes.
- id-spaces ties ORGANISATIONS (publishing systems/offices, independence) to LAW (enactment numbers) and TIME (coverage floors, parcel roll years): an identifier joins two records only if published by independent offices. This is the product's only machine join between public records by key; it never reads what the enactment says.
- docprofile is where TIME meets LAW and ORGANISATIONS in documents: meeting calendar → minutes expected-by (temporal connections), enactment captions and code citations (referential connections), body names and staff directories. Its lateness threshold is profile data and only RAISES a question (DOCTRINE).
- office-readers is the product's only spreadsheet path (ANALYSIS) and it ends at text plus cell/range references: a spreadsheet becomes evidence that can be cited cell by cell, not a dataset that can be filtered or summed. Its tracked-change and comment dates/authors (TIME, ORGANISATIONS) and redline superseded text (LAW: amendment history of a draft) sit in an envelope that is not searchable (envelope extent kind designed, not built).
- extraction (layer 4) is where the AI's EXTRACT role meets grading (QUESTIONS × DOCTRINE): AI proposals are capped at B and graded by computation. Its R52 is the only place agenda structure (TIME: meetings) is tied to the legislative file (LAW), and it waits on profile data (N96).
- content is the citation substrate every construct's evidence must pass through (a law passage, a court order page, a spreadsheet range, a dated minutes paragraph); its version notice is the record's only TIME-over-documents comparison, and its `passageText` → `consequences` R2 is the declared route from a cited passage to layer-9 calculation (ANALYSIS), skipping layers 5–8.
- entities is the only ORGANISATIONS store, and it is shared with Declared Bias's subject registry (DOCTRINE): any richer organisational model (positions, holders over time, reporting lines, obligations) would have to reconcile with safeguard 4's "relations are constitutive, never traversed" (R26) — the construct Bob asks for (traversable lines of responsibility) is exactly what R26 forbids for these relations. A separate, evidentiary relation type (graded, cited, time-bounded) would be needed, not a widening of the three constitutive kinds.
- connections joins ORGANISATIONS (entities) to evidence: any two documents naming one office are connected, but never through a declared relation (DOCTRINE R34), and only the first 32–100 documents per entity (ANALYSIS bound). Agenda→file containment is the only machine-derived legislative-history link (LAW × TIME); docprofile's temporal "expected by" connections (TIME) have no home here.
- progressions is a TIME engine lower in the order (layer 5) than action-clocks (layer 9): investigative "overdue" over declared procedural flows (minutes after a meeting, award → contract → payments), but with calendar-only intervals and no link to jurisdictions' holidays/deadline rules or to the law that prescribes the flow (LAW only as a cited basis). Its deferred junction checks are the meeting point of ANALYSIS (amounts as values) and ORGANISATIONS/LAW (contracts, awards, obligations).
