## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
- **TIME depends on ORGANISATIONS** (research-oakland-calendar): which holiday list and which hours apply depends on which office/body/venue and on that body's parent or employer (Grand Jury: court vs county; SCO: CalHR as employer; City departments differ). A business-day deadline cannot be computed without resolving the addressee to an organisational unit and its governing calendar (L136–138, L304–306).
- **TIME rests on LAW** (research-oakland-calendar): the zone comes from Gov. Code §6808; court holidays from CCP §135; City holidays from MOUs (contracts with expiry dates); state elective holidays from Gov. Code §19853. Calendar facts are law-derived facts with versions in force (MOUs expired 2026-06-30).
- **COURTS as ORGANISATIONS** (research-oakland-calendar): the profile models a court only as a filing venue; its departments, clerk's offices and channels are flattened into a `closed_note` string (L227–229).
- **Layer order workaround** (draft-planning-skill R47, R51, L21, L26): a layer-6 AI (agent-worker) reaches layer-9 law and conformance (`standards.standardRead`, `conformance.determinationRead`, consequences, `filings.availableActions`, `jurisdictions.combine`) not by module import but through plane ops declared in `op-declarations` (layer 11) under the run's credential; ai-runs (layer 6) learns about plans (layer 9) only through a registered callback. This is direct evidence for the brief's structural observation: the AI needs law, consequences and deadlines, and today gets them only at the plane boundary and only for planning (after publication).
- **QUESTIONS × LAW × TIME** (draft-planning-skill L7): the one assistant mode designed to use law, consequences and deadlines together is the planning run in layer 9's territory; it does not answer questions, it proposes options.
- **Placement of local facts (TIME × architecture)** (draft-filing-templates §3, L98; K921): `local-facts` — the module that makes calendars trustworthy (confirmation, correction, lapse) — was placed in layer 9, first, so only `action-clocks` and `filings` read calendar status; an inquiry (layer 6), the assistant or monitoring before layer 9 cannot. Yet it is "Generic by key so any `UNMEASURED` fact (an office, a deadline) can later be confirmed the same way" — a general mechanism for member-confirmed facts about organisations and law, placed late.
- **LAW × ORGANISATIONS × TIME in a template** (L17, L71, L186): a template cites a profile's law and venue; its use under another jurisdiction must be stated; the venue's hours and holidays decide deadlines — jurisdiction ties law, organisation (venue) and calendar together, but only as profile data with free-text citations.
- **COURTS × LAW** (K921/K924, L182, L195): Tier 3 matters go to counsel via briefings; the product's court-facing role is to prepare a brief, not to track proceedings.
- **Two kinds of obligation-with-a-clock** (PATH §1, §3; UX-ANSWERS OQ-25): the group's own (checkpoint = intention; queue obligation) vs the counterparty's (deadline = condition on the action). This is the seed of an obligations thread (who owes what to whom, by when) but only inside action plans, after a plan exists; and "obligation" here means a member's queue item (the taken-word clash noted in the brief).
- **TIME × ORGANISATIONS × LAW in an option** (PATH steps 4–9): an option carries the addressee by role and organisation, regulated dates with basis and a tier — the three constructs meet in the option record, but only at layer 9.
- **The assistant turns natural language into structure** (PATH step 4, L16): it fills a structured option form from a member's description — a QUESTIONS-style capability in a planning context.
- **Law → time → action pipeline** (action-plans Terms, R18): a regulated date's basis names "the statute, order or commitment"; on start, dates become "pending clock entries with their bases" in `action-clocks`. Time, law and courts (orders) meet here, but the basis is a name, not a link to a held provision or order.
- **Determinations keyed by government act** (action-plans R5): `conformance.determinationsFor({act})` — a suspected inquiry's act links forward to determinations, joining inquiry (layer 6) to conformance (layer 9) only from the layer-9 side.
- **Placement consequence**: action-plans reads every layer-9 module and so must be last; the AI planning run that needs standards, consequences and deadlines must therefore reach them via plane ops (see draft-planning-skill).
