## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **inquiry-grammar** — pure grammar of an inquiry document: entry requirements per state, the division block, the leg grammar (grounds, testimony, earned, inherited arms), recheck coverage (C-15.1), the lead checker (C-54.1), the imported-finding reference (R11, another group's published finding) and the refusal rows the inquiry's acts mint. Registered into record-core's grammar seam; reads no record. Moved out of `legacy-checks` at T19.
- **inquiry** — the inquiry ("question") as the one recursive object of case-making: its state machine, basis legs and how each is graded, the earned registry (what the record can earn for a leg), grounds (DEC-32), supersession, division, dispose/divide/ground acts, and the contradiction inquiry's vocabulary (coordinates, plurality differences, norm canons, resolution kinds, R46–R48). Holds no version, conclusion or strength.
- **citation** — the member's act of citing material or a question into a case (`cites` edge) or a question (a basis leg), plus sever/reinstate on a case and the one "may this be cited now" predicate (`retiredNotCitable`). It is about the member citing evidence, not the assistant citing sources in an answer.
- **strength** — derives "what a claim is worth" as a pair (capture, connection) plus testimony, never one value: weakest-link and AND/OR arithmetic over grounds, version strength and what-ifs, independence of grounds by shared provenance origin, anonymous-testimony rules (DEC-102, DEC-119), the versioned grading method for recomputation (DEC-112), and the project's evidentiary bar.
- **basis-versions** — the frozen, named alternative accounts ("versions") of an inquiry's basis, their six-act state machine (suggested/considering/accepted/rejected), a project's CURRENT version, the project's append-only conclusion record (stance), narrowing a leg to a part, and `appendVersion`, the door for the AI's one write.
- **run-rules** — pure rules of an AI run (bounds, endings, statuses, principal and project gates, skill-version form, state size), the one deployment order of run modes and `DEPLOYED_MODES`, and the AI-run refusal rows. Split from ai-runs at T18.
- **ai-runs** — the AI run as a durable, bounded, attributed record object: open/tick/close, the one exit (with absence roll-up), lease reaping, wake and dispatch to agent-worker, read/log/list, the surfacing step (AI may create a question within a `surfaces` bound), and the hooks productions use (`runFor`, `consumeBound`). Mode `plan` added by K660.
- **run-productions** — what a run may produce: `suggest` (one basis version in state `suggested`, checked plane-side by eight verdicts) and EXTRACT's proposed readings within the `mints` bound, labelled machine work.
- **skills** — the "skill pack" (`investigative-session`): the instructions an AI run works under, rendered from published vocabularies and fences into a resident layer and progressively disclosed layers, with the judgement doctrine (what the skill may decide), five prohibitions, absence by level, and layers for action planning, filing drafting, edition statements and wizard scripts. Holds no gate.
- **agent-worker** — the separate Cloudflare Worker ("fleet member") that performs an AI run: a deterministic control-flow table (gate-mode, plan, fanout across four levels, collect, compose, dedup, submit, adjust, close) whose judged steps are filled either by a caller's supplied judgements or by model turns; the Claude-account payer cascade; mode `plan` (R50–R53).
- **capture-requests** — the AI never fetches: a run files a capture request (a row, no bytes); the drain alone fetches through `capture`'s trusted arm after DEC-47 conduct (legible agent, purpose, host pacing), attributing to the daemon at the session's request; 24-hour expiry, retry with member-supplied credentials, sweep-scoped requests.

- **The assistant reaches TIME, ORGANISATIONS, LAW and ANALYSIS only through plan mode, which is off.** agent-worker R51 is the one AI read of deadlines, venues, `legal_organisations`, standards, determinations, consequences and available actions.   - no plane path starts a first segment;
