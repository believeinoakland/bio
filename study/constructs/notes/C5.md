## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **INVESTIGATIVE-SESSION.txt** (`INVESTIGATIVE-SESSION.md`, v3 2026-08-07, front matter to 2026-10-01): the Level-2 design of the investigative session — a skilled AI run (fleet member, separate process acting through the plane's interface) that reads an inquiry, searches four levels (meaning, content, documents, open internet), and writes SUGGESTED basis versions a member accepts or rejects. Carries DEC-60 (proactive claim formulation), DEC-61 (device-local transcripts), DEC-62 (pursue+investigate one session), the BOB-2/3/4 rulings, and later BOB #15–#33 rulings on conclusions-per-project, run provenance, replay, bounds; DEC-113 litigation hold. Build declared COMPLETE 2026-09-13 (43 rows); long "Incomplete sections" front matter records what each later REC/D/UI row built and the residual gaps. Its construct content is overwhelmingly QUESTIONS/DOCTRINE (what the AI may do) and ANALYSIS (strength pair arithmetic, query surface); TIME/LAW/ORG/COURTS appear only as examples and as the group's own litigation hold.
- **ASSISTANT-PILOT.txt** (`ASSISTANT-PILOT.md`, written 2026-08-04 by BOB, amended 2026-08-05, 2026-09-14, 2026-10-03): the Level-2 pilot design for "the assistant" — a prompt-driven helper (tag → prompt → INTERPRET → classify FIND/HELP/CREATE/ACT → cited answer naming its level, or a wizard that navigates to the surface and stops). Governed by DEC-27, DEC-24, DEC-55, D-82, D-90. Its own Status says it is "PARTIALLY COMPLETE AS A DESIGN AND LARGELY UNBUILT": the training pack is built as the investigative session's pack, the `ai` credential class is built, the surface registry (18 surfaces) and wizard-script format are built, but the prompt entry point, INTERPRET, classifier, FIND, wizard and pilot log are ABSENT. Directly answers Bob's "I thought it could do [natural-language questions] through the assistant": designed, not built.
- **RETRIEVAL-SUBSTRATE.txt** (`RETRIEVAL-SUBSTRATE.md`, probe 2, measured 2026-07-25, Status as of 2026-09-25): the measurement study and settled design (Bob, 2026-07-25) for the DOCUMENT-grain retrieval surface — five verbs (search, filter, list, sort, select), FTS5 inside the Durable Object, the `query.mjs` parser/compiler (34 filterable fields), facet counts, server-side selection leases (300 s), and the single D-15 viewer-gate compilation point.
