## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
- DEC-4 links ANALYSIS to capture grades: any figure (budget, count) extracted from an image is bounded by transcription fidelity, and only member attestation of the cited region lifts it.
- DEC-5 joins TIME, ORGANISATIONS and ANALYSIS: edit instants by named people/offices, aggregated across documents, are evidence of departmental conduct; this presupposes knowing which office a person belongs to and what documents an office has business in (remit), which the entity registry (DEC-6) does not yet name as a relation kind.
- DEC-8's single-authority rule (one authority, published or dry-run; no surface copy) is a template for any computed legal/time rule (e.g., a deadline or a conformance check): it must be computed once by the plane and rendered, never re-derived in a surface or by the assistant.
- DEC-9's progressions are a built construct spanning TIME (stage order) and LAW (lawful exceptions discharge a required stage) inside layer 5 (`progressions`), i.e. below Publication — evidence that some process-law reasoning already lives low in the order.
- DEC-9/DEC-10 together define a built TIME+ORGANISATIONS+LAW construct below Publication: a progression (layer 5) states that a body must produce stage B within N days of stage A unless it publishes an exception; the overdue/missing condition is derived on read and notified via the case connection. The interval is authored by the group, not sourced from a statute; no calendar rules (business days, holidays) appear.
- DEC-10's "the due-by was AUTHORED" is the doctrinal licence for any machine-tracked deadline to bind (notify) without a human decision: it binds because a member authored the expectation. A deadline the machine derived from law it read would not obviously fall under this licence.
- DEC-13 makes the right-of-reply window a second authored clock of the progression shape (TIME), addressed to the case's subject body (ORGANISATIONS), modelled on GAGAS audit practice (COURTS/administrative). Authored windows (DEC-10, DEC-13) are a recurring pattern: the group sets the interval, sourced precedent informs it, the machine only tracks it.
- DEC-12 (editions; a citation pins an edition; supersession surfaced not followed) and DEC-14 (sequence vs causation) give a template for as-of reasoning: every derived claim is tied to a fixed version and a date, and change over time is reported as dated sequence, never as inferred cause.
