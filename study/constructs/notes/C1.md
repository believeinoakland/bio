## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **RM (Complete Roadmap v5)** — the mission of record: the sewer-fund origin (Oakland, $52.6M diverted, CPRA 26-3028), four core values, eight operational principles, the fifteen design requirements in summary, the eight-skill AI architecture, the five-phase group workflow, the trust hierarchy (now an origin mark, DEC-92/96/111/112), the seven-category UX, plus Appendices A (Oakland officials and their tenures) and B (California legal framework and precedents). Its banner: §§1–12 are current doctrine, §§13–15 are history. It is Oakland- and California-specific throughout; it is the richest source of concrete TIME/ORG/LAW/COURTS examples among my files.
- **DR (Design Requirements v2)** — the fifteen requirements, "the system fails if any requirement is violated"; governs the Roadmap on conflict. Load-bearing for these constructs: Req 6 (roles not individuals; reproducible methodology metadata), Req 7 (seven-stage escalation with timelines, amended so a member advances a proposed stage), Req 8 (risk tiers; counsel packet with chronology and binding deadlines), Req 12 (AI tools advisory, never gate), Req 15 (location-neutral). Carries DEC-111 on the directory and the DEC-20 bias addendum.
- **FA (Functional Architecture v3)** — the three concurrent functional layers (Information, Analysis, Action), every function a group needs, the eight skills, and where human judgment is required. Most directly relevant document for LAW and ANALYSIS needs: "compare actions to standards", cross-referencing sources (ACFR vs OpenGov; budget vs actuals), the Compliant/Noncompliant/Unclear outputs, and the Government Compliance Analysis and Legal/Policy Lookup skills. Its July 2026 resolution notes describe the retired substrate (Focus, bundles, daemon); its 2026-07-27 "three-layer workflow" is ruled an annotation outside canon (AC §5 row 14).
- **SD (System Design v0.1 DRAFT)** — the level-0 map: purpose and stance, sixteen major constructs with importance, relations, home and a rendered STATE column (BUILT/PARTIAL/ABSENT claims), the dependency narrative, a 14-class diagram, the M0–M10 ladder mapping, runtime shape and doctrine spine. Its state cells are dated (rendered from construct-status.json, which row 16 says is now retired); row 16 (Action) was added per AC §6.
- **AC (Action v0.1, canon 2026-09-30)** — the level-1 home of the Action layer (build layer 9): seven constructs (Standard, Determination, Consequence, Action plan, Action, Filing and communication, Escalation) with home modules, thirteen rules, sixteen reconciled contradictions, canon edits and Bob's rulings. The single most important source in my set for LAW (Standard), TIME (clocks, holidays in profile), ORGANISATIONS (addressees by role and body) and COURTS (venue sets the standard of evidence).
- **MS (MILESTONES)** — the old process's capability ladder M0–M10 (established 2026-07-31; the process is retired per CLAUDE.md, so this is history plus design reasoning). Relevant: M1 (unattended scheduler, ageing of temporal expectations), M4 (ordinance as one query; meeting→agenda→minutes and need→award→contract progressions; missing predecessor as finding), M5 (content search), M9 (per-axis strength, undetermined), M10 (action names its finding; non-response is a finding).
- **UK (UI-KICKOFF)** — Bob's standing UX brief (2026-07-27/28): audiences, constraints that are law (fence, capabilities absent not greyed, server-stamped identity, refusals teach), Bob's UX principles and refinements verbatim, and the state-semantics table architecture. Little on the six constructs; relevant for how change over time, stories/visuals in publications and AI summaries must be presented, and for the historical sequencing "Layer 2 the analysis layer fills in afterward".

## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
1. **Law is needed during investigation, by the canon's own functional map.** FA treats Analysis ("compare government actions against legal and policy standards") as concurrent with Information gathering, not after publication (FA L103-109, L114-115). It puts Legal/Policy Lookup in Layers 1 and 2 (L564-565), and RM §10 makes "compare to legal requirements" part of Investigate (L650-652). FA's Compliant/Noncompliant/Unclear outputs are produced per examined government action before Document (L327-341). AC by contrast defines Standard and Determination in layer 9, with a Determination "resting on published findings" (AC L20-21). AC does let "an inquiry open a plan (suspected)" (L28) and lets a plan rest on unestablished premises (rule 2). [CONFLICT] So the sources differ on when comparison against a standard happens. This bears directly on the brief's structural observation: the canon expects the standards and the comparison to be reachable by investigation and by the assistant (Skills 4, 6), and as specified only the Determination needs publication.
2. **TIME depends on LAW, and LAW on TIME.** Every deadline names its statute, order or commitment (FA L424-428; AC §2, rule 5). A Standard carries its "period in force" (AC L20). Statutes are recodified (RM App B "Recodified 2023 by AB 473"). The CPRA deadline example implies a business-day roll-forward (RM L253-254: 10 days from Thu Mar 19 → "expired March 30", a Monday). AC rule 11 puts "deadlines and holidays" in the jurisdiction profile. So deadline arithmetic needs the law (the basis), the profile (holidays and the counting rule) and the as-of date of the version in force.
3. **An obligation is ORG × LAW × TIME, and no single construct in these documents holds it.** The examples are all of the shape "who owes what to whom, by when, under what authority": the City Administration's own October 2023 deadline for the auditor's recommendations (RM L230-232); the department's report filed "two days late" (FA L311-312); the Controller's 10-day CPRA duty (RM L253). DR §6 makes accountability attach to the role, with predecessor holders still answerable (L149-156), and RM App A shows positions with successive, approximately dated holders. AC addresses actions "to a government office by role and body" (rule 6). The entity axis (SD row 6) has registry, aliases and constitutive relations, but none of my documents describes positions, holders over time, or obligations as data.
4. **Two separate homes for lateness.** Meaning (SD row 6, layer 5) builds progressions with "missing-predecessor and overdue-successor findings" and "temporal connections" (MS M4: meeting→agenda→minutes, need→award→contract, "an award with no solicitation surfaces as a finding"). Action (layer 9) keeps clocks with a basis, where overdue is "derived when read" (AC §5 row 4) and an overdue-scan runs on the alarm (MS M1). Non-response becomes a finding (MS M10; AC `references` edge). Whether these two mechanisms share one time model is not stated.
5. **COURTS has no object of its own.** Courts enter as a kind of Standard ("court decision or order", AC L20), as a venue that sets the evidence standard (AC rule 13), as risk tiers keyed to precedent risk (DR §8), as outcome words on correspondence ("reversed, affirmed", AC §5 row 7), and as sources to discover ("case law, consent decrees", FA L132-133). The mission examples need more: dockets, parties, consent decrees running 23 years, settlements with amounts, grand jury powers, audit opinions, recalls (RM §1, App A/B). [GAP]
6. **ANALYSIS sits between extraction and law.** The canonical analytic needs are cross-source reconciliation (ACFR vs OpenGov; budget narrative vs actual expenditure, FA L271-276), trends over fiscal years (RM §1), and the size of a breach (Consequence "computed from the record", AC L22). The substrate gives cell-level citation (sheet-cell and sheet-range extents, SD row 4/5; "Sheet1!B14", MS M2), CSV dialects and tidy/long JSON (FA L156-163). Doctrine bounds what a derived number may claim: grades never compose across scales (SD §4), there is no score or significance field (AC rule 3; MS M9), "an outcome that costs nothing to produce is not evidence" (SD §1), and methodology must be reproducible (DR §6). UK adds that charts in publications must also carry the narrative in print.
7. **QUESTIONS has two meanings in these documents.** The first is "a member asks a question and gets an inquiry" (MS M9), the structured object. The second is natural-language help, from the eight skills (Context, Legal Lookup, Compliance Analysis, Escalation guidance) and the assistant (SD row 11: FIND/PURSUE/EXTRACT/CHECK; AC adds "compare, compute, propose and draft"). The prompt entry and INTERPRET are ABSENT (SD L121). Skills 7 and 8 are "unbuilt as named skills" (RM L12). SD's class diagram gives the assistant edges only to Retrieval and Capture, none to Action, standards or calculation (SD L237-238), although AC rule 10 says the project's kind of work "shapes what the assistant suggests". So the assistant's reach into law, time and calculation is unspecified here.
8. **The jurisdiction-free product and the Oakland-specific mission.** RM and MS are dense with Oakland and California material: officials, Gov. Code sections, Prop 218, "every document class Oakland publishes", Legistar, OpenGov. DR §15 and AC rule 11 require all of it to live in profile data: offices, venues, legal organisations, deadlines, holidays and kinds, where "a missing fact reads undetermined". The mission examples are therefore the requirements catalogue for what a jurisdiction profile must be able to express: statute hierarchy, venues and fees, counting rules, office rosters with tenures.
9. **The UNDETERMINED / "Unclear" lineage runs through every construct.** FA's "Unclear: insufficient information" (L338-341) became AC's Determination value "unclear" (L21), the risk tier "UNDETERMINED … never defaulted to 1" (SD L111; AC row 10), a missing profile fact that "reads undetermined" (AC rule 11), an ungraded leg that "SUSPENDS its axis" (MS M9), and search that names "WHICH absence is true" (SD row 9).
