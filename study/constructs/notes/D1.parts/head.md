## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **design-journeys.txt**: "Civicsmith Journeys", design phase step 3 of 5, a draft for Bob dated 4 October and revised 5 October (kept at docs/development/ux-substrate/journeys.html). It reconciles the 20 audiences into three rings (members, invited in, outside) and gives eight "wide path" rules. §3 "Where members start" maps 5 problems members live with and 10 "something they read" entry points (code, court case, news, budget, meeting, contract, regulatory proceeding, dataset, tip, another group's case) to a first step and to journeys. It gives **seventeen** journeys with their steps (the task brief says fourteen; the file says seventeen at L11, L124 and J4 L553), outlines 11 wizards (3 required), lists 12 "Gaps to close" in §6 (proposed 5 October, nothing decided), and asks Bob questions J1–J11. It is the best direct evidence of construct needs from the members' side.
- **design-ux-audiences.txt**: a JSON array of the 20 audiences, from group operator to "described audience addressed by an action". Each has whoTheyAre, goals, whatTheyAlreadyKnow, context (device, timePressure, frequency, stakes), trustConcerns and whatTheyMustNeverSee. Every line is marked basis "fixed" (citing requirement ids or DEC/K rulings) or "open". There are no coverage flags. The construct-bearing audiences are the government office, oversight body, named counsel, the AI run (the assistant), the professional member and the participant (who sets reminders).
- **design-ux-useCases.txt**: a JSON array of 172 use cases, UC-001 to UC-172. Each has a functional layer (1 Information 40, 2 Analysis 42, 3 Action 50, Cross-cutting 40), function, audience, goal, trigger, interface "constructs" (standard, determination, entity, progression, action, filing, ai-run, and others), acts, outcome, coveredByRequirements, requirementIds, src and coverageNote. Coverage is yes for 150, partial for 10 and no for 12. 27 use cases are "built at the plane; no member surface yet", mostly in Action.
- **design-ux-journeyExperience.txt**: a JSON array of 11 experience journeys, (a) through (k): setup, question to case, acting on a finding, daily queue, delegating to the assistant, knock, review copy, records request, source change, administrators, suspicion to closed plan. Each step gives the actor, what they know, the decision, what can go wrong (refusal codes), how long, the handoff, the feeling or risk, the src and open points. It is older than useCases on at least one point: it calls the overdue marks "not yet built", while UC-127 says they were built in T18.
