There is also the project's evidence "bar" (MS L65). "Docket" in PR L66 and BR L28 is the case's public response log, not a court docket. "Matter" (BR L100, VM, VP) is the plan-level name for a government act under examination (DEC-114, per BR L236), and "subject" is used for the same thing on the plan page (VP L19–L36).
