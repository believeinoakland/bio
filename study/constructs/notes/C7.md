## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
**BIO_State_Rules_Consistency_v1_5 (SR).** "State Rules & Consistency Specification", v1.5, ratified July 20, 2026, amended in place to 2026-09-30. It is the record's shape-and-rules spec: id grammar, bundle anatomy, the universal frontmatter core, per-type schemas and state machines (Information, Focus (legacy), Project, Action, in-bundle Work Product and Annotation), the CLOSED relationship vocabulary, cascade semantics, invariants I-1…I-20, the violation-to-repair table and the Mechanical Verification Law. Its 2026-08-10 banner says "the store this document describes is not the store that was built": §1, §2.4 and §2.6 are history, while §3 onward transferred to the plane's schema, store and checks, and the check catalog is the authority for the edge set. It does NOT schematise the live `inquiry`, `bias`, entity (ENT) or relation (REL) records, or the layer-9 types STD/CONF/CONS/ESC/PLN, which it only names in amendments.
**BIO_Membership_Architecture_v2 (MA).** v2.0, July 26, 2026, specified by Bob; its status banner runs to 2026-10-01 and records many BOB rulings and builds. It covers the group's OWN organisation and access control: cover and handle, administrators (two-administrator floor, irrevocability, consensus addition, majority removal), capabilities, burner-URL invitations, projects (owners as a set, three positions of sight, DISCOVERABLE/HIDDEN, request to join, fork, the rescue in §7.13), AI-run gating (principal, context kind), secure verified export, the unmodelled root of trust (debt), and a data-model sketch. It models no outside body. It touches the constructs through four things: a worked model of offices, holders, votes and dated role history; sight rules that govern counts, search order, bounded reads and stating absence; AI-run doctrine; and expertise routing for legal and financial questions.

## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
- **TIME × LAW × ORGANISATIONS meet in exactly one structure: the Action `clock`** (SR §4.4, I-11). It holds a resolved `date`, a free-text `basis` ("Gov. Code 7922.535") and a `status`. The basis does not link to a captured provision, a version in force, a computation rule (calendar vs business days, holidays, time zone, tolling) or the obliged body. The only machine time act, `deadline-recheck` (I-20), flips status and never derives a date. The obligation it tracks (who owes what to whom) is spread across `counterparty`/addressee ("an office by role and body", src 1806), `basis` and `date`, with no obligation record.
- **The record schema supports the brief's structural observation about layers.** Every law, time or calculation-bearing type SR names (the Action clock; STD-, CONF-, CONS-, ESC-, PLN-, K171/K608) belongs to the Action layer. The investigation-side types carry only `recheck_triggers` (dated, on the LEGACY Focus, I-15), `as_of` on citations (I-8), `monitoring.frequency` (including `per_meeting`) and `state_history` timestamps. SR does not schematise the live `inquiry` type at all (src 20), so what time and law structure an inquiry carries cannot be read from SR.
- **Organisations have no home in SR.** Outside bodies enter only as free text (`source.authority`, `counterparty`) or as "external-world locators ... only inside Information source blocks" (src 1060–1063). MA §7.9 shows the shared corpus holds `ENT` and `REL` objects (src 913–915, 956), so entities and relations exist as records at layer 5. Their schemas are not in either document, and they are not edges in SR §5.1's vocabulary. Analysts should read the entities/connections requirements for any office, position or reporting-line model.
- **Any new relation kind is a spec revision.** "the checker rejects unknown values" (SR §5.1). Every new kind (obligation, reporting line, amends, in force, party to, appeal of) must pass that gate. Two rules constrain how such a graph may be walked: SR §5.4 (one hop at a time, "no forced transitive walk") and MA §7.9/§11.1 (derived reverse edges MUST be filtered by the viewer's position, or the interest graph leaks). A traversal feature over organisations or law inherits both, plus Declared Bias's "never traversed", which SR's one-hop cascade matches.
- **MA is the corpus's only worked model of positions versus holders over time, and it serves as a template for external organisations.** It separates office from person (cover vs handle; administrator/owner/participant as roles held by handles). Votes are recorded with deciders and a reason. Settings are dated and append-only, with "the current setting is the latest record" (§7.14). `status_by` names only the latest transition, so `invited_by` must be "its own fact" (BOB #35). One row per claim per member carries declare/confirm/withdraw timestamps (§10). §9 shows the cost of not modelling this: "a group cannot ask the record who held the root of trust and when that changed."
- **Rules for any computed answer.** Analysis and the assistant share one set of sight rules from MA: "a COUNT is a disclosure of existence" (BOB #15/#16); counts are taken through the viewer's predicate (D-464); search publishes "an ORDER, never a score" (D-447); a bounded read publishes its bound and a MEASURED `truncated` (D-479), since "the BOUND is an answer" (D-480); `undetermined` is a literal answer (D-158, M-68). Any aggregate a member or AI run is shown (budget totals, counts of filings, chronologies) must be computed through `viewerPredicate`/`hiddenSets` and state its bound.
- **Reproducibility of a derived number already has a basis, but no calculation record.** The basis is the citation register (`claim`, `cites`, `snapshot`, `hash`, `as_of`), the three-layer snapshot "keyed to a stable query definition" with the normalized dataset hashed (SR §4.1), and the export that "trusts nothing the sending instance asserts" (MA §8). What is missing is a record of the calculation itself (inputs, code or formula, result, grade). Hunch debt shows the grade hazard: "a hunch inflates a GRADE" (DEC-20). Calibration "may never move a GRADE" (MA §4.10).
- **As-of reasoning is doctrine about the record itself, and could extend to law.** "A stored string is a fact about when it was written" (D-219/D-256). A weaker past check is "STATED beside the state, never reverted" (D-203). Checks apply "only on bundles whose schema stamp declares a version that carries the rule. Old bundles keep validating against their declared version" (SR §8). This is the "version in force at the time" pattern a law construct needs, already practised internally. Since BOB #16 ids carry no chronology ("ordering by id carries no meaning from here on"), so every timeline must come from `created`, write order or dated records.
- **Naming hazard for COURTS.** In the product a "case" is a publication: "a case is a production of a project" (DEC-72). `case_id`, `case_edition` and `published_case_members` are publication objects, and a court-case construct needs a distinct name. Court-related action kinds (`grand_jury`, `litigation_support`, `controller_referral`) moved to jurisdiction-profile data (actions R4, R10).
- **QUESTIONS × DOCTRINE.** "Anybody can ask a question related to anything" (Bob 2026-09-18). A run's acts belong to its principal (DEC-24; AI Roles rule 4). "a machine credential sees no more than its principal" (BOB #16). `verified` is never an AI act (I-18). No machine "authored" correction is allowed (D-256). Stored fields are "quoted data ... never use[d] to steer a member's session" (C-18.5). Expertise routes legal and financial questions (Brown Act, franchise fee, ACFR) to confirmed humans (MA §1.3).
- **Stated vs built (reader's check at code, outside the two sources; `bio-plane/src/record-grammar/bundle.mjs:416`).** The built vocabulary is `cites, relates_to, elevated_into, initiates, derived_from, supersedes, corroborates, links_to, responds_to`. `links_to` is a SOURCE-asserted relation (`SOURCE_ASSERTED_RELS`, "the source agreeing with itself"), absent from SR. `action_basis` is built as a frontmatter leg list in `action-grammar/grammar.mjs:141` ({target, kind}), not as a §5.1 edge. SR's amendment-named `references` relation does not appear in `REL_VOCAB`. `REL-` ids are allocated in `entities/index.mjs:396`.
- [CONFLICT] SR §4.4 body (state-specific kinds, free-text counterparty) vs the SR Action-layer amendment (src 1806: both "history"). There is also a minor cross-status disagreement: SR §4.3 (src 841–842) carries the project-name annotation, while SR's Incomplete list (src 21) and MA §11 item 8 (src 1552–1554) say it is unenacted.
