## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
- **"Authority" is overloaded, and the ORGANISATIONS construct is where it should land.** In Int §2 (L237-238) and A&T (L45-49, L101-108, L249-256) "authority" is the body that ISSUED a document: a free-text string, "controlled vocabulary revisited when the corpus justifies one", three-valued (`determined`/`undetermined`, dated basis), assigned by a caller and never derived (mechanical determination ABSENT, A&T L9). The examples are all public bodies and their vendors (a city parcel layer in a mapping vendor's viewer; "crediting a city with a vendor's copy", A&T L66, L93; Akamai acting for oaklandca.gov, SA L113-119). An organisation model (bodies, vendors acting for them, offices) is the natural referent for `authority`, and mechanical authority determination (A&T's unbuilt first clause) would need it. Note that legal authority ("under what authority an obligation exists") is a different sense not present in these documents.
- **The record's TIME doctrine is mature for its own acts and absent for dates in the world.** These documents rule precisely on instants and precision (D-543, `stampInstant`), undetermined order within a precision band (D-516, D-573), dated histories never overwritten (group name/domain, source identity DEC-78 item 5), as-of-signing statements (Pub rule 18), version pinning ("a reference is pinned to the version it was made against", Bob's 00:40Z doctrine; `case_citations[].version`), editions with "what changed" (DEC-101), a per-case docket chronology (DEC-100/116), rolling windows (activity level over 13 weeks; knock limits by sliding window), monitoring thresholds (3 failures/14 days) and cadence of standing intent. Nothing here models deadlines, effective/in-force dates, fiscal periods, meeting calendars or notice rules — those appear only as raw material (FY2025-27 budget, ACFR, council agendas, "CPRA request and nonresponse record"). The same version doctrine (pin to the version as it stood) is what a law-in-force or office-holder-over-time model would need, so it is reusable.
- **LAW and COURTS enter as venues, audiences and risk, with jurisdiction pushed to profile data.** DEC-105 (Pub L730-736) places researched audience standards as "a sourced fact in the jurisdiction profile", on "the form already ruled for legal venues (K597 (3), K600 (b))", undetermined where unresearched and never a default; DEC-81/Action §4 rule 13 says "the venue sets the standard of evidence" and Grade A is only a ceiling (Int L6, L344-348). This ties the ANALYSIS of grades (capture grade, strength pair, the bar) to COURTS (what a venue requires). But the doctrine documents themselves still carry California law: Cal. Gov. Code 54953.5 (Int L428-430), and CP's tiers (CPRA, Brown Act, Prop 218, CCP §526a). If the product is jurisdiction-free, these belong in a profile, as Pub §8 already implies by keeping only the tier words and `risk_tier` (half-built, D-182).
- **The precedent rationale (CP L288-324; Pub §8 L753) is a COURTS reason for an ACTION-layer rule:** Tier 3 actions get no filing templates because "a loss on the merits could create adverse precedent binding on future litigants", and the published classification is meant as evidence in a later court's preclusion analysis. Supporting this needs knowledge of which remedies exist in a jurisdiction, which venue hears them, and whether errors are with or without prejudice — court/venue facts that, per DEC-105, would be profile data.
- **ANALYSIS: the reproducibility bar for any derived number is already set by publication.** DEC-112 (Pub §5C L399-408) requires the grading method's version inside the signed case document, a grade "recomputed by hand", an open spec and standalone checker, and import that recomputes each grade by the stated method version (Recreated / in part / Did not recreate). Rule 17 (D-470) versions the check catalogue; rule 16 verifies renderings by pixels. Any calculation, dataset filter or chart a case relies on would have to meet the same bar (method versioned, inputs whole and fingerprinted, recomputable outside Civicsmith). The founding evidence is financial (sewer-fund transfer series across auditor report, fund statements, budget, OpenGov data — Int L166-171, CP L348-350; ACFR and budget book in SA), yet Int's only analysis constructs are the "fact, analysis, or judgment" classification and "the normalized dataset with its content hash"; "extraction and normalization standards per document type" are not yet forced (Int L892-893), and "Accuracy and credence are separate questions the catalog does not yet model" (Int L558-559).
- **QUESTIONS: the assistant's permitted role in these documents is drafting and suggesting, always labelled, never deciding:** drafting "what changed" (DEC-101, record keeps it began as a machine draft), suggesting outside responses "under the six guards" (DEC-95 (3)), resolving undetermined authority as "a human or an AI" (A&T L84-87), flagging redaction candidates, proposing gathering requests, the gatekeeper labelled machine work. Constraints: collected material is untrusted data never instructions (Int L614-619; A&T L171-182); the review surface shows the source "never only an AI summary" (Int L409-412); release and actions are member acts (Int §4a, §9). The routing of a question to the best-placed member (`member_expertise`, A&T L184-188) exists as data but there is no notification channel (D-52).
- **ORGANISATIONS outside the group are needed by publication features that are ruled but not built:** the case subject has "party-like standing" and its responses and public statements (press releases) are tracked on the docket (DEC-100, Pub L415-419); "working on" notices may name "the public body and the matter" (DEC-111) and the network directory files them "under the public body examined and shown on every place page that body touches" (Pub L390); standing watches over "a city's press page, council agendas" (Pub L421); the Legistar chain (Int L705-707). Each presupposes a stable identification of public bodies; Pub's only modelled organisation identity is the GROUP's own (slug, display name, verified domain — whose verification exists because "a group could claim a city's domain", Pub L663-664).
- **Doctrine relevant to every construct, stated in these documents:** undetermined is first-class and stated, never back-filled or written as "nobody" (Pub rule 13; A&T L97-99; Int §8 D-533); the record may claim neither more nor less than it holds (Pub L247-248, L593-595); no gate may pressure a member into inventing a value or attribution (Pub rule 14, §6A.4; A&T L258-265); exact match only for attribution, "a fuzzy match would invent an attribution" (Pub rule 15(d)); a fact is "recorded because it is true and dated, not because it is explained", and a public body's intent is not inferred (SA L136-138); bad actors are "identified by evidence rather than the default assumed by role" (SA L233-235); the machine never releases, files or concludes (Int §4a, §9; A&T L280-281).
- **Layer placement signal:** the documents put authority determination at intake (layer 3: capture/provenance) and venue/audience standards in the jurisdiction profile consulted at publication (layer 8) and action (layer 9, `risk_tier`). Nothing in these five documents requires law, courts or analysis to be available before publication, except: authority determination at capture (needs ORG), standing watches of public-body sources (needs ORG and TIME), and the venue-set standard of evidence, which a project's bar (set during investigation, Pub rule 8, DEC-105) would want to consult before publication.
