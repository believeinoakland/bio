## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **affordances** (layer 11): the plane-sourced act pre-flight (DEC-8). For one object and one caller it says which acts exist, their capability, mode, rung on the correction ladder, prompt and consequence statement; with no target it publishes the catalogue, every vocabulary (entity/relation kinds, law levels, correspondence stages, norm canons…), the rung ladder and set acts. Pure/read-only; refuses nothing itself. It is also the source of the AI's "pack" (control-plane R41).
- **op-declarations** (layer 11): the frozen tables that say what each of the instance's 446 ops is: classes, mutating, stamps, which session reaches it, capability needed (`OPS`, `SESSION_OPS`, `NEEDS`, `UNATTENDED_BY_DECISION`, act lists). No `ai` row: an AI credential is admitted by task scope only.
- **wizard-scripts** (layer 11, first): library of authored step lists a wizard walks a member through on real screens (DEC-120/121): Civicsmith library (Bob-approved, read-only) and group libraries governed like filing templates. Checks every script; serves scripts per screen with no AI and no key; unattributed daily tallies. Built T31, but screen registry and library are empty.
- **tasks** (layer 11): the "obligation inbox": internal work items routed from captures whose authority is undetermined (one kind only: `authority-undetermined`), listed, forwarded, resolved. "Obligation" here is the group's own to-do, not a public body's duty.
- **queue** (layer 11): the member's one feed: items classed To do / Noticed / Signal (OBLIGATION / FINDING / CONDITION), each filed under every case it belongs to, with its acts; mute/snooze personal, dispose a record act. Holds the catalogue of ~70 item kinds, many of them time-driven (checkpoint due, reminder, overdue clock, fact due, notice lapse).
- **control-plane** (layer 11): the two doors (Worker HTTP entry, store dispatch): routes each op to its module, stamps identity from the credential, one envelope, store-silence rule, purge gates (incl. litigation hold), renders the AI pack into `op=affordances`.
- **publication** (layer 8): the published record. A "case" is the group's own production over its findings (not a court case); editions signed, content-addressed, one-way. Holds case documents, the published projection, attribution levels (as-of an edition), source-consent re-read at commit, the read `conformance` uses.
- **corpus-export** (layer 8): verified export of the whole working corpus and an append-only export log; verifying import that trusts nothing the manifest asserts. Exit/portability, not dataset export.

## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
- The interface layer already carries time-driven and law-driven work (queue kinds action-clock-overdue, action-reminder, local-fact-due, plan-checkpoint-due, escalation-stage-proposed, litigation-hold) whose producers sit in layer 9 (action-clocks, local-facts, action-plans, escalation, actions). Every one is about the group's own action after publication; the queue has no kind for a public body's duty or a statutory deadline arising during investigation.
- TIME support as built is a member-typed calendar date plus a UTC "today" comparison (queue `due`, actions' overdue). Holidays and office hours exist only as unconfirmed profile facts with their own To-do; nothing computes a deadline, a business day or a time zone.
- ORGANISATIONS and LAW meet in `entity_kinds` (ordinance, contract, body, office as registry entities) but `relation_kinds` has three values and no relation links an entity to a standard, an office to a holder over time, or a body to an obligation. Laws on an action are free-text `{level, citation}` rows, not links to `standards`.
- COURTS appear only as correspondence stages and a litigation hold; "case" and "docket" in this codebase mean the group's own publication. An analyst must not count docket/case-import as court support.
- The pre-flight (affordances) only offers acts on information, inquiry, action and project objects; standards, determinations, consequences, entities and plans get rungs and NON_ACTS sentences but no per-object offer. A new interface built "from the plane's publication" (DEC-8) would still need hard-wired knowledge of where layer-9 acts live.
- Reach gap: the layer-9 law, analysis and time modules (standards, conformance, consequences, action-clocks, local-facts, escalation, action-plans, filings, counsel packet, litigation hold) are built and member-reachable on the plane, but the only member interface (legacy UI, frozen as Bob's UX, K633) calls none of them. The AI (agent-worker R37) reaches none of them either. Their only visible trace is a queue To-do whose door the UI cannot open: the UI offers "Mark resolved" on every To-do instead.
- QUESTIONS: no member-facing op accepts a natural-language question; the browser cannot open an AI run; wizards (the no-AI guide) are built but have no screens or scripts. Assistant answers therefore cannot today draw on law, time, organisation or analysis services.
- Layer order shows in the reads: conformance's determination rests on `publication.publishedEditionsOf` (ratified editions), which agrees with the brief's observation that law/standards serve only after publication.
