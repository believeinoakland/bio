## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **local-facts.** Approved by Bob on 2026-10-01 (K921); first in layer 9.
  - Members confirm, correct or dispute a jurisdiction profile's holidays, office hours and time zone, and each confirmation lapses at a horizon.
  - The module answers which value governs on this instance and lists the facts due for re-check.
  - Only a member acts. It keeps one append-only table.
- **standards.** Approved on 2026-09-26 (K102).
  - It holds the law and commitments a government act is measured against, as `STD-` record objects.
  - Each standard has a citation, one of six kinds, an issuer, a captured text, a period in force, a profile-source match (or undetermined) and a supersession chain.
  - It answers in force / not in force / undetermined at a date.
  - A machine proposes; a member declares or adopts, giving the member's own reason.
- **conformance.** Approved on 2026-09-26 (K102).
  - It records the group's determination (`CONF-`) that a named government act (`ACT-`, done by an office) is compliant, noncompliant or unclear against named standards.
  - A determination rests on published findings only, gives one outcome per standard (never composed) and may carry an evidenced cause.
  - The machine only proposes a comparison, which may start from a contradiction inquiry.
- **consequences.** Approved on 2026-09-26 (K102, K12).
  - It records what a breach did and to whom: the affected class, fund or office; a measure (money, benefits, services, time, count); and a period.
  - Each part is computed (the module's own arithmetic over cited figures, graded by the weakest link), assessed by a member, or undetermined.
  - Causation is either established or unproven.
  - It also records whether each part is addressed, which is what escalation's exit reads.
- **action-grammar.** Drafted 2026-10-01 and met in T19 (K835).
  - A pure library split from `actions` for size.
  - It holds the action vocabularies and the grammars for records law, quotes, the records-request lifecycle, request-for-comment basis and the audit arm.
  - It also holds the catalogue rows that `actions` mints. It reads no record.
- **actions.** Approved on 2026-09-26 (K102).
  - It is the Action object: what the group sends or does outside, to whom, why, under which governing laws, at what risk tier, by when, and what came back.
  - The addressee is never a private individual. The clock gives every entry its basis. What came back is held as a ledger, quotes and a lifecycle.
  - It also holds pressure marks, litigation holds (DEC-108, DEC-113) and the action's own outcome.
  - It is about 2,900 lines after two splits.
- **action-clocks.** Split from actions (K617).
  - It reads deadlines across actions: which are pending and which are overdue.
  - It proposes clock entries from profile deadline rules, in calendar or business days, stating whether the local calendar is confirmed.
  - It holds members' own reminders (DEC-94) and lists the calendar facts that live deadlines read.
- **filing-templates.** Approved on 2026-10-01 (K921, K924).
  - The group's governed, versioned library of filing and briefing templates, whose blanks come from a closed set.
  - Reviews come from members or from professionals with revocable grants, and approval rules depend on the tier.
  - Profile templates are read-only. A machine only proposes wording and comments.
- **filings.** Approved on 2026-09-26 (K102, K13). It prepares what the group sends, from the record.
  - Tier 1 and 2 filings come from a template or the member's own words. Each blank is filled from a named record source or marked UNFILLED.
  - At any tier it assembles a counsel packet: facts, chronology, exhibits, standards, candidate theories, deadlines and an optional briefing.
  - It also prepares communications and the available-actions block.
  - A member approves and records the sending. The instance transmits nothing.
- **escalation.** Approved on 2026-09-26 (K102, K14).
  - It runs the 7-stage protocol from a live noncompliant determination.
  - Triggers are derived from the record, and a member advances or declines each with a reason.
  - It ends only when compliance is restored and consequences are addressed.
  - It also supports declining to escalate, a status read (DEC-89) and a machine-labelled draft of the opening reason.
- **action-plans.** Reviewed by Bob on 2026-09-30 (K608(3)); last in layer 9.
  - A project's plan (`PLN-`) over suspected matters (an inquiry) or determined ones (an outcome).
  - It holds options bound to matters, their dispositions, up to 3 scenarios with relative checkpoints, and starts that turn an option into an action, with a preview.
  - The planning run (K660) proposes options five at a time, with no score.
  - A plan is never published and holds no costs or assignees.

## Cross-construct observations
- **Placement (verified).** Law (standards), calculation (consequences), the local calendar (local-facts) and all deadline arithmetic (action-clocks) sit in layer 9.

  - Mode `plan`, the agent-worker's one reader and writer of layer 9, is `deployed: false`.
  - The proposal acts the canon assigns to AI skills have no AI caller: standards R9 (Legal/Policy Lookup), conformance R12 (Government Compliance Analysis), filings R14 (theories) and R23 (communications), and filing-templates R6.
- **Organisations run through law, time and courts.** The office `{role, body}` plays many roles, and each is a text pair, optionally beside profile `counterparties` or an `entity_id`:
  - the actor of an act (conformance);
  - the addressee (actions R9);
  - an affected party (consequences);
  - the key for holidays and hours (local-facts R6, action-clocks R10);
  - the holder of the `elected` and `oversight` flags (escalation R12);
  - the target of available actions (filings R15).

  A richer organisation model would be read by six of these eleven modules at once.
- **Law and time are disconnected.** A deadline's legal basis is always a citation string, never a held standard. The strings are profile `deadlines` (jurisdictions R26), `clock[].basis`, `due_cite` and an action-plans regulated-date `basis`.
  - The only time construct that uses standards is a standard's own in-force period.
  - So "every deadline names the statute, order or commitment it comes from" is met by a string, not by a link to the law's text or the version in force.
- **Time handling is UTC and date-only throughout**, as the requirements state (actions R12; action-clocks Terms).
  - The profile time zone and office hours are researched, confirmed and lapsed (local-facts), but never applied.
  - Business days use a fixed Saturday–Sunday weekend.
- **Analysis is bound to a breach.** The only arithmetic service (consequences R2) runs after a noncompliant determination, over figures cited verbatim in passages, because the record holds no amounts as values (T32 A37). Spreadsheet cells are only operands, not a working medium.
- **Doctrine is uniform and enforced by refusal codes:** acts are members' only; machine proposals are labelled and stored apart; undetermined is never defaulted; nothing is composed; nothing is scored; what a viewer may not see is withheld whole; records are append-only. Moving any of these services to a lower layer would carry its refusals unchanged. The assistant would then be reading member-declared standards and determinations, not making them.
