## What these documents are (3–6 lines each)
- **MKD** (v0.1 design by BOB #14, 2026-09-14, amended to 2026-10-01). Covers the member's own firsthand OBSERVATION as evidence: an authored bundle, graded `testimony: D` on a third grade axis. Also covers the LEAD (a tip, never evidence; the authored half of the frontier at the `internet` level), attribution in a published case (group · project · cover · name, chosen by the observer per case edition), and an OPINION as a case element that is never a basis leg. Status: §2, §3, §4 and §5 are BUILT (MK-1, MK-2, MK-4, MK-6, MK-7); §6 (MK-5) is unbuilt. DEC-102 (Bob, 2026-10-01) makes identity level set testimony's weight. Its bearing on the six constructs is mostly DOCTRINE and QUESTIONS (disclosure, counts, absence).
- **EBD** (v0.1 draft by BOB #11, 2026-09-14, amended to 2026-09-25; not reviewed by Bob). Covers extraction breadth: a census-ordered list of content-type readers (minutes, staff report, ordinance/resolution, staff directory, budget/dataset, financial report), tables and images as content (`sheet-range`, `doc-table`, `image`), the extraction half of the AI EXTRACT role (`ai(function, version)` step, SK-8 BUILT), opt-in read-time re-extraction to OCR tier 3, the per-page tier-2 rule, and external OCR (DEC-74, not funded). It carries BOB #32's ruling that a budget is a plan and an audited statement reports actuals.
- **DP** (design of record, 2026-07-30, built as `docprofile/`). Covers recognising a document's kind on two axes, stack and content type: three region kinds, three digests, five verdicts, fidelity levels, and change recognised in six layers with a `trail`. Monitoring is a different contract per kind (substance / membership / unmonitorable), and the meeting calendar's date-window reasoning lives here. REFERENTIAL and TEMPORAL connections are kept distinct, including "an absence with a due date". This is the richest of the six on TIME and on meeting bodies.
- **OF** (research 2026-07-31, now the plan of record; BUILT end to end by COFF-1..10 and FW-23). Covers the FORMAT axis: an OOXML/ODF container reader; DOCX/XLSX/PPTX/ODT/ODS/ODP entries; a CSV entry (2026-09-24); and the DEC-5 evidentiary envelope (formulas beside cached values, tracked changes, comments, speaker notes, hidden sheets, core properties). It records what is NOT extracted (tables and charts as content, embedded file content, legacy `.xls`, very large workbooks) and the unsettled CSV bound and text decode (D-593).
- **CSD** (v0.1 draft by BOB #11, 2026-09-14, heavily amended to 2026-09-25; items 1–2 and 4–6 BUILT). Covers content-grain search: the `capture_text` index with one unit per element reference; the `content:` and `passage:` arms with `rows=content`, `rows=passage` and `rows=leg`; bounds and truncation stated; and an answer envelope that names its level and a five-bucket content-axis tally. It records the UI-62 rules for showing a bounded figure. This is the main source here on how search states absence and coverage.
- **SCH** (decision record, REC-1, 2026-07-31; superseded in place 2026-09-30 by the `scheduler` module). Covers the plane's periodic work: one reconciling Durable Object alarm with registered consumers `{name, due, wake, tick}`, earliest-wake, idle self-termination, no cron. Consumers it describes include calibration re-probe (30 days, chosen not measured) and bias-debt. It is the clock substrate any TIME consumer joins.

## Cross-construct observations (connections between constructs these documents make or imply)
1. **TIME, ORGS and LAW already meet in a layer-1 library, not in layer 9.**
   - `docprofile/` (layer 1 Foundations) defines content types that `parse` "entities with stable keys and named facts" and emit typed connections. The connections are referential (meeting `held_by` body; at code, "staff member sits in that department", "agenda item cites that ordinance") and temporal with `expected_by` (absence with a due date). Their thresholds come from jurisdiction-profile data (`practice.minutes_due_days`).
   - This is a working, jurisdiction-free pattern for time-aware facts about bodies and documents. It sits below every layer, so it partly answers the brief's structural observation: some TIME handling is not confined to layer 9.
   - But the plane's monitor keeps only the COUNT of connections (monitoring/index.mjs:535). The per-kind monitoring contract is not reached (DP L13), and the contemporaneity bracket cannot fire (DP L11). The facts are produced and mostly dropped. Whether `progressions` (layer 5) consumes them is not in these documents.
2. **Two kinds of time rule are visible and must stay distinct.**
   - A practice expectation (minutes due ~21 days, "UNMEASURED") raises a question and "never" asserts a violation (DP L332–334).
   - A legal deadline (the brief's layer 9: "every deadline names the statute…") can ground a breach.
   - Any richer TIME construct needs both, each labelled with its basis: measured practice, ruled law, or undetermined.
3. **UTC is used throughout, and the jurisdiction's zone is unused where checked.**
   - `observed_at` is a date or UTC instant (MKD L77). The calendar parser reads M/D/YYYY into UTC. `deadline-recheck` marks entries overdue on the UTC day (monitoring/index.mjs:2746, 2783).
   - Yet the profile carries `time_zone: America/Los_Angeles` (oakland-alameda.mjs:184).
   - Nothing in these six files covers business days, holidays or local-day boundaries. A deadline falling on a local evening could flip a day early or late. This is inferred from the code, not stated in the sources.
4. **ANALYSIS is supported as citation of figures, not as calculation.**
   - The record can hold and cite a cell (`Sheet1!B14`), a range (`sheet-range`), a formula beside its cached value, hidden sheets, and CSV cells (with gaps: D-593, the size bound, `.xls` unread, the 20 MiB office bound excluding police Stop-Data workbooks).
   - OF L117–122 names the need: "the DERIVATION is frequently the finding".
   - None of the six documents describes computing anything: no totals, no budget-versus-actuals, no re-running a formula. BOB #32's plan/actuals ruling creates the TYPE distinction (budget versus financial report), but neither type has a reader. PDF tables are NO-GO and scanned ACFRs are invisible. Workbooks are not indexed per sheet (CSD L51, possibly stale after FW-19).
5. **Doctrine on derived numbers already exists and would bind any analysis feature.**
   - A count discloses existence, so a count must subtract hidden projects and leads (MKD §5).
   - One name, one quantity; and a same-named field counting different things is a false comparison (EBD §5.2).
   - No proportions over a sample, the denominator named in the heading, SAMPLE stated (CSD UI-62).
   - Give a range when the inputs disagree (CSD D-391); one statement can obey a rule another ignores (CSD REC-115).
   - Charts or percentages in publications would need to inherit these.
6. **QUESTIONS: the assistant's retrieval substrate is disciplined, but it is text- and row-grained.**
   - FIND uses `passage:` as one of the four levels (CSD §6). Answers carry level, scope and a five-bucket tally. Hidden and absent answer identically. A hit is an address, never a minted row (DEC-24). EXTRACT proposes entities and facts graded ≤ B, labelled, never attested.
   - Nothing here gives the assistant a structured way to answer a TIME question ("is this late?"), an ORGS question ("who is responsible?") or a LAW question ("what does this section require?") beyond text match and typed connections.
   - The query language (`content:`, `leg:`, `rows=leg`) is structured, not natural language, and has known ambiguity hazards (MKD L14; CSD `leg:grade>=B` defect).
7. **ORGANISATIONS knowledge is document-borne and weakly typed.**
   - Bodies appear as `body:<name>` from calendars, with `renamed` notices. Staff appear in directories, recognised only where emails share one domain. Org charts and committee rosters fall to `generic` (DP L16–17). Staff names also appear in office metadata (`lastModifiedBy`, tracked-change authors), whose surfacing sits in the D-77/invariant-7 neighbourhood (OF L157–160).
   - No position-versus-holder model, reporting line or responsibility appears in these six files.
   - Terminology hazard: "OBLIGATION" in NOTIFICATIONS (SCH L175) means a member's queue item, not a body's legal obligation.
8. **LAW: there is a recogniser, not a model.**
   - The `regulation` content type is registered (DP L14), and the census ranks ordinances and resolutions third. Monitoring can notice a `LegislationDetail` page or an item's status changing (DP L184, L208).
   - Self-naming-as-rate stops "a reference read as membership": a document citing an ordinance is not one.
   - Nothing covers structure, definitions, cross-references, amendments, versions in force or hierarchy. As-of reasoning over text is limited by design: the index holds only the current chain (CSD L181), and content rows and bytes are the versioned truth.
9. **COURTS are absent from all six documents.** The SCH/DEC-35/D-115 rule ("never a vendor key and never a second account"; Free tier) constrains any court-docket or legal-database integration a COURTS or LAW architecture might propose. That is an inference, to be weighed against SOURCE-ACCESS and other readers' files.
10. **The scheduler is not the bottleneck for TIME.** The reconciling alarm already hosts `deadline-recheck`, `overdue-scan`, `notice-sweep`, `intent-age`, `calibration-reprobe` and `bias-debt`, and a new clock joins by appending a consumer (SCH L115–119). It sits in layer 10 and is fed by modules in their total order. What is missing is the time SEMANTICS (business days, holidays, local zone, fiscal periods), not the trigger.
11. **Grades age over time.**
   - A fidelity letter is "a MEASUREMENT of a named engine AT A DATE" (SCH L131–134), re-probed on a chosen 30-day cadence.
   - Testimony weight changes when identity changes, with re-evaluation notices (DEC-102).
   - An AI run's bias lens can "move" (bias-debt).
   - So TIME also governs the grade of every derived thing, which ties TIME to ANALYSIS's "grade of a derived number".
