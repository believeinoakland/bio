### Errors

1. ✗ **Line grading (§5 "Line") misapplies CF §8.1, and as written almost no line would ever be established.**
   - CF §8.1 (src 727–745) grades how a connection between two captured ends was established. A means "the SOURCE's own identifier, with both ends captured and hashed". C means "correspondence rather than identity: a name, a title". connections R34 says "a `C` at either end is never established".
   - The relations a group most needs come from one document naming two bodies in words. A charter says "the City Auditor reports to the Council"; an org chart draws a box under Finance; a franchise names the hauler. Under the study's scale ("C name correspondence"), every such line is grade C. It is therefore never established, and a chain walk whose weakest hop governs answers nothing established.
   - The study conflates two different things: **who asserts the relation** (a captured source states it, a system rule, or a member's testimony) and **how each end was resolved to a registry entity** (entities R9's A/B/C).
   - **Correction:** a line carries (a) its assertion, with the source passage cited and `asserted_by` as in CF §8 src 803–806, and (b) each end's resolution grade.
