**Can a consumer Claude subscription lawfully serve it? No.** "Lawfully" here means under Anthropic's terms, which are a contract, not a statute.
- **The legal and compliance page** says (https://code.claude.com/docs/en/legal-and-compliance):

- **The same page allows** "configuring an API key in a … secrets manager … for use by the customer's own authorized users", provided the usage is billed to the key owner. That is the group's lawful path.
- **The Consumer Terms** (effective 8 October 2025) bar automated access "Except when you are accessing our Services via an Anthropic API Key or where we otherwise explicitly permit it". They also say one "may not make your Account available to anyone else", and the instance level is one account serving the whole group (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms).
- **The canon's basis no longer holds.**
  - The "Claude subscription" idea comes from TAD v10, §"Provider abstraction and onboarding" (l.670–675): "log into a Claude subscription … Raw API keys are advanced-only".
  - It also comes from TAD's three execution modes, "As of the June 15, 2026 Anthropic billing change" (l.597–617), with a separate monthly Agent SDK credit.
  - Anthropic paused that change on 15 June: "nothing has changed … The previously announced monthly credit … isn't available" (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15036540-use-the-claude-agent-sdk-with-your-claude-plan).

## Verdict

**ANALYSIS: sound, with the corrections listed.** The three that matter most:
1. The engine is a candidate pending stage 2's measure. Carrying it beside the checker amends case-checker R13. The R13 change and the consequences R2 change go to Bob in §8.
2. Visibility and personal data: state how D155 and the withholding rules (DEC-36, DEC-85) apply to table inputs. Before workbooks and tables travel "whole", rule on the person-level rows they carry (D176; D-77, invariant 7).
3. Make the grade rule consistent: recipe arithmetic is not a step; a third-party engine's workbook values are a measured derivation step (D92). Label a `compare` result as a computed fact, never a determination (D275). Store and key results rather than recompute 20 MiB tables on every read.

**QUESTIONS: sound, with the corrections listed.** The three that matter most:
1. **The credential is a fact, BOB's to state:** an Anthropic Console API key, paid per use. No consumer, Team or Enterprise subscription can lawfully serve it, under the legal-and-compliance page, the Consumer Terms, and the paused Agent SDK credit. 2. **Correct and contain the cost:**

   - Add a real spend ceiling. Today the only bound is a 1 GB byte bound.
