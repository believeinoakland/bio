## H21 · 2026-10-11 · Actions D18 ruled: the breach override narrowed to stated grounds

**Carries:** Bob, 2026-10-11, typed in BOB #155's session and relayed verbatim (K2752): "D18: as recommended" (b) (page §7 D18). **Requirements must say:** the open override of Action rule 2 (an act asserting a breach without a determination) is available only on one of two stated grounds, each with the member's reason: (1) a **legal deadline** shorter than the time to determine, cited with its rule and date (e.g. Gov.
