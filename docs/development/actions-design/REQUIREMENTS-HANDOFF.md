## project-roster / membership (L2)

- A **counsel matter** is a project; its members alone see its correspondence and dates; never published or used as evidence (rule 15; D4).
- A member's **source role** stays in her hidden project; the advocacy text and its evidence check live in her own hidden project (rules 9, 15; D4, D11a).
- Project setting, by owners: acts (by kind, tier, or "public") that need a **second member's approval** before sending; default one (rule 20; D27 B).
- On a member leaving a group or project: owners told once which dated items route to her (rule 18; D14) — producer in queue-producers.

## standards (L5)

- A spoken commitment becomes a standard only once confirmed in writing or stated publicly; members' "as heard" accounts stay narrative; silence after the group's confirming letter is a dated fact (§3 Standard; D7).

## duties (L5)

- Duties proposed from a report's required responses and a reply's promises, adopted by a member with dates and quotes (rule 16; COURTS L3).
- The group's own deadlines to a venue are **not** body duties (`duties` R23 kept); their home is action-clocks (rule 6; D15).

## publication, docket, case-authoring (L8)

- **Checked and held** (D23): a case may pass every publication check and be held unsigned with no limit; any change to the evidence the check covered voids it until re-run; shown as a state.
- **Signing view**: one fact line per open request for comment (whom, when asked, window close, what the reply answers), once, no reason, no gate; via a registration seam from `actions` (rule 8; D13; H17).
- **Docket**: dated outcome entries (cure, harm addressed) and relevant replies written by a member's act through the docket's op (rules 16, 17; D5, D20); a `legal` pressure mark readable by `actions` and `queue-producers` (rule 12; D10; S1 §D 10).
- **Published record of actions** (registered by `actions`): outward acts only; never deliberation, confidential acts or counsel matters; escalations with state incl. "stopped pursuing" and date (rules 15, 17; D4, D6a, D22).
- **Advocacy evidence check**: the case-account check run over a member's own text in her hidden project; supported / not supported / contradicted per claim and position; nothing stored outside her project, no mark (rule 9; D11a).
- The success record may be carried into the next edition as an outcome by a member's act (layer 9 calling layer 8) (§3; D6c).

## conformance (L9)

- R2: a determination may rest on finding-versions that are published, members of a **checked-and-held** edition (pinning the check), or **accepted foreign findings** (read-only, never stronger than the source edition) (§3; D16, D16a, D24).
- Always made by a member of the group, against a standard in its own record; another group's determination never adopted (D16a).
- Voiding: on a voided check, or source withdrawal/revision of the accepted edition, the determination reads undetermined until re-evaluated; dependent actions marked (machine mark, rule 1) (§3).
- On publication of a held edition, re-pin to the published edition (D24).
- A curing act: a later act of the same office determined compliant against the same standard, linked by a member as the cure (`cures`) (rule 17; D5).

## consequences (L9)

- Harm addressed: a member's reasoned statement with evidence per part, optional words (remedied, mitigated, compensated, acknowledged, cannot be undone), no percentage (rule 17; D5).
- **Success record** (new construct here): per matter, statement of what changed and when, evidence, optional figures computed from cited sources; sequence may be stated; causation only on evidence other than the group's own act; consequences stay breach-only (§3; D6c).

## actions (L9)

- **Kinds and shapes** (DESIGN §3): records request (product; v0.2 §5 incl. reconsideration as a stage, mediation/ethics/petition as linked actions; stages per law from the profile), request for comment (product; quoting rule stated, claim-by-claim reply), complaint/referral, demand to cure (Tier 2), public comment/testimony, party participation (Tier 2, never says whether to become a party), press, meeting with officials, hosted meeting/awareness, recognition, joint/sign-on, counsel briefing/defence (rules 5, 8, 13; D3, D12, D15).
- **Addressing**: office by role from the registry; one action per office, others copied; delivery point on the venue; reporter by role and outlet with the name kept as the role's holder inside, never published; partners as organisations by role (field) (rule 7; D12, D16).
- **The gate** (rule 2; D1, D18, D3): text classed by what it does; assertion sentences pointed out from a per-language phrasing list (profile-extendable) plus AI where enabled, each resolved by the member (rewrite / confirm-asks, recorded / treat as claim); without a determination, only the two grounds (cited legal deadline with date; imminent irreversible act, what and when) plus reason, disclosed on everything; a groundless override refused naming both grounds; window shown beside the refusal with the quick route (one-finding case); an action sent on grounds may join its escalation once determined, override in history.
- **Disclosure lines**: "rests on findings checked for publication on <date>, not yet published"; "rests on findings of <group> (edition of <date>), accepted by us on <date>" with that group's identity and stake (rule 3; D24, D16a, D28d).
- **Receipt** as a recorded fact (how, when); "no reply owed" state; outcomes in the fixed vocabulary with the office's own wording quoted; response found elsewhere linked by a member; claim-by-claim bearing on replies to requests for comment (rules 6, 16; S1 §C 7–11).
- **Scopes**: `confidential` field set and removed by an act with a reason, never retroactively; excluded at the producer of the published record of actions (R66) (rule 15; D4).
- **Promises to people** as notes on the action every participant sees (rule 15; D4).
- **Press terms** note; review copy with embargo and release time (rule 8; D12).
- **Holds**: any member who can see the action or case places one with a reason (also from a docket `legal` mark or a counsel matter); hold notice prepared and sent by a member's own hand; no per-member read or compliance record (rule 12; D10).
- **Pressure** recorded wherever it arrives, without an action to the sender; lawful demands complied with openly (rule 12; S1 §C 16–17).
- **"arose_from"**: a new matter from a response, same or linked project, linked both ways (rule 16; D20).
- **Misuse guards** (rule 21; D28): open items with the same office shown at preparation as facts; asks for the group's own benefit never prepared; office and responsible official named, junior staff only where the evidence turns on their act, pointed out; the group's public identity and stake on everything sent (stamp fields).
- **Recognition** may rest on a compliant determination or a success record; a fix only promised is shown; staff named in role with a one-time reminder, no consent (rule 5; D6c, D12).
- **Several speakers** on one "for the group" public comment as several sendings with member links visible only inside (rule 8; D12).
- Affordances derive every act an action offers (filing, escalation, hold, plan, reminder) (S1 §D 12).

## action-clocks (L9)

- Five basis kinds: rule, commitment, dependency, the group's window, the other side's stated deadline (never presented as legal) (rule 6; K1431).
- Local-day computation incl. time of day and counts from service; receipt as clock start where the law counts from it (rule 6).
- **One date, one reminder**: de-duplicate a step's "by when" (reads `steps`, new edge) with the action's entry; an adopted duty's "Noticed" replaces the overdue item (rule 6).
- **Our deadlines**: the group's obligations to a venue as dated items owed by the group; reminders only as asked; a miss notifies once (rule 6; D15).
- Reminders as v0.1 rule 5 (member's own request, defaults shown, response offers another or none, overdue once, no outside channel) (rule 6).

## filings, filing-templates (L9)

- R8: a counsel packet needs no determination or override; shows each matter's support as it stands; **defence purpose** (challenged statements with findings and sources, pressure received, defence deadlines); never "you have a claim" nor whether a protection applies (rule 12; D9).
- **Record check** on every filing and communication: unsupported pointed out; contradicted held by default, sent only by an open act with a reason; questions never flagged (rule 3; D2).
- Drafting from the findings and inquiries the member chooses, before publication (DESIGN §2.2).
- **Templates**: a Tier 1 set per profile (records request; with immediate disclosure; follow-up; reconsideration; mediation; written public comment; grand jury complaint; Brown Act demand at Tier 2) written by a named person, professionally reviewed, approved, attributed; offered only after the act is chosen; no court-petition templates (rule 11; D8). Fixes `wizard-scripts` R22's `@records-request`.
- **Joint texts**: one text approved in each group's own copy, matching body-only fingerprint, signatories by each organisation's act; built with the first action screens; stamp per-group fields (rule 14; D16).
- **Documentation of an infraction** as a prepared communication per audience (violating party, members, an oversight office, the public, or broad), assistant-drafted where enabled, member reviewed, revised and approved; to the violating party as a request for comment with the quoting rule (rule 19; D25).
- **Chance-to-fix notice**: the correction and remedy under the standard and a time (law's period or the group's window); asks limited to the standard, remedy and the law's routes (rules 19, 21; D26, D28b).
- Election notice (campaign-spending rule, cited) shown once when text names a pending measure or candidate, incl. thanks to a candidate-holder; position sentences in testimony pointed out (rule 9; D11).

## escalation (L9)

- R14: compliance restored by a **curing act** (`cures`), not only the same act (rule 17; D5).
- State **stopped pursuing**: member act with reason; never success or ended; no next stage proposed; reopened by a member with a reason at the stage where it stopped; published with its date, reason inside (rule 17; D6a).
- R23: an action sent on the override's grounds may join once determined, override kept (rule 2; D3).
- Evidence-seeking acts shown in its history as context (S1 §C 13); stage names as questions for UX (S1 §C 14).

## action-plans (L9)

- R34: every option listed, no cut-off or paging by five; collapsed, unscored, unticked undecided (rule 4; D17).
- R14: **any number of scenarios**; set aside with a reason, kept, restorable; comparison of two or three is UX (§3; D19).
- Plan states `open`, `paused` (reason, optional look-again date; running items continue, shown), `closed` (rule 20; D27).
- For a matter that may be an infraction: **"document the infraction" listed first**, and **"chance to fix it"** always present and re-offered after each later step, never withdrawn; another outward step first is an act with a reason (rule 19; D25, D25a, D26).
- No advocacy suggestions (R12 `lobbying` only to enforce or restore) (rule 9; D11b).
- **Re-examination driver** on `actions`' correspondence-recorded event: recompute dates, open phases, mark options with why, offer the profile's next routes, re-offer the chance to fix; AI proposals on the paying account per Ladders §2 for the recording act (project account where held, else the recorder's) within limits and settings; reach = the project and projects linked by `arose_from`; chosen items change only by a member's act (rules 1, 16; D21).
- Per matter: intended outcome (text) and derived state (documented, fix offered, acting, fixed, stopped, ended; no score) (rule 20; D27).
- Plan timeline and "what's next" (reads actions, action-clocks, escalation); decision log from history; plan reviews at a member-set rhythm opening with "what changed since"; dated review entries (rule 20; D27).
- **Preparation items** on an option, taken up by a member herself with her own target date, visible to the group; R26's assignee refusal kept for assignment by others (rule 20; D27 A).
- **Notes and comments** on options, actions and drafts, never published (rule 20; D27 C).
- Closing: reason, closing summary = frozen "where it stands" (never prose, never published, never rated), optional note with a lessons prompt read by later planning runs (rules 17, 20; D6b, D27).
- **Where it stands** view and member-drafted status reports (rule 18; D14).
- Take up: any member who can see an item routes its to-dos to herself by one act (rule 18; D14).

## monitoring, following (L10)

- Watches carry end dates; at plan close and when every duty adopted from a matter's promises reads met, the once-only question per watch the plan set running: keep until a member-chosen date, or stop; watches another plan or project relies on shown, not offered (rule 17; D6d).
