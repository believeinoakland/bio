- **Interface:** I3. I5 does not move: no table, column, index or write is added.
- **Classification (proposed):** MINOR, ADDITIVE — a new op; no existing op's shape or refusals change. CONDUCT resolves the version.
- **The op:** `GET op=versionnotice&target=<INQ-…>` (every live-basis leg of one question, in `ord` order, bounded at 200
  with `truncated`) or `&content=<content id>` (one passage) — exactly one of the two. Classes admin/member/probe,
  `mutating: false`, in both session sets, no capability, `viewer` stamped fail-closed (beside `versionchain`).
- **The answer:** `{ ok, target, content, notices[], count, limit, truncated, states, wrote: false, proposal_only: true,
  visible_to, says }`. Each notice: `{ ord?, target?, content_id, capture_sha, bundle_id, extent_kind, ref, state, newer,
  chain_read, chains[], addresses_asked, addresses_truncated, candidates[], says, why }`.
  `state` ∈ `no_newer_capture` (says `null` — §18.1's earned silence) · `newer_capture_matched` · `newer_capture_undetermined` ·
  `chain_unread` (`newer: null`), plus `not_asked` on a leg that rests on no passage. `newer` is `true | false | null`, and
  `false` is returned ONLY when every chain was read. Each candidate: `{ capture_sha, bundle_id, first_retrieved, extent,
  matched, reason, why, existing_content_id, candidate_only: true, identity: "not_established", says }`, `reason` ∈
  `extent_in_newer_capture` · `whole_document` · `outside_newer_capture` · `newer_capture_unread` · `bound_not_held` ·
  `not_testable` · `extent_unreadable`.
- **Refusals (DEC-49, C-80):** `VERSION_NOTICE_NO_SUBJECT` (C-80.1, neither or both), `VERSION_NOTICE_NO_INQUIRY` (C-80.2),
  `VERSION_NOTICE_NO_CONTENT` (C-80.3) — the last two byte-identical for absent and not-visible. One new governed region,
  `is-version-notice-subject` in `Store#versionNotice`.
- **Consumers:** none today (measured: no UI caller — `4.cross-version-ui` reads ABSENT). The member surface is UI's.
