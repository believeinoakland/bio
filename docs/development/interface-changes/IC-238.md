## IC-238 · I3: the search answer stops publishing a relevance SCORE, and relevance is computed over what the viewer can see · PROPOSED 2026-09-23 (D-447; minted with `node tools/mintid.mjs IC` and checked against every `land/*` tip — IC-233 and IC-234 were minted first and are held on `land/conduct/c17-batch7`, so they were passed over) (D-447; minted on its clone as IC-235, which collided with D-126's IC-235 on `land/conduct/c17-batch7`, and RENUMBERED IC-238 by CONDUCT #18 at c18-d456; its measurement M-121 likewise renumbered M-122, FW-20 holding M-121 on c18-batch8)

- **Interface:** I3 (`op=search`, and every op that reads its ORDER: select-all `mode=ids`, a query selection's snapshot through
  `op=select`/`op=selection`). I5 does not move: no table, no column.
- **Classification:** MAJOR, BREAKING. Two changes a consumer could see:
  1. **`hits[].score` is REMOVED**, on ranked and unranked hits alike (it was a number on a text query and `null` on a
     metadata-only one; the key is now absent in both). A hit's place in `hits` IS the order. `snippet` is unchanged.
  2. **The relevance ORDER is computed by a different formula.** It was `bm25(bundles_fts)`, whose IDF and average row
     length are statistics of the WHOLE index, hidden projects included. It is now `visibleBm25` (`bio-plane/src/query.mjs`):
     per distinct positive term, IDF over the rows the viewer's gate passes, term frequency from FTS5's `highlight()`
     marks in the row itself, saturation k1 = 1.2, **no length normalisation** (b = 0: FTS5 exposes no per-row token count
     to SQL, and an average length over the viewer's rows would mean reading every visible row's text per search).
     Relevance ties still break on `bundle_id`. A query with more than 8 distinct terms weighs them as ONE term and says so
     in `query.warnings` (`relevance weighs these N terms as one: …`) — a NEW warning string.
- **Why (the disclosure):** Membership v2 §7.9, *"Not its existence"*. Measured at the op before the change
  (MEASUREMENTS M-122): revising a project the reader cannot see moved the score of EVERY visible hit that reader was served, and moved
  the ORDER of the page, of select-all `ids` (which carries no number) and of a query selection's members. Dropping the
  score alone would have left the order leaking.

  - the skill pack (`bio-plane/src/skillpack.mjs`, `skilldoctrine.mjs`) — describes no search score; `skilldoctrine`'s
    "No single confidence score" is doctrine about strength and is unrelated. No change.
  - `newgroup/` — carries the plane only as the embedded release bundle (DIST's artifact); no reader of its own.
  - **Tests, CORRECTED with the reason at each site, never exempted:** `bio-plane/test/search.test.mjs` (asserted a numeric
    `score` and `score: null`; now asserts the key is absent), `bio-plane/test/query.test.mjs` (asserted the page computes

  term frequency; a rare term costs little. Named fix if it matters at a real instance's size: take per-term frequencies
  from an `fts5vocab` instance table (no re-tokenising) for single-token terms, keeping `highlight()` for phrases.
