### The change, on the wire

On a rendered document's `render` block (and nowhere else):

- `render.wait` was `{ asked, fired }` and is now `{ asked, fired, fired_class }`.
  - `fired` is UNCHANGED: the renderer's own word, as it reported it — `"networkidle"`, a selector, `"timeout"` — or
    `null` when it reported none.
  - `fired_class` is the PLANE's reading of that word, and it is THREE-valued: `"condition"` (the word is the condition
    this plane asked for), `"timeout"` (the word names the timeout), `"undetermined"` (anything else, including a word
    this module has never seen, and including a `fired` that is absent).
- `render.completeness`: `"condition_met"` when `fired_class` is `"condition"`, otherwise `"undetermined"`.
  `"condition_met"` says the wait ended on the condition ASKED. **It does not say the page was finished**, which no
  renderer reports and this plane never claims.
- `render.undetermined[]` gains ONE sentence whenever `completeness` is `"undetermined"`:
  - on a timeout — `completeness: render may be incomplete (wait timed out) — the renderer's wait ended on its timeout
    (15000 ms asked) rather than on the \`networkidle\` condition, so what the page would have shown had the condition
    been met is undetermined. The capture keeps its grade and its method; these bytes are not presented as the whole
    page (BOB #32, 2026-09-24).`
  - on a word the plane does not recognise — `completeness: the renderer reported the wait fired on \`<word>\`, which is
    neither the \`<asked>\` condition this plane asked for nor a timeout, so whether the render ran to its condition is
    undetermined and this rendering may be incomplete.`
  - on no wait reported — the same sentence with `the renderer did not report which wait ended the render`. (The
    existing `wait.fired: not reported by the renderer` gap is ALSO still stated: one names the missing field, the other
    its consequence.)
- `provenance_chain[0].asserts` on such a capture ends `…(render.of); render may be incomplete (wait timed out), so they
  are not asserted to be the whole page` — derived from the block by `completenessReading`, never retyped, so the
  assertion and the record cannot drift.
- **UNCHANGED, and that is the ruling's point:** `capture.grade` (the ordinary direct-capture grade), `capture.method`
  (`rendered`), the pair, and the authority fields. **Completeness is not authority**: a timed-out render of an
  all-same-host asserted page is still `authority_state: determined` (suite J7). **Nothing is refused** — no C-83 row is
  added and none fires on a timeout (suite J1).

### What this cannot see

Whether the renderer's word is TRUE. `fired` is the renderer's claim, like its request and script lists; the plane
hashes only the rendered document. A renderer that reports `networkidle` on a render it abandoned is recorded as
condition-met, and nothing here would know.
