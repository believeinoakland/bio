### The change, on the wire

- **Two ADDITIVE keys on `op=monitor`'s ok answer, present only for a harvestable Drive document** and absent from every
  other tick exactly as before: `fetched_address` (the OpenDocument export address this instance composed) and `drive`
  (`document_address`, `export_address`, `kind`, `file_id`, `export_format`). A caller that ignores them is unaffected.
- **Two NEW refusal codes in `DRIVE_CAPTURE_CHECKS`,** each with its canned translation and its own DEC-49 region in
  `src/index.mjs`'s `fetch`: `DRIVE_TICK_EXPORT_IS_THE_SHELL` (C-48.8, the declared content type) and
  `DRIVE_TICK_EXPORT_BYTES_ARE_THE_SHELL` (C-48.9, the first kibibyte). They are NOT `op=acquire`'s C-48.5/C-48.7 firing
  from a second site: a capture that meets the shell has captured nothing and the remedy is to share the file; a TICK that
  meets it has lost the CHECK while the record stands untouched, and one canned sentence cannot be true of both.
- **THE HALF THAT IS NOT ADDITIVE, AND IT IS WHY THIS IS AN IC RATHER THAN A SILENT FIX.** Three requests that ANSWERED
  before now REFUSE, all of them on a Drive address `op=acquire` already refuses to capture: a monitored bundle whose
  `source.locator` is a Drive FOLDER (C-48.2, 422), a Drive FILE id with no kind (C-48.3, 422) or a Drive shape this
  instance does not read (C-48.4, 422). Each answered a tick before — a tick over Google's listing or application page,
  which is what the refusal exists to stop being recorded as a document's state.
- **The catalogue version moves MINOR** (`CATALOG_VERSION` in `src/gate.mjs`), which is the declared channel for a
  catalogue that gained rows, and `test/d470-catalog-census.test.mjs` records the census. **THE VERSION AND THE CENSUS ARE
  PROPERTIES OF THE MERGED CATALOGUE: CONDUCT re-reads both at integration**, as it did at c20-batch13, c20-batch14 and
  c20-batch18.

### What a consumer must do

Nothing, unless it monitors Drive-linked documents. A surface that renders refusals reads both new sentences out of the
catalogue as it does every other DEC-49 row. A caller that stored `op=monitor`'s answer keeps every key it had.
