### The change, on the wire

`op=reviewcopy`'s `statement_acknowledgements` object, on BOTH doors (member by session, recipient by grant secret):

| key | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `statement_sha` | unchanged | unchanged |

| `withheld_stated` | — | **NEW, ALWAYS PRESENT, one sentence** naming the writer and §3 rule 11, or naming the withholding as UNDETERMINED with its reason, or saying plainly that nothing is withheld |

**A RECIPIENT's row is never withheld by either arm** — a grant's holder is never the member who wrote the statement
(§3 rule 13's `statement_by` names a project participant), so the narrowing cannot touch the reader this surface exists
for. Nothing else in the answer moves; in particular `statement_by` and `statement_by_stated`, which REC-193 put at the
answer's top level, are UNCHANGED in spelling and meaning.

**`inband` MOVES AND IS SUPPOSED TO.** `op=reviewcopy`'s DEC-31 quartet takes a SHA-256 over the answer as served minus
`inband` (REC-148, IC-229), so adding keys moves that hash for the same draft. It is computed at the act from the bytes
served, so no stored value goes stale and the container's matching quartet moves with it
(`bio-plane/test/reviewcopy-inband.test.mjs`, 22/0 before and after, its cross-door agreement arm included).

### What this does NOT change

- `op=publish`, `op=caseratify`, `op=publishedcase`, `op=casedocument` and the case document's bytes: untouched. The
  document has withheld the writer since REC-212 (IC-272) — **this item makes the review copy agree with it, and does
  not move what it agrees with.**
- `op=statementack`: untouched. Its `STATEMENT_ACK_BY_ITS_AUTHOR` refusal already read `statement_by` on the draft door
  (REC-193), so an acknowledgement withheld here is one taken when it was honest and left in the record: **withheld,
  never deleted**, which this item's suite drives.
- The one-case-identity narrowing (REC-194): the `draftId` argument and the identity match are unchanged, so a NEW
  case's document still lists none of a draft's readings and counts them unbindable.
