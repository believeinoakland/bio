## IC-232 · I5 + I3: a member CHOOSES the on-point mention of a connection — `connection_pair_choices` (I5), `op=connectionchoose` and the portion grade answering from the choice (I3) · PROPOSED 2026-09-23 (REC-122; minted on its clone as IC-228, which collided with D-148's IC-228 on `land/conduct/c17-batch7`, and RENUMBERED IC-232 by CONDUCT #18 with `node tools/mintid.mjs IC` at c17-batch7)

- **Interfaces:** I5 and I3. Bases as read on `origin/main` 02603e88; CONDUCT resolves the versions at integration.
- **Id note:** the allocator in this cloud clone first minted IC-224 (and C-68), both already taken on in-flight `land/*` branches
  (`land/conduct/c17-batch7`, `land/worker/D-278`); they were abandoned and IC-232 / C-74 minted past every remote landing ref's
  maximum. See the report's finding on `mintid`'s floor.
- **I5 — ADDITIVE (MINOR).** New table `connection_pair_choices` (`choice_id` PK AUTOINCREMENT; `a_capture_sha`, `b_capture_sha`,
  `entity_id`, `side` ('a'|'b'), `ref`, `a_bundle_id`, `b_bundle_id`, `chosen_by`, `at`, `superseded_at`), keyed to a connection by
  its own primary key plus the end; one index on `(a_capture_sha, b_capture_sha, entity_id, side)`. Append-only: a re-choice stamps
  `superseded_at` on the current row and inserts the next. Cleared in both `op=purge` arms (per bundle by either bundle id) and
  counted as `connectionPairChoices` in the purge proof. `connections` is UNCHANGED — the machine's pair is never rewritten.
- **I3 — ADDITIVE (MINOR).**
  1. **New op `op=connectionchoose`** (mutating; admin, member, probe classes; capability `contribute`; `author` and `viewer`
     stamped by the control plane, never the caller). Params `capture` (the end chosen on), `other`, `entity`, `ref` (the mention, as
     `resolutions.ref` holds it). Answer `{ ok, wrote, a_capture_sha, b_capture_sha, entity_id, side, chosen: {ref, grade, position,
     chosen_by, at}, superseded: {ref, chosen_by, at} | null, machine_pair_ref, says }`; the same choice twice answers `wrote: false`.
     Refusals, new family **C-74** (`CONNECTION_CHOICE_CHECKS`, region `is-connection-choice`):
     `CONNECTION_CHOICE_NOT_A_MEMBER` (C-74.1 — every machine shape, by `isMachineIdentity` on the stamped author),
     `CONNECTION_CHOICE_NO_CONNECTION` (C-74.2 — no such connection, or its chosen end out of the viewer's sight, answered identically),
     `CONNECTION_CHOICE_NOT_A_MENTION` (C-74.3 — the document carries no such resolution to that entity, or no `ref`).
  2. **`op=connections&content=`** (`connectionGradeForContent`): where a current choice exists on the cited document's end, that
     connection's entry is decided by the CHOSEN mention alone — `reaching` (grade = weaker of the chosen mention's grade and the other
     end's, the other end's own choice honoured), `outside` (C-49.1), or `undetermined` (C-49.2, the chosen mention has no position) —
     and carries `on_point: {ref, position, grade, chosen_by, at}`. A choice whose mention the document no longer carries is LAPSED:
     `on_point.lapsed: true` and REC-120's path answers. Whole-document citations are untouched. **With no choice, every answer is
     byte-identical** (REC-120's suite, whose goldens were taken on a pristine tree, is green at 29/0).
  3. **`op=connections&id=` / `&sha256=`**: a connection with a current choice gains `on_point: {a, b}`; one without is byte-identical.
  4. **C-49.4's canned translation** no longer says the choice is impossible; it says a member may choose and the answer then follows.
