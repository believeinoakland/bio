## IC-260 · I3: `op=projectdirectory` is bounded and publishes its bound — `count`, `limit`, `truncated`, and an optional caller `limit` that may lower the cap, never raise it · ACCEPTED 2026-09-24, MINOR (D-479, `land/worker/D-479` @ 4fef5238; minted by CONDUCT #20 at integration with `node tools/mintid.mjs IC`)

- **Interface:** I3 only. No table changes (I5 untouched).
- **Design:** `docs/architecture/BIO_Membership_Architecture_v2.md` §7 (item 7.14, "The directory"), with D-36's bound rule; §7.14 gained the bound as design in D-479's landing.
- **Proposer:** the D-479 worker, 2026-09-24. **Owner:** RECORD. **Resolved by:** CONDUCT #20.

### What a caller sees

`op=projectdirectory` answered every discoverable project the member is outside, unpaged. It now answers at most `Store.PROJECT_DIRECTORY_LIMIT` (200) of them, with `count`, `limit` (the cap APPLIED after clamping) and `truncated`, MEASURED by reading one visible project past the cap. `ok`, `projects` (each row still exactly `{id, name, request}`) and the `requests` sentence are byte-identical for any answer under the cap.

### Classification

**MINOR, ADDITIVE** — I3 81.1.0 → 81.2.0 on this integration's base. Three keys and one optional parameter are added; nothing that answered is refused. The one visible change is a group with more than 200 discoverable projects outside the member, which now answers `truncated: true` with the first 200, and says so. MEASURED consumer impact (the worker, 2026-09-24): `civicos-ui` makes no literal call of `projectdirectory`.
