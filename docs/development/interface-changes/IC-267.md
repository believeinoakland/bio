  base. Read the base AT RESOLUTION.** Why MINOR, by IC-25's test: ONE new op, and ONE new key on an existing answer. Every

  `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`, `NOT_AN_ACTION`, each already at several sites) and with the four C-73 shape codes a member already meets

### The change, on the wire

- **`op=actionlawspropose&target=<ACTN-…>`**, body `{ laws: [{ level, citation }, …] }` — classes `admin`, `member`, `probe`
  (and the `ai` class through the DEC-55 floor when its minted `writes` name it), `mutating: true`, capability `contribute`,
  in BOTH `SESSION_OPS` sets. `themepropose`'s cut and route exactly, for its reason: proposing is the machine's half of the
  ruling, and the label rather than a fence is what the record owes.
  - The control plane stamps `proposer` — a session's own member id, `class:<cls>` for a machine credential, or
    `class:ai/<tokenId>` — and a caller-supplied `proposer` is OVERWRITTEN, never honoured. It also stamps the `viewer`, so an
    action the caller may not see refuses `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` exactly as an absent one does.
  - **Answer:** `{ ok: true, target, weight: "single", proposal: { by, state, machine_work, says, at, laws }, governing_laws,
    evidence: false, says }`. `proposal` is the catalogue's own label block (`lawProposalLabel`) plus the proposed list;
    `governing_laws` is the action's list read through `governingLawsOf` — the ONE reader — so the act's answer states, at the
    moment it writes, that the list did not move.
  - **Refusals, all with codes the plane already mints:** `NO_AUTHOR` (no proposer stamped), `NO_TARGET`, `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`,
    `NOT_AN_ACTION`, and `NO_LAWS` (C-73.2), `BAD_LAW_LEVEL` (C-73.3), `BAD_CITATION` (C-73.4), `TOO_MANY_LAWS` (C-73.5) from
    the shared grammar.
- **`op=projection`'s action block gains `governing_laws_proposals`**, beside the unchanged `governing_laws`:
  `{ proposals: [{ by, state, machine_work, says, at, laws }], limit, truncated, says }`. Bounded at 12 proposals, the cut
  published; every proposal returned is complete. The empty answer carries `says` and is never a bare `[]`.
- **The two keys are NEVER composed.** `governing_laws` is the member's statement or its honest undetermined, and nothing in
  either answer says a member adopted, accepted or acted on a proposal — that a list came FROM a proposal is a claim about why
  somebody acted, and the record cannot support it.

### The catalogue and the store

- `checks/bio-checks.mjs` gains `LAW_PROPOSAL_STATES` (three states, each with the published sentence), `lawProposalState()`
  and `lawProposalLabel()`. **Deliberately NOT published in `VOCABULARIES`:** a proposal state is never CHOSEN by a surface —
  unlike `law_levels`, which a chooser must offer — and the sentence travels on every proposal, so publishing the map would
  add a second copy of words no caller has to pick between.
- **The four C-73 shape rows moved their `where` from `actionLaws` to `#lawEntries`** (`src/store.mjs #lawEntries >
  is-laws-entry`), the private helper both acts now ask. A `where` names the SMALLEST SPAN in which the refusal is enforced,
  and two acts enforcing the same four conditions in their own bodies would be two spans for one row — D-484's consolidation,
  anticipated rather than repaid. Codes, C-numbers and translations are unchanged.
- **I5:** `CREATE TABLE action_law_proposals (bundle_id, proposed_by, ord, level, citation, proposed_at)`, primary key
  `(bundle_id, proposed_by, ord)`, cleared in BOTH `op=purge` arms through the `TABLES` list (D-113) and counted in the
  purge's proof as `actionLawProposals`.

### Consumer impact, measured

- **`civicos-ui`: nothing breaks and nothing is gained.** `grep -c actionlawspropose civicos-ui/app.html` = 0 and
  `grep -c governing_laws_proposals civicos-ui/app.html` = 0: the action page reads `action.governing_laws` and renders
  the plane's sentence for an empty list (UI-90), and it does not read the new key. A page that ignores an added key is
  unaffected — which is the whole content of "additive" here.
- **A MEMBER SURFACE FOR THE PROPOSAL IS OWED AND IS UI'S**, stated as an act with its actor rather than implied:
  `civicos-ui/app.html`'s action page should render `action.governing_laws_proposals` beside the list, each proposal under
  the plane's own `says` and never a sentence of the page's own, and must not offer a proposal as a way of setting the list.
  `construct-status.json` 8.governing-laws states the same gap.
- **The fleet and the installer:** no change. No fleet role calls the op, and `newgroup` is untouched.
