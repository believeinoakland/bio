## IC-262 · I3: `op=knock`'s rate limit becomes a BOUND — refused on a two-bucket weighted sliding window estimate instead of a fixed bucket's count, and the 429 publishes the bound in words as `stated` · PROPOSED 2026-09-24, MAJOR (D-496, `land/worker/D-496`; minted by the D-496 worker with `node tools/mintid.mjs IC`; **CONDUCT takes the version bump and the RESOLUTION**)

- **Interface:** I3 only. **No table changes (I5 untouched)** — the two buckets are the two `knock_rate` rows already written, kept alive by widening the prune from `win` to `win` and `win-1`. No schema change, no migration.
- **Design:** `docs/architecture/BIO_System_Design.md` §3 construct 14 (the inbox), whose `14.inbox` claim in `construct-status.json` carries the rule as landed. **Authority: BOB #32's ruling of 2026-09-24 04:28Z — A PUBLISHED LIMIT IS A BOUND** (cited until folded; construct 14 has no level-1 document, as `INBOX-GRAMMAR.md`'s Place-in-the-system states, so there is no level-1 home to fold it into and this file says that rather than inventing one).
- **Proposer:** the D-496 worker, 2026-09-24. **Owner:** RECORD. **Resolved by:** CONDUCT (unresolved at this landing).

### What a caller sees

`op=knock` counted into a bucket NAMED for `floor(now / 10min)` and compared that bucket's count alone. The count therefore restarted at the edge: a caller who sent the limit in the last millisecond of one bucket and the limit again in the first millisecond of the next took **twenty-four knocks inside two milliseconds** — and 450 instance-wide against a published 300 — while every count stayed under its limit. Nothing was broken; the record's claim was false.

`Store.knock` now weights the previous bucket by how much of it is still inside the trailing window — `est = prev x (1 - elapsed/W) + cur`, refused at `est >= limit` — with `index.mjs` passing the previous bucket's name and the elapsed fraction for both the per-source 12 and the instance-wide 300. **The limits are unchanged literals: 12 and 300.**

The 429 body gains one key. `{ ok: false, reason: "RATE_IP" }` becomes `{ ok: false, reason: "RATE_IP", stated: "at most 12 knocks from one source in any 10 minutes, estimated by a sliding window" }`, and `RATE_GLOBAL` the instance-wide form. `reason` is unchanged, the status is unchanged at 429, and an accepted knock's answer is byte-identical.

### Classification

**MAJOR, and the `stated` key is the only additive part of it.** By IC-25's test the direction is what decides: **a knock that was ANSWERED at a bucket edge is now REFUSED**. A caller sending at up to twice the published rate across an edge — and any caller whose burst lands there by accident — receives a 429 where it received a receipt. That is a refusal where an answer stood, so it is MAJOR however small the window in which it bites.

**It is the record being made true rather than the fence being tightened.** The new refusal happens only where the old answer contradicted the published number; a caller inside the published rate is unaffected, and that is asserted rather than argued — the over-strictness arm drives twelve knocks spread evenly over twenty minutes and none is refused, because the decayed carry and the current count sum to the caller's real rate rather than to a bucket's contents.

**THE ESTIMATE IS APPROXIMATE IN BOTH DIRECTIONS AND THE PUBLISHED TEXT SAYS SO.** It assumes the previous bucket's knocks were spread evenly through it, so a caller who front-loaded a bucket is charged for knocks that have already aged out and one who back-loaded it for fewer than it really holds. Measured: one millisecond past an edge the previous bucket is weighted 0.9999983, so seven knocks behind the line weigh 6.999988 and **one more knock than twelve is admitted**. The sentence therefore ends "estimated by a sliding window" rather than claiming an exactness the window cannot hold — swapping one overstatement for another would be the same defect wearing the opposite sign, and `CLAUDE.md` §2's rule is that a record claiming more than it can support is worse than a missing feature.

**MEASURED consumer impact (the worker, 2026-09-24).** `civicos-ui` makes **no literal call of `op=knock`** — its two references are an op-name roster in `version-predecessor.test.mjs` and `bound-sweep.test.mjs` and a descriptive line in `check-mock-envelope.mjs`, none of which reads the refusal body. What the matcher can see: literal `op=knock`, `"knock"` and `'knock'` across `civicos-ui/` excluding `node_modules`. What it cannot see: a call composing the op name at runtime from a variable, and any consumer outside this repository — a sovereign instance's own caller is by construction not in this tree, which is why the bound is published in the refusal at all.

### Where the caller is told

Before this row the limit was published to **nobody**: the 429 carried a bare code and the number was discoverable only by hitting it. `KNOCK.statedPerIp` and `KNOCK.statedGlobal` in `index.mjs` are BUILT from `KNOCK.perIp`, `KNOCK.global` and `KNOCK.windowMs`, so the words and the numbers cannot drift apart — the drift being the defect this row exists to remove. The suite asserts the sentence as a LITERAL and does not read it off the plane: a limit taken from its subject is a limit the subject can widen.
