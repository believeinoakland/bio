## IC-284 · I3: `op=reviewcopy` answers `last_change`, and DEC-31's in-band `date` is the copy's LAST CHANGE rather than the draft's last edit · PROPOSED 2026-09-24 (REC-200; `node tools/mintid.mjs IC`)

- **Interface:** I3. I5 does not move (no table, no column, no migration — every fact read here was already stored).
- **Classification:** MINOR. One new key, and one key's VALUE redefined inside its own stated meaning:
  - `op=reviewcopy`'s answer gains `last_change: { at, by, by_kind, kind, stated }`, beside `updated_by`/`updated_at`,
    which keep their meaning (the draft's last EDIT and its editor). `at` is the newest dated act the answer SERVES —
    the draft's edit, a comment in `comments`, a grant in `grants` (or the recipient's own) with its issue and its
    revocation, an acknowledgement in `statement_acknowledgements`; `kind` is `edit` | `comment` | `grant` |
    `revocation` | `statement acknowledgement`; `by_kind` is `member` | `recipient`; `stated` is the sentence saying
    what the date is and what it cannot see. All five are `null` only if the answer carries no dated act at all.
  - `op=reviewcopy`'s `inband.date` is now `last_change.at` where it was the draft's `updated_at`. On a draft nobody
    has commented on, granted or acknowledged the two are the SAME instant, so no existing answer's date moves
    backwards; what changes is that a comment, a grant or an acknowledgement now moves it. `inband.author` is
    UNCHANGED (the draft's `updated_by`): BOB #32 ruled on the date, and a recipient who comments has not authored
    the copy.
  - NOTHING moves on the container side: `op=ratify` / `op=caseratify`'s `container.inband` already carried the
    edition's `ratified_at` and the case document's attestor, which is the stamp BOB #32 ruled it carries.
- **Consumers measured:** `grep -rn "inband\|reviewcopy\|last_change" civicos-ui agent-worker newgroup/src` — the UI's
  `pub-inband` block is the PUBLISHED container's rendering (`op=publishedcase`), not the review copy's; no surface
  reads `op=reviewcopy`'s `inband` at all, and none reads its answer by exhaustion of keys. A reader that re-hashes
  the served answer is unaffected: the hash is still taken over the answer minus `inband`, and `last_change` is inside
  those bytes — which the suite proves by moving it and watching the hash stop matching.
- **Proposer:** REC-200 worker (CONDUCT #20, cloud), 2026-09-24. **Owner:** RECORD. **Design:**
  `BIO_Publication_v0_1.md` §6A.3 point 1, with BOB #32's ruling of 2026-09-23 23:08Z.
- **Evidence:** `bio-plane/test/reviewcopy-inband.test.mjs` 22 → 31 assertions (block 7 drives each dated act through
  its own op, on both doors); `reviewcopy-inband.control.mjs` baseline 31/0, (d) KEEP THE OLD DATE 26/5 — the five
  date arms failing by name and nothing else — (e) over-strictness 31/0, (f) the date = the moment of the read 22/9.
- **What this unblocks:** nothing waits on it; it corrects what §6A.3 point 1's precondition MEANS, so an export built
  on it cannot carry this moment's hash under an older date.
