### The change, on the wire

`op=promote` carried TWO statements of what was being promoted and believed the wrong one. `bundles.object_type`
was written from `normalizeType(meta.object_type)` — the CALLER'S envelope — and the `action_basis`,
`correspondence`, `action_quotes`, `bias_statements`, `inquiry_basis` and `inquiry_basis_versions` projections
were gated on the same envelope; but `Store#projectRow`'s action COLUMNS (`action_kind`, `action_risk_tier`,
`action_counterparty_state`, `action_resolution`, `action_clock_next`, `action_clock_overdue`) are read off the
promoted DOCUMENT's own front matter. An ACTION promoted under `meta: { object_type: "information" }` therefore
landed TYPED INFORMATION, with its risk tier in the indexed column and its basis and correspondence in no table
at all — a record holding an action it does not index as one.

- **Newly refused** (`{ ok: false, reason: "ENVELOPE_TYPE_DISAGREES", code, check: "C-86.1", translation,
  document_type, envelope_type, detail }`): any `op=promote` that is NOT a `replay` whose `bundle.md` states an
  `object_type` and whose `meta.object_type` states a DIFFERENT one, compared through the catalogue's
  `normalizeType` on both sides. Before this change such a promotion landed, typed by the envelope. Every
  credential: this is a property of the request, not of the caller.
- **Newly typed by the document** (no refusal, an answer that changes): a `replay: true` promotion whose envelope
  and document disagree now lands typed as the DOCUMENT says, with the projections its type earns; and a
  promotion whose envelope states NO `object_type` at all now takes the document's type, where before it wrote
  `undefined` into a `NOT NULL` column and failed.
- **Unchanged:** every promotion whose envelope and document agree — which, measured below, is every promoting
  caller in this repository. A legacy spelling is not a disagreement (`focus`/`problem` normalise to `inquiry`).
  A document that states no type is not a disagreement (the envelope is then all there is). `isAction`'s union
  (IC-266 / D-505) is untouched and still reachable — by the envelope-states-no-type shape, which
  `risk-tier.test.mjs` §7 arm (i-b) now drives.
