| D-37 | gap | 2026-07-25 | **The 300-second selection keep-alive.** CLOSED by Bob's decision, 2026-07-25, on the grounds that a selection timeout is not a failure mode but a term of the contract, the same way an idle logout is: intrinsic to the relationship rather than an error, and its consequences the client's to decide. The client may re-select against a possibly different record set, tell the user the window closed, or warn as expiry approaches and offer to hold. than deleting the row: the history of what was wrong is worth as much as the
record of what was fixed, and a ledger that only shows current problems teaches
nobody anything.

the row. They are not resolved and should be re-read when circumstances change.
