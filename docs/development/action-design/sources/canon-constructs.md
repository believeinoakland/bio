## 1. Actor roles: kinds of groups, roles within a group

### 1.1 Kinds of groups: nothing models them
- **One group per instance, identified by one slug.** SR §3.1 and the D-436 amendment: `group` is "the producing group's slug: one value in the store's durable state, written once". PUB §7 point 1: "The slug is PUBLIC". A group has a display name and a verified domain (PUB §7 points 2–3). **No group type, sector, purpose or role attribute exists** anywhere in SR, PUB or MEM.
- **Membership is explicitly not a network construct.** MEM §2: "Membership is scoped to one group's instance… nothing here crosses a group boundary" (this preserves Design Requirement 1: "no hierarchy, no headquarters").
- **A group may be one person.** MEM §4.1 "A group of one… the entire membership apparatus stays invisible until a second person exists". PUB §3 rule 11: "a group may be one person (Design Requirement 2)".
- **Other groups appear only as sources of incoming work.** SR §4.5: "Incoming Work Products received from other groups are stored as Information objects… subject to a locally recorded Argument Evaluation before any citation" (later relaxed: AUTHORITY-AND-TRUST "RULED: transitive trust is accepted, with disclosure and adjustment"; "what is inherited is the FACT OF PUBLICATION, never the CREDIBILITY OF THE CONTENT"). UXE audience "Activist or partner group (outside this instance)": "Evaluate and accept or reject another group's work into its own body of work; rerun it under its own declared bias" and, marked open, "How strength composes across an instance boundary… is unanswered."
- **Coalitions, unions, special interests, joint or cross-group actions: no text anywhere** in the listed documents (searched: coalition, union(s), special interest, advocacy, alliance, federat*, partner group; the only hits are the word "union" in a technical sense). Cross-group discussion and the directory sit outside CivicOS (Comms Platforms §Function 1 and §Function 3, reference only). UXE OQ-29 (open): "no module or canon text says whether CivicOS itself submits to, reads from or links to them." PUB §9: "no built surface serves the directory".
- **Bob's own words gesture at group role, and the record declined to model it.** `docs/development/CONTENT-EXTENT-DESIGN-SPACE.md` §5.5, lines ~235–245 (only §6 of that file is canon, but §5.5 records Bob's words and his acceptance), Bob 2026-09-14: *"the burden of proof varies based on the type of work being published, which itself depends on the role of the member, project, or group."* The record's reply, which Bob accepted with "Okay": "the bar is set by the project and the production's audience, not by an individual member's role", so no claim object and no role field. PUB §3 rule 8 restates it: the standard of proof attaches to the production, "not to an individual member's role".

### 1.2 Kinds of outside actor: five separate concepts, not unified
1. **Audience = a READER of a published case.** PUB §6: "The eight audiences are the professional roles (Bob, 2026-09-13), and an audience is a READER of a published case, distinct from the requirements' user types and archetypes, though one person may be both (D-156)." The eight (from AUDIENCES.md §10, reference): media, activists, government administrators, lawyers, oversight bodies (auditors, controllers, grand juries), affected residents, researchers/peer instances, the publisher's future members. Funders were declined: "their need is compellingness, which the doctrine forbids". CM §The frame (Bob): media **reporting**, activists **fixing**, government administrators "**understanding, tracking, adjusting, responding**", lawyers "**supporting a claim, or changing a claim**", "…and so on | the list is open".
2. **Counterparty = the addressee of an action.** SR §4.4: `counterparty: "Oakland Finance Department, Controller's Bureau"`. (It becomes an office by role and body, never a person, in `actions` R9, which UXE cites.)
3. **Review recipient = a named outsider holding a grant.** PUB §6A.2: "a scoped, revocable, read-and-comment GRANT against one production… it is not an account, not membership, and not a weaker class of member."
4. **Subject = who a case is about.** PUB §3 rule 6: "The subject's right of reply is a declaration, not a gate" (DEC-13). CM §4: "the subject registry (D-83) must carry no adversarial attribute".
5. **Named counsel** (Tier 3): UXE audience "Named counsel" (from `filings` R8–R13 and DR8 as amended).
UXE lists 19 audiences (the product's own roles plus these outsiders): "Journalist or media", "Activist or partner group (outside this instance)", "Government office or official addressed by an action or filing", "Oversight body (grand jury, controller, auditor)", "Named counsel", "Review recipient (outsider with a grant)", "Public reader of a published case", "The group's future members" (UXE `audiences`).

### 1.3 The verdict that decides where actor differences go: the action, not the case
PUB §6, adopting AUDIENCES §8's verdict: every divergence between audiences is *"a property of the OUTPUT ACT rather than of the case: who certifies it, whether it persists, and to whom it is delivered."* The falsifier: *"If the second audience built costs a new `action_kind` and a rendering, this is right. If it costs a field on the case, it is wrong."* AUDIENCES §10 item 2 (reference): activists' "unit of work is `action`, not `case`"; §8: "**`action` is the home of audience divergence, not `case`.**" PUB §6 table: certification "unmade", persistence "unmade", addressing answered by the review copy (§6A).

### 1.4 "User type" exists as a design idea with no record behind it
CM §THE ACTION PLAN, Bob 2026-08-03: *"This action plan may differ based on the user type (journalist, activist, lawyer, etc.) or other very important (strategic, tactical, political, financial, temporal) factors."* CM plan §4: "**capabilities, journeys and surfaces vary by USER TYPE** (`AUDIENCES.md` §0, unchanged)… The plan therefore belongs to a PROJECT + FINDING, never to a person: a lawyer's project and a journalist's project may build different plans from the SAME finding". **No field, capability or record anywhere encodes a member's or group's user type.** The project and its bar (`required_strength`, DEC-17, DEC-72) stand in for it.

### 1.5 Roles within a group: what exists
- **Capabilities** (MEM §5): contribute, publish ("additionally requires a registered signing key"), create projects, administer. "A capability a member does not hold is absent from their interface".
- **Custodial and working roles** (MEM §4, §7): founder / administrator ("audit everything and direct nothing", MEM §4, quoted at PUB §6A.2); project **owner** (sole initial owner is the creator, MEM §7.1; "Only owners invite", §7.2), **joined participant** (§7.5: "the working rights their capabilities allow"), **invited, not joined** ("view rights only"; sees the skeleton, "the Focuses it stands above, the Information it cites, and the Actions it initiates", §7.9).
- **Declared expertise**: the nearest thing to role-by-profession. MEM §1.3: "A group needs to know which participants are lawyers, CPAs, engineers, doctors, barbers. This is operational routing information… **Confirmation gates nothing.**" MEM §5: expertise is "metadata, not capability. They inform humans; they gate nothing". It routes TASKs: IC §T "routing through `member_expertise` to the project manager falling back to a group admin". AUTHORITY-AND-TRUST RULED: undetermined authority goes to "the project manager… where a project has no manager, to a group admin… may forward it".
- **Attribution levels** (a member's choice in how they are named, relevant to "perspectives"): PUB §3 rule 7: "the group, the project, the member's cover, or the member by name; a source who spoke off the record to preserve anonymity is valid".
- **Certification by a licensed professional is unmade.** PUB §6: "who stands behind the rendering: the group, or a named professional whose licence the output act needs | unmade". UXE audience "Member with declared professional expertise": "Whether a professional's name or licence appears on an output (certification) is an unmade divergence. [open]".
- **Roles in "sharing resources, information, perspectives": nothing by those names.** The nearest constructs are: plan-step RESOURCES, "an ATTACHED LIST, not a model" (CM plan §6b; Bob: "a collapsable list?"); aspirations scoped group / project / member, where "Contradicting aspirations are WELCOMED… no arbiter and no precedence" (CF §12.1); and the exclusion-statement acknowledgement by a second person (PUB §3 rule 11). There is no role such as "resource provider", "information broker", "spokesperson", "liaison" or "coordinator".

## 2. Plurality: several actions of different kinds per finding / group

- **A project initiates zero or more actions.** SR §5.1: `initiates (Project to Action)`, and §5.2: "initiates lives on the Project". MEM §7.9: "A Project `initiates` zero or more Actions. Nothing in this hierarchy is exclusive."
- **A plan holds many steps and may serve several findings.** CM plan §2: a plan-as-field fails because "the plan outlives and outgrows the finding, and may serve several findings at once". "So: a PLAN holds STEPS. A step that is taken produces an `action`… A step declined keeps its reason and stays visible."
- **Several projects may plan differently from one finding.** CM plan §4 (above). The same holds for cases: PUB §3 rule 12: "A finding is shared: several projects cite it and several cases pin it".
- **Action kinds are a suite.** SR §4.4: `action_kind: cpra_request # extensible suite: cpra_request | grand_jury | controller_referral | public_comment | media | litigation_support | other`. CM plan §4: "The seven `action_kind` values already shipped… are that repertoire's first draft."
- **Escalation attaches many actions.** UXV2 relationships: `escalation → action` "attaches breach actions at stages 2, 5, 7", "1 to many"; `escalation → determination` "one open escalation per determination"; `action → determination` "rests on (breach actions)", "many to 1..many".
- **Limits on plurality.** The action carries ONE `counterparty` field (SR §4.4), and CM plan §6 says "S10 THE ACTION PAGE holds ONE outward engagement". One communication to many recipients (a press release to several outlets, a coalition letter to several offices) has no stated shape.

## 3. Actions not premised on a breach

**What allows it:**
- **The action schema carries no breach premise.** SR §4.4 kinds include `public_comment`, `media`, `other`; the lifecycle (`planned → active → awaiting_response → resolved | abandoned`) presumes only an outward engagement and a reply.
- **CM's definition is outward engagement, not breach.** CM §What does NOT collapse: "`action`: outward-facing engagement with a `counterparty` and correspondence. An inquiry is a thought; an action is a thing done to the world." CM §2: "a records request, a letter to a council member, a complaint, a demand". CM plan §2 quotes the definition: *"an outward engagement: something SENT to somebody outside the group"*.
- **Records requests are information-gathering actions generated by objectives, not by breaches.** CF §12 "Satisfaction conditions": "The 12 Grade C instances name exactly what measurement would raise them, and the 5 missing solicitations are records requests with the request already specified." CF §12 table: "a step someone takes is an `action`". CF §12.2 pursuit record includes "the records requests made and what came back".
- **Pre-publication contact with a subject is an action** (DEC-13; UXE OQ-17 cites `actions` R1 and R34: "A 'request_for_comment' action must name an inquiry and a clock entry… 'a non-response is recorded with its date'").
- **Doctrine is explicitly not adversarial.** CM §4, Bob: *"nowhere in the doctrine is anything like 'Stick it to the man!' It's about better government… If we start by assuming that at the highest level all stakeholders want better outcomes in the public interest, then we're all on the same side."* "**So an administrator is not an inversion. They are a stakeholder like any other**".
- **A case can be reporting.** CM §Is a case…: "A case is made TO someone FOR something: reporting to the public, a claim to a court, a fix asked of a body. That is where `action` connects: the ask is an action".
- **Aspirations can be positive.** CF §12 example: "Oakland's procurement should be traceable end to end."
- **Compliance is recorded with equal care.** `conformance` Purpose (outside the brief, cited by UXV2): "A compliant determination is recorded with the same care as a noncompliant one."

**What frames the Action layer as breach-driven:**
- **The action plan is premised on nonconformity.** CM §THE ACTION PLAN, Bob: *"the finding of a project indicate[s] that some government action… doesn't conform… In situations like this, an action plan needs to be constructed."* CM plan §1: "A finding of NONCONFORMITY says conduct departed from a standard". UXE UC-126's trigger is "A nonconformity finding."
- **The escalation protocol is breach-only.** DR7 as amended: "compliance restored and consequences addressed". UXV2 `escalation` "is opened from (live noncompliant)". UXE UC-113 "Create a breach action to an office". DR7 stage 7 (Political Accountability): "Policy advocacy and candidate support are not part of it; Operational Principle 1 stands."
- **Recommendations stop short of policy.** DEC-84 (10): "Recommendation limited to a proposed action, never a policy position."
- **Success stories, recognising compliance, commendations, relationship-building, coalition outreach: not modelled or mentioned** in any listed document. No action kind, no escalation-like path, and no publication framing exists for a compliant determination or a "what went right" case. A compliant determination leads to nothing in UXV2 (the only outgoing construct relationship is escalation, from noncompliant determinations). Nothing forbids such an action (kind `other` or `media`), but nothing names or shapes it.

## 4. The Action object and related records as designed

### 4.1 SR §4.4 (canon record shape)
- Record file: `action.md` "(plan, correspondence log, outcome record)". Required prose headings: `## Plan`, `## Status`, `## Correspondence`, `## Session Log`, `## Review Notes`.
- Fields: `action_kind` (7-value extensible suite, above); `risk_tier` "1 | 2 | 3 | undetermined, from the evidence-package classification; undetermined wherever no member stated one (D-182)"; `clock[]` entries `{text, description, date, basis, status}`, status "pending | met | overdue | waived"; `counterparty` (a string in the example).
- Lifecycle: "planned → active → awaiting_response → resolved (with resolution: complied, denied, escalated, withdrawn) | abandoned (reason-gated)".
- Clock doctrine: "The clock array is the authoritative deadline register the Monitoring skill watches; every date-bearing entry carries basis naming the statute, order, or commitment the date derives from."
- Invariant **I-11 Clock discipline** (SR §6): "Every Action clock entry has a basis and a valid date; overdue entries are marked overdue, not silently stale." Repair (SR §7): "(a) supply basis; (b) mark overdue; (c) mark waived with reason".
- **I-20**: the mechanical writer's `deadline-recheck` "may change clock entry status and last_updated".
- Edges: only `initiates` (Project→Action) in the closed vocabulary (SR §5.1). **No Action→finding, Action→reply or Action→case edge exists in SR's vocabulary.**
- Work product: SR §4.5: "workproduct.md lives in its Project or Action bundle"; a distribution freezes it into `distributions/dist-NNNN/` "with its own manifest (audience: internal | external; risk tier; evaluation results…)".
- SR §1.1 layout: root `actions/ACTN-2026-0001-cpra-26-3028/`.

### 4.2 CM additions (Bob's rulings folded there; built per its status block)
- **risk_tier (D-182, BOB #21):** meanings "**1, file freely; 2, file with caution; 3, do not file without counsel**"; "Only a member's authored act sets 1, 2 or 3, and **once a member has set a tier, no machine credential may change it, not even back to `undetermined`**"; revision by `op=actionrisktier`, "append-only… with a REQUIRED reason… the record must show that a 'do not file without counsel' tier was changed, and by whom" (BOB #33, REC-214). Intake tier author "UNDETERMINED".
- **Fee quote is evidence (D-148, Bob):** *"Yes, a price quote is evidence"*. It lives on a `received` correspondence entry; "The record asserts only what was quoted, by whom, when, for which request." A quote is read "by" the action's counterparty name "matched EXACTLY; when nothing matches, it is stated as undetermined" (also PUB §3 rule 15(d)).
- **Governing laws (D-149, Bob):** *"ALL records laws apply"*, following the AGENCY ASKED; a list "each named by CITATION and set by a member's authored act; the machine may propose… never sets it". "**The plane encodes no law's rules**: fees, clocks, appeals". Direction adopted (BOB #32): a law-neutral `records_request` kind with a `law` field; `cpra_request` stays readable.
- **Records-request lifecycle (D-147, BOB #27):** "Each stage after the request is its OWN correspondence entry naming the entry it answers or follows". `sent` = request | fee-waiver request | appeal | court filing; `received` = acknowledgement | fee estimate | waiver decision | extension notice | production | denial | appeal/court decision. Outcome vocabulary: "granted · denied · partial · reversed · affirmed · none stated". "**The clock is never encoded**… a member may state on an entry the date by which the NEXT stage is due and the citation it comes from… with none stated the due date is UNDETERMINED." The plane derives "the time elapsed between entries, and that a stated due date passed with no answering entry: D-128's declared-versus-observed flow measured on our own request." Built in the plane (keys `stage, follows, outcome, exemptions, due_by, due_cite`); "Not built: a member surface, and a cross-action index of passed due dates."
- **An action is never a leg of a question's basis (D-181, BOB #29, CM plan §8):** "A leg is what GRADES a claim; an action is our own act". A non-response is an OUTCOME (DEC-14): "*'We asked on this date and nothing came back by that date'*… the act it is about travels as the edge that names it (`references[]`; a reply travels as `responds_to`, REC-24)". Open: whether an observed ABSENCE of a reply may be a leg; "Trigger: a real group's non-response case needs that absence GRADED".
- **The missing loop (CM §2):** "What is missing is not the object but its CONNECTIONS: nothing links an action to the findings that justified it, to the case it advances, or to the outcome it produced… **The loop from evidence to action to consequence is the thing that is unbuilt**". This is partly superseded: CM's status block (D-510) names an `action_basis` projection, and `BIO_System_Design.md` §1 says "outward **action** can say which findings justified it".

### 4.3 The action PLAN (CM §THE ACTION PLAN; designed, not built, no module)
- A distinct object holding candidate STEPS. Each step has `stage_no`, `after_stage` (dependency) and `within_interval` (deadline), borrowed as vocabulary from progressions but "do NOT borrow the progression object", plus: "**outcome-keyed branching**… a decision tree, not a chain"; "**resources**"; "**disposition.** chosen · declined-with-reason · done · blocked".
- Why a plan and a progression must not share an object: "A progression models what SOMEBODY ELSE is supposed to do… A plan models what WE intend to do. If the two shared machinery, **our own missed deadline would surface as a finding about the record**".
- Support status on every element: "established | short of the standard | hypothetical"; "a hypothetical premise… is **HUNCH DEBT**"; "An outward act's pre-flight REFUSES when its step is not `established`". Principle: "**the gate belongs at the ACT, not at the reasoning.**"
- Forward and backward exploration. The BACKWARD question ("what additional findings… would ALSO need to be true for action X") is deferred as D-165. Preconditions are deferred; resources are "an open list… No enumerated categories".
- Machine: SUGGEST (PURSUE) and CHECK ("an unhandled branch: an outcome with no next step" is "the most valuable check"); "The machine never adopts a step."
- Surface S11 (plan) vs S10 (one action). Journey: "finding → S11 PLAN → S10 individual actions → consequence → back in as evidence".
- **DEC-25 (deferred, Bob 2026-08-03):** "What, if any, part of an ACTION PLAN is published?" Provisional: "**THE PLAN IS WORKING MATERIAL AND IS NEVER PUBLISHED**". If ever answered otherwise it applies "PROSPECTIVELY only". Trigger: "a group asks to include any part of a plan in a published case".
- UXE OQ-9 (open) and UC-126 (coverage "no"): "No requirement or module holds a plan, so none is built. Undecided:… how a plan relates to the escalation module's built seven-stage protocol".

### 4.4 Clocks and deadlines: at least six designs
1. The action `clock[]` with stored `status` (SR §4.4, I-11, I-20).
2. `due_by`/`due_cite` on correspondence entries, status derived at read (CM §2, D-147).
3. The TASK "an obligation with an assignee, and sometimes a clock" (IC §T). D-86: "*an obligation with a clock, attached to an object… settleable in batches*… two schedulers would be two sets of bugs about one thing."
4. Progression `within_interval` for the body's declared flow (CM plan §3; NOTIFICATIONS D-128).
5. Plan-step deadlines (CM plan §3).
6. DR7 stage 3 "Clock Starts"; UXV2 escalation state "3 clock: a deadline with its basis".
Doctrine on waits: DR Operational Principle 3 "The clock runs. Deadlines are deadlines" and DEC-69 "must not nag" (both quoted in UXE OQ-14).

### 4.7 Outcomes

### 4.8 Member and machine roles in acting
- Only members set tiers and laws (CM §2). UXV2 role "Assistant / AI run" **cannot**: "set a tier, state a law, determine, file"; **can** "propose readings, standards, comparisons, laws, tiers, theories, filings".
- AIR §3 rule 4: "**A checker raises; it never resolves. No machine credential performs the attested act.**" An attested act is delivered only by "a HUMAN's own authenticated session… never a bearer token and never a machine credential". AIR §1 (DEC-24): *"THE MACHINE MAY DO THE LOOKING; THE MEMBER DOES THE CONCLUDING."* AIR §2: the assistant "may **initiate an act** that then runs its four beats with the member acting (DEC-27: *'the assistant CONDUCTS and the member ACTS'*)". AIR §3 rule 1: "**Show the reasoning; never the sentence.**" AIR does not mention actions, filings, correspondence or drafting letters. The "never files" boundary is in `filings` ("The AI prepares; a member approves and files"), not in AIR.
- CF §12: "**An assistant may propose at any point in the loop and adopt at none of them.** It can draft the decomposition, run the junction checks, assemble the progression and write the records request. The member's adoption is what makes any of it the group's position."
- CM §4a: "nothing that drafts framing FOR a member: the same rule as never prefilling a justification, extended from a field to a whole argument."
- Rungs of action acts (UXE surfaceRules "Standards… actions and escalation"): `actionmove`/`actionrisktier` reasoned; `actionlaws` reversible; `actioncorrespond` "not assigned (affordances R27)". **No action act is attested**: sending a filing or letter carries no signature, unlike publishing (IC rung ladder: attested = "public, requires a key").
- Who may act on an action: "member" / `contribute` (CM §2: `op=actionrisktier` "member class, `contribute`"). Whether action acts are fenced by project position (owner or joined) as MEM §7.9 REC-134 does for project acts is not stated in the construct docs.

### 4.9 Where outputs leave the instance vs where actions leave
- **Publication (a case):** signed, into the separate PUBLISHED projection. PUB §1: "the only act that cannot be walked back". PUB §3 rule 2: "Only a project publishes, and only its manager". Readers are the audiences; the read path is credential-free (PUB §3 rule 10).
- **Review copy:** "stands BESIDE publish… **never leaves the instance**… MUTABLE" (PUB §6 table, §6A). "**ONE irreversible act, not two**" (§6A.1). A member may export it: every page carries the DEC-31 quartet, and "The record does NOT log that a copy left, and says why" (§6A.3 point 3(c)).
- **DEC-31 in-band rule (binding):** PUB §3 rule 9: "Anything leaving the instance addressed to someone carries hash, date, author and both threshold floors in-band".
- **Actions leave only through the member's own hands.** DEC-31 provisional (2026-08-03): addressed non-public delivery is "modelled provisionally as an ACTION: the member performs the delivery outside the system and records having done it… nothing non-public leaves the instance by any system path." UXE UC-124: "An action recorded after it is done outside the system", with coverage note "No requirement shapes the communication itself." UXE audience "Oversight body": "A confidential referral delivered without publication has no bucket; DEC-31 models it as an action recorded after the member delivers it outside the system. [open]"
- **The plan never leaves** (DEC-25 provisional).
- **SR's legacy "distribution"** (§4.3 `workproduct_state … distributed`, §4.5 `distributions/` with "audience: internal | external") is a third, older outbound model that PUB never mentions (see §5).

### 4.10 Communications, press, journalists, stories
- The "communications" in PUB's title is only cross-group discussion and the directory (PUB §10: it does not own "the platform selection"). No construct models composing or sending a press statement, letter or story. UXE UC-124 (partial): "Media outreach, public comment and testimony… No requirement shapes the communication itself."
- Journalists are readers (PUB §6). UXE journalist entry: "Never receives unratified material through any system path; an embargoed advance copy is a review copy that never leaves the instance." `media` is an `action_kind` (SR §4.4), and "Media outreach is a Tier 1 action" (DR8, via UXE).
- Story vs narrative (CM §4a): "a **story** is what the record supports… a **narrative** is a frame imposed on the material… **The tool should make a supported case easy to build and an unsupported one hard to state.**" CM §The frame: "CivicOS is about telling a story, equivalently, making a case."

## 5. Contradictions between documents

1. **Clock: stored and mechanically updated, or never encoded.** SR §4.4 makes `clock[]` "the authoritative deadline register the Monitoring skill watches", I-11 requires overdue to be "marked", and I-20 lets `deadline-recheck` write clock status. CM §2 (D-147, D-149): "**The clock is never encoded**", due dates live on correspondence entries, and status is "derived at the read's instant". UXE (from `actions` R35): "Overdue derived at read". SR was not amended.
2. **Counterparty shape.** SR §4.4's example is a free string; `actions` R9 (quoted in UXE and UXV2) refuses "a bare string" and requires `{state: named, role, body}`, "never a person".
3. **`action_kind` set.** SR §4.4 has 7 kinds including state-specific `cpra_request`, `grand_jury`, `controller_referral`. CM §2 adopts a law-neutral `records_request` "since a sovereign group may sit outside California". UXV2 `action` keyFields: "kind (product: records request, request for comment, other; more from profile)". PUB §6's test counts new audiences in new `action_kind`s, while the requirements move kinds out of the product into jurisdiction profiles.
4. **Outcome vocabularies differ three ways.** SR resolution (complied/denied/escalated/withdrawn); D-147 correspondence outcomes (granted/denied/partial/reversed/affirmed/none stated); escalation evaluation "complied / partial / denied / none" (UXV2). "partial" has no action resolution, and how `resolved: escalated` relates to the escalation construct is unstated.
5. **The closed edge vocabulary is behind the design.** SR §5.1 lists cites, relates_to, elevated_into, initiates, derived_from, supersedes, corroborates. CM §8 names `responds_to` (REC-24) and a `references[]` edge from a non-response finding to the action, and CM's status names `action_basis`. None is in SR, which says "New relationship kinds require a spec revision, not an inline invention".
6. **Distribution vs case publication.** SR §4.3 and §4.5 keep a `workproduct_state` ladder ending in `distributed`, with distributions to "internal | external" audiences and "the cascade flagging known recipients' copies stale via the directory". PUB (DEC-72 case, DEC-12 editions) never mentions distributions or `workproduct_state`, treats corrections as pull ("supersession is surfaced, not followed", PUB §2 table), and keeps addressed delivery inside the instance (§6A). SR §4.3's 2026-09-29 addition keeps the readiness ladder for display (K364). Two outbound models coexist.
7. **Risk tier: per action or per kind.** SR §4.4 puts `risk_tier` on each action instance "from the evidence-package classification". Comms Platforms (reference) classifies action TYPES, puts templates in the evidence package and publishes the classification in package metadata. UXV2 `filing`: "governing tier (stricter of kind and action tier)". PUB §8 names only the action field. Whether the tier is published is unstated in PUB.
8. **Plan vs escalation.** CM plan §2 makes a plan a set of options including declined ones. The requirements' seven-stage escalation also sequences outward acts. UXE OQ-9, option 3: folding the plan into escalation "conflicts with Case Making §2, since an escalation is one pursued breach, not a set of options." Nothing reconciles them.
9. **Case Making's canon status.** `requirements/README.md` lists CM as canon "whole". CM's own status: "Its 2026-08-10 status correction rules the file non-authoritative… Read it for WHY; several body sentences are now false as written." Its action-plan section is also "not yet in the review document".
10. **Audience vs user type.** PUB §6 defines audience as a READER, distinct from user types (D-156). CM plan §4 grounds plan variation in USER TYPE via AUDIENCES §0, and AUDIENCES itself "mixes both" (its correction banner). No record models user type (§1.4).
11. **"Communications" is owned twice, loosely.** PUB claims construct 13 including communications but "does not own… the platform selection" (§10). Comms Platforms "Owns the platform half" (its Place). Neither owns outbound communications to outsiders as an act.
12. **Transitive trust.** SR §4.5 and I-9 say "no transitive trust" (a local Argument Evaluation before citing incoming work). AUTHORITY-AND-TRUST RULED: "Transitive trust is accepted so long as it is disclosed". UXE OQ-12: "The canon also shifted… whether the levels replace, sit beside or derive from the record's own grades is undecided."
13. **Name collisions touching the Action layer.**
    - **act vs action.** UXV2 glossary: "Action… avoidConfusionWith: an act (any button that changes the record)". IC's ACT construct is unrelated to the Action object.
    - **OBLIGATION.** It means the body's civic duty (NOTIFICATIONS, Bob) but labels member tasks in the queue (UXE OQ-25).
    - **consequence.** It means the action's own outcome (DEC-14) and also a breach's harm (the `consequences` module). UXE flags this via `actions` R26 ("never call it the breach's consequence").
    - **rung ladder.** It means a sequence of authored acts (PUB §1, System Design row 12) and also act weight (IC, affordances R2); UXV2 `contradictions`. PUB §1 has since been corrected (K356).
    - **finding.** It means a concluded question in case-making and an unjudged item in the queue (UXE meta).
14. **PUB's bar table.** UXE OQ-22: "The Publication table still says 'strictest wins' for the bar while DEC-72 removed composition across projects" (PUB §2 table, "the bar": "`required_strength`, strictest wins").
15. **Interaction Constructs omits the Action.** IC never mentions the Action object, action kinds, counterparties or correspondence. Its only clock is the TASK's. Yet IC §What this changes names the test: "when the next capability lands… it should need a place in an existing construct rather than a new screen". CM plan §6 adds screens S10 and S11, and UXV2 surfaces add "Actions and filings" and "Escalation".

## Gaps / undefined
1. **No level-1 home for the Action layer.** No System Design row; the schema is in SR §4.4, the reasoning in CM, and the substance now in approved requirements and DR7–8, which the construct docs do not reflect.
2. **Group types and actor roles.** No group-type attribute, no coalition or multi-group construct, no joint action, no inter-group request. User type (journalist / activist / lawyer) is invoked by Bob and CM but has no record. Within a group, only capabilities, project position and expertise-that-gates-nothing exist. No roles for sharing resources, information or perspectives.
3. **Non-breach actions.** They are allowed by the schema (`public_comment`, `media`, `other`, records requests, request for comment) but have no designed shape. Success stories, recognising compliance, commendation, outreach and relationship-building are absent. A compliant determination leads to nothing, and the plan and escalation are both nonconformity-triggered.
4. **Multi-recipient communications.** One `counterparty` per action; no model for a message to many (press, several offices, a coalition). No construct for composing or sending communications. The in-band quartet rule (PUB §3 rule 9) is the only constraint on anything addressed that leaves.
5. **How the group learns of a government response.** Responses arrive outside the system and enter only when a member records a `received` entry. No notification generator covers replies or action clocks. Overdue marking is "not yet met". The only transport is the in-app queue (email undecided, D-98).
6. **Six clock designs and no stated relation among them** (action `clock[]`, correspondence `due_by`, TASK clocks, progression intervals, plan-step deadlines, escalation stage 3). SR and CM contradict each other on whether clock status is stored.
7. **The action plan.** Designed in CM, no module, no requirements. D-165 (the backward question) deferred; preconditions deferred; DEC-25 (publish any of it?) deferred. Its relation to escalation is unresolved (UXE OQ-9).
8. **The Action→finding / case / reply edges** (`action_basis`, `responds_to`, `references[]`) are outside SR's closed vocabulary. Whether an observed absence of a reply may be a leg is deferred on a trigger (CM §8).
9. **Outcome vocabulary** is split three ways, and the link between `resolved: escalated` and the escalation construct is undefined.
10. **Audience-specific output acts.** Certification (a licensed professional standing behind an output) and persistence are "unmade" (PUB §6). The catalogue of standards by audience is "OWED… does not exist" (PUB §7, §9).
11. **Whether risk tiers are published**, and whether they attach to the kind or the instance, is unreconciled (SR vs Comms Platforms vs filings).
12. **Confidential referral / addressed delivery to an oversight body** is still the DEC-31 provisional: "an action recorded after the member delivers it outside the system", with no bucket and no record of what left.
13. **SR's legacy distribution model** (`workproduct_state: distributed`, internal/external audience manifests, recipient-copy staleness via the directory) is neither retired nor reconciled with case publication.
14. **Action-act authority.** Which project position may create or move an action, and whether any action act (sending a filing) should be attested, is not stated in the construct docs. Action act rungs are partly "not assigned" (affordances R27).
15. **Cross-group consumption.** How strength composes when one group cites another's case is "unanswered" (UXE partner-group audience; AUDIENCES §6C). No act records one group accepting another's work (UXE OQ-12). Directory and "working on" scope is undecided (UXE OQ-29).
