Every scale a member meets, the plain question each one answers, and how they fit together. Hover over, focus or tap any grade or bar step to see what a member would see.

  • An answer is only as strong as the weakest thing it depends on. A question's answer rests on documents and on links between them. Its strength shows two things side by side: the least trustworthy document copy it depends on, and the least certain link it depends on. The two are never merged into one score, because they measure different things.
  • A grade tells you how easily someone else could check it for themselves. It never tells you whether it is true. It does not rate a source's honesty or how much a matter matters.
  • What we don't know is shown, never hidden or guessed. "Undetermined" (we can't tell, and here is why) and "Nobody looked" (no one has searched there yet) are answers, not errors.

Letters grade evidence

Every evidence scale uses the same letters with the same meaning: A is the easiest for someone else to check, D rests on a person's word. The colour and icon tell you which scale a letter belongs to; the colour never changes with the letter.

How the letters combine: strength

1 · Each support is graded

Every document, link and account a question's answer depends on carries its own letter.

2 · The weakest one counts

Among the document copies the answer needs, the lowest capture letter counts. Among the links it needs, the lowest connection letter counts.

3 · Shown side by side

BC testimony shown beside

4 · Against the project's bar

The project declares the strength its work requires. The answer is shown against that bar: meets it, or short on which of the two.

Weights mark acts

Every button carries its weight, so a member knows before acting whether it can be undone. Weights never appear on evidence or progress.

Marks that say what we don't know, or who did it

"Undetermined"

We can't tell, and here is why. Never shown as an error, never filled with a guess.

"Nobody looked"

No one has searched there yet. Different from "looked and found nothing"; the record says which is true.

"Machine work"

Proposed by the assistant. Not evidence until a member acts on it.

"Newer version"

What you cited has changed since. Nothing moves until a member decides.

"Withheld"

Something exists that you may not see. Stated as incompleteness, never as a count or a name.

"In tension"

This claim conflicts with another in our record. Open it to see both sides (DEC-77).

Making each scale distinct at a glance

Where your review of the first draft landed, and what this draft now applies.

Applied · your call

Colour marks the scale, never the value

Each evidence scale has one colour, used for every letter on it. A capture C and a capture A are the same blue.

Applied · your call

A descriptive icon for each scale

Document, link, speech and person. The icon also carries the scale for anyone who can't tell the colours apart.

Removed · your call

A different border shape for each scale

Every badge now has the same shape. Colour and icon do the work.

Kept · your call

One A–D scale shared by every evidence scale

A is the easiest to check everywhere; D rests on a person everywhere.

Applied

Hover and click on every grade and every bar step

Hover, focus or tap shows what the grade or step means. Clicking a reached bar step shows how it was reached. On a grade, a click will open the scale's full ladder with the item placed on it (not drawn on this page).

Applied

A colour family and texture per progress ladder

Solid, striped and dotted, with dashed for "not yet evaluated", so ladders stay distinct when colour can't be seen.

What a newcomer meets first: only strength against the project's bar, and "Undetermined". Every other mark appears where it is used, with its explanation one hover away.
