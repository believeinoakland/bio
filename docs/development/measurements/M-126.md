### What the class IS, judged from bodies

One class, Bob's fifth named type, with TWO arms recorded per document so the count splits without a re-run:

- **budget** — its body sets out or reports the allocation of public money over a fiscal period. **Money density is
  necessary** (≥ 40 amounts and ≥ 4 per 100 words), joined by self-naming in a title line OR a fiscal period named ≥ 3 times with
  ≥ 3 of 11 ledger terms (appropriation, expenditure, revenue, fund balance, general fund, FTE, personnel, O&M, transfers,
  carryforward, encumbrance).
- **dataset** — its body IS a table of records: ≥ 20 rows of one shape (≥ 3 cells), a header naming the columns, the table ≥ 60%
  of the block's lines, rows distinct and carrying values, and the sheet NOT computed (≤ 30% formula cells). A workbook is
  judged sheet by sheet; commas are cells only in a body that is a CSV by its own signature.
