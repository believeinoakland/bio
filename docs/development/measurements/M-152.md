### D-557 — THE CENSUS NEVER CLASSIFIES TIER-3 TEXT, AND ITS READER LABEL SAYS IT DOES

The provenance showed this on the first escalations it read. `plane_text` labels the reader from the ACQUIRE reading's
`text_tier` (`row.get('structure_tier') or row.get('text_tier')`). The text it judges, though, is the PLAIN
`op=pdfstructure` answer, and that answer stops at tier 2: only `&ocr=1` reaches tier 3 (D-319).

Measured: for 34 of the 38 documents labelled `plane (text tier 3)`, the served text is EMPTY while the acquire
reading's own provenance holds the OCR text. For example, the second escalation in the walk had 0 characters served
against 867 characters at tier 3 on `ocr-worker`. The other 4 carried a tier-1 or tier-2 text layer, which is what
they were judged on.

**So M-143's reader line "38 tier 3" counts documents whose OCR text never reached `judge`.** Its classes are unaffected
in the direction of over-counting: those documents fall to UNCLASSIFIED, `no text layer`. They are under-read.

**The fix, named:** `fw20-decode-census.mjs` should hand the census the text the acquire reading classified. That text
is in the acquire document's `text_units`. The alternative is to call `op=pdfstructure&ocr=1` for a document the
acquire read at tier 3. In either case the reader label comes from `structure_provenance.producers`, not from
`text_tier`. **Not fixed here:** it changes the instrument's text source, and so the measurement. It is SCHEDULER's to
place.

**Blind spots.**
- Documents the instrument's own tier 1 read (668) never reach the plane and carry no provenance.
- The 47 documents with no answer from `op=pdfstructure` are undetermined twice over.
- A second read within one session is not a second session. Fleet members' isolate state is shared across the two
  reads of one miniflare process, but not across the two processes used here, which were one per document per read.
