#### (c) Assessor APNs against Legistar — 80 of 121, and a TIME problem nobody had named

**The join works, and it needed a normalisation M-119's pattern could not express.** Oakland's record writes an
APN two ways: Legistar pads every part (`011-0836-017-00`) while the assessor's roll does not (`48-6298-3-2`, the
sub-parcel omitted when zero). **M-119's pattern `ddd-dddd-ddd[-dd]` cannot see the roll's form at all**, so the
two systems could never have met. The roll publishes its own canonical column, **`apn_sort` = book(3) + ' ' +
page(4)parcel(3)sub(2)** — verified against the roll itself (`apn 48-6298-3-2` has `apn_sort "048 629800302"`;
`apn 48-6313-23`, sub-parcel empty, has `"048 631302300"`). Both forms fold onto that key. **Zero-padding a part
is not folding a digit** — it is the roll's own spelling of the same part — and that distinction is what keeps
this consistent with M-119's rule that normalisation NEVER folds digits.

- **The instrument's figure: 80 of LEG's 121 distinct APN values are in the roll.**
- **A dedicated join on the padded form alone** (`tools/m132-apn-join.py`, re-run to completion, exit 0):
  102 canonical keys from the 32,976 matters, **69 present in the roll, 33 absent.**
- **M-119's own two APNs are among the ABSENT.** `011-0836-017-00` and `011-0836-018-00` — the Walker Avenue
  sewer easement vacation, matter 03-0339, 2003 — **are not in the current roll.**
- **The 33 misses cluster hard: 18 of them are in book 008, and 13 of those in page 0644** — downtown Oakland,
  matters from 2003–2004 about redevelopment takings and easement vacations.

**What that means, and it is a finding the row did not anticipate: the APN space is shared but TIME-VARYING.** An
APN is retired when the parcel is merged, split or taken, and the roll published today is the roll as it is today,
not as it was. So **a 2003 Legistar APN failing to resolve in a 2026 roll is not a broken identifier and not a
matcher failure — it is the identifier working correctly on a parcel that no longer exists.** A recogniser that
treats a miss as "no such parcel" will be wrong in exactly the cases that matter most to this project, because
takings, vacations and mergers are what the legislative record is about. **A join across this space needs the roll
of the right vintage, and this measurement did not establish whether any historical roll is published.** That is
the next unknown, and it is named, not closed.

**The institutional caution, stated because the instrument's label does not carry it.** The APN is assigned by the
**Alameda County Assessor**, not by Oakland; dataset `c3xp-qcgn` is Oakland's republication of it, and the portal
gives it **no attribution and no description** (both fields empty — checked). So an LEG ↔ ASR pair is a
**cross-institution** match, which is the interesting kind for §8.3, but **whether `c3xp-qcgn` is a copy of the
county roll or Oakland's own GIS layer derived from it is UNDETERMINED from the portal's metadata**, and this
measurement did not read the county's own assessor system to settle it. The instrument counts ASR alongside the
Oakland systems; that choice is visible in its `SYSTEMS READ` line and should be read with this paragraph.
Accela was reachable at 200 and **was not read**, so the APN ↔ permit half of (c) is **NOT MEASURED**.

### What the wider corpus found wrong with the instrument

Four defects, each found by RUNNING M-119's instrument at 80× the corpus, each corrected here with its reason at
the site and an arm in the selftest. **Three of the four make the record claim LESS than it can support — the safe
direction — and the fourth made it claim nothing at all by not terminating.**

1. **The enactment-number field accepted only a bare `\d{4,5}`, dropping 8,470 of 17,692 (47.9%).** Legistar
   writes `MatterEnactmentNumber` five ways: bare, `NNNNN CMS` (5,330), `NNNNN C.M.S.` (1,159), `NNNNN C.M.S`
   (705), `CMS NNNNN` (68), plus non-enactment forms (`9999-9999`, `2000-58`). **7,370 of the dropped values were
   C.M.S.-suffixed — the exact spelling M-119's RUNNING-TEXT pattern already folds.** So one half of the
   instrument folded the marker and the other half required its absence. Corrected via `enactment_value()`; the
   selftest gains four arms, including that `2000-58` is **REFUSED, not coerced**.
2. **The leading-zero near-miss scan was O(n²) and does not terminate at scale.** Nested over every pair of
   distinct values: unnoticeable on M-119's corpus, ~1.5×10¹¹ comparisons with the 394,597-row parcel roll. **The
   first run of this measurement was killed after 10 minutes with no output.** Grouping by the zero-stripped key
   is the same relation in one pass: **14.0s for the whole corpus.** The old code was not wrong, it was
   unrunnable — **which for an instrument is the same defect**, because a figure nobody can produce is a figure
   nobody has.
3. **A CSV column was pinned to one spelling of its name.** Socrata's *export* heads the column `Fund Code`;
   Socrata's *API* heads the same column `fund_code`. M-119's corpus came from an export and this one from the
   API, so the FY13-15 budget — 17,862 rows, the one file whose row count reproduces M-119 exactly — **scored
   ZERO project and ZERO fund values on the first run, silently, with no complaint.** That is precisely this
   project's "a classifier grading one literal" failure and it cost the entire `project` class one run: **the run
   before the fix reported `project SHARED 0`, which read exactly like a real negative result.** Corrected with
   `col()`, comparing on letters and digits only; two arms added, one of them the over-strictness arm.
4. **The APN pattern could not express the roll's form**, so the class's two systems could never meet — see (c).
   Corrected with `apn_key()`; four arms added.
