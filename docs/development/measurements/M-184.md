## M-184 · 2026-09-25 · D-685 — A UNIT OVER THE ACQUIRE WIRE'S BUDGET: DROPPED WHOLE BEFORE, CARRIED AS ITS CAPPED PREFIX AFTER

**Headline.** The acquire wire's budget loop (`textUnitsFor`, `bio-plane/src/index.mjs`) charged each unit's
WHOLE text against `ACQUIRE_TEXT_UNITS_BUDGET` (524,288 B, plus a 128 B envelope per unit) and dropped any
unit that did not fit, though `#writeCaptureText` keeps only a unit's first `CAPTURE_TEXT_UNIT_CAP` (131,072)
characters. **Measured BEFORE: a workbook of ONE 600,599 B sheet reached the store as NO unit** — acquired
with `text_units` absent and `text_units_over_bound: 1`, promoted `indexed_partial` over zero indexed units,
and its first row unfindable through `passage:`. AFTER: the same sheet is carried as its first 131,072
characters marked `truncated: true`, the store writes it with `truncated = 1`, and `passage:` finds its first
row on that one unit. The diagnosis at the code (D-672's worker) is confirmed; it reproduces.

**What the budget was spent on BEFORE:** in Z4 the first sheet charged 300,427 B (text + envelope), of
which the store kept 131,072 B — 169,227 B of the wire's budget, 32 % of it, bought text that was thrown
away one step later, and it was that spend that pushed the second sheet out.

### What is still dropped — the residue, by arithmetic from the constants and confirmed by Z5

A full-cap unit costs 131,072 + 128 = 131,200 B. Three cost 393,600 B and leave 130,688 B, so **a fourth
full-cap unit does not fit and is dropped whole, counted into `text_units_over_bound`, and the capture
reads `partial`**; a later unit that does fit is still carried (Z5's S5). So one capture carries at most
three units at the cap, plus whatever smaller units fit. The residue is not cut to the remaining budget:
the unit is carried at the cap or not at all.

### A finding on the way, recorded

The first cut of the fix EXPORTED `CAPTURE_TEXT_UNIT_CAP` from `store.mjs` for the wire to import. The gate
went RED on 22 plane suites: workerd reads every named export of a MAIN module as an entrypoint, and those
suites load `store.mjs` as the worker's main module, so the runtime refused to start (`Incorrect type for
map entry 'CAPTURE_TEXT_UNIT_CAP'`). The constant now lives in `checks/bio-checks.mjs`, which both files
already import; the 22 suites are green again, and this suite and its controls were re-run with the same results.

### Blind spots

- ASCII only. The cut is in characters (the store's `slice`) and the charge in UTF-8 bytes, so a unit of
  multi-byte text is charged up to 3× its cap in bytes (393,216 B at worst) — asserted by nothing here.
  `slice` can also split a surrogate pair at the cap, exactly as the store's own cut always could.
- `.xlsx` only. The loop is shared by every unit arm (`pdf-page`, `doc-para`, `slide-shape`,
  `sheet-range`), so the change reaches them all; only the sheet arm is driven over the budget here, as a
  sheet is the unit that reaches the cap (M-20's largest non-sheet unit was 21,224 B).
- How many live captures lost a unit this way is UNDETERMINED; a capture is corrected only when re-promoted.
