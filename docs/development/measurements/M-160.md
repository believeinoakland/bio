**A second defect, found by this sweep and NOT fixed here — D-591.** `CAFR-2009` holds **163 pages at 0 characters with no
page marker**, the same *read-and-empty* class, from a different cause: their content streams do not decode.
`pageShowsText` answered `null` on all 163, and the instrument traced it: page 0's stream is `/FlateDecode` with
`/Length 687`, the zlib stream ends at byte 686, and the 687th byte is `0x0a`. Node's `zlib.inflateSync` inflates it to
1,488 bytes (`/GS1 gs BT /TT2 1 Tf …` — a real text layer); `DecompressionStream("deflate")`, which `pdfstructure.mjs`'s
`inflate` uses, refuses it `Trailing junk found after the end of the compressed stream`, and the `deflate-raw` fallback
fails too. Tier 1 therefore records a document-level `content_stream_undecodable` note and nothing on the page. **Named
fix:** `inflate` tolerates bytes after the end of the compressed stream (retry with trailing PDF whitespace trimmed, or
keep the output a strict decoder produced before the trailing-data error), and a page whose content stream Tier 1
could not decode carries a page marker naming it, so it can never read as read-and-empty. Minted D-591 and sent to
SCHEDULER; out of D-585's scope, which is the text-showing predicate.
