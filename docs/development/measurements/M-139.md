### 5. THE FLOOR THAT DID NOT MOVE, AND WHY THAT IS THE FINDING

`check-refusal-codes.mjs` first printed **`r3Fed` measured 81 against a floor of 80** and FAILED. The
+1 was **not** a hand-off. `partitionSuiteLiterals` harvests `/["'`]([A-Z][A-Z0-9_]{2,})["'`]/g` over a
suite's WHOLE SOURCE — **comments included** — so two block comments this item wrote, which name
UNKNOWN_OP and REQUIRED_ARGUMENT_MISSING in backticks as ordinary prose, were counted as codes those
suites FEED to a surface. **A floor that rises because somebody wrote a sentence is not a ratchet**, and
it would have fallen again the moment anybody reworded the comment. The codes were unquoted in the
prose, with the reason recorded at both sites, and the guard then printed **`r3Fed` measured 80, slack
0, exit 0** — matching the pristine base exactly. No floor moved, no ceiling moved, `untranslated`
stands at 300.

**Both suites really do FEED those codes now** — through `unknownOpWire` and `requiredArgumentWire` —
and the walk cannot see either, for the reason UI-84 recorded as its finding (2): it keys on literals
and these arrive through a function. **So the walk is wrong in BOTH directions at once**, which is one
cause and two defects: it UNDERCOUNTS a code fed through a variable (UI-84's, rowed as M0-148) and it
OVERCOUNTS a code merely NAMED in a comment (this one). **The fix is one change and it is nameable:**
blank the `/* */` and `//` spans out of `src` before the harvest in `partitionSuiteLiterals`, using the
parenthesis-span technique the same function already runs for `obsSpans`. That closes the overcount
outright; the undercount needs the separate structural move M0-148 names.

### 7. Three arms came back wrong before they came back right, and none is smoothed

1. **ARM A was GREEN against a RED declaration.** It retyped the `op=queue` fixture by hand — but the
   drive that renders `.q-feed-why` is `makePlane({ resAbsent:true })`, the TASKS feed, so it patched a

   have read as proof that the REACH assertions were watertight. Re-pointed at `resAbsent`: RED 5 of 145.
2. **ARM D killed `case-frozen-pair` against a green declaration.** Renaming `DISPATCH_CHECKS` in the
   catalogue alone does not rename a family — `index.mjs` IMPORTS it by name and `dispatchRow` reads it,
   so the rename STOPS THE PLANE and every suite that boots one through miniflare dies at
   `ERR_RUNTIME_FAILURE`. It is UI-84's own arm-C finding one level out. The arm now renames at all
   three sites that NAME the family (the catalogue's export, the plane's import, and
   `refusal-translation-surface`'s import), leaving one variable unmoved: whether a lookup that
   DISCOVERS the family still finds the row. It does — GREEN in all eight.
3. **ARM E killed `publishedcase` too, which it had declared safe.** `assertDerived()` runs at IMPORT
   and checks BOTH halves of the wire, so every importer stops, not only the ones reading the broken
   half. Stronger than declared, and the declaration is corrected rather than the behaviour.
