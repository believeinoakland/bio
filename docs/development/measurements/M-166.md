## M-166 · 2026-09-25 · D-515: NO HELD PDF PAGE HAS TIER 2 DECODING FEWER GLYPHS THAN TIER 1. THE SEARCH IS EMPTY, over 19 documents and 2,107 pages

**Headline.** D-515 asked for one real page where tier 2 (`unpdf`, the `pdf-worker` member) decodes strictly
**fewer glyphs** than tier 1 (`pdfstructure.mjs`), so that D-501's degradation arm (the award keeping tier 1 by glyph
count) could be driven on a real page instead of a synthetic one. **I searched every PDF the record holds and
found none.** The population was all 8 committed PDF fixtures (52 pages) and all 11 distinct PDF captures in store
`bio` on instance `biosmoke7` (2,055 pages by tier 2's count). On **0 of 2,107 pages** does tier 2 decode fewer
glyphs than tier 1. On 1,841 it decodes more, and on 266 the two counts are equal. The degradation arm therefore
stays synthetic (`tier-pagewise.test.mjs`, "THE RULE ITSELF"), and this file is why. **REPORT-ONLY (W82):** no gate
reads these figures. They record a search, and a later capture could change the answer.

**Why it comes out this way, as far as the figures show it.** On this corpus the two engines are not symmetric.
Where tier 1 reads a page at all, tier 2 recovers the same glyphs or more. Where the two differ, the gap runs one way:
tier 1 under-reads. Some of that it admits (undetermined counts) and some it does not (D-608, below). A page where
tier 2 genuinely loses text would need tier 2 to fail on something tier 1 handles, and nothing held exercises that.

  spelled independently of `textchain.mjs`'s `glyphCount`.
- **Pairing:** page index against page index. A page absent on one side counts as 0 glyphs there, so a page tier 2

- **Node v26.10.0**, 2026-09-25, logs written 03:53Z–03:55Z, cloud session WORKER D-515. The harness was
  `d515-census.mjs` in the session scratchpad. It is not committed (W15), and its logic is the four bullets above.
- **One harness deviation, stated.** `unpdf`'s pdf.js called `Math.sumPrecise`, which this Node lacks. Only

  document the harness installed a naive-sum polyfill, only when the function was absent, and re-ran. The polyfill is

### D-608, found on the way: TIER 1 DOES NOT READ TEXT INSIDE FORM XOBJECTS, AND DOES NOT SAY SO

This finding runs in the opposite direction to the row, and the census surfaced it. On **1,464 held pages**, tier 2
decodes more glyphs than tier 1 on a page tier 1 read (g1>0), while tier 1 reports **zero** undetermined characters.
So the shipped rule keeps tier 1 there, correctly by its own terms, and a reader sees no sign that anything is
missing. The sizes of those gaps, relative to tier 2's count:

| document | <1% | 1–10% | 10–50% | ≥50% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| INFO-2026-0104 adopted budget book | 797 | 53 | 4 | 0 |
| INFO-2026-0301 FY23-25 budget book | 564 | 41 | 2 | 0 |
| INFO-2026-0103 ACFR FY2023-24 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 1 |

**Diagnosed on the ≥10% pages, all 9 of them.** Every one carries text-showing operators inside a Form XObject,
counted from pdf.js's operator list (`showText`/`showSpacedText` between `paintFormXObjectBegin` and `…End`).
ACFR FY2023-24 p38 has 34 of its 40 text-show operators inside forms. Tier 1 reads 80 glyphs there, the page's
running header, while tier 2 reads 546, the header plus every label of the page's two pie charts. Tier 1's
`extractPageText` interprets no `Do`. The only `Do` interpreter in `pdfstructure.mjs` is CPDF-18's image walk,
which already descends into Form XObjects through `/Matrix` and `/Resources`. The page therefore reads as fully
decoded while missing most of its text, and the record claims more than it holds (`CLAUDE.md` §2). **The fix, named:**
the tier-1 text walk descends into Form XObjects the way the image walk does. At the least, a page whose painted
Form XObject carries text-showing operators tier 1 did not read gets a page marker with a count, so condition (1)
can see it. **Not diagnosed:** the 1,361 pages under 1% and the 94 between 1 and 10%. They may be the same cause at
small scale, or a different glyph-representation difference such as ligatures or separate accents. That is
undetermined.

### What this measurement can and cannot see

**CAN:** every page of every PDF the repository commits and every PDF capture `biosmoke7`'s `bio` store holds,
both tiers, one counter, with a control showing the census flags a planted degradation.

**CANNOT:** pages it does not hold (other instances, the probe's live Legistar sample). Tier 2 as the deployed member
runs it: this is `unpdf` under Node, not under workerd, and CPDF-12 measured the two runtimes differing on a platform
service. Fidelity: a page where tier 2 emits the same count of different or wrong glyphs is scored equal, and only
CPDF-13's calibration can see that.
