### What the class IS, judged from bodies

**A FINANCIAL REPORT reports the ACTUALS of a period that has ENDED, on an auditor's authority.** Money density is
NECESSARY (≥ 40 amounts and ≥ 4 per 100 words) — for D-66's own reason and its receipt, that a staff report *transmitting*
the ACFR self-names in its subject line and holds no statements of its own. It is then joined by ONE of three:

- **self-naming in a title line** — ACFR/CAFR, single audit report, audited/basic/annual financial statements;
- **an auditor's opinion**, ≥ 2 of 6 (independent auditor's report · we have audited · in our opinion · present fairly, in
  all material respects · Government Auditing Standards · auditing standards generally accepted);
- **the audited-statement spine**, ≥ 3 of 10 (statement of net position/activities/cash flows, statement of revenues,
  expenditures and changes in fund balance, notes to the financial statements, MD&A, RSI, balance sheet, GASB, GAAP),
  **together with a period that has ENDED**, ≥ 2 occurrences.

**That last conjunct is the plan/actuals line itself**, and it is what keeps a budget book out: an adopted budget carries
fund-balance schedules, a balance sheet, governmental funds and GASB in its notes — three spine hits — and names a period
that has NOT ended. A control arm drives it both ways on one text.

**THE EXCLUSION SITS INSIDE THE BUDGET TYPE, not beside it.** `budget_arm` calls `is_financial_report` directly, so
neutering the `financial_report` class does NOT re-admit an ACFR to the budget count, and ARM 5's *"neutering one class
touches no other"* stays true rather than convenient. `budget_arm_d66` is kept beside it — D-66's arm exactly — so the
recount is a difference the instrument PRINTS. **The DATASET arm is untouched on purpose:** a dataset is a FORM (a table of
records), not a subject, and a workbook of audited figures is honestly both; such a document is reported MULTI-CLASS.
