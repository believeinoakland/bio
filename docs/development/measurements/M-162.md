**WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW — and it is the larger finding.** A perfect score from two model families, agreeing on every
pair, says the 26-pair synthetic corpus **no longer discriminates**: every pair in it is easy for a model. The corpus
was written by one hand, in clean single sentences, with every contradiction explicit (a negation, *only* against a
counter-case, two dates for one deadline). Real documents are long, hedged, and contradict each other across pages. So
0/17 here is a floor any competent model clears, not evidence of how the judgement does on captured text, and the
THRESHOLD (0) remains M-118's provisional one. Owed, and not built here: a corpus drawn from REAL captured documents,
several times larger, with hard negatives (a figure that changed legitimately between dates, a summary that omits a
qualifier) — §7's Incomplete note already names it; this measurement is the reason it is now the binding gap.

**A recording is a measurement of the runs it records.** The suite re-scores the recorded answers on every battery;
it does not re-run a model. An edited prompt fails the suite by its digest; a changed corpus text leaves a pair the
recording cannot answer, and the gate fails `JUDGEMENT_ABSENT` by name. Either means: re-run, record, and write a new
measurement.
