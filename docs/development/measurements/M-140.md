## M-140 · 2026-09-24 · D-501 — THE TIER-2 AWARD'S MARGIN IS WHITESPACE: ON EVERY FIXTURE PAGE TIER 1 READ, BOTH TIERS DECODE THE IDENTICAL GLYPHS

**Headline.** `perPageTierWinner` awarded a page to tier 2 only when tier 2 had *strictly more decoded
characters*, and it measured that with `text.length` — the whole string, whitespace and all. Over the four
committed CPDF-20 PDFs, **on all six pages tier 1 read in full the two tiers decode the SAME number of
non-whitespace code points** (1503/1503, 2601/2601, 914/914, 2856/2856, 915/915, 486/486). Every margin the
old instrument saw on those pages — **40, 39, 22, 4, 104 and 77 characters** — **is whitespace, all of it.**
So the second condition was deciding the fixture's own degradation trap on a whitespace artifact. **Under the
corrected counter (non-whitespace code points) ZERO of the 15 awards change**: the fix removes a fragility, it
does not move a page. What it removes is measured below.

**Two things this says.** (1) `strip` alone flips the award, which is the defect demonstrated rather than
argued, and it is the arm `nc-cpdf20.mjs` A7 fires on. (2) `collapse` — a policy any second decoder might
plausibly have — leaves the old instrument holding the trap page by a margin of **ONE character**. The margin
was 129 before D-481 and 77 after; nobody had to intend anything for it to reach 0.

### What this measurement can and cannot see

**CAN:** every page of the four committed PDFs, both tiers, both instruments, in one run.

**CANNOT — and this is the finding a future reader needs.** **This corpus contains no page where tier 2
genuinely decodes FEWER glyphs than tier 1.** On every page tier 1 read at all, the counts are equal; on every
page tier 1 failed, tier 2 has far more. The suite's degradation control has therefore never been driven by a
real degradation — it was driven by a whitespace difference that looked like one. The second condition is
still load-bearing (it is what stops a whitespace "gain" from moving a page, which `strip` proves), but a page
where tier 2 loses real text is **not among these four PDFs**, and synthetic arms are the only place that case
is exercised. Four documents is the whole corpus here.

**CANNOT:** distinguish code points from UTF-16 units on anything real — all 15 pages are BMP-only
(code-point count equals `.length`), asserted in the suite so the claim fails rather than goes stale when a
fixture changes. That half of the counter is driven synthetically and by nothing in the wild.
