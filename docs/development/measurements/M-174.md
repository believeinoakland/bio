**Why 0301 moved by flag and not by glyph.** Every text-bearing page of 0301 draws its folio, a three-digit page
number, in a form that uses a simple Arial font with no `/ToUnicode`. Tier 1 decodes no simple font without one.
Tier 2 does, from the standard encoding. There are now 642 `no_tounicode|Arial` markers, 1,818 characters, where
before there were none. These pages were short by the folio and said nothing. Now they say so, and §5.2's condition
(1), u2<u1, can award them to tier 2. Decoding simple fonts without `/ToUnicode` is not this row's work.

**Why 24 of 0104's pages are left, diagnosed.** For each of the 24 I compared the two tiers' per-page multisets of
non-whitespace characters. On all 24 the whole difference is **exactly** ligature expansion: tier 1 holds `ﬃ` or
`ﬁ` code points, and tier 2 holds the same count of `f`, `i` (and `f`, `f`) in their place. No character is missing.
It is a representation difference that scores as a glyph gap. It is not a defect of reading, and it is not minted.

### What else moved, stated

- **`no_text_layer` on 9 pages of 0301 (page indices 633, 634 and 645–651) is gone.** The same folio forms sit on 9
  full-page images. Before, those pages had no font at page level, so tier 1 read nothing and marked them
  `no_text_layer`. Now it sees the folio (3 codes, `no_tounicode`). Nine pages bearing text is the truth, and D-585's
  `pageShowsText` (on `land/worker/D-585`, **not on `main` at `5e8a65a8`**) would say the same, because it descends
  into forms.
  - **Consequence, read from the code and not driven through an op:** this document does not escalate to tier 2.
    Its 150,887 undetermined markers are fewer than its 964,612 glyphs. So `needsTier3` over its tier-1 text no
    longer finds a `no_text_layer`, and these 9 pages are no longer routed to OCR.
  - **Tier 2 already treated them this way:** it reads the folio and emits no `no_text_layer`.
  - Whether a page whose only text is a folio over a full-page image wants OCR is a **design question, routed to BOB**.
    It is not minted, because the fix cannot be named without a rule.
- **`Td` with `ty = 0` now asks the device baseline**, as `Tm` always did. Without this a form, which enters through
  `cm`/`/Matrix` and starts `BT 0 0 Td`, glued its first line to the page's. **On all 1,840 real pages above plus the 8
  committed fixtures, it moved 0 pages.** Only the synthetic suite exercises it.
- **Committed fixtures:** all 8 committed PDFs give a byte-identical `extractPdfStructure` output before and after,
