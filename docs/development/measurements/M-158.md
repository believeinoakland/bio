### What the drive measured

- **An unpinned leg rests on the PROJECTION's history, not on its bytes.** The same leg bytes (`target: INFO-…-doc`,
  no pin) projected BEFORE a later capture rested on capture A (`b73ecfd1…`), and projected AFTER it rested on capture
  B (`a7100a35…`). The first question kept A across a re-promotion only because REC-82's carry-forward copied the
  prior row; a rebuild from bytes re-derives it. Printed: *"Q2 (unpinned, first projected before B) rests on b73ecfd1
  … Q4 (the SAME leg bytes, projected after B) rests on a7100a35"*.
- **The resolver's "earliest" is not the earliest HELD** (D-580). It orders `register.registered` (the server's
  registration instant) and `readings.at` (a reading's OWN date, from the provenance bytes) in one column, so a
  capture the record came to hold LATER, of a document dated earlier, sorts first. That is what moved the fixture.
- **A re-registration does NOT move it.** The first fixture re-sent capture A's register entry to overwrite
  `registered`; the resolver's answer did not change (the suite's arming assertion failed and the hypothesis was
  dropped). Recorded because the upsert's `registered=excluded.registered` reads as if it would.
- **The pin holds where the projection cannot help.** A leg pinned by `op=cite`, copied into a NEW question after
  the later capture (no prior row to carry), resolves to A — the same content row. Control `nostamp` (no pin
  written) makes it resolve to B; control `newest` (the resolver ignores the pin) does the same.
