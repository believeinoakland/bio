## M-182 · 2026-09-25 · D-665: NO MEASURED SIGNAL SEPARATES A CHART UNDER A TITLE FROM A PHOTO PAGE, SO ROUTING STAYS OFF. THE PER-IMAGE `image_unread` FLOOR IS 0.001, IN A 127x GAP ABOVE 781 BULLETS

**Headline.** BOB #35 (2026-09-25 06:25Z) ruled two things for the third class M-178 found, a chart or table painted as
an image under a text title. First, the true statement is per IMAGE: every painted image above a size floor carries
`image_unread` with its rect and area share. Second, ROUTING that class to OCR waits on one measured signal, "image
pixel dimensions against the page's text area, or glyph density outside the painted rects", over M-178's 49
classified pages. I measured both.

- **Routing: neither signal separates the classes, so routing stays off.** Of the 49 pages, 17 are charts, tables or
  text bodies painted as an image under a title (C), 12 are memo images beside text (M), and 20 are photo or graphic
  pages (P). At its best single threshold, every variant of the two signals misclassifies at least **5 of the 37 C and
  P pages** (and at least 9 of 49 when M counts with C). The ranges overlap in every figure. `image_unread` is NOT a
  tier-3 reason. `d665-image-unread.test.mjs` pins that it routes nothing (CHART ROUTE, PHOTO WALL).
- **The floor: 0.001 of the page's visible box.** The three held documents paint **1,104 placements**. **781** of them
  are 12x12-pixel bullets of 5.4 to 5.5 points, with shares of at most **0.0000624**. The smallest other placement has
  **0.0079** (a 68x56-point photo, 0104 p481). Nothing falls between, a 127x gap. Every floor in the gap gives the same
  split. 0.001 sits nearer the bullets, so an image smaller than any measured one is stated rather than hidden.
- **The marker as built:** 323 `image_unread` markers on 209 pages (0301: 170 on 102; 0104: 144 on 98; 0103: 9 on 9).
  **All 49 classified pages carry at least one, all 17 C pages among them.** The class M-178 said "reads no marker"
  now reads one per image. The count of D-627's page marker and of the decode markers is unchanged (0301: 150,896 non-image
  markers against 964,612 glyphs, as M-178), and no document's tier-2 routing moves.

**GATED, not report-only (W82).** `bio-plane/test/d665-image-unread.test.mjs` pins the floor (0.001), the bullets and
both floor edges (0.0012 stated, 0.0008 not), the rect and share of each marker, and through the op, that the chart is

### What the marker does downstream, and who else reads it

- **`needsTier2`** compared the marker count with the glyphs. The new markers are not undecoded characters. Without a
  correction, a page of photos with short captions would escalate on its picture count (the suite's PHOTO WALL: 30
  photos, 25 glyphs). It now reads the text through `decodeView`, which takes `image_unread` out.
- **`readText`** (`docprofile/readtext.mjs`, FRAMEWORK's) takes `counts.undetermined` as the undecodable regions.
  It FAILS a reading when they outnumber the decoded characters, and names the count in the basis. It is handed the
  same `decodeView` at both call sites in `index.mjs`, so its sentence and its verdict are unchanged.
  `docprofile/**` is not edited.
- **`needsTier3` / `tier3Pages`**: `image_unread` is not in `TIER3_REASONS`, so nothing routes on it.
- **`mergeTier3Text`**: a page OCR fills takes the engine's markers, so its `image_unread` is discharged. That is
  right, because the pass read the page. **D-635's APPEND** must do the same for a page it appends to.
- **`mergeTier2Text`**: a page tier 2 wins takes tier 2's markers and LOSES `image_unread`, which is still true of
  it. **D-633's carry must include `image_unread`** with the `image_content_*` markers. Neither function is edited here.
- **`op=pdfstructure`** returns the markers (THE OP, through the op).
- **Suites corrected, not exempted, each with its reason at the site:** `cpdf18-pdf-images` (its text digest is taken
  with these markers removed, and equals the old pin exactly), `d627-image-content` (SCAN), `reextract` (the scan's
  plain-read literal is re-taken, with a kept assertion that removing these markers yields D-536's literal exactly),
  `tier3-layer-parts` (the scan page's reasons, and the escalation input as 22 raw and 21 decode markers).

### What this cannot see

- The classes are one reader's, by eye, at 45 dpi, over three documents from one publisher. They are the population
  BOB named. Another publisher's photos or charts could sit elsewhere, but the ranges here overlap too far for that to
  rescue a threshold on this corpus.
- A signal from the image's own pixels (colour count, flatness) was not measured: tier 1 has no image decoder, and
  37 of the 49 largest images are JPEG 2000. That is a design question, not a threshold. It is in the report.
- A placement's share is its painted extent. `/BBox` and soft-mask clipping are not modelled (CPDF-18's caveat), so
  a clipped image can be stated larger than it shows.
- A page whose `images` list is NULL (encrypted, or a walk that did not finish) gets no `image_unread`, as in M-178.
