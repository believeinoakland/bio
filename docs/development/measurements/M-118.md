**THE THRESHOLD: 0.** No `precision` or `unrelated` pair of this corpus may be labelled `world` or `record`, on any key or
over all. It is set by this measurement (§7) for three reasons. The first candidate measured 0/17. Each key holds only 4
or 5 negatives, so any threshold under 20% already means zero on a key. And §1 names a false conflict as the failure that
gets the detector switched off. **It is provisional.** Reversing it is one constant (`THRESHOLD` in
`contradiction-gate.mjs`) and a later entry in this file. The alternative, a non-zero rate, only means something on a
corpus several times this size.

**What the gate cannot see, measured rather than assumed.** An always-`precision` judgement PASSES the false-conflict
gate with recall 0 (the suite prints it). §7 makes recall a figure reported BESIDE the gate, not part of it, so the recall
column is what catches a detector that is strict by saying nothing. The baseline reached 0/17 largely by abstaining: 14 of
26 pairs came back `undetermined`, which §5 permits (never coerced) and which is not a conflict. Its two correct positives
were both negation cases (`k2-never`, `k4-rule`). It missed every change-of-state, deadline and date-contradiction shape. One miss is the opposite error: `k3-due` (June 30 against September 30) was labelled `precision`, because the rule sees the same day number on both sides and cannot read the month.
