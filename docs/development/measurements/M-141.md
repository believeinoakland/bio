(−2.69, −3.14, −8.39, −15.59), so one constant applied to the magnitude separates both directions.

### THE SWEEP, which is the part that says the constant is not load-bearing

Re-reading the seven text-bearing documents at seventeen values of `WORD_GAP_EM`:

| threshold (em) | tokens | words | glue | 1-char tokens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | **17,292** | 12,707 | 17 | **4,578** |
| 0.0005 | 14,067 | 13,409 | 17 | 652 |
| 0.01 | 14,044 | 13,399 | 17 | 639 |
| 0.03 … 0.20 | 14,041 | 13,398 | 17 | 638 |
| **0.25 … 1.0** | **14,039** | 13,396 | 17 | 638 |
| 3.0 | 14,033 | 13,390 | 17 | 638 |
| ∞ (the rule off — D-481) | 13,981 | 13,338 | **57** | 638 |

**The reading is flat from 0.03 to 1.0 — two tokens in 14,039 across a factor of thirty — and the glue count is
17 at EVERY finite value.** So the value chosen is not what does the work; what does the work is being clear of
the ONE cliff, at exactly 0, where float noise in the matrix arithmetic becomes a word gap and the corpus reads
**17,292 tokens with 4,578 of them a single character long**. 0.25 em is the valley's midpoint and is what a
space is in a text face (0.25–0.33 em) — two independent readings meeting.

### What this can and cannot see

- **Nine documents is the whole corpus**, and eight of the nine are City of Oakland PDFs from two producers
  (Legistar's writer and Skia/PDF). A producer whose per-glyph placement carries systematic rounding at, say,
  0.3 em would fragment under this constant and is not represented here. **The sweep is the guard**: it is
  re-runnable over any corpus, and its shape — a flat plateau with one cliff at zero — is what a reader should
  check on a new one, not the number.
- **The glue counter cannot tell a concatenation from a compound.** The 17 that survive across the corpus include
  `eComment`, `CalTrans`, `McGuire`, `HiiiWAV.`, `CopLink/Crimetracer` — the documents' own words. It also cannot
  see two lowercase runs glued (`ofthe`), which is M-133's own stated blind spot and is unchanged.
- **A font that declares no widths is not read differently at all**, by design, so any document whose fonts omit
  `/Widths` keeps D-481's reading exactly. Its share of the corpus is not counted here.
- **Cell boundaries within a line are still not marked.** Two table columns on one baseline now read as two
  tokens separated by a space; nothing says they were two cells. That is a layout question this item did not
  answer and did not pretend to.
