### What remains UNDETERMINED, stated rather than rounded off

**Whether each of the thirteen forwards actually carries a code ON THE WIRE is measured for the ops the sweep
reaches and UNDETERMINED for the ops it does not.** The sweep drives MEMBER-class ops only, and this walk is
STATIC — it reads the forward, never the source it forwards. Three things follow and none of them is closed
here:

1. A forward from a store answer that itself refuses without a code would arrive codeless, and neither the
   static walks (no literal) nor the member-class sweep (if the op is outside the class) would see it. **Not
   measured. Not asserted either way.**
2. `op=purge` was exactly this residue in its other form — a codeless refusal on a non-member-class op — and
   REC-185 closed it by DRIVING it (section 6b), not by widening the sweep. **The rest of the non-member-class
   surface is still undriven by this suite**, and that is the same gap one op narrower.
3. The undecidable site L11418 is undetermined by construction, not by omission.

**The named fix for (1) and (2)**, for SCHEDULER to place rather than for this entry to hold: drive the
sweep's `record()` over the ADMIN-class and PROBE-class ops as well as the member-class ones, so the pinned

will surface findings that belong to other owners, which is why it is a row and not a line in this item.
