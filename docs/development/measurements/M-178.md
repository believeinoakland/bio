## M-178 · 2026-09-25 · D-627: WHERE IMAGE-ONLY PAGES SEPARATE FROM TEXT PAGES. 17 OF 1,788 HELD PAGES SHOW AT MOST A FOLIO OVER A PAINTED IMAGE, AND ALL 17 ARE IMAGES OF CONTENT

**Headline.** BOB #35 (2026-09-25 05:50Z) ruled that a page whose painted images cover at least a measured share of
its area, while its text is under a measured glyph floor, carries `image_content_unread` and is routed to OCR. I
measured both figures on every page of the FY23-25 budget book and of M-174's other two documents.

- **1,788 pages; 307 paint an image.**
- **17 of those 307 show at most 4 glyphs.** I looked at every one of the 17 rendered. All 17 are images of content:
  tables, a certificate, a screenshot of a web page, an organisation chart. Their image shares run from **0.1897 to
  0.6542**.
- **The other 290 show at least 22 glyphs.** No page that paints an image shows 5 to 21 glyphs.
- **So the thresholds are: at most 4 glyphs, and a share of at least 0.18.** Every glyph threshold from 5 to 21, and
  every share threshold from 0.01 to 0.18, gives the same split of this corpus. The ones chosen sit at the measured
  edges of the image-only class. The gaps between the classes read `image_content_undetermined`, and no measured
  page falls in them.
- **INFO-2026-0301's pages 633, 634 and 645–651 carry `image_content_unread` and are routed to OCR** (the row's
  accepts-when). The 8 other marked pages are in the ACFR (3) and the adopted budget book (5).
- **The two figures cannot see a third class** (below): a chart painted as an image under a text title.

**GATED, not report-only (W82).** `bio-plane/test/d627-image-content.test.mjs` pins the thresholds (4, 0.18, 22),
the nine real pages' figures, and each edge (4 vs 5 glyphs, 21 vs 22, share 0.20 vs 0.15). A threshold that moves
fails there by name.

### Instrument

- **Code:** `bio-plane/src/pdfstructure.mjs` as landed on `land/worker/D-627` (sha256 `a83d9e45…5707f54`), stacked on
  D-608 (`ffcc300b`), run with `extractPdfStructure` in Node v26.10.0 (cloud session WORKER D-627).
- **Image share:** a page's painted images are CPDF-18's `images` (D-420 stores the same list as
  `container_extent.images`). Each rectangle is clipped to the page's visible box: CropBox inside MediaBox, both
  inherited. The share is the area of the UNION of the clipped rectangles over the box's area, so an overlap
  counts once and a part off the page counts nothing. It is rounded to 4 places.
- **Glyphs:** the text a page SHOWS is its decoded non-whitespace code points (M-166's unit) PLUS the `count` of
  its undetermined markers, which is the characters it shows that tier 1 could not decode. A folio in an Arial with
  no `/ToUnicode` is 3 either way.
- **Bytes:** fetched 2026-09-25 from `biosmoke7` store `bio` with read-only `op=list`, `op=image` and `op=capture`
  GETs and the member token. Each blob matched its sha256 on arrival:
  - INFO-2026-0301 (FY23-25 budget book, 653 pp): `159a0b7e…` + `7e6e1501…`, which reassemble to `3f21377d…`
    (41,502,904 B)
  - INFO-2026-0104 (adopted budget book, 911 pp): `b4ef4149…` (32,521,404 B)
  - INFO-2026-0103 (ACFR FY2023-24, 224 pp): `4cb9b8ad…` (5,995,747 B)

  No write was made. As in M-166 and M-174, no witness counters were read: the no-write claim rests on those ops
  being GETs.
- **Looking at the pages:** I rendered the 17 folio-only pages and 49 of the 70 pages that paint an image and show 22

### The third class, which the two figures cannot see

Among the 49 pages I looked at that paint more than a sliver and show 22 to 400 glyphs, I found three kinds:

- **A chart or table painted as an IMAGE under a text TITLE.** For example 0301 p40 and p41 (organisation charts, 30
  glyphs: the title and folio), p447, 0104 p660, p824 and p492, 0103 p20, 0301 p644 and p629 (a glossary body and a
  debt-limit table as images), 0104 p876, and 0104 p694 and 0301 p286 (charts at 361 and 376 glyphs). Their content
  is unread just as the 17 pages' is.
- **Photo pages:** covers and council-member photos with captions. Here the text layer carries the words.
- **Memo images beside text** (0104 p20–26).

These kinds have the same two figures: shares from 0.10 to 0.56 and glyphs from 22 to about 380. **No glyph floor
and no share threshold separates the first kind from the other two.** So they read no marker. That is what they read
before this row: it claims nothing false, and it states nothing true. It is a DESIGN GAP (a third signal is needed,
such as the image's pixel size against the page's text area), routed to BOB. It is not a threshold to tune.

### What the route does downstream, measured through the op

In a scratch copy of the suite, I let the stub tier-2 member ANSWER (decoding each folio) and let the stub OCR
member answer every page it was asked about:

- **D-633.** When tier 2 wins a page, `mergeTier2Text` (`textchain.mjs`) replaces that page's markers with tier
  2's. `image_content_unread` goes with them, and **the OCR member is never asked**: the nine pages route nowhere.
  0301 as held does not escalate to tier 2 (150,896 markers against 964,612 glyphs), so the row's pages are
  routed. But a document that does escalate loses the route. The nameable fix: carry a base page's
  `image_content_*` markers onto the page tier 2 wins, since they are facts about the images and not the decode.
- **D-635.** `mergeTier3Text` fills a page only when it holds no glyph (its condition 2). A routed page whose folio
  DECODED holds 1 to 4 glyphs, so its transcription is refused and the page keeps its folio. **8 of the 17 measured
  pages are this shape** (0103 p19, p21, p22 and 0104 p11, p38, p170, p881, p882): they are asked about and never
  filled. The 0301 pages are not affected, because their folio is undecoded (0 glyphs). The nameable fix is to fill
  such a page by APPENDING the transcription after its layer text, so nothing is lost. But that gives one page two
  derivations in the chain, which is a design question.

### What this cannot see

- The two figures cannot see the third class above. That is a design gap.
- A page whose `images` list is NULL (an encrypted file, or a walk that did not finish) gets no share and no marker.
- `/BBox` clipping of a form and soft masks are not modelled. The share is the painted extent, not what survives
  clipping (CPDF-18's caveat).
- The 17-page class is from three documents by one publisher. Another publisher's folio could be longer ("Page 12
  of 40" is 9 glyphs). Such a page reads `image_content_undetermined`, not unread, which is the ruling's answer for
  a page the measurement did not reach.
