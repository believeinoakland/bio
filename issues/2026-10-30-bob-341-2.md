# A scale figure in a requirement becomes a module test, and the review makes the job run it in its session

**The flaw.** Nothing in the process separates a measurement (a figure at a scale or on a deployment) from a module test. P7 says every requirement is tested and the tests confirm full compliance, so a requirement that writes a scale figure into its Test line forces the job to run it; the review brief (reviewDecide) counts any unproven figure as a gap; and the job runs it in its session, against P11 (test effort kept as small as possible) and P13.

(2) The review brief: a measurement not yet taken is not a gap. (3) The format check flags a Test line naming a fixture above a set size (e.g. 100,000 rows).
