## Why it is a process flaw

1. `proc merge` merges only a green tip that holds the current `tranche` (INT 3, INT 5). Every BOB commit to `tranche` invalidates every open PR's green result and costs another full gate run.
2. P18 and P19 require BOB to prepare later stages on the tranche branch while a layer runs, without idling. That preparation is a steady stream of `tranche` commits.
3. BOB's Stop hook refuses a turn end with uncommitted work ("PROCESS: uncommitted changes") or unpushed work ("1 commit(s) on tranche not pushed"). So BOB cannot hold its writes back while a gate runs. Background helpers writing files make it worse, because every turn end forces a commit and push.

So the faster BOB prepares, the longer finished jobs wait to merge. With four layer-5 jobs running and more to come, every merge will hit the same race.

## What would fix it (for the lane to judge)

- Let `proc merge` accept a green tip when the only commits on `tranche` since the tip's base are BOB's own build-state paths (`build/` outside module `paths` and `tests`), or regenerated artifacts the gate re-verifies at merge. A merge-queue style "re-test only if the new commits touch code" would do.
- Or keep BOB's preparation on a separate branch folded into `tranche` only at layer close, so `tranche` moves only by merges.
- Or let the Stop hook accept staged-but-unpushed BOB work while a `MERGE` item's gate is running.
