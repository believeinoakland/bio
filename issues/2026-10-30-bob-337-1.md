## Flaw A: the last service of a Provides section takes every line after it

`serviceBlocks` ends a block only at the next `**name(` line, so the last service in a Provides section absorbs everything up to the next `###`. Measured: money's set counts jurisdictions' `combine` block at **50,855 bytes**: `combine` is the last `**name(` line of jurisdictions' Provides (line 75), and the block runs to line 117, before `## Private`. Whether all of those lines are `combine`'s is unknown (the file was not read whole in this session). record-core's `getSetting` counts 5,958 bytes; whether that is its own text is likewise unknown. A measure that charges a job for another service's text overstates its set.

## Flaw B: the 40 KB limit against this product's module sizes

With the measure as now written, 73 % of the remaining modules cannot start. Bringing them under means either trimming requirements by half or more, or splitting some 60 modules (REQ 8), before layers 5 to 11 can run. P6 asks only that one session can read its set whole; 40 KB is a small share of a session's window. Which is meant (split the product to the limit, or set the limit from what a session can read) is the process's to say; until it does, BOB trims and starts what fits.

## What would let the work go on

A ruling on B (the limit, or that splitting to it is intended), and A fixed so a block ends where its service's text ends.
