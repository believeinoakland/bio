## The flaw

`proc shims` writes the gate's `npm ci` step only when `package-lock.json` is at the product root (proc.mjs ~1502: `has('package-lock.json')`). Civicsmith has no root lock; its locked trees are per member: `agent-runner`, `bio-plane`, `file-scanner`, `newgroup`, `ocr-worker`, `pdf-worker`, `reader-container`, `sheet-worker`, `site` (each `<dir>/package-lock.json`), plus `bio-plane/src/journey-check`. The product's own `.github/workflows/regression.yml` installs each (lines 37, 40). So the gate in CI builds every bundle with no dependencies and the artifacts' verify fails on every pull request; tests that load a dependency fail there too (whether the one red of 62 and the users' reds are from this is unknown until the gate runs with dependencies).

INT 3 forbids merging around the gate, so no job on this product can merge until the gate installs what each member locks.

## What would let the work go on

The gate's install step covering every `package-lock.json` the product has (or the product's own install, as `regression.yml` does it), regenerated into `gate.yml` by `proc shims`.
