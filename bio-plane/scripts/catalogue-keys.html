Civicsmith AI catalogue keys

Runs entirely in this tab. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Make the two keys

One press makes both: the catalogue signing key, which signs the catalogue, and the catalogue recovery key, which is kept offline and signs only the catalogue that replaces a lost or stolen signing key. Copy the two public lines into the session, and keep the two private keys apart from each other and from the passphrase.

Both private keys are locked with this passphrase, and loading one back asks for it. Without the passphrase a private key cannot be used.

Load a key you already have

Paste a private key from a previous run, and its passphrase. The key says which of the two it is.

The public lines

The two public lines, together, ready to paste into the session. Public keys are public by design.

Sign a catalogue statement

Paste the catalogue statement exactly as Civicsmith gave it. This page signs nothing else. It first says in words what the statement says; read that, then sign.
