RECOGNITION

Digital Counties Award Winner

Alameda County has once again been recognized as one of the nation's leading digital governments, earning Third Place in the 2026 Digital Counties Survey in the 1 Million or More Population category. The annual survey, conducted by the Center for Digital Government (CDG) in partnership with the National Association of Counties (NACo), honors counties that are using technology to improve government services, strengthen cybersecurity, and enhance quality of life for residents.
Read the press release.
