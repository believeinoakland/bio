Civicsmith signing keys

Runs entirely in this tab. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Make your keys

One press makes both keys. Copy the two public keys into the session, and keep the private keys wherever you keep things.

With a passphrase, every private key this page gives you is locked with it, and loading one back asks for it. Keep the passphrase apart from the keys: without it they cannot be used.

Make a recovery key

A second release key, kept offline. It signs one release only: the one that replaces a lost or stolen release key. Make it once, copy its public key into the session, and keep its private key apart from the release key.

Load a key you already have

Paste a private key from a previous run. The key says which job it is for, so there is nothing to choose.

This key is protected with a passphrase

Sign a release

Choose the release asset (bio-plane.bundled.mjs). The signature covers the exact bytes of that file, so a rebuilt asset needs a new signature.

Sign a ratification

Copy the record id and its current hash from its page in your group's Civicsmith. The signature covers both, so it authorizes publishing that exact revision and no other.

Sign a notice

Copy the notice id, its revision number and its hash from its page in your group's Civicsmith. The signature covers all three, so it puts your name to that exact revision of the notice and no other. It is signed with your ratification key.

Sign a docket entry

Copy the case id, the entry number and the entry hash from its page in your group's Civicsmith. The signature covers all three, so it puts your name to that exact entry on that case's public docket and no other. It is signed with your ratification key.
