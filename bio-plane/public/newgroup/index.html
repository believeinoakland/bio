Civicsmith

Start your group's own accountability record

Civicsmith is a public, tamper-evident record: every capture signed, every change kept, nothing quietly rewritten. Your group can run its own copy for the government it watches, the agency it holds to account, the fight it is in. Not an account on someone else's servers. Your own copy, in your own infrastructure, from the first second.

What you get

  • Your own copy of Civicsmith, running in your own Cloudflare account, at your own web address.
  • Full control: your passwords, your credentials, your data. No one else, the publisher of Civicsmith releases included, holds a key to any of it or can see inside it.
  • An append-only history, so what your group has established can be shown and cannot be silently altered, by anyone, including you.

What it needs, honestly

  • A Cloudflare account. An email address and a couple of minutes. Create one here if you do not have one.
  • The Workers Paid plan, $5 a month from Cloudflare. Your copy reads documents and assembles evidence, and the free plan's processing allowance is too small for that work.
  • A payment method on the account. Cloudflare requires one before it turns on the file storage your copy keeps its evidence in. The installer checks for the plan and the storage before it creates anything, and stops, saying which is missing, if either is.
  • About five minutes, in a browser. No terminal, no code, nothing to download.

What happens, and what we can and cannot see

The setup assistant asks Cloudflare to show you a permission screen naming exactly what it may do in your account: install the software and set up its storage. You approve it on Cloudflare's own page, and you can revoke it any time from your dashboard. That permission passes through our assistant for the seconds the installation takes, is stored nowhere, and is never seen by a person. After setup, nothing your group does routes through the installer, or its publisher, at all.

Prefer that nothing pass through the installer even once? The manual path is documented and permanently supported, using only the Cloudflare dashboard. It exists so your group could stand a copy up even if the publisher of Civicsmith releases disappeared tomorrow. Find it in the project documentation.

One thing before you press the button. It takes you to newgroup.believeinoakland.workers.dev. That is the Civicsmith setup assistant, run by the publisher of Civicsmith releases on Cloudflare's worker platform, which is why the address ends the way it does. You will see the name in your address bar the whole time. We send you there with a button rather than a silent redirect, so you always know where you are.
