Believe in Oakland

Start your group's own accountability record

Believe in Oakland runs on a public, tamper-evident record: every capture signed, every change kept, nothing quietly rewritten. Your group can run the same system for your own city, your own agency, your own fight. Not an account on our servers. Your own copy, in your own infrastructure, from the first second.

What you get

  • Your own copy of the record, running in your own free Cloudflare account, at your own web address.
  • Full control: your passwords, your credentials, your data. Believe in Oakland holds no key to any of it and cannot see inside it.
  • An append-only history, so what your group has established can be shown and cannot be silently altered, by anyone, including you.

What it needs, honestly

  • A Cloudflare account. Free. An email address and a couple of minutes. Create one here if you do not have one.
  • No card. A new group's record fits comfortably in the free tier. The one optional extra, storage for very large attached files, does require a card on file with Cloudflare even though it stays free at this scale. You can add it later, deliberately, if your group ever needs it.
  • About five minutes, in a browser. No terminal, no code, nothing to download.

What happens, and what we can and cannot see

The setup assistant asks Cloudflare to show you a permission screen naming exactly what it may do in your account: install the software and set up its storage. You approve it on Cloudflare's own page, and you can revoke it any time from your dashboard. That permission passes through our assistant for the seconds the installation takes, is stored nowhere, and is never seen by a person. After setup, nothing your group does routes through Believe in Oakland at all.

Prefer that nothing pass through us even once? The manual path is documented and permanently supported, using only the Cloudflare dashboard. It exists so your group could stand a copy up even if Believe in Oakland disappeared tomorrow. Ask us for it, or find it in the project documentation.

One thing before you press the button. It takes you to newgroup.believeinoakland.workers.dev. That is Believe in Oakland's setup assistant, running on Cloudflare's worker platform, which is why the address ends the way it does. You will see the name in your address bar the whole time. We send you there with a button rather than a silent redirect, so you always know where you are.
