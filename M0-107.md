- **Node v26.0.0:** an expired budget sets `error.code === "ETIMEDOUT"` (`execFileSync` throws with the same code). A
  child that dies by SIGTERM on its own has the same `signal` but no error, so **ETIMEDOUT is the only reliable test**.
  M0-103's "or signal is set" would turn a crashed subject into NOT MEASURED — recommended NOT taken.
- **npm 11.12.1** passes exact exit codes through (124 stays 124).
- **The push guard:** `readRuns` accepts only GREEN or RED; an older guard (or the clone-wide copy) meeting a NOT
  MEASURED record files it as unreadable, prints UNDETERMINED and does not refuse — the safe direction.
- **Headline readers** (`battery-verdict` L147, `fieldread.control` L427) expect
  `N/N suites green · (N skipped · )?M assertions passing`; a new segment must come AFTER `assertions passing ·`.
