# Bob's product rulings: the scheduler, the record's size and service levels

**Status** · Bob's product rulings on the scheduler, the record's size and service levels, by subject (Bob, K5323), read whole by the work that plans or changes the scheduler, storage or service levels. Each line keeps its source ids; `build/rulings.md` is the full log. Moved from `rulings-active.md` when the process redesign was adopted; a ruling of Bob's on this subject is added here in the commit that records it.

- The scheduler heals itself unseen (Bob, K4565): a stall is never shown to anyone; a past alarm is never trusted, any request re-arms; the scheduled trigger is a watchdog, "efficient - but mission-critical bulletproof" (K4566), that never fails silently.
- The record's size (Bob, K4635): teams are told it depends on their Cloudflare plan and the infrastructure; now one Durable Object per team with a measured storage gauge and stated warnings; for the beta, split across objects with bulk tables in R2 (N1271). Service levels (K4746, in the requirements) name their design stage and Cloudflare plan, figures measured before a team sees them (K4747: the minimum in the alpha).
- Speed at every level (Bob, K5438: "keep the current bar, but also recognize that different sized teams will require different levels of capabilities (Team, Institution, Enterprise)..."): K4354's 2 s and 15 s hold at every level; each level states the workload it holds them for, and a copy that outgrows its level is told it needs the next.
- The region picker (Bob, K5440, on P2: "as recommended"): "near me" (speed only) and "keep my data in this region" (Cloudflare's jurisdiction setting), worded honestly about the difference.
- Region after install (Bob, K5441, on P3: "a recommended"): fixed at Level 1; a move is a new copy by export and import, said before the region is chosen.
- Choosing a level (Bob, K5444, on P4: "Shouldn't, for example, a newsroom be able to start a CS copy at the institution level ...? ... let's start that study."): a copy picks Team, Institution or Enterprise at install; the Levels 2-3 study (N1271) runs now in PLATFORM; unmeasured figures are marked so.
- Enterprise (Bob, K5454, on P5: "we'll need to work with a CS team interested in setting up an enterprise scale copy before we can provide that support"): not yet available; built with the first team that wants it; Team and Institution in the beta.
