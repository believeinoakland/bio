# Bob's product rulings: the assistant and AI accounts

**Status** · Bob's product rulings on the assistant and AI accounts, by subject (Bob, K5323), read whole by the work that plans or changes the assistant, AI accounts or providers. Each line keeps its source ids; `build/rulings.md` is the full log. The four AI choices BOB never raises, and T52's assistant scope, are in `scope.md` (K3804).

- Anthropic is one provider of the multi-AI design (Bob, K4421); development aims at an assistant for several AIs chosen by team, project and member, explored first by AI-DESIGN (N1220). T51 and T52 add no assistant work (K3717: "Don't disable the assistant ... Just don't put any more effort into getting it to work.").
- AI accounts (K4443, reconciled K5433): a member's own subscription serves only that member, never a project, team or another's failover list (AIR41); an account at its limit refuses, never moving on, except along an owner-declared ordered list (K4421), another owner's account joining only by that owner's standing opt-in. API keys (K5429): Bob's K3347 was for the Anthropic-only assistant; his later words ("Agreed on how teams connect", OpenRouter, "Okay: C.", K4443, K4421, K4464) govern the multi-AI assistant, which supports API keys from its first build; BOB still never raises K3347.
- The beta's AI (K5428, K5430-K5432): the assistant is the beta's (A41's condition met); privacy is evidence each catalogue entry carries and seven team-set checks, required by default, an administrator's ticked check binding below it (K4443); the origin rule ("models from Chinese developers follow the same rules as the privacy checks") is the seventh check, not an exclusion; K3735's and K3804's open AI questions are closed.
- The assistant is understood bottom up (Bob, K3558): documentation, then low-level tests, each layer proven live before the one above, end-to-end only once every layer below is certain. It is investigated, not passed (K3574): records state what was measured, what held on repetition, what is unknown and the tradeoff, never pass/fail; where it runs is decided after every viable option is tested and compared (K3538). The journeys lane works a real investigation by hand through every stage it reaches (K3578).
- A member sees the assistant's progress as it happens and its answer only after Civicsmith's check (Bob, K3602). The assistant may read any public site to plan research, but nothing it reads enters the record: only the substrate's capture does (K1880).
- Cost per member (Bob, K5459, on U1: "If the team or project is paying for the AI usage, then they should see who's using what"): the paying team's or project's administrators see each member's use and cost on that account; a personal subscription stays private.
- What an AI proves before members use it (Bob, K5475, on AI1: "as recommended"): a part reaches members once the AI passes Gate 1 at marks Bob sets after the first measuring run; the real public-matters set, once written, is a standing accuracy check shown to teams, a part below its mark there switched off for that AI.
- Moving to another payer (Bob, K5477, on AI3: "as recommended"): along an ordered list, a move within the same owner's accounts goes ahead, shown and stoppable; a move to another payer asks the member first.
- Cloudflare credits charged in place of the team's key (Bob, K5478, on AI4: "as recommended"): the answer is kept, the team's administrators are told, and the account's further calls stop until an administrator clears it.
- A personal subscription and the team's allowed list (Bob, K5479, on AI5: "as recommended"): a member's own subscription serves the team's work only if its AI is on the team's allowed list.
- Re-testing the list (Bob, K5480, on AI6: "A, with the ability for an admin to turn off retesting - with a queue entry reminding the admin that retesting is off."): only AIs teams use, within a monthly cap Bob sets; the paying accounts' administrator may switch it off, a queue item reminding them while off.
- Marking an AI on the list (Bob, K5481, on AI7: "as recommended"): a standing rule marks watch, lapsed or retired on a stated trigger (shutdown date, failed re-test, a different model answering), Bob told each time; adding or restoring an AI stays Bob's.
- Teams' test results (Bob, K5482, on AI8: "A, but the shared results page shows all AI, even those not approved yet by the team. They stay active for 6 months and are statistically joined to present overall results"): the team sees its own at once, unconfirmed; the shared page shows every tested AI; results count six months, combined into overall figures.
- A copy out of touch (Bob, K5483, on AI9: "as recommended"): it keeps its last list of AIs and says after two weeks that the list is old.
- The five unruled AI requirements (Bob, K5484, on AI10: "B"): each is brought to Bob separately (AI12-AI16); its lines stay drafts until he answers.
- The beta's assistant parts (Bob, K5485, on AI2: "as recommended"): every built part plus the intake interview, the case's account and charge-or-rule answers; investigating, extracting and exploring switch on per AI as each clears its bar.
- A shared team account on projects (Bob, K5486, on AI11: "B"): any project the member has joined, once the owner allows sharing.
