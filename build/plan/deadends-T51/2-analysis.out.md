## Shared causes

1. **No determination can be recorded** (`determine` has no screen; the design registry draws none). Breaks UC-065, UC-066, UC-067, UC-154. UC-067 and UC-154 find the matter and escalation controls built and correct but unreachable.
2. **Intent, bias and bias-debt ops have no screens at all** (objectivecondition, goal*, aspiration*, triage, biasadopt, biasdebtresolve, biasmanifest). Breaks UC-072, 073, 074, 075, 077, 078, 079. Side effects: the queue draws bias-debt and objective-gap rows with no act, and the ceremony lists a "Bias" step with no content.
3. **Contradiction: no member surface** and every candidate needs an assistant run. UC-068, 069, 155, 159 (HELD), and the candidate route of UC-070. When N1220 returns, the screen is still owed (queue words already say "dismiss it or take it up").
4. **Progressions cannot be started**: no link to define one, the define form is hidden for an unknown key, the document screen's thread button sends only `{sha256}`, and the matter view needs a prior thread. UC-080, UC-081.
5. **The question screen sends calls the ops refuse**: `narrow` without `ord` or `extent` (UC-050), `hypothesishold` without `about` (UC-061).
6. **Question acts bind to the question's home project**, not the project the member is working in: UC-055 (PARTIAL), and UC-056, 057, 060 for a drawn-in question.
7. **Strength detail not drawn**: the `ungraded` and `hunches` lists and per-leg inert reasons are never shown. UC-048, UC-053.
8. **Reopen, divide, classify, regrade, in-force-on-date have no control or no module**: UC-058, 059, 027, 076, 063.
9. **Machine-only versions**: readings come from the assistant, `narrow` (dead) or one own reading, so UC-054 and UC-055 are thin without the assistant.
