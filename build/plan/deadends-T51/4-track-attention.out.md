## Shared causes

- **S1. Plane ops with no screen (the member surface was never built).** `monitoring`, `monitorslate`, `monitorpause` (UC-034, UC-036); `intentproposals` (UC-037); `reevaluations`, `reevaluationrecord` (UC-039); `escalationevaluate` (UC-121); `consequencerecord`, `consequencerevise` (UC-128); `sweeps` and cadenced named requests (UC-035); a project close act (UC-151). Each is declared and implemented; a person cannot reach them.
- **S2. Escalation chain starts from a determination that no screen can record.** `determine` is on no screen, so `matter` (reached from a plan's determination subject, plan.mjs:85) has nothing to open in a normal team; with UC-121 and UC-128 having no controls, the whole escalation track (also the stage half of UC-127) is unreachable. Items: UC-121, UC-127, UC-128.
- **S3. The queue screen draws only its 16 registered act names (queue.mjs:47-52) and a subject link only for the kinds in item-words.mjs:16.** Any other door (`cite` for a lead, `escalationadvance`/`escalationdecline`, `checkpointrecord`, `reminderanswer` aside) is drawn only as "Open", and "Open" is empty when the subject kind is unknown (`capture_request`, `escalation`). Items: UC-045, UC-127, UC-040 (action kinds), UC-046.
- **S4. Captured bundles are born with `monitoring.frequency: none`** (capture.mjs:123), and the document screen's "Watch" does not choose a frequency. Item: UC-031 (and any Home/capture path that leaves `none`).
- **S5. Result drawn too thin.** `monitor` answers status and reason; screens print a fixed sentence (UC-032). The queue ignores the plane's `snoozed` mark (UC-043).
- **S6. Stale coverage notes.** UC-162, UC-165, UC-170 say "no member surface yet"; plan.mjs now draws open, scenario and checkpoint (UC-170 still has none). UC-045's note (set-aside unproven) is the reverse of the screen: set aside is drawn, take-up is the broken half.
