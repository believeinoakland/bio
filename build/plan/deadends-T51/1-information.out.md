## Shared causes

**S1. No door to Held captures; Home and Queue lack the "Vouch" act the registry gives them.** `/app/held` is linked from nowhere (`interface-shell/index.mjs:44` is only its crumb; every capture sentence "waits in Held captures" is plain text, `setup-words/rows.mjs:2356,2394`). The registry lists `release` on `home` and `queue`; `screens-daily/home.mjs` and `queue.mjs` build no such act and no "photos to vouch for" strip. The only built doors are the document page box (own capture, filed under a project) and the project dashboard. A capture from the Add rail, from Find's "Capture it", a testimony, a knock pulled into the record, or another member's capture cannot be vouched. Items: UC-021 (PARTIAL); downstream of UC-009, UC-010, UC-014 (works, but the result is stuck at collected for these paths).

**S2. No door to the Inbox (`/app/inbox`).** Built screen, working acts, no link, no queue notice, no rail entry (`interface-shell/index.mjs:42,92`; `doorbell/index.mjs` writes no queue item). Items: UC-013, UC-149, UC-150 (its only drawn act), UC-012 downstream (what a knock is for).

**S3. Plane ops and modules with no member surface (declared, implemented, drawn nowhere).** `lead`, `leadshare`, `leadlook`, `leadread` (UC-006, UC-007); `provenanceroute` (UC-020); `transcriptionattest` (UC-017); `pdfstructure&ocr=1` (UC-015); `connectionassert` (UC-026); `resolutiondefect` (UC-160); the `source*` ops and `knocksof` (UC-149, UC-150); `export`, `exportlog` (UC-030). Without an op at all: `declareOrigin` (UC-020) and the capture-source credential supply (UC-011). Several coverage notes still say "no member surface yet" and are true; UC-129's is stale (the action screen has it).

**S4. First-use chicken and egg.** Themes: the screen that declares one is linked only when one exists (UC-025). Progressions: the define form lives inside an existing progression's page that nothing links to, and the document page's "Thread" always refuses `NO_KEY` (UC-024).

**S5. A case can never be prepared.** The ceremony's `publish` (and `publishpreflight`) send no statement, scope, subject position or reasoning, exclusion list, bias acknowledgement or roles; no screen has fields for the last four; `publish` does not read the draft's params, and the review module's own gates run `publishCase` over the draft (`review/index.mjs:604`) so the "External" rung is always refused. Items: UC-099, UC-098 (attribution can never be asked), UC-101, UC-096 (the missing list that would say why is not drawn). This sits at the end of the chain (finding to case to publication) and is outside this batch's other items; BOB should route it to the publication walk.

**S6. Capture screen sends only the address.** No render flag, no archive route, no credential. Items: UC-010, UC-011.
