## Shared causes

**SC-1. The publish act is never given what it needs (UC-102, 103, 104, 105, 152, 153, 158; also UC-100's rung, UC-097 member door, UC-107).** `ceremony.mjs:247-251,300-307` sends `{draft, project, targets, caseId or newCase, tieAttested}` only. `publishCase` requires, as arguments: statement (`case-authoring/index.mjs:619`), scope, `excluded[]` (629), `subjectPosition` (623), `subjectJustification` (626), `biasAcknowledgement` (636), `roles` (1391), and the disclosures `tensionsDisclosed`, `selfAttested`, `flagsDisclosed`, `calculationsDisclosed` (710-715). `draft` only binds readings. `casedraft` stores scope, statement and targets only; four of those fields have no authoring control anywhere, and `account` is dropped (`review/index.mjs:88-89,469`). A second "Prepare the draft" on an existing draft sends no `targets` and replaces the stored params wholesale (`review/index.mjs:498`), losing the findings. The walk (`journey-check`) never publishes through the screens, so this was not found.

**SC-2. No member screen records a determination (UC-111, 112, 122, 123, 167; part of 113, 117).** `op=determine` is a member-only act (`skilldoctrine.mjs:792`) drawn on no screen and absent from the design registry. Matters, escalations, consequences and "plan from a compliant outcome" all start there. (BOB may rule this belongs with the held assistant; as built, no member can do it.)

**SC-3. Offices exist only through lines nobody can record (UC-120; part of 113, 114).** `addresseesuggest` reads `custodian_of` or `responsible_for` lines; the only line a screen records is `party_to` (`proceeding.mjs:167`), and Places seeds `post_in` and `part_of` only. The records request cannot start; its empty-state advice does not fix it.

**SC-4. Plane ops with no control (UC-112, 113, 122, 123, 153, 168, 172, 119 partly).** `consequencerecord`, `consequencerevise`, `escalationattach`, `escalationdecline`, `escalationsuspend`, `escalationresume`, `reattest`, `captureaccount`, `templatesave`, no contact op, `actioncorrespond` for no_response and quotes.

**SC-5. Dead and missing doors (UC-111, 112, 115, 122).** The project page lists "Matter" and "Action" with the project id as subject (`project.mjs:220-236`), but both screens need another id (`NO_SUCH_ACTION`, no such determination). `counsel` has no door at all.

**SC-6. Composer-made actions cannot reach text preparation (UC-124 direct, 113, 123).** Only plan-started actions (and records requests) lead to `start-send` or `request`.

**SC-7. The published page omits most of what the answer carries (UC-107, 152, 158, 097's disclosure).** Exclusions, acknowledgements, bias, tensions and highlighted contradictions, per-document grade and co-attestation.

**SC-8. The domain proof file is never shown (UC-109).**
