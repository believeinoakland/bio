## Core path (discover to action), after corrections

1. **Discover (Find):** WORKS. Search, scope choice and the wider reading draw; thin only in facet counts and frontier levels (C33). Searching outside sources is HELD.
2. **Capture:** WORKS for an address, a file and a firsthand observation. Broken downstream: vouching a capture made outside a project has no door (C13, C14); rendered copy and archive (C19) and source logins (C20) are not offered.
3. **Read / extract:** WORKS for reading, citing a part, retiring, timestamping. Gaps: OCR re-read (C21), second-member transcription check (C22), declaring origin (C23, C24).
4. **Question:** WORKS to open, cite, group, read strength, accept and make current. Broken: Narrow (C34), Keep a hunch (C35, C36); strength detail not drawn (C37).
5. **Finding (conclude):** WORKS to conclude, withdraw, set aside (bound to the question's home project, C52). A finding cannot be reopened (C9).
6. **Case:** BROKEN. Saving the case editor drops the chosen findings from the draft (C3); no fields for exclusions, position, bias acknowledgement or roles (C2).
7. **Publish:** DEAD END. Every publish and pre-flight is refused (C1, with C2); the ceremony also lacks the key-making door (C4), disclosures (C5, C6). Nothing can be published by a team.
8. **Action / request:** PARTIAL. A plan-started action with the member's own words works through approve and record-sent unless the text trips the approval gate (C71); a records request cannot start (C67, C68); breach, escalation, consequences and plan-from-outcome stop at no determination (C41, C42, C43, C44); direct actions have no route to text (C72); tier and counsel have no door (C69, C70).

## Causes

How DRAWN versus UNDRAWN was told: each op name was searched by whole word in `registry.json` (act `op` entries per screen, and the free text) and in `library.json`; the registry lists acts as op plus label only, never fields, so a missing field on a drawn op is called CONTROL-DRAWN with that caveat stated in the evidence. Substring hits were discarded (for example `determine` occurs only inside "determined", `lead` only in prose, `export` only inside "exported").
