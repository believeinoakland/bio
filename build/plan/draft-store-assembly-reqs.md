## Public

### Purpose

The record store's assembly and the world the screens' tests talk to. It builds every module of the record store on one Durable Object's storage, in the modules' order; migrates them before any request; composes their registrations and ops maps into the one route map; passes every store request through `store-door`'s frame; makes the store's request from a page's request (every stamp set from the admitted caller, every stamp the caller names deleted) in one function the Worker's door calls; and publishes `storeWorld()`, a real store with a real claim and sign-in, a door, recipes of real acts and a pinned clock, in which a screen's test asks real questions of real code. It imports no platform module, so it runs in plain node; `plane` hosts it as the Durable Object class and the Worker's entry.

### Provides

Terms. The **assembled store** is what `assembleStore` answers (R1). The **owner map** is the arms of the ops whose owners export a service and no ops map (R87). The **door function** is `innerRequest` (R91). A **world** is what `storeWorld()` answers (R98); a **recipe** is a function of a world and a member that performs real acts (R103). The record is the Durable Object's storage; a **page's request** is what the browser sends to `/api/?op=…`.
