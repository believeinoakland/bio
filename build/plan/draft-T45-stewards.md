## Choices made (BOB's details, P17; to record once and report)

1. **Shape.** One Civicsmith-run Worker, `stewards/`, built as `newgroup/` is (account pinned, DEPLOY.md, bundled module), but with one store of its own: a SQLite Durable Object, plus one send-e-mail binding. It holds no binding, credential or route to any group's Civicsmith and makes no outbound request but the notice (R16, R17): remedies are limited by construction, not by policy.
2. **Remedies.** A closed vocabulary: `dismiss` or `withdraw {service, item}` over `shared_profile`, `template_library`, `public_listing`, plus `reinstate`; each with a required reason, appended, never edited. The service's only outward effect is a withdrawal register the shared services' maintainers apply at their next release (R13). It names no record, bundle, member or instance anywhere.
3. **Not yet open until configured.** The contact address, both retention periods, the stewards' keys and the source key are configuration with no module default; until all are set, `GET /` says "not yet open", `POST /report` answers `NOT_YET_OPEN`, and the deploy script refuses (`checkOpen`, R1, R2). This also enforces K2799's "no public release without the address".
4. **Protective limits (K1881, reported, not asked).** Body 16 KiB; description 8,000; concerns 2,048; contact 254; reason 4,000; 5 reports per source per hour and 20 per day, 200 per day for the service, intake closed at 1,000 undecided; steward sign-in 5 failures per source per 15 minutes; sessions 12 hours; lists 100 per page. Sources are keyed fingerprints, never addresses (as `admission` R21). The intake fails closed; none of these limits what a group may do.
5. **Credentials (K1874).** Stewards sign in with a name and key in a POST body; a `SameSite=Strict`, `HttpOnly` cookie carries the session; any query string on `/steward/` is refused; keys are held only as SHA-256 digests in a secret.
6. **Notification.** One mail to the stewards' address per report, carrying only `ref`, kind and time, never the description, the target, the contact or a credential, so third parties' words do not travel by e-mail; nothing is sent to reporters or groups (R14).
7. **The guide.** `GET /guide` carries installer R48's guide words byte for byte (tested against `newgroup/README.md`), then what this service holds and who could compel it (R7).
8. **Uses.** `instance-setup` (`GROUP_SLUG_RE`) and `test-support` only; both earlier. `modules.json` already holds the empty entry (K1043); its `uses` are filled with the job's COMPLETE, and membership's `MODULE_ORDER` entry is owed with it (K657). No AI is used, so no terms citation is needed.

## For Bob (values and legal exposure only)

1. **Who the stewards are, and how they are named.** Options: (a) you alone for now; (b) people you name, listed publicly by name; (c) people you name, each recorded by name in the service's own records but shown to the public only as "Civicsmith's stewards", with Believe in Oakland named as responsible. *Recommendation: (c)*: a public name invites the harassment the service exists to handle, while each act still carries a steward's name inside the service.
2. **How long reports are kept.** Two values, counted from a report's latest decision (an undecided report is kept until decided): the reporter's contact, and the whole report. Options: (a) contact 90 days, report 1 year; (b) contact 30 days, report 6 months; (c) both 2 years. A withdrawal's own record (no description, no contact) is kept while it stands either way. *Recommendation: (a)*: long enough to handle a reply or a repeat, short enough to hold little about third parties.
3. **Whether anonymous reports are accepted.** Options: (a) yes, the contact optional (the stewards cannot reply to an anonymous one); (b) a contact required; (c) a contact required but never shown to anyone outside the stewards. *Recommendation: (a)*: people being harassed are often unwilling to identify themselves, and the stewards' only power is to withdraw a shared service, so an unverified report cannot harm a group's record.
4. **What a report may hold.** Options: (a) text and links only, up to 8,000 characters (as drafted); (b) also attached files (screenshots), which means holding and scanning third parties' files. *Recommendation: (a)*: a link to the published case or page is enough to examine it, and no files means far less to hold or be compelled to produce.
5. **Whether withdrawals are made public, and the affected group told.** Options: (a) a public register of withdrawals (which service and item, the date, the kind of abuse; never the reporter or the description), and the stewards write to the affected group where a contact is known; (b) the register kept private, the group told; (c) private, the group not told. *Recommendation: (a)*: rule 21 guards against abuse "through evidence, visibility", and a group whose shared item is withdrawn should know why. Until you rule, the draft keeps the register private (R13).

Not a question (already ruled, K2799): the contact address itself, which you will name before any public release; until then the service stays "not yet open".
