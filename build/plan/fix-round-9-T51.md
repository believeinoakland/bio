Absent means none; old readings carry none (new data only, R-OLD).
10. **The unit in flight, `stampUnit(unit)`** (plane R110, job 84; reading-pipeline R59, job 87; extraction R109, job 88; K4980). Plane passes `stampUnit` in `readHeld`'s options for a paged kind; extraction passes it to the reader unchanged; the reader calls `stampUnit("p<page>")` (or `"c<cursor>"`) synchronously before each unit's member call; plane writes that unit as the one in flight in `job_unit_attempts`, so `reapInterrupted` charges a kill to it. Absent `stampUnit`, readers behave as before. Plane's half-budget list is named `halfBudgetPages` (as today); extraction reads that name.
