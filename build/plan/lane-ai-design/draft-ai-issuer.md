# ai-issuer — requirements (draft)

**Status** · DRAFT, AI-DESIGN #3, 2026-10-27. Not in force and not yet met (all of it). A **new Civicsmith-run Worker**, proposed **layer 11** (`not_product: false`, a product of this repository, as `site` and `stewards` are), deployed on **Civicsmith's own Cloudflare account only** (the one `CLAUDE.md`'s standing safety rules name) by BOB with Bob's approval like any deploy. `uses` `signatures`, `record-grammar`, `ai-catalogue` (its `validatePayload`, R36 there), `test-support`. It is not part of a team's copy. Frame: `DESIGN-BRIEF.md` §1; partner: `draft-ai-catalogue.md` (cited as C-Rn); the suite: `SUITE-REQUIREMENTS.md` (SR-n). Ids are this draft's and may be renumbered before Bob approves.

**Size (P6).** Estimated 600–900 lines. Well under 4,000.

## Public

### Purpose

Civicsmith's one outward-facing service for the AI catalogue and the AI tests. It publishes the signed catalogue and signed advisories that every team's copy pulls; serves a single-use challenge to a test container; receives the sealed results bundle a container makes, checks its envelope, and keeps it for Civicsmith's scorer; keeps what Civicsmith's scorer concludes as results; and records Bob's decisions, without which nothing enters the catalogue. It **signs nothing, opens no sealed payload, stores no team's name unless the team agreed, and learns nothing about the copies that pull from it.**

### Provides

Terms. The **catalogue key** list is `ai-catalogue`'s `CATALOGUE_KEYS` (C-R33). The **operator** is a Civicsmith session or person holding the operator credential (R19); **Bob's act** is a decision he gave, recorded by a ruling reference. A **document** is a payload, an advisory or the seal key. `at` is the store's clock (R22).

**Publishing** (AIR33, AD25, AD11)
- **R1** Three static, read-only addresses under one fixed host: `GET /ai/index.json`; `GET /ai/catalogue/<seq>.json` and `GET /ai/advisories/<seq>.json`, each immutable once published (the same bytes ever); `GET /ai/seal-key.json` (R8). Bytes are served exactly as stored, with `cache-control` public (immutable for numbered documents, a short `max-age` for the index, BOB's) and no cookie. Any other method or path is `404`/`405`. Test: a stored payload is served byte-identical and its SHA-256 equals the index's. Control: `POST /ai/index.json` is refused.
- **R2** (AD25; the update check's rule, `instance-setup` R88: nothing identifying) A request to R1 leaves no record of its own: no address, user agent, count per requester, or log row in the store; no `set-cookie`; the answer does not vary by caller. Test: after 100 requests with different headers the store holds no new row and every answer is byte-equal. Control: a challenge or bundle request (R9, R12) does write its own rows.
- **R3** `publishCatalogue({bytes, statement, armored, by})` (an operator act) stores a payload for serving only when, in order: `armored` verifies in `NS_AI_CATALOGUE` over the statement against `CATALOGUE_KEYS` (the signatures' reasons pass through); the statement's SHA-256 and byte count equal those of `bytes`; its `seq` is higher than every published `seq` (`NOT_NEWER`) and the same counter as advisories (R4); `ai-catalogue.validatePayload` holds (`CATALOGUE_PAYLOAD_INVALID` naming the path); and **every entry in it has a `list` decision (R6) for that `entry_id` and `version`** (`ENTRY_NOT_DECIDED` naming it). Acceptance is atomic and rebuilds the index (R5). Test: a payload signed with a throwaway key in the list is stored and served. Controls: one changed byte `BAD_SIGNATURE`; an entry with no decision `ENTRY_NOT_DECIDED`; an old `seq` `NOT_NEWER`.
- **R4** `publishAdvisory({advisory, armored, by, trigger?})` stores an advisory only when it verifies in `NS_AI_ADVISORY`, its `seq` is higher than every published `seq`, its move is legal for the target's last published state (C-R24, else `ADVISORY_BAD_MOVE`), and its decision is as R7 states. Test: a `lapse` for a listed entry is stored. Control: a `retire` of a listed entry is `ADVISORY_BAD_MOVE`.
- **R5** The index `{index_version, catalogue: {seq, schema_version, issued_at, sha256, bytes, path, armored}, advisories: [...]}` names the newest published payload and every advisory whose `seq` is higher than that payload's, each with its own signature; it is rebuilt only by R3 and R4 and holds no field the signed statements do not (it is not itself signed: a withheld or stale index delays a copy but cannot change it, since a copy checks each document). Paths are relative and match `^[a-z0-9._/-]+$`. Test: after a payload (seq 7) and two advisories (8, 9) the index names 7 and lists 8 and 9. Control: after payload 10 the advisories 8 and 9 are no longer listed.

**Bob's decisions** (AD9, AD11, AD13)
- **R6** `decide({entry_id, version, decision, ruling_ref, by})` (operator act; append-only) records `{entry_id, version, decision, ruling_ref, by, at}`; `decision` is `list`, `hold`, `lapse`, `retire` or `clear`; `ruling_ref` matches `^K[0-9]+$` and is required; a decision is never edited, only superseded by a later one. R3 reads the latest decision per `(entry_id, version)`. Test: `list` then `hold` leaves the entry undecided for R3. Control: a decision with no `ruling_ref` is refused `DECISION_NO_RULING`.
- **R7** (rule pending For Bob 1) An advisory that **narrows** (`watch`, `lapse`, `retire`) is accepted with a `ruling_ref` of the standing rule that lets it, plus a `trigger` from `canary_drift`, `deprecation`, `served_mismatch`, `team_divergence`, `terms_change`; one that **widens** (`clear`, or a payload listing a lapsed entry again) is accepted only with a `list` or `clear` decision (R6) naming that act's own ruling. Every narrowing advisory is also appended to a list the operator reads for reporting to Bob (`advisoriesSince({seq})`). Test: a `watch` with the standing ref and `canary_drift` is stored. Control: a `clear` without its own decision is `ENTRY_NOT_DECIDED`.
- **R8** (SR62) `GET /ai/seal-key.json` answers `{key_id, public_key, not_before, not_after, seq, armored}`: the public key containers seal to, signed in `NS_AI_CATALOGUE` so a container can check it against `CATALOGUE_KEYS` and a compromised address cannot swap it. `publishSealKey` is an operator act with R3's verification; a retired `key_id` keeps its document. The matching private key is **not** held here (R15). Test: a container-side verification accepts the document. Control: the document signed by a key outside the list is not published.

**Challenges** (AIR15, SR80, AD8)
- **R9** `POST /ai/challenge` `{provider, plan, entry_id?, declared_model?, image_digest, bank_version, run_public_key}` answers `{challenge_id, nonce, bank_version, selection_seed, issued_at, expires_at}`: a nonce of at least 128 random bits, single use, and a seed from which the container draws its items from the signed bank it holds, so **no answer, item text or expected result is ever sent or held here**. It records which nonce went to which declared provider, plan and digest. It accepts no team name; `declared_team` is not a field. The request is refused `ISSUER_DIGEST_UNKNOWN` unless `image_digest` is in the registry (R11), `ISSUER_RATE_LIMITED` past the caps (R10), `ISSUER_BAD_REQUEST` naming the field otherwise. Test: a request with a released digest answers a nonce, recorded with its provider and plan. Controls: an unreleased digest is refused; two requests never answer the same nonce.
- **R10** A nonce expires at `expires_at` (BOB's, default 72 hours: a full run takes time); the number outstanding and the number issued per day are capped (BOB's numbers) and past them the issuer answers `ISSUER_RATE_LIMITED`, issuing none. Expired and used nonces are kept as rows (R12 reads them). Test: the cap-plus-one request is refused and mints nothing. Control: after expiries free the cap a request succeeds.
- **R11** `registerDigest({image_digest, release, ruling_ref, by})` and `withdrawDigest` (operator acts, append-only) maintain the registry of container image digests Civicsmith released; a digest is `sha256:` plus 64 lowercase hex. Test: a registered digest passes R9; a withdrawn one fails. Control: a malformed digest is refused.

**Bundles** (AIR16, SR60–SR64, AD8)
- **R12** `POST /ai/bundle` takes one bundle: a plaintext manifest and a sealed payload (the container's format, SR60–SR62). It checks the **envelope** only, in order, each failure answering as R13 states and the bundle kept as R13 and R14 state: size within the cap (`BUNDLE_TOO_LARGE`, refused); manifest schema and version readable (`rejected`, `BUNDLE_UNREADABLE`); the nonce was issued by this issuer (`NONCE_UNKNOWN`), is unexpired (`NONCE_EXPIRED`) and is **marked used in the same atomic step** (`NONCE_REUSED`); the image digest equals the one the nonce was issued for and is registered (`DIGEST_MISMATCH`); the sealed payload's SHA-256 equals the manifest's; the signature by the run key, whose public half the nonce was issued against, verifies over manifest and payload hash (`BAD_SIGNATURE`); and the manifest lists no field outside the manifest schema. Test: a well-formed bundle from a throwaway run key answers `verified_envelope`. Controls, one each: a reused nonce, an unissued nonce, a changed manifest character, an unknown digest.
- **R13** The reply is `{status, reason?, receipt_id}`, `status` one of `verified_envelope`, `unverified`, `rejected`; never "failed" (SR64). `unverified` bundles are kept for an operator to read but produce no result (R16). `rejected` bundles are not stored. Test: each failure of R12 answers `unverified` or `rejected` as stated and never `verified_envelope`. Control: only a bundle passing every check answers `verified_envelope`.
- **R14** (AD15) What is stored of a bundle is its sealed payload (opaque), its manifest **redacted to the manifest schema**, the receipt, the envelope verdict and its time. `declared_team` and any other field naming a team, member or address are dropped before storing **unless** the manifest also carries `agree_named: true`, which is inside the signed manifest so the container's run key vouches for it; the request's address, headers and user agent are never stored. Test: a manifest with `declared_team` and no `agree_named` is stored without it. Control: with `agree_named: true` the stored manifest keeps the name and the result row may name the team (R17).
- **R15** The issuer holds **no private key that opens a payload, and no key that signs the catalogue**. It holds only public keys (R3, R8) and one operator credential (R19). Opening, secret scanning and scoring happen in Civicsmith's scorer (`ai-gate`) where the seal private key is kept; the issuer hands it sealed bundles (R16). A bundle later found to carry a secret is destroyed (R18) and its sender cannot be told by the issuer (it keeps no address): the receipt id is the way a team asks. Test: no export, binding or table of the Worker holds a key marked private. Control: a planted private key in a fixture environment is refused at start.

**Results** (AIR36, AD12, AD15, SR81–SR83)
- **R16** `bundlesToScore({after?, limit?})` (operator act) lists `verified_envelope` bundles (receipt, manifest, sealed payload bytes) for the scorer; `recordResult({receipt_id | own_run, entry_id | declared_plan, parts, speed, task_cost, suites, scorer_version, by})` stores the scorer's conclusion with grade `REPORTED` for a receipt and `REPRODUCED` only for `own_run` (a Civicsmith run on its own account, with `run_ref`); a result for a bundle not `verified_envelope`, or one already scored, is refused `RESULT_REFUSED`. Test: a scored bundle stores a `REPORTED` row; a canary run stores `REPRODUCED`. Controls: a result for an `unverified` bundle is refused; `REPRODUCED` for a receipt is refused.
- **R17** `libraryRowsFor({entry_id})` (operator act, for building the signed payload) answers rows in `ai-catalogue`'s library schema (C-R43, C-R44): `tested_by` `civicsmith` or `team`, the grade, `repeated_by_civicsmith`, the date; `team_name` and `consent_ref` only for a bundle whose manifest carried `agree_named` (R14); no transcript, exchange, address or team id exists in the issuer to return. A team's agreement to be named applies to rows built after it is withdrawn (`withdrawName({receipt_id})`, by the operator on the team's request); rows already in a signed payload change at the next payload. Test: a result from an unnamed bundle yields a row saying only `team`. Controls: a named one carries its name; after `withdrawName` the next call omits it.
- **R18** `destroyBundle({receipt_id, reason, by})` (operator act) deletes the sealed payload and the redacted manifest, keeps a tombstone `{receipt_id, reason, at}`, and removes any unpublished result from it; bundles older than `BUNDLE_RETENTION` (BOB's, default 180 days after scoring) are destroyed the same way. Test: a destroyed bundle is gone and its tombstone remains. Control: the results of other bundles are untouched.

**Operator door and availability**
- **R19** Every act named above (`publish*`, `decide`, `registerDigest`, `withdrawDigest`, `bundlesToScore`, `recordResult`, `libraryRowsFor`, `withdrawName`, `destroyBundle`, `advisoriesSince`) is behind one operator credential, refused `ISSUER_NOT_AUTHORISED` with nothing done and no hint which part failed; each act appends `{act, by, at, ruling_ref?, outcome}` to an audit that no act edits. The credential is never printed, logged or returned. Test: an act without it is refused and appends only its refusal. Control: with it the act is audited with `by`.
- **R20** The three public routes (R1) depend on stored documents only, never on the dynamic store: with the store unavailable the index, payloads, advisories and seal key are still served, and `challenge` and `bundle` answer `ISSUER_UNAVAILABLE` (a copy's last good catalogue and a team's retry are the fallbacks). Test: with the store stubbed to fail, `GET /ai/index.json` answers 200 and `POST /ai/challenge` answers `ISSUER_UNAVAILABLE`. Control: with it healthy both succeed.

## Private

### Uses

- `signatures`: `verifyAiCatalogue` and `verifyAiAdvisory` (owed, `draft-ai-catalogue.md` Uses), the run key's signature check (R12).
- `record-grammar`: `canonicalJson`.
- `ai-catalogue`: `validatePayload` (C-R36), `CATALOGUE_KEYS`, the library row schema (C-R43, C-R44), the advisory move table (C-R24).
- `test-support`: the sandbox and throwaway keys (tests only).

### Invariants

- **R21** The Worker is bound to Civicsmith's own Cloudflare account only and to no team's; it reads no team's record, holds no team's credential, and calls no AI provider (the scorer's canary calls are the scorer's, on Civicsmith's accounts). Test: the Worker's declared bindings name no account but Civicsmith's. Control: a binding to another account fails the deploy check.
- **R22** Every instant reads the store's clock (a handed `now`, else `env.BIO_NOW_MS`, else the wall clock). Test: a nonce issued at a pinned instant expires at that instant plus its life. Control: with no pin the wall clock is read.
- **R23** Every code it mints (`ENTRY_NOT_DECIDED`, `DECISION_NO_RULING`, `ISSUER_DIGEST_UNKNOWN`, `ISSUER_RATE_LIMITED`, `ISSUER_BAD_REQUEST`, `ISSUER_NOT_AUTHORISED`, `ISSUER_UNAVAILABLE`, `NONCE_UNKNOWN`, `NONCE_EXPIRED`, `NONCE_REUSED`, `DIGEST_MISMATCH`, `BUNDLE_TOO_LARGE`, `BUNDLE_UNREADABLE`, `RESULT_REFUSED`, with `ai-catalogue`'s `NOT_NEWER`, `ADVISORY_BAD_MOVE`, `CATALOGUE_PAYLOAD_INVALID`) has its row in `checks.mjs` and a test; no answer carries an English sentence a person reads; a team that gets a refusal reads the container's words for the key (K3810, K5418). Test: each code is answered with its row. Control: a code with no row fails.
- **R24** Tables (one SQLite-backed store; blobs beside it): `documents`, `decisions`, `digests`, `challenges`, `bundles`, `results`, `audit`; all append-only except a bundle's destruction (R18) and a nonce's `used` mark. No table holds a request address, a team's secret, a plaintext payload or a record text of any team. Test: a sentinel in every field of a request, bundle and manifest appears in no row except where R14 allows. Control: with `agree_named` the name is the one stored sentinel.

### Satisfies

- AIR15, AIR16, AIR22, AIR24 (advisories), AIR33, AIR36; AD7–AD9, AD11–AD13, AD15, AD25.
- BR30, BR31 (evidence as data, health reports opt-in: not in this draft, For BOB 9); SR5, SR60–SR64, SR80–SR84.
- `instance-setup` R88–R90 and `signatures` R45–R48 (fixed address, plain GET, own namespace, rollback guard); `agent-runner` R10, R30 (the container's allowed hosts include this one only for R9 and R12).

### Suggestions

- **Shape.** One Worker; static documents from the Worker's asset or object store, the dynamic routes (R9, R12) on one SQLite-backed Durable Object so a nonce's single use is atomic; the operator door a separate path. The repository's `ai-issuer/` folder, deployed to the account `CLAUDE.md` names, never another.
- **The operator's tools** live outside the Worker: a script on an operator's machine builds a payload from the reviewed data files, signs it with the catalogue key (the way the release is signed), and calls R3; Bob's decision is a K ruling in the repository that `decide` cites.
- **Tests.** Throwaway keys for catalogue, run and operator; one negative control per R; the container's bundle built by `test-support`'s fixture, not a live container.

## For BOB

1. **Signing key custody (R15, R3).** Recommended: the catalogue key lives offline with the operator, the Worker holds none; the cost is that every payload and advisory is signed by a person's act and a lapse waits for one (R7's standing rule keeps that quick).
2. **Where bundles are opened and scored (R15).** Options: (a) the issuer holds the seal private key and scores; (b) the issuer only stores sealed bundles and Civicsmith's scorer, off the Worker, holds the key. Recommended **(b)**: the online Worker then cannot read any team's raw test exchange, even though it is synthetic. A secret found in a bundle is then found after receipt, and the bundle destroyed (R18).
3. **Storage (R24).** Recommended one SQLite-backed Durable Object for rows and R2-style object storage for blobs; BOB confirms against Cloudflare's current limits at the job's START (a bundle with repeats can be large; if it exceeds a Worker request body, the upload goes straight to object storage by a short-lived grant).
4. **Operator credential (R19).** A Worker secret held as the deploy secrets are, or an access policy in front of the operator paths. Recommended the access policy, with a service credential for BOB's sessions; BOB verifies the product's current form and never prints it.
5. **No signature on the challenge (R9).** SR51 asks for "the challenge and its signature". Recommended: none; the issuer's own table of nonces is authoritative and a second online key would only add a thing to steal. If wanted: a challenge-only key, distinct from the catalogue key.
6. **Digest registry (R11).** Recommended: the operator registers each release's container image digest at release, citing the release's ruling; BOB decides whether it derives from the signed release manifest instead.
7. **Numbers (R10, R12, R18).** Nonce life 72 h, outstanding cap, per-day cap, bundle size cap, `BUNDLE_RETENTION` 180 days, the index `max-age`. Recommended as listed; BOB sets them at the job's START.
8. **The public upload route (R12)** may be left out of the first build: SR63 lets a team send a bundle by any route, an operator then ingests it through the same checks. Recommended: build the route, because the container's allowed hosts already include this Worker for R9.
9. **Health reports (AD38) are not in this draft.** Opt-in, content-free counts per entry would be a fourth door and a table; recommended after the first build, when drift (AIR24) is seen to need them.
10. **Abuse of the open routes (R9, R12).** They are unauthenticated by design (the container holds only its nonce). Recommended Cloudflare's rate rules in front plus R10's caps; BOB checks the current product.
11. **Layer and registration.** Layer 11 and a `modules.json` row (`not_product: false`); the deploy check for R21 belongs to the deploy process, as the other Workers' do.

## For Bob

1. **Who may take an AI off the list in a hurry.** What enters the catalogue is your decision. Taking an AI out, or putting it on watch, protects teams but is not the same as adding one. Options: (a) every change, in either direction, waits for you; (b) you decide once the rule, and Civicsmith may put an AI on watch, lapse or retire it without waiting when a stated trigger fires (a failed regular check, the provider announcing it is withdrawing the model, the AI answering as a different model than listed, teams' results diverging, a change in the provider's terms), and tells you each time; bringing one back, or adding one, still waits for you. **Recommended: (b)**: the quick, protective step is taken at once, and everything that widens what teams may use stays yours. If you agree, BOB records the standing rule once and each use cites it (R7).
