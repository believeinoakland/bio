## 1. What it must achieve
A team, a project or a member can choose the AI they want behind Civicsmith's assistant, from the providers, plans and types they already have or can get, through a connection that Civicsmith explains or walks them through; every claim Civicsmith makes about an AI is backed by evidence; and the assistant keeps working, fully and economically, whichever AI is chosen.

## 2. Providers, plans and types supported [AIR1, AIR12, AIR20, AIR23, AIR34]
1. Any legitimate AI provider can be supported: proprietary or open-weight; cloud, hosted or local; direct provider, aggregator (OpenRouter), gateway (Cloudflare AI Gateway), built-in (Cloudflare Workers AI), or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
2. **Anthropic is a provider like any other**: same gates, no exemption, no exclusion **[Bob]** (supersedes K3717's exclusion for this design).
3. Both **API accounts and subscriptions** are supported **where the provider's terms and a documented route allow**; the support is as broad as it is because teams and individuals want to use provider relationships they already have **[Bob]**. A subscription plan entry carries the provider's stated restrictions as data (e.g. only the subscriber; only for the connected app; no pooling, resale or charging others; where the runtime and token may live), each with quote, page and date, or OPEN. **Personal subscriptions [Bob, 2026-10-21, AIR41]:** a member decides whether to use their own personal subscription for the acts they run; it is personal because the member runs it, and its results are that member's work and may be shared; it is never a project's or team's account, and no member other than its owner can run the assistant on it, including as a failover account, whatever opt-in is given [Bob]. The product shows the provider's stated restrictions at connection, records the member's choice, and enforces only that level rule.
4. Adding a provider needs one adapter and one catalogue entry; nothing outside them names a provider. Adding a **model** at a supported host needs only a catalogue entry with its evidence (no code). One generic adapter serves the OpenAI-compatible family through a per-host profile; bespoke adapters only where an API genuinely differs.
5. **Origin policy (interim) [Bob]:** models developed by Chinese organisations are not offered for now, whichever host serves them; **since 2026-10-21 a default the member can change (AIR43)**; held as policy data on the entry, revisitable without code. **[Ask]** confirm "developer, not host".
6. **Sequencing [Bob]:** OpenRouter support (with its sign-in flow and host pinning) is built first, so the product's own path can then be used to open an OpenRouter account and complete the open tests.

## 3. Evidence, gates and the catalogue [AIR11, AIR13, AIR14, AIR17–AIR19, AIR24, AIR33, AIR35, AIR36, AIR38, AIR39]
1. Every statement the product makes about an AI or plan carries a **grade** (Reproduced / Reported / Stated), its source and date; an unconfirmed point is OPEN and restricts nothing.
2. **The offered list is closed and vetted [Bob]:** a model can be selected only if it is in the catalogue, and an entry exists only if it (a) meets Civicsmith's privacy requirements, (b) passed Gate 1 for its roles, (c) has characterization measurements showing it capable and efficient enough. An aggregator's full list is never exposed.
3. An entry is a **(model, host) pair** and the host is pinned: through an aggregator each request is locked to the vetted host with no fallback; a response from any other host is refused and recorded.
4. **Gate 0 · privacy** — **revised by Bob 2026-10-21 (AIR42): six checks (no training; no human reading by default; stated retention with zero-retention where offered; location and law; no hidden onward passing; works with keep-away limits and the notice), each answered for every entry, all required by default and each waivable by the member choosing, the choice recorded; terms-permit-use and breach commitments shown only. **Further revised the same day (`REQUIREMENTS.md` §15): the team's administrators set the six first (all required by default); a check they leave required cannot be unticked by a project or member; a check they untick may be ticked or unticked by a project owner or member; every change recorded.** A team may thus choose to work with anonymity or in a more publicly visible mode. Gate 0 is therefore evidence and a member filter, not a bar on entering the catalogue.** Earlier text, kept for traceability: (written, versioned checklist; each point answered from the host's own text with quote, page and date, or OPEN; an OPEN required point keeps the entry off). Draft points: no training on content; no human review by default; stated retention and ZDR where offered; jurisdiction and processing location; terms permit this use and the key handling; no unstated sub-processors; incident commitments; compatibility with the keep-away limits and disclosure. The content of the checklist is Bob's policy **[Ask]**.
5. **Gate 1 · capability:** a CS-owned, versioned conformance suite (tool use, structured output, grounding, restraint, long-context and image reading, consistency, honest "not found", efficiency) scored by code; results per role: passes / passes with limits / not suitable. A weaker AI may serve some roles and is refused the rest by name. Thresholds are set by Bob after the first measurements **[Bob]**.
6. **Characterization:** session start cost/time; average turn latency; privacy and training terms; operational consistency; individual/team/enterprise limits; stated and effective context window; all-in cost of standard tasks; uptime evidence labelled with its period **[Bob]**.
7. **Catalogue updates are available at all times**, independent of software releases **[Bob]**: a signed, versioned data channel any copy can pull, with a cached last-good copy, rollback, schema version, and a "needs a newer release" state for an entry whose adapter type a copy lacks. Bob decides what enters it.
8. **Drift and retirement:** entries pin an exact model version; lifecycle listed → watch → lapsed → retired; signals: scheduled canaries on CS accounts, provider deprecation dates, served-model mismatch, divergence of team bundles from baseline, optional opt-in content-free health reports; state changes ship as signed advisories; notice before a provider's shutdown date; a lapsed entry refuses new runs but never alters results already produced.
9. Terms and policies bearing on use (including usage-policy limits on political, legal and high-risk work, indemnities, no-SLA) are recorded per plan with quote, section, version and effective date, including **future** effective dates, and shown to the team leader at connection; entries are re-confirmed on a changed date.
10. A **central results library**, maintained by Civicsmith in the manner of filing templates **[Bob]**, makes gate results available to teams: summaries with grade and date; raw transcripts stay private.

## 4. Testing by Civicsmith and by outside teams [AIR15, AIR16]
1. Civicsmith's own tests run on accounts Civicsmith controls (sample accounts) and yield **Reproduced** results. Sample accounts are Bob's to obtain.
2. Outside teams and members can test a plan not yet in the catalogue only through a **locked, digest-pinned Civicsmith container** that holds the suite, holds the key in memory for one run, limits egress to the provider under test, builds and seals the results bundle inside itself (sealed to Civicsmith's public key, no secrets, manifest visible to the team first) **[Bob]**. Results from teams are **Reported** until reproduced. Scoring is done on Civicsmith's side from the raw exchange against answers the team never holds; fixtures are synthetic only; items rotate by a challenge nonce.
3. The container runs in the team's own account or machine (the machine route is the way to test local models); running it in Civicsmith's account for outside teams is deferred because it would make Civicsmith a holder of teams' keys **[Bob]**.
4. A team's own unlisted or local endpoint may be used with the assistant **only through the test flow** until a release includes it **[Bob]**.
5. Test cost is shown before a run; the suite is tiered (a cheap screen first, repeats and long-context probes only for models that pass).
6. **Gate 1 extends the existing test bar** (D14; `run-rules` R19, R29; `ai-runs` R75): a verdict is held per (catalogue entry, part of the assistant), and a part the chosen AI has not passed is refused by name for accounts using it. Civicsmith's own runs use its frozen set of matters plus a synthetic bank; outside teams' container runs use the synthetic bank only, whose answers never leave Civicsmith. Full requirements: `SUITE-REQUIREMENTS.md` (Q4). In code and words the suite is the "Gate 1 suite", not "conformance", which names an existing module.

## 5. Connection and credentials [AIR2, AIR3, AIR9, AIR30–AIR32, AIR37]
1. **The UX describes or guides the user through every step of connecting; it never leaves them to sort it out unsupported** **[Bob]**. A provider/plan without guidance is not offered.
2. Plan **scope** is data (one person / team / application / unconfirmed) and is enforced: a one-person plan (including every personal subscription, AIR41) cannot be set as a team's or project's account. The cascade (project, else member, else team) is unchanged.
3. **Credentials may be held sealed, referred to in a password or secrets manager or other permitted means, or issued by a provider's own sign-in flow** (user-owned token) **[Bob]**; each plan entry states which it allows; a reference is resolved at call time and never stored in logs, records or bundles. **Gateway-held keys (found 2026-10-21, `NOTES.md` §Q-secrets):** Cloudflare AI Gateway can store a provider API key in the team's own Cloudflare account and add it to requests itself, so Civicsmith's code never holds or sees the key; it is the one means found that keeps a key out of the application entirely (password and secrets managers hand the key to the application at call time). Limits: an administrator-level role is needed to store a key; use is not limited per member; providers covered, cost after beta and audit logging OPEN; sign-in tokens not covered. For a member's personal subscription these statements are shown, not enforced; the member decides (AIR41). Each plan states where requests may originate and where a token may live (e.g. a member-controlled runtime).
4. Signup: guided links and, where a provider documents it, a sign-in/connect flow; the product never signs anyone up, never handles payment details, never states a price the provider has not confirmed; special Civicsmith pricing is shown only once an arrangement exists.
5. **Free tiers that read or train on CS content are allowed only after a plain warning is shown and accepted**, with the acceptance recorded (who, when, plan, warning version) **[Bob]**.

## 6. Using the assistant with the chosen AI [AIR5–AIR8, AIR25–AIR28]
1. **Full function:** every role (FIND, PURSUE, EXTRACT, CHECK, ask, draft, read, transcribe, standing question) runs through the same harness, control table, checks and fences for every AI; the canon's boundary rules hold unchanged.
2. **Token efficiency:** adapters expose and use each provider's savings (caching, batch for non-interactive work, trimmed context); usage is recorded in a provider-neutral shape (null, never zero, where unstated).
3. **Sessions:** a series of turns in one session is used where efficient; the plane's run record is always the source of truth, so a run can resume on another AI or later; provider-held state is only an optimisation and is not used where zero-retention or the keep-away limits forbid.
4. **Model and effort** are offered exactly as each provider offers them, per model; the product never offers a control a model lacks.
5. **Cost visibility:** per account, owner, member and run: spend against limit, an estimate before a run, actual after, the added cost of a switch; units provider-neutral; plans that report no money show "not measurable in money", never zero; alerts at owner-set thresholds; reconciled with the provider's figures where available.
6. **Budgets and ordered accounts [Bob: failover allowed]:** Civicsmith-side limits plus recognition of provider-side limits (caps, quotas, plan allowances) as limits reached; an owner may declare an **ordered list of accounts** (within a scope and across the cascade); work continues down the list only along that declared order, at a step boundary, only to an entry that passed Gate 0 and Gate 1 for the run's mode, never to a weaker privacy tier or another owner's account without that owner's standing opt-in (off by default) **[Bob]**; every switch recorded and shown, the member can decline to continue; each result keeps the label of the AI and account that produced it; if the list is exhausted the act is refused in plain words saying who can raise a limit or add an account. (This is an explicit exception to K2352's "refuses, never passing the act on"; BOB to record.)
7. **Outages:** errors are classified (rate limit, spent cap, overload, timeout, revoked key, model not found, refusal) with defined responses: bounded retries honouring the provider's retry delay, a circuit breaker per account and model, a health state, failover per §6.6; interactive asks fail fast with plain choices (wait, switch, continue without AI); non-interactive runs can pause and resume on recovery; partial results survive; retried turns cannot duplicate writes; never a silent degrade.
8. **Provider conduct rules for high-risk civic output:** where a provider's policy requires qualified human review and AI disclosure for legal, public-benefit or adjudication advice, the product states what is asked of the team once at connection; Civicsmith does not itself give advice on a person's legal matter.

## 7. Configuration by leaders and members [AIR10]
A team leader chooses which AIs, models and plans members may use; a project owner may narrow; a member chooses among those allowed and, where the team permits, adds their own; **narrowing only** (a lower level never allows what a higher level forbids); keep-away limits bind every AI; every change recorded with who and when.

**Streaming [Bob]:** where an AI cannot stream, turn-level progress is acceptable and the adapter states the limit. **Health reports [Bob]:** opt-in per team, content-free.

## 11. Decided by Bob 2026-10-21 (AI-DESIGN #2's questions, all as recommended) [Bob]
1. **Gate 1 is scored by code only;** a reviewer's reasonableness reading is a separate, labelled characterization note and never changes a verdict.
2. **Civic work not refused:** Gate 1 includes a test group checking that the AI does ordinary civic research (elections, lobbying and campaign-finance records, police and court records, public benefits, public officials' recorded acts) without refusing it; it is required for every part of the assistant that reads records.
3. **A switched-off use refuses;** failover along an ordered list applies to limits, outages, lapsed or retired models and capability gaps, never to a use the owner switched off.
4. **Disclosure is per provider and host:** before a member's or team's material first goes to a recipient not yet disclosed, including after a failover hop, the notice is shown and the step waits; a non-interactive run waits rather than proceeding.
5. **Provider first:** connection options are grouped by provider, then each provider's plans and types (UX requirement for the UX lane).
6. **How teams connect [Bob, 2026-10-21]:** pasted key, the provider's own sign-in, and the AI built into the team's own Cloudflare account; built-in is the default for a team with no outside AI account (§10 (c), answered).
7. **Personal subscriptions [Bob, 2026-10-21]:** the member's choice at the member level only (AIR41); this replaces §10 (d)'s "not available unless a documented route" recommendation.
8. **Privacy checks [Bob, 2026-10-21, AIR42 as revised]:** six checks, required by default; the team's administrators decide which are required for the team, and those stay locked; projects and members may tick or untick only what the administrators left unticked; every change recorded (§3.4).
9. **Origin rule [Bob, 2026-10-21, AIR43]:** by developer, as a default the member can change (exact meaning of "selectable" being confirmed).
10. **Ordered lists [Bob, 2026-10-21, AIR44]:** administrators for team accounts, project owners for project accounts, members for their own; others' accounts only with their owner's opt-in; never a personal subscription.
11. **Results library [Bob, 2026-10-21, AIR36]:** every team sees, per AI, the parts it passed and its limits, measured speed and cost of standard jobs, who tested it (Civicsmith, or a team not yet repeated) and the date; word-for-word test records and the sending team's name (unless it agrees) stay private.
