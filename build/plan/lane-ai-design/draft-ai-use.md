## Public

### Purpose

What each AI account has spent and may spend, whichever provider serves it. It counts each use against the account that paid and the member whose act it was, in a provider-neutral record that keeps money the provider reported apart from money Civicsmith estimated; holds each owner's limits and recognises the provider's own limits as limits reached; judges a use against them; estimates before a run and reports after; raises owner-set alerts; and reconciles its totals with what the provider and the gateway report. It holds no credential and does not choose which account pays (that is `ai-accounts`', including every move down an ordered list).

### Provides

Terms. As today, with: an **owner** is `group` (its administrators act), `project:<id>` or `member:<id>`; a **use** is an `ai-accounts.USE_KINDS` entry; a **limit** is `{owner, scope, unit, period, amount, inclusive}`. The **usage record** is `agent-model` R5's: tokens (input, output, cache read, cache write, reasoning), `calls`, `cost_reported {amount, unit, source}|null`, `cost_estimated {amount, unit, price_date}|null`, `unstated`. A plan's **money** is the catalogue's `reported` (the plan reports cost), `priced` (the catalogue's per-token prices apply to it), `none` (a flat or unmetered plan) or OPEN (null). A **provider limit** is one `agent-model` R52 returned as a `limit` outcome. An **entry** is a catalogue entry id; no model string appears here. Every refusal names `code` and carries its row's `check` and `translation` (R8).

**Counting** (`countUsage`)
- **R1** (AMEND; was `ai-runs` R48, R49; B8; BR22) `countUsage({owner, member, use, mode, entry_id, label, usage, calls, at})` is called in the caller's transaction.
  - `owner` is `group`, `project:<id>` or `member:<id>`; `entry_id` and `label` (`agent-model` R26's: provider, host, model version, effort, account level, owner) replace the model string and carry the effort (R15).
  - `usage` is `agent-model` R5's record. It adds to the counter `ai_usage`, kept per owner, member, local day (`civil-time.localDay`) and `use`: the token sums (reasoning included), `calls` (a `null` counted as one), `cost_reported_sum` and `cost_estimated_sum` per unit, kept apart and never added together, and `unpriced_calls`, the calls that stated no cost and had no estimate. A figure `unstated` is never counted as 0: a sum over a day with any unstated figure keeps `unstated` listing it.
  - `countAskUsage({member, mode, entry_id, label, usage, calls, at})` counts an ask or a draft the same way, `mode` `ask` or `draft`, its owner as `accountFor` answered it; any other `mode` is refused `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID`. It sums the figures `usage` carries and reads no price table (`agent-model` R13 estimates; K2376).
  - A malformed entry is refused `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID`, counting nothing. The counter holds no content, question, address or secret, and is `export: "admin-only"`. Rows from before T40 are "not recorded" and count toward no limit; rows from before this change keep their dollars as `cost_estimated_sum` in `usd` with `entry_id` null.
  - Test: two calls, one stating a cost and one stating none but priced, sum into `cost_reported_sum` and `cost_estimated_sum` separately; a call with neither adds to `unpriced_calls` and to no sum; `cache_read` unstated stays unstated. Control: a record with a negative token count is refused and counts nothing.

**Limits** (`aiLimitSet`)
- **R2** (AMEND; D39; B4; was `ai-runs` R50's setting half; AIR26) `aiLimitSet({owner, scope, unit, period, amount, inclusive?, by})`.
  - `scope` is `overall`, a `USE_KINDS` entry, or `per_member` for a team or project account. `unit` is `usd`, `tokens` or `calls`. `period` is `day` or `month`. `amount` is a positive number (`usd` to the cent; the others whole), or `null` to remove the limit. `inclusive` (default `true`) is allowed only for a use scope. Anything else is `AI_LIMIT_INVALID`, naming the field.
  - **`usd` is allowed only on an account whose plan's money is `reported` or `priced`; on `none` or OPEN it is refused `LIMIT_UNIT_UNAVAILABLE`.** The refusal follows the plan, not the kind of account: a sign-in plan reporting money would allow `usd`, a flat-fee API-shaped plan would not. (The old clause "`usd` on a member's sign-in is refused" retires.) `tokens` and `calls` are always allowed.
  - Who may set it is the owner's own act (`NOT_YOUR_CEILING`; the project owner refusals of `ai-accounts`; `NOT_AN_ADMIN`). At most one limit per owner, scope, unit and period; each set appended with who and when. The migration and the dropped `ai_ceilings` (T42) are as before. Idempotent.
  - Test: `usd` on a fake plan with money `none` refuses `LIMIT_UNIT_UNAVAILABLE`; on `reported` and on `priced` it is set; on OPEN it refuses; `tokens` is set on all. Control: the same owner and plan changed to money `reported` accepts `usd`.

**Judging** (`useCheck`)
- **R3** (AMEND; D38 C, D39; B3, B5; K2400; BR23) `useCheck({owner, member, use, at, entry_id?})` answers `null` or `AI_LIMIT_REACHED` with `{whose, scope, unit, period, source, resets_at?, walkable}` and its plain-words row, naming no cost.
  - `source` is `own` (a limit set by this account's owner, judged **before** the call from the counter) or `provider` (a provider limit recorded by R18 and not yet reset, judged from the record **after** the provider has said it; R18). It refuses when, for the current period: the owner's `use` limit, its `overall` limit (over the uses that are not exclusive, unless `use`'s limit is exclusive) or its `per_member` limit (over `member`'s use of that account) has reached its amount; or a provider limit covering the account (or the entry) has not reached its `resets_at`.
  - It judges only the paying account's limits (D38: never another account's money limits). A `usd` limit on an act whose `entry_id` carries neither a price nor a reported cost is refused `LIMIT_UNIT_UNAVAILABLE` with `unjudged`, because the act's cost cannot be measured (R21); never counted as zero.
  - `EXPLORE_NOT_ENABLED` when the owner's `explore` setting is `no` (A5, B6). With `yes` and no `explore` limit held, `explore` is judged against `overall` alone; `ask` without that day's approval is R6's answer. The owner's `explore` value is read through `ai-accounts.accountUsesOf` (its R17), no viewer; it reads `no` when the account is not held, cannot be read, or holds a value other than `no`, `ask` or `yes` (fail closed).
  - It writes nothing and never throws. A counter that cannot be read answers the refusal (fail closed) with `unjudged: true` and no `scope`, `unit` or `period`.
  - `walkable` is the ordered-list exception (R20): `true` only on an own or provider limit; `false` on `EXPLORE_NOT_ENABLED`, on an `unjudged` refusal and on any use its owner switched off.
  - Test: an own limit reached answers `source: "own"`, `walkable: true`; a recorded provider limit with a future `resets_at` answers `source: "provider"` and that time; after `resets_at` (pinned clock) it answers `null`; an unreadable counter answers `unjudged` with `walkable: false`. Control: under every limit it answers `null`.

**Reads** (`aiUsage`, `aiUsageMine`, `limitsReached`)
- **R4** (AMEND; was `ai-runs` R51; B8; T41 D12; BR22) `aiUsage({owner, viewer, month})` answers that account's owners the month's use per `use`, summed over members and naming none, with `cost_reported_sum` and `cost_estimated_sum` apart per unit and, where the plan's money is `none` or the entries are unpriced, `cost: null` with `not_measurable: true` (never 0). `aiUsageMine({viewer, day?, month?})` answers a member their own use per payer and per use against each limit that bound them, with a cost per answer only to the paying account's owners (R10, R11); nobody sees another member's spending. Neither writes. Test: a `none` plan's month answers tokens and calls and `not_measurable: true`, no cost field equal to 0; a priced plan answers both cost sums; a non-owner is refused as R2 refuses. Control: an owner of a reported plan sees `cost_reported_sum`.
- **R5** (AMEND; B7; BR23) `limitsReached({viewer, at})` answers, for each account the viewer owns, each limit first reached in its current period: `{owner, scope, unit, period, period_start, reached_at, source, resets_at?}`, with a stable key per owner, limit and period (for a provider limit, per owner, kind and `resets_at`). It is for `notice-producers` R16, writes nothing and never throws. Test: an own limit and a recorded provider limit each appear once with their keys; a second read mints no new key. Control: a provider limit past `resets_at` no longer appears.

## New requirements

**Provider-side limits** (BR23; AIR27)
- **R18** (BR23) `recordProviderLimit({owner, account_id, entry_id, kind, scope, resets_at, at})` records a provider limit that `agent-model` returned as a `limit` outcome (`kind`: `spend`, `quota`, `rate_window`, `plan_window`; `scope`: `account` or `entry`; `resets_at` null where the provider gave none), in the caller's transaction, and is idempotent per (account, entry, kind, `resets_at`). It is a **limit reached, not an error**: it never counts as a failure for health. A recorded limit stands until `resets_at`; with `resets_at` null it stands until a re-probe interval (provisional 15 minutes, BOB's) passes, after which `useCheck` lets one call through and a repeat limit re-records it. A malformed entry is refused `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID`. Never throws. Test: a spent-cap `limit` outcome recorded with a reset time makes `useCheck` refuse (`source: "provider"`) until that time and not after; the same call recorded twice stores one row; a null reset time allows one probe after the interval. Control: a plain rate-limit `error` outcome is not recorded here.
- **R19** (BR23) `aiProviderLimits({owner, viewer, at})` answers, to that account's owners only (R12's), each recorded provider limit still standing: `{entry_id, kind, scope, resets_at, recorded_at}`, naming no member, with the history of past ones; anyone else is refused as R12 refuses. It writes nothing and never throws. Test: an owner reads the standing limit and its reset time; a limit past its reset time is in the history only; a non-owner is refused. Control: an account with no recorded limit answers an empty list, not an error.
- **R20** (BR18, BR19; AD14, AD36; K2352 exception) **The ordered-list exception to refuse-at-limit.** `useCheck` still refuses an act on an account at its limit (R3) and never reads another account's limits (D38); the only way an act then continues on another account is `ai-accounts`' hop rules, which call `useCheck` for the next account with that account's own limits. `useCheck` marks which refusals a walk may continue from (`walkable`, R3): own and provider limits yes; `EXPLORE_NOT_ENABLED` (a use switched off), unjudged refusals no. Test: a walk helper over fake accounts moves from an account at an own limit to the next account judged on its own limits, never on the first's; it does not move from a switched-off use. Control: with no list declared, the act is refused as before.

**Money and estimates** (BR22, BR24; AIR26)
- **R21** (BR22; AIR26) **Money appears only where the plan reports it or the catalogue prices it.** A figure in money is `cost_reported` (the provider's or the gateway log's statement) or `cost_estimated` (the entry's catalogue price at its date); wherever neither exists the answer is `not_measurable` (word key `ai.use.notmeasurable`), with tokens and calls; money is never 0 for an unmeasured use, and a sum of money says how many calls were `unpriced_calls`. A plan whose money is `none` never has an estimate shown as spend. Reported and estimated are shown apart, and a unit other than `usd` is summed only with its own unit. Test: a `none` plan's act is `not_measurable`, tokens shown; a priced act shows `cost_estimated` apart from a reported one; a euro-unit cost is not added into a `usd` sum. Control: an act with a reported cost shows it and no `not_measurable`.
- **R22** (BR24; AIR26) `estimateSwitch({run, from_account, to_entry, at, viewer})` answers the added cost of moving the run to another entry, offered **before** the hop is shown (`ai-accounts`' hop notice): the cold-cache cost of resending the run's prefix, `{low, high, unit}` from the run's latest stated input tokens times the target entry's input price (low) and cache-write price (high), plus `payer_change` as `ai-accounts` R50 states it (`{from_owner, to_owner}` or null); null with `not_known` where the target is unpriced or the run states no input tokens, never zero. To the paying accounts' owners and the member whose run it is. Writes nothing; never throws. Test: a run with 20,000 stated input tokens on a fake target with prices answers the range; an unpriced target answers `not_known`; a same-owner hop says `payer_change: null`. Control: a non-owner, non-member is refused as R12 refuses.

**Thresholds and alerts** (BR24; AIR26)
- **R23** (BR24) `aiThresholdSet({owner, scope, unit, period, kind, level, by})` sets an owner's alert level: `kind` `percent` (of the matching limit, 1 to 99) or `amount` (a positive number in the unit); several per owner, scope, unit and period; `level: null` removes one. `unit` and `scope` follow R2's rules (including `usd` only where the plan's money allows). A threshold with no matching limit and `kind: "percent"` is refused `AI_THRESHOLD_INVALID`, naming the field. Who may set it is the owner's own act, as R2. Each set is appended with who and when. Test: 80 percent of a set day limit is stored; 80 percent with no limit is refused; a member who is not the owner is refused as R2 refuses. Control: an `amount` threshold needs no limit.
- **R24** (BR24) `thresholdsReached({viewer, at})` answers, for each account the viewer owns, each threshold first crossed in its current period: `{owner, scope, unit, period, period_start, level, crossed_at, key}`, a stable key per owner, threshold and period, so `notice-producers` raises one alert (word key `ai.alert.threshold`) and no more; it writes nothing and never throws. A threshold in `usd` is judged on `cost_reported_sum` where the account's calls all stated one, else on `cost_estimated_sum`, and says which (`basis`). Test: crossing 80 percent produces one entry whose key is the same on every read that period; a new period produces a new key; a `usd` threshold on a `none` plan was refused at set. Control: usage under the level answers none.

**Reconciliation and tests**
- **R25** (BR24; AIR26) `reconcile({owner, period, provider_figures: {tokens?, cost?, unit?, source}, viewer})` compares Civicsmith's totals for that account and period (R4's sums) with the figures handed in (the provider's own usage report, or the gateway log's per-call costs summed), to that account's owners only. It answers `{ours, theirs, difference, basis, within_tolerance}` per figure, `within_tolerance` against a constant (provisional 2 percent, BOB's); a figure the source did not state is null, never zero, and is not compared; a difference outside tolerance is recorded in the account's history as `reconcile_gap` with who and when. It writes only that record, and never throws. Test: matching fake figures answer `within_tolerance: true`; a 10 percent gap is flagged and recorded; a null `theirs` is not compared. Control: a non-owner is refused as R12 refuses.
- **R27** (K5459, Bob, 2026-10-27: "If the team or project is paying for the AI usage, then they should see who's using what so that they can manage usage with that member before it gets out of control."; varies K1450 for a paying owner's own account) `accountUseByMember({owner, viewer, at})` (`op=aiusebymember`) answers, for a **team or project** account only, to that account's owners (R12's: the team's active administrators, a project's owners), each member's use of that account over the thirty local days ending with `at` and over each limit's current period: `{member, calls, tokens, cost}` per member, `cost` as R14's (`not_measurable`, never zero), sorted by cost then tokens. A member's own account (`member:<id>`), a personal subscription above all, is never answered to anyone but that member: asked for one it refuses `NOT_A_PAYING_ACCOUNT`.
