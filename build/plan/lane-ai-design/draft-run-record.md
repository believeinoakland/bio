# The run record: deltas to observation-log and ai-runs (AI-DESIGN #3)

**Status** · READY for PLANNING, AI-DESIGN #3, 2026-10-27. Deltas to `build/requirements/observation-log.md` (highest id R39; new ids from R40) and `build/requirements/ai-runs.md` (highest id R90; `draft-agent-run-path.md` takes R91–R98, so new ids from R99). They answer N1219 (`build/plan/next.md`: the run log keeps no `read` or `disagree`, so `screens-inquiry` R58 cannot draw a run's reads or disagreements from `op=airunlog`) and DEC-466 U1 and U2 (`docs/development/DECISIONS.md`): each part of a run, the AI and account that did it, each switch with its reason, the member's refusal to go on, and the AI's name on every machine result. Not a ruling; every [rec] is BOB's. Ids are never reused. Words are `words.json` keys, tests assert keys, codes and fields, never English (K3810, K5418); "team" in every word people read (K3472).

**Conventions.** A changed clause is given in full; text not quoted stands. The run path's terms are `draft-agent-run-path.md`'s: `ai_account`, `label` (`{provider, host, model_version, effort, account_level, owner}`, answered by `agent-model`), a **segment** (R91: the stretch of a run between dispatches, on one account) and a **hop** (R92). A **part** here is a segment as the member reads it ("Part {n}"); not Gate 1's part ids.

## observation-log

- **R40** (T53; N1219; K3637, K3850; amends R1 and R2; `agent-steps` R36, R38) **A run's log entry may carry its reads and disagreements as data.** The entry written by the one writer gains two optional structured fields, stored with the row and served by every reader of the row, absent as `null`: `read` `{record, n, total, outcome}` (`record` a non-empty id string; `n` and `total` integers with `1 <= n <= total`; `outcome` `answered` or `refused`) and `disagree` `{record, levels}` (`record` as above; `levels` a list of at least two distinct names from R1's four levels, in the order the judgement named them). An entry carries at most one of the two, and only with authority kind `run` or `step` (R1); the fields change no state, coverage or sight of the row: R13 withholds a row whole, never with a field blanked, and the fields are exported and purged with the row. The refusals join R2's, in order after C-22.4, nothing written on any: a field of another shape, `OBSERVATION_FIELD_MALFORMED` naming the field; both fields, or either with another authority kind, `OBSERVATION_FIELD_NOT_ALLOWED` naming it (a new C-22 row, its number BOB's at START, one site each, K231; its sentence read by key). Test: an entry with `read: {record: "r1", n: 2, total: 3, outcome: "answered"}` is appended and read back with that field exactly and `disagree` null; one with `disagree: {record: "r1", levels: ["document", "content"]}` likewise; control: `n` 4 of `total` 3, one level, an `outcome` of `maybe`, both fields on one entry, and `read` on authority kind `sweep` are each refused with nothing written, and a row without either reads both as `null`, with its `detail` text unchanged.
- **R41** (T53; N1219; amends R13) **The fields have no sight of their own.** A viewer who may see the row sees both fields exactly; a viewer who may not sees neither, the row withheld whole (R13), so a `read` naming a record the viewer may not open never reaches her. Test: a viewer of the run reads the row's `read`; control: a viewer outside the run's sight reads no row and so no `record` id from it.

## ai-runs

- **R99** (T53; N1219; K3637; amends R12 as `draft-agent-run-path.md` amends it for naming, and `agent-steps` R36, R38) **A tick passes a log entry's `read` and `disagree` through.** R12 in full: in one transaction, each log entry is appended through observation-log's one writer as `authority_kind: run`, `actor_class: machine`, actor the run's AI principal, carrying the `read` and `disagree` fields the tick's entry holds unchanged (R40; a refused entry is returned in `refused`, the others appended); each consumed figure is added; the lease is extended; `state` replaces the run's state when given; ticks rise by one. If a bound is then exhausted the run ends through R14 (a `runtime` stop with condition `runtime-ceiling-reached`) and the answer carries `ended`. An entry whose field the writer refuses (R40) is returned in `refused` with that code and the rest of the tick stands. Test: a tick of three entries with `read` fields `1 of 3`, `2 of 3`, `3 of 3` appends three rows each with its field; control: an entry with `n` above `total` is returned in `refused` as `OBSERVATION_FIELD_MALFORMED` and the other two are appended.
- **R100** (T53; N1219; amends R24) **`op=airunlog` serves the fields.** R24 in full: absent or invisible: `found: false`, no entries. Otherwise the run's log entries in order, numbered from 1, an observation `result_ref` rewritten to that ordinal, each with its coverage and its `read` and `disagree` as stored (`null` when the entry has none; a screen draws "Reading {document} · {n} of {total}" and a disagreement from these fields and parses no `detail`, `screens-inquiry` R58); `limit` clamped to [1, 5,000] (200 by default) with `truncated`; `stopped`; and the vocabulary (states, levels, bounds, endings, coverage). Test: a run with a read entry and a disagreement entry answers both fields in order; control: an entry written before this change answers both `null`, and a viewer outside the run's sight gets `found: false` with no entries.
- **R101** (T53; AIR25, BR19; DEC-466 U2; amends R91) **Each segment records what the member reads as a part.** `ai_run_segments` (R91) also holds, per segment: `label`, the label of the segment's first conversation (the first of R91's `labels`, a cheaper sub-session's AI staying among `labels`), carrying `display_name` (R104), `provider` and `model_version`; and the account as `{level, provider, plan}` (`level` `member`, `project` or `group`; words say team), from `ai_account` at the dispatch, never an account id on the wire to the viewer, never the owner's name. `from` is the segment's `started` and `to` its `ended` instant (null while it runs), as the answers of R103 name them. Nothing is stored that the dispatch did not carry: a part's label is never composed here (`agent-model`'s, BR2) and never taken from a request. Test: a two-segment run on two accounts stores two rows, each with its label, account `{level, provider, plan}` and instants; control: a request-body `label` is ignored, and the first part's label is not rewritten when the second dispatches.
- **R102** (T53; AD37; DEC-466 U2; amends R92, R94) **A hop and a refusal to go on are in the record.** Each hop R92 writes is answered with `{reason, from, to, payer_change}` (`reason` of `ai-accounts` R50, `from` and `to` the accounts as `{level, provider, plan}` and entries as `entry_id`, `payer_change` `{from_level, to_level}` or null: levels, never owners' names). When the member answers a payer-changing hop with `op=aistop` (R94, decline), the same act that ends the run `cancelled` writes on the segment she would have continued from `end_refusal` `{by, reason, from, to, payer_change, at}` (`by` the run's member), the hop as it was offered; an accepted hop (`aicontinue`) writes none, and a hop refused by `hopFor` (`LIST_EXHAUSTED`) is not the member's refusal and writes none. Test: a `limit` hop onto the team's account from her own writes the hop with `payer_change` `{from_level: member, to_level: group}`; her `aistop` writes `end_refusal` with that hop and the run is `cancelled` with partial results kept; control: `aicontinue` writes no `end_refusal`, an exhausted list writes none, and a same-payer hop's `payer_change` is null.
- **R103** (T53; DEC-466 U2; N1219) **`runParts({run, viewer})` (`op=airunparts`) answers "How this run went".** A read, never throwing, writing nothing, to the run's viewers (R91's: the run's member and the paying owners; anyone else, and an absent run, `{found: false, parts: []}`): `{found: true, run, status, parts, truncated}` with `parts` in order, each `{n, label: {display_name, provider, model_version}, account: {level, provider, plan, own}, from, to, hop_in, end_refusal}`: `own` true when the account is the viewer's own; `hop_in` R102's hop that began the part (null for the first); `end_refusal` R102's (null when none); at most `RUN_PARTS_LIMIT` parts with `truncated` measured. A run that never switched answers one part with `hop_in` null; the screen decides whether to list one part. The screen's sentences are keys: `screen.run.parts.head`, `screen.run.part` (n, AI, account, `from`, `to`), `screen.run.switch` (AI, account, reason) and `screen.run.declined` (member), DEC-466 U2. Nothing in the answer is a secret, an account id, a content word or another owner's name. An unreadable store answers `{unreadable: true, parts: null}`, never an empty list (fail closed). Class member and admin, `machineClasses` [], read act, no rung (`draft-assistant-screens.md` §4); declared with the fields `accepts` `run`, `requires` `run`, the viewer the door's stamp and never the body. Test: a three-part run answers three parts with their labels, account levels, instants, two hops and, for a run she declined, the last part's `end_refusal`; a one-part run answers one with `hop_in` null; the paying owner reads the same parts. Control: another member who cannot see the run gets `found: false` and no part; a body naming a viewer is ignored; an account of another member's (opted in) shows `own: false` and no name; the answer carries no id or secret (a scan finds none); an unreadable store answers `unreadable`.
- **R104** (T53; BR2, BR29; DEC-466 U1; amends R72, R88) **The machine label names the AI, never the account.** Each `usage` entry's `label` (R72, R88) must carry `display_name`, a non-empty string equal to the display name `ai-catalogue` gives the entry `entry_id` (`entryOf(entry_id).display_name`); a label without it, or with another, is `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID`, counting nothing (the label itself stays `agent-model`'s, composed from the served identity and never from the account). The `{ai}` of `screen.machine.*.ai` is filled from `label.display_name` alone: no account name, owner, level or id enters that sentence, and rows the code reads today keep their English (DEC-466 U1). The same label is what R101 stores and R103 answers, and it is on every machine result a screen draws (an ask's, a draft's, a transcription's, a run production's, `draft-agent-run-path.md` R122). Test: a `usage` entry whose `label.display_name` equals the catalogue's is counted; a result drawn on the team's account and one on a member's own account carry the same `{ai}` for the same entry; control: a missing `display_name`, one that differs from the catalogue's, and one that equals the account's provider-and-plan name instead of the entry's are each `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID` with nothing counted.

- **R105** (T53; AIR28; BR27; K5489: "A, but allow the member the option of choosing whether to wait before switching."; amends `draft-agent-run-path.md` agent-worker R124's choices) **Wait a set time, then switch.** When a member is told her provider is down or slow (`aimoments` (d)), the choices are wait, switch, continue without AI, and a fourth: **wait a set time, then switch if it is still down**. `waitThenSwitch({run, member, wait})` (`op=aiwaitswitch`) takes `wait` from `WAIT_THEN_SWITCH_CHOICES` (5, 15 and 60 minutes: DEC-475, confirmed by BOB #323). The plain wait (`aiwait`, "Wait for") takes a length from the same list; at its end, still down, the member is told again (moment d). It records `{run, member, wait, from, at}` on the run. It holds the step until the wait ends or the provider recovers (`op=aipathready` answers `ready`), whichever comes first. If the provider has recovered, the step continues where it is and nothing switches. If not, it asks `ai-accounts.hopFor` with reason `unavailable` and switches at the step boundary (R102's record). The switch is shown and the member can stop it with `aistop`. A switch whose `payer_change` is not null still waits for her `aicontinue` first (AI3, K5477), even after the wait. Only the run's own member may choose it. Test: a wait that ends while still down switches and records the hop with reason `unavailable`; a provider that recovers within the wait continues with no switch; a switch to another owner's account still waits for `aicontinue`. Control: a `wait` not in `WAIT_THEN_SWITCH_CHOICES` is refused `WAIT_INVALID`; another member choosing it is refused as R103's viewers rule refuses.
