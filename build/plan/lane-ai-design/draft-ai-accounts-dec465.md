## New

- **R69** (T53; DEC-465; AIR32; BR13; amends R43, R61) **`op=aikeyseal`, the sealed hold.** aiconnect's step 3 has a fifth way, "Keep it sealed in your team's Civicsmith" (its words by key, DEC-465): `aikeyseal({account_id, secret, by})` replaces the credential of an existing account with a sealed one: means `sealed`, the secret sealed by the team's own copy exactly as R2 and the seal of `build/requirements/ai-accounts.md` R24 seal any secret (`sealText({owner, kind, text})`: AES-256-GCM under the owner's key; the owner is the account's), under the account's owner, and from then on read only through `accountFor` (R7) or `accountSecretFor` (R3) for the act that runs. The secret is never shown again, never logged, in no answer, error, history row, export or digest (R2, R64); the answer is `{ok, account_id, means: "sealed", held: true, since}`. It is `accountSet({account_id, secret, means: "sealed", by})` (R40) and so refuses exactly as `accountSet` does and in its order: the owner's authority (an active administrator for `group` (`NOT_AN_ADMIN`), a project's owner for `project:<id>` (`PROJECT_ACT_NOT_THE_OWNER`), the member alone for `member:<id>` (`NOT_YOUR_ACCOUNT`; `by` the door's stamp, R38); a machine credential `MACHINE_CANNOT_HOLD_ACCOUNT`); `NO_SECRET` for an empty secret; `MEANS_NOT_ALLOWED` when the plan's `means_allowed` lacks `sealed`; `DISCLOSURE_DUE` (R23) for a member while the disclosure is not recorded; an account that does not exist or is another owner's is `NOT_YOUR_ACCOUNT`. Each refusal writes nothing. Replacing a credential is recorded with who, when and the old and new means, never a secret (R16). Rung: ground `credential` (`op-grades` R2); class write act; not served to a machine. Test: an administrator seals a key on a team account, a project's owner on the project's, a member on her own; the account answers `held: true` with means `sealed`; a sentinel secret appears in no table but the sealed column, and in no answer, log, error, history row or export, and `accountSecretFor`/`accountFor` return it only to the owner's act. Control: a plain member sealing on the team's account is `NOT_AN_ADMIN` and another member on hers `NOT_YOUR_ACCOUNT`, a machine credential is refused, an empty secret is `NO_SECRET`, a plan not allowing `sealed` is `MEANS_NOT_ALLOWED`, none of them leaving a row; the same sentinel scan finds a sentinel deliberately written to a log.
- **R70** (T53; DEC-465; AIR44; BR18; amends R41, R61) **A member orders her own list.** `aiorderset` serves a member's own list as well as the team's (DEC-420): its body is `{owner: "group" | "member", account_id, position}` with `position` the 1-based place the account is to take; `owner: "member"` takes the member from the door's stamped `by`, never the body (R38), and `accountOrderSet({owner: "member:<id>", accounts, by})` (R41) is what it calls. The screen's "Move up" and "Move down" are an explicit position change (the account's place minus or plus one), never a toggle: the door removes the account from the list as read at that instant and inserts it at `position`, every other account keeping its relative order; a `position` outside 1 to the list's length is `ORDER_POSITION_INVALID` (`ai.refused.orderpositioninvalid`), an account not on the list `ACCOUNT_NOT_LISTED`, a machine credential `MACHINE_CANNOT_HOLD_ACCOUNT`, `owner: "group"` by a non-administrator `NOT_AN_ADMIN`. The list may hold the team's account only while its `share_with_lists` is on (R49), and her own accounts, a personal subscription among them (R48); shared accounts move like any other. The change is recorded with the new order (R16). Test: through the door, a member with three accounts moves the last to position 1 and the list reads in that order, the other two keeping theirs; `accountFor` for her act then walks that order (R11); a body naming another member acts on the caller's list only. Control: position 0 and 4 on a list of three are `ORDER_POSITION_INVALID` and the list stands; an account not on the list is `ACCOUNT_NOT_LISTED`; a plain member sending `owner: "group"` is `NOT_AN_ADMIN`, and the team's list is unchanged by her moving her own.

- **R72** (T53; DEC-465; AD37; K5457) **Turning a share flag off.** When `crossOwnerOptInSet` (R49) turns `share_with_lists` or `share_with_projects` off, in that same act the account is removed from every list it is on that is not its owner's own (R41), each removal a change (R16) recorded with the list's owner and the instant, and the owners of those lists (each owner of a project whose list held it, each member whose list held it) are told once through `notice-producers` that the account has left their list, naming the account by provider and plan and never carrying a secret. A run's segment already dispatched on the account ends as it ends; the next hop no longer finds the account (R50), a run that needed it takes R93's refusal of `draft-agent-run-path.md` if its list is then exhausted. Turning the flag on again restores no place: each list's owner adds it afresh. Test: a team account on two projects' lists and one member's: turning `share_with_lists` off removes it from all three, writes three removal rows and the flag row, and tells three owners once each; control: the team's own list keeps it, turning off an already-off flag writes and tells nothing, turning it on again leaves the three lists without it, and the notice carries no secret.
- **R73** (T53; DEC-466 U5; AIR45; amends R44) **`GATEWAY_UNAVAILABLE` names who can grant it.** The refusal R44 gives a `gateway` account when no gateway port is registered (the copy holds no Cloudflare permission for AI Gateway and its keys, `draft-instance-setup.md` R108) carries `grant_by: "hosting_account"` as its `extra` and its word key `ai.refused.gatewayunavailable` names who can grant it: whoever holds the team's Cloudflare account (the same person `instance-setup` R47 calls the hosting account's holder); it also says that the built-in AI, a sealed key (R69) and a reference (R43) work without it. Nothing else changes in R44. Test: with no port registered, `accountSet` for a `gateway` account and `gatewayKeyEnter` both refuse `GATEWAY_UNAVAILABLE` with `extra.grant_by` `hosting_account` and the key's words name it; control: with a stub port registered neither refuses, and a `sealed` account is set in the same copy with no port.
- **R74** (T53; DEC-466 U3; amends R25) **The readiness summary.** `accountsOnSummary({viewer})`, in-plane, reached by no route and answered to `instance-setup` alone (R25's family), answers `{team_on, projects_on}`: `team_on` the number of the team's accounts that are on and held (`accountsOf({owner: "group"})`'s `on` and `held`, R2), and `projects_on` the ids of the projects that hold at least one account that is on and held and that `viewer` may see (`membership`'s sight, R10: an invisible project is absent from the list, not named). A member's own accounts and personal subscriptions are never counted and never named (they are hers, R48). It carries no secret, writes nothing, never throws; unreadable it answers `{unreadable: true}` (fail closed: no account reads as on). - **R75** (T53; K5478; DEC-471: "the same sentence is the administrators' Queue item") **The credits stop is a Queue item.** When R45 flags an account it asks the queue for one item of the new kind `ai-credits-stop` (class `OBLIGATION` in `queue` R1), assigned to whoever may clear it (R68: the team's administrators, a project's owners, or the member for their own account), naming the account, the time of the call and the cause, read by key per cause: `screen.aiproviders.stands.credits` (`credits`, DEC-471), `screen.aiproviders.stands.othergateway` (`other_gateway`) and `screen.aiproviders.stands.logunreadable` (`log_unreadable`) (DEC-475, U10); one kind, the cause in the item (K5492). One item per flagged account: a later flagged call adds none. It is not dismissable; `aicreditsclear` (R68) closes it, and so does removing the account (`aiaccountremove`). This item replaces R45's one notice through `notice-producers`. Test: a credits-paid call creates exactly one item for the team's administrators with the account and time; a second flagged call creates none; clearing closes it. Control: a dismissal while flagged is refused and the item stays; an unflagged account has no item.
- **R76** (T53; AIR39; K5493: "They can choose to read them, or not.") **A dated terms change is a notice, never a step.** `termsNoticesDue({viewer, at})`, in-plane, for `notice-producers`, answers for each account the viewer may manage (R68's who) each binding term of its plan (`ai-catalogue` R22, R23 `terms_pending`) whose `effective_from` is within `TERMS_NOTICE_DAYS` after `at`: `{account_id, plan_id, doc, version, effective_from, key}`, a stable key per (account, doc, version), so one notice is raised (word key `ai.notice.termschange`, with the same link to the quotes as the connect act) and no more. Nothing waits on it: no account is turned off, no act is refused, no acceptance is asked. It writes nothing and never throws. Test: a term effective in five days on a team account's plan answers one row to its administrators, with the same key on every read. Control: a term past its date, or beyond the window, answers none, and the account serves throughout.
