**The account** (AIR1, AIR32, AIR45; BR13, BR17)
- **R40** (T53; BR17) An account is `{account_id, owner, provider, plan, means, entries, on, opt_in, flagged, set_at, by}`: `owner` spelled as R14 spelled it; `provider` and `plan` rows of the catalogue; `means` one of `gateway`, `sealed`, `reference`, `issued`, `none` (the built-in AI, no credential); `entries` an ordered allow-list (R46); an owner may hold several. `accountSet({owner, provider, plan, means?, entries?, secret? | reference?, terms_version?, account_id?, by})` refuses, writing nothing and in this order: the owner's authority (R1, R5, R10); `UNKNOWN_PLAN` when the catalogue has no such plan for that provider, `CATALOGUE_UNAVAILABLE` when it cannot be read (fail closed); `PLAN_SCOPE_REFUSED` and `PERSONAL_SUBSCRIPTION_LEVEL` (R42); `MEANS_NOT_ALLOWED` and the means' own refusals (R43); `ENTRY_NOT_ALLOWED` (R46); R47's refusal. It answers `{ok, account_id, owner, provider, plan, means, entries, on: false, set_at}`, never a secret, appends the account to its owner's list (R41) and records it. With `account_id` it replaces that account's credential, entries or means (same owner and plan), recorded. An omitted `means` is `gateway` for a team or project account of an API plan allowing it, `sealed` for a member's, else the plan's first allowed. Test: each of the three authorities sets a list; a list naming another owner's account with opt-in is kept; control: a member ordering the team's accounts, an unconsented account and a personal subscription in the team's list are each refused and the old list stands.

**Entries and narrowing** (AIR10; BR21)
- **R46** (T53; AIR10, AIR17) An account's `entries` is an ordered allow-list of catalogue entry ids, each in the plan's `plan_ids` (else `ENTRY_NOT_ALLOWED`), non-empty (else `ENTRIES_EMPTY`), changed by `accountEntriesSet({account_id, entries, by})` with the owner's authority. Above accounts, `offeredSet({owner, entries | null, by})` sets a ceiling of entries for work at that level (null: none): the team's by administrators, a project's by its owners, a member's own by that member. A lower level's set must lie inside every higher level's, else `NARROWING_ONLY` naming the entry. For an act, the entries allowed are the intersection of the account's allow-list, the team's ceiling, the project's when the act is directly in the project, and the acting member's own, so a later shrink above drops out below with no rewrite.
