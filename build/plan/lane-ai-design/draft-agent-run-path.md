**Conventions.** A changed clause is given in full; text not quoted stands. Tests assert word keys and codes (K3810). "team", never "group", in words (K3472). Ids are never reused. Every number is a named parameter (Suggestions).

**Shared terms.**
- **`ai_account`** (the account on the wire, as `ai-accounts.accountFor` answers it, its R7, without a secret): `{account_id, level, owner, member, project?, provider, plan, means, entry_id, entries, personal, suggestions, disclosed}`. `level` `member` | `project` | `group`; `means` `gateway` | `sealed` | `reference` | `issued` | `none`; `entry_id` the head entry, `entries` the allowed entries in order; `disclosed` the `{provider, host}` recipients already disclosed. Never `kind`, never a secret.
- **`label`**: `{provider, host, model_version, effort, account_level, owner}`, answered by `agent-model` from the served identity (BR2, BR29); no module here composes one.
- **`need`**: `{capabilities, cost}`, `cost` one of `low`, `standard`, `high`; a role asks for a need, never a model.
- **`ai_stop`**: `{reason, class?, retry_after?, recipient?}`, `reason` one of `limit`, `unavailable`, `lapsed`, `capability`, `auth`, `disclosure`.
- **part**: one of the six ids `ask`, `draft`, `read`, `transcribe`, `run`, `account` (`ai-catalogue` R14; they are the six gate parts, `run-rules` `GATE_PART_OF`'s values; Gate 1 verdicts are `ai-gate`'s).
