How can an administrator be removed when the team has only two?

How it works today. Removing an administrator takes a majority vote of the other administrators. With three or more, that works. With exactly two (you as founder, plus one other), one vote is never a majority, so nobody can remove anyone and the page shows no remove button at all. That is what you found. The rule exists so that one administrator can never throw out the other. You, the founder, can never be removed from inside Civicsmith, because you hold the hosting account. Whoever holds that can always take the team back from the hosting dashboard.

Why it matters. The protection doesn't really hold against the founder, who already has the hosting power. Meanwhile it leaves a founder stuck with an administrator who has left, lost their password, or should no longer have access.

  • RecommendedA · The founder may remove an administrator alone, with a reasonThe removal and its reason are recorded and shown to the team. Other administrators still need a majority vote, and still can't remove the founder. This gives the founder in the app the power they already hold at the hosting account, and nothing more.
  • B · Any administrator may remove one who has never signed inA safety valve for unused accounts only. Removing an active administrator still needs three administrators and a vote.
  • C · Keep it as it isAt two, add a third administrator first, then vote. Or use the hosting dashboard.
B2BOB · member-roster · adding an administratorasked 27 Oct

When an administrator can't act, how is another one added?

How it works today. The founder may add a second administrator alone. A third or later needs the approval of every administrator who has joined. People invited but not yet joined are not counted, so two unaccepted invitations don't block each other. What blocked you on plat-s1 was different. It already had two joined administrators: you and the account PLATFORM's earlier session used, which nobody could sign in to. Your new administrator therefore waited for that account's approval, which could never come. A waiting proposal like that also can't be cancelled; only a sent invitation can be withdrawn.

  • RecommendedA · The founder's approval is enough; a waiting proposal can be cancelledThe founder may add any administrator alone, as they already can at the hosting account. Other administrators' proposals still need every administrator's approval. The founder or the proposer can cancel a waiting proposal.
  • B · A majority of joined administrators is enough, not allOne absent or unreachable administrator no longer blocks. A waiting proposal can be cancelled.
  • C · Keep all-must-approve; only add cancelling a waiting proposalAn unreachable administrator still blocks additions until removed (see B1).
