## 2. What this means for the levels (reading of §1; mine, not Cloudflare's)

1. **Enterprise does not change the limit behind the 5xx.** The room's memory (128 MB) and its storage (10 GB) are stated for Free and Paid only, with no Enterprise figure; I found no page that offers to raise either. So the resets measured on `plat-s1` (the store object exceeding its memory) would happen the same way on an Enterprise account until the reading's garbage is cut (K5798) or the reading leaves the room. **Unknown:** whether a contract can raise the memory; no page says it can or cannot.
2. **Enterprise changes the upload ceiling** (5 GB through the Worker against 100 MB, 200 MB with Business), the **FedRAMP** jurisdiction, **Regional Services** (where requests are decrypted and Workers run; not on `workers.dev`), the billing (by contract) and the uptime promise's wording. Each is stated, not measured.
3. **Correction to my own drafts.** `service-levels-draft.md` §2 puts "region-held data" in Level 3's column, and §4 calls it Enterprise's. The current pages tie the `eu` and `us` jurisdictions to no plan (DL 58-66, RL 141-153), and K5443 (2) already places the R2 bucket in a jurisdiction at install. So region-held *storage* is available at Levels 1 and 2; what is Enterprise-only is FedRAMP and Regional Services (where requests are *processed*). The line for Bob (K5440's "keep my data in this region", worded honestly) stands. The level table is corrected (K5800, 2026-10-29: `service-levels-draft.md` §2 Region row, §4).
4. **A request body over 100 MB is refused before the Worker sees it** (W 53, the 413). The product's documents bound is 100 MB (`read-bounds.mjs`) and its archive bound 500 MB (524,288,000 B), so an archive over 100 MB reaches a Team or Institution copy only by a direct-to-R2 or multipart path (`captureuploadbegin` / `part` / `complete`, `study/s1-tooling-notes.md`); through one request it needs Enterprise. **Not measured:** the multipart path at 100-500 MB on `plat-s1`.

## 3. Level 3's added design (S2's figures; costed on paper)

Level 3 is Level 2 plus further splitting and read caching (`service-levels-draft.md` §2). From S2: **B packed** (bulk text and index in R2) reads 100 units in 136 ms against the room's 29 ms and searches a common term in about 258 ms against the room's 246 ms, at 1.24 times the text's bytes (`study/s1-b-ramp-memory.md`, packed layout); the room's own search read 341,131 rows for one common term at 1.4 million units and grows with the room, the packed index does not. **C** (a Worker-side cache): no gain shown on the screens; its hit rate under load is not measured (P-49). **A** (a room per project): paper only (`study/s0-options-for-the-split.md`). So for Level 3 what can be costed is B's bytes (1.24 times the text) and its read penalty (about 0.1 s a screen); C's value and A's per-room costs stay **not yet measured**.
