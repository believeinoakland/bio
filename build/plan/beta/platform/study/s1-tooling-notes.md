Copy: `https://plat-s1.believeinoakland.workers.dev` (installed alongside by Bob, signed 0.87.0, Level 1). Route: the agent proxy only (K4733), Chromium with `proxy: {server: process.env.HTTPS_PROXY}`, Playwright at `/opt/node-tools/node_modules/playwright` (require it by that path; `playwright-core` is beside it).

- **Accounts.** The copy holds the founder `sparky` (Bob's), and, from PLATFORM #1, administrator `plat-s1-admin` and member `plat-s1-member`. **Their passwords and the administrator's ten recovery codes are in PLATFORM #1's scratchpad only and cannot pass to a successor.** A successor needs a fresh administrator invitation from Bob (Members, invite with the role administrator, link `<address>/#invite=<token>`, single use, 7 days), then enrols itself at `/app/join` (`#jn-handle`, `#jn-pw`, `#jn-pw2`, button "Join with a handle and password"; fill the handle and passwords after any redraw, they empty), and invites its own member on the Members page (`#mi-id`, `#mi-cover`, `#mi-caps-0..3`, button `[data-act="memberadd"]`). Keep credentials in a 0600 file in the scratchpad, never print them.
- **Sign-in** (as the old lane's `probe.mjs` does): open `/`, fill `#fs-handle` and `#fs-pw`, click `[data-act="login"]`, wait for `/app`. A session's API token is in `localStorage` (the key whose JSON has `.t`); calls are `fetch('/api/?op=<op>', {headers: {Authorization: 'Bearer ' + t}})`.
- **Acts that ask for a reason.** Wait 4-5 s after the page loads before clicking an act (the shell binds late; an early click does nothing). The click opens a reason box in the act's slot: textarea `#cs-reason-<slot>`; send with `button[data-shell="reason-send"]`. The Projects page's "Start a project" is a disclosure: click the text first, then `#projects-title`, radio `#projects-visibility-discoverable`, `button[data-act="promote"]`.
- **The project made.** `PROJ-2026-7919-plat-s1-study-project` (owner `admin2`, discoverable), made through `op=promote`.
- **Upload.** One request: `POST /api/?op=captureupload&statement=<text>&name=<file>&type=<mime>` with the raw bytes as the body and the bearer header; `statement` is three lines ("How you got it: ...", "Where and when: ...", "What it shows, in your own words: ..."), at most 2,000 characters; a file over 64 MiB goes in parts (`captureuploadbegin`, `captureuploadpart`, `captureuploadcomplete`). The act answers 200 with an empty first body and the page then sends `op=promote` for the document bundle. **A captured file waits in Held captures until a member vouches; it is not in a project.** How a document is filed into a project (for the Project and Document screens) is **not yet found**: `/app/add?project=<id>` redirected to `/app/capture` and filed under no project; try the project page's own Add door or the vouch act (`op` names via a network trace: `page.on('request')` printing the op).
- **Address capture.** `/app/add`, `#ci-address`, radios `#ci-render-0` (as the site sent) and `#ci-render-1` (as a visitor saw it), `button[data-act="acquire"]`.
- **Timing.** The old lane's `probe.mjs` (`build/plan/lane-performance/tools/probe.mjs`; copy it, point its `require` at `/opt/node-tools`, set `BASE` and `JOURNEY_<ROLE>_HANDLE/PASSWORD` from your own variables) prints per page `api_done_ms` (raw), `net_done_ms` (proxy delay taken out), `requests`, `api`, `byOp`, `slowest_srv` with Server-Timing (`do-session`, `in-session`...). Run it 5 times per role and take medians. Paths: `/app`, `/app/queue`, `/app/held`, `/app/inbox`, `/app/add`, `/app/projects`; with data: `/app/project/<id>`, `/app/document/<id>`.
