In the beta, a team, a project or a member chooses which AI the assistant runs on, from a list Civicsmith keeps. Each AI on the list carries Civicsmith's evidence: the privacy checks the team set, and a test of whether it can do each part of the assistant's work (asking, drafting, reading documents, reading unclear pages, checks and plans, drafting a case's account).
