What must an AI prove before members can use it in the beta?

Why this matters. The assistant's work is split into parts: answering questions, drafting, reading documents, reading unclear pages, running checks and plans, and drafting a case's account. Before any part is switched on for members with a given AI (say Claude, GPT or Gemini), Civicsmith has to know that AI does that part well enough. Otherwise a member could get a wrong answer that looks right.

Today's rule (from 2026-10, N829, K2479). A part reaches members only after the AI passes a test set of real public matters: real Oakland documents and questions, with the correct answers written by people, not machines. That set was meant to be written later. It has never been written: it ships with no matters in it, and who writes it is yours to decide. So under today's rule, no AI can pass, and no member could use the assistant in the beta at all.

What Civicsmith can do without that set. It has its own automatic test for each part (Gate 1). It uses invented cases whose right answers are known, so a program can score the AI without a person reading each answer. It is cheaper and quicker, but the cases are made up, so they may miss how real documents trip an AI up. The pass marks for this test are yours to set (AD13), once a first measuring run on your sample accounts shows what the AIs actually score.

Example: before "read an unclear scanned page" is switched on with a given AI, Gate 1 feeds it 200 invented scanned pages with known text and scores how much it gets right. Under option B, if that AI clears the mark you set, members can use that part with that AI. Later, once the real Oakland test set exists, the AI is also scored on it, the score is shown to teams, and the part is switched off for that AI if it falls below the mark.

  • A · Keep today's ruleNothing reaches members until the real test set is written (by you, or by people you name) and each part passes it. The safest, but the beta has no assistant until that work is done, and that could take weeks.
  • RecommendedB · Gate 1 now; the real test set later, as a standing checkA part reaches members once the chosen AI passes Civicsmith's automatic test, at the marks you set after the first measuring run. When the real test set exists, it becomes an ongoing accuracy check shown to teams, and a part that falls below its mark there is switched off. The beta gets a working assistant, and every AI is still held to a measured bar. What it needs from you: setting the pass marks after the first run, and later, naming who writes the real set.
  • C · Let members use untested parts, labeled "untested"As test copies do today. The quickest, but members could rely on an AI that has never been checked for that kind of work.
