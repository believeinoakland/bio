## 0. Placement and what BOB's review changed

"Places" is not a module. Under N1257 the place's records are layers (`N1257-jur` R99, R101): Civicsmith's shipped layers (read-only) and the team's own layers. A government is not a place layer (a stated year has no level, and a state has no team layer), so the stated year is an entry of the one placeless team-rules layer (`N1257-mod local-facts` R24), which holds the team's own rules and per-government facts beside any number of place team layers (R115). The governing view (`combine`) carries it beside the place's records, tagged `origin: team`; `civil-time.fiscalPeriod` and money read the view and need no second path. Two facts about the draft N1257 that this file meets: (1) `fiscal_year` is not in R116's `TEAM_LAYER_SECTIONS`, so A2-R1 adds it, for the team-rules layer only; (2) R115/R23 want a `basis` (a held capture), `status: researched` and a second member's confirmation for every team entry, while DEC-458 says "a member states it ... with how they checked it" (Bob's K5456 words: "stated by a member with how they checked it"); that was Q-A2-1, decided by K5456: one member's statement with how they checked, no basis, no held source, no second confirmation. A2-R2 states the exception.
