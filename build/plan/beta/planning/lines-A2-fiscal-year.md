## 0. Placement

From the files: the place's records are `jurisdictions` (profile section `fiscal_year`, R49: `[{body, start MM-DD, named_by, label, status, basis}]`, `body` a counterparty body or `*`); it is pure (R17), layer 1. A member-stated fact with how they checked it is `local-facts` (layer 5): `factConfirm` takes `how`, and for `correct` a `value` and `source` (R1). The zone is the precedent: `@team/time_zone` stated with no profile (R21), each correction also setting the `record-core` setting `team_time_zone` (R22) read by earlier readers through pure `jurisdictions.governingZone` (R98); its screen is `screens-settings` R45. Date-to-year mapping is `civil-time.fiscalPeriod` (R21). Money reads it in `#period` (R29, R34). `instance-setup` is not involved. So the fiscal year copies the zone: local-facts holds the acts, a mirrored setting plus one pure reader in jurisdictions carries the value, civil-time and money read it. A **government** here is the `body` string R49 and money's period already use (6.3).

## 3. Draft lines

### jurisdictions

**R99** NEW (amends R49; pattern of R98; DEC-458) `governingFiscalYear(profileIds, teamYears, body)`, exported, pure: answers a government's fiscal year and where it comes from. `teamYears` is the map `{<body>: {start, named_by, label}}` the stated facts leave (local-facts R23-R24). Answer `{known: true, entry: {body, start, named_by, label}, from: "profile" | "stated"}`, in this order: the entry of `combine(profileIds)`'s view for `body`, else its entry for `*` (both `from: "profile"`; the place's records come first and a stated year never overrides them), else `teamYears[body]` (`from: "stated"`); otherwise `{known: false, why: {key, vars}}`. `body` empty, not a string or `*` answers `{known: false, why}` with a reason that no government was named; no `why`'s `vars` or key ever holds `*` as a body. A malformed `teamYears` entry, or a list that does not combine, is read as no year for that body. Never throws. `withStatedFiscalYears(view, teamYears)`, exported, pure: the view with one `fiscal_year` entry added per stated body that the view's `fiscal_year` does not already cover (its own body entry or `*`), each tagged `profile: "team"` and `status: "stated"`; the view given is not changed (R18). Test: a view whose profile gives a body its year answers `from: "profile"` even when a different stated year exists for that body; a body the profile lacks with a stated year answers `from: "stated"`; a body in neither answers `known: false`; body `*` or empty answers `known: false` and the reason holds no `*`; `withStatedFiscalYears` adds only uncovered bodies and `validate` accepts the result.

### civil-time

**R31** AMENDS R21 (DEC-458) `fiscalPeriod` over a view that carries a stated entry (tagged `profile: "team"`, jurisdictions R99) maps dates exactly as over a profile entry, and its answer also carries `from`: the entry's `profile` tag (a profile id or `"team"`). The undetermined answers (no `fiscal_year` for the body; EDTF spanning two years) are `{key, vars}` reasons (R30's rule) whose `vars` name the body the caller asked for and never hold `*`; when the caller names no body the reason says no government was named. No place is held in code (R27). Test: a view with `fiscal_year` `[{body: "B", start: "07-01", named_by: "end", profile: "team"}]` maps 2026-03-01 for `B` to the same label and span as a profile entry would, with `from: "team"`; a body with no entry is undetermined and `reasonText` of its `why` holds the body and no `*`; with body `*` or absent the reason says no government was named. Control: a profile entry's answer is unchanged.

### local-facts

**R23** NEW (amends R1, R2, R3, R6; R21's pattern; DEC-458) **A government's fiscal year is a fact the team states.** Path `@team/fiscal_year/<token>` (`teamFiscalYearPath(body)`, exported; the token encodes the name so `parseFactPath` returns it exactly; encoding BOB's; no profile id can spell it). `factPath`/`parseFactPath` (R6) name and parse it (`{team: true, fact: "fiscal_year", body}`); both stay pure. `factConfirm` accepts for it:
- `correct` with `value` `{start, named_by, label}` valid as one R49 entry (`jurisdictions.validate` of a profile holding that entry alone with `status`/`basis` supplied here), `source` (1-500) and `how` (1-500), with no profile held;
- `confirm` and `dispute` only when a stated value governs, else `NO_SUCH_FACT`.
Refusals, R1's order with these added: a body (the path's) that is empty, over 200 characters, or `*` is `NO_SUCH_FACT` for confirm/dispute and `FACT_VALUE_REFUSED` for correct (the stand-in is not a government); `FACT_HOW_REFUSED`, `FACT_VALUE_REFUSED` as R1; then `FISCAL_YEAR_IN_PROFILE` (new, row in family C-126, words by key, R16) when the active profiles' view already gives that government a year (its own entry or `*`), naming it: the place's records come first and a stated year fills only a gap (DEC-458). Machine or unstamped `by` is `MACHINE_CANNOT_CONFIRM`. The fact governs from its latest `correct`; `factStatus` of the path answers the status, the latest act's member, date and `how`, and the governing value marked "stated by <member>, <date>", per R2; with none, `absent`. Horizon 183 days (R3; local day in the team's governing zone, R21). A lapsed confirmation reads `unconfirmed` and the value still governs. R5 holds (append-only, nothing overwritten). Not listed by `factStatus`/`factsDue` without a path, as R21's. `statedFiscalYears({viewer})` (name BOB's) lists each stated government's `{body, path, value, status, by, at, how}`. Test: a `correct` with a valid value, source and how, on a team with no profile, answers ok and `factStatus` of the path answers `confirmed` or the correction's status with `how` and "stated by"; a repeat `correct` replaces the governing value and keeps both acts; a `correct` for a government the profile gives a year is refused `FISCAL_YEAR_IN_PROFILE` and writes nothing; body `*` refused; a machine `by` refused; `confirm` with nothing stated is `NO_SUCH_FACT`; a bad `start` (`13-40`) or `named_by` is `FACT_VALUE_REFUSED`. Control: `@team/time_zone` behaves as R21.

**R24** NEW (amends R22; DEC-458) Each `correct` of a `@team/fiscal_year/…` path also sets the `record-core` setting `team_fiscal_years` (name BOB's) to the map `{<body>: {start, named_by, label}}` of every government's governing stated value, in the same transaction; a copy holding corrections and no such setting has it built from them at this module's first read or write after this change. A reader that comes before this module or reads settings alone (`money`) takes the stated years from `getSetting("team_fiscal_years")` through `jurisdictions.governingFiscalYear` (R99), which answers the same value as `factStatus`. The setting holds only member-stated values: no profile's, and no key `*`. Test: after two `correct`s for two governments the setting holds both; a later `correct` for the first replaces only it; a copy with corrections and no setting reads it built; `governingFiscalYear` over the setting equals `factStatus`'s governing value for each. Control: a refused `correct` leaves the setting unchanged.

### money

**R36** NEW (amends R29, R34; DEC-458) **A fiscal key is mapped by the government's own year, from the place's records or a stated fact.** A period `{fiscal, body}` is resolved with `jurisdictions.governingFiscalYear` over the active profiles and `getSetting("team_fiscal_years")` (`civil-time.fiscalPeriod` over `withStatedFiscalYears` of the view), the place's records first. A stated year maps a key exactly as a profile's does. The fact keeps the date range as today, not the year's origin. The refusals of R29/R34 stand and add: a government named with no year in the records and none stated is refused `MONEY_BAD_PERIOD` with a sentence that names the government and says that no fiscal year is held for it and none has been stated by a member (words by key, the design's; until the row exists the job REPORTs it, as R34); no fiscal refusal, detail, key or var ever holds `*` or any other stand-in for a government. The stated year of one government is never used for another, and a period sent with no body is never resolved from a stated year (it is R34's refusal). Test: with no profile, `recordfact` with `{fiscal: "FY2026-27", body: "B"}` after a member stated B's year (start 07-01, named_by end) records the same range a profile entry would; before the statement it is refused `MONEY_BAD_PERIOD`, the sentence holds B and no `*`; a stated year for B does not map a key for another government C (refused, naming C); a profile entry for B beats a different stated one written into the setting directly. Control: the same fiscal key with `{from, to}` is accepted as before; a period with a fiscal key and no body is refused as R34 states, no `*` in the sentence.

**R37** NEW (amends R34; DEC-458; supersedes R34's last sentence) **The fiscal-year view is offered only for a government whose year is known.** `fiscalYears({viewer})` (op name BOB's; a read, writes nothing, `moneyOps` arm, declared in `DECLARED_OPS`) answers `{ok: true, governments: [...]}`: every government the active profiles' `fiscal_year` names (a `*` entry answered once as `every_government: {…}`, never as a government named `*`) and every government with a stated year, each `{body, known: true, start, named_by, label, from: "profile" | "stated", profile?, status?, by?, at?, how?}`; for a stated one `status`, `by`, `at` and `how` are `local-facts.factStatus`'s of its path. `known: false` governments are not in the list. A question `fiscalYear({body, viewer})` (name BOB's) answers for one government either the same row or `{ok: true, known: false, says: localize(key, {body})}`, the sentence the screen shows where the year is not known: it names the government, says that no fiscal year is held for it in the place's records and none has been stated, and that a member can state it. When no government's year is known (no row, no `*` entry), `fiscalYears` answers `governments: []` with `none_known: true`, and `says` the sentence for that case (no government named). Test: the test profile's bodies answer `known: true, from: "profile"`; after a member states B's year it answers `from: "stated"` with the member, date and `how`; a government neither answers `known: false` and `says` holds the government's name and no `*`; with no profile and nothing stated, `none_known: true`; a profile `*` entry answers once as `every_government`. Control: `fiscalYears` writes nothing; nothing it answers holds `*` as a government.

**R38** NEW (amends R37; DEC-458) Money reads a stated fiscal year's status through `local-facts.factStatus` (not in tests only). A stated year whose confirmation lapsed (`unconfirmed`) is still known and still maps keys, and R37's row says so; a **disputed** one is still known and still maps, with the dispute named in the row (see open point 6.2 on whether it should). Test: a lapsed stated year (183+ days) answers `known: true` with `status: "unconfirmed"`; a disputed one answers `known: true` with `status: "disputed"`.
