## Where the revoking acts live

- `projectRemove` (membership R36) is the only act that ends a member's place in a project: `projectLeave` (R35) only records `leaving` and "removes nobody", and R54's `isJoinedParticipant` is `joined` or `leaving`, so a leaving member is still joined (K5486) until an owner removes her. `projectOwnerRemove` (project-roster R4) takes ownership away and leaves her a participant: she is still joined and can still place a shared account, so it is not a revoking act here.
- Team revocation (member-roster R5, R16; tells R25's `onRevoked`) leaves the participant rows in place (R127 answers them "whatever the member's status"), so no project act fires. It reaches ai-accounts only through `onRevoked`.
- Layers (`build/modules.json`, `layers.md`): `member-roster`, `membership`, `project-roster`, `ai-accounts` are all layer 2, in that order. `projectRemove` stays in `membership` (project-roster note "Stays in membership under C1'") and `onRevoked` is in `member-roster`, both earlier than `ai-accounts`. A listener registered by `ai-accounts` is a downward use: no upward edge, no new module edge (ai-accounts already uses membership and `onRevoked`).

## membership: the provided line

- **R158** (T53; K5501; AD37; amends R36; R27's pattern in `ai-accounts`, `member-roster` R25) `onProjectLeft(module, fn)`: a later module (`ai-accounts`, its R77) registers once at start; a malformed registration, or a second by the same module, is refused through `member-roster`'s `listenerRefusal` (R26: `LISTENER_MALFORMED`, `LISTENER_DECLARED`). Each time a participant of a project stops being a participant, every registered `fn` is called once, in the modules' total order (`member-roster` R27's `MODULE_ORDER`), inside the act and after its writes (in the caller's transaction), with `{memberId, projectId, by, why, at}`: `by` the act's actor, `at` its time, `why` a word key and today only `removed` (R36 `projectRemove`: whether or not she had asked to leave, so the member who had recorded `leaving` (R35) is called when an owner removes her, and not before). Acts that leave her a participant call nobody: `projectLeave` (she is `leaving`, still joined, R54), `projectOwnerRemove` (project-roster R4), a re-join (R34). A listener's answer is ignored; a listener that throws changes neither the act, its answer, nor another listener's call. A refused act (R36's `NOT_THE_OWNER`, `NO_SUCH_HANDLE`, `TARGET_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT`, `OWNER`) calls nobody. Any later act that ends a participation adds its own `why` here, never a second registration. Provides (cited by `ai-accounts` R77): `onProjectLeft`. Test: a registered listener is called once inside `projectRemove` with the removed member, the project, the owner as `by` and `why: "removed"`, after the participant row is gone and in the same transaction; a member who had asked to leave is called at the removal; control: `projectLeave`, `projectOwnerRemove` and a refused removal call none, a throwing listener leaves the removal and its answer as they were, a second registration by one module is `LISTENER_DECLARED`, a non-function is `LISTENER_MALFORMED`, and a member who is no participant is `TARGET_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` and calls none.

## ai-accounts: the use

- **R77** (T53; K5501; AD37; K5486; R27's pattern; amends R41, R72, R27) **A member who can no longer place a shared account loses her placements.** (1) *Record.* An entry on a list that is not its owner's own (R41, R49) carries who placed it and when, `placed_by` (the door's stamp, R38) and `placed_at`, written in the act that puts it there and shown in R16's row for it (the list's change); an entry written before this line has `placed_by` null. (2) *Listener.* At start one listener on `membership.onProjectLeft` (its R158, refused through `listenerRefusal` as R26 is). When a member stops being a participant of a project (`why: "removed"`), in the same act, from that project's list (the owner `project:<id>`) are removed: every entry whose `placed_by` is her and whose account is not the project's own (the team's account, another member's account that she placed under R49, or her own that she placed), and her own member account (`member:<her id>`, R49's `share_with_projects`) whoever placed it, since she is no longer joined and R49 offers it only to a project she has joined; an entry placed by another member (the team's account placed by an owner, say) stays. Each removal is a change (R16) recorded with the project, the account, who had placed it, the act's `by` and the instant. (3) *Told.* The project's owners other than the removed member are told once, per act and project, through `notice-producers` that the account(s) have left their project's list, each naming the account by provider and plan (as R72 does) and never carrying a secret, digest or key; a removal that removes nothing tells nobody and records nothing. (4) *The run.* A run's segment already dispatched on a removed account ends as it ends; the next hop no longer finds the account (R50), and a run that needed it takes the refusal R72 names if its list is then exhausted. She is no longer a joined participant, so `accountFor` (R11) refuses her act naming the project `PROJECT_ACT_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` as before. (5) *No notice to her.* The act is hers or an owner's to know by the removal itself; this line sends her nothing. Test: a team account placed by member A and a second placed by owner O on project P, and A's own account placed by O: an owner removes A (`projectRemove`): the team account A placed and A's own account leave P's list, the team account O placed stays, two removal rows are recorded with `by`; P's owners other than A are told once naming each account by provider and plan; the team's own list and A's own list are untouched; a segment dispatched on the account ends as it ends. Control: an entry with `placed_by` null stays (except A's own account); a project's own account stays; a removal of a member with no placements writes and tells nothing; `projectLeave` (she is `leaving`) and `projectOwnerRemove` call this listener not at all and the placements stand; a throwing notice changes nothing of the removal; the notice carries no secret (sentinel scan, R64).
- **R27 amended (full new text)** (T53; K5501; amends R27) At start, one listener on `member-roster.onRevoked` (its R25). A revoked member's sign-in, its token and her projects' sign-in accounts are cleared in the revoking act, each a change (R16), each told through R26; and, in the same act, her placements are removed from every project's list exactly as R77 (2) removes them for a project she is removed from (the entries she placed that are not the project's own, and her own member account whoever placed it), each a change (R16), the owners of each project told once through `notice-producers` as R77 (3), the owners other than her. This is the same listener function R77 registers its removal in: no second listener on `onRevoked`. Team revocation does not call `onProjectLeft` (her participant rows stay, `membership` R127). Test: a revoked member's placements on two projects leave both lists in the revoking act, with their change rows and one notice per project; control: another member's placements and the team's own list stand, a revoked member with none writes and tells nothing, and a throwing notice changes nothing of the revocation.

## Open points for BOB (technical)

1. **Who placed it is not recorded.** R41's `accountOrderSet({owner, accounts, by})` replaces a whole list and R16 records who set the list, not who placed each entry, so "her placements" cannot be told from the owners' placements. [rec: R77 (1): `placed_by` and `placed_at` on each non-owner entry, null for entries before it; legacy entries are then kept, which fails safe.]
2. **Her own account on a project's list, placed by an owner.** R49 lets an owner place a member's `share_with_projects` account on a project the member has joined; the ruling words "her placements". [rec: remove it too when she leaves the project, whoever placed it, since R49 offers it only to a project she has joined; the owners are told as for the others.]
3. **"Leaving" is still joined.** `projectLeave` records `leaving` and removes nobody; R54 counts `leaving` as joined; K5486 reads the same. [rec: the listener fires at the removal that completes the leaving (R36), not at the request; her placements stand while she is `leaving`.]
4. **Owner role loss is not a revoking act.** `projectOwnerRemove` leaves her a joined participant; she may still place a shared account (K5486: joined, not only led). [rec: no call; if BOB later wants the placement to follow ownership, it is a different ruling.]
5. **Where the T51 split left the ids.** `onRevoked` and `listenerRefusal` are `member-roster` R25 and R26 (membership R79 and R81 are retired pointers). [rec: R158 and R77 cite them under the new ids; correct ai-accounts' Uses list in writing.]
