## Questions for Bob raised in L4

**Q-A1-1. A new subject the question's graded connections cannot carry.** A question's connections may be graded on who it is about (a document that names the old person, graded B). If a member changes the subject to someone those connections cannot support, what happens? Options: A, the change is refused and names the connections; she restates those grades first (nothing is lowered for her). B, the change is allowed, and the question's strength is read from what the record now earns for the new subject, shown before and after. Recommendation: A. It keeps your "never lower a grade on the member's behalf"; B in effect lowers the question's strength without a member's act.

**Q-A1-2. Who may change a question's subject.** You said "a member". Which members? Options: A, any member who can see the question (the same as restating its grounds). B, only the member who opened it and the project's owners. C, only the project's owners. Recommendation: A. Every change is attributed, reasoned and in the history for all to see.

**Q-A1-3. A published or divided question.** A question that is part of a published case is frozen, and a divided question is closed. May its subject still be changed? Options: A, neither: the way is to reopen the question (as for regrouping) or ask a new one. B, both allowed, the published edition's signed copy staying as published. Recommendation: A. A subject that moves after publication would make the live question disagree with the signed case.

**Q-A1-4. Taking the subject off.** You said "add or change". May a member also remove the subject, with a reason? Options: A, yes, with a reason like any change. B, no: it can be changed but never emptied. Recommendation: A. A question named about the wrong person must be correctable; the history keeps it.

**Q-A1-5. A concluded question.** May the subject change after the question reached a conclusion? Options: A, yes: the conclusion stands, and the page shows the strength before and after (this means a conclusion's strength can move without it being re-concluded). B, the conclusion must be withdrawn first. Recommendation: A, with the pair shown. If you see doctrine in a conclusion standing while its strength moves, choose B.
