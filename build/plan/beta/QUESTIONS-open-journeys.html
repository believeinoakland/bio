What the alpha walk found · from the JOURNEYS lane

The JOURNEYS lane walked every screen of the alpha (0.87.0) that needs no assistant and found 130 defects. Most are fixed in the plan without you. These seven need your decision, because each one is about who may do something, what a member may see, or what Civicsmith records.

J1JOURNEYS · R-23asked 27 Oct

Can an administrator set aside held material in their own name?

Why this matters. When a document is held back (for example, it might name a private person), the member who added it can set it aside. That act is defined as the member's own. The walker was signed in as an administrator, and the screen gave no clear answer.

Example: Rosa is the team's administrator and also works its cases. She adds a scanned letter that gets held. Can she set it aside from her own sign-in, or must she use a separate member sign-in?

  • RecommendedA · Yes, when the administrator is also a person on the teamTheir own sign-in counts as a member's. An administrator account that isn't a team member sees a sentence saying who can do it.
  • B · No: an administrator signs in as a member to do it
  • C · Members only, and the screen says so
J2JOURNEYS · R-29asked 27 Oct

When a member opens a record that isn't theirs to see, does Civicsmith admit it exists?

Why this matters. A link can reach a member for a record in a project they haven't joined. Saying "this isn't yours" confirms the record exists; saying "nothing here" hides it but can confuse a member who was sent the link on purpose.

  • A · "This isn't yours", which confirms it exists
  • B · "Nothing here", the same as for a record that doesn't existHides that it exists.
  • RecommendedC · "You can't open this", with who to askThe project's owner is named only when the member can already see that project.
J3JOURNEYS · R-66asked 27 Oct

What kind of proceeding can a team register before it has chosen its places?

Why this matters. The kinds of proceeding (a rate case, a hearing, a grand jury complaint) come from a team's places and their rules. A new team that hasn't chosen its places is offered nothing, and the walk stopped there (journey 14).

  • A · The member types the kind's name
  • B · A short built-in list of general kinds, such as "a court case" or "a hearing"That's local fact under another name, which you've kept out of the product.
  • RecommendedC · The screen says to choose the team's places first and links there, with A beside it so the team isn't stuck
J4JOURNEYS · R-72asked 27 Oct

What can a person's page record?

Why this matters. A person's page has a heading promising posts, memberships, credentials and interests, but its "add" offers six other kinds. The walker recorded a board membership and it was saved as "Born".

  • RecommendedA · Add membership and credential to what can be recordedThe page already shows them; only adding them is missing.
  • B · Change the heading to match the six kinds offered today
  • C · Memberships only through ties between people and bodies
J5JOURNEYS · R-94asked 27 Oct

Who files a correction, a withdrawal or a court order on a case's docket?

Why this matters. The docket page promises these can be filed there, but the walk found the owner couldn't file anything. A withdrawal is already the project manager's.

  • RecommendedA · The project's owner, from the docket page
  • B · Any member who has joined, for a correction; the owner for the others
  • C · Not from the docket page at all, and the page stops promising it
J6JOURNEYS · R-96asked 27 Oct

When a new edition of a case is published, is that recorded on its docket?

Today neither the docket nor the case page shows that a new edition came out.

  • RecommendedA · Automatically, with the edition's "what changed" textReaders look for changes in both places.
  • B · The owner files it
  • C · The case page shows the change; the docket doesn't
J7JOURNEYS · R-107asked 27 Oct

Is an outside reader's acknowledgement shown publicly by name?

Why this matters. Someone outside the team, such as a city clerk, can acknowledge a published case. Today it isn't clear whether their name will be public, and they aren't told before sending.

  • RecommendedB · Shown by name only if they tick that it may beNaming a person publicly should be their choice.
