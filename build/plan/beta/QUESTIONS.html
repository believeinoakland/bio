Speed and where a team's data lives · from the PLATFORM lane

You ruled that a team's copy of Civicsmith has three service levels (Team, Organization, Coalition), and that its data store is created near the team (K4746, K4747). Today only Level 1 exists: one data store per team. These questions set the beta's speed bar and what teams are told.

Only Level 1 exists and can be measured. Levels 2 and 3 depend on a study of how a record is split across data stores.

  • RecommendedA · Show Level 1's measured figures; name Levels 2 and 3 as "when your record outgrows this", with no figuresFollows your rule that no figure is shown before it is measured.

Reading versus interpreting big documents · from the CAPABILITIES lane

Settled already: every document a member adds is kept whole and read whole, so every line can be searched and cited (K4571). Interpreting is a separate, selective act: turning parts of a document into structured facts, such as a sheet's figures, names or dates. You said deciding what to interpret "is itself an investigation of a different kind" (K4632). Today, a sheet's cells are interpreted only for the range a member opens or cites.

C1CAPABILITIES · N1272asked 27 Oct

Who chooses which parts of a document are interpreted?

Picture a 400-page budget PDF or a 600,000-row register. It is kept and searchable, but only a few pages matter to this investigation.

  • A · Only what a member opens or citesToday's rule, extended to every kind of file. Predictable, and nothing is interpreted unasked.
  • B · A member marks parts to interpret for a named investigationFor example, "interpret pages 3–9 for the sewer fund". A deliberate act, with a record of why.
  • C · Civicsmith proposes parts from the investigation's own words, without AIMatched headings, search hits, names and amounts, listed for a member to accept or edit. Nothing is interpreted until a member accepts.
  • RecommendedA now, B as the member's deliberate act, C once tested on real documents; the assistant's proposals only after the assistant is built. Never "interpret everything up to a size"Interpreting everything would make work and storage grow with every document, which your rule was written to avoid.
C2CAPABILITIES · N1272asked 27 Oct

How is this shown, and how does a later investigation find what wasn't interpreted?

Every line stays searchable whichever you choose.

  • RecommendedA · One plain line on the document page, plus an offerFor example: "Read in full and searchable. Interpreted for the sewer fund: pages 3 to 9. Not interpreted: the rest." A later investigation sees "Interpret parts of this for this investigation", with who interpreted what before, and when.
  • B · A, plus a list on each investigationThe list shows held documents that matched its searches but haven't been interpreted for it. More helpful, but more on the screen. Could follow later if members ask.
C3CAPABILITIES · N1269asked 27 Oct

When do members learn the size limits?

You said "we may need to communicate some limits to members - like a maximum file size for each type".

  • RecommendedA · Before adding a file, by kindThe add screen states the largest file read whole: documents 100 MB or 5,000 pages, spreadsheets 50 MB or about 1 million rows, images 50 MB, archives 500 MB. A larger file is refused in plain words, with its size and the limit, never half-read.
  • B · Only when a file is refusedLess to read, but the member learns only after waiting.
  • C · A "comfortable" size and a larger "possible but slower" sizeMore honest about waiting time, but harder to explain.
C4CAPABILITIES · N1269asked 27 Oct

What about a file over the limit that still matters?

For example, a 5 GB register.

  • RecommendedA · Refuse it, say why, and offer a way inFor a register, bring in only part of it by a filter (N1259). Otherwise, the member brings in the part they need.
  • B · Keep it, read only the first part and the parts a member asks for, and say exactly what was readRisks a member believing it was all searched. Only after C1's proposals work well.
  • C · Not in the beta; study it once real members hit the limitThe fallback for anything beyond A.
Places in layers · from the CAPABILITIES lane

You decided (DEC-404) that Civicsmith ships four read-only layers of places and rules (federal, state, county, city). Teams report errors but don't edit a layer, and a team's own research sits beside a layer and serves only that team. Three points remain.

C5CAPABILITIES · N1257asked 27 Oct

What does Civicsmith promise about how current the layers are?

Laws change, offices are renamed, and holiday lists come out late. In October 2026, neither the City's nor the court's 2027 holiday list had been published. Teams' deadlines rest on these layers.

  • RecommendedA · Show beside each rule where it came from and on what dateFor example, "read from the Oakland city clerk on 3 October 2026", and how far its calendars reach. A count past that shows as "not known", never a guess. Civicsmith promises the date it read, not that the law hasn't changed since, and says "not legal advice". Updates come with releases, and an administrator is told once which rules changed.
  • B · Promise a delay, such as "within 30 days of a change"Needs people and a process Civicsmith may not have, and a broken promise about a deadline is the worst failure.
  • C · Show no datesCheap, but a team can't judge a rule.
C6CAPABILITIES · N1257asked 27 Oct

What does "read-only" mean when a team knows a fact is wrong today?

Today a member can correct a holiday date, an office's hours or the time zone for their own team, marked as theirs, with how they checked it (K921, K925). DEC-404 says a team does not edit a read-only layer.

  • RecommendedA · The layer never changes; a team's own marked correction to a calendar fact stands, and laws and deadlines can only be reportedA closed office is a fact the team can see, and deadline counts must follow it. A team may also add its own rule, under a different name, beside a shipped one, never over it.
  • B · Reports only, for everythingThe strictest reading of "does not edit". A team with a wrong holiday waits for the next release.
  • C · A team may put its own value over a shipped one, markedContradicts DEC-404.
C7CAPABILITIES · N1257asked 27 Oct

What about a team outside the United States?

The four levels, and the federal layer, are the United States'. Today a team elsewhere gets nothing filled in (DEC-150).

  • RecommendedA · Say so when the team choosesCivicsmith holds United States places. A team elsewhere names its place, uses none of the four levels, and adds its own offices, as today. The level names stay.
  • B · Rename the levels in general terms (nation, region, county, city), and let a team add a national layerA bigger change to the words and to your decision.
