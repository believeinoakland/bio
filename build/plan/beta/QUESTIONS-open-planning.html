L13PLANNING · A1 · a question's subjectasked 27 Oct · explained more 27 Oct

If a member changes who a question is about, and the evidence already graded doesn't support the new subject, what happens?

Background. You ruled (A1, DEC-457) that a member can name who a question is about, and change it later with a reason. A question's strength comes from its connections: each piece of evidence a member has linked to the question and graded (A strongest to D weakest) for how well it supports the question about its subject. So a grade is tied to the subject it was given for.

Example: the question "Did the Controller's Bureau approve the overtime payments?" has a memo signed by the Bureau, graded B. A member changes the subject to Jane Ruiz, the Controller herself. The memo is signed by the Bureau, not by Jane, so the B no longer clearly holds for her.

You also ruled that Civicsmith never lowers a grade on a member's behalf: only a member changes a grade.

  • RecommendedA · Refuse the change until the grades are restatedCivicsmith names the connections that no longer fit (here, the memo). A member re-grades each for Jane, then the subject change goes through. No grade moves without a member's act.
  • B · Allow it, and let the strength fallThe subject changes at once. Civicsmith recomputes the question's strength from what the evidence supports for Jane, and shows the strength before and after. Quicker, but the question's strength drops without anyone choosing it.
