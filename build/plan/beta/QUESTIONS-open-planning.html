When a typed page is cited, does the record grade it automatically?

This is a point of doctrine. Today, a connection nobody has graded counts for nothing until a member grades it.

The question: does a typed page's grade apply on its own when the page is cited (rising by itself when it is checked), or is the typed grade only a ceiling that the member states?

  • RecommendedA · The record grades it, as it already does for derived connections
  • B · A ceiling only; the member states the grade, at or below it

Do further checks raise a typed page higher?

  • RecommendedA · No: one check raises it from D to C; later checks are named but raise nothing
  • B · Each further check raises it again, up to a limit

Who chooses how a typist or checker is credited in a published case, and what is the default?

  • RecommendedA · Each person chooses for their own typing, per edition; the default is "a member of the team"; it never blocks signing
  • B · The team chooses for everyone; the default is their name
