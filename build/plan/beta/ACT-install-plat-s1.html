You asked for the study of Levels 2 and 3 to start (K5444). Its first measurements need a copy of Civicsmith separate from biosmoke8, which teams use for testing. Two things to avoid. On step 5 the page also offers to "update" or "remove" biosmoke8: press neither. On step 7 a one-time password appears: do not paste it into the chat. Keep it to yourself.

Before you start: be signed in to Cloudflare in your browser, on the account you use for Civicsmith.

  1. Open https://newgroup.believeinoakland.workers.dev/
  2. When asked "Do you already have a Cloudflare account?", press Yes.
  3. Type the short name plat-s1 and press Continue. A box says the name can never be changed: confirm it.
  4. Press Continue to Cloudflare, and on Cloudflare's page allow the six permissions it lists.
  5. You'll see a page saying Civicsmith already exists in this account, with the words "Nothing was created or changed". That is expected. Under "Install alongside it", type plat-s1 again, press that button and confirm. If Cloudflare asks again, allow it.
  6. Watch the progress lines for about 20 minutes. There's nothing to choose along the way.
  7. At the end you'll see the copy's address and a one-time password. Don't paste the password into the chat.
  8. Press "Continue to your team's Civicsmith: become its first administrator". Choose your own handle and a password of at least 12 characters, and save the ten recovery codes. These are yours alone.
  9. In the copy, open Members and add a member with the role administrator. It shows an invitation link once, starting with the copy's address and containing #invite=. Copy that link and paste it into the chat with me. That's how the study lane gets in. It works once, and the lane uses it within the hour.
