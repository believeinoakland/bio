Reading versus interpreting big documents · from the CAPABILITIES and PLANNING lanes

Settled already: every document a member adds is kept whole and read whole, so every line can be searched and cited (K4571). Interpreting is a separate, selective act: turning parts of a document into structured facts, such as a sheet's figures, names or dates, done only for what a member opens, cites or marks (K5445, K5496).

Who prepares the answer key for testing which parts Civicsmith suggests interpreting?

Why this matters. Before the beta, Civicsmith's suggestions of which parts of a document matter to an investigation are measured on a fixed set of real files: how many of its suggestions are right, and how many right parts it misses. That needs an answer key prepared by a person who knows civic investigations, without seeing Civicsmith's suggestions first. The result decides whether suggestions ship.
