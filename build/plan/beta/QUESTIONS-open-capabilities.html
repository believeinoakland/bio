Settled already: every document a member adds is kept whole and read whole, so every line can be searched and cited (K4571). Interpreting is a separate, selective act: turning parts of a document into structured facts, such as a sheet's figures, names or dates, done only for what a member opens, cites or marks (K5445, K5496).
