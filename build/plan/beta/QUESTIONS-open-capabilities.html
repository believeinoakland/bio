Reading versus interpreting big documents · from the CAPABILITIES lane

Settled already: every document a member adds is kept whole and read whole, so every line can be searched and cited (K4571). Interpreting is a separate, selective act: turning parts of a document into structured facts, such as a sheet's figures, names or dates. You said deciding what to interpret "is itself an investigation of a different kind" (K4632). Today, a sheet's cells are interpreted only for the range a member opens or cites.

Files and photos · from the FILES lane
F4FILESasked 27 Oct

When a case is published, does a photo in it show where the photo was taken?

Why this matters. Most phone photos carry the place they were taken, often to within a few feet. You ruled (DEC-460) that Civicsmith keeps that data beside the photo's safe copy, and the member sees it in their own record. A published edition is public. Showing the place there can reveal a member's home, a source, or where a witness was standing.

Example: Ana photographs a leaking gas line from her front porch and adds it to a case. If the case is published with the photo's place showing, anyone reading it can work out where she lives.

  • RecommendedA · The author chooses, per photo; off unless they turn it onNothing private leaks by accident; when the place is the point (the leak is at this corner), the author shows it.
  • B · Always shownSimplest, and the most evidence, but it leaks places nobody meant to publish.
  • C · Never shown in a published editionSafest, but an author can't show a place when it matters.
