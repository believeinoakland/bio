## C. The doorbell's discard clearing, and the scheduler's edges (C9 (3))

### C.1 doorbell (next free R29)

`doorbell` R3 says the discard archive's one-week clearing (DEC-108 (5)) "no requirement here yet provides"; the code has the guard (`mayClearDiscarded`, `doorbell/index.mjs:201`) and nothing that clears. This line is that requirement, owed since DEC-108. It concerns knocks only (anyone's, left in the inbox): forwarded mail is not a knock; it waits in its member's private list, seen by her alone, until she adds her statement, and `mail-door` R6 deletes it unconfirmed after `MAIL_WAITING_DAYS` (K5506).

- **R29** (T53; N824; C9 (3); DEC-108 (5); fills R3's "no requirement here yet provides it"; reads R3's guard; `scheduler` R35) *(not yet met: T53)* **Discarded knocks are cleared one week after receipt, and never while a hold is in place.** `clearDiscarded({now, limit})`, with `discardClearDue(now)` and `discardClearWake(now)` for the scheduler. A knock whose status is `discarded` and whose received instant is `DISCARD_DAYS` (7) days or more before `now` at the instance clock (`capture` R115) has its inbox row deleted, in one `record-core` transaction (R22), and then its bytes under `bio/inbox/<digest>`; bytes left by a kill between the two are removed by the next call. `DISCARD_DAYS` is `runtime-limits`' (`boundOf("doorbell", "discard_days")`, N824 R37), no literal here. Before it deletes anything it asks R3's `mayClearDiscarded()`, at every call and never remembered: `may: false` (no reader registered, a reader that throws or does not answer synchronously, or a hold in place) clears nothing and answers `{ok: true, cleared: 0, held: true, basis}`. Only `discarded` is ever cleared: `new` and `pulled` knocks never are, and a knock put back to `new` before the clearing is not. Bytes the record holds through a capture are under the record's own key and are untouched, though the inbox's copy of the same digest goes. It clears at most `limit` per call (default 200, clamped 1 to 1000, N90), oldest received first, answering `{ok, cleared, truncated, held}`; `discardClearDue(now)` answers the earliest instant a discarded knock becomes due, or `null` when none is, and `discardClearWake` is the same instant or `null`. The clearing is never done at read time (`inboxList`, `inboxGet`), writes no tally and no record of what it cleared beyond the count in its answer, leaves R19's tally unchanged, and never throws. The week counts from receipt (BOB's decision, C12 (b)); so a knock discarded after its sixth day is cleared by the next call. Test: with the reader registered and answering `false`, a knock discarded and received 7 days and 1 minute ago is gone with its bytes; one received 6 days 23 hours ago stays; `new` and `pulled` knocks received 30 days ago stay; with the reader answering `true`, throwing, answering a promise, or not registered, nothing is cleared and `held: true`; a hold placed between two calls stops the second; bytes also captured in the record survive in the record; `limit: 1` over three due knocks clears one and states `truncated`; a second call over the same instant clears nothing more. Control: `inboxList` and `pullKnock` answer as before, and R19's tally is the same after a clearing.
