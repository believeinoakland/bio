## 7. interface-shell (next free **R136**)

- **R136** (T53; N1260; DEC-410 (1) `follow(kind)`; DEC-447 "in place" and `FOLLOW.fund`; amends R21, R90) *(not yet met: T53)* **The kit gains `cs.follow({item, onEntry})`: one "Follow from here" control, and its in-place fold.** It draws a closed fold `screen.follow.label`, explained by `screen.follow.tip` (R15), and reads `followfrom` for the item (no `entry`) only when it is first opened, never on load. The control is the same on every item kind: the menu it draws is whatever `followfrom` answers for the item (affordances R73), so a person, organization, fund, payment, donation, event or document differ only in the entries answered. Each menu entry is an act button labelled `screen.follow.<entry>`, drawn under R12 (absent, never greyed, when the session may not call its op); with no entry, the control is not drawn (the common line above: nothing to show draws nothing). Pressing an entry calls `onEntry(entry)`. **In place means a fold of that dimension's rows for the item.** An entry whose op is `followfrom` is sent as `followfrom {item, entry}` and its answer drawn by the screen in a fold under the item (`data-follow-result`), the page, its scroll and its other folds unchanged; pressing again closes it; a failed read is drawn `state.failed` in the fold (R10); a fold whose answer holds no row is not drawn. **Under the rows the fold draws `screen.follow.open.whole`, "Open all of it in {screen}"** (key `screen.follow.open.whole`, DEC-447): a link, by `cs.go`, to the whole screen of that dimension narrowed to the item, `{screen}` that screen's name by its registry word. The screen is chosen by the entry's dimension from a table in the screen module (money and donations: `money`; events: `timeline`; organizations: `person`; documents: `finder`; registers: `registers`), and the link is drawn only when the target screen takes the narrowing; for `law` the rows each open their `standard` and no whole-screen link is drawn. The control sends no write. Test: a fixture item draws the fold; opening it sends one `followfrom` read; a menu of three entries draws three buttons; an entry the session may not call is absent; an empty menu draws nothing; the result fold keeps a second fold's open state; a fold with rows draws "Open all of it in Money" for `who.money` and its link opens `money` narrowed to the item, and for `who.laws` draws no such link; a fold with no rows is not drawn. Control: an unopened control sends no request; the menu is drawn from the answer, not from a table in the kit.

## 11. journey-check (next free **R32**)

- **R32** (journey-check; T53; N1260; DEC-409 "acts counted against a target set before the walk"; K4354) *(not yet met: T53)* **An act counter with a target set before the walk.** For each journey of the live arm the walk counts acts: each press of a control, each choice made and each field submitted through a screen, once however many requests it sends (a navigation by address is one act; the walk's own observation reads, `whoami` and guard reads are not acts). The Sewer Service Fund journey (R34) compares its count with `target_acts` in `sewer-target.json` (`{target_acts, set_by}`, `set_by` a K id), a file that must exist before the first walk: its absence fails `TARGET_UNSET`; a count above the target fails `ACTS_OVER_TARGET` naming the count, the target and the step where it passed; a press that sends `explorecontinue` counts as one act and is named in the output. Test: a fixture journey of three steps passes with target 3 and fails with target 2 naming step 3; a step that sends five reads counts one; control: observation reads are not counted; no file fails `TARGET_UNSET`.
- **R33** (journey-check; T53; N1260; K4354 "every screen and act within 2 s on the test copy at 1280 px") *(not yet met: T53)* **A 2-second assertion on the live arm.** For each act and each screen drawn in the Sewer journey the walk records the milliseconds from the press (or the address) to the screen's settled drawing (no "still loading", `interface-shell` R10) at 1280 px, writes them in its output and fails `SLOWER_THAN_BAR` naming the step when one passes 2,000. It asserts only on the live arm against a named test instance; the local arm records the times and never fails on them (a developer machine is not the bar). The new walks stop at 1,500 ms by default (explore R34), so a press that ends in "Keep looking" is still inside the bar. Test: a fixture step answering in 2,100 ms fails by name on the live arm and passes on the local arm; control: every step under 2,000 ms passes and all times are in the output.
- **R34** (journey-check; T53; N1260; DEC-409 "a real Oakland matter with a known answer"; DEC-447 (4); K4354; R3, R14) *(not yet met: T53)* **The Sewer Service Fund journey.** The live arm walks `sewer-journey.json` (steps `{screen, control, expect}`) through the screens' own controls: open the fund, "Follow from here" into its money, its events and its documents (a fund's menu holds no offices, laws or donations, DEC-447 (4); the walk asserts those three are absent), then Explore from the fund to the ordinance with an end chosen, asserting "Most direct path" first and "Every connection between them" second, then building a longer chain through the trail and asserting "A chain you built" offers a shorter path. Each asserted sentence is compared with `word(key)` (R14). After the last step it compares what the screens drew with `sewer-known-answer.json` (the series, years and passages BOB derives from the held captures, in a K-line before the first walk). **This journey writes nothing, which is stricter than R3.** R3 lets a journey write "what the journey's acts write" and stays as it is for the other journeys; this one is held to nothing because every act of it is a read, because its known answer and its 2-second figures must not depend on rows its own run wrote, and because the test copy is shared: the instance's `op=stats` figures are equal before and after. A step the instance cannot reach is noted, never passed (R21's rule). Test: a fixture journey passes; a known answer with a changed year fails naming the field; a journey that writes a row fails; the fund's menu with `who.offices` present fails by name. Control: a missing `sewer-known-answer.json` fails `KNOWN_ANSWER_UNSET`; R3's other journeys may still write what their acts write.
