## local-facts (the team layer, corrections and reports)

- **R23** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; DEC-151 (2); row 15; amends R10, R12) *(not yet met: T53; per C3)* **A team writes its facts about its counties and cities, any number, into its one team layer, each entry confirmed by a second member.** `layerEntryDraft({section, entry, government, source, reason, by, viewer})` (`op=layerentrydraft`) drafts one entry of the team layer (the sections of `jurisdictions` R116; `government` the `{level, place}` it is about, `jurisdictions` R115; a team's work may be in many places, K5452) as `ruleDraft` drafts a rule: `entry` valid as that section's entry, `source` a held capture extent, `reason` required, a machine refused. `layerEntryConfirm` and `layerEntryWithdraw` follow R11 and R13 (a member other than the drafter; a Tier 2, 3 or undetermined entry needs the confirming member's qualification statement). A drafted entry is listed and never applied; a confirmed one joins the team layer and the governing view (R12), tagged `origin: team`. `local-facts` holds the team layer (R30) and hands it to `combine`; `instance-setup` holds none. Refusals add `LAYER_READ_ONLY` (a `layer` is named, optional, and is a Civicsmith layer) and `LAYER_ENTRY_IN_SHIPPED` (a key a shipped active layer already gives with a different value; the route is `layerReport`). The one exception to the second member is a stated fiscal year (`jurisdictions` R124): a member states it with `how`, by `factConfirm` `correct` as R21's zone, needing neither confirmation nor the capture basis. Test: a city holiday year drafted by one member and confirmed by another appears in the governing view with `origin: team` and `government`; the drafter cannot confirm; entries about two cities are in the one layer. Control: the same entry aimed at the California layer is `LAYER_READ_ONLY` and writes nothing.
- **R24** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R10) *(not yet met: T53)* **Today's member-drafted rules are the team's layer for deadlines and legal facts.** The rules `ruleDraft` records (R10 to R13) are read as entries of THE one team layer (`jurisdictions` R115: placeless, within the federal layer), which holds the team's rules and its per-government facts (R23), `origin: team`, whichever level they name and wherever the team is; R10's `RULE_IN_PROFILE` now reads "in an active layer". No stored rule is rewritten. Test: a rule confirmed before this change appears in the view with `origin: team` and the same `basis`. Control: its confirmation date is unchanged.
- **R25** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R1, R2) *(not yet met: T53; per D2)* **A correction of a shipped layer's calendar fact stays the team's, marked, and the layer is unchanged.** `factConfirm` with `correct` on a fact of a Civicsmith layer (a holiday year, an office's hours, the zone) records the correction in the team's record only, as today (R1, R2); the layer is not written, the governing value is marked "corrected locally by <member>, <date>", and `factStatus` states the layer it corrects. A correction can be reported (R26). Test: after a correction, the layer's data is byte-identical and `factStatus` states the layer. Control: a `correct` of a deadline's amount is `NO_SUCH_FACT` (a deadline is not a fact of R6).
- **R26** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R13) *(not yet met: T53)* **A team reports a rule it believes wrong.** `layerReport({layer, key, said, believes, source, by, viewer})` (`op=layerreport`) records, for a member, a report on one entry of a Civicsmith layer: `layer` its id, `key` the entry's path (a deadline's `rule` and `applies_to`, a law's name, a holiday year with its list, an office), `said` the entry's value as the layer gives it, `believes` the member's words (1 to 1,000 characters) on what it should say, `source` a held capture extent or an address with the passage (at least one), and the layer's version and `as_of`. It holds no record content beyond that source and no handle in what is carried. `layerReports({viewer})` lists the team's reports with state `open`, `carried` (a member marked it carried) or `answered`. `layerReportCarry({report, by})` answers the canonical bytes with their digest for a member to carry to Civicsmith, as `ruleOffer` does (R13); the instance transmits nothing. Refusals: a machine, `LAYER_UNKNOWN`, `LAYER_NOT_SHIPPED` (a team layer is edited, not reported), `REPORT_NO_SOURCE`, `REPORT_NO_BELIEF`. Test: a report on a deadline carries its key, the layer's value, the belief and the source and no handle; carrying marks it. Control: no network request is made by the act.

- **R27** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R6, R5) *(not yet met: T53)* **A path that names a package still reads its history.** `factPath` and `parseFactPath` (R6) accept a fact path whose profile part is a package id (`jurisdictions` R114) and resolve it to the layer that holds the fact now; `factStatus`, `factsDue` and `factConfirm` answer the same history under either path, and a new act is recorded under the layer's path. No stored row is rewritten (R5: append-only). Test: a confirmation recorded under `oakland-alameda/holidays/2026/...` before the split is the status of the same fact under its layer's path after it, with the same member, date and `how`. Control: a path naming no layer or package is `NO_SUCH_FACT`.

- **R30** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; row 17; amends R28; `record-core`; moved from `instance-setup` R110) *(not yet met: T53; PLATFORM)* **`local-facts` holds the team layer in the record and hands it to `combine`.** The table `team_layers` (the layer's id `team`, and its confirmed entries by section, each with its `government`) and its drafts (R23) are declared to `record-core` (`declareTable`, exempt from purge, append-only; R28), `sight: "group"`; `combine` receives the layer from this module beside the ids `instance-setup` records, so the setting `jurisdiction_profiles` never holds it. Nothing of it is in a public read, a network notice, a directory submission, a published edition, an export or a request to another host (as R60 states of the place name). Test: no op other than the group's own authenticated reads returns the team layer; a published edition built in a copy with a team layer holds none of it; `profiles()` shows it, read from this module. Control: `placeWanted`'s name is still held on the same terms; the setting holds no team layer id.

## instance-setup (choosing the shipped layers; it holds no team layer)

- **R107** (T53; N1257; N1256; DEC-404; K5452; amends R12, R13, R14, R50) *(not yet met: T53; per C1)* **A team chooses the layers of the places its work is focused on.** `layersSet({layers, by})` (`op=layersset`, an administrator's act, `PROFILES_NOT_ADMIN` otherwise) records the set as the setting `jurisdiction_profiles` (the ordered list of ids of Civicsmith layers: federal, then the others by level and place, each after the layer it sits within; the team layer is `local-facts`' and never in it, R30), so record-core R26 and every reader are unchanged. `layers` is any set of held (Civicsmith) layer ids of any level and any number: a state alone (state-wide), a region of several counties, one city, a state and one city of another state, or none (federal only); it is where the work is focused, not where the team is (K5452). Nothing below the federal layer is preselected: only what the team names is recorded. The federal layer is preselected and may be removed; a state, county or city layer needs it (`LAYER_NO_FEDERAL`), and with it removed (a team whose work is outside the United States, K5497) the set is empty. A county or city chosen needs the layer it sits within: the page adds its held parents, shows them added and lets the administrator see why (`LAYER_PARENT_MISSING` from the op when the ids are sent without them). `profilesSet` keeps its refusals and accepts the ids of held layers; the id of the team layer is `LAYER_NOT_SHIPPED`. Federal offices are not seeded by `officesSeed` (R50): they are held in the layer and found by search when a member names the office of a request (`entities` R68). Test: federal, California, Alameda County and Oakland are recorded in that order; California alone records federal and California and no county or city layer; two counties and a city of a second held state record three branches; a county without its state is refused. Control: choosing no place is allowed and leaves the federal layer only; removing it leaves no layer, valid (`jurisdictions` R16); a test layer is still refused (`PROFILE_IS_TEST`) and so is `team` (`LAYER_NOT_SHIPPED`).
- **R108** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R12, R76) *(not yet met: T53)* **`profiles()` tells what each layer is.** The answer lists the chain (the setting's Civicsmith layers, and the team layer read from `local-facts` R30) with, for each layer, `level`, `place`, `origin`, `coverage` (`jurisdictions` R108) and the number of facts it gives, each with the layer it sits within (the branches), with `conflicts` as R102 of `jurisdictions` states them, and `view.layers`. `stage_findings` (R76) read over the whole chain. Test: the four-layer chain answers four layers in level order with their `as_of`; a footprint of two counties and three cities answers eight layers (with federal and the state) and each one's `within`. Control: an empty chain answers "no active profile", valid.
- **R109** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R60, R62) *(not yet met: T53)* **A place not held is named at its level, and its arrival is told once.** `placeWantedSet` (R60) accepts a set of names, each a state, a county or a city of the places the work is focused on; at each start an arriving layer whose `place` or `covers` matches a name set is recorded as an arrival (R62), one per layer, and an administrator makes it active with `layersSet`. Test: a team naming a county that arrives in an update is told once, and its own entries for that county are neither deleted nor made inactive. Control: a layer already active is not an arrival.

## installer

- **R94** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R21) *(not yet met: T53; per C1)* **The install asks where the team's work is focused, a set of places, and preselects nothing below what the team names.** The page asks "where does your work focus?" (not where the team is, K5452) and offers the held places of every level as one list grouped by state: whole states, counties, cities, any number checked, each by name; a team may check a state alone (state-wide), several counties, one city, or places in two states. The page says that a team may add facts about its counties and cities, with their sources, to its team layer after install. An update never changes the choices (R21 kept). Test: choosing California alone binds the federal and California layer ids, in that order, and no county or city; choosing Alameda County and Oakland binds federal, California, Alameda County and Oakland; choosing nothing and removing federal binds none and the page says so; the page states that only United States places are shipped. Control: a test layer is not offered, and a fresh page has only the federal layer checked.
