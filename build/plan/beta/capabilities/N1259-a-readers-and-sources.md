Terms. **Definition**: profile data (or a team's own, validated alike) saying how a register's file is read. **Piece**: one file, zip member or table. **Filter**: the closed criteria for rows to keep (R6). **Reduced copy**: only the kept columns of only the matched rows, canonical; the original is never stored (K4403 (5), K1485). **Import**: one run bringing a register in, whole or by a filter; its captures are its reduced pieces.

K4403 numbers are BOB's mapping: (1) two new modules, (2) whole or part by filter, listing, delta, remembered filter, pre-read, mechanical import, large-set storage, (3) FEC guard, (4) cited row as labelled extract, (5) reduced copy on arrival, (6) officers out of the alpha, (7) one queue item per watch per change with a count; automatic watches only for a project's stated interest.

## 1. jurisdictions (highest bold id R98; draft ids R127 to R133)

- **R127** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2), (5), (6); K1485, K1492; amends R4, R57) *(not yet met: T53)* **A profile may hold `registers`: the definitions the file reader and the listing use.** `registers`: `[{key, label, publisher, system, url, pieces, delimiter?, header_from?, columns, row_key, row_key_basis, schemes?, use_limit?, terms_basis, gate, phase, listing, refresh?, count?, basis}]`. `key` is `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*$` and unique. `system` is an R4 `origin`; `url` is the register's own public https page and its host is one of that system's `hosts`. `pieces` is a non-empty (but for R133's officers) `[{key, label, locator, member?, delimiter?, header_from?}]`: `locator` an https address, or a template with the named holes `{cycle}` or `{table}`; `member` the name of a zip member; `delimiter` one of `comma`, `tab`, `pipe` (a piece's overrides the register's); `header_from` absent (the first row is the header) or the `key` of another piece (a separate header file). `columns` is `[{name, role, keep, date_format?, currency?}]`; `role` is one of `name`, `organization`, `amount`, `date`, `filer_id`, `form`, `licence_no`, `docket_no`, `company_no`, `city`, `state`, `zip5`, `home_address`, `phone`, `other`; `keep` is a boolean. `row_key` is a non-empty list of column names; `row_key_basis` is `source` (the columns are the source's own key) or `content` (no source key: the key is the kept cells plus their occurrence number). `gate` is `none`, `account`, `fee` or `people_search` (refused, R128); `status` is absent or `not_measured` (then only `key`, `label`, `publisher`, `url`, R133). `phase` is `alpha` or `beta`. `schemes` is a list of R52 scheme names the register issues. Malformed: `REGISTER_INVALID` (new), naming field and register. `SECTIONS` and the `systems` fields gain these and R132's; `combine` unions `registers` by `key`. Test: a complete fixture register is accepted; each field absent, of the wrong type or outside its list is refused naming it; a duplicate key is refused; a `url` whose host is not among its system's hosts is `REGISTER_INVALID`. Control: a profile with no `registers` is valid.
- **R128** (T53; N1259; K1485 row 9, K4403 (5), DEC-408; K1492 (2), (3); amends R127) *(not yet met: T53)* **A definition that would keep contact data, hide its terms, or reach a paid people-search is refused.** Exported `validateRegisterDefinition(definition, view)` answers `{ok: true, definition}` or `{ok: false, code, field, key}` and never throws. It refuses: `REGISTER_KEEPS_CONTACT` for any column whose `role` is `home_address` or `phone` with `keep` other than false; `REGISTER_NO_TERMS` for an absent or empty `terms_basis` (the source's stated terms, or "none stated" with the date read); `REGISTER_NO_USE_LIMIT` for a register whose `system` carries a `use_limit` (R131) and which does not name it; `REGISTER_PEOPLE_SEARCH` for a `gate` of `people_search` (K1492 (3): a member's own act; results enter as cited sources); `REGISTER_ROW_KEY_INVALID` for a `row_key` naming a column the definition does not list, or `row_key_basis: source` with a `row_key` of columns whose role is `other` only. A column the file holds and the definition does not list is not an error: the reader drops it (`register-readers` R3). It also validates a team's own definition (`registers` R7). Test: a definition keeping a `phone` column, one with no `terms_basis`, one of an FEC-flagged system without its `use_limit`, one with `gate: people_search` and one with a `row_key` naming an unlisted column are each refused by name; every shipped definition passes. Control: the same definitions with the fault removed pass.
- **R129** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); amends R127) *(not yet met: T53)* **The listing facts each carry their basis.** `listing`: `{bytes, rows, source_date?, measured_at}`; `bytes` and `rows` are each `{value, basis}` with `basis` one of `stated` (the source says it), `counted` (this product counted it), `estimated` (computed, with the method in `how`) or `not_stated` (`value` null); `measured_at` is the civil date the figures were taken (`basis` is R2's form, a dated entry such as `2026-10-21 M-R1`). A figure with a basis other than `not_stated` and no `measured_at` is `REGISTER_INVALID`. `count`: `{kind, template, supports}` optional: `kind` `source_count_query` (the source can state the number of rows for a filter, e.g. a SoQL `count(*)`), `template` an https address template, `supports` the filter criteria (`register-readers` R6) the source's query can apply. `refresh`: `{cadence_hours, validators, append_only?}` optional: the least interval between looks, which of `etag`, `last_modified`, `content_length`, `count` tell the source has not changed, and `append_only` true when the source never changes or removes a row. Test: a figure with a basis and no date, and a `count` naming a criterion the filter lacks, are refused; a `refresh` with no validators means "always fetch". Control: `not_stated` with a null value is valid.
- **R130** (T53; N1259; K1484, K1492; amends R52) *(not yet met: T53)* **Identifier schemes for filers, licences, dockets and companies, and the query that composes a register's own address from a number.** `identifier_schemes` (R52) may also hold the schemes `filer` (FPPC or NetFile filer id), `committee` (FEC committee id), `licence` (board plus number), `docket` (CourtListener docket id and a court's docket number) and `company` (a company number), each as R52 requires plus `registers`, the keys that issue it. A template with a hole for anything but a value of a listed scheme, two holes, or a hole in the host is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_INVALID`; a template naming a field `name`, `first`, `last` or `person` is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_NOT_NUMBER` (K1484: a watch is by a number). Malformed schemes stay `SCHEME_INVALID`. Test: a docket template composes the address for a fixture id, refusing a value not of the form; a `{name}` hole is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_NOT_NUMBER`; a hole in the host is refused. Control: a scheme with no template validates and cannot be watched.
- **R131** (T53; N1259; K4403 (3); K1492 (5); 11 CFR 104.15; 52 U.S.C. 30111(a)(4)) *(not yet met: T53)* **`use_limits`: the data-use limit of a source, as data.** `use_limits`: `[{key, citation, text_key, export, on_publish, basis}]`. `citation` is the rule as cited (for the first profile's federal campaign-finance limit: 11 CFR 104.15 and 52 U.S.C. 30111(a)(4)); `text_key` a `setup-words` key whose words state the limit (the notice a row carries, drawn by the design); `export` is `never` (no list export; the only value accepted now); `on_publish` is `warn`, `refuse` or `allow` (what a case citing a row of such a register meets; counsel's answer sets it, K4403 (3); this release ships `warn`); `basis` the measurement or ruling (`measures-T52-DEC406.md`). A `systems` entry (R4) may name a `use_limit` key; every register of that system inherits it and may not clear it. A limit with an `export` other than `never`, or an `on_publish` outside the three, is `USE_LIMIT_INVALID`. Test: the first profile's federal system names the limit and its registers inherit it; a register omitting it is `REGISTER_NO_USE_LIMIT`; `on_publish: maybe` is refused. Control: a limit-free register carries none.
- **R132** (T53; N1259; K1492 (1), (4); K1449; amends R4) *(not yet met: T53)* **A system states its hosts, its stated call limits and whether it is gated.** A `systems` entry (R4) may carry `rate_limits`: `{per_minute?, per_hour?, per_day?, basis, measured_at}` (a keyed service's stated limits; CourtListener's defaults are 5, 50 and 125, M-R5), `gated`: `none`, `account` or `fee`, `terms`: the sentence its definitions' `terms_basis` quote, and `volatile`: patterns of text that change on every read (a clock, a counter), which `following` R38 (part (c)) ignores when it compares two reads. `rate_limits` is read by `acquisition` (R131); a register of a `gated` system is listed but never fetched by the product (R127 `gate`). - **R133** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2), (6); R57's rule; amends R57) *(not yet met: T53)* **The first profile lists what was measured only; the test profile supplies every shape.** The first profile's `registers` hold exactly the registers whose files were read for `measures-T52-DEC406.md` M-R1 (rows 1, 3, 4, 7): the City's open-data copies of the campaign filings (one register per schedule; NetFile's export is not confirmed, so NetFile is not claimed), the City's lobbyist datasets, the state's raw campaign-finance zip (tab-separated, one table per piece) and the federal individual-contribution zips (pipe-separated, separate header file, per cycle) with the committee master. ## 2. register-readers (NEW module; proposed layer 1; draft ids R1 to R11)

**Purpose.** Reads the bytes of a public register file into rows under a definition, filters them by a team's criteria, drops the columns that must not be kept before any other code sees a row, and writes the reduced copy that is stored. It touches no record, store, clock or network, and calls no AI.

**Provides.** `readRows`, `listMembers`, `streamZipMember`, `reduce`, `contactShape`, `indexTerms`, `validateFilter`, `matches`, `autoWatchable`, `filterFingerprint`, `prefillFrom`, `createReducer`, `estimate`.

**Proposed layer and order.** Layer 1 (no access to the record; pure). After `court-doctypes` in `modules.json` order. `MODULE_ORDER` is assembled from the file (`member-roster` R61; membership R83 is retired): no re-pin. It carries product capability; K4403 (1) rules it (`layers.md` ruling 5).

**Uses (earlier layers only).** `record-grammar` (`createSha256` R20, `createResumableSha256` R64), `civil-time` (`isCalendarDate`; a `date_format` is read here), `id-spaces` (`recognise(view, space, value)`, for ids in a filter), `ooxml` (`decodeText` only: its zip services refuse a member over `MEMBER_MAX`, an archive over `ARCHIVE_TOTAL_MAX` (256 MiB each) or 10,000 entries, R30, which the measured files exceed, so R2 has its own directory reader and inflate unless BOB lifts them for it), `csv-reader` (`createRecordWalk`, the piecewise walk: exported, not Provides, so promoted when imported), `runtime-limits` (`boundOf`), `setup-words`, `test-support` (`sheetgen` builds CSV and TSV, not pipe; `zipChunks64`). Not `jurisdictions`: a definition and a view are arguments.

**Satisfies.** DEC-406 (I1, I2); K4403 (2), (5); K1485, DEC-408 (no home address or phone copied); K1484 (closed criteria, no pattern); "No jurisdiction in the product".

- **R1** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) *(not yet met: T53)* **`readRows(source, definition, piece, opts)` reads a delimited file as rows, streaming.** `source` is an async iterable of `Uint8Array` chunks (any sizes); `header` (opts) holds a separate header file's bytes when the piece's `header_from` names one. It yields `{row_no, key, cells}` in file order, `row_no` the 1-based number of the data row (the header excluded), `cells` a map from the definition's column names to decoded text, `key` the `row_key` columns joined by a unit separator (or, for `row_key_basis: content`, the kept cells' digest plus the occurrence number). Delimiters comma, tab and pipe; double-quote quoting with doubled quotes and line breaks inside quotes; CRLF, LF and a final line without a break; encoding by BOM (UTF-8, UTF-16) else UTF-8, else windows-1252, stated per piece as `encoding` and `assumed`. Memory is bounded by two windows plus one row whatever the file's size. A data row whose field count differs from the header's is not yielded as a row: it is yielded as `{row_no, undetermined: true, why: "field_count", fields, expected}` and counted, never padded, cut or coerced; a quote left open at the end is `why: "unterminated_quote"` with the row it opened on. A header that lacks a column the definition lists is `{ok: false, why: "column_missing", column}` before any row. Test: comma, tab and pipe fixtures read to the same rows; a row split at a window edge and one byte either side reads as whole; a ragged row is `undetermined`, counted, and the next row intact; a separate header file supplies the names; a 200 MiB generated stream reads under a 64 MB heap cap. Control: an unragged file's rows equal `csv-reader`'s cells.
- **R2** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); K1852) *(not yet met: T53)* **A zip is read by its directory and one member at a time.** `listMembers(range)` takes `range = {size, read(offset, length)}` (a source that honours ranges) and answers `{ok: true, members: [{name, method, compressed, uncompressed, offset, crc32}]}` read from the central directory, ZIP64 included (a member over 4 GiB, a directory with more than 65,535 entries), or `{ok: false, why}` (`not_a_zip`, `zip64_record_invalid`, `directory_unreadable`); it reads the directory and nothing else. `streamZipMember(range, name, opts)` yields the member's decompressed bytes as chunks for `method` stored or deflate, checks the CRC-32 at the end (`why: "member_crc_mismatch"` as the last result, never silently), reads no byte outside the member's compressed span, and refuses another method `unsupported_method` before reading. A member name is matched exactly; a name that is not unique in the directory is `ambiguous_member`. Test: a sparse ZIP64 fixture with a member over 4 GiB lists its true sizes; the second of 3 members streams reading only its span; a flipped byte ends `member_crc_mismatch`; a bzip2 member is `unsupported_method`. Control: a member streams identically from a whole file.
- **R3** (T53; N1259; K1485 row 9, K4403 (5), DEC-408) *(not yet met: T53)* **`reduce(row, definition)` drops every column that must not be kept before any other code sees the row.** It returns `{kept}`, a map of only the columns whose definition `keep` is true, in the definition's order. K5499: of a home address only the street lines are removed; ZIP, map coordinates and city stay as the source gives them (a nine-digit ZIP stays nine; the search term `zip5` is its first five digits). A column the definition does not list, and a `home_address` or `phone` column, are never in `kept`. `droppedColumns(header, definition)` returns the names of the header's columns that were dropped, as `{name, why}` with `why` `contact_role` (a `home_address` or `phone` role) or `unlisted` (not in the definition), names only, never a value. A definition that keeps a contact role is refused (`jurisdictions` R128) and, if handed here, `reduce` answers `{ok: false, why: "definition_keeps_contact"}` and returns no row. Test (DEC-408's import test): a fixture whose `home_address`, `phone` and an unlisted column hold sentinel values reduces to rows with no sentinel anywhere in the serialised output; `droppedColumns` names the three and holds no value; a nine-digit ZIP, a city and a map coordinate stay as the source gives them. Control: kept values are unchanged.
- **R4** (T53; N1259; K1485, K1493, DEC-408) *(not yet met: T53)* **`contactShape(text)` finds a phone number or street address typed into a free-text cell, and a kept cell that has one is withheld.** `contactShape(text)` is pure and answers `{found: boolean, kinds: ["phone"|"address"]}` for North American phone forms (with or without area code in parentheses or dashes, extension forms) and street-address forms (a number, a street word and a type, optional unit; UNVERIFIED: local vocabulary, `layers.md` rule 1); it never throws and answers `{found: false}` for a non-string. `reduce` applies it to every kept cell whose role is `other`, `name`, `organization` or `form` (free text), replaces a cell where it is found with `{withheld: "contact_shape"}`, keeps the rest of the row, and counts withheld cells per piece (`withheld_cells`). It is not applied to `amount`, `date`, `filer_id` or the id roles. Test: sentinels "(555) 010-7731" and "4012 Sentinel Ave, Apt 6" in a free-text cell are withheld and counted, the row's other cells intact; 40 non-contact strings (amounts, dates, ids, "Measure 5500") are not. Control: a cell with no shape is unchanged.
- **R5** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2), (7)) *(not yet met: T53)* **`indexTerms(kept, definition)` gives the search terms of a row, one rule for the filter and the store.** It returns `{name_tokens, org_tokens, amount, date, filer_id, form, place}`: tokens are case-folded, diacritics stripped, punctuation removed, split on spaces and commas, suffixes and honorifics (Jr, Sr, II, III, Mr, Mrs, Dr) kept as tokens, order not significant; `amount` is `{units, scale, currency}` an exact decimal in the definition's currency, from a text that reads as one amount (parentheses or a leading minus a negative), else null with the cell undetermined; `date` is a civil date by the column's `date_format`, else null; `filer_id` and `form` trimmed exact; `place` `{city, state, zip5}` case-folded (`zip5`: the first five digits). A cell that cannot be read as its role is `undetermined` in the answer's `undetermined` list, never coerced. No phonetic, nickname or fuzzy matching in this release. Test: "MARTINEZ, ROSA M" and "Rosa Martinez" give equal token sets; "(1,000.00)" is -1000.00; "13/45/2026" is undetermined; "Bob" does not match "Robert". Control: a clean row has no `undetermined`.
- **R6** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); K1484) *(not yet met: T53)* **The filter is a closed set of criteria; `validateFilter` and `matches` are its one meaning.** A filter is `{names?, organizations?, amount?: {min?, max?}, date?: {from?, to?}, filer_ids?, forms?, places?: [{city?, state?, zip5?}], licence_numbers?, docket_numbers?}`; the empty filter `{}` is the whole register. `validateFilter(filter, definition, view)` answers `{ok: true, filter}` or `{ok: false, code, field}`, refusing `FILTER_CRITERION_UNKNOWN`; `FILTER_ROLE_ABSENT` (a criterion for a role the register's definition does not have, e.g. an amount for a register with no `amount` column); `FILTER_VALUE_INVALID` (a name under 2 characters, an amount that is not a decimal, `min` above `max`, a date not a civil date or `from` after `to`, an id not of the scheme's form per `id-spaces.recognise`); `FILTER_PATTERN_REFUSED` (a `*`, `?`, regular expression or "all filers of X": a wildcard is not a name or a number, K1484); `FILTER_TOO_LARGE` (over 1,000 names or organizations, or 500 ids). `matches(row, filter, definition)` is true when every present criterion holds, a list holding when any item does; a name item holds when all its tokens are in the row's name tokens (R5); an amount or date criterion on a row whose value is undetermined does not hold and the row is counted `undetermined` by the caller; the empty filter matches every row. `filterFingerprint(filter)` is the SHA-256 of the canonical JSON (keys sorted, list items sorted and de-duplicated, names token-normalised), equal for equal filters. All three are pure and never throw. Test: each refusal by name; "MARTINEZ ROSA" matches "Rosa Martinez" and not "Rosa Martin"; a list of two names holds on either; one filter in two orders has one fingerprint; `{}` matches every row. Control: a filter naming an id in its scheme's form validates.
- **R7** (T53; N1259; K4405 (1), (5); K1484; K4403 (7)) *(not yet met: T53)* **`autoWatchable(filter)` says whether a filter may be watched without a member's act.** It is true exactly when the filter holds no `names` and no `organizations` (a name or an organization may be a person) and holds at least one other criterion; false for `{}`. It is pure. A filter that is not `autoWatchable` is kept, shown and used by a member's search or re-check, never by an unattended read (K4405 (1)). Test: `{filer_ids}` and `{amount: {min: 500}, places: [{zip5}]}` true; `{names}`, `{organizations, amount}` and `{}` false. Control: without the name it is true.
- **R8** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) *(not yet met: T53)* **`prefillFrom(facts, definition)` offers a filter from what the project already names, and invents nothing.** `facts` is `{subjects: [{kind: "person"|"organization", label}], identifiers: [{scheme, value}], period?: {from, to}}` handed by the caller. It returns `{filter, basis}`: the criteria the register's roles support (a person or organization label to `names` or `organizations` only where the register has a `name` or `organization` role; an identifier whose scheme the register issues to `filer_ids`, `licence_numbers` or `docket_numbers`; a period to `date`), and `basis` the fact each criterion came from; criteria a register cannot apply are left out and listed in `left_out`. A fact of any other kind (an address, a phone, a note) yields nothing.Test: two subjects, a filer id of the register's scheme and a period prefill those four; an `address` fact and a note's text prefill nothing; a register with no `amount` role never receives an amount. Control: no facts give `{}`.
- **R9** (T53; N1259; K4403 (5); K4405) *(not yet met: T53)* **`createReducer({definition, piece, filter, header, sha256})` (`sha256`: `createResumableSha256`) turns the original stream into the reduced copy as it passes, recording the original's fingerprint.** `write(chunk)` takes the piece's decompressed bytes and returns the reduced bytes ready to store (possibly empty); `end()` returns the rest and `{ok, original: {sha256, bytes, covers}, reduced: {sha256, bytes, rows_in, rows_out, rows_undetermined, kept_columns, dropped_columns, withheld_cells}, filter_fingerprint}`. `original.sha256` is the SHA-256 of every byte passed in (as served), `bytes` their count, and `covers` states what they are (`whole_file`, or `member:<name>` of an archive the caller read by range): a fingerprint is never claimed for bytes not read. The reduced copy is delimited text, one canonical form: UTF-8, comma, LF, quotes only where needed, the header row of the kept columns plus a first column `src_row` holding each row's 1-based number in the original piece, then the rows of `matches` in source order; unmatched rows are not written. Equal input, definition and filter give equal reduced bytes, whatever the chunking; the original is held in no more than two windows. Test: a fixture split at every position gives one reduced digest; `original.sha256` equals an independent hash; a filter matching 2 of 10 rows gives 2 rows each with its `src_row`, `rows_in` 10, `rows_out` 2; sentinel contact columns never appear in the reduced bytes; `covers` is `member:<name>` for a member read by range. Control: the empty filter gives every row.
- **R10** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) *(not yet met: T53)* **`estimate(sample, totals)` gives a row count from a sample and says it is an estimate.** `sample` is `{bytes, rows, spread}` (head, middle and tail of a piece, or its first window), `totals` `{bytes}` (the piece's uncompressed size when known). It answers `{rows: {value, low, high, basis: "estimated"}, bytes_per_row}`; `low` and `high` come from the spread of the sampled windows' bytes-per-row. It never answers a count and never `basis: "counted"`; with no sample it answers `{rows: {value: null, basis: "not_stated"}}`. Test: uniform rows give an estimate within 10% of the true count; a fixture whose tail rows are 5 times longer has the true count inside the interval; no input gives `counted`. Control: a sample of the whole file gives `low` equal to `high`.
- **R11** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) *(not yet met: T53)* **Invariants of the module.** (a) Pure: no store, network, clock or randomness (the SHA-256 and date reading are arguments or earlier modules'). (b) Never throws: every failure is a returned `{ok: false, why}` or a yielded `{error}`. (c) It knows no register by name and no place: definitions and views are arguments (`layers.md` rule 1); its tests use the test profile's definitions and at least one that is not Oakland's.
