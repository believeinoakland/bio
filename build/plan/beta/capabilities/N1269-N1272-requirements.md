## 0. Read this first

**Terms.** A **part** of a held document is a range of a kind the reading already names as an extent (R98's sheet and row range; a page range; a paragraph range; a slide), never a byte range. **Interpreted** means: for one investigation, a part was turned into facts the record relies on (a sheet's typed cells and figures; for other kinds, entities, dates and figures made from that part), and the fact that it was is recorded (section 1). For files that are not sheets (K5496, option A): finding names, organisations, dates and figures over a whole document for search is reading, not interpretation; only a part turned into facts the record relies on is. **Read** is the other act and is unchanged: every document a member adds or captures is held whole and its text read whole, every line searchable and citable (K4571, K4636). **Investigation** here is a project and its questions (`investigation`, `inquiry`); the record is kept per project. **A proposal** is a candidate part Civicsmith lists for a member to accept or refuse; it is mechanical, makes no AI call and gives no score (section 3). Accepting one is DEC-77's act, **accepting a proposal** (item 3b, c): attributed like any other act, the record keeps whether the member chose unaided or accepted a standing proposal and which, and nothing is preselected unseen. The shape is `contradiction`'s (R30's acceptance basis `accepted` or `unaided`, `ACCEPTANCE_NOT_STANDING` C-93.25, `acceptanceRates` R39); these lines reuse it and do not invent another.

**Settled by Bob, carried by these lines.** (C1, K5445) A for every kind now: a part is interpreted when a member opens or cites it. B: a member's deliberate act, "interpret these parts for this investigation". C is the objective: Civicsmith's mechanical proposals, made without AI from the investigation's own words, accepted by a member before anything is interpreted; built in the beta if it tests well on real documents, else right after. The assistant's proposals come only with N1220; this draft builds none. Never "interpret everything up to a size" (D). (C3, K5446) Limits are shown per file kind before a file is added; a file over its limit is refused with its size and the limit. (C4, K5447) A file over the limit is refused with the reason and a way in: a register by filter (N1259), else the member's own part; reading only the first part waits until proposals work well.

## 1. extraction (highest bold id R112; N1259's draft takes R113; draft ids R114 to R119)

- **R114** (T53; N1272; K4636, K5445; K4571; amends R19, R98) *(not yet met: T53)* **Adding or reading a document interprets nothing; interpreting is a recorded act on a part, for an investigation.** `interpretPart({capture, part, project, how, by, proposal?})` interprets one part of a held capture for one project and appends one interpretation row `{id, capture, part, project, by, at, how, basis, proposal?, withdrawn: false}`. `how` is exactly one of `opened` (a member opened the part's cells or text in the part viewer), `cited` (a member cited a passage or range in it), `marked` (the member's act of R26), `accepted_proposal` (R28, with `proposal` the proposal's id and what it matched). Each row also carries its acceptance basis as DEC-77 (3b, c) and `contradiction` R30 keep it: `accepted`, naming the proposal (exactly when `how` is `accepted_proposal`), else `unaided` (R117). `by` is the signed-in member's stamp (a machine identity is refused `MEMBER_SESSION_REQUIRED`, as `capture` R86); `at` through `time.at`, never a date typed by the page. `project` is one the viewer may see and is a member of; a capture the viewer may not see is `NOT_FOUND`. Nothing here is called by intake, `read` or any batch path: reading a document, re-reading it, watching it or importing a register interprets no part (K4636). The interpreted content is made once per `(capture sha, part)` and kept; a second investigation interpreting the same part writes its own row and reuses the content (`reading-pipeline` R64). Never throws. Test: adding a 30-sheet workbook and reading it whole writes no interpretation row and no typed cell; opening rows 10 to 40 of one sheet writes one row, `how: opened`; the same range cited by another project writes a second row for that project and no second copy of the cells; a machine identity is refused. Control: re-opening the same range for the same project writes no duplicate row (idempotent on `(capture, part, project)`; the first row's `how` and `by` stand, the later open is not recorded as a second act).
- **R115** (T53; N1272; K5445 A; K5496; amends R98) *(not yet met: T53)* **Opening or citing a part is the way in for every kind, and `cellsOf` is one of its callers.** A sheet's `cellsOf` over a range the member opened or cited calls R114 with `how` `opened` or `cited`, and answers the range as before with `interpreted: {sheet, from_row, to_row}` now also naming the row it recorded; the same holds for a page range of a PDF or document, a paragraph range of a text and a slide range, each through the part viewer or a citation. The bounds stand: at most 20,000 in-extent cells a call (R98), a larger range answers the first block with `next_row`, and the interpretation recorded covers exactly the block returned, never the range asked. Merely opening the document page or a search hit interprets nothing. ## 5. screens-intake (highest bold id R71; draft ids R72 to R74) and acquisition (highest R124; N1259's draft takes R125 to R134; draft id R135)

The sizes are N1267's (Bob, K4628; K5453 point 2): the beta builds to them, so there is ONE limit per kind and the add screen states exactly the limit the copy enforces, with no second "standard" figure beside it. ## 6. A measurement on real documents (plan item M-N1272, to be entered in `next.md` by BOB)

**What it decides.** Whether Civicsmith's proposals (R27) are good enough to offer, per file kind, in the beta (C1: "built in the beta if it tests well on real documents, else right after"). It is the measure behind R29: until it has run and met its bar a kind gets the stated absence of R145, as K491 does for an unmeasured recommender.

**Set before measuring (DEC-77, K491's rule).** BOB records, before the first run, in `MEASUREMENTS.md`: the corpus (documents named by digest, pinned), the investigations and their questions as the input (pinned text), the gold set, and the bar for each kind. The bar is not changed after the run; a different bar is a new run with the old one kept. The proposer's version is pinned by digest.

**Which documents.** Real documents of the kinds a beta member will meet, from the acceptance corpus N1268 builds on the live copy, plus at least these shapes: a long budget PDF; a spreadsheet register (hundreds of thousands of rows); meeting minutes; a scan; a workbook of several sheets; a long text report. Each paired with a real investigation's questions (titles, names, amounts) written by a person who knows the topic and not by the proposer's author.

**The gold set, blind.** A member who knows the investigation marks, before seeing any proposal, the parts they would interpret for it. The proposals are then run and compared: nobody tunes the proposer to the gold set it is measured against; a fixture used for tuning is a different set.

**What is measured, per kind, with counts (K447 point 15's form).** (1) Offered-and-accepted: of the proposals offered, how many a member accepted (precision), reported with the count offered. (2) Found: of the gold parts, how many appear among the proposals (recall), reported with the count. (3) Size: proposals offered per document as a share of its parts (the list must be shorter than the document by a stated factor, since "propose everything" has perfect recall). (4) Time: the 2 s bar (K4354) for a document at the standard's top size. (5) Stability: the same input, the same list.

**Draft bar for BOB to set (proposed, not decided).** Review at 95 percent accepted-or-edited over at least 30 offered per kind, as K447 (15) did for the recommender, PROVISIONAL; recall at or above a figure BOB sets with the member who made the gold set (draft: 80 percent); list size at or under a tenth of the document's parts for documents over 100 parts (draft). These are the shape; the numbers are BOB's to fix before the run, and a kind that misses stays in the stated-absence state until a later run meets its bar.

**Outcome.** Pass for a kind: record `PROPOSALS_MEASURED[kind]` met (R29) with the corpus and bar digests; the kind's proposals go live in the beta. Miss: the kind stays unavailable; C ships right after, per C1; opening, citing and marking work meanwhile (A, B).
