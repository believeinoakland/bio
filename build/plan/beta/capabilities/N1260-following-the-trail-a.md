## 1. Technical choices (BOB's, not Bob's; P17)

1. **Additive ops; `explore` R2 untouched.** DEC-410 draws two buttons, so `exploreall` and `explorepath` are new; `explore` with `to` keeps its shortest-level answer.
2. **`followfrom` is one read in `affordances`** (layer 11, which already uses explore, money, lines, events, entities, provenance, membership, connections, content): no `entry` answers the item's menu; an `entry` answers the owner's own answer. `search`, `registersearch` and `explore` entries stay those ops.
3. **Strength order:** no hunch hop, no declared hop, better weakest `assertion` letter, better weakest `end` letter; then date, then ids. No `rank` or `score` is answered (K1471); a comparator is exported. The exported names avoid the words the pinned name scan refuses (`invariants.test.mjs`, R17 case: `walk`, `path` not followed by a final `s`, `explore`, `store`, `save`, `count`, `rank`, `score`, `central`, `connected`): `chainStrength`, `strongerChain`, `foldParallel`, `CHAIN_LIMITS`, `chainLimitOf`, `budgetMsOf`.
4. **Limits are not in `BOUNDS`.** `BOUNDS` stays frozen with its 7 keys (two tests pin them); the new limits are a second frozen export, `CHAIN_LIMITS` (R25). `EXHAUSTION_REASONS` (pinned, five reasons) is not extended: a list cut by `limit` is told by `listed < found`, not by a sixth reason.
5. **Time:** the four new ops default to 1,500 ms (K4354 leaves 0.5 s for the round trip) and take `budget_ms` up to 10,000; the existing walks keep their default of 10,000 (R25, R34). **Resume token:** self-contained, bound to viewer and parameters, 15 minutes, over 256 KB not offered.
6. **Money:** `money_flow` becomes five family kinds; `behested` goes with contributions; a money set's source-document edge takes `money_other` (it is no fact's kind). "Donations" in a menu are the `money_contribution` family.
7. **"A chain a member built"** = the graph trail; the screen runs `exploreshorter` itself at two or more steps (DEC-410 shows "best has 2 steps, against your 3" before any press). Its hops carry `from` and `to` because the trail keeps the `exploregraph` steps as answered (R21's steps carry both).
8. **Refusal codes are unique across the plane** (the design keeps one sentence per code, `refusal.<CODE>`): the first draft's `PATHS_INVALID`, `BAD_CHAIN` and `CHAIN_BROKEN` clash with existing codes of other meaning, and `NO_TARGET`, `NO_SUCH_ITEM`, `BAD_ROLE`, `BAD_LIMIT`, `BAD_CURSOR` also exist elsewhere with other sentences. Every new code below is prefixed by its family (`CHAIN_LIMIT_*`, `OWN_CHAIN_*`, `EXPLORE_*`, `FOLLOW_*`, `MONEYBY_*`) unless a grep found it nowhere.
9. **Hub chains** are listed when the hub is a meeting node (DEC-410). `named_in` is **conditional on M-F1**.
10. **Dropped:** per-dimension counts, `by_kind`, the "equal length but stronger" list (DEC-410/411 draw none).

## 5. explore (next free **R30**)

Terms for R30-R36. A **link** is a grouped connection (`connection-grammar` R24); a **chain** is a loop-free sequence of links, shown to a member as steps. A **meaningful chain** between `from` and `to` is a chain of at most `depth` steps (default 8) in which no node appears twice, each member hop is valid or undetermined at `at` (R4), is of an asked kind, a hunch hop is walked only within `scope` (R6), and no hub node is asked for its neighbours (R5, R20; a chain through a hub is meaningful only as R30 says). **Budget** of the four new ops: R34.
