Terms. A **layer** is a profile (`jurisdictions` R1) that states one level of a place. Its **origin** is `civicsmith` (shipped in the release, read-only) or `team` (written by a team, held in that team's record). A **branch** is a layer and the layers it sits in, up to the federal layer. The layers active together form a **forest of chains** (K5452): one federal root, and below it any number of layers of any level, each sitting `within` one layer of a higher level. A state-wide footprint is a state layer with no county or city layer; a team's work in several counties holds several county layers. A **layer-qualified name** is `<layer id>:<name>`, the form in which a layer's rule, list, law or office name is addressed across layers. The **team layer** (R115) is the one placeless layer of `origin: team`: no level, no place, within the federal layer; it holds the team's rules and its facts about governments (PLANNING row 15).

**The layer** (DEC-404)
- **R99** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R1, R10, R11) *(not yet met: T53)* **A profile may be a layer.** A profile may carry `layer: {level, place, within?, origin}`: `level` one of `LAW_LEVELS` (R31), `place` the place's name (1 to 200 characters, one line), `within` the `id` of the layer it sits in, a layer of a higher level (absent on a federal layer; a city may sit within a county, within a state, or within the federal layer when its state is not held), `origin` `civicsmith` or `team`; `level` and `place` are absent on the team layer alone (R115). Several Civicsmith layers of one level are valid (several counties); a team holds the one team layer. Names a layer gives to a rule, list, law or office are unique within the layer (in the team layer, within the government an entry is about, R115) and addressed across layers as `<layer id>:<name>`; an unqualified name resolves to the asking layer's own, else to one in its branch, else to the one active layer that gives it, and when two active layers give it `NAME_AMBIGUOUS` (never a guess). A profile with no `layer` is valid and read as today (the test profile, a whole-place profile). A malformed `layer` is `LAYER_INVALID`, naming the field. Test: a layer of each level validates; a level outside `LAW_LEVELS`, an empty `place`, an `origin` outside the two, a federal layer with `within`, and a city `within` a layer of its own or a lower level are each `LAYER_INVALID`; two city layers each giving a list named `city` are addressed `a:city` and `b:city`, and the bare `city` from a third layer is `NAME_AMBIGUOUS`. Control: a profile with no `layer` validates exactly as before.
- **R100** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R31, R88, R11) *(not yet met: T53)* **A layer holds only its own level.** In a layer of level L, every `records_laws` and `standard_sources` entry that carries a level (R31) carries L, and every counterparty's `level` (R24, R88) is L, or `district` in a city or county layer. Otherwise `LAYER_LEVEL_MISMATCH`, naming the entry. A fact that carries no level of its own (a deadline, a holiday year, a pointer) is placed by its citation and is judged by R113's test, not by `validate`. The team layer has no level and is not judged by level as a layer; each of its entries carries `government: {level, place}` (R115) and the same test is made against that `level`. Test: a state law inside a city layer is `LAYER_LEVEL_MISMATCH`; a team entry carrying a city law with `government.level` `county` is the same. Control: the same law in a state layer validates.
- **R101** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R12) *(not yet met: T53; per C1)* **The forest is checked by `combine`, branch by branch.** When any profile combined carries a `layer`, `combine` also checks: each layer id appears once (`LAYER_ID_TWICE`); at most one federal layer (`LAYER_FEDERAL_TWICE`); each layer's `within`, when it has one, names a layer in the list (`LAYER_PARENT_MISSING`) of a higher level (`LAYER_PARENT_LEVEL`), and following `within` reaches the federal layer without a loop (`LAYER_CYCLE`); a federal layer is present (`LAYER_NO_FEDERAL`, a refusal only when a state, county or city layer is present). Any number of layers of one level is valid (several counties, several cities, several states); a state layer with no county or city layer below it is valid (a state-wide footprint). Each branch is checked and a refusal names the branch (the layer ids from the failing layer up). The team layer (R115) is the one layer with no level or place: a second is `LAYER_TEAM_TWICE`; it is `within` the federal layer when that is active, else a root (`LAYER_NO_FEDERAL` does not apply to it); it is listed last. `view.layers` lists `{id, level, place, origin, within}` in level order, then by place. Layers combine side by side; none overrides another. Test: federal, California and Oakland with Oakland `within` a county layer not in the list is `LAYER_PARENT_MISSING` naming the branch; a county `within` a city is `LAYER_PARENT_LEVEL`; two `within` pointing at each other are `LAYER_CYCLE`; federal, California, two county layers and three city layers in any order give one view, `layers` in level order. Control: the four shipped layers in any list order give one view with `layers` in level order; federal and California alone (state-wide) is valid.
- **R102** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R15, R29, R55) *(not yet met: T53; per C2)* **One key, two layers, two answers.** Where two layers give different values for one key (R15), the fact stays withheld and reported in `conflicts`, each conflict now carrying each giver's `layer` level, `origin` and id, so a screen can say which layers disagree. `combine` chooses nothing. Two layers on different branches (neither sits in the other) never conflict on a fact addressed by layer-qualified name (R99): their rules, lists, offices and laws stand side by side. A team-wide one-value fact (`time_zone`, `locale`, `weekend`) stated differently by layers on different branches is a conflict at team level, withheld; `fiscal_year` is not team-wide, it is **the fiscal year of one government** (R123), read by government and body, so two governments' years never conflict; a rule, office or law is still read in the value of its own branch (`governingZone`), so two counties in two zones each count in their own. Test: two layers giving different `locale` values give one conflict naming both levels and ids; two city layers on different branches each giving a list `city` give no conflict; two county layers in two zones give a team-level zone conflict and each county's clock reads its own zone; two cities with different fiscal years give no conflict and each reads its own. Control: two layers giving the same value give none.
- **R103** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R46, R55) *(not yet met: T53)* **Two layers may state the same weekend.** The weekend compares equal when its `days` are equal; the citations are then joined, as `time_zone` and `locale` already agree on a value whatever the basis (measured 2026-10-27). - **R104** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R26, R47; `civil-time`) *(not yet met: T53; per C1)* **A rule may count on a closure list a layer above it holds.** A deadline's `closures` and `observed.closures` name a list held by its own layer or by a layer in its branch (its `within` chain up to the federal layer), resolved in the combined view by layer-qualified name; never a list of a layer on another branch. `validate` of a layer alone accepts a name it does not hold only when the layer has a `within` (today `CLOSURES_UNKNOWN`, `index.mjs:1049`, 1076: "no holidays entry of this profile"); `combine` refuses a name no layer of the branch holds, `CLOSURES_UNKNOWN`. A list name is unique within its layer; the same bare name in two layers of one level (two cities each with a list `city`) is two lists, `a:city` and `b:city`, each counted by the rules of its own layer. A second layer of one branch giving a list of the same name for the same year and offices is R102's conflict. Test: a California-layer deadline counting on a list the federal layer holds validates alone only with `within`, and combines; with the federal layer absent, `CLOSURES_UNKNOWN`; a city-layer rule naming `city` counts on its own city's list when a second city layer also holds `city`. Control: a layer's rule naming its own list validates alone.

- **R106** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R7, R76, R96) *(not yet met: T53)* **A records law says whose records it governs.** A `records_laws` entry carries `governs`: a non-empty list of levels of office (`federal`, `state`, `county`, `city`, `district`), each from the statute's own definition of the bodies it binds (`citation` shows it). (Applies if Bob rules G-b; under G-c only for guides of kinds of Tier 2 and 3; under G-a this line is dropped.) Each `procedure_guides` entry (R80) carries, beside its `kind`, `items`, `status` and `basis`, `authored_by` (a non-empty name), `reviews` (R40's shape: `[{reviewer, kind, organisation?, credential?, scope, outcome, at}]`), `approved_by` (a non-empty name different from `authored_by`) and `approved_at` (`YYYY-MM-DD`); its `basis` is a ruling `K<n>`, or `TEST` in a test profile, as R40 says of a template's (R80 states no form for it till now). No guide-level `sources` field is added: the sources are the items' `citation`s (R80), at least one item being required. A guide missing a field is `GUIDE_UNATTRIBUTED` naming the first missing field (the form of R40's `TEMPLATE_UNATTRIBUTED`); a value of the wrong form, or an approver equal to the author, is `VALUE_INVALID` naming the field, as R40 does for a template; a basis that is not a ruling is `BASIS_INVALID`. The review the guide needs to be offered is judged by `reading-guides` R18, not here. Test: a guide with all fields passes; one with no `approved_by`, or with `approved_by` equal to `authored_by`, is refused naming the field; a guide in the test profile with basis `TEST` passes (the test profile's existing guide, R84, gains the fields). Control: R80's other rules and a profile with no `procedure_guides` (the Oakland profile today) are unchanged.

Control: an empty profile answers `[]`.

**Registers, use limits and system terms** (from the N1259-a draft; PLANNING row 18)
- **R127** (N1259-a R99; T53; N1259; K4403 (2), (5), (6); K1485, K1492; amends R4, R57) *(not yet met: T53)* **A profile may hold `registers`: the definitions the file reader and the listing use.** `registers`: `[{key, label, publisher, system, url, pieces, delimiter?, header_from?, columns, row_key, row_key_basis, schemes?, use_limit?, terms_basis, gate, phase, listing, refresh?, count?, basis}]`. `key` is `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*$` and unique. `label` and `publisher` are plain text (the listing's name and source). `system` is an R4 `origin`; `url` is the register's own public https page (the listing's URL) and its host is one of that system's `hosts`. `pieces` is a non-empty (but for `phase: beta` and `status: not_measured`, R133) `[{key, label, locator, member?, delimiter?, header_from?}]`: `locator` an https address, or a template with the named holes `{cycle}` or `{table}`; `member` the name of a zip member; `delimiter` one of `comma`, `tab`, `pipe` (a piece's overrides the register's); `header_from` absent (the first row is the header) or the `key` of another piece (a separate header file). `columns` is `[{name, role, keep, date_format?, currency?}]`; `role` is one of `name`, `organization`, `amount`, `date`, `filer_id`, `form`, `licence_no`, `docket_no`, `company_no`, `city`, `state`, `zip5`, `home_address`, `phone`, `other`; `keep` is a boolean. `row_key` is a non-empty list of column names; `row_key_basis` is `source` (the columns are the source's own key) or `content` (no source key: the key is the kept cells plus their occurrence number). `gate` is `none`, `account`, `fee` or `people_search` (always refused, R128); `status` is absent or `not_measured` (R133). `phase` is `alpha` or `beta`. `schemes` is a list of R52 scheme names the register issues. Malformed: `REGISTER_INVALID` (new), naming field and register. `SECTIONS` (`index.mjs:19`) gains `registers`, `use_limits`, `query_templates`; R4's allowed `systems` fields (`index.mjs:415`) gain R132's; `combine` unions `registers` by `key` (R14, R15). Test: a complete fixture register is accepted; each field absent, of the wrong type or outside its list is refused naming it; a duplicate key is refused; a `url` whose host is not among its system's hosts is `REGISTER_INVALID`. Control: a profile with no `registers` is valid and lists none.
- **R128** (N1259-a R100; T53; N1259; K1485 row 9, K4403 (5), DEC-408; K1492 (2), (3); amends R127) *(not yet met: T53)* **A definition that would keep contact data, hide its terms, or reach a paid people-search is refused.** Exported `validateRegisterDefinition(definition, view)` answers `{ok: true, definition}` or `{ok: false, code, field, key}` and never throws. It refuses: `REGISTER_KEEPS_CONTACT` for any column whose `role` is `home_address` or `phone` with `keep` other than false; `REGISTER_NO_TERMS` for an absent or empty `terms_basis` (the source's stated terms, or "none stated" with the date read); `REGISTER_NO_USE_LIMIT` for a register whose `system` carries a `use_limit` (R131) and which does not name it; `REGISTER_PEOPLE_SEARCH` for a `gate` of `people_search` (a paid people-search is a member's own act on their own account, K1492 (3); results enter as cited sources, never as a register); `REGISTER_ROW_KEY_INVALID` for a `row_key` naming a column the definition does not list, or `row_key_basis: source` with a `row_key` of columns whose role is `other` only. A column the file holds and the definition does not list is not an error: the reader drops it (`register-readers` R3). The same function validates a team's own definition (`registers` R7): one rule. Test: a definition keeping a `phone` column, one with no `terms_basis`, one of an FEC-flagged system without its `use_limit`, one with `gate: people_search` and one with a `row_key` naming an unlisted column are each refused by name; every shipped definition passes. Control: the same definitions with the fault removed pass.
- **R129** (N1259-a R101; T53; N1259; K4403 (2); amends R127) *(not yet met: T53)* **The listing facts each carry their basis.** `listing`: `{bytes, rows, source_date?, measured_at}`; `bytes` and `rows` are each `{value, basis}` with `basis` one of `stated` (the source says it), `counted` (this product counted it), `estimated` (computed, with the method in `how`) or `not_stated` (`value` null); `measured_at` is the civil date the figures were taken (`basis` is R2's form, a dated entry such as `2026-10-21 M-R1`; a bare `M-R1` is no basis). A figure with a basis other than `not_stated` and no `measured_at` is `REGISTER_INVALID`. `count`: `{kind, template, supports}` optional: `kind` `source_count_query` (the source can state the number of rows for a filter, e.g. a SoQL `count(*)`), `template` an https address template, `supports` the filter criteria (`register-readers` R6) the source's query can apply. `refresh`: `{cadence_hours, validators, append_only?}` optional: the least interval between looks, which of `etag`, `last_modified`, `content_length`, `count` tell the source has not changed, and `append_only` true when the source never changes or removes a row. Test: a figure with a basis and no date, and a `count` naming a criterion the filter lacks, are refused; a `refresh` with no validators means "always fetch". Control: `not_stated` with a null value is valid.
- **R130** (N1259-a R102; T53; N1259; K1484, K1492; amends R52) *(not yet met: T53)* **Identifier schemes for filers, licences, dockets and companies, and the query that composes a register's own address from a number.** `identifier_schemes` (R52) may also hold the schemes `filer` (FPPC or NetFile filer id), `committee` (FEC committee id), `licence` (board plus number), `docket` (CourtListener docket id and a court's docket number) and `company` (a company number), each as R52 requires (`scheme`, `label`, `entity_kinds`, `space`, `form`, `systems`, `basis`) plus `registers`, the keys that issue it. R3 has no space for committees, licences or companies: `filer` and `licence` take `person` (R3 names filer ids and licence numbers), `docket` `proceeding`, `committee` and `company` `institution` (BOB confirms); each needs a form in that space, else `SCHEME_INVALID` (`index.mjs:1280-1281`), basis `UNMEASURED` where none was read (R2). `query_templates`: `[{scheme, register, template, basis}]`: `template` is an https address with exactly one hole `{value}`, in the path or one named query parameter, on a host among the register's system hosts; the address a watch reads is composed from the template and a value that matches the scheme's `form`, percent-encoded, never typed by the member. A template with a hole for anything but a value of a listed scheme, two holes, or a hole in the host is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_INVALID`; a template naming a field `name`, `first`, `last` or `person` is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_NOT_NUMBER` (K1484: a watch is by a number). Malformed schemes stay `SCHEME_INVALID`. Test: a docket template composes the address for a fixture id and refuses a value not of the form; a `{name}` hole is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_NOT_NUMBER`; a hole in the host is refused. Control: a scheme with no template validates and cannot be watched.
- **R131** (N1259-a R103; T53; N1259; K4403 (3); K1492 (5); 11 CFR 104.15; 52 U.S.C. 30111(a)(4)) *(not yet met: T53)* **`use_limits`: the data-use limit of a source, as data.** `use_limits`: `[{key, citation, text_key, export, on_publish, basis}]`. `citation` is the rule as cited (for the first profile's federal campaign-finance limit: 11 CFR 104.15 and 52 U.S.C. 30111(a)(4)); `text_key` a `setup-words` key whose words state the limit (the notice a row carries, drawn by the design); `export` is `never` (no export of the register's rows as a list; the only value this release accepts); `on_publish` is `warn`, `refuse` or `allow` (what a case citing a row of such a register meets; counsel's answer sets it, K4403 (3); this release ships `warn`); `basis` the measurement or ruling (the rule's text is in `measures-T52-DEC406.md`). A `systems` entry (R4) may name a `use_limit` key; every register of that system inherits it and may not clear it. A limit with an `export` other than `never`, or an `on_publish` outside the three, is `USE_LIMIT_INVALID`. Test: the first profile's federal system names the limit and its registers inherit it; a register omitting it is `REGISTER_NO_USE_LIMIT`; `on_publish: maybe` is refused. Control: a limit-free register carries none.

Test: a non-positive count, a limit with no `measured_at`, and a `gate: none` register on an `account`-gated system are refused. Control: a system with none is valid.
