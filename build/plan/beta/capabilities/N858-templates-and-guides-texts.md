## 1. Bob's points

*Explanation.* A template is wording a team may send in its own name. ## 3. The process (the people's steps; BOB to place it in the repository's docs; not a requirement of any module)

**The text process, for a template or a procedure guide in a jurisdiction profile:**
1. **Choose.** Bob names the kind and purpose (B2). Nothing is written for a kind whose venue is a court (R28).
2. **Source sheet first.** The writer lists, before writing, every public source the text will rest on: citation, the address where one exists, the date it was read. Sources are public law and agencies' own published material only; nothing confidential, nothing from a member's record. Each item of a guide, and each legal statement of a template, traces to a source on the sheet.
3. **Write.** The named person writes, or takes a machine's initial draft and makes it theirs (K5511): any machine-composed or machine-suggested wording is declared in the text's notes, and the person stays the writer of record; a machine never reviews or approves. In plain words; blanks only from `FILING_BLANKS`; the text takes no position on policy (v0.2 rule 9); it is legal information, not advice; no place named in product code (the text lives in the profile, which is data). A template's `text` and a guide's `items` are what the writer produces; nothing else is a text.
4. **Self-check by machine.** What the machinery already rejects, each where it runs: at the profile's load, `jurisdictions` refuses a text with no source and a guide item with no `citation` (`VALUE_INVALID`, R80, R87 (b)), a template not fully attributed (`TEMPLATE_UNATTRIBUTED`, R40) and a basis that is not a ruling (`BASIS_INVALID`); at read time `filing-templates` refuses a blank outside `FILING_BLANKS` (`TEMPLATE_BLANK_UNKNOWN`, R15) and a court petition (`TEMPLATE_COURT_PETITION`, R28); `reading-guides` refuses, for a team's own guide, an item with no `citation` (`GUIDE_ITEM_UNSOURCED`, R13) and one that carries conduct (`GUIDE_CARRIES_CONDUCT`, R4, in the part R13 applies). Whether `checkGuide` also runs on a profile guide is UNVERIFIED (R4 lists draft, review, adoption and render, none of which a profile guide goes through).
5. **Professional review** (B3) of the exact text and its sheet. A change after review needs a new review (K5510): the changed text is not offered until reviewed again (`jurisdictions` R137). The reviewer is whoever Bob finds, a volunteer open-government lawyer or a paid firm; the review records name, organisation and stated credential, and the text shows "reviewed by [name], [organisation], [date]". Exception (K5508): a member who is the only member of their team may opt out of this review for the team's own guide, and with it out of step 6's separate approver; the opt-out is recorded with the member's name and time, and the guide says it was not reviewed (`reading-guides` R20). A profile text has no team and no opt-out; a team with more than one member cannot opt out.
6. **Approval** by a person other than the writer: Bob (section 2, item 6), with his K ruling.
7. **Entry.** A module job writes the text into the profile with its attribution (section 4's fields). The profile release is signed as every release is. Nothing is offered by the machinery until all three exist in the profile's data, however the text was obtained (a machine's first draft included, declared in the notes).
8. **Keep true** (B5).

**What the machinery offers until texts exist** (all built, drafted here only to be stated and tested): `templatesForAction` answers no template and lists the profile's unreviewed or absent ones under `not_offered` with `lacks`; a court-filed kind answers `court_petition: true`; a member may write her filing with no template (K921, K924); a team may write its own template (project-scoped, reviewed by its members, with its own tier rule, `filing-templates` R3-R10) and its own guide (`reading-guides` R2, R3: another member approves; citations required); the Tier 3 counsel packet needs no template; the profile's laws, deadlines and offices are shown with their citations as facts (`screens-action` R31 (3)); the action screen says "No profile text for this yet" (UX need) rather than offering a generic fallback (DEC-340: "no generic fallback").
