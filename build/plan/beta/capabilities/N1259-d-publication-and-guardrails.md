## 1. citation (highest bold id R25; draft id R26)

- **R26** (T53; N1259; K4403 (4); `content` R63; `inquiry-grammar`) *(not yet met: T53)* **A register row is cited like any other content, and a member's interest line may cite one.** Test-only. A register row's content id (`registers` R28) is cited as a leg of a question's basis and as a `cites` edge on a case; a `lines` statement of a member's interest (`owns_interest_in`, Form 700) cites the row's extent; `inquiry-grammar`'s leg-extent check accepts the extent (a `sheet-range` of a segment held as a register). A cited row later removed under K1493 (`registers` R30) leaves its leg standing, marked removed, with no content shown. If a check in `inquiry-grammar` restricts extents to kinds with a reading, the job REPORTs and BOB opens that module. Test: cite on a question and on a case; sever and reinstate; a leg over a row whose segment is replaced by an expunge shows `removed` and no text; the interest line cites the row. Control: a cell range of an ordinary workbook cites as before.

## 2. case-grammar (highest bold id R34; draft ids R35 to R36)

- **R35** (T53; N1259; K4403 (4); K1483 (a); amends R12) *(not yet met: T53)* **A cited register row travels as a labelled extract, and the source file does not.** The `materials:` block (R12) gains `kind: "register_extract"`: a row `{ref, kind, sha, text_sha, origin, included, rests_under, title, extract}` where `sha` is the reduced segment's SHA-256, `text_sha` the SHA-256 of the extract text, `included` true (the extract travels whole), `archived_copy` null, and `extract` is `{register, file, reduced_sha256, original_sha256, original_covers, source_date, imported_at, src_row, kept_columns, dropped_columns, header, row, label_key: "register.extract", use_limit?}`. The extract text is the header of the kept columns and the cited row's kept cells verbatim, as the segment holds them; the SHA-256s are 64 hex; `source_date` and `imported_at` are dates; `src_row` the row's number in the original piece. K1483's "relied-on documents travel whole" is, for a register row only, "the cited rows travel as an extract; the file is not shipped" (Bob, K4403 (4)). `registerExtractRow(args)` builds the row and `materialsOf` reads it back; both pure and never throwing. A malformed extract is refused `EXTRACT_INVALID` naming the field. Test: a built row round-trips through `materialsOf`; each field absent, a short digest, a non-date `source_date` and a `src_row` of 0 are `EXTRACT_INVALID`; two rows cited from one segment are two rows of one `sha`. Control: a `document` row is read as before.
- **R36** (T53; N1259; K4403 (3)) *(not yet met: T53)* **A cited row of a limited register carries its limit.** When the register has a `use_limit` (`jurisdictions` R103), the extract holds `use_limit: {key, citation, text_key}` and `materialsOf` reads it back. Test: the limited fixture's extract holds the limit; a limit-free one holds none. Control: removing the limit from a limited extract makes the checker's R30 below fail.

## 3. case-disclosures (highest bold id R34; draft ids R35 to R38)

- **R35** (T53; N1259; K1483 (a); amends R24) *(not yet met: T53)* **Every person named in a cited register row is a person the case names.** `peopleNamed` also lists, once each, every person named by the `name` role of a cited row's extract (a donor, payee, lobbyist, filer, licensee or officer), with the place `register row <ref>`; a name the record does not resolve to a person entity is answered in `unresolved` with that place, never dropped and never resolved here. `peopleJudged` (R25) then asks a recorded basis for each, as for any named person; an organization in a row is not a person and is not asked. Test: a case citing two rows naming "A" and "B" lists both with their places; one without a basis is `PERSON_BASIS_UNRECORDED` naming the person to the publisher only; a row naming an organization adds no person. Control: a case citing no register row lists the same persons as before.
- **R36** (T53; N1259; K1493; K1485; DEC-408; amends R26) *(not yet met: T53)* **A phone number or street address typed into the group's own words or an extract is refused before signing; a recorded public-office basis is the only way through.** Pre-flight runs `register-readers.contactShape` over every authored statement, claim, account sentence, observation text the case quotes, `title` and each `register_extract` cell. A hit not listed in the owner's `contactBases: [{text, basis: "public_office", ref}]` (`ref` a held capture that states the number or address as a public office's own) is `CONTACT_SHAPE_IN_PUBLICATION`, naming where it is to the publisher only; a listed hit whose `ref` the record does not hold is `CONTACT_BASIS_NOT_STANDING`. The refusals are pre-flight answers and are never written into the document. The materials a finding relies on still travel whole (R26 / DEC-100 (2)): this rule governs the group's words and extracts, not relied-on documents. The detector's choices (what shape counts, the override) are BOB's, because a rule that refuses an office's address, or lets a home number through, is wrong either way; the false-positive rate is M-R10. Test (the DEC-408 publication test): a sentinel phone and a sentinel street address in a member's note, turned into an observation by the member and cited in a case, are refused at pre-flight; the same sentinel in a question's text and in an extract cell are refused; a city hall address listed with a `public_office` basis passes; an unlisted one is refused; a sentinel in a `people` address fact is never passed to the document (R26). Control: the case without the sentinels passes.
- **R37** (T53; N1259; K4403 (3); K1492 (5); 11 CFR 104.15; `jurisdictions` R103) *(not yet met: T53)* **A case citing a row of a limited register meets the register's `on_publish`.** With `warn` (this release's value), pre-flight answers `USE_LIMIT_NOTICE` naming the register and the limit's citation, which the owner acknowledges (`limit_acknowledged: [register]`) by a member's act recorded with who and when, and the published case carries the notice by key beside the extract; with `refuse` it is `USE_LIMIT_REFUSES_PUBLICATION`; with `allow` nothing is asked, and the notice is still carried. Counsel's answer (K4403 (3), before the first beta) changes only the profile's `on_publish`. Test: with `warn`, a case with one limited row refuses signing until acknowledged and then passes with the notice in the document; with `refuse` it refuses whatever is acknowledged; a limit-free register asks nothing. Control: acknowledging a register the case does not cite is `NOT_CITED`.
- **R38** (T53; N1259; K1483 (a)) *(not yet met: T53)* **A register extract is bounded.** A case carries at most `EXTRACT_ROWS_MAX` cited rows from registers (a draft figure, 500, BOB's) in total; a case over it is `EXTRACT_TOO_MANY` naming the count and the bound, nothing partly signed. Test: 500 rows pass, 501 are refused with both numbers. Control: a case with none passes.

## 4. case-checker (highest bold id R28; draft ids R29 to R32)

- **R29** (T53; N1259; K4403 (4); amends R4, R8) *(not yet met: T53)* **The checker checks a register extract against what it can see, and says what it cannot.** For each `register_extract` material (`case-grammar` R35) it checks: (a) the extract's `text_sha` equals the SHA-256 of the carried extract text, and the extract's header and row have the same number of cells; (b) every passage a finding relies on from the row is found in the extract text, and its `content_id` recomputes by `content.contentIdFor` from the reduced segment's `sha`, the extent and the chain; (c) `reduced_sha256`, `original_sha256`, `source_date`, `imported_at` and `src_row` are present and well formed; (d) the label `register.extract` is present; (e) when `use_limit` is held on the register's row, the notice is present (R30 below). A failure is a `differs` entry naming the field; a missing extract is `missing`. R8's presentability is amended: a `register_extract` is presentable when its extract is carried (`included: true`), and neither the source file's bytes nor the reduced file's are asked. Test: each failure above is a `differs` or `missing` by name on a changed byte, a moved cell, a short digest, a removed label; a good extract passes. Control: a changed byte in an unrelated material still fails as before.
- **R30** (T53; N1259; K4403 (3)) *(not yet met: T53)* **A limited register's notice must be present.** An extract whose register had a `use_limit` and whose document does not carry the notice (`text_key`, by key) is `differs` naming it. Test: remove the notice, fail; restore, pass. Control: a limit-free extract needs none.
- **R31** (T53; N1259; K4403 (4); DEC-112) *(not yet met: T53)* **The checker says plainly that the source file is not in the package.** Its answer for a case with extracts carries `unverifiable: [{ref, what: "row_in_source_file", why}]` for each extract: that the row exists in the file the fingerprints name cannot be re-derived from the package, and a reader who fetches the source can compare the original's fingerprint only if the source is unchanged (the original is re-fetched as it then is). "Recreating shows a case intact and consistent, not true." Test: a case with two extracts lists two `unverifiable` entries and the standalone program prints them after the findings; a case with none lists none. Control: the other checks' answers are unchanged.
- **R32** (T53; N1259) *(not yet met: T53)* **The standalone program is rebuilt with these checks.** R13's single file includes R29-R31 from the same code; the same build gives the same bytes. Test: the program run on a fixture case with an extract prints R29-R31's results; its SHA-256 equals the rebuild's. Control: a case with no extract gives the former output byte for byte.

## 5. publication (highest bold id R79; draft id R80) and public-read (R45; R46)

- **R80** (publication; T53; N1259; K4403 (4)) *(not yet met: T53)* **The package carries each extract and its labels, and never the register's file or index.** The container and the in-band quartet carry, for each `register_extract` material, the extract text and its block (`case-grammar` R35); the reduced segment, the original, the row index and the full capture are never packaged, whatever the case cites. Test: a fixture case with 3 extracts packages the 3 extracts and no segment bytes (the package scan finds no byte of a segment not cited, and none of the segment at all); the package digest is stable. Control: a case with ordinary documents packages them whole as before.
- **R46** (public-read; T53; N1259; K4403 (3), (4); DEC-408) *(not yet met: T53)* **A published page draws the extract's labels and the limit's notice by key, and holds no phone or address shape the group wrote.** The evidence-package block beside a published case draws, for each extract, the label ("extract of a register; the source file is not shipped"), the reduced and original fingerprints, `source_date`, `imported_at` and the row position, and, where held, the limit's notice, each by key from the design; it writes nothing. A published surface (the case page, the evidence block, the public reads by hash, finding and case, the projection) holds no sentinel phone or street address from a member's note, a `people` address fact or an import. Test: a published fixture case with an extract draws the labels by key; the scan of every public read for the sentinels finds none; a limited extract draws the notice. Control: the scan finds a deliberately planted sentinel in a test copy.

## 6. corpus-export (highest bold id R15; draft id R16)

- **R16** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2), (3); K1493; amends R9) *(not yet met: T53)* **A register is exported as its descriptors and tombstones, never as rows; a limited register's rows in no form.** For each import the export carries `{register, scope, filter, filter_fingerprint, source, source_date, imported_at, counts, segment_shas, original_fingerprints, reduction}` and each tombstone `registers` lists (table, key, ground, instant; none of the removed content); it carries no cell, no index row, no segment and no row of a register with a `use_limit`, and the export log records it. An import of an export re-creates the descriptors and shows "rows not carried: bring them in again from the source" and where a removal was; it restores no row and never a tombstoned key. Test: an export of a fixture instance with two imports holds two descriptors, one tombstone and no cell (a scan for the fixture's cells finds none); importing it shows both descriptors and the tombstone and no row; a limited register's descriptor holds no cell either. Control: other exported tables are carried as declared.

## 7. The guardrail tests, as requirement lines

Each has a Control (the same call without the fault passes) and uses fixtures under the test profile, not Oakland's. - **R61** (registers; T53; N1259; K1484; G1) *(not yet met: T53)* **G1 · A watch is by a number or a stated interest, never an unattended search for a person.** Scenario: a project states an interest with a name; a `register-query` is attempted with a name, a partial number, two registers, an unlisted scheme and a wildcard; a machine credential attempts each. Each is refused by name (`QUERY_NOT_NAMED`, `INTEREST_NOT_WATCHABLE`, `MACHINE_CANNOT_*`) with nothing written; the alarm's tick fetches exactly one composed address per `register-query` and follows no link; the name interest is never ticked; the ops map holds no op that fetches by name. Control: a filer-id interest and a docket-number watch land and tick.
- **R62** (registers; T53; N1259; K1484; G2) *(not yet met: T53)* **G2 · A name search is over held rows only.** After a battery of searches by name, amount and date: zero requests; no `entities` resolution, entity, link or hunch created (row counts of `entities`, `lines`, `connections` equal before and after); answers hold rows as written and no `score`, `rank`, `suspect` or `flag` field. Control: `entities.addIdentifier` by a member still works.
- **R63** (registers; T53; N1259; K1485; DEC-408; G3) *(not yet met: T53)* **G3 · An import never copies a home address or phone number.** A fixture file with sentinel values in `home_address`, `phone`, an unlisted column and a free-text cell is imported whole and by filter, searched, cited, compared, refreshed, expunged and exported. A scan of every table, the object store, the index, every minted content text, every export, every queue item, every log and every error and answer finds no sentinel; the dropped columns are named, never valued; `people` R10 `CONTACT_NOT_IMPORTED` is unchanged. Control: the scan finds a sentinel planted in a test row (it works), and the kept columns' values are present.
- **R64** (registers; T53; N1259; K4403 (5); G12) *(not yet met: T53)* **G12 · The original is never stored; its fingerprint and the dropped names are.** After an import by a filter that matches 2 of 10 rows, no stored object holds the 8 unmatched rows or the original's bytes (a scan for their distinctive values); the hop holds the original's SHA-256 (equal to an independent hash of the fixture), its byte count and `covers`, the dropped column names, the filter fingerprint and "reduced under K1485". Control: the 2 matched rows are present.
- **R65** (registers; T53; N1259; K1492; G5) *(not yet met: T53)* **G5 · Lookup conduct.** (1) A gated or fee-bearing register: import, refresh and watch answer `member_act_required`, fetch nothing and record nothing but that. (2)(3) A definition naming a login-gated site or a paid people-search is refused at validation. (4) Every request carries R9's agent and passes `host-governor`; a definition without `terms_basis` is refused. (6) After a battery of searches no table, observation row, tally or log holds a query string or the member's id against it (sentinel scan). Control: a public register's import proceeds.
- **R66** (registers; T53; N1259; K4403 (3); 11 CFR 104.15; G6) *(not yet met: T53)* **G6 · The FEC guard.** Every `searchRows`, `rowOf`, `citeRow` and `compare` answer carrying a row of a limited register carries the limit; no op returns contributor identity as a list or file (the ops map asserts it; an unfiltered search is `NO_CRITERIA`; a search is capped at 1,000 rows); nothing solicits (no op sends row data to a recipient); `corpus-export` carries no row (R16 above); the definition of a limited system without its `use_limit` is refused. Control: a limit-free register is unaffected.
- **R134** (acquisition; T53; N1259; K1449; G7) *(not yet met: T53)* **G7 · Keyed services are off by default and the key is secret.** With the key off or absent no docket poll fetches (zero requests); the key appears in no answer, receipt, log, error or queue item; a free key is read by the alarm only for a named follow (`standing`) and a fee-bearing service never; with the key on and the limit reached, polls stop and say `rate_held`. Control: the key on, a poll for a named follow fetches.
- **R33** (notice-producers; T53; N1259; K4403 (7); G8) *(not yet met: T53)* **G8 · Told once.** One change yields one item; it is not raised again until disposed and changed again; a watch ended (`ends`, stop, forget) raises none; a hidden-project watch's item reaches only its participants; a name in the changed rows is in no item. Control: a second change yields a second item.
- **R67** (registers; T53; N1259; K4403 (2); G9) *(not yet met: T53)* **G9 · The background import.** An import survives a restart between slices and ends with the same rows, segments and counts as an uninterrupted one (digest of the row table); a paused daemon runs no slice; the slice bound is stated and respected on a fixture of 1,000,000 rows; a member's screens answer within budget during it. Control: an uninterrupted run, and unpaused.
- **R68** (registers; T53; N1259; K4403 (2); G10) *(not yet met: T53)* **G10 · Mechanical: no AI.** A full cycle (plan, delta, import, search, cite, compare, interest, refresh, change item) makes zero calls through the AI boundary, changes no AI account or `ai-use` counter, and sends no request to an AI provider host; neither `registers` nor `register-readers` lists an AI module in `uses`. Control: the spy counts a call made by a deliberately AI-using fixture.
- **R69** (registers; T53; N1259; K1493; G4) *(not yet met: T53)* **G4 · Removal.** An expunged row leaves a tombstone and no byte (R30 of part (b)); `searchRows` and `rowOf` no longer answer it; a re-import does not restore it (tombstone by `row_key`); the export carries the tombstone and not the content; a `lawful_demand` of a kind the profile lists is accepted as a ground; any other ground is refused. Control: another row of the same segment is intact.
- **R39** (case-disclosures; T53; N1259; K1493; DEC-408; G4 published, G11) *(not yet met: T53)* **G11 · A home address or phone number in a member's note never reaches a published surface; an import never copies one.** Scenario: sentinel phone and address in (a) a member's note, (b) the same note turned into an observation and a question by the member, (c) a `people` address fact, (d) a register fixture's address and phone columns; the member builds a case citing the observation, the question and a register row, runs pre-flight, signs (with the blocking items removed) and publishes. Control: the scan finds a sentinel planted in a copy; the note is intact.
- **R70** (registers; T53; N1259; K1489; G13) *(not yet met: T53)* **A hidden project's import is invisible to everyone else, in every read.** Scenario: a hidden project's import with sentinel names, a team pre-read, and a non-participant's `registerList`, `registerDelta`, `searchRows`, `compare`, `importStatus`, `changesSince`, queue and export: no trace, and `already_held` counts only what the viewer sees. - **R71** (registers; T53; N1259; K4405 (4); K1492) *(not yet met: T53)* **A team's own register is held to the same conduct.** A team-defined register with a contact column asked kept, no terms, a gated host or a `people_search` is refused; its first import drops every unassigned column (sentinel scan); it is listed only to its team. Control: a valid own definition imports.
- **R72** (registers; T53; N1259; K4403 (3), (4)) *(not yet met: T53)* **A cited row is an extract whose labels are true.** Cite a fixture row, package a fixture case, and check it: the extract's two fingerprints equal the segment's hop, its `src_row` locates the row in the fixture, the case checker passes it with `unverifiable` stated, and a changed byte in the extract fails it.
