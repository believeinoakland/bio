HIGH RISK
High risk: it opens in the safe view, a picture of each page, until a deeper check passes. Its grade and its place in the record are unchanged.

Why: Handed in, not fetched by your group's Civicsmith from where it is published.

A copy made by your group's Civicsmith: each page as a picture, nothing in it can run.
