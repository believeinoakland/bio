Settings
YOURS
Your account
Your own settings: your handle, password, signing key, language and expertise.
The assistant
Whether and how the assistant serves you: on your group's key, your own Claude account, or not at all. Set your limit and suggestions here.
Your ties
Your own ties to people or organizations the group may look into. Only you and the administrators see this list.
Your notes
Your private notes. Only you can see them in your group; turn one into an observation, a hunch or a question when you choose.
Recovery codes
Your own recovery codes, which let you set a new password if you lose yours. Each works once.
THE GROUP'S
Your group
Administrators only: your group's name, website, languages, court notice, hosting account and bar.
Who your group is
What kind of group yours is, what it focuses on and why it exists. It shapes how members are welcomed and locks nothing.
Members
Everyone in your group and what each may do. Administrators invite members, set what they may do, and see who holds the hosting account.
Places and offices
Administrators only: the places whose rules your group's Civicsmith follows (deadlines, holidays, offices, records laws), and the offices and seats your group deals with.
Security
Administrators only: how hard your group's Civicsmith is being tried, by kind and by hour, against its own usual. Counts only; nothing names who tried.
Updates
Administrators only: the release your group's Civicsmith runs, the release ready to install and what changes in it, and the choice: Update, Not now or Skip this version.
Translations
The words members see, in your group's languages. Members given the grant translate them, the assistant drafts where it can, and protected words are checked twice.
Wizards
Step-by-step walk-throughs of the real screens. Civicsmith's own come with each release; your group can write its own or copy one and change the copy.
