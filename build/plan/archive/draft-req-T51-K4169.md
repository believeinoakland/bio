## 1. C12 — a case's standard details (UC-101)

### 1.1 Owner and why

UC-101 lists nine items (Design Requirements 6). Six already live somewhere: summary (`case-authoring` R14 scope and statement), all data sources and methodology (`case-disclosures` R4, R5), status with links to challenges and updates (`docket`, `public-read` R37), and the group's stake (`project-roster` R31, R32; `public-read` R34, R36). Five have no place: **area of government, content classification, point of contact, related work, relationship disclosure.** (The group's own stake covers part of "relationships"; a case's disclosure of ties to the bodies it examines is not that, and R22 says so.)

Owner: `case-inputs`. Its Purpose is "what other acts record about a case" and it already holds the ops map (`caseInputsOps`, R16), the purge declaration (R11) and the dead-answer rule (R14). `case-authoring` is the wrong owner on three counts: it is 3,020 lines of its 4,000 after four splits; its Purpose is authoring the signed document, and these details do not enter the document; and its authority rule is the same owner-only rule, which `case-inputs` can copy without importing.

**Why the details stay out of the signed bytes (K4164 goal 1).** Putting five fields into `bio-case-document` changes `case-grammar` (format `/8`, DEC-124), `case-authoring` (writes), `case-checker` (checks), `case-import` (recreates), `public-read` and `case-carriage`: six modules for one capability. The group's stake is the precedent: it is "never written into the signed document or the case file" (`public-read` R34, R36) and is read current. Details follow it. They also change after signing (a contact moves), which a signed edition cannot do honestly. The cost is stated in R25: the details are the group's current statement, not part of the edition's signature, and the public read says so.

**Not blocking.** Design Requirements 10 has the compliance skill display a work product's compliance with the metadata standard, and Declared Bias (DEC-20) discloses and never blocks. So an unstated field is shown "not stated", never refused at publication, and never filled in (undetermined stays first-class).

## 2. C49 — regrade another group's case under a lens, or rerun its work (UC-076)

### 2.1 Owner, and whether "lens" needs a Bob decision

Owner: `case-import`. It already holds the imported editions, every finding's recreated pair, the importer's bar and, since R23, each finding under the importing group's own lens (K2471). `case-checker` R23 already does the mechanical regrade (`as_published`, `removed`, a reader's own lens) offline and `reweigh` is pure. What is missing is the member's way in: to choose a lens, see the difference by statement, and start and keep a rerun. A new module is not needed; the order is unchanged.

**The lenses are settled by canon; no Bob decision on which exist.** Declared Bias "Differential traversal" (amended 2026-10-09, D59, K2420) and Investigation §11 give exactly three: **as published, bias removed, the reader's own**. `case-checker` R23 and `case-import` R23 already implement them, "the reader's own" being the importing group's statements in force at scope `instance` (`bias.statementInForce`, its R49). Two details are BOB's (recorded in `rulings.md`, reported to Bob as done): (a) the rerun comparison of R31 reads the rerun project's own effective lens (project scope, `bias.biasManifest`) as "own", since the rerun is the project's work; (b) a `null` (undetermined) lens is stated, not read as false (R23). The honest limit is already worded (`case-checker` R23, K2529) and is repeated on every answer.

The "rerun" half is human (Declared Bias): another group's work, redone under its own bias, the two side by side with the disagreement localised to named lens differences. The system cannot know that two independent findings answer the same claim, so the pairing is a member's act and the system names only the lens differences it can compute. Nothing is sent to the source group (v0.2 rule 14, Publication §5D: nothing is pushed).

### 3.1 Owner

### 3.2 `network-forum` — requirements (drafted for a new file, `build/requirements/network-forum.md`)

**Purpose.** Lets a group put a post, or a reply to another group's post, at its own public address, signed by one of its owners, and lets its members read the posts of the groups they list. A thread is the posts that name one another. Nothing is sent anywhere: a post is read by pulling the public address of the group that made it. No group, and no one in Civicsmith, can remove another group's post; a group takes back its own with a reason; each member can mute for herself.

**Terms.** A **post** is one signed statement of a group. Its **digest** is the SHA-256 of its canonical JSON. A **thread** is a root post and every post whose `parent` chain reaches it. A **feed** is a group's public list of its posts and take-backs. A **reading list** is the set of other groups' public addresses this copy reads. A **take-back** is a later signed entry naming a post and a reason.

## 4. C62 — the decisions the record routes to a member (UC-046)

### 4.1 What exists and what is missing

`queue` R1 already names the kinds `authority-undetermined`, `endorsement-owed` and `expertise-confirmation-owed`; `tasks` R1 drains the first into an inbox task (routed to the project owner, else the first citing project's owner, else the earliest administrator, else unassigned); its only way out is `taskresolve`, "mark done" (`queue` R12: `taskresolve` otherwise). Nothing determines authority; nothing raises the other two. The acts for the other two exist: `adminEndorse` (`member-roster` R3, `op=adminendorse`), project-owner votes (`project-roster` R3, R4, R30) and `expertiseConfirm` (`member-roster` R18).

### 4.2 Owners and why (one source of truth)

- **The fact "who issued this document" is `provenance`'s.** It owns the register and the per-document authority fields (R42, C-18.9); `declareOrigin`/`originOf` (R29, R30) is the exact precedent: per document, dated, append-only, the latest standing, a machine refused by name. The determination goes there so every reader of a document's authority (C-18.9, `case-disclosures` R4) sees one standing value with no change of its own.
- **The obligation and its one act is `tasks`'.** A member deciding from her to-do list should make one act. `tasks` is the inbox, layer 11, and uses `provenance`; it owns the op `taskdecide`, which asks `provenance` for the fact and resolves the task in the same act. (A separate decide-then-resolve pair would be two calls from one screen, the fragility K4164 names.)
- **The items for the other two are `queue-producers`'** (its Purpose: "each derives, on read and writing nothing, the items one provider's facts earn"). Each owner exposes its own truth as a read: `member-roster` answers what endorsements and confirmations are owed; `queue-producers` turns them into items. No new module; no layer.
