## 1. DEC-152 · T34-90 · the assistant drafts the group's description

**`instance-setup`** (next free R65)
- **R65** (DEC-152; K1818; K1364; K1755) `groupDescriptionDraft({answers, viewer, by})` (`op=groupdescriptiondraft`): on "Who your group is", the administrator asks the assistant to help write the group's focus and its reasons for existing (`membership` R109's `focus` and `purpose`).
  - It takes the administrator's answers to a few questions. Their wording is the design stream's. `answers` is a list of `{question, text}`, each `text` 0–1,000 characters.
  - It answers `{focus, purpose}`, each `{text, label: {kind: "machine", asked_by: <handle>}}`. The UX stream words that label "Draft · the assistant's, asked by <handle>". Each text stays within R109's limits (1,000 and 4,000 characters).
  - The draft is built only from the answers and what the group holds (DEC-153 (2), which amends DEC-152). It never adds a fact (R25's check, in `wizard-scripts`).
  - It writes nothing. Nothing is saved until the administrator edits it and keeps it through `op=groupdescriptionset` (`membership` R109), the administrator's own act. Once kept, the words are the group's (K1364).
  - It runs on the account that `credentials.accountFor` answers for the administrator (its R35; K1755), within that account's daily ceiling (`ai-runs` R50).
  - Refusals, in order:
    1. `NOT_AN_ADMIN` (`membership` R84);
    2. `ASSISTANT_OFF` (R55);
    3. `AI_NO_ACCOUNT`, `AI_USE_CEILING_REACHED`, `AI_USE_COPY_CEILING_REACHED` (`run-rules` R20; answered at the door, `control-plane`);
    4. `GROUP_DRAFT_NO_ANSWERS` (C-64.8, new): every answer is empty, so there is nothing to draft from.
  - Without the assistant, the screen is unchanged: the act is not offered (the design stream's, NOTICE). The registry's `owed:groupdescriptiondraft` is registered once `op-declarations` R29 declares the op (`wizard-scripts` R13).
  - Row draft, **C-64.8 `GROUP_DRAFT_NO_ANSWERS`**: "Tell the assistant a little about your group first: it drafts only from what you tell it and what your group already holds. Nothing was saved."

**`wizard-scripts` R13, amended.** The machine-draft ops registered at start (`machineDrafts`) gain `groupdescriptiondraft` and `writinghelp`, so a script's step may carry `{machine: <op>}` for either. R12's exception for a registered machine draft on an act in `machineRefused` does not extend to these two ops: R24's refused set governs them (§2).

## 2. DEC-153 · T34-91 · "Help me write this" in own-words fields

**`wizard-scripts`** (next free R24, R25). This module already holds the labelled-draft rules (R2, R12; K1364 B3), so the rule goes here.
- **R24** (DEC-153 (1), (4); K1818) `writingHelpAt({op, draftHeld, viewer})` (pure; in-process, and read by the interface through `wizardRegistry`, R21) answers `{offered: true}` or `{offered: false, code}` for an own-words field of `op`.
  - Help is not offered where the assistant is refused or the act is irreversible (`WRITING_HELP_REFUSED`). That covers:
    - every act in `machineRefused` (R13);
    - every act whose rung is `irreversible` (`affordances` R2; registered at start beside `machineRefused`);
    - DEC-153 (4)'s named acts `release`, `conclude`, `withdrawconclusion`, `reopen`, `caseratify`, `publish`, `personexpunge` and `bootstrap` (the installer's short name);
    - `groupdescriptionset`, which keeps its guided draft (R65 of `instance-setup`; DEC-153 (4)).
  - Help is not offered where a labelled draft already fills the field (`draftHeld`: a wizard's, a template's or a machine draft; `WRITING_HELP_DRAFT_HELD`).
  - Help is not offered where no account serves the viewer, or the assistant is off (`AI_NO_ACCOUNT`, `ASSISTANT_OFF`): "without the assistant nothing appears" (DEC-153 (5)).
  - Which fields take a member's own words (a note, an observation, a reason, a request, a case's scope, what changed) is the screen registry's and the design stream's, not this module's.
  - R12 is amended to match: `WIZARD_STEP_CONCLUDES` refuses a step carrying `{machine: writinghelp}` or `{machine: groupdescriptiondraft}` on any act R24 refuses.
- **R25** (DEC-153 (2), (3); DEC-152; K1364) `checkDraft(text, {told, readLog, firsthand})` (pure) answers `{ok, text, withheld}`. It follows `answers` R4's form: a sentence that fails the check is withheld and never rewritten.
  - A sentence fails when it states a figure, a date, a name or a quotation that is neither in `told` (what the member wrote or answered) nor in what `readLog` records as read from the group's record (`WRITING_HELP_FACT_ADDED`).
  - With `firsthand` true (the field records what the member saw: `testify`, and the acts BOB lists at START from the registry's capture and notes screens), `readLog` must be empty. The draft words only what the member told it (DEC-153 (2): "testimony stays the witness's"), and any read is refused (`WRITING_HELP_FIRSTHAND_READ`).
  - The draft text is answered with its label `{kind: "machine", asked_by}` and is never stored.
  - It becomes the member's words only when the member keeps or edits it through the field's own act. That act runs its own checks, reason and receipt (R2; K1364 B3).
- **The op** `writingHelp({op, field, told, viewer, by})` (`op=writinghelp`) is served once the model turn exists (§0; N-draft-1). It answers R25's draft for R24's offered fields, on the account that `credentials.accountFor` answers for the member (K1755), within that member's ceiling. Refusals, in order:
  1. R24's codes;
  2. `WRITING_HELP_NOTHING_TOLD` (`told` empty or over 4,000 characters);
  3. `run-rules` R20's ceiling codes.
- Row drafts (C-131.33–.37, new, each stamped at the next promotion job):
  - **`WRITING_HELP_REFUSED`**: "The assistant does not help word this: it is an act the assistant may never take, or one that cannot be undone. The words here are yours alone."
  - **`WRITING_HELP_DRAFT_HELD`**: "This field already holds a draft. Keep it, edit it or clear it before asking the assistant."
  - **`WRITING_HELP_NOTHING_TOLD`**: "Tell the assistant what you want to say first: it works only from what you tell it and what your group holds."
  - **`WRITING_HELP_FACT_ADDED`**: "A sentence was left out because it said something you did not tell the assistant and your group's record does not hold."
  - **`WRITING_HELP_FIRSTHAND_READ`**: "For what you saw yourself, the assistant only helps word what you told it, and reads nothing else."

## 3. DEC-158 · T34-92 · "Copy a wizard to change it"

**`wizard-scripts`**
- **R1, amended** (DEC-158 (4)): a script gains `based_on`, which is `null` or the version id `<script>@<version>` it was copied from. It is set once, at the copy, and never changed.
- **R3, amended** (DEC-158 (1)–(3); K1818): `wizardDraft` takes `copy: <version>` as a third start, beside `recorded` and `from`. The rules for `from` stay as they are.
  - `copy` names any approved version the author may see, whether Civicsmith's or the group's (R20).
  - It creates a **new** script with `origin: group`, `scope: {project}`, `required: false` and `based_on` set. Its first draft's steps are that version's steps, and those `(screen, act)` pairs count as the draft's recorded pairs (R4).
  - It is never a version of the base script, and the base is unchanged and still offered.
  - The copy is a draft until approved like any wizard the group writes: the same editor grant (R4, R8), checks (R6, R12) and approval (R7).
  - A copy of a `required` Civicsmith script never replaces it. The Civicsmith script stays the required one, still offered and still checked by R14.
  - Refusals: as R3's, with `NO_SUCH_WIZARD` for a `copy` that is absent, not approved or not visible (one answer).
- **R4, amended** (DEC-158 (4)): `adopt` may also be `{base: <version>}`, meaning the steps of a newer approved version of the copy's base. Its `(screen, act)` pairs join the version's adopted pairs, so a member without the advanced editor may bring the change across. `NO_SUCH_WIZARD` answers when the version named is not a newer approved version of `based_on`'s script.
- **R10 and R21, amended**: `wizards`, `wizardRead` and the registry form answer `based_on`, with the base's name, so the copy "shows what it is based on".
- **R26** (DEC-158 (4); K1818) `baseUpdates({after?, limit?, viewer})` lists each pair (copy, newer base version): an offered or draft copy whose base script has an approved version later than `based_on`'s, with both version ids and both step lists, so its editors "can see what changed".
  - Each pair is listed once, in (instant, copy) order, at most 500 per page, with `cursor` and `truncated`, for `queue-producers`.
  - It writes nothing but the instant the pair was first found.
  - The recipients are "the group's wizard editors" for the copy. BOB reads this as the members holding a live editor grant (R8) who may see the copy, and the copy's approvers (R7's right) when none holds a grant. BOB's reading (P17), to confirm at START.
