## 2. sources — a new module (layer 3, after `capture`; see Q1)

**Purpose.** It holds each source, meaning the knocker, the person who handed material over, and later the person revealed behind them, as a dated, attributed history of disclosures, never as one overwritten field (DEC-78 item 5). It also answers what of a source may be published, and to whom.

- **R1** A source exists for each pulled knock (capture R65): one per pseudonym, and one per unlinked knock. `sourceOf({captureSha, viewer})` answers the source as it stood when the capture was received (the capture's own `source`, verbatim) and, beside it, the source's current history. A capture's stated source never changes. *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R2** **recordDisclosure({source, revealed: {kind, value?}, how, knownTo, evidence, recorded, by})**. `kind` is one of `pseudonym_link`, `attribute` (occupation, employer, role), `name`. `how` is one of `self`, `filing`, `third_party`, `hostile`. `knownTo` is one of `member`, `group`, `public`. `evidence` is required. The entry is appended with `by` (a stamp) and the instant, and never edited; a later entry supersedes it on read. Refusals: `NO_SUCH_SOURCE` (C-121.1), `BAD_DISCLOSURE` (C-121.2, naming the field), `NO_EVIDENCE` (C-121.3). *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R3** A `hostile` disclosure is stored as the exposer's claim, `{claimed_by, at}`, and read as "named by <claimed_by> on <date>; not confirmed by the group", with `confirmed: false` always. A confirmation is its own R2 entry, and R7 requires the source's consent for it (DEC-78 item 5(b)). *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R4** `recorded: false` records that a detail is known to the group without storing its value ("known to the group, not recorded", item 5(c)). The entry holds no value, and no read answers one. *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R5** A stored value (`recorded: true`) needs `sight: [member ids]`, a non-empty list (`NO_SIGHT_LIST`, C-121.4). Only those members read the value; any other viewer gets the entry with `value` withheld and `withheld: true`. Each read that answers a value appends `{source, entry, reader, at}` to the source's read log. `readLog({source})` answers that log to the listed members and to administrators. *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R6** **linkClaim({source, to, evidence, by})** claims that two sources are one person. It is recorded as a `pseudonym_link` disclosure with its evidence. When the revealed person presents the same knocker secret (R11's arm), the claim's basis is `same_secret`, the strongest. The claim does not merge the sources: each keeps its own history. `NO_SUCH_SOURCE`. *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R7** **recordConsent({source, entry, audience, evidence, by})** and **withdrawConsent({source, entry, audience, by})**. A consent covers one entry for one audience and is stated as permanent for anything published under it. A withdrawal binds only later publications: what is published stays published (item 5(d)). `CONSENT_NOT_STANDING` (C-121.5) when the entry does not exist, or when the audience is lower than what is already consented. *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R8** **publishableAt({source, audience, at?})** answers each entry that may be shown to `audience`, each with its basis: `consent` (R7, not withdrawn at `at`) or `public_elsewhere` (a `knownTo: public` entry whose evidence is a citation, and never a `hostile` one alone, which answers as R3's claim sentence). Every other entry is left out, and nothing is said about it. The group is never the first to make a detail more public (item 5(a)). It writes nothing and never throws. *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R9** **rungOf({source, viewer})** answers the ladder: `unknown`; `same_knocker` (proved by the secret); `partly_known`; `known_to_group`; `publicly_known`. Each rung comes with who knows and how, from the history, and a withheld value stays withheld (R5). *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R10** `onDisclosure(listener)` is a registration later modules fill once at start. Each listener goes through `membership.listenerRefusal`, and each is called after every R2, R6 or R7 commit with `{source, entry, rung_before, rung_after}`. A listener's failure never undoes the act. *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R11** `consentBySecret({knockerSecret, entry, audience})` (`op=sourceconsent`, no account): a source proves who they are by presenting their knocker secret, and consents to (or withdraws from) one entry for one audience. `SECRET_NOT_RECOGNISED` (C-121.6) is answered identically for every failure, and is rate-bound as a knock is. *(not yet met: N345; see Q4)*
- **R12** (invariant) The capturing member is never recorded as the source of what someone else gave them (Membership v2 §1.2): no act here names a capture's `actor` as its source. *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R13** (invariant) Every table here is exempt from purge. A value is never written to a log, an error or a listener payload. *(not yet met: N345)*
- **R14** (invariant) No place is named in this module's behaviour or outward text; C-121.1–C-121.6 are held in its own table. *(not yet met: N345)*

**Uses:** `legacy-checks`; `record-core` (`transact`, `stampInstant`, `mintOpaqueId`, `declarePurge`); `membership` (`listenerRefusal`, the session stamp, `activeAdmins`); `capture` (the inbox rows, `knocksOf`, the capture's `source`).
**Satisfies:** Intake Doctrine §2a (DEC-78 items 2, 3, 5); Membership v2 §1.2.

## (a) Only Bob can decide

1. **Where a source's disclosure history lives (new module).** *Recommend* a new module, `sources`, at layer 3, directly after `capture`. It is sensitive, sight-restricted, read-logged data about people who are not members. `capture` is near its 4,000-line limit, and a home in `membership` would need a backwards edge to `capture`.
2. **Who may register a browser key (security policy).** *Recommend* the member registers their own (R89), from a signed-in session, with every administrator notified and able to revoke. The alternative is to keep registration with administrators, as today, which adds a step for every owner.
3. **The capture grade of doorbell material (doctrine).** Nothing states it, and today it would read `undetermined` (no recorded route). *Recommend* the §3a pattern: route `doorbell`, no fetched letter, and a basis of "received, not fetched: chain of custody from the knock's receipt", with the pull's timestamp proving existence at that time.
4. **Whether a pseudonym is an identity detail, and how an anonymous source consents (meaning, UX).** *Recommend* yes: a published case shows "an unnamed source" unless the pseudonym link is consented. Consent comes either from the source presenting their secret at the doorbell (R11, the strongest) or is recorded by a member with evidence (R7).
5. **Hand-carried material has no intake route today.** The rule is recorded (sources R12), but nothing files such material. *Recommend* it waits for the redesign's upload act and is not built now.

BOB's own rulings, to record: the pre-flight stays write-free, so DEC-81's retry is a separate act (`op=reattest`) that the ceremony runs before the read; the fields fold into `/5`; a secret must be at least 20 characters; the C-121 and C-122 families; and capture R20's stale mark.
