§13.1 Across accounts (new)

Sessions on your two accounts cannot see each other: get_session, send_message and the doorbell (create_trigger) all fail across accounts. The repository is the only place they meet. This clause gives them the same mailbox rules as BOB and its jobs (§13), without a doorbell. It serves P2 (the process and the product are separate, and so are their two streams of rulings), P13 (state lives in git, not in a session) and P17 (you decide; nobody re-asks you a question already settled).

1

Participants and addresses

A registry, build/channels.md on main, has one row per participant:

nameaccountmail branchoutboxwrites
BOBsecondarymail/BOBmail/BOB.mdBOB and its successors
UX-DESIGNprimarymail/UX-DESIGNmail/UX-DESIGN.mdthe UX design session and its successors

The current session id is not in the registry. It is the first line of each outbox, **Writer** · <name> · <session id> · <account> · since <date>, and a successor rewrites that one line at takeover, on its own branch. The registry therefore changes only when a participant is added or a branch is renamed, which BOB does at a tranche's close or the UX session does through its PR.

Where I differ from your outline. You suggested each outbox live on its writer's working branch. BOB's working branch changes every tranche, and the UX session's changes with each PR, so a reader would first have to look up a moving address in a registry that sits on main, which is frozen while a tranche runs (P12). A dedicated branch per writer never moves, holds only that writer's outbox, and needs no merge into main ever. Each branch is still written only by its owner, which keeps your rule that a session pushes only to its own branch.
2

Mailboxes

An outbox holds numbered, append-only entries and one cursor line per correspondent:

**Writer** · BOB · session_01SxgYvH1iLUimhr8gRsNm2Z · secondary · since 2026-10-01
**Read** · handled UX-DESIGN U4

## B1 · NOTICE · 2026-10-01 · session_01SxgYvH1iLUimhr8gRsNm2Z · secondary
K904 (Bob): a project's stage is computed; only forming and closed are written.
Folded: record-grammar R35, promotion R56 (T21).

Entry numbers carry the writer's letter (B for BOB, U for UX-DESIGN) so a reference such as re U3 is unambiguous. To send: append to your own outbox and push. To receive: fetch the other side's mail branch, read every entry past your cursor in order, act on each, then move your cursor. Never write the other side's file. Never put a secret in an entry.

3

When each side reads

There is no doorbell across accounts, so each side reads its correspondents' outboxes:

  1. at takeover;
  2. at every backstop check it already runs (BOB's every 20 minutes while a layer runs);
  3. before recording any ruling of yours, DEC or K, because a ruling on one side may settle or change a question on the other;
  4. whenever you say "check the channel".

A session with send_later or a scheduled check-in may also look on its own schedule. No session ever fires a standing routine to reach anyone (§13 already forbids it).

5

Ownership, so neither side collides with the other

  • The design session never writes build/ (rulings, plans, requirements, next.md), save its own registry row through its PR. BOB never edits docs/development/ux-substrate/ or docs/development/DECISIONS.md, and keeps the design session's text in any merge conflict (K945).
  • DEC numbers are the design session's. It takes the next free number across main and its own open branch and posts a NOTICE for each. If main gains the same number first, the branch renumbers (K473's practice).
  • K numbers are BOB's alone.
  • When you rule on a UX question through BOB, BOB posts a NOTICE, so the design session cites the ruling instead of re-asking. K899 (1), K899 (7) and K903 (4) settled questions 32, part of 31, and 23 this way; the design session found them only by reading build/rulings.md.
  • A DEC's owed: work reaches BOB as a HANDOFF; BOB folds it into requirements (P5) once the DEC is on main or you name it, and answers with the K that folds it.
6

Tooling and check

tools/mail.mjs gains five commands, beside the job commands it has:

node tools/mail.mjs xpost  --as BOB --kind NOTICE [--re U3] --body-file f   # append to my outbox, push
node tools/mail.mjs xinbox --as BOB --from UX-DESIGN                       # entries past my cursor
node tools/mail.mjs xdone  --as BOB --from UX-DESIGN --through U4          # move my cursor
node tools/mail.mjs xcheck --as BOB                                         # who has unread entries for me
node tools/mail.mjs xwriter --as BOB --session <id> --account secondary  # the Writer line, at takeover

The design session runs the same tool from its own clone of civicos-process (read access is enough). A fifth check joins checks/run.mjs, channels: over each mail branch's history, an entry once pushed is never rewritten or removed, numbers rise by one, and a cursor never moves backwards (P15). It is added with your approval, as §8 requires for a new check.
