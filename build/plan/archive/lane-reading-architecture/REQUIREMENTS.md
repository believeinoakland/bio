## 0. How to read this

**Ids.** `RR-<area><n>` a consolidated requirement (areas A to M, §2); `CF-<n>` a conflict (§4); `GP-<n>` a gap (§5); one numbering each across the file. The consolidations' own `RQ-`/`MECH-`/`CF-`/`GP-` ids collided between files and are not used. ## 1. The governing rules (Bob's rulings on reading)

1. **Never silently in part (K4571).** "CS should never set a member's expectation that documents are fully read but just scan those documents that are larger than some metric." A large text file is "answered at once and read in the background in pieces until every line can be searched and cited" (option B). A bound that must stay (a platform limit) is stated.
2. **Within its bound, read whole in bounded memory; only above it, refused with its size (K4630).** "Failures like that shouldn't be failures." K4785: "ALWAYS operate correctly within the bounds of the system requirements", not only in test cases.
3. **The file standard (K4628 point 3, option B; agreed K4632, N1267).** Documents, scans, PDFs, plain text 100 MB or 5,000 pages; spreadsheets 50 MB or about 1M rows; images 50 MB; archives 500 MB total, each file within its kind's bound. Above its bound refused in plain words with its size, "never half-read" (N1267); "a maximum file size for each type" is communicated. Stretch: "any file that would realistically be of value in an investigation" (N1269).
4. **Types (K4628 point 1, N1266, N1270).** PDF text and scanned; .docx/.doc; .xlsx/.xls; CSV/TSV; PowerPoint; the OpenDocument trio; plain text; RTF; HTML; JSON; XML; images JPEG/PNG/TIFF/HEIC/GIF/WebP by OCR; .eml/.msg with attachments; ZIP (each file read). Audio, video, forums: "the next release after 1.0". The list "will be closely studied before finalizing" (K4632).
5. **Adding is not interpreting (K4632, K4636, K1468).** "The act of adding a document to the record does not require that every line/cell/slide be interpreted immediately." "All documents be read and searchable, though we design a means whereby not all content is interpreted" (K4636, "for now"). Extraction is targeted "as it relates to a basis or claim" (K1468). What is interpreted, and for which investigation, is stated on the document page (K4636).
6. **Speed and background (K4354, K4562).** Every screen and act within 2 s; web capture under 15 s. A larger capture is answered at once and fetched in the background "as long as the UX leaves the member understanding what's happening, why, and informed when the download completes". "The load of the copy shouldn't cause an error" (K4787).
7. **The scheduler heals itself unseen; its watchdog only checks (K4563, K4565, K4566).** "This is something that must work without human awareness or intervention"; "when one happens, we need to identify it as quickly as reasonably possible"; the Worker alarm "is a watchdog timer ... efficient - but mission-critical bulletproof".
8. **Understand, then test at the platform's shape, before deploying (K4426, K4428, K4785, K4798).** The PDF cause-family review extends to the other major types (SCAN #1, SHEET #1, DOCS #1).
9. **Reaching Bob's figures is engineering (K4803, K4809):** brought back "only with measured evidence that the platform cannot". Tokens are not spared (K4782).
10. **Protective limits (K1881).** BOB may set "size and count caps on downloads and archive unpacking"; "a limit only refuses or slows abuse; one that would change what a member may do ... is still Bob's".
11. **Storage (K4633, K4635, K4638, K4746, N1271).** The administrator sees a gauge; "teams should be aware that the realistic size of their record is affected by several real world factors" (K4635, "A now and perhaps B for the beta"). Upload size is a per-service-level figure (K4746), confirmed by measurement (N1274).
12. **The lane (K4812).** Decides nothing; the G91 to G95 fixes are held (K4812, K4814); the final pass waits for the confirmed architecture (K4812 (4)). Member words are the design stream's (DEC-98: "Nothing here", "Still loading", "Could not read this, because…", "Undetermined, because…"; every wait says what, from whom and by when).

### 1.1 The two stages that "interpret" names

| | **READ** (whole) | **INTERPRET** (selectively) |
|---|---|---|
| Bob's words | K4628 point 2, glossed by BOB and agreed: "interpret" = read whole, searchable to the last line, citable at page/row/cell, structure kept, scans OCR'd, the unreadable stated | K4632 "does not require that every line/cell/slide be interpreted immediately"; K4636 "not all content is interpreted"; K1468 |
| What | Text and structure of the whole file, searchable and citable | Typed content (typed cells, figures, dates, people, events, entities) chosen for the investigation |
| When | At intake, always, whole (K4571, K4630, K4636) | On request or when a range is opened or cited; "Further extraction ... can be ongoing" (K1468) |
| Stated where | The reading's state (being read, partly read and why, finished, refused with its bound) | On the document page (K4636) |
| Machinery | reading pipeline, index segments, `reading` cursor | `cellsOf(sha, sheet, {fromRow,toRow})` (K4636 re-plan, K4661); beyond a range opened or cited = N1272 (open) |

(a) "Read whole" is never traded for "not all interpreted" (K4636 holds both). (b) K4628 point 2 and the charter's "interpret" mean READ. (c) A later investigation must be able to find data in a held document not yet interpreted (K4632; N1272; GP-36). (d) N1269 ("interpreting only those sections ... of interest") is consistent if it means INTERPRET, and contradicts K4636 if it lets the text be indexed in part (CF-41). Above both sits meaning and entity resolution (`Fw §3`, `§14.2`); AI extraction yields labelled proposals only (`EXB §4`).
