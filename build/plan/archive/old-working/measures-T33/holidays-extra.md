## Doubts
- No primary Judicial Council list or archived CCP s.135 for 2018/2020 retrieved: web.archive.org is blocked by the fetch tool and rate-limited (HTTP 429) in Bash. 2018/2020 lists come from a Superior Court (San Diego) plus a calendar PDF, not courts.ca.gov. Verify against archived courts.ca.gov/holidays.htm.
- Columbus Day appears in both court lists, but current CCP s.135 text (S1) excludes the second Monday in October as a judicial holiday. Unresolved; the 2018/2020 statute text was not seen. Treat Columbus Day as uncertain for the statutory (CCP 135) definition.
- The 2018 Mar 30 and 2020 Jul 3 Friday shifts rest on S1 allowing Judicial Council alternative days; per-year Judicial Council order not seen.
- OPM page returned Access Denied to curl; the table is the WebFetch summary of that URL, not a verbatim copy. It matches the statute's rules for 2026.
