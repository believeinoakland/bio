WHY YOU ARE ASKED
A published case is trusted only if readers know of anything that could pull the people behind it one way. Saying so openly protects you and the group.
When a case your group publishes concerns one of your ties, the case says so, in the way you choose below.
Before the group publishes, each member who signs confirms they have no tie they have not listed here.
Only what could matter: an employer, a relative in a public body, a business you have an interest in. Not your opinions, politics or private life.
WHO SEES IT
Inside the group: only you and the group's administrators. Other members never see this list.
Outside the group: no one, unless a published case concerns the tie. Then its readers see the tie, with or without your handle, as you chose.
It is kept with your group's records, so a court order could reach it like the rest of them.
