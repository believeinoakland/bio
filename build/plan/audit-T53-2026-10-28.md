# Audit of T53 merged jobs, by model (bio ruling K5793)

Auditor: independent opus session, 2026-10-28. Copy audited: `audit-bio` at `origin/tranche/T53` = e5a5c870 (history deepened from GitHub into the copy only; job branches fetched from GitHub into the copy only). Nothing was written to /home/user/bio or /home/user/civicos-process; no push, no `proc`, no session messaged. Two probe tests were run in the copy and deleted.

Models were read with `get_session` for 52 of the 79 T53 job sessions. In every one, `configured_model` equals `external_metadata.last_served_model` (no fallbacks seen).

## Method

For each sampled job I read: its START (`build/plan/starts-T53/<m>.txt`), its plan rows (`build/plan/T53-R1/<m>.md` or the layer archives), its whole record, the requirement lines it claims Met (at the merge commit), and the whole diff of every merge commit `K<n>: merged <m>` against its first parent, excluding generated files (`**/dist/**`, `setup-words/rows.mjs`, `words-unreviewed.json`, `case-checker/program.mjs`). For the three opus jobs, which built whole modules, I read the merged source files whole and the test names (not every test body of register-readers and mail-reader). Lines below are insertions plus deletions on those files.

Severity classes: **B** wrong behaviour; **T** weak or wrong test; **U** unapplied entry or requirement clause; **F** false report claim; **P** process rule broken (counted apart). "Minor" means the defect does not change product behaviour.

## What this sample cannot show

- The model is confounded with the task. Haiku got test-only conversions, sonnet behaviour changes, and opus whole new modules. Rates per job or per line compare different work, not the models on the same work.
- Three opus jobs is too few for a rate. The two largest opus jobs were skipped as too large to read whole.
- Effort level per session is not recorded in what I could read.
- The defects are the ones one reader found. For the opus modules I read all the source but not every test body. I ran only a few suites and probes, so defects that need integration, load or a deployed copy are not counted.
- Record states were taken from the merges and job branches. A later BOB fix (for example the baseline lowered at K5659) can make a false claim harmless without making it true.
- Nothing here measures what a defect costs downstream. A false report claim and a privacy leak are counted alike within their class.
