## How `/` and the old UI are served today

- `GET /` (no `op`) and `GET /app`, `/app/<screen>/<subject>`, `/app/assets/<name>`: `interfacePage(APP_SCREENS)` through the namespace and address gates, `no-store` (`control-plane/index.mjs:699-706`, `control-plane/app.mjs`). Unknown addresses draw the public `unavailable` page with 404 (`app.mjs`, R104). `/doorbell` and `/tour` draw their screens.
- `GET /sign`: `SIGN_HTML` (`control-plane/index.mjs:693-694`, imported at `:62`).
- `GET /version`, `/favicon.ico`: plain/204. `/api/?op=...`: the op door.
- Nothing in `bio-plane/src` serves `civicos-ui/app.html`. The only route to it is the separate Worker `civicos` (`civicos-ui/worker.template.mjs:5-22`: `/`, `/civicos` -> `app.html`; `/build`; `/api/*` -> `https://biosmoke7.believeinoakland.workers.dev` with the `Authorization` header), deployed by `civicos-ui/deploy-ui.mjs` (script name `civicos`, binding `PLANE -> biosmoke7`, `keep_bindings: ["secret_text"]`).

## Method and limits

`app.html` was read in full as a comment-stripped copy (line numbers preserved; 13,508 lines from `<body>` at 853 to the end; the CSS at 58-851 and the SVG sprite were not read as features; lines longer than 400 characters were cut at 400). Lines are `civicos-ui/app.html` unless a path is given. The inlined docprofile/doctype copies (about 3560-6850) were read and are not user surfaces. New-side claims come from reading and grepping `build/modules.json`, `bio-plane/src/wizard-scripts/screen-registry.mjs` (generated from `docs/development/ux-substrate/screens/registry.json`, 71 screens), the nine `screens-*` modules, `interface-shell` and the design mocks; "grep = 0" means no hit in the named files. "Drawn" means the screen is in the registry and its mock in `screens/mock-screens.js`. Not run: any test, build or browser. `build/plan/legacy-inventory.tsv` classifies 150 `civicos-ui/test` files from an older census; only 36 entries exist now.
