# Process flaws found by BOB #336, 30 Oct 2026, for the neoProcess lane (GOV 5)

Filing them by `proc issue` was refused: this session's GitHub scope is `civicsmith/civicsmith` only (R-BOB-336-1), and attaching `neobobkrause/neoprocess` was refused by the auto-mode classifier (R-BOB-336-2, not in the act table). So they are written here and sent to ROOT. Each is one issue.

## 1 · readingSet measures more than BLD 1's set: every module of layers 5-11 is refused at layer start

`readingSet` (`tools/proc.mjs:873-884`) counts, against `MODULE_SET` 40 KB (`:826`, the refusal at `:926`):
- the whole `build/layers.md` (26,803 bytes here), and
- every used module's whole Public part.

BLD 1 and SESSIONS §3 name "its layer's contract" and "of each used module, the services its entries touch". Measured on `tranche` at 91e2a6c2, all 127 modules of layers 5-11 are over. The smallest is interface-assets at 41,699 bytes; the largest are plane at 4,889,242 and control-plane at 4,481,172. So `proc layer start` starts nothing on this product.

The product's own part, mine to fix (REQ 8): 60 of those 128 modules are over 40 KB on their own requirements file plus their layer's section. Overall, 78 of the 190 requirements files are over 40 KB alone (median 36,267, max interface-shell 140,326).

**Needed:** the tool measures the rule's set. Entries cannot yet name the services they touch, so the lane decides how they are named or measured.

## 2 · `proc tranche carry` fails at its last step when the checkout holds the old `tranche/<T>` tracking refs

After the prune runs deleted `tranche/T50`…`T53` on the remote, the re-run committed and pushed `tranche` (436f6aa). Then `git branch --set-upstream-to origin/tranche` failed (exit 128). Its fetch (`R.fetch`, `proc.mjs:78`) cannot create `refs/remotes/origin/tranche` while `refs/remotes/origin/tranche/T50` and the others still exist locally ("cannot lock ref … 'refs/remotes/origin/tranche/T50' exists"). The fetch error is swallowed, so the command ends in a stack trace and never prints its `carried` result, including its `next` and `oldSessions`.

I deleted the five stale local tracking refs by hand (`git update-ref -d`; nothing on the remote), fetched, and set the upstream. A re-run of the carry would not be safe: it would rebuild `tranche` from `carried/T53` and push a non-fast-forward.

**Needed:** the carry deletes the local `refs/remotes/origin/tranche/*` it pruned before it fetches `tranche`.

## 3 · `proc round close` makes `next.md` current, but here `next.md` is the next tranche's plan and the completion round has no file the tool opens

On this product, `build/plan/next.md` is T54's entry list (K5747). T53's completion round (PLN 7, the tranche's last round) is in `build/plan/T53-completion.md`. `roundClose` (`proc.mjs:1527-1542`) writes `next.md` as the new `current.md` whenever it exists. So closing T53's layers 5-11 round would open T54's list inside T53, and the completion round would never open. A plan of layers 5-11 plus the completion round together is over `PLAN_LIMIT` (51,200).

**Needed:** a home the tool reads for a tranche's completion round (or a round-close option naming the next round's file), distinct from the next tranche's `next.md`.

## 4 · `proc takeover` printed no reading set

The process text says the takeover "prints the rest of your set". On the first takeover here (no predecessor), it printed only `{commit, previous: null, queue: [], working: [], acts: [], check}`. I read the set from SESSIONS §3 by hand: the manifest, the area index (empty), the lane register (empty), no channel named.

**Needed:** the takeover prints the set, or the process text stops saying it does.

## 5 · `add_repo` of the process repository is not in the act table

R-BOB-336-2. The act table's `issue_write(process issue)` row needs "the process repository readable by the session's GitHub identity". A product session is scoped to its product only, so the route needs either the repository attached at the session's creation (ROOT's `create_session` sources) or another carrier.

## 6 · `archivedTranche` reads the last file under `build/plan/archive`, not the last archived plan

`proc refused routed` between rounds refused with "no running plan and no archived one". Here, `archive/T53-R1.md` exists, but the last file listed under `build/plan/archive` is `words-check/words-report.txt`. `archivedTranche` (`proc.mjs:1524`) takes `.pop()` of every file and then matches `<T>-R<k>.md`, so it finds none. The refusal's own text suggests `--tranche <T>`, but `refusedRouted` does not read that option.

**Needed:** take the last name matching `<T>-R<k>.md`, and read `--tranche` where the refusal names it.

## 1, continued · what faithful trimming achieves (measured 30 Oct)

Two pilot rewrites, wording only, every id, mark, code and number kept, each by a worker on Opus:
- events: 55,721 to 34,731 bytes (−38 %);
- registers: 52,177 to 41,298 bytes (−21 %).
What remains is interface text (names, fields, codes), the required line start of each id, and the conditions themselves.

So, of the 71 round-2 modules over 30 KB on their own requirements (4.46 MB in all; sizes in this file's flaw 1), most cannot fit a 40 KB set by trimming. They would have to be split (REQ 8, copy then delete, each new module with its own job). On this product that roughly doubles the job count in layers 6 to 11, and the new modules enter `modules.json`.

That is a large change driven by the 40 KB budget (SESSIONS §6.3) and by how the set is measured, which flaw 1 asks the lane to settle. I am holding the splits until the lane says what a job's set is and what its limit is. Meanwhile the two trims are being checked for meaning and, if clean, committed: they shorten text without loss, whatever the limit.
