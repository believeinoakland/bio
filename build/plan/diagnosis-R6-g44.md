## Findings in one page

1. **Both defects reproduce at the real shape, and the fault is wider than the two `text()` calls.** A 10 MiB-declared short-row workbook (73,792 rows x 3 cells = 221,376 cells, a 1,437,681 B file, the shape OFFICE-READERS #15 measured) kills the real `addWorkbook` and the real `safeView` at the object's cap (both die; section 2). Both survive at 7.5 MiB (166,380 cells) and die at 10.
2. **`addWorkbook` has a second, earlier wall: the workbook it holds cannot be read back.** `readWorkbook`, `lint` and `inputsOf` each load every stored cell into JS objects (`workbooks/index.mjs:169-172`, callers :365, :389, :420, :431, :524). At the real-shape cap they die at about 100,000 cells (4.5 MiB declared survives, 5 MiB dies; with two formulas a row, 60,000 cells survive and 99,000 die). So a bound that only fixed the add (making it list cells in windows) would let a member add a workbook the record can then never open. The bound belongs on the add, and is about 50,000 cells (section 4).
3. **The safe view draws every cell of every sheet, with no paging** (`screens-intake/open-file.mjs:57-58`), and the answer for the 10 MiB workbook is 10.9 MB of JSON. The bounded form (first rows per sheet by `cellsIn`, with a stated cut) answers 45 KB and peaks 46-51 MB above idle for a 10 or 20 MiB workbook (the object leaves about 78).
4. **The safe view is also dead for a CSV of 4 MB or more and for an ODS of about 20 MiB declared**, through the same function (`file-safety/formats.mjs:108-119`); the CSV entry has the opt-in cap and `cellsIn`, the ODS entry has neither.
5. **A silent empty view, found on the way (K4571).** A workbook or CSV above the entry's whole-read bound is answered by `safeView` as `ok: true` with `cells: null` (xlsx) or no sheets (CSV, ODS), which the screen draws as an empty table or nothing. `addWorkbook` refuses it as `NOT_A_WORKBOOK` with "no cells were read", not stating the size (K4630). Section 1.
6. **Owners:** workbooks (76), file-safety (51), screens-intake (164, the drawn sentence), plane (171, the harness), odf-reader (17, only if ODS is to be bounded by the entry). **No new `uses` edge** is needed (section 6). The bound for `addWorkbook` and the first-screen size are BOB's technical choices reported to Bob (K1881); the new drawn sentence and any "show more" control are UX, Bob's by P17 (B491 pattern, as G41's `cells_held` sentence).

## 1. Cause, per caller

### 1.1 addWorkbook (module `workbooks`)

- **Who calls it.** Op `workbookadd` (`workbooks/ops.mjs:23`; the screen act is `addworkbook`, declared `ops.mjs:47`, op map `op-declarations/index.mjs:104`; the Calculation screen offers it, `screens-investigate/calculation.mjs:92, 128`). It runs in the Durable Object: `workbooksOf` is composed in `plane/store.mjs:242`.
- **What it does.** `bytes = await this.bytesOf(sha)` (:185), with **no size test before it**: `#evidenceBytes` (:110-115) loads the whole capture (`arrayBuffer()` into a `Uint8Array`), whatever its size; then `xlsxEntry.text(bytes)` with no cap (:188), which lists every typed cell of every sheet (`s.cells`); then the cheap refusals (:189-198, `NO_QUESTION`, `NO_PERIOD`, `NO_PROJECT`) and the `already` repeat check (:199) **after** the full read, so a refused or repeated add pays the whole read each time; then `xlsxEntry.structure(bytes)` (:205, a second parse); then, in one `record.transact` (:221), **one `INSERT OR IGNORE` per cell** into `workbook_cells` (:223-230; table `workbooks/schema.mjs:23-34`, columns `sheet, cell, r, c, type, value, formula, cached`).
- **The SQL value bound (`record-core/schema.mjs:33-35`, 2,098,176 B) is not what fails.** Every row is a single cell (measured largest `value` 7 B in the fixture; Excel caps a cell at 32,767 characters). The unreproduced corner is one hostile cell value above about 2.1 MB (insert :226-229): it would throw `SQLITE_TOOBIG` inside `transact` out of `addWorkbook`; `platform-sqlite.mjs` (TEST-SUPPORT #9) is not merged, so it was not run.
- **What it truly needs.** The cells, stored: R1 says the workbook is "held" and R2/R3/R5/R9/R12 read the stored cells (`workbooks.md` R1-R12). `bind` and `#compare` read by SQL range (:317), but `readWorkbook`, `inputsOf`, `lint`, `recompute` (:389 `pair(answer, this.#cells(wb))`) and `explainLint` load **all** cells and index them (`indexWorkbook`, `workbooks/cells.mjs`). So the module is built on "every cell of the workbook is in the table and in memory when it is read". It does not need a cell the reading-pipeline interprets for a range; it needs its own copy of the whole grid, which is why `cellsIn` cannot simply replace the call: the cost moves from the add to every later read.
- **Existing bounds that apply.** None at the add, other than the entry's: the xlsx entry lists no cells above 20 MiB declared text (`ooxml.mjs:114`, `MEASURED_OOXML_TEXT_BOUND_BYTES`), which is why a 26.2 MB-declared workbook is refused today (`NOT_A_WORKBOOK`, why "no cells were read"; measured) but without its size. The recompute member states its own bound with the measured value: `sheet-worker/src/contract.mjs:33-38`, 9,000,000 B unzipped and 250,000 cells, `OVER_BOUND` (`member.mjs:44-51`), "above a bound is refused, never partly recomputed" (`sheet-worker.md` R14). The plane cannot recompute a workbook it could not read in the first place: 250,000 cells is past what `readWorkbook` survives (finding 2).

### 1.2 The safe view (module `file-safety`)

- **Where it runs: in the Durable Object**, not the browser and not a container. `fileSafetyOf` is composed in `plane/store.mjs:438`; the op `safeview` is in `file-safety/index.mjs:2003` (`safeview: () => fs.safeView(...)`), its JSON answer is returned by the store door (`store-door/dispatch.mjs:411-418`, a `Response` unwrapped) to the Worker and then to the browser. The renderer container is used only for the PDF and office-document routes (`index.mjs:933-945`); the data route (`xlsx`, `ods`, `csv`: `file-safety/formats.mjs:26-28`, `ROUTE`) is made in the object. So the 128 MB object bound applies, with the plane idle (diagnosis-R6-g40.md finding 1: D = 39, cap `128 + 39 - N` for a file of N MB).
- **The path.** `safeView` (`index.mjs:895`) -> data route (:922-928): `whole = look.whole ? look.bytes : await this.#bytes(sha)` (`#bytes` :367-382 refuses above `GRADE_READ_MAX` = 20 MiB, :69, so the file is held whole up to 20 MiB), then `sheetsOf(format, whole)` (`formats.mjs:108-119`) whose line **:112 `const t = await entry.text(bytes)`** has no cap; it maps every cell to `{ref, value, type}` (:114-117) and the whole list is returned as `sheets: s.sheets` (:927).
- **What the first screen draws.** `sendSafeView` (`screens-intake/open-file.mjs:157-172`) stores the whole answer (`cs.set("safe", a)`); `safeDrawn` (:57-58) draws one `<table>` per sheet with **a row per cell**, all of them. There is no paging, no cut, no row range; the only cut the module knows is the plain-text view's (`SAFE_VIEW_TEXT_MAX`, R45, `open-file.mjs:52-56`, `screen.open-file.safetext.cut`). The 10 MiB workbook's answer is 10,865,889 B of JSON and 221,376 table rows to draw.
- **What it truly needs:** the sheets' names and the first screenful of cells, with the cut stated; R11 says "spreadsheets ... `sheets:[{name, cells:[{ref, value, type}]}]`, the cached values the readers read" (`file-safety.md` R11) and R51 already says "the spreadsheet cells load no capture above `GRADE_READ_MAX`". R45 is the precedent for a stated cut of a safe view.
- **Silent empty views (K4571), measured.** `sheetsOf` returns `ok: true` for an entry whose `text()` answered `ok: true` with `cells: null` (an xlsx above 20 MiB declared: `cells: null` -> drawn as a header and an empty table, `open-file.mjs:58`'s `Array.isArray(sh.cells) ? ... : []`) or with `sheets: []` and the size only in `undetermined` (a 20,971,571 B CSV: `over_size_bound`, size and bound named in `undetermined`, dropped by `sheetsOf`; a 20 MiB+ ODS). None states the size.

## 4. Options, per caller, with a recommendation

**Ruling frame.** K4630: a file within its per-kind bound is read whole in bounded memory; only a file above its bound is refused, stated with its size. K4571: never a silent partial read. K4636: documents read whole and searchable; cells interpreted selectively, a range when opened or cited. K1881: a protective bound is BOB's, reported to Bob. Neither caller reads the document for search: reading-pipeline already does that whole and searchable (G41). These two are *views of cells*, so K4636's "a range when opened" applies directly.

### 4.1 addWorkbook

- **A (recommended). A stated workbook bound, enforced by the entry's own opt-in cap.** Pass `{inlineCellsMax: WORKBOOK_CELLS_MAX_BYTES}` at :188. A workbook whose cells' JSON is under the bound is listed exactly as today and stored exactly as today; one over it comes back with `cells: null, cells_held: true` and `counts.cells`, and `addWorkbook` refuses it as `NOT_A_WORKBOOK`-class `OVER_BOUND` **stating its cell count and the bound** (as `sheet-worker` states `OVER_BOUND` with measure and bound). Memory is bounded by construction (the cap is bytes of cells listed; the capped read peaks +37-55 MB at 10-20 MiB, measured 2.3). Also gate on the capture's size before :185 (the capture's head size from the evidence store, as `file-safety` `#sizeOf` does, :350-359) so a 100 MB capture is never loaded whole by `#evidenceBytes` (:110-115), and move the cheap refusals and the `already` check before the read (:189-199 above :188). **The figure:** the reads, not the add, set it (section 2.2): they die at 17.9 MB of cell JSON (constants) and between 10.0 and 16.5 MB (formula-heavy). A bound of **8 MiB of cell JSON (about 50,000 cells at 161-167 B)** leaves a margin of about 1.2x on the formula-heavy case and 2.1x on constants; the job sets it by its own measure including `recompute`'s `pair(answer, cells)` (not measured here) and the `readWorkbook` answer (10 MB at 60,000 formula cells, section 2.2). It is a protective bound, reported to Bob (K1881). It stays inside R1's words (`NOT_A_WORKBOOK`: "over the size guard included") and moves no requirement's meaning; what the sentence says to the member is a drawn word. Risk the owner must size: `sheet-worker` accepts up to 250,000 cells, and the corpus the recompute was measured on (288 workbooks, 131 with formulas, 645,886 formula cells, `build/plan/archive/old-working/measures-T33/courts-workbooks.md:103`) may hold workbooks between 50,000 and 250,000 cells that are addable today and would be refused. The owner counts the corpus's cells per workbook first and reports the share.
- **B. Hold the workbook, interpret ranges (the G41 shape, K4636).** `bind`/`#compare` read by `cellsIn` windows, store nothing at the add. Not the smallest: lint's `short_range` / `cross_foot`, `inputsOf`, `recompute`'s pairing and the grade facts need the whole grid (or a streamed grid), so every read op is rewritten around windows (12 ops, `indexWorkbook`). It would lift the bound from about 50,000 cells to the entry's 20 MiB; it is a module redesign and a requirements change (R1/R2 "the workbook is held" and "the file's own cells"), so **Bob's**, brought with options. Recommended only if Bob wants workbooks above the bound addable; entered as an N-item, not this round.
- **C. Ingest in windows (`cellsIn`, 5,000-20,000 cells at a time) into the same table.** Fixes the add's memory (survives the entry's 20 MiB) but not `readWorkbook` / `lint` / `inputsOf` (section 2.2: die at about 100,000 cells), so it makes a workbook addable and unopenable. Rejected unless paired with B's read rewrite.

### 4.2 The safe view

- **A (recommended). A first screen by range, with the cut stated, in the module that owns it.** `sheetsOf` (`formats.mjs:108`): `entry.text(bytes, {inlineCellsMax: <file-safety's own figure, e.g. 512 KiB>})` for names, extents and hidden state, then `entry.cellsIn(bytes, {sheet, fromRow: 1, toRow: usedRows, maxCells: SAFE_VIEW_CELLS_MAX})` per sheet, and for a sheet whose cells came listed (small workbook) the same first `SAFE_VIEW_CELLS_MAX` of them; each sheet answers `{name, cells, rows: {from, to}, total_rows, truncated: true|false, next_row}` (`truncated` and the row range are the stated cut, as R45's text view states `truncated`). A small workbook answers exactly what it does today (`view.test.mjs:47-49` deepEqual holds with `truncated: false`). Measured (2.5): 46-51 MB above idle at 10 and 20 MiB, 45 KB answers; CSV the same at 4, 8, 17.7 MB. `SAFE_VIEW_CELLS_MAX` of 1,000 cells was the prototype; the draw is then 1,000 rows, not 221,376. `file-safety` defines its own figure (it cannot import reading-pipeline's constant: 51 < 54, no edge; the entry "takes whatever number it is given").
- **A2. The same plus a "show more" act** (`safeview` with `sheet` and `fromRow`, answering the next range by `next_row`). This is the paging K4636 describes ("a range when opened"). Adds an op argument (`op-declarations` field list) and a control on the screen: **UX, Bob's** (P17); the alpha does not need it if the cut is stated and the original can be opened (the safe view is a preview; `openoriginal` is the other act).
- **B. A stated refusal above a bound** (the safe view of a workbook over N cells is `SAFE_VIEW_FAILED` with its size). Smaller to write, but refuses files the reader reads well (K4630 would call it a read failed for a file within its bound); rejected.
- **ODS (and any entry without the cap).** `odsEntry` has neither slot, and the ODS safe view dies at 19.8 MiB declared (185,000 cells; 2.4). Options: (1) odf-reader adds `inlineCellsMax` and `cellsIn` as csv-reader and office-readers did (the clean route, owner odf-reader #17, not in G41's list: ODS "not proved this round"); (2) until then `sheetsOf` takes the first screen from a capped listing of the ODS read (the walk still makes every cell, so memory is not bounded) or states a refusal above a declared-size bound read from the package central directory. Recommendation: (1) as a job, with (2)'s stated refusal in file-safety meanwhile, the figure from odf-reader's measure.
- **Over-bound files must be stated, not empty** (all options): `sheetsOf` returns `ok: false` with the entry's `over_size_bound` size and bound for an entry that answers `undetermined` / `piecewise`, so the screen draws `safeRefused` (`open-file.mjs:164-171`) with the size, not an empty table. This is K4571/K4630 and needs no decision from Bob beyond the words.
