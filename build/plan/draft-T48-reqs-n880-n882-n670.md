- **filings R57** (T48; N880; amends R40) R40's "carries the sentence" is `filing-templates.rfcQuotingHeld` (its R33), asked at R6 on the approved text, so a template and a draft are judged by one reading. `RFC_QUOTING_MISSING`'s code, number, order and translation are unchanged. Control: a text that holds the key's words is approved, and one that does not is refused.
- **action-replies R10** (T48; N880; Actions D13; v0.2 rule 8, "unless it says otherwise") `actionReplyQuoting({target, ord, quotable, words?, author, viewer})` (`op=actionreplyquoting`): a member records, for a `received` entry of a `request_for_comment`, whether the reply says it may not be quoted (`quotable: false`), with the reply's own `words` that say so (at most 500 characters on one line), or records that statement withdrawn (`quotable: true`). Refusals in order, each writing nothing: `MACHINE_CANNOT_RECORD_QUOTING` (an empty or machine author); `NO_TARGET`; `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`; `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `BEARING_NOT_RFC` (another kind, or an entry that is not `received`); `QUOTING_REFUSED` (a `quotable` that is not a boolean, or `words` that are malformed). Records are appended and the latest stands. `bearingsOf` answers it per entry as `quotable` (`true` when nothing is recorded), with its words, who recorded it and when. It refuses no other act of any module and enters nothing published: the rule is a declaration (DEC-13). Control: `quotable: false` on a received reply lands; on a `sent` entry it is refused.
- **action-replies R11** (T48; N880; amends R3, R8) Each open-request row also answers `quotable`: `false` when R10's latest record for any received reply of the request says so, else `true`. Control: a request whose reply asks not to be quoted answers `quotable: false`, and one with no such record answers `true`.
- **case-inputs R18** (T48; N880; amends R8, R10, R17) A row may answer `quotable`; a value that is not a boolean makes the row malformed (R8's `undetermined`). For a row answering `quotable: false`, `openRequestLine` also answers `quoting: {key: "signing.openrequest.noquote", says}`, its words read by key from `words.json` and filled with `{whom}`. The words are the design stream's. Until the key exists, the job names the missing key and the row is marked not yet met. The line is shown once with the row's line, asks no reason and refuses nothing. Control: a row answering `quotable: true`, or not answering it, carries no `quoting`.

## 2. N882 (Actions D15, H19)

**Owners.** `filings` (L9, 126) offers the counsel packet. `following` (L10, 131) follows the proceeding.

**Does following a proceeding need more than `following`?** Yes, but no new module. Today `following` has no proceeding subject: its kinds are body, meeting, register, person-query, portal and policy. `events` R38 (`followedRegister`, already coded for `cpuc_proceeding`, `courtlistener_docket` and `ecourt_roa`) writes a register's rows "only for a proceeding a member follows", and nothing yet records that a member follows one. `actions` R65 already links an action to a proceeding. What is missing is the follow and one read that joins the proceeding's own dates with the group's dated items, so `following` gains a subject kind and two edges.

- **filings R58** (T48; N882; Actions D9, D15; v0.2 rule 13) A filing prepared at governing tier 2 also answers `offers: ["counsel_packet"]` beside `advisory` (R4): R8's packet, offered and never required. A Tier 1 filing answers no offer. No answer of this module says whether the group should take an action, becoming a party included; it lists, prepares and checks. Control: a Tier 1 draft answers no `offers`.
- **following R29** (T48; N882; Actions D15; v0.2 rule 17; U5) `followProceeding({proceeding, register?, ends?, author, viewer})` (`op=followproceeding`) records a member's follow of a proceeding, subject kind `proceeding`. Refusals in order, each writing nothing: `MACHINE_CANNOT_FOLLOW`; a proceeding absent or unseen `NO_SUCH_ENTITY` (one answer, `entities`' refusal); an entity of another kind `NOT_A_PROCEEDING`; a `register` that is not a public https locator `NO_LOCATOR`; R22's `WATCH_END_REFUSED`. On the tick (R12, R13) a `register` is read as R7 and R8 read one (an account-gated or fee-bearing register records `member_act_required`), each read a capture through `acquisition` attributed to the follow, and handed to `events.followedRegister({captureSha, proceeding, by})` (its R38), which writes the filing and order events. A follow with no `register` reads nothing on the tick and is listed with `unscheduled: "no_register"` (R14): its dates are the events members record. It is a watch for R22–R26 (kind `follow`), and R19's listeners are told. Control: a follow naming a register reads it once per cadence; one without is never read.
- **following R30** (T48; N882; Actions D15; v0.2 rules 6, 17; U5) `proceedingDates({proceeding, from?, to?, viewer})` (`op=proceedingdates`; a read) answers the proceeding's own dates and the group's dated items tied to it, in two lanes never interleaved. `proceeding` is its events as `events.eventsFor({entity: proceeding})` answers them (hearings, filings, orders, comment windows recorded as events), each with its `when` as held (precision, or undetermined with its bounds), its kind, its label and the captures it rests on, past and future, with the stage `events.proceedingStatusAt` answers today (undetermined with why). `group` is, for each action the viewer may see whose `proceeding` names it (`actions.actionsFor({proceeding})`, its R65), each pending clock entry with its date, basis and `owed_by` (`action-clocks` R18), so the group's own deadlines are shown as owed by the group, apart from a body's. Bounded as `events` R27 (at most 500 a lane, `truncated`). It answers no finding, states no lateness of anyone, and writes nothing. An absent or unseen proceeding answers as R29 refuses. Control: an action linked to another proceeding contributes nothing.

**Uses.** `following` gains `actions` (`actionsFor`, its R30, R65) and `action-clocks` (its R18 read), both L9 and earlier (131 > 121, 123). `entities` and `events` already exist.

## 3. N670 (DEC-127 (3), (6))

**(3) "Read in my language."** It belongs with the assistant's other drafts on the record, behind the plane's draft door (`plane` R40, R41: the door assembles from the asking member's own reads only, never wider than she sees, and handles keep-away, the account and the limit through `answers.askAccount`). Notes (`hypotheses`, its author's alone or shared), questions and findings (`inquiry`) and notices (`queue`'s items) are all earlier than `plane` (163). So no new module and no store is needed.

BOB's decision: nothing is held. A note's earlier text is "kept nowhere" (`hypotheses` R11, R14), and a held translation would outlive a deleted note and need its sight re-judged. Each reading is therefore answered and never stored, as R21's drafts are, on the asking member's own account (K1502). The labels around it ("Machine translation · original in …", the one tap back) are interface words, read from the group's held translations (`instance-setup` R74's `interfaceWords`). "Reading the group's held translations" is met there.

- **run-rules R33** (T48; N670; DEC-127 (3); amends R22) `DRAFT_KINDS` gains `reading`: the assistant's translation, for one member, of a passage of the group's own writing into that member's language. It is R21's mode in every other respect: interactive, no run, no run row, read-only, R17's bounds, started only at the asking member's own act (R18), deployed under `draft`'s own flag, its test-bar part `draft:reading`. Its reach (`DRAFT_REACH`) is `none`: only the passage the door hands in, so `draftMayRead` is `false` for it whatever the rest. Pure. Control: `draftMayRead({kind: "reading", suggestions: true})` is false.
- **skills R49** (T48; N670; DEC-127 (3), (4)) The `reading_translation` layer, in `disclosed` after `interface_translation` (R5): `sourcing` `authored`; `load_when` "a member asks to read a note, question, finding or notice in her language"; its body the clauses R21's normaliser finds in DEC-127 (3), (4) and `BIO_Interaction_Constructs_v0_1.md` §L: translate the passage whole and faithfully, adding, summarising or softening nothing; leave quotations, figures, dates, ids, citations and official local names as they stand; the translation is labelled machine work and never replaces the original; a signed or published record is never re-worded. `acts`: `readtranslate`, read from `published.catalog` by id as R39's are, with R39's stated absence when it is missing. No clause carries control-flow authority.
- **agent-worker R86** (T48; N670; `run-rules` R33; `skills` R49) `POST /draft` takes `task` `{op: "readtranslate", language, text, from_language?}`, sent by the plane's draft door. `language` is a BCP 47 tag; `text` is 1 to 20,000 characters. It takes `account` as R59 does and never a `grant` (`DRAFT_READ_NOT_ALLOWED` if one arrives). Refusals and answers follow R68–R70's form (`BAD_TASK` for a malformed task), instructed by `skills`' `reading_translation` layer only, offered no tool, with `text` reaching the model only in the user turn as data (R61, R62's fixtures: a passage carrying an instruction is translated as words). It answers `{ok: true, task, draft: {text, language}, label: {kind: "machine"}, usage, calls}`, stores nothing and makes no plane call.
- **plane R50** (T48; N670; DEC-127 (3); amends R40, R41) The draft door takes `op` `readtranslate` (`{subject: {kind, id}, language?}`, `kind` one of `note`, `question`, `finding`, `notice`), gated by R40 under `reading`'s flag. It reads the subject only as the asking member may: a note through `hypotheses`' read for that member (her own, or shared with her); a question's or finding's text through `inquiry` for that member; a notice's member-written text as `queue` answers that item to her. A subject she may not see answers `NO_SUCH_SUBJECT`, the same as an absent one, before any account is read. `language` defaults to her own (`instance-setup` R64), and with none set it is refused `LANGUAGE_NOT_SET`. It answers `{translation, language, label: "machine", original: {kind, id, text}}`, so the original is one tap away. It writes nothing, and keeps nothing but the usage `ai-use` counts to her account. With the assistant kept away or no account, her refusal is answered as `answers.askAccount` gives it, and the screen shows the original. Controls: a member reading another member's private note answers `NO_SUCH_SUBJECT`; her own note is translated.
- **op-declarations R64** (T48; N670) declares `readtranslate`: a member's session only (`machineClasses: []`), `by` and `viewer` stamped, the body `{subject, language?}`, no project named (it names a subject). **op-grades R38** grades it a read-weight member act on the phone; `affordances`' `ACT_HELP` gains its explanation from the design stream's `mock-acts.js`, or names it as lacking (T36-35's rule).

**(6) Offering a translation to Civicsmith's library.** The group's side can be built now. `reading-guides` R6 is the precedent: `guideProposeToCivicsmith` exports bytes marked as a proposal, "Civicsmith's adoption is a release's".

- **instance-setup R87** (T48; N670; DEC-127 (6); amends R75) `translationOffer({language, by})` (`op=translationoffer`): an administrator exports the group's `shown` translation of `language` as one canonical document marked as an offer to Civicsmith's library. It answers its bytes and SHA-256, labelled with the group's slug (`producingGroup`). It holds, per word, the key, the English it translated (so a reviewer sees an English that has since changed), the text, the protected mark and whether a protected word was confirmed. It holds no member id, handle, mark, note or draft. Refusals, each writing nothing: `MACHINE_CANNOT_TRANSLATE`; `NOT_AN_ADMIN` (`membership` R84); `LANGUAGE_MALFORMED`; `TRANSLATION_NOTHING_SHOWN`. Each export is recorded with who, when, the language and the digest, append-only. The instance transmits nothing: the administrator carries the file. R75's "no translation leaves the group's Civicsmith" now reads "except by this act". Control: a language with shown words exports, and the same bytes twice give the same digest.

**What does not exist.** There is no channel from a group to Civicsmith for library offers. The group can export, but nothing at Civicsmith receives, reviews or tracks an offer. The `stewards` Worker takes abuse reports only (v0.2 rule 21), the library ships only in releases, and no rule says who at Civicsmith reviews a language its team does not speak. Building a receiver is the open point below. Until it is decided, R87's export is the whole of what T48 can do. Guides have the same gap (`reading-guides` R6's `registerGuideChannel`, "BOB's").

**Uses.** None new: `plane` already uses `hypotheses`, `inquiry`, `queue`, `instance-setup` and `agent-worker`, and `instance-setup` already uses `membership` and `record-grammar`. If T48's `ai-accounts` split lands first, the account wording cites `ai-accounts` (its R7) in place of `credentials` R35.

## Open points for Bob

1. **Where a group's offered translation reaches Civicsmith, and who reviews it** (DEC-127 (6); money and how real groups behave). The group's side, an export the administrator carries, is BOB's and drafted above. The receiving end does not exist.
   - (a) **An address you name.** The offer is sent by hand to an address you name, as with the stewards' contact (K2782). Civicsmith's maintainers review it and ship it in a release, and a language the team does not read waits for a reviewer you name, such as a second group's granted speaker.
   - (b) **A receiving service.** A Civicsmith-run service accepts uploads, with a review queue. This costs operation and moderation, and touches rule 14 only in that the member uploads, never the instance.
   - (c) **Defer.** No offers until a group asks.
   - *Recommend (a).* It needs no new service, keeps "the instance transmits nothing", matches how guides are proposed, and can become (b) later.
