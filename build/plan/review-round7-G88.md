## Findings

### DEFECT 1 — the docprofile test as written breaks the layering and cannot run in that module
Plan (3), first bullet: "through the real entry `docprofile/registry.mjs` with the real eight types registered (as `r7-contention-entry.mjs` does)". `docprofile`'s `uses` is `jurisdictions, site-profiles, setup-words`; `doctypes` is position 22 and itself uses docprofile. `docprofile/test/stubs.mjs:7-9` says "doctypes' own types are a later module's and are never imported here". `seam.test.mjs:29-30` asserts the registry is empty in its own process. The test would import a later module from an earlier one.
Fix to the plan text: replace with "through `docprofile/registry.mjs` with the corpus stub types (`g48-readings.mjs` `registerCorpusTypes`, plus stub types whose `detect` is CERTAIN or LIKELY by a marker in the text), a text of 300 KiB (not 12 MB: the test is about order, not time), and every registered type's `detect` re-registered wrapped (same key, so the slot and the order are kept)". Move the "real eight types over a 12 MB text" assertion to the reading-pipeline test (`r7-classify-yield.test.mjs`). That module uses `doctypes` and can register them as `r7-contention-entry.mjs` does.

### DEFECT 2 — "same answer as `doctypeFor`" is stated but the plan's tests do not cover the places where a hand-copied loop would differ
The twin must re-implement `recognise` (`recogniser.mjs:54-66`: the `|| {match:false}` default, the rank comparison, `break` on CERTAIN, the not-matched answer) and `alsoOf`/`askAll` (`registry.mjs:107-129`). The two have different throw rules: `recognise` lets a throwing `detect` propagate; `askAll` catches it and records `{confidence:null, error}`. The five planned texts (minutes, agenda, regulation, none, one CERTAIN mid-list) do not cover: a best-by-rank winner that is LIKELY (so `alsoOf` returns early, :110); a `detect` throwing in the main walk (must still propagate, :54-66) and in the `also` walk (must be recorded); `NO_TYPE` when nothing is registered (`out.why`, :94-95); a fallback member skipped in `askAll`. Also the plan says "same arguments" but `ctx` is mutated nowhere; keep it so.
Fix to the plan text: (a) build the twin so drift is impossible by construction: one generator holding the walk (`function* walk(ctx)` yielding a marker before each `detect`), driven synchronously by `doctypeFor` and awaited-per-marker by `doctypeForYielding`. Then `doctypeFor` is still synchronous and the answer is shared code. If the job prefers a copy, (b) add the four cases above to the equality test. Whichever, say that `recognise` is `site-profiles`' engine and that the twin uses only its exported `CONFIDENCE`/`confidenceRank` (both exported), with no change to `site-profiles`.

### DEFECT 3 — the new contention assertion ("longest wait under 500 ms for the 12 MB text") may be unattainable as written
`r7-contention.test.mjs:5-9` itself says the 12 MB text "still shows a longest wait near 0.5 s in Miniflare, the R2 stand-in's own copy of the object included", and the test prints a control (`rawget`, lines 36-40) for exactly that reason. The remaining non-classification phases measured by the probe (decode 103, blank-line scan 66, glyph 114, flatten 60 ms) plus the stand-in's copy can put the longest wait at or above 500 ms with the classification perfectly sliced. A hard `< 500` on the max would be red or flaky for a reason unrelated to G88.
Fix to the plan text: assert `max(waits) < max(500, controlMax + 250)` (or `max(waits) - controlMax < 500`), printing both, and say that the acceptance is the five alone-runs of the L step, each under that bar. Alternatively assert the p95 as today plus "the longest wait is at least 25% below the pre-fix figure recorded in `fix-round-7-G88`'s evidence (631-806 ms)". Note that it can only be green after docprofile is merged AND the call site is changed, and that the red must be seen before the call-site change.

### GAP 1 — how the reading-pipeline test counts yields is not specified, and the "(same job, second step)" wording contradicts the job row
Plan line 11 says "(same job, second step)"; line 17 says a separate reading-pipeline job that cannot start before docprofile is merged. They are two jobs (one owner each, K4427). Also `read` imports its own `yieldNow` (`index.mjs:21`), so a test cannot inject a counting `yieldNow`; "counts the yields between the start of `classify` and its end (>= 7)" has no mechanism.
Fix to the plan text: "the reading-pipeline job (second job, started after docprofile merges) writes `r7-classify-yield.test.mjs`: it wraps every registered `detect` and wraps `globalThis.setTimeout` (the function `yieldNow` calls at each call) to push `'yield'` onto the same log; over a 12 MB text, `read` with `cursor: null`, it asserts that between every two consecutive `detect` entries of the log there is at least one `'yield'`, the first `'yield'` precedes the first `detect`, and the first tick's answer deep-equals a copy recorded on the tip before the change (`make-*-answers` pattern, as docprofile's G48 control)". The red state (zero yields between detects) is seen before the call-site change, so it needs no docprofile code, and goes green after the docprofile merge and the call-site change.

### GAP 2 — `classify()` is a memoised synchronous closure with five callers; making it await is not described
`classify` is used at :2423, :2436, :2681, :2682, :2711 and :2716. Only :2436 (the text-read-as-text branch in pieces mode) needs the yielding twin. The other callers are in failure paths, the entry's own text, where the intake text is empty or small, and they are in sync `failed(...)` expressions. Also R42 requires one classification per read, and "a read that is not in pieces is never suspended" (`breatheIf` comment, :2227) so the whole read must keep the synchronous call.
Fix to the plan text, reading-pipeline step: "add `classifyYielding()` used only at :2436 when `piecesOn` and `text.length > BREATHE_AT`; it awaits `doctypeForYielding(ctx, {yieldNow})` and stores its result in the same `classified` memo, so every later `classify()` returns the one answer (R42: one classification per read); not in pieces, :2436 keeps the sync call. Append the sentence to R42 (the classification requirement), not 'R42/R40'." Say which of R40/R42 the line is appended to (R42, `reading-pipeline.md:111`).

### GAP 3 — the yield inside the `also` pass after a CERTAIN winner is in the plan's words but not in its tests
`alsoOf` (:107-112) runs `askAll` over every member registered after a CERTAIN winner: the same long scans. The plan's R39 text says "before each type's `detect`" which covers it, but the only CERTAIN test (the answer equality) does not assert yields in that pass, so a twin that yields only in the main walk would pass.
Fix to the plan text: the interleaving assertion (a yield between every two `detect` entries, including those in the `also` pass) is made on a CERTAIN-winner text with several types registered after the winner, and on a no-match text.

### GAP 4 — a yielding hook that throws or rejects
The sibling G82 providers ignore it (`pdfstructure.mjs:4288` `tick`, `csv-reader/index.mjs:539-543` `letPass`: "one that throws or rejects is ignored (the open goes on, and its answer is the same)"). R39 as drafted is silent; a rejecting `yieldNow` would turn a classification into an exception.
Fix to the plan text: "a `yieldNow` that is not a function is no yield; one that throws or rejects is ignored and the answer is the same", with a test.

### GAP 5 — the residual per-detect stretch is already decidable at the admitted maximum, and the plan defers it as a question
`WHOLE_READ_MAX = 24 MiB` (`index.mjs:51`, text of one piece 20 MiB at :47). The plan's own figure (regulation 237 ms at 12 M chars, linear) gives about 490 ms at 24 MiB in node (measured here 233-286 ms per 12 M, so 490-590 ms), and workerd is about 1.2x slower than node (plan: 827 node vs about 1.0 s workerd; probe header: 1,014 ms), so about 600-700 ms: over the bar on regulation's `detect` alone, at a text size the read admits. The plan's "if it is over 24 MB, file the case to `doctypes`" is therefore already true at the limit, not conditional. Also the sum of two adjacent detects does not matter (a yield sits between every pair), but `identify` (77-97 ms here at 12 M, so about 200 ms at 24 MiB) is a separate stretch before the first yield, which is fine.
Fix to the plan text: replace the conditional with "the read admits a text up to `WHOLE_READ_MAX` = 24 MiB; regulation's `detect` alone is about 0.5-0.7 s there (it is windowed, `scanFlat`, but never yields); filed as a question to `doctypes` (its `detect` taking an optional async `yield`, or a sampled first pass) and BOB decides whether the residual is stated or built; this plan's bar is met for texts up to about 20 M characters". And state the 12 MB figure the L step must meet.

### GAP 6 — `identify` needs nothing, but say so; and the walker's text does not exercise a matched type's `parse`
`identify` is 77-97 ms (12 M) in node, so one stretch of about 100-250 ms up to 24 MiB, under the bar: no yield is needed (state it so the job does not add one). Add one matched case to the L step (a minutes-like 12 MB text) so the claim "longest wait under 500 ms" is shown for a text that a real type reads, not only for one nothing recognises.
