[envelope-docs-T51-s5.md 5.1: docgen.mjs, the ZIP writer, the truth file, the two oracles]
### 5.1 How it is made

**One generator, seeded, that knows its own truth** (the PDF plan's design, so one runner serves both). A new `docgen.mjs` in **test-support** (`bio-plane/test/` beside `make-zip.mjs` and `pdfgen.mjs`; BOB adds the path to `modules.json`). It writes through the **one** ZIP writer, `make-zip.mjs`, which already makes hostile and conforming ZIPs byte-exactly, plus `zipBomb` and `nestedZip`, and has two external oracles (`unzip -t`, Python `zipfile`) (H20). The six independent ZIP writers in module tests (office-readers `fixtures.mjs`/`large.mjs`, odf-reader `pkg.mjs`, ooxml `zip.mjs`, doc-clean, file-safety, `xlsx-gen.mjs`'s `zipChunks`) and the eighteen inline ones are moved onto it as each module's job touches its tests (P7: the module owner does it). Two things the shared writer lacks and must gain: **ZIP64** in the streaming writer (`zipChunks` has none; needed for archives over 4 GiB declared and 65,535+ entries) and a **lazy streaming mode** so a 100 MB or 500 MB file is never in the generator's memory (H20 ran: 500 MiB-declared ZIP 2.9 s, 100 MiB RTF 0.25 s, 5,000-slide PPTX 0.8 s, 10 MiB random-text DOCX 1.9 s: generation is cheap).

Its input is a spec of these axes:
- **kind** (text, html, docx, odt, pptx, odp, rtf, eml, doc/ole, zip, a served page);
- **size axes**: bytes, decoded characters, paragraphs/slides/cells/links/tables, entries, entry-name length, nesting depth, compression ratio, requests, DOM size;
- **content axes**: part kinds present (footnote, header, comment, deleted, notes, hidden slide, text box, image-only), unit length (one 3 MB line; 25-byte lines), line endings, the encoding (UTF-8, UTF-8 BOM, UTF-16LE/BE with BOM, Latin-1, Windows-1252, a bad byte), a charset declaration (header, `<meta>`, XML declaration), markup shape (nesting depth, unterminated tags, `<main>` present/absent, link-only lists);
- **package axes**: classic vs ZIP64, central directory in physical or shuffled order, a lying directory, an encrypted member, trailing garbage, macro-enabled flavour, a polyglot prefix;
- **served-page axes** (for the loopback origin of §5.2 path W): status, content type, `Content-Encoding`, redirects, a delay, a body that never settles, a client-rendered shell and its hydrated DOM;
- **a seed**.

Its output is the bytes (or the origin's script) plus a **truth file**: each unit's expected text with its part kind (every paragraph/slide/line carries `<seed>-u<n>-k<part>` and a word from a small vocabulary with accents, ligatures, CJK, RTL and a combining mark, so a unit is checkable alone); the first, middle and last markers; the units expected *unread* with their named reason; the **expected statement** for a refusal (its code and its figures: `TOO_LARGE {size, bound}`, `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT {kind}`, `ENCODING_UNSUPPORTED {name}`, `ENCRYPTED`, `STRUCTURE_OVER_BOUND {part, bound}`, `TOO_MANY_OBJECTS {n}`); the archive's expected member table (name, state, bytes, sha); the visitor view's expected fidelity statement. The generator is deterministic in its seed; large files are written once to `BIO_FIXTURE_CACHE` by spec hash and never committed. Real public files (the four government pages in `reading-pipeline/fixtures/`, plus N1268's fixed set once chosen) stay as they are, SHA-pinned.

**Two oracles, never the reader's own past output** (pdf-pixels R40's rule).
- The generator's truth, for every file.
- An independent reader: **LibreOffice** (`soffice --headless --convert-to txt`, present on the build machine and in the file-scanner image) for DOCX, ODT, PPTX, ODP, RTF, DOC; `unzip -t` and Python `zipfile` for every archive and package (as `make-zip` does); **Chromium** (`/opt/pw-browsers`, Playwright) for the visitor view's visible text and the HTML visible-text truth; ClamAV with EICAR for the scanner files. Where the oracle and the truth differ the generator is wrong and the file is fixed. Cases where the reader is *deliberately* stricter than LibreOffice (a tracked deletion is a unit with part kind `deleted`, not dropped) are listed in the truth file.

[envelope-scans-T51.md 5.1: imggen.mjs]
### 5.1 How it is made

**One generator, seeded, that knows its own truth.** `imggen.mjs` under **test-support** (layer 1, beside PDF #1's `pdfgen.mjs`; BOB adds the path), no dependency on the network:
- **Pages.** Text lines are rasterised in node from a built-in bitmap font at a stated line height, so the generator needs no font file and the truth (the words) is exact. Each line carries `<seed>-p<page>-l<line>` and a word from a small vocabulary. Degradations are seeded and independent: tilt (±10°), 90/180/270 turns, uneven light, noise, blur, inversion, low contrast, colour, two columns, a table.
- **Encoders.** PNG (grey, RGB, palette, alpha, 1/8/16-bit, interlaced or not) and BMP and GIF (LZW) and TIFF (uncompressed, PackBits, Flate, LZW, G4, JPEG-in-TIFF; single and multi-page; II and MM; predictor 2) are written by the generator. Baseline and progressive JPEG (4:4:4, 4:2:2, 4:2:0, grey, CMYK, EXIF orientation 1-8) come from the committed Pillow/libjpeg generator (`make-picture-fixtures.py`) with its hashes; WebP and HEIC come from the same generator where an encoder exists and from committed tiny fixtures where it does not (no HEIC encoder was available here). CCITT G4, JBIG2 generic and JPX scan pages inside PDFs are produced by PDF #1's generator with its independent decoders (libtiff, jbig2dec, OpenJPEG).
- **Hostile files.** Declared-size files are patched headers (IHDR, SOF, SIZ, JBIG2 page info, PDF `/Width /Height`) over a few bytes of data; bombs are streamed zlib; chunk-count files are generated as bytes.
- **Truth file.** For each file: the page count, each page's words and orientation, the pages expected unread and the named reason, the expected statement for a refusal, and the markers on the first, middle and last lines.

**Two oracles, never the reader's own past output.** (1) The generator's truth. (2) An independent decoder for pixels: Pillow, libjpeg-turbo, libtiff, jbig2dec, OpenJPEG (the repository's own references); the OCR text is judged against the truth by the floor of SI8, not against a stored OCR output.

## 3. The corpus (K4785 (3))

### 3.1 How it is made

**One generator, seeded, that knows its own truth.** The generator is a new `pdfgen.mjs` under **test-support** (layer 1, beside `pdf-worker/test/make-pdf.mjs` in its paths; BOB adds the path). It reuses `test/m/pdf-reader/pdf.mjs`'s and `indexed.mjs`'s object writers, `reading-pipeline/bigpdf.mjs`'s padding, and `pdf-worker/test/make-pdf.mjs`'s image pages.

Its input is a spec of these axes:
- pages; lines per page;
- page kinds (text, scan, mixed, folio, blank, vector);
- the font mix (ToUnicode identity; WinAnsi, MacRoman or Differences with no ToUnicode; Type0 Identity-H with and without ToUnicode; Type3; mixed codespace);
- the content filter chain;
- the structure (no xref, classic, xref stream, object streams, revisions with their rewrite mode, linearized, damaged offsets, truncation point, two catalogs, fake `obj`, untyped leaf);
- encryption (none, empty-password RC4 or AES, user password);
- image codecs (DCT, CCITT G4, JBIG2 generic, JPX, Flate raw), sizes and dpi; page size and rotation;
- target bytes; and a seed.

Its output is the bytes plus a **truth file**: each page's expected text (normalised per C4), the first, middle and last line markers, the pages expected unread with their reason, and the expected statement for a refusal.

Every text line carries `<seed>-p<page>-l<line>` and a word drawn from a small vocabulary with accents and ligatures, so a unit can be checked against its page alone. Scan pages are rendered by the generator from the same text, as a 1-bit or grey bitmap at the chosen dpi. Their truth is "OCR words ≥ 90 % of the page's words", the floor pdf-pixels R44 uses. The generator is deterministic in its seed; large files are written once to `BIO_FIXTURE_CACHE` by spec hash, never committed. Real external files (BUDGET-2024-APP) stay as today, fetched once, SHA-pinned.

**Two oracles, never the reader's own past output (R40 of pdf-pixels).**
- The generator's truth, for every file.
- An independent reader for the structure and font files: pdf.js through the committed pdf-worker bundle, already the tier-2 member. pdf.js must agree with the truth on the page count and on the text of the decodable pages. Where pdf.js and the truth differ, the generator is wrong and the file is fixed.
