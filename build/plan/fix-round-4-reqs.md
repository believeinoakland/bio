## RC-R1: `control-plane`, id R120 (new)
[control-plane]
- **R120** *(not yet met: T51)* (fix round 4, C5a/P8; K4429, K4354's 15 s bar; amends R105, R117; `acquisition` R96, `extraction` R80) **A capture is answered when it is kept; a reading that will not fit the bound follows.** `op=acquire` answers as soon as the capture is kept (`acquisition` R96's `keep` done) and its receipt is written, and never waits on the reading when the reading will not fit in what is left of the bound (R105's 25 s, less 8 s) or when the capture is a PDF over 2 MB. In that case the answer carries `reading_pending: true` and no `reading` or `text_units`, the capture is promoted for the member as any kept capture is, and the reading follows by `extraction.readHeld` (R117), started after the screen's promotion and, if the object is cut short, finished by the plane's alarm (`plane` R75). A capture whose reading fits is answered with its reading as before. The answer never carries empty `timings` on a capture that was kept: it states every stage that ran. Whichever way it goes the member's own act (`acquire`, `captureupload`) reaches an answer within R105's bound with the capture kept, or a named refusal. Test, through the door and the real objects with the real limits: a 2.5 MB, an 8.8 MB and a 15.9 MB PDF each answer within 15 s with the capture held and the reading either present or `reading_pending`, never `timings: {}`; `op=reading` by the digest answers `found: true` once the follow-on read ends; a 140 KB PDF still answers with its reading. Control: a small page is answered with its reading, no `reading_pending`; a refusal of the reading never turns a kept capture into a failed one (R117).

## RC-R2: `control-plane`, id R121 (new)
[control-plane]
- **R121** *(not yet met: T51)* (fix round 4, C5a, P9; K4429, K4428; amends R105, R115) **The door times each op and each phase of an acquire.** Every answer of the door carries a `Server-Timing` header with the whole milliseconds the door spent in that op (the op's name as the metric, and, where the op made calls to the object, the time of each); no header carries a credential, a name or an address of a member's. An `acquire` refused or timed out (502, 504, `ACQUIRE_TIMED_OUT`, `STORE_DID_NOT_ANSWER`, `CAPTURE_NOT_KEPT`) carries `timings.door`: `{acquire, reading, promote}`, each `{started_ms, elapsed_ms}` or `null` for a phase not reached, clocked in the door's own isolate so that it survives a stop or restart of the object, and `timings.stages` as R95 states them when the object answered them (`{}` only when the object answered none, and then `timings.door` is still present). The times change nothing a capture records or answers but the header and the `timings` key. Test: a call stopped in the reading states `timings.door.reading` as running and `acquire` as ended, with the object's stage record forced empty; an answered op carries `Server-Timing` naming the op. Control: an answer in time carries no `timings.door`; a read op carries a header and no body change.
