NO CODES TO HAND? ASK AN ADMINISTRATOR FOR A RESET LINK

An administrator can make you a reset link, like an invitation: it works once, for seven days, and lets you choose a new password. Ask an administrator in person or however your group talks. No administrator can see your password, and you keep your handle and everything you did.

IF YOU CANNOT SIGN IN

Your group's administrators can help you: ask one of them for a reset link.

If none of them can sign in either, whoever holds your group's hosting account can open the way back in for them.
