## 1. BOB's decisions (P17; technical, each reported to Bob as done)

- **D1 · One identity for a multipart capture (F3).** provenance owns it: at promotion the register also holds a row for the whole sha of a capture held in parts, marked `whole` (no file of its own; the parts' rows unchanged; bundle verification unchanged), so `homeOf(whole)` and every module that reads `register` by `capture_sha` resolve it. Old bundles: a repair at the plane's next wake back-fills the row from each bundle's `data/provenance.json` (R-OLD: a repair entry, not "new data only"). extraction R81's lookup stays (harmless) until a later touch.
- **D2 · One ledger of owed work, which never forgets (F1, F5, F9, F11).** plane's `keeps_pending` becomes the owed-work ledger, kinds `keep`, `reading`, `ocr` (pages left), `subresources`, `unpack`. An entry ends only when its work is done, or when its owner answers a final reason that is stated to the member on the document's page; never by a count or a clock. Retries back off to at most one hour and continue. `NO_HOME` never ends an entry. A second registration of a digest re-arms it (attempts 0, due now). An op `owedwork` lists the entries (for the member's document page and for BOB's diagnosis).
- **D3 · A reading follows every way a document enters the record (F1, F2, F6).** After `op=promote`, after `captureupload`, and after `inboxpull`, the door reads a document stored with no reading (inline if it fits the bound, else a `reading` ledger entry), and the answer carries `reading_pending`; the capture screen says the reading is on its way (stand-in by the nearest held row, K4269).
- **D4 · A failure is final only when its cause is (F4).** reading-pipeline marks each failed reading `transient` (store read error, a member down or answering 5xx, an entry throwing) or `final` (the document's own content); extraction's `readHeld` replaces a transient failed reading, and the ledger keeps it owed.
- **D5 · Caps defer or state, never drop (F7, F8, F9, F10).** The 512 KiB units budget binds the acquire answer only; `readHeld` and re-read index every unit within extraction's own index bound. OCR past 24 pages continues by the ledger (`ocr`, a page cursor) until every page is read. Office, ODF and CSV text over 20 MiB is read in bounded pieces. A whole too large for the object's memory (step 0 measures the real ceiling under workerd's 128 MB) is never loaded: the reading is recorded `final` with its size and the page says so (no crash loop, no silent retry).
- **D6 · Fail closed (F3a).** file-safety never serves a capture whose home it cannot find: `home` null is a refusal, never "no sight check". A security defect, first in its job.
