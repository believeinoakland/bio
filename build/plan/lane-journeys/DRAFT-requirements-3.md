## Not decided (Bob's)

1. **Who files a correction, a withdrawal and a court order, and from where?** (R-94.) Options: (a) the project owner, from the docket page; (b) any joined member for a correction, the owner for the others; (c) not from the page at all, and the page stops promising them. Recommendation: (a), since `docket` R12 gives the withdrawal to the manager, and the page's own promise says it can be done there.
2. **Is the edition entry on the docket made when an edition is published?** (R-96.) Options: (a) automatic, with the "what changed" text; (b) the owner files it; (c) the case page shows the change, the docket does not. Recommendation: (a), because readers look for a change in both places and the register shows neither draws it.
3. **In what order are an action's replies listed?** (R-119.) Options: (a) the day each arrived, then the order recorded; (b) the order recorded; (c) newest first. Recommendation: (a).
4. **Is a handled 4xx in the console a defect?** (R-130; also R-62, R-105.) Options: (a) a journey fails on any failed response; (b) a failure that the app asks for itself (the 400 for case drafts, the 404 for a revoked link) is a defect, and the step-up 403 is accepted; (c) none. Recommendation: (b).
5. **Is an outside reader's acknowledgement shown to the public by name, and does the reader know before she sends it?** (R-107.) Options: (a) shown by name, said before sending; (b) shown only if she ticks that it may be; (c) never public, the owner alone sees it. Recommendation: (b), because naming a person publicly should be her choice.
6. **Where does a signed case say what is ungraded?** (R-110.) Options: (a) the long paragraph at the top, as now; (b) one short line at the top with the paragraph in a fold; (c) by each finding only. Recommendation: (b).
