# JOURNEYS #4: brief for the 0.87.0 screen-by-screen walkers

**What.** Release 0.87.0 runs on https://biosmoke8.believeinoakland.workers.dev (`/version` reads `0.87.0`; a development copy, nothing it holds is secret). The alpha's A–E live walk (`build/plan/walk-T51b-live/A.md` … `E.md`, causes in `causes-*.md`, placed in `placement-*.md`) walked the *journeys*. This pass walks every *screen* of `docs/development/ux-substrate/screens/registry.json` that needs no assistant, against `lane-journeys/PLAN.md` §2 (the eight tests of "works"), to find what the journeys missed, and gives every defect (new or already known) its cause, its owner and how it was missed. Nothing is fixed; no product code, no `docs/`, no other lane's files are touched.

**Read first, whole:** this brief; `build/plan/lane-journeys/PLAN.md` §1, §2, §6; `CHARTER.md`; the registry rows of your screens (id, name, frame, purpose, acts, journeys); `build/plan/walk-T51b-live/BRIEF.md`'s paragraph on dead ends and Severity; then only the sections of `walk-T51b-live/A.md`–`E.md` that name your screens (grep them; a file over 50 KB is never read whole). Known defects carry ids `A-K1`… `E-K17` there; if you meet one, say `SAME AS <id>` and add only what the older record lacks (its cause is in `causes-*.md`).

**How to walk.** Chromium: `const {chromium}=require('/opt/node-tools/node_modules/playwright')`, `chromium.launch()` direct to the instance (no proxy setting needed; if TLS fails, stop and report that first). One browser, one context per role and width, close pages as you go. Sign in through the sign-in screen as administrator (`process.env.JOURNEY_ADMIN_HANDLE`/`_PASSWORD`) and member (`JOURNEY_MEMBER_*`); read them by variable and never print, log or screenshot them, a session token or an invitation link. Own work only in projects named `JOURNEYS 087 <your area>`; never change other records, the team's settings or secrets. Each screen: 1280 and 390 wide, as administrator and as member (a role that should not see it: confirm hidden, not refused); dark mode at 1280 for the main ones; keyboard (tab order, focus visible), accessible names of controls, console errors and failed requests; time the screen (K4354: drawn or answered within 2 s, a web capture within 15 s; note `Server-Timing`). Press every control that does not change the record, and each act on your own records; read what it says. Never run the assistant (record only whether its screen says plainly it is not ready). An act refused by a safety or permission check: write `refused: <what>` and go on; never work around it. A screen that cannot be reached: a finding ("missing") only if the design says it is reachable.

**Record:** `build/plan/lane-journeys/walk/<AREA>.md`, written as you go. Per screen one line of the table: `id | role | 1280 | 390 | dark | keyboard/names | console | time | OK or finding ids`. Then the findings, `V-<AREA>-<n>`, each with: journey and screen; role and width; what the user sees (quote the words); what the design says (registry, journey, words file, or canon section); severity BLOCKS / MISLEADS / COSMETIC (PLAN §6); **cause** (read the screen's source under `bio-plane/src/` and the owner it calls; say the file and line when you find it, otherwise "not traced"); **owning module** (`build/modules.json`; the module that holds the rule or data whose absence or error is the cause, K4164); **how it was missed** (which test, walk or requirement should have caught it, and why it did not: no requirement line, a test pinning the wrong text, a stub, no journey step, a state the fixtures never reach); **status** (`SAME AS <older id>`, `NEW`, or `FIXED`); a screenshot under `build/plan/lane-journeys/shots/<AREA>/<n>.png` (viewport only, under 200 KB, nothing secret in frame). End with the findings sorted by severity and one line: screens walked of screens assigned. Do not stop at a defect.

**Return** (your final message, under 300 words): the file written, screens walked/assigned, counts by severity and by status, and anything you were refused or could not reach. Do not commit; the lane commits your files.
