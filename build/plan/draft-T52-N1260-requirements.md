## 1. Technical choices I made (not Bob's)

1. **Additive ops; `explore` R2 untouched.** DEC-410 draws two buttons, so `exploreall` and `explorepath` are new; `explore` with `to` keeps its shortest-level answer.
2. **`followfrom` is one read in `affordances`** (layer 11, already using explore, money, lines, events, entities): no `entry` answers the item's menu; an `entry` answers the owner's own answer. It serves the entries the registry lists as `owed:followfrom`; `search`, `registersearch`, `explore` entries stay those ops. `moneyby` is the service under three entries.
3. **Strength order:** no hunch hop, no declared hop, better weakest `assertion` letter, better weakest `end` letter; then date, then ids. No `rank` or `score` is answered (K1471); a comparator is exported.
4. **Time:** default 1,500 ms (K4354 leaves 0.5 s for the round trip), ceiling 10,000. **Resume token:** self-contained, bound to viewer and parameters, 15 minutes, over 256 KB not offered.
5. **Money:** `money_flow` becomes five family kinds; `behested` goes with contributions.
6. **"A chain a member built"** = the graph trail; the screen runs `exploreshorter` itself at two or more steps (DEC-410 shows "best has 2 steps, against your 3" before any press).
7. **"In place"** = a fold under the item (11.8).
8. **Dropped:** per-dimension counts, `by_kind`, the "equal length but stronger" list (DEC-410/411 draw none). **Hub chains** are listed when the hub is a meeting node (DEC-410). `named_in` is **conditional on M-F1**.

## 2. connection-grammar (next free **R22**)

- **R22** (T53; N1260; DEC-409 "the strongest weakest hop"; K1471, K1487; reads R12, R13) *(not yet met: T53)* **`pathStrength(path)` and `strongerPath(a, b)`: one meaning of "strongest weakest hop".** `pathStrength(path)` takes a path of connections or of grouped connections (R24; a group counts as its strongest member on each axis) and answers `{assertion, end, lead, hunch}`: `assertion` and `end` are `chainGrade`'s (R12), `lead` is true when any hop is declared or a hunch (R13), `hunch` is true when any hop is a hunch (then `assertion` and `end` are null, as R12 answers). A path R12 refuses is answered with that refusal (`PATH_EMPTY`, `GRADE_INVALID`). `strongerPath(a, b)` answers -1 when `a` is stronger, 1 when `b` is, 0 when equal, comparing in this order: no hunch hop beats a hunch hop; no declared hop beats a declared hop; the better `assertion` letter (A best); the better `end` letter. Both are pure, never throw and answer no number and no key named `score` or `rank`. Test: hops graded [B,B] beats [B,C]; [A,D] and [D,D] tie on `assertion`, then compare on `end`; an evidenced weakest-D path beats a declared-hop path, which beats a hunch path; equal paths answer 0; an empty path is `PATH_EMPTY`. Control: `chainGrade` and `chainLabel` answer as before.
- **R23** (T53; N1260; DEC-409; K1471; amends R14) *(not yet met: T53)* **`orderPaths` orders by strength between hop count and date.** `orderPaths(paths, {by?})` orders by: `by` first when given (unchanged); then hop count, fewest first (a grouped connection counts as one hop); then `strongerPath` (R22), the stronger first; then the earliest `valid.from` (compared by `civil-time.compare`, undetermined last); then the hops' ids. It accepts paths of connections or of grouped connections. The order is total and independent of input order. Test: three 3-hop paths with weakest hops B, C, D come in that order whatever their dates; equal on hop count and strength: earliest date first; all equal: by ids; shuffled input gives one output; a 2-hop path with a D hop precedes a 3-hop path of all A. Control: with `by`, that quantity first.
- **R24** (T53; N1260; DEC-409, DEC-410 "each chain with its steps"; reads R12) *(not yet met: T53)* **`groupParallel(hops)` makes many parallel hops one step.** `hops` is a list of connections (R1 shape). Hops with the same `kind`, `from` and `to` (as given, direction kept) are one grouped connection `{id, from, to, kind, owner, word?, count, members, valid, grade, label?, undetermined?}`: `members` are the hops exactly as received, ordered by `valid.from` (undetermined last) then id; `count` is their number; `id` is the least member id; `valid.from` is the earliest stated `valid.from` of the members (null when none states one) and `valid.to` the latest stated `valid.to`; `grade.assertion` is the strongest member's and each of `grade.ends` the strongest member's for that end; `label` is the members' (one kind has one class); `undetermined` is present only when every member is undetermined at the date. The weakest-hop rule (R12) applies between grouped connections, never within one; every member stays cited and graded. A hop that is not a connection, or a list over 50,000 hops, is refused `HOPS_INVALID`; an empty list answers `[]`. Pure, never throws. Test: 40 payments between two funds are one group, `count` 40, 40 members each with evidence; a payment the other way is another group; members graded B, C, D give `grade.assertion` B; two kinds between the same nodes are two groups. Control: a member's own grade is unchanged.
- **R25** (T53; N1260; DEC-409, DEC-411 "stop at 2 seconds"; K4354, K4403 (9); amends R10, R15) *(not yet met: T53)* **Bounds for listing every chain and for a member's 2 seconds.** `BOUNDS` gains `paths`: `{default: 50, max: 200}`, and `pathsOf(requested)` answers 50 for none, the requested count from 1 to 200, and refuses `PATHS_OVER_MAX` above 200 and `PATHS_INVALID` for a non-integer. `time_budget_ms` is 1,500, and `time_budget_max_ms` is 10,000; `budgetOf(requested)` answers 1,500 for none, the requested milliseconds from 1 to 10,000, else refuses `BUDGET_OUT_OF_RANGE`. `exhausted` (R15) also takes the reason `paths` (a list cut at `BOUNDS.paths` or the caller's limit), answered in the same shape. Test: `pathsOf()` 50, `pathsOf(200)` 200, `pathsOf(201)` refused; `budgetOf()` 1,500, `budgetOf(0)` refused; `exhausted({visited: 3, reason: 'paths'})` answers the R15 shape. Control: the other bounds are as before; `BOUNDS` stays frozen.

## 5. explore (next free **R30**)

Terms for R30-R36. A **link** is a grouped connection (`connection-grammar` R24); a **chain** is a loop-free sequence of links, shown to a member as steps. A **meaningful chain** between `from` and `to` is a chain of at most `depth` steps (default 8) in which no node appears twice, each member hop is valid or undetermined at `at` (R4), is of an asked kind, a hunch hop is walked only within `scope` (R6), and no hub node is asked for its neighbours (R5, R20; a chain through a hub is meaningful only as R30 says).

- **R30** (T53; N1260; DEC-409 "every meaningful connection", DEC-410; K1471, K1487; R2-R9, R15, R20, R22) *(not yet met: T53)* **`connectionsBetween({from, to, kinds?, at, depth?, scope?, limit?, budget_ms?, viewer})` (`op=exploreall`): every meaningful chain between two ends.** Refusals, in R1's order, then `NO_TARGET` (no `to`), `SAME_NODE`, `PATHS_OVER_MAX` / `PATHS_INVALID` (`limit`), `BUDGET_OUT_OF_RANGE`. It answers every meaningful chain, fewest steps first, ordered by `orderPaths` (connection-grammar R23), at most `limit` (default `BOUNDS.paths` 50, at most 200). A chain through a hub node is listed, its step at the hub marked `hub: {node, kind, bound, set_size}`, exactly when both of its steps at the hub were returned by the answers of the chain's other nodes (the hub itself is never asked), and the hub is named in `hubs` (R5); a chain that would need the hub's own list is not listed. Answer `{ok, from, to, at, chains, listed, found, more_may_exist, complete_through_steps, stopped?, resume?, resumable?, hubs, unread, fanout_truncated, depth_stopped, at_depth, visited, depth, budget_ms, elapsed_ms}`. Each chain `{steps, step_count, grade (chainGrade), strength (pathStrength), label (chainLabel), undetermined?, level_complete}`; each step a link whose members are the hops as their owners answered, with `from_label`/`to_label` (R26) and R6's marks. `complete_through_steps` is the largest L such that every meaningful chain of at most L steps is counted in `found`; `listed` is the number in `chains`; longer chains found in the time follow with `level_complete: false`, not claimed to be the first of their length. `more_may_exist` is false only when the walk finished to `depth` with no bound cut and `found` equals `listed`; otherwise true with `stopped` the R15 shape (reason `paths`, `time`, `nodes`, `fanout` or `depth`). With no chain, R8's statement of level; a stop by time as R33. Writes nothing, logs no one (R9). Test: a graph with a 2-step, a 3-step and a 4-step route lists all three in that order (`explore` with `to` still lists only the 2-step); 40 parallel payments between two nodes are one step with `count` 40; no chain repeats a node; `limit: 2` of 3 answers `listed` 2, `found` 3, `more_may_exist` true, `stopped.reason` `paths`; a hub H with A-H and H-B answers the chain A-H-B marked `hub` and a chain needing H's own list is absent with H in `hubs`; over 200 random graphs with no bound hit, the chains equal a reference one-direction enumerator kept in the test; a 6-step pair whose one-direction walk stops at 5,000 nodes is answered whole; a hop hidden from the viewer leaves `chains`, `found` and `visited` as if absent; a declared hop is marked a lead. Control: the same graph asked with `kinds` excluding the 3-step's kind lists two; `explore` R2's answer is byte-identical to before.
- **R31** (T53; N1260; DEC-409 "the most direct path", DEC-410 "Most direct path"; K1471, K1487) *(not yet met: T53)* **`mostDirect({from, to, kinds?, at, depth?, scope?, budget_ms?, viewer})` (`op=explorepath`).** Refusals as R30 (no `limit`). It answers `{ok, from, to, at, most_direct, equally_short, most_direct_evidenced?, ...R30's bound fields}`. `most_direct` is the first chain under `orderPaths` among the chains with the fewest steps, a chain as R30 shapes it (hub, lead and `undetermined` marks kept), or null when the walk stopped before the level at which `to` is first met was complete (then `undetermined: true` with the R15 `stopped`) or no chain exists (with R8's statement of level). `equally_short` is the number of other chains with the same step count and the same `strongerPath` result (decided only by date or ids). When `most_direct` is a lead, `most_direct_evidenced` is the first chain with no declared or hunch hop within the same bounds, or null with `searched: {depth, complete}`; a finding never rests on a lead (K1487). Writes nothing. Test: two 2-step chains with weakest hops B and C answer the B one with `equally_short` 0; two with B and B on different dates answer the earlier with `equally_short` 1; a 2-step chain through a declared hop and a 3-step all-evidenced chain answer the lead as `most_direct` and the 3-step as `most_direct_evidenced`; a fake clock that expires before the first meeting level answers null with `stopped.reason` `time`; no answer carries `score`, `rank` or `central`. Control: with an evidenced 2-step chain, `most_direct_evidenced` is absent.
- **R32** (T53; N1260; DEC-409 "simplified", DEC-410 "A chain you built", DEC-411; K1467, K1487, K4403 (8); extends R23's `chain`) *(not yet met: T53)* **`shorterThan({chain, kinds?, at, depth?, scope?, limit?, budget_ms?, viewer})` (`op=exploreshorter`): shorter chains beside a member's own.** `chain` is a path as R3 answers it, 1 to 10 hops, each with `id`, `kind`, `from` and `to`; its two ends are its first and last nodes in walking order (a one-hop chain: `from` and `to`). A hidden hop never shortens anything. No key or text says better, worse, wrong or roundabout (R36). Writes nothing (R9). Test: a 3-step chain with a cited 2-step alternative answers it in `evidenced` with each hop cited and graded, `fewest_steps` 2; a chain that is already most direct answers empty lists and `searched`; an alternative through a declared hop is in `leads` and labelled a lead; an equal-length alternative is in neither; a chain with a gap answers `CHAIN_BROKEN` naming the hop. Control: a 1-hop chain answers empty lists.
- **R33** (T53; N1260; DEC-411 "Keep looking", K4403 (9), K4354; amends R5; `connection-grammar` R25) *(not yet met: T53)* **A stop by time keeps what was found and can be continued (`op=explorecontinue`).** `connectionsBetween`, `mostDirect` and `shorterThan` stop when `budget_ms` (default 1,500) is spent, checked between owner calls, and answer what is found, each chain whole, with `stopped` reason `time`, `more_may_exist: true` and `resume`, an opaque string holding the walk's parameters, both frontiers, the visited ids, the chains found, the op's name, the viewer's digest and the time issued, no key and no stored state; the answer says `resumable: false` with a reason instead when the token would exceed 256 KB. `continueWalk({resume, budget_ms?, viewer})` carries the walk on for another slice of at most `budget_ms` and answers in the shape of the op it continues, with `slice` (2, 3, ...) and the chains found in all slices so far; it re-reads the record as it then stands and applies the viewer's sight again. - **R34** (T53; N1260; DEC-409; amends R3, R10, R21) *(not yet met: T53)* **Existing walks order by `orderPaths` as connection-grammar R23 now states.** R3's order of paths (`explore`, the presets, the nodes and steps of R21) is `orderPaths`: fewest hops, then the stronger path, then the earlier date, then ids; `sortBy` still first. The default budget of every walk of this module is `connection-grammar` R25's, 1,500 ms, and every op of this module accepts `budget_ms` (1 to 10,000). `pathBetween` and every other preset otherwise answer as before. Test: `explore` over the chain fixture of R20 (M-X1a) answers within 1,500 ms and the 4,000 votes are walked; two equal-length paths differing in strength order by strength; the answer states `budget_ms`. - **R35** (T53; N1260; K4168; R27, R28; `op-declarations` R80) *(not yet met: T53)* **The four new ops are declared.** `DECLARED_OPS` gains rows, each classes `admin`, `member`, `probe`, `mutating` false, weight `read`: `exploreall` ("read: every meaningful chain between two ends, cited and graded, most direct first; writes nothing"; accepts `from`, `to`, `kinds`, `at`, `depth`, `scope`, `limit`, `budget_ms`; requires `from`, `to`, `at`); `explorepath` (accepts the same without `limit`; requires the same); `exploreshorter` (accepts `chain`, `kinds`, `at`, `depth`, `scope`, `limit`, `budget_ms`; requires `chain`, `at`); `explorecontinue` (accepts `resume`, `budget_ms`; requires `resume`).
