## 1. C50 — cite another team's accepted finding (UC-082)

**Owner.** `citation`: it writes a question's legs (`cite`, `drawQuestion`). The rest exists: the leg's grammar (`inquiry-grammar` R11: target `imported:<import>/<finding>`, positive `target_edition`, no grade, axis, source or part, not in `references[]`), the refusal unless the edition is accepted (`accepted-work` R3, R4, run inside the write), the grade read as the edition publishes it, never stronger (`strength` R33), acceptance and the origin facts (`case-import` R6, R4). Missing is the act that writes the leg: `cite` takes bundles and refuses anything else `NOT_CITABLE` (`citation/index.mjs`:511-526). Not `case-import` (read-only, R2; layer 8), not `inquiry` (past the P6 mark; cite was split from it, N55), not `basis-versions` (versions are frozen). Canon: DEC-92 (citing inherits the fact of publication, never the credibility of its content), Roadmap §11, DEC-112 (6). For another team's finding "no stronger than its edition" is met by the ref leg, not `grade_source: inherited` (that is Publication §3 rule 5 for the team's own published case; no op writes it either, and it is not C50's row).

Terms: an **imported leg** is a leg on an imported finding reference. Purpose gains "…or another team's accepted finding at a named edition."

## 2. C36 — a hunch-graded leg (UC-061)

**Owner.** DEC-15 and Declared Bias: a hunch is "a connection asserted ahead of its evidence", with "the only authored grade permitted above D", an author and a date, "visible as a hunch on every surface from the moment it is made", cleared before publication (DEC-20). Existing: the grammar (`grade_source: hunch`, connection axis, author and date; `inquiry-grammar/grammar.mjs`:755-767), inertness (`strength` R5, met), the publication refusal (`case-disclosures` R16). Missing: any act that writes or clears one. `cite` writes only a grade the record earns (`citation/index.mjs`:671-690; R8) and no op takes a grade argument. Owner `citation`, which composes a question's legs and acts on existing ones (`sever`, `reinstate`). Not `hypotheses` (its hunches are "never legs", R5, R8), not `strength` (met), not `inquiry` (size), not `case-disclosures` (it only asks). K1467 says hunches "are never legs"; BOB's K1470 reads that as met by an inert, visible, publication-blocking hunch leg. This draft follows K1470; BOB records the reading in `rulings.md`.

R8 is amended: "…except a hunch (R19), a member's declared bias: it states a grade the record does not yet earn, is inert in strength (`strength` R5) and refuses publication until cleared (R20)."
