## Public

### Purpose

The AI run's rules without a store: the bounds, endings and statuses a run can have, the checks a bound, a consumption, a principal, a context and a skill version must pass, the size a run's resumable state may reach, the one deployment order of the run's modes, and the rows of every refusal a run's acts carry. Pure: no storage, no clock, no viewer. `ai-runs`, which holds the run itself, and every module that produces under a run read them here.

### Provides

**The vocabulary and pure rules: RUN_BOUNDS, RUN_ENDINGS, RUN_STATUS, RUN_NEVER_STARTED, runStatusFor, STANDARD_BASIS, RUN_CONTEXTS, checkBound, checkConsume, PLANE_COUNTED_BOUNDS, PLANE_DECIDED_BOUNDS, finishedBound, projectGate, PROJECT_GATE_GROUNDS, runConsultsProjects, checkRunContextKind, runPrincipalOf, runPrincipalGate, checkSkillVersion, parseSkillVersion, translationOf, AI_RUN_CHECKS** Pure; never throw.
- **R1** (was `ai-runs` R1) The bounds and endings are exactly those above; `runStatusFor` answers `never-started` for `mode-not-deployed`, `stopped` for a bound, `finished` otherwise.
- **R2** (was `ai-runs` R2) `checkBound`: a name that is neither a bound nor an ending is `AI_RUN_BOUND_UNNAMED` (C-22.5).
- **R3** (was `ai-runs` R3) `checkConsume` (list form at open, map form at tick): a non-list or non-map shape, or an unknown bound, is `AI_RUN_BOUND_UNKNOWN` (C-22.15); any figure for `lease`, or a non-zero figure a caller sends for `mints` or `surfaces`, is `AI_RUN_BOUND_PLANE_COUNTED` (C-22.14); a declared allowance absent or zero is `AI_RUN_BOUND_NO_ALLOWANCE` (C-22.16); a figure that is not a non-negative safe integer is `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID` (C-22.13).
- **R4** (was `ai-runs` R4) `finishedBound`: an offered bound wins; else the first exhausted bound (allowed above 0, consumed at or past it) in R1's order; else `lease` when expired; else `completed`.
- **R5** (was `ai-runs` R5) `runPrincipalGate` compares caller and principal with a member credential's `/<tokenId>` removed; equal and non-empty passes, anything else is `AI_RUN_NOT_PRINCIPAL` (C-22.12) naming the act.
- **R6** (was `ai-runs` R6) `projectGate`: no actor passes unapplied (`NO_MEMBER_BEHIND_CALLER`); an inquiry context passes unapplied (`INQUIRY`); a project context passes when the actor has joined one of its projects (`PARTICIPANT`), else `AI_RUN_NOT_PROJECT_MEMBER` (C-22.8).
- **R7** (was `ai-runs` R7) `checkRunContextKind`: a context type outside `RUN_CONTEXTS`, or a context whose held kind (an unseen one is absent) differs, is `AI_RUN_NO_SUCH_CONTEXT` (C-22.11).
- **R8** (was `ai-runs` R8) `checkSkillVersion`: a blank version, or one not `<pack>@<edition>`, is `AI_RUN_SKILL_VERSION_UNNAMED` (C-22.7); any well-formed version is accepted, current pack or not. Held here and re-exported by `skills` (K82 (4)).

**DEPLOYMENT_SEQUENCE, GATE_ADDRESS, DEPLOYED_MODES, DEFAULT_MODE, DRAFT_MODE, deployedModesFor(flags)** (N156, K182 (3); K1982: `DEPLOYED_MODES` is `deployedModesFor()`, the one computation, `flags` naming any of `verification_recorded`, `plan`, `ask`, `draft`, a name left out taking today's value)
- **R9** (was `ai-runs` R44) (K182 (3), N156) `DEPLOYMENT_SEQUENCE`, the one deployment order: `order` `["check", "investigate", "extract"]`, `first_deployed_mode` its first member, `enforced_by` `["C-109.1"]` (`ai-runs` R40's refusal), and `GATE_ADDRESS` naming `agent-worker`'s gate. `skills` and `agent-worker` re-export it and hold no copy.
- **R13** (K660 (2); amends R1 and R3) `RUN_BOUNDS` gains `proposals`, after `surfaces`: the number of proposals a planning run may make, consumed by `action-plans` (its R31) through `ai-runs.consumeBound`. It is counted by the plane, as `mints` and `surfaces` are: a non-zero figure a caller sends for it is `AI_RUN_BOUND_PLANE_COUNTED` (R3). Its allowance is the one the run declares at the open; no figure of five or any other caps it here: five is the size of a page of the member's tray (`action-plans` R34), not a bound.
- **R14** (K660 (5); amends R9; T33: Q0-5, §7 item 10) `DEPLOYMENT_SEQUENCE.order` becomes `["check", "investigate", "extract", "plan"]`. `plan` is deployed only by its own flag in `DEPLOYED_MODES`, set by an explicit reviewed change of its own (`agent-worker` R53), never as a side effect of model turns running; `investigate` or `extract` being deployed or not changes nothing for it. The same change sets `agent-worker`'s `MODES.plan` (its R42). Until then an open in mode `plan` is refused by `ai-runs` R40 (C-109.1).

**AI_RUN_STATE_MAX_BYTES, checkRunState(state)** (N293, REC-169)
- **R10** (was `ai-runs` R45's share) `AI_RUN_STATE_MAX_BYTES` is 262,144: the most a run's `state` may hold, measured as the UTF-8 length of its JSON. `checkRunState(state)` answers null for an absent or null `state` and for one within the figure, else `AI_RUN_STATE_TOO_LARGE` (C-22.18, a row in this module's own table, R11), naming `bytes` and `limit`: the one site that mints the code, which `ai-runs`' open and tick relay (its R45). Pure; never throws.

**T33: the mode `ask`, its bounds, the standing exception, verification, the ceiling** (T33-49; Q0-5, Q1-2; K1450, K1481, K1502, K1503)
- **R16** (Q1-2) The mode `ask`: read-only (no write op of any module is in its reach; `answers` R1's `ASK_SCOPE` is its whole reach), interactive (it answers one member's question inside that member's act and is no run: it writes no run row, K1450), and `deploys_apart`: it is not in `DEPLOYMENT_SEQUENCE.order` and is deployed only by its own flag in `DEPLOYED_MODES`, set by a reviewed change of its own, whatever the run modes' state. `ASK_MODE` exports the description, frozen. (T35; N686) The mode `draft` (R21) is the second interactive mode and is deployed apart in the same way, by a flag of its own: `ask`'s flag never deploys `draft`, nor `draft`'s `ask`, and `DEPLOYED_MODES` holds each exactly when its own flag is true.
- **R17** (Q0-5; R-3 Q-E3) `ASK_BOUNDS` names the per-ask bounds, `turns`, `bytes`, `wall_ms` and `reads`, each a positive integer an ask declares when it starts, with a provisional default (M-Q7 measures the values at the release, plan T33). `checkAskBounds(bounds)` refuses an unknown name, a missing or non-positive figure, or a figure above the default's ceiling, with R3's codes; `askBoundReached(bounds, used)` answers the first bound reached in `ASK_BOUNDS`' order, or null. Pure; never throws.
- **R18** (K1481; the canon audit) An AI run or ask starts only at a member's act, with one exception: the AI half of a member-authored standing question (`answers` R19), which is read-only (R16's reach), bounded by R17's bounds, carried by the account that serves its author (their own, or the group's API key while held and on, `credentials` R35; K1755), and within its author's use ceiling (`ai-runs`). `startAllowed({startedBy, mode, standing?})` answers `{ok: true}` for a member's act; for no member's act, `{ok: true, exception: "standing_question"}` only when `standing` names an author and the mode is `ask`; otherwise `AI_RUN_NOT_A_MEMBER_ACT`. Pure; never throws. (T35; N686; DEC-153) A draft (R21) starts only at a member's own act, the member who asked for the help: `startAllowed({startedBy, mode: "draft"})` answers `{ok: true}` when `startedBy` names a member, and with no member's act it is `AI_RUN_NOT_A_MEMBER_ACT` whatever `standing` holds, since the exception is the mode `ask`'s alone.
- **R19** (Q0-5; VF-4) `VERIFICATION_RECORDED` is the shape of the act that records a mode's first live run verified, `{mode, run, verified_by, at, evidence}`; `checkVerification(v)` refuses a mode outside `DEPLOYMENT_SEQUENCE.order`, a missing run, a machine `verified_by` or no evidence. `deployable(mode, verifications)` answers whether `mode` may be deployed: the first mode of the order always; each later one only when a well-formed `verification_recorded` is held for every mode before it in the order (`investigate` only after `check`'s), so the chain that enables `investigate` is the record's, never a parameter. Pure; holds nothing: the act is written by `ai-runs` (its job states where).
- **R20** (Q0-5; K1450, K1502; D79) R11's table gains the ceiling refusals, each with its plain-words translation: `AI_USE_CEILING_REACHED` (the member's own daily ceiling, set by the member, is reached), `AI_USE_COPY_CEILING_REACHED` (the lower ceiling an administrator set for the copy's own load is reached), `AI_NO_ACCOUNT` (no account serves the member's act: none of their own connected, and the group's API key not held or off; `credentials` R35; K1502, K1755), R18's `AI_RUN_NOT_A_MEMBER_ACT`, `NOT_YOUR_CEILING` (a ceiling set or read by another than the member it belongs to, or an administrator for the copy's) and `AI_CEILING_INVALID` (a ceiling figure that is not a positive integer or null; K1601); minted by `ai-runs` and `answers` and read here by key, as R11's `ai-runs` rows are. A translation names no cost per answer (K1450: members see no per-answer cost). (T40; N812; K2373, B11) `AI_USE_CEILING_REACHED` and `AI_USE_COPY_CEILING_REACHED` retire, their numbers never reused, for `ai-use`'s `AI_LIMIT_REACHED`; `AI_CEILING_INVALID` retires, its number never reused, for `ai-use`'s `AI_LIMIT_INVALID` (K2400); `NOT_YOUR_CEILING` stays (`ai-use` R2). `AI_NO_ACCOUNT`'s translation adds "or the account that would serve has this use switched off" only where `AI_USE_SWITCHED_OFF` (`credentials` R56) is not answered. `ai-use`'s rows are its own, in its own table (its R8), not this module's. *(not yet met: T41)*

**T35: the mode `draft`** (T35-43; N686; DEC-152, DEC-153; K1837, K1841)
- **R21** The mode `draft`: the assistant's labelled draft of a member's own words ("Help me write this", DEC-153; the group's description, DEC-152), answered into the member's field and kept only by the member's own act (`wizard-scripts` R24, R25; K1364). It is interactive: it answers one member's request inside that member's act and is no run, so it writes no run row and keeps nothing (the draft is answered, never stored). It is read-only: its reach lies within `answers` R1's `ASK_SCOPE` and holds no write op of any module; a draft for a field that records what the member saw (`wizard-scripts` R25's `firsthand`) reads nothing at all. It is bounded by R17's `ASK_BOUNDS`, declared when it starts and judged by `checkAskBounds` and `askBoundReached`. It is deployed apart: not in `DEPLOYMENT_SEQUENCE.order`, taking no part in R19's chain, and deployed only by its own flag, set by a reviewed change of its own whatever the run modes' state and whatever `ask`'s flag; the flag is false until the change that serves `agent-worker`'s `POST /draft`. `DRAFT_MODE` exports the description, frozen, as `ASK_MODE` does. `RUN_MODES` does not hold `draft`, so a run opened in mode `draft` is refused (`ai-runs` R40, C-109.1). (T37; N669; K2200) A draft is of one of two kinds (R22): `own_words`, everything above, and `translation`, whose reach is R22's and not `ASK_SCOPE`.

**T37: the translation draft** (T37-49; N669; DEC-127 (5), DEC-157 (2), (4); K1755, K2200, K2201)
- **R22** `DRAFT_KINDS` is `["own_words", "translation"]`, frozen, and `DRAFT_MODE` (R21) names them. A `translation` draft is the assistant's draft of the interface words a group's language lacks, or its reading of one kept word back into English for an administrator's check (DEC-157 (2), (4); `instance-setup`'s `translationdraft`, its two directions). It is R21's mode in every other respect: interactive, no run, no run row, read-only, R17's `ASK_BOUNDS`, deployed by `draft`'s own flag. Its reach is nothing of the record: no read op of any module, `ASK_SCOPE` included, whatever the member's suggestions switch, so the run is given only the words it is asked about (at most 100 a draft, K2201). The draft is answered to the plane, never kept by the run; the words recorded as a labelled draft, adopted or confirmed are `instance-setup`'s, which stores a translation draft (K2201), never the mode's. `draftMayRead({kind, firsthand, suggestions})` answers whether a draft may read through a grant: `true` only for `own_words`, not `firsthand`, with `suggestions` true (R21; K1841 (2)); `false` for `translation` whatever the rest, and `false` for an unknown `kind`. `startAllowed` (R18) answers for a translation draft as for any draft: only at a member's own act. Pure; never throws.
