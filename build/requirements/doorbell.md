## Public

### Purpose

The doorbell: the public intake by which anyone, with no account, hands the group material (Intake Doctrine §2a), and the member's inbox over it. A knock is not a capture and not a bundle. This module takes the knock, bounds it by size and by a published rate, holds its bytes and one inbox row, and answers a knocker who presented a secret a pseudonym that is the same at this instance each time and names no one.

A signed-in member lists, reads and resolves knocks with a reason, and brings one into the record (`pullKnock`), which writes one acquisition receipt and answers a provenance document graded received, never fetched; the control plane promotes it. The knocks the doorbell turns away are counted, by day only, in its own tally and in `credentials`' security tally, holding nothing that could single out a knocker.

It also answers `sources` the knocks pulled into a capture, the knocks under a pseudonym, a secret's digest and pseudonym, and a rate check for its consent by secret. Like `capture`, it writes no bundle: no intake path writes live state.

### Provides

Terms are `capture`'s. A **knock** is material handed to the group through this door; the **inbox** is the knocks this module holds. A **knocker secret** is a string a knocker presents so that later knocks read as continuous. The **caller class**, whether the caller is a member session, the connecting address (`sourceAddress`) and `country` are the control plane's stamps. Every refusal names a `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check` and `translation`.

**The doorbell: knock({contentB64 | contentText, note, contact, knockerSecret, generateSecret}, {sourceAddress, country}), inboxList(status, {sort, dir, limit, after}), inboxGet(knockId), inboxResolve({knockId, status, by, reason}), doorbellTally({viewer})**
- **R1** (was `capture` R30) Anyone may knock, with no account. The envelope over the byte cap plus 4 KiB is `KNOCK_ENVELOPE_TOO_LARGE` (C-85.3); no content is a required-argument refusal; bad base64 likewise; empty content is `KNOCK_EMPTY` (C-85.5); content over 8 MiB (with an evidence store) or 64 KiB (without, stored inline) is `KNOCK_PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE` (C-85.4).
- **R2** (was `capture` R31) At most 5 knocks per source (a fingerprint of the connecting address, never the address) and 10 per instance in any 10 minutes, estimated by a two-bucket sliding window (`prev × (1 − elapsed/W) + cur`, refused at `≥ limit`) counted in the same transaction as the write: `RATE_IP` (C-85.1), `RATE_GLOBAL` (C-85.2), each with `stated`, the published bound composed from the same constants. A store that does not answer is reported as silence, never as a rate refusal. (DEC-108 (3); K1019)
- **R3** (was `capture` R32) An accepted knock writes one inbox row (`KNOCK-<date>-<8 hex>`, digest, size, `note` to 2,000 characters, `contact` to 300, received time, status `new`) and, with an evidence store, the bytes under `bio/inbox/<digest>`; nothing else changes. Only a signed-in member lists, reads and resolves knocks (`pulled`, `discarded` or back to `new`, else `BAD_STATUS`), recorded with who and when; a knock id no knock answers to, read or resolved, is refused `NO_SUCH_KNOCK` (404, its own row in this module's table, K275; not `capture` R63's `EVIDENCE_NOT_HELD`), with nothing written. Every resolve, to any of the three statuses, takes the member's own `reason`: one absent, not a string, blank (white space only) or over 2,000 characters is refused `RESOLVE_NO_REASON` (C-118.7, 400), tried after `BAD_STATUS` and `NO_SUCH_KNOCK` and before anything is written, so the row, its status and its resolver stay as they were. An admitted resolve records the reason on the inbox row with who and when (DEC-88 (2); J1 (4), J2). A knock is not a capture and not a bundle. A knock becomes `pulled` only by R13: `inboxResolve` with `status: "pulled"` is R13's act for that knock and answers as R13 does, once its reason is admitted; the reason is recorded on the row with the pull. `pullKnock` called directly (`op=inboxpull`) is not `inboxResolve` and takes no reason (R13 unchanged). The inbox row also holds `knocker_digest` and `pseudonym` (R14), each null for a knock sent without a secret. `inboxList(status, {sort, dir, limit, after})` lists at most `limit` knocks, with `truncated` and `next`, sorted by `sort`: `received` (the default, newest first: today's order), `status`, `secret` (whether a knocker secret was presented: `knocker_digest` not null) or `project` (the project of the document a pulled knock was brought into, `provenance.homeOf`; a knock not yet pulled has no project and sorts last in either direction); `dir` is `asc` or `desc`; ties fall back to received time, newest first. An unknown `sort` or `dir` is the required-argument refusal naming it, with nothing read (DEC-108 (2)). No act of this module's clears a discarded knock's row or bytes while any litigation hold is in place (`actions` R52: an entry whose latest hold is `in_place`); this binds the discard archive's one-week clearing (DEC-108 (5)), which no requirement here yet provides. A later module states whether a hold is in place through a reader it registers once at start, as `capture` R44's listeners are registered; with none registered, or one that fails or does not answer, nothing discarded is cleared (DEC-108 (5)). *(bounded by N90, K175: `limit` defaults to 200, clamped 1…1000; each answer publishes `limit` and `truncated`, a paged read also `next`, the cursor passed back as `after`)* (DEC-88 (2); DEC-108 (2)(5); K1019)
- **R10** (was `capture` R53) `knock` is a POST; any other method answers 405 and nothing else happens. The refusals are tried in this order and the first that applies answers: the envelope (R6); a body that is not JSON carrying `contentB64` or `contentText` as a string (the required-argument refusal, 400); `contentB64` that is not base64 (the same refusal naming `contentB64`, 400); empty content (R8); oversize content (R7); a weak knocker secret (R14's `KNOCKER_SECRET_WEAK`); the per-source rate (R4); the instance rate (R5). A refused knock writes no inbox row, stores no bytes and counts toward neither rate window; it is counted in R19's tally and nowhere else. A method other than POST is not a knock and is not counted. (DEC-108 (6); K1019)
- **R11** (was `capture` R54) An accepted knock answers 200 `{ok: true, knockId, sha256, bytes, received}`: `sha256` the digest of the decoded bytes, `bytes` their length, `received` a sentence saying the material is in the group's inbox awaiting member review. With an evidence store, the answer is sent only after the bytes are held under `bio/inbox/<digest>`, and an inbox row never stands without its bytes: when they cannot be stored, the knock answers a failure and leaves no row. The answer also carries `pseudonym` (R14; null for a knock sent without a secret) and, when the doorbell generated the secret, `secret`: shown in that answer only and held nowhere.
- **R19** (was `capture` R80) The doorbell keeps a count-only tally of the knocks it turns away: every knock R10 refuses (the envelope, a required argument, base64, empty, size, a weak secret, either rate limit) and every refusal R17 answers. For each UTC day it holds two counts: the knocks refused that day, and how many of them found the whole-doorbell limit reached (R5). It also holds the day the whole-doorbell limit was last reached, as a date only. It keeps the last 30 days and drops any older day. It holds no address, fingerprint, time of a knock, knocker digest, pseudonym, note, contact or content. `doorbellTally({viewer})` (`op=doorbelltally`) answers, to a member session only, `{days, last_limit_reached}`: `days` the kept days with at least one refusal, newest first, each `{day, refused, limit_reached}`, and `last_limit_reached` that date, or null when the limit has never been reached. It writes nothing. The tally is status, read where the inbox is read: no queue item and no notification is ever made from it. It never decides whether a knock is refused or admitted, and a count that cannot be written never changes the refusal's answer. (DEC-108 (6); K1019; BOB's privacy ruling on the tally)
- **R20** (was `capture` R85) (N703; K1875, DEC-165, DEC-166) Every knock R10 refuses, and every refusal R17 answers, is also counted in the security tally, through `credentials.securityCount({kind: "handover", country})` (its R44), once per refusal, beside R19's tally; a method other than POST is not a knock and is not counted, and an accepted knock never is. `country` is Cloudflare's two-letter label for the request as the control plane read it, passed to `knock` and `knockAttempt` as a stamp beside `sourceAddress`, never from the body, or null. No address, fingerprint, knocker digest, pseudonym, note, contact, time within the hour or content reaches the count. A count that cannot be written is dropped and never changes the refusal's answer, R10's order or R19's tally; this module keeps no table for it.

**Bringing a knock in, and the knocker's pseudonym: pullKnock({knockId, by, within}), knocksOf({pseudonym, limit, after}), knockerDigestOf(secret)** (N364; DEC-78 items 1–4)
- **R13** (was `capture` R65) **pullKnock({knockId, by})** (`op=inboxpull`). Refusals in order: the member-session fence (R3's); `NO_SUCH_KNOCK` (C-118.2); `KNOCK_DISCARDED` (C-118.4) for a knock at `discarded`; `capture` R63's `EVIDENCE_NOT_HELD` when its bytes are gone. A knock already pulled answers `existed: true` with the same capture. Otherwise, in one act: the bytes are held under their own digest; one acquisition receipt is written (`provenance.recordReceipt`) with `via: "doorbell"`, naming the knock as its address (`knock:<knockId>`); the knock becomes `pulled` with the capture's digest, `by` and the instant; `by` is recorded as the capture's actor in the table `capture` R69 reads (through `capture.recordCaptureActor`); and the answer carries a provenance document (`acquisition` R16) whose `source` is `{kind: "knocker", named: false, pseudonym, receipt: {knock_id, sha256, bytes, received}}` and whose `capture.actor` is `by`. The note travels as `knocker_note: {text, words_of: "the knocker", evidence_of_truth: false}`. The document never carries `contact`. Like `acquire`, it writes no bundle (`acquisition` R25); the control plane promotes the document at `collected` in the same act (`store-door` R7). (N380, K580) `pullKnock` also takes `within`, a synchronous function (store-door R7): it is called with the pulled document inside the pull's own transaction, after the receipt, the knock's `pulled` update and the actor are written. An answer `{ok: false, …}` rolls the whole pull back (no receipt, the knock unchanged, no actor) and is the pull's answer, with `knockId`; a throw, or an answer that is not synchronous, rolls it back likewise and answers `PULL_WITHIN_FAILED` (500, a programming fault, no row); its `detail` is one fixed sentence, never the thrown message (N409, K655). Any other answer is carried as `within` beside the pull's own. A knock already pulled does not call it. The bytes held under their own digest before the transaction stay (content-addressed). The receipt's first hop names this instance by `acquisition.firstHopWho` (its R33; N541).
- **R14** (was `capture` R66) `knock` takes an optional `knockerSecret` (a string) or `generateSecret: true`. A supplied secret shorter than 20 characters is `KNOCKER_SECRET_WEAK` (C-118.3), and nothing is stored, and nothing counted but R19's tally. `generateSecret` makes one of at least 128 random bits. The row holds only `knocker_digest`, an HMAC-SHA-256 of the secret under an instance key held as R12's key is held, but a separate one (`KNOCKER_SECRET_KEY`), and `pseudonym`, a fixed readable derivation of the digest. The secret appears in no row, log or answer except R11's one-time `secret`. The same secret gives the same pseudonym at this instance, and a different one at another. `knockerDigestOf(secret)` answers `{knocker_digest, pseudonym}` for a presented secret at this instance (for `sources`' consent by secret), writes nothing and never throws. (DEC-108 (6); K1019)
- **R15** (was `capture` R67) `knocksOf({pseudonym, limit, after})` answers, to a member session only, the knocks sharing that pseudonym, oldest first, with the continuity sentence "the same knocker secret was presented", never an identity. It answers at most `limit` (default 200, clamped 1…1000), with `truncated` and `next`, the cursor passed back as `after` (N90's bound).

**For `sources`: knockAttempt({sourceAddress, now, country}), pulledKnocksOf(captureSha)** (K539)
- **R17** (was `capture` R71) **knockAttempt({sourceAddress, now})** (for `sources` R11; K539). It asks R2's two windows exactly as `knock` does and answers R2's rate refusal (`RATE_IP` or `RATE_GLOBAL`, with `stated`) or null; an admitted attempt is counted in both windows as a knock is. It writes nothing else.
- **R18** (was `capture` R72) **pulledKnocksOf(captureSha)** (for `sources` R1; K539) answers, to a member session only, every knock pulled into that capture (R13), oldest received first, each with `knock_id`, `sha256`, `bytes`, `received`, `pseudonym` and `knocker_digest`, never `contact` (R16); none answers `[]`. It is one keyed read.

**The source fingerprint**
- **R12** (was `capture` R56) The source fingerprint R2 counts by is a keyed digest of the connecting address under a secret held by the instance (never an unkeyed hash, which can be reversed by trying every address), so neither a row nor a rate bucket lets anyone recover the address. The key is the secret binding `KNOCK_FINGERPRINT_KEY` when set, else a random 256-bit key generated once and kept in a table this module declares exempt from purge (R25), returned by no op; the digest is HMAC-SHA-256 (K120).

**The instance: doorbellOf(ctx, deps)** (K61; the map's doubts 4 and 5, K2607)
- **R25** (new) `doorbellOf(ctx, deps)` answers the one instance for a storage (K61): every caller for the same storage gets the same instance. At its creation it: (K2629) Before declaring its own tables it calls `capture.declareTables()` (`capture` R87), so capture's declaration is always first and the held arm is the one taken, in whatever order a host builds the two.
  - creates its six tables (`inbox`, `knock_rate`, `knock_key`, `knocker_key`, `doorbell_tally`, `doorbell_limit_last`, with the `inbox` additive columns) with the same DDL `capture` created them by (`CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`), so a running store's inbox, keys and tally are kept as they are, with no data move;
  - declares the six to `record-core`'s purge as exempt (the inbox, the keys of R12 and R14, and R19's tally are never cleared by the whole-store purge, as `capture` declared them). A declaration refused `TABLE_DECLARED` whose `declaredBy` is `capture` is held, not a failure: the classes are the same (exempt), so the instance is created and nothing is thrown while `capture` still declares them; any other refusal of the declaration throws, naming the table, before the caller gets an instance.

  Its `env` (the `CAPTURES` evidence bucket, `KNOCK_FINGERPRINT_KEY`, `KNOCKER_SECRET_KEY`, `INSTANCE_NAME`, `VERSION`) is read, at each use, from `capture`'s instance for the same storage (`captureOf(ctx).env`), so `capture` R58's adoption rule is the only one that decides which `env` a storage holds; this module takes no `env` option of its own.
