## Public

### Purpose

The group's governed library of filing templates: wording, with named blanks, that a group may file in its own name (Tier 1 and 2 kinds) or send to counsel as the basis of a briefing (any tier). Each template is versioned, never edited; every version carries its notes, its whole attribution, its reviews (by members, or by professionals through a revocable grant) and its approval by a member who is not its sole author. Every approved version (the current one and those it updated) is offered for a filing, each with its metadata, the latest by default; a retired template offers none. A machine proposes wording and nothing else. The profile's templates are read here as approved versions, read-only. This module also publishes the closed set of blanks every template and filing uses.

### Provides

Terms. A **template** is R1's record; a **version** R2's. A version's **state** is one of `draft`, `in_review`, `approved`, `updated` (an approved version a later approved version follows, naming it; K924) or `withdrawn` (`TEMPLATE_STATES`, R21). A template is **retired** when withdrawn from use whole (R11). A template's **use** is `file` (wording the group files in its own name) or `brief` (the basis of a briefing to counsel) (`TEMPLATE_USES`). Its **origin** is `group` or `profile` (`imported` reserved, never written in this version). A **blank** is `{{name}}` in a text, `name` a key of `FILING_BLANKS` (R19). A version is **offered** when it is `approved` or `updated` and its template is not retired (R14); nothing else is. The **tier** of a kind is the profile's (`jurisdictions` R25) in the active profiles' combined view (`jurisdictions.combine`), the strictest across the profiles a template names (for a `general` one, across the view), undetermined when none states one. A version id is written `<template>@<version>`. A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, code, check, translation, ...}`. `author`, `by`, `proposer`, `viewer` and `secretSha` are the control plane's stamps.

**The template and its versions**
- **R1** A template is `{id, kind, use, profiles, name, scope, origin, versions}`. `id` is an opaque `TPL-` id, never a counter. `kind` matches `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*$` (`TEMPLATE_KIND_REFUSED`, C-115.33); when `profiles` is a list it is an `action_kinds` kind of every profile named (`TEMPLATE_KIND_UNKNOWN`, naming the profile). `use` is `file` or `brief` (`TEMPLATE_USE_REFUSED`). `profiles` is a non-empty list of held profile ids (`TEMPLATE_PROFILE_UNKNOWN`, naming one not held) or `general`: a general template is offered for its kind under every profile (R14). A `file` template on a kind whose tier is 3 in any profile it names, or for a `general` one in the active view, is refused `TEMPLATE_TIER3_FILE` (C-115.36, re-keyed from `TEMPLATE_KIND_TIER3`); a `brief` template may serve any tier. `name` is one line of 1–200 characters (`TEMPLATE_NAME_REFUSED`, C-115.32), not held by another of the scope's templates that is not retired (`TEMPLATE_NAME_TAKEN`, C-115.37). `scope` is `{project}`, the project it was drafted in, or `group` once R10 widens it.
- **R2** A version is `{template, version, text, sha, state, notes, author, contributors, derived_from, reviews, approved, ended}`. `version` counts from 1 within its template. `text` is non-empty UTF-8 of at most `FILING_TEXT_MAX` bytes (`TEMPLATE_TEXT_REFUSED`, C-115.35), every blank in it a key of `FILING_BLANKS` (`TEMPLATE_BLANK_UNKNOWN`, naming the first unknown). `sha` is the SHA-256 (lowercase hex) of `text`. Once the version leaves `draft`, its text and `sha` never change. `derived_from` is null or one of `{filing, sha}`, `{version}`, `{proposal}` (R3).

**templateDraft({template?, project?, kind?, use?, profiles?, name?, text, from?, notes?, author, viewer})** (`op=templatedraft`) → `{ok, template, version, sha, state: "draft"}`
- **R3** Creates a template and its first draft (`project`, `kind`, `use`, `profiles`, `name` given; R1 judges them), or a new draft version of the named `template`, keeping its kind, use, profiles, name and scope. `from` is a version the author may see (a derivative, of a group's or a profile's template), a proposal (R6) or, only through `filings`' own service (`filings` R32), `{filing, sha}`, the approved filing draft whose text `filings` hands in. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_TEMPLATE` (a machine or unstamped author); `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE` (C-115.38; a named template absent or not visible, one answer); `TEMPLATE_SCOPE_REFUSED` (the author is not a joined participant of the project, `membership` R54); `TEMPLATE_DRAFT_OPEN` (the template has a version in `draft` or `in_review`, naming it); `TEMPLATE_RETIRED` (the template is retired, R11); R1's refusals; R2's; `TEMPLATE_FROM_REFUSED` (a `from` naming nothing the author may see, or `{filing, sha}` sent through the op).

**templateRevise({version, text, adopt?, notes?, author, viewer})** (`op=templaterevise`)
- **R4** Replaces a draft's text by a new revision, keeping every earlier revision with its author and time; the version's `sha` is its latest revision's. `adopt: <proposal>` takes a proposal's text (R6) as the revision. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_TEMPLATE`; `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE`; `TEMPLATE_SCOPE_REFUSED`; `NOT_A_DRAFT` (the version is past `draft`); R2's text refusals; `TEMPLATE_FROM_REFUSED` (an `adopt` naming no proposal the author may see).
- **R5** `contributors` lists, in time order, every member who revised the version and every adopted proposal's run (`run`, model, skill pack version, the proposal id), each dated. It is written by R3, R4 and R6's adoption and by nothing else.

**templatePropose({template? | kind, text, why, proposer, viewer})** (`op=templatepropose`)
- **R6** Any credential may propose wording, for a named template or for a kind. It is stored apart under an opaque `TPP-` id, labelled `proposalLabel(proposer, "template")` (`record-grammar` R42), with `why` of 1–1,000 characters; it is a template's text only when a member names it as `from` (R3) or `adopt`s it (R4), and then its run joins `contributors`. Refusals in order: `TEMPLATE_NO_PROPOSER` (no stamped proposer); `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE`; `TEMPLATE_KIND_REFUSED` (for a kind); R2's text refusals; `TEMPLATE_WHY_REFUSED`.

**templateSubmit({version, reviewers, author, viewer})** (`op=templatesubmit`)
- **R7** Moves a draft to `in_review`, fixing its text and `sha`, and records the members asked to review. `reviewers` lists members who may see the template; professionals are asked through R8's grants. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_TEMPLATE`; `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE`; `TEMPLATE_SCOPE_REFUSED`; `NOT_A_DRAFT`; `REVIEWER_UNKNOWN` (a listed reviewer who is not a member who may see it, naming the first); `NO_REVIEWERS` (no member listed and no live grant on the version).

**templateReviewGrant({version, recipient, organisation, secretSha, by, viewer})** (`op=templatereviewgrant`) and **templateGrantRevoke({grant, by, viewer})** (`op=templategrantrevoke`)
- **R8** A participant of the template's project gives a named non-member a revocable read-and-comment-and-review door to one draft or in-review version, by a secret whose SHA-256 the control plane took (`control-plane` R44), as `review` R6–R8 do for a review copy: an opaque `TRG-` id; `recipient` and `organisation` one line of 1–200 characters each (`GRANT_RECIPIENT_REFUSED`); `secretSha` 64 lowercase hex (`GRANT_NO_SECRET`). A grant is live while it is not revoked and its version is `draft` or `in_review`. Revocation records the revoker and instant; a second answers `existed: true` with the first, unchanged. Refusals before those: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_TEMPLATE`; `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE` (or `NO_SUCH_GRANT` for a revocation); `TEMPLATE_SCOPE_REFUSED`. Every caller through the door with a secret that is not live, of any kind, receives one dead answer, byte-identical (`NO_TEMPLATE_GRANT`).

**templateReview({version, outcome, scope, comment?, credential?, author | secretSha, viewer})** (`op=templatereview`)
- **R9** Records one review against the version's present `sha`, by a member who may see the template (`kind: member`, with their declared expertise from `membership`, shown) or by a live grant (`kind: professional`, with the grant's recipient and organisation and the `credential` as they state it, at most 200 characters, shown as stated and never verified). `outcome` is `no_concerns`, `concerns` or `changes_requested` (`REVIEW_OUTCOMES`; `REVIEW_REFUSED` otherwise); `scope` the words the reviewer gives, 1–200 characters (`REVIEW_REFUSED`); `comment` at most 4,000. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_REVIEW_TEMPLATE` (a machine author; a machine's critique is a comment, R13, never a review); the dead answer (R8) for a secret not live, `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE` for a member; `NOT_IN_REVIEW`; `REVIEW_REFUSED`; `REVIEW_STALE` (a `sha` sent that is not the version's). A reviewer's later review of the same `sha` stands in place of their earlier one, which is kept.

**templateApprove({version, widen?, reason?, by, viewer})** (`op=templateapprove`)
- **R10** Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE_TEMPLATE`; `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE`; `NOT_IN_REVIEW` (or, with `widen`, `TEMPLATE_NOT_APPROVED`); `NOT_AN_APPROVER` (an owner of the template's project, `membership` R65, approves; `widen: true` makes an approved project template group-wide and needs an administrator, `membership` R64); `APPROVER_IS_AUTHOR` (the approver is the version's author and no other member contributed, R5); `REVIEWS_INSUFFICIENT` (a `changes_requested` stands, or the version lacks the reviews its tier requires, each `no_concerns`: Tier 1 one member review; Tier 2, a Tier 3 kind's `brief`, or an undetermined tier, one professional review, or in its place the approver's `reason` of 1–1,000 characters for going without, shown wherever the version is shown). On success the version is `approved` with the approver, the instant, the tier, the profiles and the reason, if any; the template's previous approved version becomes `updated`, naming its successor, and stays offered (K924). Widening records the administrator and instant and changes no version.

**templateRetire({template, version?, reason, by, viewer})** (`op=templateretire`)
- **R11** Without `version`, retires the whole template (K924), by an approver of its scope (R10's right): none of its versions is offered again (R14), each stays readable with the retirement's reason, and no new version may be drafted of it (`TEMPLATE_RETIRED`). With `version`, withdraws a draft or in-review version, by its author (`withdrawn`). `reason` is 1–500 characters (`TEMPLATE_REASON_REFUSED`). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE_TEMPLATE`; `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE`; `NOT_AN_APPROVER` (or, for a withdrawal, `TEMPLATE_SCOPE_REFUSED` for anyone but the author, and `NOT_A_DRAFT` for a version past review); `TEMPLATE_REASON_REFUSED`; `TEMPLATE_ALREADY_ENDED` (a template already retired or a version already withdrawn, answered with the first ending). An approved or updated version is never withdrawn or retired alone; nothing is deleted.

**Notes and comments: templateComment({template, version, text, author | secretSha, viewer})** (`op=templatecomment`), **templateComments({template, version?, limit?, author | secretSha, viewer})** (`op=templatecomments`)
- **R12** Each version carries `notes` of at most 8,000 characters (`TEMPLATE_NOTES_REFUSED`), each edit kept with its author and time, editable through R3 and R4 while the version is a draft; after that notes are added (R13's comment with `note: true` by a member who may revise), never changed. A new version starts with its predecessor's notes, marked as carried.
- **R13** A comment is 1–4,000 characters (`COMMENT_REFUSED`), names its version, and is attributed to the member, the grant, or a labelled run (`proposalLabel(author, "template")`). `templateComments` lists them newest last, `limit` clamped to [1, 500] (500 when absent), saying whether it was cut. A grant's door (R8) reaches only its own version's comments.

**templatesFor({kind?, profile?, use?, state?, viewer})** (`op=templates`), **templateRead({template, version?, author | secretSha, viewer})** (`op=templateread`)
- **R14** `templatesFor` lists the templates the viewer may see (R24), newest first, at most 200, with `truncated`. By default it lists every offered version (`approved` and `updated`, of templates not retired), each with its state, author, contributors, reviews' summary, approver and dates, the latest approved version marked `default: true`; under `profile`, the templates naming that profile and the `general` ones, each saying whether it was written for that profile. `state` lists instead drafts, versions in review, withdrawn versions, retired templates' versions (each with its ending's reason), or open proposals (`proposed`); none of these is offered. `templateRead` answers one version (the latest approved by default) with its whole attribution (R2, R5, R9, R10, R12, R16), its proposals adopted, and its comments' count; a grant's door reads only its own version.
- **R15** The profile's templates (`jurisdictions` R40) are read as templates of origin `profile`, each an approved version with the attribution the profile carries, scope `group`, its `profiles` the profile it is in. None of R4 and R7–R11 applies to them; R3 may take one as `from` (a derivative, the group's own template) and R13 may comment on one. A profile template whose text names a blank outside `FILING_BLANKS` is not offered and reads `TEMPLATE_BLANK_UNKNOWN`.

**The blanks** (K922 (1); was `filings`' `FILING_BLANKS` and `FILING_TEXT_MAX`)
- **R19** `FILING_BLANKS` (frozen: each blank's name and the sentence saying what fills it) and `FILING_TEXT_MAX` (65,536 UTF-8 bytes) are exported, unchanged from `filings`' at the move, and `blanksOf(text)` answers the blank names a text holds, in order of first appearance, and the first name outside `FILING_BLANKS` or null. `filings` reads them here and holds no copy.

**The queue's read: reviewsRequested({after?, limit?, viewer})**
- **R20** Lists every (version, member) pair where the member was asked to review (R7), the version is `in_review`, and the member has given no review of its present `sha`: the template, the version, its name and kind, the template's `project` (its `scope`'s project, R1; null for a `group` template), the member, the asker and the instant asked; at most 500 per page in (version id, member) order, with `cursor` and `truncated` as `action-clocks` R1's. Writes nothing. (`queue-producers` R20; `project`: N476, K1038) *(not yet met: T22: `project` is not answered)*

**The version a filing uses: offeredVersion({template, version?, viewer})** (`filings` R28, R31)
- **R25** Answers the version of `template` a filing or briefing may use, with its metadata as R14 lists it (and `default: true` for the latest approved), its `use`, `kind`, `profiles`, `origin` and `sha`: `version` absent, the latest approved; a named `updated` version, with `updated_by` naming its successor. Refusals in order: `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE` (absent or not visible, one answer); `TEMPLATE_RETIRED` (the template is retired, naming the reason); `TEMPLATE_NOT_OFFERED` (the version is a draft, in review or withdrawn, or no version is approved). `filings` passes each through as this module's. Writes nothing.

- **R21** `TEMPLATE_STATES`, `TEMPLATE_USES` and `REVIEW_OUTCOMES` are exported, frozen, as the Terms and R9 give them, for `affordances` (its R30) to publish.

### Invariants

- **R16** Every name in attribution (author, contributor, reviewer, approver, ender, commenter) is held by value (the handle, or a professional's name and organisation, at the time of the act) beside the member id or grant id.
- **R17** Nothing is deleted. Templates, versions, revisions, notes, proposals, reviews, grants, comments, approvals, updates, widenings, withdrawals and retirements are append-only, in this module's own tables, declared to purge (K23).
- **R18** No place, law, venue or template wording is in this module's behaviour or outward text; the tests use the test profile (`build/layers.md`, rule 3).
- **R22** A machine writes only a proposal (R6) and a labelled comment (R13): it never drafts, revises, submits, grants, reviews, approves, widens, retires or withdraws (Design Requirement 12; DEC-24).
- **R23** Each refusal carries its row (R25's included), held in this module's own table (`checks.mjs`), each `{check, where, translation}` with its `where` naming this module's site: C-115.31 (re-keyed `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_TEMPLATE`), .32, .33, .35, .36 (re-keyed `TEMPLATE_TIER3_FILE`) and .37 move from `filings` with their numbers, .31's and .36's translations re-worded for their new codes; C-115.38 (`NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE`) moves from `filings`, which passes this module's answer through; every other code above is a new row of this module's family, **C-125** (K933). Each moved or new row is stamped by 1.52.0 (T22's promotion job).
- **R24** Visibility: a template of a project is seen by whoever may see the project (`viewerPredicate`); a `group` template and a profile template by every member; every read and act answers a template the viewer may not see as absent (`NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE`), and no count names one.
