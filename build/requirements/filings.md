## Public

### Purpose

What the group sends, prepared from the record. For an action whose governing tier is 1 or 2, a filing pre-filled from the record into an approved template version the preparer chooses (`filing-templates`; the latest the default) or into the member's own words, every filled blank naming its source, a Tier 2 filing carrying its advisory note. At any tier, a **counsel packet** (a briefing), for counsel the group names (required at Tier 3, optional below it) or for the group's own use: the facts with their citations, a chronology, exhibits with provenance, the standards' text, candidate legal theories and remedies, and any deadline that binds a claim, marked as prepared for counsel's review, never published, never in a form that can be filed as it stands. At Tier 3 counsel drafts and files. The AI prepares; a member approves and files, and records that it was sent. Kinds and venues come from the jurisdiction profile; templates from `filing-templates`.

### Provides

Terms. The **governing tier** of an action is the stricter (higher) of its kind's tier in the profile (`jurisdictions` R25) and the action's risk tier (`actions` R25); it is `undetermined` when the action's tier is undetermined, and the action's tier alone when the profile gives the kind none (stated so). A **blank** is a named gap in a template or a member's text (`{{name}}`, a key of `filing-templates`' `FILING_BLANKS`, its R19); a **filled blank** is `{name, value, source}`, `source` the record id (action, determination, finding edition, standard, content, capture, profile entry) the value is read from. A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, ...}`.

**filingPrepare({action, template?, text?, preparer, viewer}) → `{ok: true, id, tier, text, blanks, unfilled, advisory?, venue, label, template}` or refusal**
- **R1** Refusals in order: `FILING_NO_PREPARER` (no stamped preparer; K254); `NO_SUCH_ACTION` (absent, invisible or not an action, one answer); `ACTION_CLOSED` (the action is `resolved` or `abandoned`); `FILING_TIER_UNDETERMINED` (R2); `TIER3_COUNSEL_PACKET` (governing tier 3: no filing is prepared, R8 is the route); then R28's. (`KIND_NO_TEMPLATE` retired, K921: a filing may be written without a template, R28.)
- **R2** A filing is prepared only for a governing tier of 1 or 2. An undetermined action tier is refused, never read as 1 (D-182): a member states the tier first (`actions` R23). The stricter of the two tiers governs (K102): a Tier 3 kind is never templated even if a member marks the action 1, and a member may raise a Tier 1 kind to 3 and get a counsel packet (R8).
- **R3** Every blank of the template version or of the member's text (R28) is filled from the record, or left in the text as a visible `[UNFILLED: <name>]` marker and listed in `unfilled` with why (the record holds no value; the value is undetermined; the viewer may not see its source). A value is never invented, defaulted or taken from the preparer. Blanks are filled from: the action's addressee (`actions` R9: `role` and `body` for an office, `role` and `organisation` for a reporter, organisation or group, `description` for an audience; a blank the addressee's arm does not hold is left unfilled with why); the action's determination (the act, its date, the standards' citations, each finding's published edition); the action's governing laws and its `law`; its clock entries with their bases; the venue (`venue.name`, `venue.how`); the producing group (`promotion`'s fact `producingGroup`, promotion R40; while no provider answers, `FACT_UNAVAILABLE` or `FACT_FAILED`, the blank is left unfilled as undetermined, never as unrecorded); the preparation date.
- **R4** A Tier 2 filing carries the profile's advisory note recommending legal review before filing, first in the text and as `advisory` in the answer; a Tier 1 filing carries none (Design Requirement 8).
- **R5** Any credential may prepare. The draft is stored apart from the action, labelled with its preparer and whether it is machine work (`record-grammar`'s `proposalLabel(preparer, "filing_draft")`: `lawProposalState`'s three states, K171), answered with `evidence: false` and a sentence saying it is a draft nobody has approved or sent. Preparing again makes a new draft; drafts are never edited.

**filingApprove({filing, text?, author, viewer}) → `{ok: true, filing, approved_by, at, sha}`**
- **R6** Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE` (an empty or machine author); `NO_SUCH_FILING` (absent or invisible, one answer); `FILING_STALE` (since the draft: the action's tier, counterparty, determination or governing laws changed, or its determination was superseded; it names what changed); `STILL_UNFILLED` (the approved text still holds an `[UNFILLED: …]` marker); `TEXT_UNWRITABLE` (over the length bound, or not UTF-8 text). A member may approve the draft's text or an edited text; the approved text is the member's, recorded with who, when and its SHA-256, and a filing is approved at most once (`ALREADY_APPROVED`).

**filingRecordSent({filing, at, medium, artifactSha? | account?, author, viewer}) → `{ok: true, filing, action, ord, proposed}`**
- **R7** Refusals: `MACHINE_CANNOT_FILE`; `NO_SUCH_FILING`; `NOT_APPROVED`; `ALREADY_SENT`; then `actions.actionCorrespond`'s refusals. It records the sending as one `sent` entry on the action (`actions` R15–R16), held as the capture of the bytes sent or the member's account, and links the entry and the filing both ways. It moves no action state and writes no clock entry: `proposed` carries the next state a member may choose and the clock entries `action-clocks.clockPropose` offers for the kind's deadlines that start at filing or receipt. The instance transmits nothing: a member files by the venue's means (K102).

**counselPacket({action, counsel?, template?, reason, author, viewer}) → `{ok: true, id, version, sections, marking, fileable: false, template}` or refusal**
- **R8** A packet may be assembled at every governing tier (K924; `NOT_TIER3` retired). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_NAME_COUNSEL` (a machine or empty author: the group names its counsel); `PACKET_NO_REASON` (C-115.44, a new row of C-115, stamped by 1.53.0: the `reason`, the author's words on why the packet is assembled, absent, not a string, blank or over 2,000 characters; recorded with the packet's version; DEC-88, K1025); `NO_SUCH_ACTION`; `NO_COUNSEL` (at a governing tier of 3 or undetermined, `counsel` is absent or lacks a name or an organisation; at Tier 1 or 2 counsel is optional, K924, and one given still needs both; contact is optional); R31's template refusals; `NO_DETERMINATION` (the action rests on no live determination, `actions` R8, and states no `premise_override`; a packet for an overridden action is assembled with R24's disclosure and its facts section says in words that no determination is held). (T35, N653; K1739) A standard whose `access`, as `standards` holds it with the edition (T35-31), is not `free` is carried in the packet's standards section (R9), in every version and in every export (R11), as its designation, edition, issuer, citation, adoption and access, and only the passages relied on: those the determination's comparison rows quote as what it requires (`conformance`'s `requires`) and those the findings' legs target, each with its content id, quoted. (K2019) An access of `null` on a law kind (anything but kind `standard`) states no restriction: public law is free to read, so it is carried as free, with R9's `text` list; kind `standard` whose access is not `free`, `undetermined` included, is carried as not free. Its whole text, and any other passage, is never in the packet. An access `standards` does not state is carried as not `free`. A `free` standard is carried as R9 states. (T45; N858; Actions D9; v0.2 rule 12; S1 §D 6) `NO_DETERMINATION` is retired, its number never reused: a packet needs no determination and no override, at any stage; its facts section shows each matter's support as it stands (R9). `counselPacket` takes `purpose`, `claim` (the default) or `defence`. Control: an action resting on no determination and stating no override assembles a packet.
- **R9** The packet holds six sections, each item naming its record source: **facts** (each finding the determination rests on, with its published edition, its claim and its citations); **chronology** (R33); **exhibits** (each capture a fact or event cites, with its SHA-256, locator, capture time and attestations, from `provenance`, the attestations by `attestation.attestationsOf(captureSha)` (its R7; `provenance` R49 before N512's split), never by parsing the bundle document; K171); **standards** (each standard's citation, kind, issuer, text content ids and in-force answer at the act's date, `standards` R5, R7); **candidate theories and remedies** (each labelled as a candidate with who proposed it and whether it is machine work; never stated as a conclusion; the section says in words when it is empty); **deadlines** (every profile deadline that applies to `claim`, `jurisdictions` R26, with its period, count, start event and citation; its date computed only from a recorded start event and the rule's `count`, `calendar` or `business` on the profile's holiday calendar (`jurisdictions` R33), else `undetermined` with why, including a count reaching into a year the calendar does not list; K108 (5), N72). The breach consequences (`consequences.consequencesOf`) are included as recorded, states kept apart. (T45; N858; Actions D9; v0.2 rule 12; reconciliation R7) The facts section holds, for each matter the action rests on, a live determination with its findings (as now) or, with none, the inquiry and its findings, each with its kind (published, checked and held, accepted from another group, or unpublished) and grade, stated as not determined. A `defence` packet adds three sections: **challenged statements** (each statement of the group the opposition challenges, as the member names it, with the findings and sources it rests on), **pressure received** (each pressure mark and record on the action and its project, `action-holds.pressureFor`, its R12, and each `legal` mark on the case's docket, `docket` R27, with its date) and **defence deadlines** (each deadline with `defends: true`, `jurisdictions` R87 (c), with R9's date rule). The deadlines read are `local-facts.governingView`'s (its R12). No sentence this module writes into a packet states that the group has a claim, or whether a protection applies; candidate theories stay candidates (R14). Control: a `claim` packet has no defence sections; a search of a packet's own words finds neither statement.
- **R10** Every section, the packet's head and every export carry the marking "Prepared for review by <counsel's name, organisation>. Not legal advice. Not for filing.", or, with no counsel named (Tier 1 or 2), "Prepared for the group's own review. Not legal advice. Not for filing." The packet holds no caption, court or venue heading, signature block, prayer or form of relief, and nothing from a `use: file` template; a `brief` template's text enters only R31's `briefing` section; `fileable` is `false`. (T45; Actions D4; v0.2 rule 15) A packet for an action of a counsel-matter project (`project-roster.projectScope`, its R23) carries, beside this marking, the privilege marking and a warning on sharing outside, keyed `packet.privileged` and `packet.privileged.warning`. Control: the same packet in a project of scope `none` carries neither.
- **R11** The packet is never published: it is not a bundle, has no path to `publication`, and is read only by a member who may see the action and the project of every determination and consequence the packet draws on (`counselPacketRead`, `NO_SUCH_PACKET` otherwise, as `conformance` R15 and `consequences` R13 withhold them) (K316). `counselPacketExport({id, version, author})` hands a member the packet's bytes and records who exported which version, when and for which counsel; a machine is refused `MACHINE_CANNOT_EXPORT`.
- **R12** Assembling again makes a new version; earlier versions stay readable. A version is flagged `basis_changed`, naming each cause, when the determination it drew on is superseded or flagged (`conformance` R10), a finding is published in a later edition, or a standard is superseded; nothing in it changes.

- **R33** (EVENTS 2a; K1494) The packet's chronology is a timeline read: `events.timeline` (its R28–R30) over the set of the determination's act event (`conformance` R25), the counterparty office entity and the entities the determination's findings concern, from the act's date (or the earliest `when` among them) to the packet's assembly. It answers the world's lane ("what they did") and the registered sources' lane ("what we did": the action's correspondence, moves and clock through `actions`' source, the findings' publication and docket through `docket`'s) apart, each in `events`' order, never interleaved into one list; an item whose order is undetermined is shown so with its bounds, and one placed nowhere is listed apart. Each item names its record source (R18). A set the reader may not see in part is withheld as R27 states. A `truncated` lane says so.

**filingsFor({action, viewer}) → `{drafts, packets}`**
- **R13** Lists the action's drafts (each with its tier, label, approval and sending), and its counsel packets (each version with its counsel, `basis_changed` and exports), in creation order; `NO_SUCH_ACTION` for an absent or invisible action. A draft or packet drawing on a determination or consequence in a project the viewer may not see is left out of the list, and nothing of it (its id, its determination, its words) is named, `basis_changed` included (K316). It is the read `escalation` uses.

**Candidate theories: theoryPropose({packet | action, theory, remedy?, standards, why, proposer, viewer})**
- **R14** Any credential may propose a candidate theory and remedy against named standards, stored apart, labelled (`proposalLabel(proposer, "theory")`, K171), with a `why` of at most 1,000 characters; `THEORY_NO_STANDARDS` when none is named (K254), `NO_SUCH_STANDARD` naming one (standards' own refusal, passed through), `THEORY_STANDARD_UNREADABLE` while standards cannot be read, and `THEORY_NO_PROPOSER` for no stamped proposer (K254). It is included in the next packet version (R9) as a candidate, never as the group's position.

**The evidence package's available-actions block (registered with `publication`, the K31 pattern)**
- **R15** For a published case whose finding a live determination rests on, the block lists the kinds available against its counterparty's offices from the profile, each with its tier and words; for Tier 3 kinds, the standards the theories rest on, the factual basis (the determination's findings) and the sentence that such an action requires competent counsel; and for every kind, the risk classification in the package's metadata. For Tier 3 kinds it also lists the profile's legal organisations equipped to evaluate them, with their contacts (`jurisdictions` R32), or says the profile names none. It never includes a Tier 3 template or any counsel packet content. The evidence package is `publication`'s published case edition; this module supplies only this block, through the registration `public-read` offers (its R8, was `publication` R36; K651), composed by R21.

**availableActions({determination, viewer}) → the block of R15, or refusal**
- **R21** Answers R15's block for a live determination's counterparty offices, from the same composer R15's registration calls, so `escalation` (its R8) reads the available actions without reading a published case. `NO_SUCH_DETERMINATION` for an absent or invisible one, one answer (conformance's own refusal, passed through), and `DETERMINATION_UNREADABLE` while conformance cannot be read (K254).

**The Action layer (K608): the in-band stamp, communications, the override's disclosure, the venue's standard, the template library**
- **R22** (Publication §3 rule 9; BOB) Every filing draft's approved bytes (R6), every counsel-packet export (R11) and every communication's approved bytes (R23) carry `public-read.inbandQuartet` (its R7, was `publication` R16; K651) in-band: its hash over the bytes, the date, the author and both threshold floors. A draft not yet approved carries none. (T45; N883, N892 (d); Actions D16, D28d; v0.2 rules 14, 21) The stamp carries the group's public identity and declared stake, `public-read.groupFields()` (its R34) passed as `inbandQuartet`'s `group`; a joint text's body fingerprint (R36) is computed without them.
- **R23** (D2, K590) `communicationPrepare({action, text, purpose, preparer, viewer})` (`op=communicationprepare`): a draft message, briefing or statement for an action whose addressee is anyone (`actions` R9), stored apart and labelled as R5's drafts are (`proposalLabel(preparer, "communication")`), answered with `evidence: false`; any credential may prepare, a machine from the published case and the plan; no template is read. Refusals: `COMMUNICATION_NO_PREPARER`; `NO_SUCH_ACTION` (`actions.noSuchAction`); `ACTION_CLOSED`; `COMMUNICATION_TEXT_REFUSED` (empty, over the length bound, or not UTF-8 text); `COMMUNICATION_PURPOSE_REFUSED` (empty or over 500 characters). `filingApprove` (R6) and `filingRecordSent` (R7) apply to it unchanged: a member approves it, sends it by their own hand, and the sending is recorded with the bytes sent. Nothing is transmitted by the instance (R7); `filingsFor` (R13) lists it among the drafts, marked a communication. (T45; DESIGN §2.2; S1 §C 6) A communication is drafted from the findings and inquiries the member chooses (`from`), published or not, any the preparer may see (`NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` alike for one not seen); a request for comment's claims are assembled from them as a sourced list labelled machine work, each claim naming its finding. It never needs a published case. This replaces "a machine from the published case and the plan".
- **R24** (K600 (a)) A filing draft, counsel packet or communication prepared from an action carrying a `premise_override` (`actions` R8) carries, first on its face and in every export, "Rests on an unestablished premise:" with the override's reason, author and time. One prepared from an action without one carries none. (T45; N884; Actions D18; v0.2 rules 2, 3; K2780 (K600 (a) revised)) The disclosure names the override's ground, a legal deadline (its citation and date) or an imminent irreversible act (what and when), with the member's reason, as `actions` R74 holds it; every `disclosures` item `actions` R75 answers is printed first, in its order (R35).
- **R25** (K597 (3), K600 (b): the venue sets the standard) No draft, packet or communication is refused for the capture grade of what it rests on. Every filing draft and counsel packet shows, for each exhibit, its capture grade and whether it is co-attested (`provenance`); where the kind's profile entry states `evidence` (`jurisdictions` R39), it shows the venue's standard beside the grades, and flags an exhibit below the grades the venue `accepts`, or at a grade the profile marks `contestable`, so counsel and members can prepare. Where the profile states none, the venue's standard reads undetermined and the grades are shown alone.

**Templates, the member's own words, and the calendar** (K921, K922, K924; `build/plan/draft-filing-templates.md` §3, §5)
- **R28** `filingPrepare` (R1) takes at most one of `template: {id, version?}` and `text`. With `template`, the version is `filing-templates.offeredVersion` (its R25: absent `version`, the latest approved; a named `updated` version, which the answer says is updated, naming its successor; its refusals `NO_SUCH_TEMPLATE`, `TEMPLATE_RETIRED`, `TEMPLATE_NOT_OFFERED` passed through as its own); then `TEMPLATE_USE_BRIEF` (a `brief` template: it serves R31) and `TEMPLATE_KIND_MISMATCH` (C-115.39; a template for another kind). With `text`, the member's own wording, judged as a template's text (`filing-templates` R2: `TEXT_UNWRITABLE` for empty, over `FILING_TEXT_MAX` or not UTF-8, `TEMPLATE_BLANK_UNKNOWN` naming an unknown blank), its blanks filled as R3 says. Both given is `TEMPLATE_AND_TEXT`. Neither: the latest approved version of the profile's `use: file` template for the kind, when the profiles' view holds one (`filing-templates` R15); else `TEMPLATE_NOT_NAMED` (C-115.40, re-worded: name a template or write the words), its detail listing at most 20 offered `file` templates for the kind.
- **R29** Every filing draft records `template: {id, version, sha, origin}`, or `template: null` for the member's own words. A template naming profiles none of which gave the action's kind (its `profile` tag, `jurisdictions` R13) is used, and the draft says first, after any advisory, that the template was not written for that jurisdiction, naming it; a `general` template says nothing of it. `filingsFor` (R13) shows the template with the version's state, approver and reviews, and flags a draft whose version was later updated ("prepared from version <n>, since updated by version <m>") or whose template was retired (naming the reason). Nothing in the draft changes, and no approval is refused for it.
- **R30** R9's deadlines, and the clock entries R7's `proposed` carries, state the calendar's status as `action-clocks` R10 states it, read through `local-facts.factStatus` (its R2) for every holiday year a business count reads, on the value that governs on this instance. The reader is `action-clocks.factReader` (its R12), not a copy of it here. (K998; D6; K1038)
- **R31** (K924) `counselPacket` (R8) may take `template: {id, version?}`, a `use: brief` template (`TEMPLATE_USE_FILE` for a `file` one), its version and refusals as R28's (`offeredVersion`, `TEMPLATE_KIND_MISMATCH`). Without a template the packet has no `briefing` section.
- **R32** (K922 (1)) `templateSave({filing, template?, project?, name?, kind?, use?, profiles?, notes?, author, viewer})` (`op=templatesave`): a member starts a template draft from an approved filing draft (R6), or a draft of a new version of a named template, by handing the approved text and `from: {filing, sha}` to `filing-templates.templateDraft` (its R3) and answering its answer. Refusals in order: `NO_SUCH_FILING` (absent or invisible); `TEMPLATE_FROM_UNAPPROVED` (C-115.34, kept here: the draft is not approved); then `filing-templates`' refusals passed through as its own. (T51 fix round 2, C65; K4319) A new template's `project` is the one the request names, else the action's, else the project the draft's basis names; with none of the three, `filing-templates` refuses `TEMPLATE_SCOPE_REFUSED` (its R3).

**The Action layer at T45: the record check, disclosures, joint texts, documenting and the chance to fix, notices, quoting, templates after the act, second approval** (T45; K2780; N828, N858, N861, N880, N883, N884, N887, N890, N892 (b)–(d))
- **R34** (T45; Actions D2; v0.2 rule 3; reconciliation R7) **The record check.** Every filing draft, counsel packet `briefing` section and communication is checked sentence by sentence (one split, R43 reading the same) by `case-account`'s sentence judgment (as its R13 judges), against the findings of R35's three kinds and the group's own dated correspondence: `supported` (naming what supports it), `unsupported`, `contradicted` (`ACCOUNT_CONTRADICTED_BY_RECORD`, naming what contradicts it); this module classes `question` itself (a sentence that asks) and `own_doing` (a sentence the member marks as about the group's own doing). An unsupported sentence is pointed out, never refused; a question is never flagged. `filingApprove` (R6) refuses a text holding a `contradicted` sentence `CONTRADICTED_HELD` unless the approval names it with a reason (`release: [{ord, reason}]`), recorded with the approval. The check is re-run on the approved text; a judgment that throws reads each sentence undetermined, never supported. Control: without a contradicted sentence no `release` is asked.
- **R35** (T45; N828, N883; Actions D16a, D24, D28d; v0.2 rules 3, 21; reconciliation R7) Everything prepared from an action resting on findings checked for publication and held carries "Rests on findings checked for publication on {date}, not yet published." (`prepared.rests.held`); on another group's accepted findings, "Rests on findings of {group} (edition of {date}), accepted by us on {date}." (`prepared.rests.accepted`), with that group's public identity and declared stake (canon's words), each read from `actions` R75's `disclosures` (one source). One resting only on published findings carries neither. Control: each of the three kinds.
- **R36** (T45; N883; Actions D16; v0.2 rule 14) **Joint texts.** For an action with `partners` (`actions` R78), the approved text has a body fingerprint, SHA-256 over the text without R22's per-group fields. `jointSignatory({filing, organisation, body_sha, account, author, viewer})` (`op=jointsignatory`): a member records that a partner approved the same text in its own copy, by its own act (`account`, how it is known). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE`; `NO_SUCH_FILING`; `NOT_APPROVED`; `NOT_A_PARTNER`; `JOINT_FINGERPRINT_MISMATCH`. `filingsFor` (R13) lists each signatory; no shared plan is made. Control: a matching fingerprint from a listed partner lands; one character changed mismatches.
- **R37** (T45; N887; Actions D25; v0.2 rule 19) **Documenting an infraction.** `communicationPrepare` takes `purpose: "document_infraction"` with `audience` (`violating_party`, `members`, `oversight`, `public` or `broad`). Without AI its text is assembled from the record: the act and its office, the standard, the evidence for each point with its grade, and what is still uncertain, each part naming its source and an undetermined part said so; the assistant's draft (`agent-worker` R76's `infractiondraft`), where enabled, is a labelled proposal (R5). Each version is approved per R6. A version for `violating_party` is a request for comment: it carries R40's sentence and a time for review and response (a date with its basis, `action-clocks` R7). Control: assembled with AI off, each part sourced.
- **R38** (T45; N887, N892 (b); Actions D26, D28b; v0.2 rules 19, 21; K2781 (5)) **The chance to fix.** `purpose: "chance_to_fix"` drafts a notice stating what correcting the act and remedying its harm would look like under the standard, and a time: the law's period (a profile deadline, cited) or the group's window (`window` basis). Each sentence matching `action-grammar.pointOut` for `own_benefit_ask` (its R18) is pointed out, and R6 refuses approval `SELF_BENEFIT_ASK` until each is rewritten (no override; the list holds no remedy, K2781 (5)); the same holds for any demand.
