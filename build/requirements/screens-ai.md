## Public

### Purpose

The screens through which a team's administrators, a project's owners and each member choose, connect, order, limit, test and read about the AIs that serve the assistant: `aiproviders` (the team's), `aiconnect` (the guided connection), `aimine` (a member's own), `aiproject` (a project's), `aimoments` (what the assistant panel says when an AI cannot go on), `aitest` (testing an AI that is not listed) and `airesults` (what every tested AI has shown). They replace the alpha's `connect`, `teamai` and `projectai` (`screens-settings` retires its two; `screens-inquiry` its third). Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`; the screens decide nothing about who may or what is offered, which `ai-accounts`, `ai-catalogue`, `ai-gate`, `ai-use` and `ai-runs` answer.

### Provides

Terms. The **cascade** is project, else member, else team. An **account** is `ai-accounts`' record of one provider plan with its means (`gateway`, `sealed`, `reference`, `issued`, `none`); an **entry** is `ai-catalogue`'s model at a host; a **part** is one of the six: `ask`, `draft`, `read`, `transcribe`, `run`, `account`. Words say "team", never "group" (K3472); words say "limit", never "budget" (DEC-421).

**`SCREENS`** (the screen contract, `interface-shell` R21; each sentence read by key)

- **R1** (T53; DEC-420, DEC-465, DEC-471; K5457) **aiproviders: how the team's AI stands, and what is in use, in order.** An administrator's screen (a member who does not administer reads only that it is the administrators', and who they are). It leads with a card: the accounts that are on, in order, the spend against the limit, and the health. It draws "In use, in order": one row for each account of the team, giving provider, plan and type; what it sends where and how long it is kept; On or Off; health; the listing word (listed, watch, lapsed, retired) with its reason; Move up and Move down (`aiorderset`, which sends an explicit position, never a toggle), Switch off (`aiaccountswitch`) and Remove (`aiaccountremove`, drawn with its terminal weight, `op-grades`). Each team account carries an unticked "Projects and members may add this account to their own lists" (`aioptinset`), off until an administrator turns it on, with its note saying what the switch does. A note says that when the work continues on a later account the member is shown the move and can stop it, and that when that account has another payer the member is asked first. The card draws the not-ready and project-only states of `assistantreadiness` and keeps its own count of accounts on otherwise. Test: over a fixture of three accounts, the rows are in the list's order, Move up on the last sends `aiorderset` with position 2, Switch off sends `aiaccountswitch`, a flag that is off draws unticked, and a member's session draws only the notice; over `assistantreadiness` answering `not_ready` and `project_only` the card draws the matching state and draws none for `ready`. Control: a failed read is drawn `state.failed`, never as no accounts; no secret is drawn. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R2** (T53; DEC-420, DEC-465, DEC-468, DEC-469, DEC-475; K5459, K5480, K5487) **aiproviders: limits and alerts, use by member, the re-test switch, the health share.** "Limits and alerts" sets a limit and alert thresholds (`aibudgetset`) and shows spend against them. Under each account the team pays for it draws "Use by member, this month" (each member by name, with cost and runs, from `aiusebymember`) and says the team pays for the account so its administrators see each member's use, and that a member's own subscription is not shown here. "Re-test the AIs your team uses, each month" (`airetestset`) is on to begin with, with its note that while it is off a reminder stays in the Queue. For administrators, "Share daily counts of errors and refusals with Civicsmith" (`aihealthset`) is off until turned on, with its note of what would be sent, and "See exactly what would be sent" (`aihealthpreview`) draws, for each AI and day, the calls, refusals, no answer and errors by kind, and never content, members or accounts. Test: over a fixture, the by-member table draws each member with cost and runs and a subscription of a member's own is absent from it; the health share is off by default and sends `aihealthset` only on the administrator's act; the preview rows equal `aihealthpreview`'s answer and carry no member, account or text. Control: a member who does not administer is drawn none of it. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R3** (T53; DEC-420, DEC-465; K5430, K5431) **aiproviders: the seven checks, who may use what, and adding an AI.** The seven privacy and origin checks are each drawn "Required for the whole team" (locked for project owners and members) or left to them (`aicheckset`, recorded with a reason when a check is unticked); the origin check is an ordinary check (a model that fails it is an ordinary catalogue entry drawn with the origin "Not met" and "Not offered while your team requires the origin check", and the page has no standing "Not offered" category for origin). "What members may use" is drawn from the team's allowed set. "Add an AI" is a fold, provider first and then that provider's plans and types, each with scope, price and estimate, the checks' answers each with grade, source and date, and the parts it passed; entries not offered and entries held until a fact is confirmed are listed apart, each with its plain reason; and the changes are listed with who, when and at which level. Test: over a fixture catalogue, a provider's plans are grouped under it; a check ticked by the team is drawn locked to a project owner and to a member; an entry failing a required check is drawn as not offered with its reason; a changed check appears in the changes list with who and level. Control: a plan whose answer is open is drawn as unconfirmed, never as met. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R4** (T53; DEC-471, DEC-475; K5478, K5483, K5492; `ai-accounts` R68, R75) **aiproviders: an account stopped, and a list that is out of date.** The state the page draws when a call was charged to Cloudflare's own credits instead of the team's key, when the call was recorded by a gateway that is not the team's, and when the team's gateway log could not be read, each states the account and the time of the answer, says the answer was kept and that the account takes no more work until an administrator clears it, and offers "Clear it and turn the account back on" (`aicreditsclear`, recorded with name and time, no reason asked); the same sentence is the administrators' Queue item. When the catalogue could not be fetched since a date it says so and that the last list is offered. Test: over a fixture account flagged for each of the three causes, the matching sentence and the act are drawn and the act sends `aicreditsclear` with the account; a stale-catalogue answer draws its state. Control: an unflagged account draws none of them. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R5** (T53; DEC-420, DEC-465, DEC-476; K5429, K5493; `ai-accounts` R69, R73) **aiconnect: the guided connection, in six steps, without a tick box for terms.** The six steps are: what is being connected, with its sources; signing up with the provider (Civicsmith never signs up or pays for anyone); the key, in five ways (entered here and passed straight to Cloudflare AI Gateway's store, `aikeyenter`; entered by an administrator in the Cloudflare dashboard by numbered steps, `aikeycloudflare`; the provider's own sign-in, `aisignin`; a reference, `aikeyreference`; and "Keep it sealed in your team's Civicsmith", `aikeyseal`), a secret field being write-only and never drawn again; step 4, where the free-tier warning and its acknowledgement are drawn for a plan that carries one (`aitermsaccept`, recorded with name, date and the warning's version; "This plan carries no warning, so there is nothing to accept" otherwise) and no one is asked to accept terms, the provider's binding terms being a fold beside the act, "Read {provider}'s terms for this use", quoting the binding terms with page and date; a test (`aiconnecttest`, estimate shown first); and turning it on and placing it in order. A member's personal-subscription variant says the subscription is only ever on her own list (`aipersonalchoose`). A refusal `GATEWAY_UNAVAILABLE` names who can grant it and says that the built-in AI, a sealed key and a reference work without it. A dated change of the provider's terms is the notice `ai.notice.termschange` to whoever manages the account, with the same link. Test: the step-3 ways are drawn as five and each sends its owner's act with the secret in the body only; step 4 draws no tick box for terms and draws the fold with page and date; a plan with a warning needs its acknowledgement before step 5 and one without needs none; `GATEWAY_UNAVAILABLE` draws its key's words naming the hosting account's holder. Control: no secret is drawn after it is entered; a gateway account is refused where no gateway is set up, and a sealed account in the same copy is not. *(not yet met: T53; the dashboard's labels in step 3 wait on a live AI Gateway dashboard, U6, AIR45's live test L1)*
- **R6** (T53; DEC-420, DEC-465, DEC-468, DEC-471; K5459, K5477, K5479, K5486) **aimine: what serves a member, and her own accounts.** Every member's screen: what serves her now; the choices among what her team allows; her own accounts (`aiownadd`, on and off by `aimineuse`, ordered by `aiorderset` with `owner: "member"`, Move up and Move down); the checks in force for her, the ones her team locks marked (`aimycheckset` for those she may change; the sources of each in force from `checksInForce`); and her cost. An account of her own carries "Projects I've joined may add this account to their lists" (`aioptinset`, off to begin with); a personal subscription says it "is only ever on your own list, never on your team's or a project's", and that it does her team's work only with an AI on her team's allowed list. The screen says that her use on accounts her team or a project pays for is shown to their administrators or the project's owners, and that her own subscription's use is hers alone. Test: over a fixture, a locked check is drawn marked and not changeable; a personal subscription draws neither share flag; Move up sends `aiorderset` with `owner: "member"`; the use note names the payers' viewers. Control: another member's account is never drawn. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R7** (T53; DEC-420, DEC-468; K5459) **aiproject: a project's owner narrows.** A project's owner sets which accounts the project uses (`aiprojectuse`), their order (`aiprojectorder`), the checks that apply to the work done directly in the project and to that work only (`aiprojectcheckset`), and the project's limits (`aiprojectbudget`); the page says which team checks are locked. For an account the project pays for it draws the use by member, as `aiproviders` does. A project's use is drawn summed unless the viewer is an owner of the paying project. Test: over a fixture, a team-locked check is drawn locked; a check ticked here is said to apply only to work directly in this project; the by-member table is drawn to an owner and not to a participant. Control: a participant who does not own the project is drawn no act. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R8** (T53; DEC-420, DEC-465, DEC-469, DEC-471, DEC-475; K5477, K5488, K5489; `ai-runs`, `agent-model`) **aimoments: what the assistant panel says when an AI cannot go on.** The panel draws, for each moment, its plain choice and never a silent change: (a) the work continued on a later account of the same payer, shown, with "Stop here" only; (k) the next account has another payer, so the answer waits until the member chooses "Keep going on {provider}" or "Stop here", naming whose checks apply to the next account; disclosure before material first goes to a provider or host not yet disclosed, including after a switch, the step waiting; (d) a limit reached or the provider down, with "Wait for" 5 minutes, 15 minutes or 1 hour, or "Wait, then switch to {account} if it's still down" (`aiwaitswitch`); a model retired; a part the AI is not suited for, by name; a use the owner switched off, refused and never moved to another account; (j) the member stopping instead of switching; cost before a run and after it; and (l) "No estimate yet for {ai}: Civicsmith hasn't measured it", with "Start the run" and "Choose another account". Each moment is a state of the panel drawn from `ai-runs`' and `ai-accounts`' answers, one sentence by key. Test: for each moment, a fixture answer draws its sentence and exactly its choices, the wait lengths are the three above, and a continuation to another payer draws no automatic continuation; a use switched off draws a refusal and no move. Control: a run that stopped draws no choice that continues it; no sentence is drawn from a moment the answer does not name. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R9** (T53; DEC-420; K5457; `ai-gate`) **aitest: testing an AI that is not listed.** For a plan not in the catalogue the screen runs Civicsmith's locked test: `aitestsetup`; the estimate and the spend ceiling before the run (`aitestrun` asks them first); progress while it runs, per part; the results by part; exactly what the sealed results bundle would send, shown before sending (the plaintext manifest); and "Send" (`airesultsend`, drawn with its irreversible weight, a sent bundle cannot be recalled) or "Don't send" (`airesultsdecline`). A team's name is sent only if it agrees. Test: the estimate is drawn before the run starts; the manifest drawn equals the bundle's manifest; Send is drawn with the Irreversible weight; Don't send sends nothing. Control: no key or secret is in the manifest as drawn. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R10** (T53; DEC-420, DEC-475; K5482) **airesults: what every tested AI has shown.** The screen draws, for every AI teams have tested, those on the team's list and those not (the latter marked "Tested, not on your team's list"), and for each AI and part: whether it passed and its limits, its speed and the cost of standard jobs, who tested it (a team named only with consent) and when, and the grade; the combined figures "Combined from {n} results reported in the last six months; Civicsmith confirmed {confirmed} of them"; a result Civicsmith has not repeated marked "Reported by teams, not confirmed". The team's own result is drawn at the top at once ("Reported by your team, not yet confirmed"; until it is scored, "Preliminary figures from your test, until it is scored: {figures}", `airesultown`) with "Open your team's result". A result older than six months is not drawn. Test: over a fixture library, a tested AI that is not on the team's list is drawn marked, the counts equal the answer's, an unconfirmed result draws the "not confirmed" word, and a result of seven months ago is absent; the own-result row appears first. Control: a team that did not agree is not named. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R11** (T53; DEC-466; N1219, K5457; `ai-runs` R99 to R104) **Which AI did the work.** Each label on the assistant's work names the AI in parentheses after "the assistant" (`screen.machine.*.ai`, `{ai}` the model's display name, never the account), with the tip saying which AI did the work and that on a long job it can change. A finished run draws "How this run went" (`airunparts`): each part ("Part {n} · {ai} on {account} · {from} to {to}"), each switch with its reason, or the member's refusal to go on. Setup and `aiproviders` draw the assistant's ready state from `assistantreadiness` (`interface-shell` R20 owns the reachable rule). Test: a label over a fixture run draws the AI's display name and never an account name; a run with a hop draws two parts and the switch with its reason; a refused continuation draws the refusal. Control: a label whose code keeps its English in the alpha is unchanged. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R12** (T53; DEC-257, DEC-258; K3366) **Words by key.** This module holds no member-facing English: every sentence it draws is read through `setup-words` `word(key, vars)`, its keys the `reads` list for it in the design's `words-reads.json`; each refusal an owner mints is drawn by its `ai.refused.<code>` word. A requirement here that quotes a sentence identifies the key and binds no wording. Test: each key it reads is asserted with `hasWord`; over fixtures, what it draws for a sample equals `word(key, vars)`; no test holds a sentence's English. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R13** (T53; K5457) **After an act, the screen re-reads what it changes; nothing is drawn as done before the answer.** A refusal is drawn by its owner's key with who can fix it; a read that failed is drawn as failed (`state.failed`), never as none or as zero; an act the viewer may not take is not drawn. Every act of these screens is refused to a machine, and the three Worker-only ops (`aicredential`, `aigatewayserved`, `aicredentialfailed`) have no control here. Test: after `aiaccountswitch` the accounts are read again; a failed read draws `state.failed`. Control: the Worker-only ops are in no screen's act list. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R15** (T53; DEC-215 (1); N1026, K3138, K3198; `screens-settings` R26; `ai-use` R14; K5571 (6); owed line drafted by a worker for BOB, who reviews it) **An account's use over time is a drawer on each account the viewer owns.** On `aiproviders` (each account of the team, to an administrator), `aiproject` (each account the project pays for, to an owner of the project) and `aimine` (each account of the member's own), an account's row carries a closed drawer (the kit's fold, as `interface-shell` R139's) headed by a key owed by design. Opening it for the first time makes the one read `aiuseovertime` with that account's `owner`; nothing is read on load, so no screen's first wave holds it. It draws `screens-settings` R26's render over the answer, `aiUsageBars(cs, view, {owner, value})`: thirty bars of estimated dollars a day, oldest first, the newest marked as today (`ai.usage.today`), and a meter for each limit the answer's `periods` holds (or `ai.usage.nolimit`), by the keys R26 names (`ai.usage.*`); this module holds no render of its own. The figures are summed and name no member (K1450); "Use by member, this month" (R2) is the separate read `aiusebymember` and is not in the drawer. A viewer who does not own the account is drawn no drawer and no word of its use (`ai-use` R12's refusal is never drawn as zeros). An answer with `unreadable: true`, or a read that fails, is drawn `state.failed` inside the drawer, never as zero bars. Test: over a fixture, an administrator's team account draws a closed drawer and no `aiuseovertime` read is made until it is opened; opened, it draws thirty bars and the meters from the answer; the same drawer is drawn on `aiproject` to an owner and on `aimine` for a member's own account; a second opening makes no second read. Controls: a participant who does not own the project is drawn no drawer on `aiproject`; a member is drawn none on another member's account; an `unreadable` answer draws `state.failed`, never zeros; no drawer names a member. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R16** (T53; DEC-420, DEC-188 (1), N721 as `screens-inquiry` R37 and R60 held them for `projectai`; K5571 (6); owed line drafted by a worker for BOB, who reviews it) **`aiproject` opened by a member who is not in the project draws what `project` draws.** A hidden project's `aiproject`, opened by a member not in it, draws the `unavailable` state b with no form beneath; a findable project's draws its name with the ask to join (`projectrequest`), as `screens-inquiry` R60 states for `project`, and nothing else of the project; a member not yet joined to a project she may join is offered the join where she may (`projectjoin`), and the accounts line is never drawn as failed. It marks its subject read `subject: true`, and bare or not found draws only the bare state (`interface-shell` R61), and the title it draws is read from the record, never a record id (`screens-inquiry` R39). Test: a non-participant of a hidden project reads the unavailable state, of a findable one the name and the ask; a bare `aiproject` draws the bare state and no form; no heading matches `PROJ-`. Control: a participant is drawn the screen whole. *(not yet met: T53)*

## Private

### Uses

- `interface-shell`: the kit `cs` and the screen contract (its R21), the act gate and refusals (its R11 to R13, R19, R20).
