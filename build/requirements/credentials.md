## Public

### Purpose

Holds the credentials a person or the instance acts by: the founder's password and claim, members' passwords and sessions, the lock and the password asked again, recovery codes and reset links, the signer keys whose signatures the record accepts, the credentials AI work runs under (a run segment's included), the short-lived grant an ask reads under, the group's own keys for keyed outside services, and the count-only tally of refused requests administrators read as the group's security level. Who the members are, and what each may do, is `membership`'s; this module reads it through membership's services and never decides it. Which AI account serves an act is `ai-accounts`'.

### Provides

Terms as `membership` states them (administrators, `by` the control plane's stamp). Every refusal names its reason; no service throws.

**Sign-in and sessions**
- **R1** (was `membership` R1) `claim({password, tokenFp, source, country})` sets the founder's password once: `SIGN_IN_PAUSED` while R38's window holds it (asked first; F3); `PASSWORD_TOO_SHORT` under 12 characters; `ALREADY_CLAIMED` once claimed, unless `tokenFp` differs from the one recorded at the claim (the bootstrap credential was replaced), which re-arms the claim. Each refused claim counts toward R38's window and R44's tally.
- **R2** (was `membership` R72) `bootstrapState(tokenFp)` answers `{claimed, rearmed, consumedAt}`: `claimed` once the bootstrap credential is spent, unless `tokenFp` differs from the one recorded at the claim (`rearmed`, R1); `consumedAt` is the instant the instance was claimed, `null` when re-armed. It names nobody and returns no secret.
- **R3** (was `membership` R73) `setPassword({role, password})` stores a salted, derived hash for `role`, replacing any earlier one, and never the password; it is an in-plane call reached by no route (`enroll`'s setter, R20; `recover`, R47). (T37; N776, DEC-182 (4)) `passwordChange({current, password, by, session, source, country})` (`op=setpassword`) is a signed-in member's or administrator's change of their own password. The role is the role of the live session `session` names (R5), `by` and `session` the control plane's stamps; a `role` in the body is never read. Refusals, in order, each writing nothing but its count: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_PASSWORD` with its row when `by` is a machine credential or an operator token; `NOT_SIGNED_IN` (R39's row) when `session` names no live session; R38's window (`SIGN_IN_PAUSED`); `PASSWORD_TOO_SHORT` under 12 characters; `CURRENT_PASSWORD_WRONG` with its row when `current` does not match that role's stored password (R41's comparison), counted toward R38's window and R44's tally (`signin`). On success, in one act, the password is set as `setPassword` sets it and every other session of that role ends, with the ask grants minted under them (R27, R39); the presenting session stays. It answers `{ok: true, role, ended}`, never either password. Neither password is logged, stored, echoed or carried in any answer.
- **R4** (was `membership` R2) `login({role, password, ttlSeconds, source, country})` returns `{token, expires}` for a role holding a credential whose password matches (R41's comparison) and, for `member:<id>`, whose member is `active` (`membership.memberFacts`, its R68). Every other case (no credential, inactive member, wrong password) answers the one refusal `SIGN_IN_REFUSED` with one fixed detail, at the same cost, so a refusal never tells which roles exist. R38's window is asked first (`SIGN_IN_PAUSED`); each `SIGN_IN_REFUSED` counts toward it and toward R44's tally (`signin`); `source` and `country` are as R38 and R44 state.
- **R5** (was `membership` R3, its sessions half) `session(token)` returns `null` for an unknown or expired token, else `{role, expires, capabilities, administer, member, handle, rootOfTrust}`, the last five being `membership.sessionRights(role)` (its R92) read at that call, so a capability change or revocation takes effect on the next request.
- **R38** (F3; K1881) `claim` (R1), `login` (R4), `recover` (R47) and `passwordChange` (R3) are under one sign-in window, counted per source and per role. `source` is the caller's keyed fingerprint of the connecting address, made as `doorbell` R12 makes one (once `capture` R56) (never the address; a call with none counts under one shared source of its own); the role is the `role` asked (`admin` for `claim`), whether or not any credential is held under it (the session's role for `passwordChange`). For each source and each role, the refused attempts in any 10 minutes are estimated by a two-bucket sliding window (`prev × (1 − elapsed/W) + cur`, W 10 minutes; `doorbell` R2's form, once `capture` R31), counted in the same transaction as the refusal; at 10 or more for the source, or 10 or more for the role, the attempt is refused `SIGN_IN_PAUSED` before any password, code or claim is judged. `SIGN_IN_PAUSED` is one answer for every arm (either bucket full; a role held or not; a member active or not): `{ok: false, reason, code, check, translation, detail, stated}`, `stated` the bound composed from the same constants, at the same cost as R4's refusal (one password derivation), so neither its words nor its time tell which bucket was full or whether the role exists. A paused attempt counts toward neither window and is written to R44's tally as `rate`. A success counts toward neither and empties neither.
- **R39** (F14; K1881) `signOut({token})` ends the session `token` names (R5); `signOutEverywhere({token})` ends every session held under the role of the session `token` names, that one included. Each answers `{ok: true, ended}`, `ended` the number of sessions ended, and every ask grant minted under an ended session ends with it (R27). A token naming no live session (unknown, expired or already ended) is refused `NOT_SIGNED_IN` with its row, ending nothing, the same answer for each. From then on R5 answers `null` for every ended token. Neither ends another role's sessions or a standing question's grant (R32).

- **R8** (was `membership` R27) `signerList()` gives each key's row: `key_b64` (the public key), `member_id`, `comment`, `added` (when it was registered), `status`, `status_by`, `status_at` (R21), `member_status` and `attests`: true exactly when the key and its member are both `active`. Each key also states `origin` (`admin` for a key R6 registered, `self` for one R9 registered) and `registered_by`.
- **R9** (was `membership` R89) **signerRegisterOwn({keyB64, comment, by})** (`op=signerregister` (served also as `signerregisterown`, `op-declarations` R21's alias, the name the screens use)). A key `by` already holds and that is `active` answers `existed: true`, unchanged (its `origin` and `registered_by` stay as first registered); one `by` holds that is revoked is refused `SIGNER_KEY_REVOKED` (C-96.16) and stays revoked, since only an administrator re-activates a key (R7). A new key is `active`, with `origin: "self"` and `registered_by: by`. Every administrator is notified: the answer's `notified` names `membership.activeAdmins()` (its R86) at that instant, and the self-registration is read from the key's row (`origin`, `registered_by`, the instant) for the notice the feed delivers (N375). (Fix round 3, C51; K4364) *(not yet met: T51)* A registration that succeeds carries `timings: {total_ms, stages}`: the whole milliseconds the arm took and each stage that ran (`standing`, `lookup`, `write`, `notify`), the stages summing to no more than `total_ms`; a registration makes a bounded number of store calls (at most six) whatever the size of the team or of the roster. Refusals carry no `timings`.
- **R10** (was `membership` R90) **signerRevokeOwn({keyB64, by})** (`op=signerrevoke` (served also as `signerrevokeown`, `op-declarations` R21's alias, the name the screens use)). An administrator revokes any key by R7.
- **R21** (N505) Each key records `status_at`, the instant its `status` last changed: set when a key is first registered (R6, R9), and each time an act changes its status (R6's re-activation, R7, R10, R16). An act that leaves the status as it was (R6 on an `active` key, R7 setting the status it has, R9's `existed: true`) leaves `status_at` unchanged. A key registered before `status_at` was recorded holds null ("not recorded"; never back-filled, D-85), until an act changes its status.
- **R11** (was `membership` R70) `attestingKeys()` answers the signer keys that attest, the one predicate R8 states, for every reader that splices it today (`signerList`, the gate's facts, a case's document facts).

**AI credentials**
- **R12** (was `membership` R28) `aiCredentialMint({who, tokenId, secretSha, principalKind, principalMember, taskScope, writes, note, confinedTo, expiresInDays})`: `AI_CREDENTIAL_MINT_NOT_A_MEMBER` when `who` is absent or a machine; `AI_CREDENTIAL_PRINCIPAL_UNSTATED` unless `principalKind` is `organisation` or `member`; `AI_CREDENTIAL_IDENTITY_TAKEN` for an empty or used `tokenId`. Records the credential with `minted_by` = `who`; never stores a secret, only its hash. `expiresInDays` is R42's.
- **R13** (was `membership` R62) An organisation-scoped credential (`principalKind: organisation`) is minted only by an active administrator (the founder included; `membership.isAdministrator`, its R64); anyone else is refused `NOT_AN_ADMIN` (`membership` R84), its `remedy` naming the member-scoped credential open to every member.
- **R14** (was `membership` R29) A member-scoped credential's principal is `who` itself; naming another member is refused.
- **R15** (was `membership` R30) `aiCredentialRevoke({who, tokenId})`: refuses a machine or absent `who` and an unknown id; revoking twice answers `already: true`. `aiCredentialLook({secretSha})` and `aiCredentials({limit})` never return the secret; the list is capped (default 200, at most 500) with a measured `truncated`. Every credential they answer carries `expiresAt` and `expired` (R42).

- **R42** (F15; K1881) An agent credential expires. `aiCredentialMint` (R12) takes an optional `expiresInDays`, a whole number from 1 to 365, 90 when not given; anything else is refused `AI_CREDENTIAL_BAD_EXPIRY` with its row, writing nothing. The credential records `expiresAt`, `expiresInDays` whole days after it was minted, and every answer showing a credential (R12, R15's `aiCredentialRevoke`, `aiCredentialLook` and `aiCredentials`) carries `expiresAt` and `expired`, true from `expiresAt` on. An expired credential is no longer live: `aiCredentialLook` answers it with `expired: true`, as it answers a revoked one with `revoked: true`, for the gate to refuse (`admission` R10); it is never renewed, and a new mint replaces it. A credential minted before expiry was recorded is given `expiresAt` 90 days after the migration that records it.
- **R53** (N761; K2129) This module's ops map takes `aicredentialmint`'s `secretSha` only from the internal request's body (the control plane sets it there after removing any a caller sent, `control-plane`'s N761 requirement), never from the request's query: a `secretSha` in the query is never read. R12 then refuses a `secretSha` that is not 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters `AI_CREDENTIAL_NO_SECRET` with its row (the next free C-29 number, C-29.33), writing nothing, so a mint that carries no digest never records a credential no lookup can find.

- **R63** (T47; N955; K2976, K2999, K3027; `ai-runs` R18, `control-plane` R77) `aiRunCredentialMint({member, run, writes})` mints the `ai` credential one segment of a member's own run is dispatched under, so no credential is ever minted by or held in the member's page. It is in-plane only: reached by no route, answered to no viewer, called only by `ai-runs` when it dispatches a segment of a run `member` opened (`dispatchMemberRun`, at her act). The credential is member-scoped (`principalKind: member`, its principal `member`, R14), minted by the plane at her act and recorded as R12 records one (`minted_by` the member; only its token's SHA-256 kept), with `run` recorded on its row: the run it is bound to. Its `writes` are the given list, each one of `AI_RUN_WRITES` (exported, frozen): the write ops the runner makes on a run in any deployed mode: `check`'s (`agent-worker` R37: `airuntick`, `suggest`, `capturerequest`, `airunclose`) and `plan`'s (`agent-worker` R53: `optionpropose`, with `airuntick` and `airunclose`); a mode deployed later adds its writes here in its own entry; `ai-runs` passes the run's mode's own writes (K3033). It expires `AI_RUN_CREDENTIAL_TTL_SECONDS` after minting (exported; under one day, the job's value), never renewed. It answers `{ok: true, tokenId, token, run, writes, expiresAt}` once; the token is never answered again, logged or stored. Refusals, each minting nothing, in order: `member` not `active` (`membership.memberFacts`) `ACCOUNT_MEMBER_NOT_ACTIVE`; `run` not a non-empty string, or `writes` not a non-empty list of `AI_RUN_WRITES` entries, `AI_RUN_CREDENTIAL_MALFORMED` (a new row, the next free C-29 number after `ai-accounts` R23's, naming what); the group's keep-away (`ai-accounts` R7's `aiKeptAway()`) `AI_KEPT_AWAY`, as R27 is refused. It is revoked, with nothing else changed: at the next segment (a new mint for the same `run` revokes every earlier credential of that run, so at most one is live per run); at the run's end, by `aiRunCredentialsEnd({run})`, the in-plane call `ai-runs` makes when the run leaves `running` (its R14), answering `{ok: true, revoked}` and never throwing; at the member's revocation (R16); and when the member disconnects her sign-in (`ai-accounts` R18's `subscriptionDisconnect`, or its clearing at revocation). `aiCredentialLook` (R15) answers such a credential with its `run`, `writes`, `expiresAt` and `expired`, so the gate (`admission` R10) refuses it once expired or revoked and admits it only for its own run and listed writes; `aiCredentials` lists it with its `run`. (T48; K3101) Its keep-away is `ai-accounts` R7's `aiKeptAway` and its `ACCOUNT_MEMBER_NOT_ACTIVE` is answered through `ai-accounts` R25's `accountMemberNotActive`; its revocation at disconnection comes through R73.
