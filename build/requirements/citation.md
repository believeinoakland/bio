## Public

### Purpose

Citing is one act in the record's own terms, "this is why I think that": material, or a question, becomes part of what a case or a question rests on. On a case (a `project`) a citation is a `cites` edge in the case's `references[]`; on a question (an `inquiry`) it is a leg of the question's `basis[]`, with the target in its references too. This module holds that act, its withdrawal and restoration on a case (sever, reinstate: status changes, never deletions), and the one rule of what may be cited now: nothing the group has retired. A question may also rest on another team's accepted finding at a named edition (R16), and take that leg back (R17). It judges nothing about a leg's grammar, its grade or the basis graph: it composes legs, and `inquiry` judges them at the write, as it judges every other leg.

### Provides

Terms. The **citing object** is the bundle a citation is written into, named by the parameter `project` whether it is a case or a question (legacy `focus` and `problem` spellings are read through `normalizeType` as a question). A **selection** is `retrieval`'s (its R19–R20), named by `handle`. An **edge** is a `references[]` entry `{rel: "cites", target, status, note, extent_capture?}` with status `proposed`, `confirmed` or `severed`. A **leg** and its **role** are `inquiry`'s terms (its `BASIS_ROLES`: `supports`, `cuts_against`). An **imported leg** is a leg on an imported finding reference (`inquiry-grammar` R11: target `imported:<import>/<finding>`, a positive `target_edition`, no grade, axis, source or part, not in `references[]`). A **part** is a leg's extent fields (`extent_*`) or `content_id`, in `content`'s extent grammar. A viewer and an identity are the control plane's stamps, read through `membership`. Every refusal names a `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check` and `translation`, and one about members names every offender (`offenders`, sorted) and never narrows the call to the rest.

**cite({project, handle, found, question, viewer, owner, note, author, role, extent, identity})** (`op=cite`; `found` and `question` since T35)
- **R1** Refusals in order: the selection at weight `report` (`retrieval` R19); an absent or invisible citing object is `membership.noSuchProject`'s answer (its R78), after its existence answer (R77); `NOT_A_PROJECT` for one neither a project nor an inquiry; a project needs the actor joined (`membership.projectAuthority`); `BAD_NOTE` (C-33.15: over 200 characters, or a quote, backslash or newline); on an inquiry `NO_ROLE` (C-33.16) and `BAD_ROLE` (C-33.17), each carrying the role vocabulary, on a project `ROLE_NOT_APPLICABLE` (C-33.18); `NOT_CITABLE` (inquiry) or `NOT_INFORMATION` (project) when any member is neither information nor an inquiry, with `citable`; `RETIRED_NOT_CITABLE` (C-33.39, by R5); an unreadable citing document `NO_BUNDLE_MD` or `UNPARSEABLE_FRONTMATTER`; `SEVERED_EDGE` (C-33.19) for a member the citing object holds a `severed` edge to; the part: an unknown field `UNKNOWN_EXTENT_FIELD` (C-45.7, listing the fields taken), a part on a project `EXTENT_NOT_APPLICABLE` (C-45.8), a part with more than one new member `EXTENT_ON_MANY` (C-45.9), an unwritable value `BAD_EXTENT_VALUE` (C-45.10: over 200 characters, or a quote, backslash, newline or `#`), then the part judged by `inquiry.checkLegExtentGrammar` (`BASIS_REFUSED`, with each finding); `EMPTY_SELECTION`; a document block that cannot be extended in place `UNSPLICEABLE_REFERENCES` or `UNSPLICEABLE_BASIS`; past 1 MiB `CITATION_TOO_LARGE` with how many would fit. A refusal from the write (`inquiry` R11: `BASIS_REFUSED`, `SELF_BASIS`, `BASIS_CYCLE`; (T42) `inquiry` R62's and `run-productions` R25's `PROPOSAL_NOT_TAKEN_UP`, `MACHINE_PASSAGE_UNCHECKED`) is answered unchanged, with the handle and drift. (T35; N698, DEC-164 (4), (5)) `found` is one match as `retrieval.findIn` answers it (T35-37), read for its document, the capture it was found in and its extent only, never its words: it stands in place of `handle` and `extent`, its document the one member cited and its extent that member's part, each judged by every refusal above that judges a member or a part, at `report` weight (R7). Refused first, each writing nothing: both `handle` and `found`, or neither, `CITE_ONE_SOURCE`; a `found` naming no document, capture or extent `FOUND_MALFORMED`; a match whose capture is not the one the leg would be pinned to (`content.captureFor`, R2) `FOUND_CAPTURE_MOVED`, naming both, so no part is written against bytes it was not found in. On a project a found extent is a part, refused `EXTENT_NOT_APPLICABLE` (C-45.8) as any part is. `question`, optional, names the question the member looked for it for: an inquiry the viewer may see, else `NO_SUCH_QUESTION` (an absent and an unseen one answered alike) or `NOT_AN_INQUIRY`; it changes no other judgement. Each new code carries its row.
- **R2** On an inquiry each new member becomes a leg with the member's role; its connection grade is filled from `inquiry.earned` (its R13) when earned (A–C, axis `connection`, source `resolution`), else it carries none; a document leg naming no content row is pinned to `content.captureFor`'s capture (REC-220); its target joins `references[]` unless already there under any relation. On a project each becomes a `confirmed` `cites` edge, a document's pinned to its capture (REC-219). The note is written on each. A member is already cited when the inquiry already holds a leg on it, or the project already holds an edge to it: such members are reported, not rewritten; with nothing new the answer is `ok`, `cited` empty, nothing is written, and when a part was named it says the part was written nowhere.
- **R3** Anything written moves `last_updated` and appends one Session Log entry naming the author, the selection, at most 20 ids and a count of the rest, the note, whether the set had moved since it was made, and on an inquiry the role and how many legs were graded and how many left undetermined. The answer carries `cited`, `alreadyCited`, `drift`, `moved`, `bundleSha`, `rowVersion`, the gate and its expiry; on a project `pinned_captures` (each target's pin or null); on an inquiry `citingObjectType`, `role`, `gradesFilled`, `gradesUndetermined` and per leg its target, role, grade, axis, source, `why`, `pinned_capture` and, when a part was named, its extent (and content id) as written. (T35; DEC-164 (4), (5)) With `found`, the Session Log entry names the match's document in place of a selection and says the part was found by search; `drift` and `moved` answer false and `handle` null. With `question`, the entry names it and the answer carries `question`; without, the answer's `question` is null.

**sever({project, handle, viewer, owner, reason, author, identity}), reinstate({…same})** (`op=sever`, `op=reinstate`)
- **R4** Both act on a project's `cites` edges, and (T47, R15) on an inquiry's legs, at weight `refuse` (`retrieval` R20). Refusals in order: the selection; `NO_SUCH_PROJECT` as R1; `NOT_A_PROJECT` (for a citing object neither a project nor, since T47, an inquiry); on a project the actor joined; `NO_REASON`, `BAD_REASON` (over 160 characters, or a quote, backslash or newline); `EMPTY_SELECTION`; `NOT_INFORMATION` for a member neither information nor an inquiry; reinstating a member R5 answers true `RETIRED_NOT_CITABLE` (C-33.39); `NO_BUNDLE_MD`, `UNPARSEABLE_FRONTMATTER`; every member's edge must be in the source state, `confirmed` or `proposed` to sever and `severed` to reinstate (`NOT_CITED`, `NOT_SEVERED`), whole call; `UNSPLICEABLE_REFERENCES`; `CITATION_TOO_LARGE`. The edge's target and relation stay; only its status moves, and the reason is appended to its note with the act and time (at most 480 characters, oldest dropped). One Session Log entry records the act, count, ids (as R3) and reason. The answer carries `severed` or `reinstated` (the ids), the reason as `why` (never `reason`), `from`, `to`, `drift`, `moved`, `bundleSha`, `rowVersion` and the gate.

- **R15** (T47; N977; K3003; SCREENS-INQUIRY #1 J4 (4); amends R4) On an inquiry, `sever` and `reinstate` act on its own legs: each selected member must be the target of a leg of the inquiry's `basis[]`, else `NOT_CITED` (sever) or `NOT_SEVERED` (reinstate), whole call, as R4's source-state test; the status moved is that of the inquiry's `references[]` entry naming the leg's target (R2 joins it there; a leg is severed exactly when that entry is `severed`, the one reading `connections.edgeSevered` and `leg-earning`'s `restsOnLive` already make, whatever the entry's relation), from `confirmed` or `proposed` to `severed` or back. The leg itself stays in `basis[]` unchanged (role, grade, part, pin): nothing is deleted, as on a project. The actor-joined test is not asked (a question is shared; R1 asks none on an inquiry), and sight is the inquiry's (R9's rule: an inquiry the viewer may not see answers as an absent one, `NO_SUCH_PROJECT`'s answer, R1). Every other refusal, the Session Log entry, the note's reason (480 characters) and the answer are R4's, the answer also naming `citingObjectType: "inquiry"`. A `divided` or case-frozen inquiry (one whose document cannot be revised) is refused as any write to it is (`inquiry` R11, answered unchanged as R1 relays it). Test: a leg severed on an inquiry then reads `severed` in `restsOnLive`, and reinstated reads `confirmed`; control: the project arm unchanged.
