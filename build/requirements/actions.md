## Public

### Purpose

The Action object (State Rules v1.5 §4.4): what the group sends or does outside the system, to whom, why, under which laws, at what legal exposure, by when, and what came back. It holds the action's kind, risk tier, counterparty, clock (every deadline with the statute, order or commitment it comes from), basis legs, lifecycle and correspondence ledger; the governing laws of a request and the tier as members' authored acts, with machine proposals stored apart; fee quotes and the records-request lifecycle as evidence; and the action's own outcome (DEC-14). A member decides every move; a machine prepares and never advances, testifies, sets a tier or states a law.

### Provides

Terms. An **action** is a bundle whose type (by its document or its envelope, D-505) normalises to `action`. Its document carries `action_kind`, `risk_tier` (1, 2, 3 or `undetermined`; absent reads `undetermined`), `counterparty`, `clock[]` (`{text, description, date, basis, status}`, status `pending`, `met`, `overdue`, `waived`), `action_basis[]` (`{target, kind, note?, date?, extent_capture?}`, kind `rests_on` or `advances`), `correspondence[]`, `governing_laws[]` with `governing_laws_by`/`_at`, `risk_tier_history[]`, `resolution`, `law` (a `records_request` only) and `consequence` (**the action's own outcome**, DEC-14; never the breach consequence `consequences` holds). The lifecycle is the catalogue's: `planned → active | abandoned`; `active → awaiting_response | resolved | abandoned`; `awaiting_response → active | resolved | abandoned`; `resolved` and `abandoned` are final. A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, ...}`; a catalogue-backed one also carries `code`, `check` and `translation`.

**At the write: a check and a projection registered with `promotion` (its R39, K31)**
- **R1** A creation or revision of an action, not a replay, is refused before any write: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_RISK_TIER` (C-32.19) when a machine or unstamped author states a tier on a creation, changes a held tier, or drops a member's 1, 2 or 3 (carrying it forward, or leaving an unset tier unset, lands); `RISK_TIER_REWRITTEN` (C-90.1) when a revision changes `risk_tier` or `risk_tier_history`, or a creation states a history, other than through R24's act; `ACTION_BASIS_REFUSED` (C-2.10) for a malformed leg, a leg onto an action, a lead or a theme, a `request_for_comment` naming no inquiry as an `advances` leg or no clock entry (DEC-13), or a leg whose target the record does not hold; `CORRESPONDENCE_REFUSED` (C-2.10) for an entry failing R14's shape rules or whose `artifact_sha` the register does not hold; `RESPONDS_TO_REFUSED` (C-6.1) for a `responds_to` reference that is malformed or names a bundle the record does not hold. Each carries the findings with their check ids and repairs.
- **R2** A creation or revision of an action, replay included, whose governing-laws block (`governing_laws`, `governing_laws_by`, `governing_laws_at`; `[]` and absent are one state) differs from the held version's, other than through R18's act, is refused `GOVERNING_LAWS_REWRITTEN` (C-73.1).
- **R3** In the promotion's transaction, for a bundle whose document's type is `action`, the action's legs, ledger entries and quotes are replaced whole from the document: one leg row per `action_basis` entry (target, its type from the id prefix, kind, note, the document's own date), one ledger row per `correspondence` entry (the captured reply's bundle resolved from the register), and one quote row per received entry whose quote passes C-72.1–C-72.5, carrying the counterparty's name when it is `named` and null otherwise. A malformed replayed entry is skipped, never half-written. No row carries a server time except the entry's own `recorded_at`.
- **R4** `action_kind: records_request` carries `law`, the citation it is made under (at most 200 characters), stated or absent (absent reads undetermined). `law` on any other kind is refused `RECORDS_LAW_REFUSED` (R6). An action already written with a kind no longer offered reads byte-identically, its kind as written. *(not yet met: REC-201)*
- **R5** A machine or unstamped author that creates a `records_request` stating `law`, or changes `law`, is refused `MACHINE_CANNOT_STATE_RECORDS_LAW` (C-32.20); it may propose one (R28's shape). An action written before this fence by a machine credential reads its kind's law as MACHINE-STATED, from its recorded author class, and is never rewritten. *(not yet met: D-689)*
- **R6** C-2.10's law arm (R4) is enforced at the write, not only by the audit: `RECORDS_LAW_REFUSED` (C-73.6), carrying the findings. *(not yet met: D-695)*
- **R7** Five C-2.10 arms are enforced at the write (C-101): a kind outside R10's set; a tier outside the vocabulary; a counterparty that is a bare string, the placeholder "to be named", or incoherent (R9); `resolved` with no resolution from `complied`, `denied`, `escalated`, `withdrawn`; a clock entry without the `{text, description}` shape, a `YYYY-MM-DD` date, a basis, or a status from the four. A missing counterparty block, and a pending entry past its date, still land and are reported by the audit (R37). *(not yet met: D-717)*
- **R8** An action whose document states `breach: true` (one recorded for an escalation's stage 2, 5 or 7, `escalation` R9) rests on a conformance determination: it carries at least one `rests_on` leg naming a live determination (`conformance.determinationRead`) the author may see, else `ACTION_NO_DETERMINATION`; a superseded determination is refused `DETERMINATION_SUPERSEDED`. A leg's `rests_on` target may be a determination, an information item or an inquiry; an action is never a leg's target. *(not yet met: layer 9's contract, "an action rests on a conformance determination"; today any `INFO`, `INQ`, `PROB` or `FOCUS` bundle is accepted; K102: the rule binds breach actions only, and an action that gathers or tests evidence, such as a records request or a request for comment, keeps resting on inquiries and information)*
- **R9** `counterparty` is `{state: named, role, body, level?, entity_id?}` or `{state: undetermined, basis}`: an office named by official role and body, never a person (`jurisdictions` R24). Named with no role or body, undetermined with no basis, undetermined carrying a role, body or entity id, the placeholder anywhere, or an `entity_id` not of the registry's `ENT-YYYY-NNNN` form or naming a person, is refused (C-2.10, R7). A counterparty written before as `{state: named, name}` reads as written, its `name` as the office's. *(not yet met: layer 9 and `jurisdictions` R24; only `{state, name, entity_id?, basis}` exists today, D-130)*
- **R10** The kinds are the product's own, whose rules this module enforces (`records_request`, `request_for_comment`, `other`), and the `action_kinds` of the active profiles' combined view (`jurisdictions` R25, R27). A creation stating any other kind is refused `ACTION_KIND_UNKNOWN`; with no profile active, only the product's kinds are offered. No kind, law, office or deadline is written in this module's code. *(not yet met: `build/layers.md`, "No jurisdiction in the product"; `ACTION_KINDS` names a state's records law and two local kinds today; K102)*
- **R11** A leg onto a document is stamped, at the write, with the capture the record then presents for it (`extent_capture`); a later capture of the document does not change what the leg resolves to. A leg without one reads "version undetermined" and is never back-filled. *(not yet met: D-579)*

**The facts `retrieval` projects (its projection registration, K75 (2))**
- **R12** `actionFacts(documentText, nowMs) → {kind, risk_tier, counterparty_state, resolution, clock_next, clock_overdue}` is pure, and all null for a document whose type is not `action` or that does not parse. `risk_tier` is 1, 2 or 3, or null when undetermined. `clock_next` is the earliest `date` among `pending` clock entries with a `YYYY-MM-DD` date; `clock_overdue` is true exactly when `clock_next` is before the UTC calendar day of `nowMs` (a deadline is met by anything on its day).

### Invariants

- **R33** A machine credential never moves an action, records correspondence, sets or changes a tier, states the governing laws or a records law, or adds, removes or re-dates a clock entry; it may propose a tier, laws and a clock entry, each stored apart and labelled (DEC-24, DEC-49). The one machine write to a clock is the mechanical `deadline-recheck` (C-20.1's field set), which may move a `pending` entry whose date has passed to `overdue` and nothing else; any other status change it carries is refused `CLOCK_STATUS_NOT_MECHANICAL` at the write (I-11). *(not yet met for the direction rule: C-20.1 bounds the fields, not the values)*
- **R34** The correspondence ledger and the tier history are append-only; each entry is either captured bytes the register holds or a named member's account, never both and never neither (DEC-13); a non-response is recorded with its date.
- **R35** Every deadline carries the statute, order or commitment it comes from; no date is computed into the record; overdue is derived at read time, and a stored `overdue` status (I-11's mark, R33) is reported beside the derivation, never in place of it (Functional Architecture Layer 3 Function 5; Operational Principle 3).
