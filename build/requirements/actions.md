## Public

### Purpose

The Action object (State Rules v1.5 §4.4): what the group sends or does outside the system, to whom, why, under which laws, at what legal exposure, by when, and what came back. It holds the action's kind, risk tier, counterparty, clock (every deadline with the statute, order or commitment it comes from), basis legs, lifecycle and correspondence ledger; the governing laws of a request and the tier as members' authored acts (the proposals stored apart, and the receipts, scopes and promises recorded beside an action, are `action-records`', which this module reads into the action's block); fee quotes and the records-request lifecycle as evidence; and the action's own outcome (DEC-14). The clock's reads across actions, the entries proposed from the profile's deadlines and the reminders members ask for are `action-clocks`' (K617). A member decides every move; a machine prepares and never advances, testifies, sets a tier or states a law.

### Provides

Terms. An **action** is a bundle whose type (by its document or its envelope, D-505) normalises to `action`. Its document carries `action_kind`, `risk_tier` (1, 2, 3 or `undetermined`; absent reads `undetermined`), `counterparty`, `clock[]` (`{text, description, date, basis, status}`, status `pending`, `met`, `overdue`, `waived`), `action_basis[]` (`{target, kind, note?, date?, extent_capture?}`, kind `rests_on` or `advances`), `correspondence[]`, `governing_laws[]` with `governing_laws_by`/`_at`, `risk_tier_history[]`, `resolution`, `law` (a `records_request` only) and `consequence` (**the action's own outcome**, DEC-14; never the breach consequence `consequences` holds). The lifecycle is the catalogue's: `planned → active | abandoned`; `active → awaiting_response | resolved | abandoned`; `awaiting_response → active | resolved | abandoned`; `resolved` and `abandoned` are final. A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, ...}`; a catalogue-backed one also carries `code`, `check` and `translation`.
