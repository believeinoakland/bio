## Public

### Purpose

The Action object (State Rules v1.5 §4.4): what the group sends or does outside the system, to whom, why, under which laws, at what legal exposure, by when, and what came back. It holds the action's kind, risk tier, counterparty, clock (every deadline with the statute, order or commitment it comes from), basis legs, lifecycle and correspondence ledger; the governing laws of a request and the tier as members' authored acts, with machine proposals stored apart; fee quotes and the records-request lifecycle as evidence; and the action's own outcome (DEC-14). The clock's reads across actions, the entries proposed from the profile's deadlines and the reminders members ask for are `action-clocks`' (K617). A member decides every move; a machine prepares and never advances, testifies, sets a tier or states a law.

### Provides

Terms. An **action** is a bundle whose type (by its document or its envelope, D-505) normalises to `action`. Its document carries `action_kind`, `risk_tier` (1, 2, 3 or `undetermined`; absent reads `undetermined`), `counterparty`, `clock[]` (`{text, description, date, basis, status}`, status `pending`, `met`, `overdue`, `waived`), `action_basis[]` (`{target, kind, note?, date?, extent_capture?}`, kind `rests_on` or `advances`), `correspondence[]`, `governing_laws[]` with `governing_laws_by`/`_at`, `risk_tier_history[]`, `resolution`, `law` (a `records_request` only) and `consequence` (**the action's own outcome**, DEC-14; never the breach consequence `consequences` holds). The lifecycle is the catalogue's: `planned → active | abandoned`; `active → awaiting_response | resolved | abandoned`; `awaiting_response → active | resolved | abandoned`; `resolved` and `abandoned` are final. A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, ...}`; a catalogue-backed one also carries `code`, `check` and `translation`.

**At the write: a check and a projection registered with `promotion` (its R39, K31)**
- **R1** A creation or revision of an action, not a replay, is refused before any write: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_RISK_TIER` (C-32.19) when a machine or unstamped author states a tier on a creation, changes a held tier, or drops a member's 1, 2 or 3 (carrying it forward, or leaving an unset tier unset, lands); `RISK_TIER_REWRITTEN` (C-90.1) when a revision changes `risk_tier` or `risk_tier_history`, or a creation states a history, other than through R24's act; `ACTION_BASIS_REFUSED` (C-2.10) for a malformed leg, a leg onto an action, a lead or a theme, a `request_for_comment` naming no inquiry as an `advances` leg or no clock entry (DEC-13), or a leg whose target the record does not hold; `CORRESPONDENCE_REFUSED` (C-2.10) for an entry failing R14's shape rules or whose `artifact_sha` the register does not hold; `RESPONDS_TO_REFUSED` (C-6.1) for a `responds_to` reference that is malformed or names a bundle the record does not hold. Each carries the findings with their check ids and repairs.
- **R2** A creation or revision of an action, replay included, whose governing-laws block (`governing_laws`, `governing_laws_by`, `governing_laws_at`; `[]` and absent are one state) differs from the held version's, other than through R18's act, is refused `GOVERNING_LAWS_REWRITTEN` (C-73.1).
- **R3** In the promotion's transaction, for a bundle whose document's type is `action`, the action's legs, ledger entries and quotes are replaced whole from the document: one leg row per `action_basis` entry (target, its type from the id prefix, kind, note, the document's own date), one ledger row per `correspondence` entry (the captured reply's bundle resolved from the register), and one quote row per received entry whose quote passes C-72.1–C-72.5, carrying the counterparty's name when it is `named` and null otherwise. A malformed replayed entry is skipped, never half-written. No row carries a server time except the entry's own `recorded_at`. (N237, N277; K351) The projection reads at most 500 `action_basis` and 500 `correspondence` entries: an action document holding more is refused where it is authored or revised, `ACTION_TOO_LARGE` with the count and the limit, a row in this module's table, and a replayed document over the limit is skipped whole, never half-projected.
- **R4** *(retired: moved to `action-grammar` R3, K617, K653 BOB-2)*
- **R5** A machine or unstamped author that creates a `records_request` stating `law`, or changes `law`, is refused `MACHINE_CANNOT_STATE_RECORDS_LAW` (C-32.20); it may propose one (R28's shape). An action written before this fence by a machine credential reads its kind's law as MACHINE-STATED, from its recorded author class, and is never rewritten.
- **R6** C-2.10's law arm (`action-grammar` R3) is enforced at the write, not only by the audit: `RECORDS_LAW_REFUSED` (C-73.6), carrying the findings.
- **R7** Five C-2.10 arms are enforced at the write (C-101): a kind outside R10's set; a tier outside the vocabulary; a counterparty (the addressee, R9) that is a bare string, the placeholder "to be named", or incoherent (R9); `resolved` with no resolution from `complied`, `denied`, `escalated`, `withdrawn`, `completed`; a clock entry without the `{text, description}` shape, a `YYYY-MM-DD` date, a basis, or a status from the four. `completed` is allowed on any kind: the action was carried out and no counterparty's answer decides it; the action's own outcome (R26, DEC-14) says what happened, and an impact claim still needs a leg outside the group's own actions. A missing counterparty block, and a pending entry past its date, still land and are reported by the audit (R37).
- **R8** An action whose document states `breach: true` (one recorded for an escalation's stage 2, 5 or 7, `escalation` R9) rests on a conformance determination: it carries at least one `rests_on` leg naming a live determination (`conformance.determinationRead`) the author may see, else `ACTION_NO_DETERMINATION`; a superseded determination is refused `DETERMINATION_SUPERSEDED` (`conformance.determinationSuperseded`, its R20; N312). Neither refusal applies when the document states `premise_override: {reason}` (reason at most 500 characters, R14's text rule) on the creation or revision that first sets `breach: true`, authored by a member: a machine or unstamped author stating or changing one is refused `MACHINE_CANNOT_OVERRIDE`. The override is stamped with who and when, never edited or removed (`PREMISE_OVERRIDE_REWRITTEN`), shown in the read (R25), and carried as a disclosure on everything prepared from the action (`filings` R24); an overridden action is never attached to an escalation (`escalation` R23). An action that seeks evidence (no `breach: true`) is never asked. A leg's `rests_on` target may be a determination, an information item or an inquiry; an action is never a leg's target. (T45; N828, N883, N884; Actions D3, D18, D24; v0.2 rule 2; K2781 (4)) (a) The determination a breach action rests on is live **and noncompliant** for at least one standard it names; a live determination with no `noncompliant` outcome is refused `DETERMINATION_NOT_NONCOMPLIANT`, asked after `DETERMINATION_SUPERSEDED`. (b) A void determination (`conformance` R33) is refused through `conformance.determinationVoided`. (c) The override is `{ground, reason}` (R74). (d) This replaces "an overridden action is never attached to an escalation": an overridden action may later gain, by a member's revision, a `rests_on` leg on a live noncompliant determination; the override stays (`PREMISE_OVERRIDE_REWRITTEN` unchanged), the read answers both, and the action may then join its escalation (`escalation` R23). (e) A `rests_on` target may also be a success record (`consequences` R17; R93). Controls: (a) the same leg on a noncompliant determination lands; (b) a determination not void lands.
- **R9** `counterparty` is the action's **addressee**, one of: `{state: named, kind?: office, role, body, level?, entity_id?}` (an office by official role and body, `jurisdictions` R24; `kind` absent reads `office`); `{state: named, kind: press | organisation | group, role, organisation}` (a reporter by role and outlet, an organisation or another civic group by role and organisation); `{state: audience, description}` (a described audience, at most 500 characters); `{state: undetermined, basis}`. Never a private individual. Named with no role, or an office with no body, a named non-office with no organisation, an audience with no description, undetermined with no basis, undetermined carrying a role, body, organisation or entity id, the placeholder anywhere, or an `entity_id` not of the registry's `ENT-` form (`record-grammar`'s `idPattern`) or naming a person, or not naming an `office` entity on an office arm, is refused `COUNTERPARTY_REFUSED` (C-2.10, R7), its findings naming the arm. An action is addressed to an office by role and body, never to a person (K1484 row 5; B1a.14): an office arm with no `entity_id` has it filled at the write from the office entity seeded for that `{role, body}` (the bridge, `instance-setup`), and is written without one, stated so, when none is held; the read (R25) shows the holder of that office on the action's date as `lines.holderAt` answers it (a person, or undetermined with why), labelled as who held the office, never as the addressee. A breach action (R8) addresses an office, else `ADDRESSEE_NOT_AN_OFFICE`. A counterparty written before as `{state: named, name}` reads as written, its `name` as the office's. R3's and R27's matching name is "role, body" for an office, "role, organisation" for the other named arms, and none for an audience. (T45; N879; Actions D12; v0.2 rule 7) The press arm may carry `holder: {name}` (`action-grammar` R8), the reporter holding the role, kept inside the group: answered only in the read (R25) and `actionsFor`, never in R66's items, any registration's answer or anything the instance publishes; on another arm it is `COUNTERPARTY_REFUSED`. The read also answers the venue's `delivery_point` (`jurisdictions` R87 (e), `VENUE_FACTS`) beside an office addressee where the profile's venue for the kind states one, labelled where it is delivered, never the addressee.
- **R10** The kinds are the product's own, whose rules this module enforces (`records_request`, `request_for_comment`, `other`), and the `action_kinds` of the active profiles' combined view (`jurisdictions` R25, R27). A creation stating any other kind is refused `ACTION_KIND_UNKNOWN`; with no profile active, only the product's kinds are offered. No kind, law, office or deadline is written in this module's code. (T45; N858; reconciliation R7) The view this module reads for kinds, counterparties, venues, records laws and response statuses (R9, R10, R81, R85, R93) is `local-facts.governingView` (its R12), so a group's confirmed rule governs here as in `action-clocks`; with no group rule it equals `combine`'s. Control: with no group rule, the kinds answered are byte-identical to `combine`'s.
- **R11** A leg onto a document is stamped, at the write, with the capture the record then presents for it (`extent_capture`); a later capture of the document does not change what the leg resolves to. A leg without one reads "version undetermined" and is never back-filled.

**The facts `retrieval` projects (its projection registration, K75 (2))**
- **R12** `actionFacts(documentText, nowMs) → {kind, risk_tier, counterparty_state, resolution, clock_next, clock_overdue}` is pure, and all null for a document whose type is not `action` or that does not parse. `risk_tier` is 1, 2 or 3, or null when undetermined. `clock_next` is the earliest `date` among `pending` clock entries with a `YYYY-MM-DD` date; `clock_overdue` is true exactly when `clock_next` is before the local day of `nowMs` in the zone of the office's or venue's jurisdiction (`civil-time.localDay`; the zone the active profile gives the counterparty office, else the profile's `time_zone`), never the UTC day (K1444 (iii)): a deadline is met by anything on its day, and an entry whose basis states a "close of business" time is overdue from that instant in the office's hours (`civil-time.overdueOn`, side `body`). With no zone held, `clock_overdue` is null, undetermined, never computed on UTC.

- **R14** Otherwise it appends a `state_history` entry (time, from, to, reason, author), sets `prior_state`, `current_state`, `resolution` on `resolved` and `last_updated`, logs the move, and promotes on the held `bundle_sha`; it answers `{ok, target, from, to, reason, resolution?, author, at, weight: "single"}`. It never touches the clock: overdue is derived on read (R25).

The answer is `{ok, target, ord, direction, at, author, recorded_at, held_as: capture | testimony, artifact_sha | account, quote?, lifecycle?, responds_to?, weight: "single"}`. An entry with no quote or lifecycle key writes exactly the bytes it wrote before either existed.
- **R17** A `received` entry with an `artifact_sha` whose capture belongs to another bundle adds to that bundle's references one `responds_to` edge naming the action (status `confirmed`), once: an edge already there answers `{bundle_id, already: true}`; a capture in no bundle answers nothing; a refused write answers `{bundle_id, refused}`, and the entry stands.
