## Public

### Purpose

The Action object (State Rules v1.5 §4.4): what the group sends or does outside the system, to whom, why, under which laws, at what legal exposure, by when, and what came back. It holds the action's kind, risk tier, counterparty, clock (every deadline with the statute, order or commitment it comes from), basis legs, lifecycle and correspondence ledger; the governing laws of a request and the tier as members' authored acts, with machine proposals stored apart; fee quotes and the records-request lifecycle as evidence; and the action's own outcome (DEC-14). The clock's reads across actions, the entries proposed from the profile's deadlines and the reminders members ask for are `action-clocks`' (K617). A member decides every move; a machine prepares and never advances, testifies, sets a tier or states a law.

### Provides

Terms. An **action** is a bundle whose type (by its document or its envelope, D-505) normalises to `action`. Its document carries `action_kind`, `risk_tier` (1, 2, 3 or `undetermined`; absent reads `undetermined`), `counterparty`, `clock[]` (`{text, description, date, basis, status}`, status `pending`, `met`, `overdue`, `waived`), `action_basis[]` (`{target, kind, note?, date?, extent_capture?}`, kind `rests_on` or `advances`), `correspondence[]`, `governing_laws[]` with `governing_laws_by`/`_at`, `risk_tier_history[]`, `resolution`, `law` (a `records_request` only) and `consequence` (**the action's own outcome**, DEC-14; never the breach consequence `consequences` holds). The lifecycle is the catalogue's: `planned → active | abandoned`; `active → awaiting_response | resolved | abandoned`; `awaiting_response → active | resolved | abandoned`; `resolved` and `abandoned` are final. A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, ...}`; a catalogue-backed one also carries `code`, `check` and `translation`.

**At the write: a check and a projection registered with `promotion` (its R39, K31)**
- **R1** A creation or revision of an action, not a replay, is refused before any write: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_RISK_TIER` (C-32.19) when a machine or unstamped author states a tier on a creation, changes a held tier, or drops a member's 1, 2 or 3 (carrying it forward, or leaving an unset tier unset, lands); `RISK_TIER_REWRITTEN` (C-90.1) when a revision changes `risk_tier` or `risk_tier_history`, or a creation states a history, other than through R24's act; `ACTION_BASIS_REFUSED` (C-2.10) for a malformed leg, a leg onto an action, a lead or a theme, a `request_for_comment` naming no inquiry as an `advances` leg or no clock entry (DEC-13), or a leg whose target the record does not hold; `CORRESPONDENCE_REFUSED` (C-2.10) for an entry failing R14's shape rules or whose `artifact_sha` the register does not hold; `RESPONDS_TO_REFUSED` (C-6.1) for a `responds_to` reference that is malformed or names a bundle the record does not hold. Each carries the findings with their check ids and repairs.
- **R2** A creation or revision of an action, replay included, whose governing-laws block (`governing_laws`, `governing_laws_by`, `governing_laws_at`; `[]` and absent are one state) differs from the held version's, other than through R18's act, is refused `GOVERNING_LAWS_REWRITTEN` (C-73.1).
- **R3** In the promotion's transaction, for a bundle whose document's type is `action`, the action's legs, ledger entries and quotes are replaced whole from the document: one leg row per `action_basis` entry (target, its type from the id prefix, kind, note, the document's own date), one ledger row per `correspondence` entry (the captured reply's bundle resolved from the register), and one quote row per received entry whose quote passes C-72.1–C-72.5, carrying the counterparty's name when it is `named` and null otherwise. A malformed replayed entry is skipped, never half-written. No row carries a server time except the entry's own `recorded_at`. (N237, N277; K351) The projection reads at most 500 `action_basis` and 500 `correspondence` entries: an action document holding more is refused where it is authored or revised, `ACTION_TOO_LARGE` with the count and the limit, a row in this module's table, and a replayed document over the limit is skipped whole, never half-projected.
- **R4** `action_kind: records_request` carries `law`, the citation it is made under (at most 200 characters), stated or absent (absent reads undetermined). `law` on any other kind is refused `RECORDS_LAW_REFUSED` (R6). An action already written with a kind no longer offered reads byte-identically, its kind as written.
- **R5** A machine or unstamped author that creates a `records_request` stating `law`, or changes `law`, is refused `MACHINE_CANNOT_STATE_RECORDS_LAW` (C-32.20); it may propose one (R28's shape). An action written before this fence by a machine credential reads its kind's law as MACHINE-STATED, from its recorded author class, and is never rewritten.
- **R6** C-2.10's law arm (R4) is enforced at the write, not only by the audit: `RECORDS_LAW_REFUSED` (C-73.6), carrying the findings.
- **R7** Five C-2.10 arms are enforced at the write (C-101): a kind outside R10's set; a tier outside the vocabulary; a counterparty (the addressee, R9) that is a bare string, the placeholder "to be named", or incoherent (R9); `resolved` with no resolution from `complied`, `denied`, `escalated`, `withdrawn`, `completed`; a clock entry without the `{text, description}` shape, a `YYYY-MM-DD` date, a basis, or a status from the four. `completed` is allowed on any kind: the action was carried out and no counterparty's answer decides it; the action's own outcome (R26, DEC-14) says what happened, and an impact claim still needs a leg outside the group's own actions. A missing counterparty block, and a pending entry past its date, still land and are reported by the audit (R37). *(not yet met: `completed`, D3, K590)*
- **R8** An action whose document states `breach: true` (one recorded for an escalation's stage 2, 5 or 7, `escalation` R9) rests on a conformance determination: it carries at least one `rests_on` leg naming a live determination (`conformance.determinationRead`) the author may see, else `ACTION_NO_DETERMINATION`; a superseded determination is refused `DETERMINATION_SUPERSEDED` (`conformance.determinationSuperseded`, its R20; N312). Neither refusal applies when the document states `premise_override: {reason}` (reason at most 500 characters, R14's text rule) on the creation or revision that first sets `breach: true`, authored by a member: a machine or unstamped author stating or changing one is refused `MACHINE_CANNOT_OVERRIDE`. The override is stamped with who and when, never edited or removed (`PREMISE_OVERRIDE_REWRITTEN`), shown in the read (R25), and carried as a disclosure on everything prepared from the action (`filings` R24); an overridden action is never attached to an escalation (`escalation` R23). An action that seeks evidence (no `breach: true`) is never asked. A leg's `rests_on` target may be a determination, an information item or an inquiry; an action is never a leg's target. *(not yet met: the override, K600 (a))*
- **R9** `counterparty` is the action's **addressee**, one of: `{state: named, kind?: office, role, body, level?, entity_id?}` (an office by official role and body, `jurisdictions` R24; `kind` absent reads `office`); `{state: named, kind: press | organisation | group, role, organisation}` (a reporter by role and outlet, an organisation or another civic group by role and organisation); `{state: audience, description}` (a described audience, at most 500 characters); `{state: undetermined, basis}`. Never a private individual. Named with no role, or an office with no body, a named non-office with no organisation, an audience with no description, undetermined with no basis, undetermined carrying a role, body, organisation or entity id, the placeholder anywhere, or an `entity_id` not of the registry's `ENT-YYYY-NNNN` form or naming a person, is refused `COUNTERPARTY_REFUSED` (C-2.10, R7), its findings naming the arm. A breach action (R8) addresses an office, else `ADDRESSEE_NOT_AN_OFFICE`. A counterparty written before as `{state: named, name}` reads as written, its `name` as the office's. R3's and R27's matching name is "role, body" for an office, "role, organisation" for the other named arms, and none for an audience. *(not yet met: the arms beyond an office, D1, K590; an `entity_id` naming a person, which needs the entities registry, not in this module's uses: ACTIONS #1's deferral)*
- **R10** The kinds are the product's own, whose rules this module enforces (`records_request`, `request_for_comment`, `other`), and the `action_kinds` of the active profiles' combined view (`jurisdictions` R25, R27). A creation stating any other kind is refused `ACTION_KIND_UNKNOWN`; with no profile active, only the product's kinds are offered. No kind, law, office or deadline is written in this module's code.
- **R11** A leg onto a document is stamped, at the write, with the capture the record then presents for it (`extent_capture`); a later capture of the document does not change what the leg resolves to. A leg without one reads "version undetermined" and is never back-filled.

**The facts `retrieval` projects (its projection registration, K75 (2))**
- **R12** `actionFacts(documentText, nowMs) → {kind, risk_tier, counterparty_state, resolution, clock_next, clock_overdue}` is pure, and all null for a document whose type is not `action` or that does not parse. `risk_tier` is 1, 2 or 3, or null when undetermined. `clock_next` is the earliest `date` among `pending` clock entries with a `YYYY-MM-DD` date; `clock_overdue` is true exactly when `clock_next` is before the UTC calendar day of `nowMs` (a deadline is met by anything on its day).

It never touches the clock: overdue is derived on read (R25).

**actionCorrespond({target, direction, at, medium?, party?, artifactSha? | account?, quote…?, lifecycle…?, viewer, author})** (`op=actioncorrespond`)
- **R15** Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_CORRESPOND` (C-32.4); `NO_TARGET`; `BAD_DIRECTION` (C-33.5; `sent`, `received`, `no_response`); `BAD_DATE` (C-33.6; `YYYY-MM-DD`); `CAPTURE_AND_TESTIMONY` (C-33.7); `NEITHER_CAPTURE_NOR_TESTIMONY` (C-33.8); `BAD_SHA`; `NO_RESPONSE_HAS_NO_BYTES`; `BAD_ACCOUNT`, `BAD_MEDIUM`, `BAD_PARTY` (over 500 characters, or a quote, backslash or line break); R22's writability refusals; `QUOTE_TEXT_UNWRITABLE` (C-72.6); `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`; `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `UNREGISTERED_ARTIFACT` (C-33.9); `LEASE_HELD` (with holder and expiry); `NO_DOCUMENT`; R20's quote refusals; R21's lifecycle refusals; `UNSPLICEABLE_CORRESPONDENCE`; then any promotion refusal.
- **R16** It takes a 30-second lease on the action under the author before reading the document (`record-core` R10) and releases it on every path after taking it through `record-core`'s `releaseLease` (its R61), never by taking a zero-length lease (N261). An entry with no quote or lifecycle key writes exactly the bytes it wrote before either existed.
- **R17** A `received` entry with an `artifact_sha` whose capture belongs to another bundle adds to that bundle's references one `responds_to` edge naming the action (status `confirmed`), once: an edge already there answers `{bundle_id, already: true}`; a capture in no bundle answers nothing; a refused write answers `{bundle_id, refused}`, and the entry stands.

**actionLaws({target, laws, viewer, author})** (`op=actionlaws`); **actionLawsPropose({target, laws, proposer, viewer})** (`op=actionlawspropose`)
- **R18** `actionLaws` refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_LAWS` (C-32.18); `NO_TARGET`; `NO_LAWS` (C-73.2, not a non-empty list); `TOO_MANY_LAWS` (C-73.5, over 12); `BAD_LAW_LEVEL` (C-73.3, with the index and the legal levels); `BAD_CITATION` (C-73.4: empty, over 200 characters, a quote, backslash or line break, or an entry repeated at its level, case-folded); `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`; `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `NO_DOCUMENT`; `UNSPLICEABLE_GOVERNING_LAWS`. `BAD_LAW_LEVEL`'s translation names the levels `LAW_LEVELS` holds. - **R19** `actionLawsPropose`: `NO_AUTHOR` (no stamped proposer); `NO_TARGET`; R18's list refusals; `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`; `NOT_AN_ACTION`. Any credential may propose. It replaces that proposer's standing proposal on the action and nobody else's, writes no file and never changes the list, and answers the proposal with its label (`lawProposalLabel`: `machine_proposed`, `member_proposed` or `unstated`, with its sentence), the list as it stands (R25), `evidence: false`, and a sentence saying it is not the list.

**The quote and lifecycle grammar of a correspondence entry** (`quoteFindings`, `lifecycleFindings`; one function each, run at R15 and in the audit)
- **R20** A quote (`quote_amount`, `quote_currency`, `quote_basis?`, `quote_answers`, `quote_revises?`) is refused, first found: `QUOTE_NOT_ON_RECEIVED` (C-72.1), `QUOTE_AMOUNT_NOT_A_NUMBER` (C-72.2), `QUOTE_NO_CURRENCY` (C-72.3), `QUOTE_ANSWERS_NO_SENT` (C-72.4, not the position of an earlier `sent` entry), `QUOTE_REVISES_NO_QUOTE` (C-72.5, not an earlier quote). A waiver is a revision to zero; both stand.
- **R21** A lifecycle place (`stage`, `follows`, `outcome`, `exemptions`, `due_by`, `due_cite`) is refused, first found: `STAGE_NOT_OF_DIRECTION` (C-94.1), `FOLLOWS_NO_ENTRY` (C-94.2), `APPEAL_NAMES_NO_DECISION` (C-94.3), `OUTCOME_NOT_ON_RECEIVED` (C-94.4), `OUTCOME_NOT_IN_VOCABULARY` (C-94.5), `DECISION_WITHOUT_OUTCOME` (C-94.6), `FEE_ESTIMATE_WITHOUT_QUOTE` (C-94.7), `DUE_HALF_STATED` (C-94.8), `DUE_NOT_A_DATE` (C-94.9); and, at the act only, against the list as it stands then, `DUE_CITE_NOT_GOVERNING` (C-94.10). No due date is computed.
- **R22** `exemptions` over 500 characters or `due_cite` over 200, or either holding a quote, backslash or line break, is refused `LIFECYCLE_TEXT_UNWRITABLE` (C-94.11); `stage`, `follows`, `outcome` or `due_by` that is not a token of 1–40 lower-case letters, digits, `_` or `-` is refused `LIFECYCLE_TOKEN_MALFORMED` (C-94.12), whose sentence is true of it.

**actionRiskTier({target, tier, reason, viewer, author})** (`op=actionrisktier`)
- **R23** Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_RISK_TIER` (C-32.19, whatever is asked); `NO_TARGET`; `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`; `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `NO_DOCUMENT`; `BAD_RISK_TIER` (C-90.2; not 1, 2 or 3, the string `"2"` read as 2); `RISK_TIER_REASON_REFUSED` (C-90.3; empty, over 500 characters, or a quote, backslash or line break); `RISK_TIER_UNCHANGED` (C-90.4); `RISK_TIER_HISTORY_UNSPLICEABLE` (C-90.5; not appendable, or 200 revisions held).

**The read: the action block of `op=projection`** (a decoration `retrieval` registers, K75 (2)); **actionQuotes({counterparty | request, answers?, viewer})** (`op=actionquotes`)
- **R25** For an action the viewer may see, at `nowMs` (the caller's `now`, else the instance clock), the block answers: `kind`; `risk_tier` (1, 2, 3 or `undetermined`) with its words, never defaulted; `risk_tier_history` (`riskTierHistoryOf`: oldest first, unreadable entries kept and marked, the intake tier's author stated undetermined); `counterparty_state`; `resolution`; `clock_next` and `clock_overdue` derived at `nowMs` beside the cached flag (`clock_overdue_cached`); `as_of`; the legs and the ledger in order; `governing_laws` (`governingLawsOf`: `stated` with who and when, or `undetermined` with a sentence that assumes no law); `governing_laws_proposals` and `risk_tier_proposals` (R28), each apart from the member's value; the records-request `lifecycle` (`requestLifecycleOf`: each entry's follows, elapsed days, and a stated due date's status `open`, `followed_by_due`, `followed_after_due` or `passed_unanswered` with days past, else `undetermined`); the action's own outcome (R26); and `responses`, the bundles holding a `responds_to` edge to it.
- **R26** The action's own outcome (`consequenceState`, DEC-14) reads `outcome`/`recorded` for a recorded outcome; for a claimed `impact`, `established` naming the `rests_on` legs outside the group's own actions and its own correspondence's replies, or `unproven` with why, never a low grade. It is answered under a name that is not `consequence`, and its words never call it the breach's consequence.
- **R27** `actionQuotes`: `QUOTE_READ_UNASKED` (C-72.7; neither or both axes); `QUOTE_ANSWERS_NOT_AN_ORD` (C-72.8); by request, `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` or `NOT_AN_ACTION`. It answers at most 500 quotes (`truncated` by reading one past), each with the action, amount as quoted, parsed value, currency, basis, counterparty, party, how it is held, author, the entry it answers with its date, what it revises and what revises it. By counterparty the name matches exactly, over actions the viewer may see. An empty answer says which level was empty (`no_request`, `no_reply`, `no_quote`, `no_quote_by_name`). No field compares one quote to another.

**noSuchAction(actionId, extra?) → refusal** (N217, K275; a module-level function)
- **R43** The one answer to one condition: no action the caller may see answers to `actionId` (absent, invisible, or a bundle that is not an action, answered alike). It writes nothing and never throws.
- **R44** (N396, DEC-13; K603) A `request_for_comment` names the specific inquiries it put to its subject and states the response window it gave: at least one `action_basis` leg of kind `advances` whose target is an inquiry, and at least one `clock[]` entry (carrying its basis as every entry does, R7, `action-clocks` R7).
