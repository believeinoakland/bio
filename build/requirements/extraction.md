## Public

### Purpose

Makes readings from captured bytes. It has `reading-pipeline` produce a capture's text through the tier ladder (the format entry in the plane, the `pdf-worker` member, the `ocr-worker` member), compose the transcription chain as it goes and run the content type's reader over the text, and records the reading with its provenance, its references as they appear, its text units and its text-source projection. It keeps every distinct reading of a capture and attributes a re-read that disagrees; re-reads a capture at a member's request; derives the transcriptions a worse calibration of their engine puts in question. It measures no engine (`calibration`'s). It mints no content (`content`'s), resolves no reference to an entity (`entities`'), fetches nothing from outside, and writes no bundle.

### Provides

Terms. **I2 text** is the text shape format entries and the two members return (`format-registry`, `text-chain`). **Tier** 1 is the format entry in the plane, 2 the `pdf-worker` member (binding `PDF_WORKER`), 3 the `ocr-worker` member (binding `OCR_WORKER`). A **glyph** is a non-whitespace code point (`text-chain.glyphCount`). A **failed reading** is `found: false` with no entities and a basis saying why; it is never an emptied document. The **viewer** is the control plane's stamp; a bundle the viewer may not see is withheld as `membership` answers (R43–R44 there).

**read(document, env) → `{reading, text_units?, text_units_over_bound?}`** Reads a stored capture through `reading-pipeline.read` (its R24, R1–R17, R23), handing it this module's evidence store (R1), the bindings, the jurisdiction view (R18) and `calibration.liveCalibration` as its callback (reading-pipeline R5). `document` is `capture`'s acquire answer's document (digest, parts, profile, transport headers, document address, provenance chain). Never throws; every failure is a failed reading.

**Writing a reading: the promote projection and `writeReading({bundleId, captureSha, reading, textUnits, textUnitsOverBound, profileFormat, author}) → {kept, indexed, listeners}`**
- **R19** One writer serves promote and the re-read (R34), inside one `record-core.transact`. In order: keeps the history (R23); replaces the `readings` row (content type, reader version, found, entity count, the reading, `at`, and the capture format from the document's profile, kept when none is given); replaces the references, one row per distinct place (the entity's `source` first, then its `occurrences`, at most 256, with one unplaced row standing for any beyond), each position re-normalised by `text-chain.readingSource` (a position that does not normalise is stored as none), each reference raw `kind:key` and unresolved; replaces the name terms per source (reference, key, label); replaces the text-source row (transcribed, terminal step, engines, derivation cap, step count, chain, calibration ids named), writing none for an absent or malformed chain; replaces the text index (R22).

- **R21** A reading carried in `data/provenance.json` that this instance did not compose is recorded as the caller's assertion, not as a reading this instance composed: its standing is the caller's standing, and the details the caller gives to justify it (for example a recorded interview with an authority) are recorded with it (K104).
- **R22** The text index: the capture's rows are deleted first, even when no unit is offered. Blank units are dropped; the rest are ordered by `seq`; a unit is stored whole, one over 128 KiB having been chunked into consecutive units by the reader (`reading-pipeline` R47), none cut and none flagged `truncated`; no cap of 4,096 units or 2 MiB of UTF-8 bounds a capture or a piece, units being written in batches (R99), and the answer's counts state only what a batch left unwritten. A unit with no extent kind, or an address already written, is counted unaddressable. The chain kind is `text-chain.chainKindFor`. The answer gives `offered`, `written`, `truncated`, `over_bound` (with the wire's own count), `unaddressable` and the chain kind, and names each unit skipped. (T51 fix round 6, G13; K4571; amends R22; R90) (Fix round 9, G128, X15; amends R22) The batch of R99 is the only bound of a write: the 2 MiB and 4,096 units that bounded each piece no longer cut or drop a unit, so the index grows to the last line.
- **R23** Every distinct reading of a capture is kept in arrival order, keyed by the digest of its JSON, before the `readings` row is replaced. A reading equal to the latest kept one is not kept again. A capture whose one reading predates the history has it kept first. Each kept reading stores `compareProvenance` against the one before it (reading-pipeline R19).
- **R24** `onReading(module, fn)`: a later module registers once (a second registration by the same module is refused `LISTENER_DECLARED`). After each write, every `fn` runs in the same transaction, in the modules' total order, with `{bundleId, captureSha, reading, chainBefore, chainAfter, unitsBefore, indexed, author}`; `unitsBefore` is the capture's indexed units (extent, reference, text, `truncated`, `seq`) as they stood before the write, in `seq` order, the whole old set handed to a listener page by page (each page at most R36's bound), never cut at the first 4,096, and null when the capture was never indexed (K141). A listener that throws fails the whole write, because a replaced reading whose cited passages were not marked stale would move an authored edge's target unseen. (N202, K293) A malformed registration is refused `LISTENER_MALFORMED` and a second by the same module `LISTENER_DECLARED`, both answered by `membership.listenerRefusal` (its R81); every `fn` runs in `membership.MODULE_ORDER`'s order (its R83), whatever order they registered in. Since K4927: `unitsBefore` is the whole old list in `seq` order, carrying `pages`, `units` and `state`.
