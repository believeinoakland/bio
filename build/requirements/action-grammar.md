## Public

### Purpose

The grammar of an action document as the record checks it: the action vocabularies (kinds, risk tiers, basis-leg kinds, correspondence directions, resolutions, the records-request lifecycle's stages and outcomes), C-2.10's arms over an action's document (its basis legs, correspondence ledger, quotes and lifecycle, counterparty, records law, governing laws and tier history), C-6.1's `responds_to` arm and C-11.1's clock discipline, the readers `actions` answers its read with, and the rows `actions`' acts mint. It reads no record: every function is pure, and the facts a check needs (the instance's kinds, the time) are handed to it. Whether a write lands is `actions`' (its R1–R11, R33). (T45; N861, N884, N892 (b); Actions D1, D11, D28b; v0.2 rules 2, 9, 21; DESIGN §10 "Assertion phrasings") It also holds the grammar of the fields T45 adds to an action and its correspondence (R15, R20–R25), and the **phrasing lists**: per language, the phrasings Civicsmith holds that make a sentence read as an assertion of a breach, a policy position or an ask for the group's own benefit, which a profile may extend, and the pure finder pointing out the sentences of a text that match them (R16–R19). Pointing out at approval and recording the member's resolution are `filings`'.

### Provides

Terms are `actions`' (an **action**, its document's keys, the lifecycle). A **finding** is `record-grammar`'s (its R11): `{check, severity, message, repairs?, code?}`.

**The vocabularies**
- **R1** (was `actions` R40) `RISK_TIERS` and `riskTierState(document)` are exported as the one vocabulary and reading of an action's risk tier, and `actionKinds(view)` answers the kinds this instance accepts (`actions` R10: the product's own and the view's `action_kinds`), so `affordances` and `instance-setup` read them here, never from `legacy-checks`.
- **R2** Each is exported with its value unchanged from the catalogue's or `actions`' before the move, one binding read by every module: `ACTION_KINDS` (the kinds an action written before reads as, `kindReadsAsWritten(kind)`, `actions` R4, R41), `PRODUCT_KINDS` (`records_request`, `request_for_comment`, `other`), `ACTION_BASIS_KINDS` (`rests_on`, `advances`), `CORRESPONDENCE_DIRECTIONS` (`sent`, `received`, `no_response`), `RESOLUTIONS` (`complied`, `denied`, `escalated`, `withdrawn`, `completed`), `CORRESPONDENCE_STAGES`, `CORRESPONDENCE_OUTCOMES`, `DECISION_STAGES`, `LIFECYCLE_KEYS`, `QUOTE_KEYS`, `isQuoteEntry(entry)`, `quoteValue(amount)`, and `lawProposalLabel(proposedBy)`, which is `record-grammar`'s `proposalLabel(proposedBy, "governing_laws")`. `LAW_LEVELS` is re-exported from `jurisdictions` (its R31), never a copy. (T45; Actions D4, D12, D13, D15, D18; v0.2 rules 2, 6, 8, 15, 16) Also exported frozen, one binding each, published by `affordances` as the others are: `OVERRIDE_GROUNDS` (`legal_deadline`, `irreversible_act`; R15); `PHRASING_KINDS` (`assertion`, `position`, `own_benefit_ask`; R16, the names `jurisdictions` R86 spells); `ASSERTION_RESOLUTIONS` (`only_asks`, `breach_claim`; R19); `PRESS_RECORD_TERMS` (`on_record`, `background`, `off_record`; R21); `FOUND_ELSEWHERE_KINDS` (`press`, `report`, `decision`, `statement`, `other`; R22); `BEARINGS` (`new_evidence`, `disputed_without_evidence`, `not_addressed`; R24); `PROMISE_KINDS` (`embargo`, `anonymity`, `other`; `action-records` R6, was `actions` R88, T47); `CLOCK_OWED_BY` (`group`, `body`, `undetermined`; R7). Control: each earlier export byte-identical to T44's.

**The records law: recordsLawRefusal(fm), recordsLawFindings(fm, findings), recordsLawOf(fm, author), RECORDS_LAW_MAX** Pure; never throw.
- **R3** (was `actions` R4) `action_kind: records_request` carries `law`, the citation it is made under (at most 200 characters, `RECORDS_LAW_MAX`), stated or absent (absent reads undetermined). `law` on any other kind is refused `RECORDS_LAW_REFUSED` (C-73.6), minted by `recordsLawRefusal` alone and carrying the findings (`actions` R6 answers it at the write). An action already written with a kind no longer offered reads byte-identically, its kind as written (`kindReadsAsWritten`, R2).

**The quote grammar of a correspondence entry: quoteFindings(entries, i)** (one function, run at `actions`' R15 and in the audit, R7) Pure; never throws.
- **R4** (was `actions` R20) A quote (`quote_amount`, `quote_currency`, `quote_basis?`, `quote_answers`, `quote_revises?`) is refused, first found: `QUOTE_NOT_ON_RECEIVED` (C-72.1), `QUOTE_AMOUNT_NOT_A_NUMBER` (C-72.2), `QUOTE_NO_CURRENCY` (C-72.3), `QUOTE_ANSWERS_NO_SENT` (C-72.4, not the position of an earlier `sent` entry), `QUOTE_REVISES_NO_QUOTE` (C-72.5, not an earlier quote). **The lifecycle grammar of a correspondence entry: lifecycleFindings(entries, i)** (one function, run at `actions`' R15 and in the audit, R7) Pure; never throws.
- **R5** (was `actions` R21, its grammar) A lifecycle place (`stage`, `follows`, `outcome`, `exemptions`, `due_by`, `due_cite`) is refused, first found: `STAGE_NOT_OF_DIRECTION` (C-94.1), `FOLLOWS_NO_ENTRY` (C-94.2), `APPEAL_NAMES_NO_DECISION` (C-94.3), `OUTCOME_NOT_ON_RECEIVED` (C-94.4), `OUTCOME_NOT_IN_VOCABULARY` (C-94.5), `DECISION_WITHOUT_OUTCOME` (C-94.6), `FEE_ESTIMATE_WITHOUT_QUOTE` (C-94.7), `DUE_HALF_STATED` (C-94.8), `DUE_NOT_A_DATE` (C-94.9). `DUE_CITE_NOT_GOVERNING` (C-94.10), asked against the governing laws as they stand at the act, is `actions`' (its R15). No due date is computed. (T45; S1 §D 14, verified) The lifecycle grammar is the same for every kind: stages are keyed by direction, never by kind, so an `acknowledgement` on a `request_for_comment`'s `received` entry is accepted. Control: an `acknowledgement` on a `sent` entry still pushes `STAGE_NOT_OF_DIRECTION`. *(not yet met: T45; met as built, its test owed)*

**The action basis: actionBasisFindings(fm, findings), correspondenceFindings(fm, findings), RFC_RESPONSE_WINDOW_PRECEDENT** Pure; never throw; each finding names its check.
- **R6** (was `actions` R44; N396, DEC-13; K603) A `request_for_comment` names the specific inquiries it put to its subject and states the response window it gave: at least one `action_basis` leg of kind `advances` whose target is an inquiry, and at least one `clock[]` entry (carrying its basis as every entry does, `actions` R7, `action-clocks` R7). A `rests_on` leg, or an `advances` leg onto anything but an inquiry, is not a disclosed inquiry. `actionBasisFindings` answers one C-2.10 finding naming each missing part, which `actions` R1 refuses a creation or revision with (`ACTION_BASIS_REFUSED`) and the audit reports (R7); another kind is not asked. The window's length is the group's: no range is enforced, and `RFC_RESPONSE_WINDOW_PRECEDENT` (`{min_days: 7, max_days: 30, source, enforced: false}`, the GAO agency-comment protocol) is exported by this module as a citation a surface may show, never compared against a date. A non-response is recorded with its date (`actions` R15, R34).

**The audit's action arm: checkActionExtension(ctx, findings)** Pure; never throws; pushes findings and answers nothing.
- **R7** (was `actions` R37) Over an action's document (`ctx.fm`; `ctx.nowMs` the time, else the clock; `ctx.actionKinds` the instance's `actions` R10 set when the caller has it, else `PRODUCT_KINDS`), it reports every C-2.10 and C-11.1 finding, including a missing counterparty block and a pending clock entry past its date (C-11.1); any other document: nothing. An entry is past its date only once the office's local day has ended (K1444 (iii), as `actions` R12): `ctx.zone`, the IANA zone of the action's office or venue that `actions` hands in, read through `civil-time`; with no zone, no entry is past its date. `actions` registers it with record-core's audit (its R51). (T45; N882; Actions D15; v0.2 rule 6) The audit also reports R15 (only for a `premise_override` that states a `ground`: one written before T45 with none reads as written and is not reported), R8's `holder`, R20, R21, R22 (with no facts), R23 (with `ctx.bundleId` when the caller has it), and C-11.1's `owed_by` arm: a clock entry's `owed_by`, when stated, is one of `CLOCK_OWED_BY`, else a C-11.1 finding; absent reads `undetermined`, never defaulted (`action-clocks` R18 reads it; the clock entry's writer is `action-clocks`, reconciliation R7). For a corpus of action documents written before T45 the findings are byte-identical, in order, to T44's. Control: a clock entry `owed_by: "us"` is reported; `"group"` and none are not.

**The readers and arms `actions` calls** (copied from `actions/checks.mjs`) Pure; never throw.

(T45; N879; Actions D12; v0.2 rule 7) `counterpartyFindings` accepts on the press arm `holder: {name}` (in the bytes the flat text `holder: "<name>"`, K2849), `name` 1 to 200 characters with no quote, backslash or line break; `holder` malformed, or on any other arm, pushes a C-2.10 finding naming `holder`, which `actions` answers `COUNTERPARTY_REFUSED` (its R9). Every finding on a counterparty without `holder` is byte-identical to T44's. Control: a press counterparty with a holder pushes nothing; the same holder on an office pushes the finding.
- **R27** (T47; N959; amends R8; `actions` R33, R98) `clockMovesNotMechanical(heldClock, nextClock, todayIso)` states R33's mechanical rule by the date alone: it answers, as `[{ord, from, to}]`, every clock entry whose status differs between the held and the next clock, except an entry moved from `pending` to `overdue` whose held `date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) is before `todayIso`'s day; `[]` when every change is such a move. It reads no time of day: a timed entry (`due_at`, `action-clocks` R3) moved on its own day is answered here like any other, and admitting it once its instant has passed is `actions`' own arm (its R98), which filters this answer; R8's "exactly what R33's mechanical rule states" reads "R33's date rule". It is pure and never throws. Controls: a `pending` entry dated yesterday moved to `overdue` is not answered; the same entry dated today is answered (with or without `due_at`); a `met` entry changed is answered.

**The gate's override ground: overrideGroundFindings(override, findings)** (T45; N884; Actions D3, D18; v0.2 rule 2) Pure; never throws.
- **R15** (T45; N884; Actions D3, D18; v0.2 rule 2) `override.ground` is one of `{kind: "legal_deadline", citation, date}` (`citation` 1 to `CITATION_MAX` characters with no quote, backslash or line break; `date` a real calendar date, `civil-time.isCalendarDate`) or `{kind: "irreversible_act", what, when}` (`what` 1 to 500 characters, the same text rule; `when` a real calendar date or `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM` with a real date and a time 00:00–23:59). A `ground` absent, not an object or of another kind pushes `OVERRIDE_NO_GROUND`, its message naming both grounds; a part missing or malformed, or a key the kind lacks, pushes `OVERRIDE_GROUND_REFUSED`, naming the part. `actions` asks it at the write that states an override (its R74) and mints the refusal. Controls: each ground well formed pushes nothing; `date: "2026-02-30"` pushes `OVERRIDE_GROUND_REFUSED`; no ground pushes `OVERRIDE_NO_GROUND`. (K2849) In the bytes `premise_override` holds flat keys (`reason`, `ground_kind`, `ground_citation`, `ground_date`, or `ground_what`, `ground_when`); `actions` presents `{ground, reason}` and hands that object here; a pre-T45 override (`reason` alone) reads `ground: null`.

**The phrasing lists and the finder: PHRASINGS, phrasingsFor, pointOut, assertionResolutionFindings, assertionsUnresolved** (T45; N861, N884, N892 (b); Actions D1, D11, D28b; v0.2 rules 2, 9, 21; K2781 (5)) Pure; never throw.
- **R16** (T45; v0.2 rule 2 "a list of phrasings Civicsmith holds for each language"; K2781 (5)) `PHRASINGS` is exported frozen: per language (a BCP 47 primary subtag), per kind of `PHRASING_KINDS`, phrasings of 2 to 100 characters on one line, naming no place, office, person or party. At T45 it holds English (`en`) alone, with at least these, pinned by the tests: `assertion`: "illegal", "illegally", "unlawful", "unlawfully", "violated", "in violation of", "broke the law", "in breach of", "breached", "failed to comply with", "covered up"; `position`: "we support", "we oppose", "should adopt", "should pass", "should reject", "vote yes", "vote no", "vote for", "vote against"; `own_benefit_ask` (kept narrow: phrasings that cannot be a remedy of harm, K2781 (5)): "pay us", "pay our", "fund our", "hire us", "contract with us", "donate to us". The job may add phrasings from canon's and S1's examples; none of these is removed without a requirement change. Control: every pinned phrasing present; no kind's `en` list empty.
- **R17** (T45; Actions D1; v0.2 rule 2 "which a profile may extend") `phrasingsFor({language, kind, view?})` answers `{state: "read", language, phrases: [{phrase, origin}], ignored}`. `language` is a tag `jurisdictions.isLocale` accepts (its R37), its list found by the whole tag case-insensitive, else by its primary subtag. `origin` is `product` (R16) or `profile`: each `view.phrasings` item (`jurisdictions` R86, the combined view the caller hands in) whose `locale` matches so and whose `kind` is `kind`. A profile only extends: a profile phrasing equal to a product one (after R18's normalisation) is answered once as `product`; a malformed item is not used and is named in `ignored` with why. It answers `{state: "undetermined", why}`, `why` one of `language_malformed`, `kind_unknown`, `no_list` (no phrasing held for that language and kind), never an empty `read`. Controls: `en` with a view adding one phrasing answers both origins; `es` with no view `undetermined` `no_list`; `es` with a view holding a Spanish phrasing `read`.
- **R18** (T45; v0.2 rules 2, 9, 21) `pointOut({sentences, language, kind, view?})` answers the sentences that match a phrasing R17 answers. `sentences` is the text as split by the caller (`filings`), 0 to 5,000 strings of at most 10,000 characters each; anything else answers `{state: "undetermined", why: "not_sentences"}`. Each sentence and phrasing is read in Unicode NFC, case-folded, typographic apostrophes and quotes as their plain forms, each run of white space as one space; a phrasing matches where no letter, mark or digit is immediately before or after it. It answers `{state: "read", language, kind, pointed: [{index, phrase, origin, start, end}]}`, every match in sentence order then position (offsets in the sentence as given); a question is not exempt; R17's `undetermined` passes through with no `pointed`. It decides nothing: a sentence not pointed out is not thereby cleared (rule 2). Controls: "Staff illegally split the contract." points out "illegally"; "We ask the Auditor to examine five purchase orders." points out nothing; "Illegality" does not match "illegal"; "IN  BREACH OF" matches "in breach of"; `sentences: "text"` answers `undetermined`.
- **R19** (T45; N884; Actions D1; v0.2 rule 2 "rewrites it, confirms in her own recorded words that the act only asks, or treats the act as a breach claim") `assertionResolutionFindings({sentences, pointed, resolutions}, findings)` and `assertionsUnresolved({sentences, pointed, resolutions})`. `pointed` is R18's answer for `assertion` over the sentences as they now stand: a rewritten sentence no longer pointed out needs no resolution. `resolutions` is a list of `{sentence, resolution, words?}`: `sentence` the exact text of a pointed-out sentence; `resolution` one of `ASSERTION_RESOLUTIONS`; `words`, the member's own words that the act only asks, 1 to 500 characters on one line, required for `only_asks` and refused on `breach_claim`. A resolution naming no pointed-out sentence, a second of one sentence, or a malformed one pushes `ASSERTION_RESOLUTION_REFUSED`, naming the item. `assertionsUnresolved` answers `{unresolved: [index], breach_claim}`; a resolution binds the exact text, so an edited sentence is unresolved again; an `undetermined` `pointed` answers `{state: "undetermined"}`, never `unresolved: []`. Who resolves, where it is recorded and the refusal at approval are `filings`' (its R43). Controls: `only_asks` with words resolves; without words pushes the refusal; edited, unresolved again; one `breach_claim` answers `breach_claim: true`.

**The action's new fields: copiedFindings, partnersFindings, pressTermsFindings, entryFieldFindings, actionFieldFindings, bearingFindings, lawStagesFindings, stageInLaw** (T45; N858, N879, N880, N883; Actions D12, D13, D16; v0.2 rules 6, 7, 8, 16, §5, §6) Pure; never throw; C-2.10 findings with codes, `actions` minting each refusal from the first finding.

Control: well-formed terms on a press action push nothing; on an office action `PRESS_TERMS_NOT_PRESS`.
- **R22** (T45; N879, N880; Actions D12, D13; v0.2 rules 8, 16; S1 §C 5, 10, 11) `entryFieldFindings(entries, i, facts?)`, run at `actions`' R15 and in the audit: `office_status: {words, status?}` only on a `received` entry carrying an `outcome` (R5's vocabulary, never replaced), `words` 1 to 500 characters with no line break, else `OFFICE_STATUS_WITHOUT_OUTCOME`; `status` one of `facts.responseStatuses` (handed in from `jurisdictions` R58, R77), else, or with none handed in, `OFFICE_STATUS_UNKNOWN` (`words` alone accepted; the audit hands none, judging `words` and the outcome only). (K2849) In the record's bytes these objects are flat keys on the entry, since the front-matter parser reads no map inside a list item: `office_words`, `office_status` (the token), `found_elsewhere` (the kind token), `found_elsewhere_note`; R22's rules apply to them and the reads present the objects. `office_words` and `found_elsewhere_note` hold no quotation mark, backslash or line break (the bytes cannot hold them), refused as `OFFICE_STATUS_WITHOUT_OUTCOME` and `FOUND_ELSEWHERE_REFUSED` respectively (K2859). `found_elsewhere: {kind, note?}` only on a `received` entry, `kind` of `FOUND_ELSEWHERE_KINDS`, `note` at most 500 characters, else `FOUND_ELSEWHERE_REFUSED`; stated with no `artifact_sha` `FOUND_ELSEWHERE_NEEDS_CAPTURE`. `speaker` only on a `sent` entry, a member id as `actions`' `contactId` reads one, 1 to 200 characters, else `SPEAKER_REFUSED`. An entry with none of the three pushes nothing new. Controls: `office_status` with an outcome and a held status pushes nothing; without `outcome` `OFFICE_STATUS_WITHOUT_OUTCOME`; `found_elsewhere` on `sent` `FOUND_ELSEWHERE_REFUSED`; `speaker` on `received` `SPEAKER_REFUSED`.
- **R23** (T45; S1 §C 2, 8; v0.2 rule 6, §5) `actionFieldFindings(fm, ctx, findings)`: `reply_owed` absent, `true` or `false`, else `REPLY_OWED_REFUSED`; `follows_action` absent or `{action, ord?}`, `action` an action bundle id (`BUNDLE_ID_RE`, type `action` in `OBJECT_TYPES`), `ord` a non-negative integer, else `FOLLOWS_ACTION_REFUSED`; equal to `ctx.bundleId` `FOLLOWS_ACTION_SELF`. Controls: `reply_owed: false` and a link to another action push nothing; `reply_owed: "no"` and a self-link push their codes.
- **R24** (T45; N880; Actions D13; v0.2 rule 16 "laid out claim by claim") `bearingFindings(claims, legTargets, findings)`: `claims` 1 to 50 `{claim, bearing, note?}`, `claim` one of `legTargets` (the action's `advances` and `rests_on` targets, handed in), none twice, `bearing` of `BEARINGS`, `note` at most 500 characters; any fault `BEARING_REFUSED`, naming the item. - **R25** (T45; N858; v0.2 §5; S1 §C 18, §D 15; `jurisdictions` R76) `lawStagesFindings(stages)` answers one `LAW_STAGE_UNKNOWN` finding per item of a records law's profile `stages` whose `stage` is not of `CORRESPONDENCE_STAGES` (either direction), naming it, `[]` for a well-formed list (run over the loaded view by `instance-setup`). `stageInLaw(stage, stages)` answers `in_law`, `not_in_law`, or `undetermined` when `stages` is absent or not a list, never `in_law` by default; a stage `lawStagesFindings` reports is never `in_law` (`actions` R85).
