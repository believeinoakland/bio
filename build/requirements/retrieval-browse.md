## Public

### Purpose

Retrieval-browse answers two reads that are not a search of the whole record's text: "Find in this", a find over one scope (a capture, a held selection, a set of ids or a project's holdings) by kind (people, money, dates, requirements, events, a term), each match a citable `{kind, words, capture_sha, extent, origin}` with who already recorded it; and browse, what the viewer may see (documents, questions, actions, plans, people and organizations), newest first, with no words, narrowed to what is new, watched, flagged or awaiting review. It says what it could not read ("Nothing here" only when every capture was read for the kind and none matched), and writes nothing. It holds the one registration of the later modules' recorded-by reads and the one registration of the watch and flag marks. It runs no search statement of its own (`retrieval` compiles and gates every one it is handed) and mints nothing: a match is an address.

### Provides

Terms. A **viewer** and an **owner** are the control plane's stamps. **Hidden** means a thing the viewer may not see (`membership` R43); a hidden thing answers exactly as an absent one, and no count includes it. A capture is **held** when a bundle the viewer may see registers it (`provenance` R48). The **host reads** are the five functions `retrieval` hands this module (R14). Every bound is published as applied, after clamping. Ids written `retrieval` Rn are `retrieval`'s own (`build/requirements/retrieval.md`); a bare Rn is this file's.

**findIn({scope, kinds, term?, limit?, cursor?, viewer, owner?})** (`op=findin`; T35-37; N698; DEC-164 (1)–(4), (7); K1468, K1865)
- **R1** (moved from retrieval R73) "Find in this": a find over one scope, by kind, that records nothing. `scope` is exactly one of `{capture}` (a capture's sha256), `{selection}` (a selection handle, `retrieval` R18–R19, resolved under the viewer as `retrieval` R19 resolves it), `{ids}` (an enumerated set of at most 200 bundle ids or capture shas) or `{project}` (every capture held in a bundle of that project: the project's holdings). Refusals, in order, each writing nothing: `VIEWER_MISSING` (no viewer stamp); `NO_SCOPE`; `SCOPE_UNKNOWN` (none or more than one form, or another key); `CAPTURE_NOT_HELD` (a capture the record does not hold or the viewer may not see, answered alike), `NO_SUCH_SELECTION` (`retrieval` R19's answer), `NO_SUCH_PROJECT` (absent or not visible, answered alike), `SCOPE_TOO_LARGE` (an enumeration over 200); `NO_KINDS`; `KIND_UNKNOWN` (naming R2's list); `NO_TERM` (kind `term` without a `term`), `TERM_TOO_LONG` (over 200 characters). Every capture of the scope the viewer may not see is left out and counted nowhere (R15); an enumerated or selected id now hidden leaves as `retrieval` R19 says. It answers `{ok: true, scope, kinds: [{kind, items, count, truncated, nothing, not_read}], captures_read, next}`: each item a match `{kind, words, capture_sha, extent, origin: "search"}` plus its kind's fields (R2), `words` the passage's words as written (at most 500 characters, cut at a word with `…` and the whole extent kept), `extent` in `content`'s extent grammar, so a recording act can take it as its citation (`events` R1, `money` R2, `people` R9, `standards` R47, `citation`'s `cite`); `nothing: true`, "Nothing here" (DEC-98), only when every capture of the scope was read for that kind and none matched; `not_read`, the captures that could not be read for it (no text, not extracted, no matcher for its language, R3), each with why, never folded into "Nothing here". At most 200 captures are read per call, in a stated order (capture sha), with `next` naming where to continue and `null` when the scope is done; `limit` bounds each kind's items (1–500, default 50) with `truncated` by reading one past. It writes nothing: no selection, no observation row (a member's own searching writes nothing, `observation-log` R24), no fact, no reading; and it calls no model. (T36; N715, DEC-164 (4); K1941, K2063) Each match also carries `recorded`: the items (`events` R49's shape) that each recording module's read answers for the match's capture under the same viewer and whose extent stands to the match's (a table item's `table.extent`) as `same`, `narrower` or `wider` (`content.extentRelation`), each with its `module`, `record`, `kind`, `field`, `relation`, `by`, `at` and `withdrawn`, so a result already recorded shows who recorded it; `recorded: []` when none. The reads are `events.recordedBy`, `standards.recordedBy`, `money.recordedBy` and `people.recordedBy`, then each read R4 registers, in the modules' total order (`membership` R83), each called once per capture the call reads, with `limit` 500. The answer carries `recorded_read` (the modules whose reads answered) and `recorded_not_read` (each read that refused or threw, with why, and while no later module is registered `{module: null, why: "no later module's records were read: none registered"}`), so an empty `recorded` is never read as "nobody recorded this" beyond `recorded_read`; a match of a capture whose read answered `truncated` carries `recorded_truncated: true`. Reading them writes nothing. Its `{selection}` scope reads the selection through `retrieval`'s `selectionRead` (R77), never `selectionResolve`, so `findIn` extends no selection's life and writes nothing (N729; K2092). (T50; N1191 (d); K3598; JOURNEYS V4 B-30; DEC-300 (3); amends R1) A recorded item is in a match's `recorded` only when `content.extentRelation` of the match's extent and the item's answers `same` or `narrower` (the item's extent is the match's own or lies inside it). `wider` no longer marks: a fact recorded for the whole document, a whole sheet or the page holding the match marks none of the passages inside it (this replaces the clause above naming `wider`). A match's `recorded` then holds only facts recorded from that passage, so "facts recorded from this passage" (`passage.facts.count`) is its length. Test: a text file of three paragraphs holding a date, an amount and a requirement, a fact recorded from the second: only the matches in the second carry it; a fact recorded with the extent `document` marks no match. Control: two matches in one paragraph both carry a fact recorded from that paragraph (`same`).
- **R2** (moved from retrieval R74) The kinds (closed; DEC-164 (2)), each answered by a deterministic matcher over the readings and text `extraction` and `content` hold for each capture, never by a model: **Amended (G41; K4664):** over a sheet named in `cells_held`, `money` and `dates` are read from its rendered text, each hit carrying R8's `cells_why`.
  - `people`: the people and offices the group follows, by their held names: each reference of the capture's readings that corresponds to a held alias of a registered entity of kind `person` or `office`, as `entities.namingDocuments` corresponds them (its R17: `reference`, `reference_key`, `name`, then the partial forms), with `entity` and how it corresponded; nothing is resolved (`entities` R9) and a name no registered entity holds is found only as a word (kind `term`, DEC-164 (7));

  A table's date or amount column is one result naming the table (DEC-164 (3); K1468): where the capture is a sheet or a held table, or a passage is a table whose column holds dates or amounts, the item is `{kind, table: {capture_sha, extent, column, rows}, words, origin: "search"}`, its `words` the column's header, never one item per row; its dates or amounts are counted through a calculation (`calculations`' declared table), never read in here. (T36; N724; K1972) A `.docx` table held as cells in the reading (`office-readers` R11 as amended, carried by `extraction`) is a held table: its date or amount column is one item as above, `table.extent` the table's extent and `rows` its row count, never one item per row or cell. (T37; N758; K2118) A `.docx` table's paragraphs are found by its cells' `paras` (`office-readers` R11 as amended, carried by `reading-pipeline` R28), never by matching cell lines in order, so a vertically merged cell's paragraphs are found exactly; an ordinal naming no `doc-para` text unit (a whitespace-only paragraph, R15 of `reading-pipeline`, or one its wire bound dropped) is passed over, never an error and never matched to another paragraph (K2197).
- **R3** (moved from retrieval R75) (DEC-99) The matchers' words (currency signs, codes and words; number multipliers; month names and date forms; period units; requirement words) are held per language in one place, `FIND_MATCHERS`, a frozen map from a BCP 47 primary language subtag to its vocabularies, and nowhere else in this module's code; English (`en`) is held in T35. A capture is matched with the set of its reading's language where `extraction` states one, else the active profiles' `locale` (`jurisdictions` R37) primary subtag; a capture whose language has no set is in `not_read` for `money`, `dates` and `requirements` with "no matcher for <language> yet", never matched with another language's words. A language is added by adding its set, with no other change; no set names a place (R17). The same input always gives the same matches.

**browse({kinds?, only?, since?, after?, limit?, viewer})** (`op=browse`)
- **R10** (moved from retrieval R85) (T50; N1092; K3314; DEC-238 (F1, F2, S23); `entities` R35, R51) `browse({kinds?, only?, since?, after?, limit?, viewer})` (`op=browse`) lists what the viewer may see, newest first by last change, with no words. `kinds` is any of `document`, `question`, `action`, `plan` (bundles of type `information`, `inquiry`, `action`, `action_plan`) and `entity` (registered entities of kind `person`, and `institution`, `body`, `movement`); absent or `all` is all five. `only` is `new_since` (needs `since`, an instant: last change strictly later), `watched` (the viewer's own watches) or `flagged` (items flagged for a second look). Refusals, in order, each reading nothing: `VIEWER_MISSING`; `KIND_UNKNOWN` (naming the list); `ONLY_UNKNOWN` (naming the three); `NO_SINCE` (`new_since` without `since`); `BAD_SINCE`. It answers `{ok: true, kinds, only, items, count, limit, truncated, next}`; each item `{kind, id, title, last_changed, new, watched, flagged}`: `kind` one of `document`, `question`, `action`, `plan`, `person`, `organization`; `title` the bundle's title or the entity's label; `last_changed` the bundle's `last_updated`, or for an entity its creation instant (the registry holds no later one); `new` true only when `since` was given and `last_changed` is later; `watched` true when the viewer watches it; `flagged` `{by, at, note}` or `null` (R11). Ties by id. `limit` 1-200 (default 50); `next` continues; `truncated` by reading one past. A bundle is listed only if `membership`'s gate passes it (an absent or unrecognised viewer sees nothing, never everything); entities are team-wide (`entities` R51). With `only: "watched"` or `"flagged"` and no module registered under R11 the answer is empty with `undetermined: {why}`, never unfiltered. It writes nothing and moves no looked-at mark. Test: a document, a question, a plan and a person, one of them changed after `since`: newest first, `kind` on each, `new` on that one; `kinds: ["plan"]` lists plans only. Control: a bundle the viewer may not see is not listed or counted; browsing twice moves nothing.
- **R11** (moved from retrieval R86) (T50; N1092; DEC-238 S23 "A"; K31's pattern, as `retrieval` R52, and R4) `registerBrowseMarks(module, fn)`: one later module (`following`) registers once at start the read of a member's watches and the team's second-look flags; a malformed registration or a second by the same module is refused through `membership`'s `listenerRefusal` (`LISTENER_MALFORMED`, `LISTENER_DECLARED`). `fn({viewer, only, limit})` answers `{items, truncated}`, the ids marked (`only: "watched"`: the viewer's own, never another member's; `"flagged"`: every flag, `{id, by, at, note}`), at most 5,000 with `truncated`; `fn({viewer, ids})` answers for those ids (at most 200) `{watched: [ids], flagged: [{id, by, at, note}]}`. R10 keeps only ids it lists under its own gate. A read that throws, answers a promise or another shape is read as no marks and the answer says so in `undetermined`; it changes nothing else. Test: a registered stub: `only: "watched"` lists the viewer's watches and none of another member's; a flag shows `by`, `at` and `note` to every viewer who sees the item. Control: a stub that throws leaves `watched: false` everywhere with `undetermined`; a hidden flagged item is not listed.

**The table and the scopes** (T51-29; DEC-330 A14, A15; N1212; K3809)
- **R12** (DEC-330 A14: *the whole record as one sortable table*, `screen.finder.table.*`) R10's answer also carries, for what the viewer may see: on each item `state`, the bundle's `current_state` as stored, or `null` for an entity (a person or organization has no state of its own); and on the answer `total`, the number of items matching `kinds`, `only` and `since`, counted over the whole set and never the page, and `awaiting_review`, how many of them are awaiting review: a document (a bundle of type `information`) whose state is `collected`, one no member has verified yet (R13's `awaiting_review` names the same items). Both are counted through the gate (R15): an item the viewer may not see is in neither, and neither states how much was withheld. `limit` and `truncated` bound only `items`; `total` and `awaiting_review` do not change with `limit`, `after` or the page. The table's four columns are therefore R10's `title` and `kind`, R12's `state` and R10's `last_changed`, its rows the pages of `items` reached by `next` (sorting is the screen's, over the pages it holds), and its count line "N items · M awaiting review" `total` and `awaiting_review`. Test: three collected documents and one verified, a question and a person, all visible, and one more collected document in a project the viewer may not see: `total` 6 and `awaiting_review` 3; with `limit` 2 the page holds two items and `total` is still 6; `kinds: ["question"]` answers `total` 1 and `awaiting_review` 0. Control: the hidden document moves neither number; browsing twice moves nothing.
- **R13** (DEC-330 A15: *the scope*, `screen.finder.scope.*`; the census says "from `searchfields`") `only` has a fourth value, `awaiting_review`: the items R12 counts as awaiting review (`ONLY_UNKNOWN` names the four). `BROWSE_SCOPES` is the closed list of scopes a member narrows a find to, each `{key, kinds, only}`: `everything` (all five kinds), `question`, `document`, `action`, `awaiting_review`, `watched` and `flagged`; none is named in English (the screen draws `screen.finder.scope.*`). `scopeMembers({scope, viewer, limit?})` answers, for a key of that list, `{ok: true, scope, ids, count, truncated, entities_in_scope}`: the ids of the bundles the viewer may see that the scope holds, at most 5,000 with `truncated` (an entity is not a bundle and is not in `ids`; `entities_in_scope` counts those matched), under the same gate and the same marks read as R10. An unknown key is refused `SCOPE_KEY_UNKNOWN` naming the list; no viewer is `VIEWER_MISSING`. It writes nothing. `retrieval` publishes the list in `searchFields` and narrows a search by it (`retrieval` R87). Test: each key over R12's corpus answers the ids R10 lists for the same `kinds` and `only`, `watched` with a registered stub the viewer's own watches and never another member's. Control: a scope over a hidden bundle lists no such id; "Cases" is not a scope here (a case is not a bundle type; the map's doubt 2).
