## Public

### Purpose

The feed's newer producers: each derives, on read and writing nothing, the items one provider's facts earn for a viewer: what the machine noticed about people and money ("Noticed", the machine's, in the hypothesis layer), a member's standing-question answers, a body's duty occurrences that have come due, and an inquiry's dated waits that have come round. It names each item's subjects and homes for `queue` to home, offer, mint and publish, exactly as `queue-producers` does for the rest.

### Provides

Terms. An **item**, a **home set**, the item's `class` (FINDING, OBLIGATION, CONDITION) and its key are `queue`'s and `queue-producers`' (its Terms). A **gate** is a check's or detector's state of being shown: open only once its false-alarm rate, measured on its gold set, is at most 20% (K1504), as its owner answers it.

**noticeItems({member, viewer, now, identity, homesOf, optionsOf}) → {items, facts}** (`queue`'s one read of this module)
- **R1** Answers every item R2–R6, R12 and R13 derive for this member and viewer, each homed through `homesOf(subjectIds)` and carrying `options` from `optionsOf(subjectIds)` (as `queue-producers` R8), and `facts` stating each producer's bound and `truncated`. Writes nothing and never throws; a provider that throws contributes no item and is named in `facts.failed`. (T36) R1's items include R14's and R15's, and `facts` states their bounds and `truncated` as for R13.

**The producers**
- **R2** (K1491, K1473; ladders §2 "Machine checks") FINDINGs `interest-check-noticed`: one per result of an interest check that `people.checkResults({project, viewer})` answers (its R24: only once the check's gate is recorded open; its R25: withheld whole from a viewer who may not see an input) and which is not switched off for the project it runs in, keyed `FINDING::interest-check-noticed::<check>::<result>`, to the members of that project who may see every input it rests on, and to nobody else. Its detail states the check's data-defined condition, its denominator and its cited derivation, and its `label` is `noticed` (the machine's, in the hypothesis layer). It names no judgment of a person: not "conflict", not suspicion; the subject is the pattern or transaction, never the person alone. It is raised once and leaves when its recipient disposes of it or the result no longer holds.
- **R3** (K1491, K1473) FINDINGs `money-detector-noticed`: one per result `money-checks.noticed({project, viewer})` answers (its R9: only detector versions whose recorded rate is at most 20% and switched on for that project), keyed `FINDING::money-detector-noticed::<detector>::<result>`, to the members of that project who may see every input, and to nobody else; detail, label, words and leaving as R2's.
- **R4** (K1481; ladders §9.5 L5) FINDINGs `standing-answer`: one per entry `answers.standingAnswersFor({member})` answers (its R20), keyed `FINDING::standing-answer::<question>::<run>`, to the question's author and to nobody else; its subject the standing question, its detail the new finds and the answer as `answers` holds it (or what held the AI half back), labelled as the assistant's machine work. It is told once (DEC-94): raised once per run and never repeated, and leaves when its recipient disposes of it.
- **R5** (K1466, K1444 (i); ladders §10 "A due threshold raises a question") FINDINGs `temporal-expectation-due`: one per occurrence of an adopted duty that `duties` answers `overdue` (only after the latest candidate due date) or `undetermined` between the candidates (its detail "possibly overdue: undetermined, because …"), read through `duties.occurrencesOf` (the sibling draft's R10, R11) for the duties `duties.dutiesOf` answers the viewer, keyed `FINDING::temporal-expectation-due::<duty>::<occurrence>::<state>` (`<state>` `overdue` or `undetermined`, so a change of state raises a fresh item; K1676), to the member who adopted the duty, else its project's owners (`membership` R65), else the administrators (`membership` R86); its subject the duty, naming the occurrence, its due date or candidates, its basis kind and its derivation, its `label` `noticed`, and `due` the local day (as `queue-producers` R25). It never states a violation. It is raised once per occurrence and state, and leaves when the occurrence is met, met late or no longer overdue. An occurrence of the group's own checkpoint is never such an item (D234; `action-plans` R23).
- **R6** (Choices 23; ladders §4.5 dated waits) Items `inquiry-recheck-due`: one per dated wait `inquiry` answers due (its recheck date reached on the local day), keyed `<CLASS>::inquiry-recheck-due::<inquiry>::<date>`, to the member who set the wait and to nobody else; its subject the inquiry, naming what is awaited, from whom and by when (DEC-98), `due` that day. It is raised once and leaves when that member records a look, sets a new date or removes the wait, or the inquiry concludes.

**The security level and a policy's silent change** (T35)
- **R12** (N703; K1874 (Q5), K1875; DEC-165 (7)) FINDINGs `security-level-high`: when `credentials.securityLevel()` (its R45: the level of the 24 hours ending at the call, at the threshold BOB set under K1881) answers `High`, one item to each active administrator (`membership.activeAdmins`, its R86) and to nobody else, a viewer who is not an administrator receiving none. An **episode** is an unbroken run of time in which `securityLevel` answers `High`: it starts at the first whole hour at whose end the level was `High` after a whole hour at whose end it was not (read from `credentials.securityMap`, its R45, under the administrator's own `by`, over at most the 90 days of counts kept), and lasts until a call answers `Raised` or `Ordinary`. The item is keyed `FINDING::security-level-high::<episode start>` (the instant, to the hour), so it is raised once per episode for each administrator and never again while that episode lasts, and a later episode raises a fresh one. Its subject is the group's Civicsmith (no bundle); its `label` is `noticed`; its detail is one plain sentence saying the level is High, since when, and whether anything got through (a `through` count in the period, `credentials` R44), counts only, naming no address, country, role, handle or member; and it carries `screen: {op: "securitymap", from, to}`, `from` the episode's start and `to` the instant the item was derived, so the item opens the security screen at that period (DEC-165 (7)). It leaves when its recipient disposes of it or the episode ends. A `securityLevel` or `securityMap` that throws or does not answer contributes no item and is named in `facts.failed` (R1), never read as `Ordinary`. The words are the design stream's; until they are given, the item says this meaning plainly.
- **R13** (N652; K1727, K1740; DEC-145 (5)) FINDINGs `policy-changed-noticed`: one per change `following.policyChanges({after, limit, viewer})` answers (its R21) whose `amendment_held` is `false`, read for the changes whose later capture is no older than 90 days, following `cursor` to at most 1,000 changes (`facts` naming the bound and `truncated`), keyed `FINDING::policy-changed-noticed::<watch>::<after.capture>`, to the member who declared the policy in `standards` (its declaration's author, R1) while that member is `active`, else to the administrators (`membership` R86), and in either case only while the recipient may see the policy (a change `policyChanges` leaves out for that viewer is no item). Its subject is the policy (the standard), naming the address watched and both captures; its `label` is `noticed`; its detail is exactly DEC-145 (5)'s sentence, "Changed without notice: the text differs from the copy captured on <date>, and no amendment was announced", `<date>` the local day (`civil-time`) of `before.at`. It never says what the change means, never states a violation and is never a finding of the record (`following` R16). It is not a hint and carries no R11 mark. It is raised once per change and leaves when its recipient disposes of it, or when a later read answers that change with `amendment_held` true (an amendment then held covers it). (T36) R13 reads `following.policyChanges({since, after, limit, viewer})` with `since` the instant 90 days before the call, following `cursor` to at most 1,000 changes, so a change older than the window is never read and never uses up the bound; the bound then cuts only a window holding more than 1,000 changes, stated in `facts` as before. "Whose later capture is no older than 90 days" is read through `since`, never by filtering an unbounded read.

**A file held after a scan, and a security tool switched off** (T36; N707, N710, its share; DEC-169 (4); K1892, K1913, K1929)
- **R14** FINDINGs `scan-found`: one per finding `file-safety.scanFindings({after, limit, viewer})` answers (its R15: each `found` note, naming no member), read following `cursor` to at most 1,000 findings (`facts` naming the bound and `truncated`), keyed `FINDING::scan-found::<captureSha>::<note_id>`, to each active member who may see the capture (the viewer for whom `scanFindings` answers the finding; a finding on a capture the viewer may not see is no item and is not counted, R7), and to nobody else. Its subject is the capture's home (`provenance.homeOf`); its detail states that the file is held (`file-safety` R16): the finding names with their engine and tool and the date, each name explained in `file-safety.findingKind`'s words (its R38), that its safe view stays open, and that the original opens again only when two members each release the hold with a reason, or when a second, different engine's clean check releases a hold the built-in scanner alone placed (`file-safety` R17, R18; DEC-169 (4): "the machine never can"). It names no member: not who captured, opened or asked about the file. It is not a hint and carries no R11 mark (a scanner's verdict, not the machine's noticing). It is raised once per finding note and leaves when its recipient disposes of it, or when `file-safety` answers that no open hold covers that finding any longer (`scanFindings`' `held`, N771; K2155) *(not yet met: T37)*. (T37; N762, N771; K2160, K2175) R14 reads `file-safety.scanFindings({since, after, limit, viewer})` with `since` the instant 90 days before the call, from the first page and following `cursor` while `truncated` is true, to at most 1,000 findings (`facts` naming the bound and `truncated`): since R15's `cursor` is null at the end, each read starts again from `since`, never from a cursor kept between reads, and a finding older than the window is never read and never uses up the bound. An item leaves on `held: false` only while its finding is inside the window; an item whose finding has left the window stays until its recipient disposes of it. *(not yet met: T37)*
- **R15** FINDINGs `security-tool-off`: one per event `file-safety.securityToolEvents({after, viewer})` answers (its R31) that switched a tool off (`PRIVATE_MODE_NOT_HONOURED`), read under the administrator's own viewer following `cursor` to at most 1,000 events (`facts` naming the bound and `truncated`), keyed `FINDING::security-tool-off::<tool_id>::<at>`, to each active administrator (`membership.activeAdmins`, its R86) and to nobody else. Its subject is the group's Civicsmith (no bundle); its detail names the tool, that it was switched off because it did not confirm the private mode its handling requires, and that it stays off until an administrator tests it again (`file-safety` R29); it names no file and no member. It is raised once per event and leaves when its recipient disposes of it or the tool is `on` again (`file-safety.securityTools`). A read that refuses or throws contributes no item and is named in `facts.failed` (R1).

### Invariants

- **R7** (K1489; `queue-producers` R11) No answer names a bundle, row or project the viewer may not see, and no count reveals one; an interest check, money detector or hypothesis inside a hidden project is neither shown nor counted to anyone outside it.
- **R8** (K1491, K1473, K1467) An item of R2 or R3 is never stored on a person, never moves a grade or a finding, and is never offered as a citation for a claim; its only acts are to dispose of it or take it up as a member's hunch or hypothesis.
- **R9** (Rule 7; K1504) While a check's or detector's gate is closed, R2 and R3 raise no item for it and count none: switching display on is the gate's opening, never a parameter of this read.
- **R10** No place is named in this module's behaviour or outward text.
- **R11** (DEC-131; K1473, K1491; K1536) Every member-facing sentence this module answers for an item of R2 or R3 (its `summary` and `detail`, and the words of its options) marks it "Hint · machine work" and calls what the machine raised a "hint", never a "signal" (DEC-131: "Signal" leaves member text). The `label` value `noticed`, the kinds and the item keys are unchanged. R4's, R5's and R6's items are not hints and carry no such mark.

### Suggestions

- **Open for BOB.**
  1. **R6's class.** The plan lists dated waits among "the new FINDING producers", but a wait the member set for themselves reads as their own to-do (an OBLIGATION, as `action-reminder`, `queue-producers` R18). The key leaves `<CLASS>` open; BOB picks one.
  2. **R2's and R3's recipients.** K1491 names no recipient; the draft sends each item to the members of the project the check runs in who may see all its inputs (K1489). A check run group-wide (no project) would then reach nobody: BOB's choice (administrators, or every member who may see all inputs).
  3. **Service names.** R2 and R3 follow the sibling drafts (`people` R24–R25 `checkResults`; `money-checks` R9 `noticed`), which already apply the gate, the per-project switch and sight; R9 here is then a second guard, kept so the queue never depends on one reader alone. The dated-wait read in `inquiry` is its job's (T33-45).
  4. **Uses** beyond Rule 3 (above).
  5. `queue` R1's `classOfKind` gains the five kinds (T33-83); the item words ("Noticed", the standing item's sentence) are the design stream's (NOTICE B36, B28), and the audit's note on "signal" (queue R48, queue-producers R24) applies to them.
- **Factory.** `noticeProducersOf(ctx, deps)`, exporting `noticeItems`; it registers nothing and holds no check row (it refuses nothing).
- **T35 (T35-67).** R12's episode start may be found by reading `securityMap` back from the call in 48-hour periods (hourly buckets) until a whole hour is found at whose end the level was not `High`, bounded by the 90 days kept; whether the level was `High` at an hour's end is `credentials` R45's rule applied to that hour (a `through` in the 24 hours before it, or that hour unusual). R13's 90-day window and 1,000-change bound are BOB's under K1881 (protective, a read's size). `queue` R1's `classOfKind` gains the two kinds (FINDING). R13's recipient reads `standards`' declaration author through the standard read the job finds (`standardRead`, its R5), the one new edge.
- **Tests.** A closed gate yields no item and no count (R9); a hidden project's check reaches no outsider (R7); a duty occurrence between its candidates reads "possibly overdue", after the latest "overdue", and the group's own checkpoint yields nothing (R5); a standing answer reaches only its author, once (R4).
