## Public

### Purpose

(T45; N880; Actions D13, D20; v0.2 rules 8, 16) What a reply to the group's action did, and what it set off: a member lays out a reply to a request for comment claim by claim (new evidence, disputed without evidence, not addressed), with the assistant's layout stored apart as a proposal; a member links a matter or project to the response it arose from; and, at signing, each open request for comment on a case is answered once as a line of fact, never a gate. A member does every act; nothing here rates a reply or counts a chain.

### Provides

Terms. An **action**, its entries, positions and legs are `actions`' (its Terms). A **response** is a `received` entry of an action (`actions` R15, R83).

**actionReplyBearing({target, ord, claims, author, viewer}), actionBearingPropose({target, ord, claims, proposer, viewer})** (`op=actionreplybearing`, `op=actionbearingpropose`)
- **R1** (T45; N880; S1 §C 7; Actions D13; v0.2 rule 16 "laid out claim by claim") For a `received` entry of a `request_for_comment`, a member lays out the reply claim by claim: `claims` as `action-grammar.bearingFindings` (its R24) shapes them, each `claim` the target of one of the action's `advances` or `rests_on` legs, each `bearing` of `BEARINGS`. Refusals in order, each writing nothing: `MACHINE_CANNOT_RECORD_BEARING` (an empty or machine author); `NO_TARGET`; `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` (absent and invisible alike, through `actions.actionRead`); `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `BEARING_NOT_RFC` (another kind, or an entry not `received`); `BEARING_REFUSED` (R24's findings), naming the item. A later layout appends; the read `bearingsOf({target, ord?, viewer})` answers the latest per entry with the earlier ones. `actionBearingPropose` stores a proposal apart, labelled (`record-grammar`'s `proposalLabel`), with `BEARING_REFUSED`'s shape check and never changing a layout; any credential may propose, a proposer's restatement replacing its own. Control: one claim per leg with a listed bearing lands; a claim not among the legs is refused.

**aroseFromLink({target, ord, arising, author, viewer}), aroseFrom({target, ord, viewer}), arisenFrom({project} | {plan, matter}, viewer)** (`op=arosefrom`)
- **R2** (T45; Actions D20; v0.2 rule 16, §6 `arose_from`; State Rules §5.1) A member records that a matter or project arose from the response at `ord`: `arising` is `{project}` (a project the author sees at `FULL`) or `{plan, matter}` (stored as given and never read here; `action-plans` sets it). Refusals in order, each writing nothing: `MACHINE_CANNOT_LINK`; `NO_TARGET`; `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`; `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `AROSE_FROM_NOT_A_RESPONSE` (the entry is not `received`); `NO_SUCH_PROJECT` (`membership.noSuchProject`); `AROSE_FROM_LINKED` (the same link stands). Appended, never edited. `aroseFrom` answers what arose from a response, `arisenFrom` the response a project or matter arose from, each withholding whole what the viewer may not see. It is the read `plan-work` R10 uses for its reach (projects linked both ways). It never counts a chain. Control: a link from a `received` entry lands; from a `sent` entry is refused.

**The open requests at signing** (T45; N880; Actions D13; v0.2 rule 8; reconciliation R2)
- **R3** (T45; N880; Actions D13; v0.2 rule 8 "each request for comment still open on the case is shown once as one line of fact") At start this module registers once with `case-inputs.registerOpenRequests` (its R8; `case-authoring` R70 before T47, N908) the source `openRequestsOn({case, members, viewer})`, in that requirement's names and shape: each `request_for_comment` the viewer may see, not `resolved` or `abandoned`, whose `advances` or `rests_on` legs name a bundle among `members` (the case's findings and inquiries, derived here from `members` itself), as `{whom, asked_at, window_closes, answers}`: `whom` R9's office by role and body as the action names it; `asked_at` its first `sent` entry's date; `window_closes` its clock entry's date as `action-clocks` answers it (null when the action states none); `answers` `{answered, claims}`, `claims` the claims of the latest R1 layout and `answered` those whose bearing is not `not_addressed`, or null with no reply recorded or no layout. It asks no reason, refuses nothing and writes nothing; it throws when it cannot read, so `case-inputs` answers `undetermined` (its R8) and `case-authoring`'s sign step says so, never `[]`. Control: a resolved request on the case is not answered.

**A reply that asks not to be quoted** (T48; N880; Actions D13, H17; v0.2 rule 8, "unless the reply itself says otherwise")
- **R10** (T48; N880; Actions D13, H17; v0.2 rule 8) `actionReplyQuoting({target, ord, quotable, words?, author, viewer})` (`op=actionreplyquoting`). For a `received` entry of a `request_for_comment`, a member records one of two things: that the reply says it may not be quoted (`quotable: false`), with the reply's own `words` that say so (1 to 500 characters, one line, no quote, backslash or line break); or that such a record is withdrawn (`quotable: true`).
  - Refusals, in order, each writing nothing: `MACHINE_CANNOT_RECORD_QUOTING` (C-150.8; an empty or machine author); `NO_TARGET`; `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` (absent and unseen alike, through `actions.actionRead`); `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `QUOTING_NOT_RFC` (C-150.9; another kind, or an entry that is not `received`); `QUOTING_REFUSED` (C-150.10; a `quotable` that is not a boolean, `words` missing with `false`, or malformed `words`).
  - Records are appended, never edited, and the latest per entry stands. `bearingsOf` (R1) also answers, per entry, `quotable` (`true` with nothing recorded) with its `words`, who recorded it and when.
  - It refuses no act of any module and enters nothing published: a declaration (DEC-13).
  - The rows' translations are BOB's drafts in DEC-83's form, their words an ASK to UX-DESIGN: C-150.8 "Only a member records what a reply says about being quoted. Nothing was written."; C-150.9 "Only a reply received to a request for comment can ask not to be quoted. Nothing was written."; C-150.10 "Say whether the reply may be quoted, and when it may not, give the reply's own words that say so, on one line of at most 500 characters. Nothing was written." They wait for T49's promotion stamp (plan rule 5 (2)).
  - Controls: `quotable: false` with words on a received reply lands and `bearingsOf` answers it; the same on a `sent` entry is refused `QUOTING_NOT_RFC`; a later `quotable: true` makes the entry read `true` again.
- **R11** (T48; N880; amends R3, R8; `case-inputs` R18) Each open-request row R3 registers also answers `quotable`: `false` when R10's latest record on any received reply of the request says so, else `true`. Control: a request whose reply asks not to be quoted answers `false`; one with no record, or whose record was withdrawn, answers `true`.
