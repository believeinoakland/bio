## Public

### Purpose

The settings a member and an administrator use day to day: the roster and the group's AI key (`members`), the member's own account (`account`), how the assistant serves the member, with their own Claude sign-in (`connect`), and their own notes (`notes`); and, since T47, who the group is (`group-identity`), its security tools and level (`security`) and its translation of the interface (`translations`). Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

**account** (Settings › Your account; the registry's 6 acts)
- **R3** Reads: `whoami` (the handle), `memberlanguage`, `expertiselist` (the member's own), `signerlist` (the member's own key). The handle is drawn fixed, with DEC-197's sentence ("Your handle is fixed: it names your work in the group and in published cases, and no one else can ever take it"), and no act changes it: no `handlechange` act is drawn, and `handle.changeable`, `handle.formerly` and `handle.refused.*` are not drawn. Links to `connect` and `notes`.

**connect** (Settings › The assistant; the registry's 7 acts and two re-points, `interface-shell` R21)

The rule of who pays is `ai.whopays`.
  - (K2960, K3004; `instance-setup` R81) `connect` also reads `bootstrap`'s `parts` and offers the sign-in only while `signin` is present.
