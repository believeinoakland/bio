## Public

### Purpose

The settings a member and an administrator use day to day: the roster and the group's AI key (`members`), the member's own account (`account`), how the assistant serves the member, with their own Claude sign-in (`connect`), and their own notes (`notes`); and, since T47, who the group is (`group-identity`), its security tools and level (`security`) and its translation of the interface (`translations`); and, since T51, what the record's terms and states mean (`glossary`, R59). Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.
