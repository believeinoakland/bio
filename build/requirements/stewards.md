## Public

### Purpose

(T45; N895; v0.2 rule 21; Bob, K2799) How anyone reports abuse of Civicsmith itself to Civicsmith's stewards, and what the stewards may do about it. The service is a Worker Civicsmith runs, apart from every group's Civicsmith, which transmits nothing to it (v0.2 rule 14). Anyone, without an account, may send a report of fabricated evidence, harassment, impersonation or other abuse, about a group's public identity or a published case. The service keeps the reports in its own store, tells the stewards one has arrived, and lets only the stewards read them and decide each one, with a reason: dismiss it, or withdraw a shared service (a shared profile contribution, an item of the template library, a public listing). No operation of the service can reach or change a group's record. It also serves a plain guide to what a group holds and who could compel it. It does not open to the public until Bob has named the stewards' contact address and the retention periods are set.

### Provides

Terms. A **report** is one accepted submission, with its reference (`ref`). A **steward** is a person named in the `STEWARD_KEYS` configuration (R10). A **decision** is a steward's recorded act on a report (R12). A **withdrawal** is a decision's remedy, standing until a steward reinstates it (R13). The service is **open** when R1's configuration holds, and **not yet open** otherwise. The **configuration** is the Worker's plain-text variables and secrets, never its source.

**Opening** (K2799; installer R48)
- **R1** The service is open only when all of these hold: `STEWARDS_ADDRESS` is exactly one e-mail address (one `local@domain`, at most 254 characters, no spaces, no comma), `REPORT_RETENTION_DAYS` and `CONTACT_RETENTION_DAYS` are each a whole number from 1 to 3650, `STEWARD_KEYS` names at least one steward (R10), and the secret `SOURCE_KEY` (R4) is at least 32 bytes. An absent, empty or malformed value is unset; the module holds no default for any of them. While not yet open: `GET /` serves a page saying the reporting service is not yet open, with no form; `POST /report` answers 503 `NOT_YET_OPEN` and stores and sends nothing; the guide (R7) is served without a contact line. The pages never name a value the configuration lacks or invent one. Control: the same request with all five set is accepted (R3).
- **R2** `checkOpen({wranglerJsonc, secretNames})` answers `null` when the deploy configuration would open the service (R1's three variables set in `vars` and `STEWARD_KEYS` and `SOURCE_KEY` among the secret names), and otherwise the list of what is missing or malformed, by name and in words. The module's deploy script runs it first and refuses to deploy, deploying nothing, on any answer other than `null`; it also refuses when `account_id` is not the project's pinned account (R21). It never prints a secret's value. Control: a configuration lacking only `STEWARDS_ADDRESS` is refused naming it.

**The intake: `GET /`, `POST /report`** (v0.2 rule 21; K1881)
- **R3** `POST /report` takes, as a form or JSON, `{kind, concerns, description, contact?}`, and needs no account, sign-in, cookie or credential. `kind` is one of `KINDS`: `fabricated_evidence`, `harassment`, `impersonation`, `other`. `concerns` is either a group's public identity (a slug in `GROUP_SLUG_RE`'s grammar, or an `https://` address of a group's Civicsmith) or the `https://` address of a published case; at most 2,048 characters. `description` is plain text, 1 to 8,000 characters. `contact` is optional, at most 254 characters, stored as given and never checked or contacted by the service. Refusals in order, each 400 with the field named in words and storing nothing: `REPORT_BODY_TOO_LARGE` (a body over 16 KiB, read no further), `REPORT_KIND_UNKNOWN`, `REPORT_CONCERNS_INVALID`, `REPORT_DESCRIPTION_INVALID`, `REPORT_CONTACT_INVALID`. An accepted report answers `{ok: true, ref}` (and, to a form, a page showing the reference and saying a steward replies only by the contact given, if any) and is stored with its received time. Nothing else of the request is stored. Control: each field one character past its cap is refused; at its cap it is accepted.
- **R4** (K1881) Protective limits, each refused 429 `REPORT_RATE_LIMITED` with `retryAfter` in whole seconds, storing and sending nothing: at most 5 accepted reports per source in any hour and 20 in any day, and at most 200 for the whole service in any day. A source is a keyed fingerprint of the connecting address (`CF-Connecting-IP`, HMAC-SHA-256 under `SOURCE_KEY`), one shared source when none is stated; the counts hold the fingerprint, the window and the count only, and are dropped once their window has passed. While 1,000 reports stand undecided, `POST /report` answers 503 `INTAKE_FULL`, storing nothing. A count that cannot be read or written refuses the report (the intake fails closed). No address, fingerprint or count is ever shown or logged. Control: the sixth report in an hour from one source is refused; one from another source is accepted.
- **R5** `GET /` serves, while open, the report form and a notice before the form saying in plain words: what a report is for (abuse of Civicsmith itself: fabricated evidence, harassment, impersonation); that the stewards may only withdraw shared services and never edit or delete a group's record; that the contact field is optional and is used only to reply; what is kept, who reads it (the stewards only) and for how long (`REPORT_RETENTION_DAYS`, `CONTACT_RETENTION_DAYS` after the decision, as numbers); that the address `STEWARDS_ADDRESS` also receives reports by e-mail; and that the service gives no legal advice. It names those who decide as "Civicsmith's stewards" and Believe in Oakland as responsible for them, never a steward's name (R23; Bob, K2819 (1)).
- **R6** Any other path or method is answered 404 or 405 with a plain page.

**The guide: `GET /guide`** (v0.2 rule 21; installer R48)
- **R7** `GET /guide` serves the plain guide to what a group holds and who could compel it, in exactly the words of installer R48's third part ("What a group holds, and who could compel it"), and, after it, what this service holds (reports, their decisions and withdrawals) and who could be asked or compelled to hand that over (Civicsmith's stewards, and the service's hosting provider under the law that binds it). It gives no legal advice and says so. A test compares the shared words with the installer README's byte for byte. (T46; N923; K2878, K2884) Only once `installer` R48's README part is written (T46-60, which waits on the address Bob names, K2799): the shared words are the installer README's third part, and R7's test reads them from `newgroup/README.md` (the earlier module's), no longer from this module's own copy of them (K2878's interim); until then that interim stands and this amendment waits. *(not yet met: T47)*

**The stewards: `GET /steward`, `POST /steward/signin`, `POST /steward/signout`** (K1874, K1881)
- **R8** A steward signs in by `POST /steward/signin` with `{name, key}` in the body. A name and key matching an entry of `STEWARD_KEYS` sets a session cookie (`HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Strict; Path=/steward`), an opaque random value, valid for 12 hours from sign-in and ended by `POST /steward/signout`; anything else answers 401 `STEWARD_SIGNIN_REFUSED`, the same answer whether the name or the key was wrong. At most 5 refused sign-ins per source in any 15 minutes (R4's fingerprint), then 429 `STEWARD_SIGNIN_LIMITED` until the window passes, even for a right key.
- **R9** (K1874) No steward key or session ever travels in an address. Every `/steward/` route but the sign-in page reads the session only from the cookie; a `/steward/` request whose address carries a query string is refused 400 `CREDENTIAL_IN_ADDRESS`, reading nothing; every state-changing `/steward/` request whose `Origin` is not the service's own is refused 403, changing nothing. No key, session value or hash of either appears in any page, log, error text or notification. A test sends a sentinel key and session in a query string and finds them refused and in no output.
- **R10** `STEWARD_KEYS` is a secret holding one line per steward, `name:sha256` (the hex SHA-256 of that steward's key, at least 32 random bytes; the key itself never held by the service). `name` is 1 to 64 printable characters. Every steward act records the steward's `name`. A steward removed from `STEWARD_KEYS` can no longer sign in, and her sessions end at their next use.

**Reading and deciding: `GET /steward/reports`, `GET /steward/report/<ref>`, `POST /steward/decide`, `POST /steward/reinstate`, `GET /steward/withdrawals`**
- **R11** With a steward session only (otherwise 401 `STEWARD_SESSION_REQUIRED`, reading nothing), `GET /steward/reports` lists reports newest first, at most 100 per page, each with `ref`, `kind`, `concerns`, received time and state (`undecided`, `dismissed`, `withdrawn`); `GET /steward/report/<ref>` shows one with its description, its contact while kept (R16), and every decision on it. An unknown `ref` answers 404 `NO_SUCH_REPORT`. No route outside `/steward/` reads a report, a decision or a count.
- **R12** `POST /steward/decide` takes `{ref, decision, reason, withdraw?}`. `decision` is one of `DECISIONS`: `dismiss` or `withdraw`. `reason` is 1 to 4,000 characters, required. For `withdraw`, `withdraw` is `{service, item}`: `service` one of `SHARED_SERVICES` (`shared_profile`, `template_library`, `public_listing`) and `item` the shared item's identifier, 1 to 512 characters. Refusals in order, each writing nothing: `STEWARD_SESSION_REQUIRED`; `NO_SUCH_REPORT`; `DECISION_UNKNOWN`; `NO_REASON`; `WITHDRAW_TARGET_INVALID` (a missing or malformed target, a `service` outside `SHARED_SERVICES`). An accepted decision is appended with the steward's name and time and never edited or deleted (R16 aside); a later decision on the same report appends and becomes its state. `withdraw` adds the target to the withdrawal register (R13) unless it already stands there (then `already: true`, the decision still recorded). Control: a `withdraw` naming any other service, a group, a record or a bundle is refused.
- **R13** The withdrawal register lists each standing withdrawal: `{service, item, since, ref}`. `POST /steward/reinstate` with `{service, item, reason}` ends a standing withdrawal by a steward's act with a reason (`NO_REASON`; `NOT_WITHDRAWN` when none stands), recorded like a decision. `GET /steward/withdrawals` answers the register to a steward as canonical JSON with its SHA-256 digest, for the maintainers of the shared services to apply; it holds no description, contact or reporter detail. The register is also public (R22; Bob, K2819 (5)).

**Notification** (K2799)
- **R14** When a report is accepted and `STEWARDS_ADDRESS` is set, one e-mail is sent to that address and no other, through the service's one mail binding, saying a report has arrived, with its `ref`, `kind` and received time and the address of the stewards' page. It carries no description, `concerns`, contact, key or session. While the address is unset nothing is sent (R1 also refuses the report). A notification that fails never changes the intake's answer and is logged by `ref` only. Nothing is ever sent to a reporter or to a group. Control: with the address set, the fake mail binding records exactly one message to it per accepted report; with it unset, none.

**Retention**
- **R15** The service purges on a schedule (at least daily): a report's `contact` is erased `CONTACT_RETENTION_DAYS` after its latest decision, and the whole report with its decisions is deleted `REPORT_RETENTION_DAYS` after its latest decision, except a decision whose withdrawal still stands, which is kept, holding no description or contact, until `REPORT_RETENTION_DAYS` after its reinstatement. An undecided report is never purged. Each purge appends a count-only line (how many contacts erased, reports deleted), never a `ref` or content. With either value unset the service is not yet open (R1) and the purge deletes nothing. Control: a report decided one day before its period is kept; one day after, it is gone and `NO_SUCH_REPORT`. The deploy configuration the module commits sets `CONTACT_RETENTION_DAYS` to 90 and `REPORT_RETENTION_DAYS` to 365 (Bob, K2819 (2)).

### Invariants

- **R16** (v0.2 rule 21; K2799) Remedies are limited by construction. The Worker declares exactly these bindings: one Durable Object store of its own (`STORE`, SQLite), one send-e-mail binding (`NOTIFY`, R14), its plain-text variables and its secrets (`STEWARD_KEYS`, `SOURCE_KEY`), and nothing else: no service binding, no bucket, no other store, no credential of any group's Civicsmith or of any hosting account. Its code makes no outbound request (`fetch` to any address) other than R14's mail. No route, decision or vocabulary names a group's record, a bundle, a member or an instance; the only act with an effect outside a report is R13's register. A test reads the configuration and the source and finds these.
- **R17** (v0.2 rule 14) A group's Civicsmith sends nothing here and holds no route to it; the service reads nothing from any group's Civicsmith.
- **R18** (DEC-122 (3); installer R35) No page the service serves names a resource on another origin in any `src`, `href`, `@import` or `url()`; there is no captcha or script from elsewhere.
- **R19** (DEC-149; installer R40, R42) Every page names Civicsmith and calls a group's own "a group's Civicsmith", never "copy", "instance" or "plane"; it names Believe in Oakland only as the publisher of Civicsmith. No place is named in this module's behaviour or words.
- **R20** Every text a reporter supplied is HTML-escaped wherever it is shown. Nothing here uses AI or sends a report anywhere but R14's notice (which holds no report text).
- **R21** Its configuration pins the project's Cloudflare account (`20b533579290b9b93168345edd3b7f72`), as `installer` R28.
- **R22** (Bob, K2819 (5)) `GET /withdrawals`, while open, serves anyone without sign-in the public register of standing withdrawals: for each, `{service, item, since, kind}` (`kind` the report's `KINDS` value), newest first, as a plain page and, with `Accept: application/json`, as canonical JSON. It never shows a report's `ref`, description, `concerns` beyond the withdrawn item, contact, reporter detail, reason or steward's name; a reinstated withdrawal leaves it. Telling the affected group is a steward's own act by mail where a contact is known (Suggestions). Control: a withdrawal shows; after reinstatement it is gone; a dismissed report never shows.
- **R23** (Bob, K2819 (1)) No page or answer outside `/steward/` names a steward: the public pages say "Civicsmith's stewards" and name Believe in Oakland as responsible; steward names appear only to a signed-in steward (R10, R11). Control: a decision's steward name is absent from `GET /`, `/guide`, `/withdrawals` and R14's e-mail.
