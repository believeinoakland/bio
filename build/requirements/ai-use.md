## Public

### Purpose

What each AI account has spent and may spend. It counts each use against the account that paid and the member whose act it was, holds each account owner's money limits, judges a use against them, and answers each owner its own use and each member theirs. It holds no credential, and it does not choose which account pays (that is `ai-accounts`').

### Provides

Terms. An **owner** is the holder of one AI account: `group` (its administrators act), `project:<id>` (any one of its owners acts) or `member:<id>` (that member alone acts) (`ai-accounts` R10, R11, R14). A **use** is an `ai-accounts.USE_KINDS` entry, its R14's nine (`ask`, `draft`, `run`, `standing`, `explore`, `enquire`, `read`, `transcribe`, `account`; T43, N862, DEC-190). A **limit** is `{owner, scope, unit, period, amount, inclusive}`. A **period** is the current local `day` or `month` of the group's calendar (`civil-time`); a month's limit resets on the 1st. With no limit set, nothing is capped here (a provider's own spend limit answers as `ai-runs` states). `viewer` and `by` are the control plane's stamps. Every refusal names `code` and carries its row's `check` and `translation` (R8).

**Counting** (`countUsage`)
- **R1** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; AMEND; B8; BR22; amends `ai-runs` R48, R49) `countUsage({owner, member, use, mode, entry_id, label, usage, calls, at})` is called in the caller's transaction.
  - `owner` is `group`, `project:<id>` or `member:<id>`; `entry_id` and `label` (`agent-model` R26's: provider, host, model version, effort, account level, owner) replace the model string and carry the effort (R15).
  - `usage` is `agent-model` R5's record. It adds to the counter `ai_usage`, kept per owner, member, local day (`civil-time.localDay`) and `use`: the token sums (reasoning included), `calls` (a `null` counted as one), `cost_reported_sum` and `cost_estimated_sum` per unit, kept apart and never added together, and `unpriced_calls`, the calls that stated no cost and had no estimate. A figure `unstated` is never counted as 0: a sum over a day with any unstated figure keeps `unstated` listing it.
  - `countAskUsage({member, mode, entry_id, label, usage, calls, at})` counts an ask or a draft the same way, `mode` `ask` or `draft`, its owner as `accountFor` answered it; any other `mode` is refused `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID`. It sums the figures `usage` carries and reads no price table (`agent-model` R13 estimates; K2376).
  - A malformed entry is refused `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID`, counting nothing. The counter holds no content, question, address or secret, and is `export: "admin-only"`. Rows from before T40 are "not recorded" and count toward no limit; rows from before this change keep their dollars as `cost_estimated_sum` in `usd` with `entry_id` null.
  - Test: two calls, one stating a cost and one stating none but priced, sum into `cost_reported_sum` and `cost_estimated_sum` separately; a call with neither adds to `unpriced_calls` and to no sum; `cache_read` unstated stays unstated. Control: a record with a negative token count is refused and counts nothing.

**Limits** (`aiLimitSet`)
- **R2** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; AMEND; D39; B4; AIR26; amends `ai-runs` R50's setting half) `aiLimitSet({owner, scope, unit, period, amount, inclusive?, by})`.
  - `scope` is `overall`, a `USE_KINDS` entry, or `per_member` for a team or project account. `unit` is `usd`, `tokens` or `calls`. `period` is `day` or `month`. `amount` is a positive number (`usd` to the cent; the others whole), or `null` to remove the limit. `inclusive` (default `true`) is allowed only for a use scope. Anything else is `AI_LIMIT_INVALID`, naming the field.
  - **`usd` is allowed only on an account whose plan's money is `reported` or `priced`; on `none` or OPEN it is refused `LIMIT_UNIT_UNAVAILABLE`.** The refusal follows the plan, not the kind of account: a sign-in plan reporting money would allow `usd`, a flat-fee API-shaped plan would not. (The old clause "`usd` on a member's sign-in is refused" retires.) `tokens` and `calls` are always allowed.
  - Who may set it is the owner's own act (`NOT_YOUR_CEILING`; the project owner refusals of `ai-accounts`; `NOT_AN_ADMIN`). At most one limit per owner, scope, unit and period; each set appended with who and when. The migration and the dropped `ai_ceilings` (T42) are as before. Idempotent.
  - Test: `usd` on a fake plan with money `none` refuses `LIMIT_UNIT_UNAVAILABLE`; on `reported` and on `priced` it is set; on OPEN it refuses; `tokens` is set on all. Control: the same owner and plan changed to money `reported` accepts `usd`.

**Judging** (`useCheck`)
- **R3** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; AMEND; D38 C, D39; B3, B5; K2400; BR23; amends R3) `useCheck({owner, member, use, at, entry_id?})` answers `null` or `AI_LIMIT_REACHED` with `{whose, scope, unit, period, source, resets_at?, walkable}` and its plain-words row, naming no cost.
  - `source` is `own` (a limit set by this account's owner, judged **before** the call from the counter) or `provider` (a provider limit recorded by R18 and not yet reset, judged from the record **after** the provider has said it; R18). It refuses when, for the current period: the owner's `use` limit, its `overall` limit (over the uses that are not exclusive, unless `use`'s limit is exclusive) or its `per_member` limit (over `member`'s use of that account) has reached its amount; or a provider limit covering the account (or the entry) has not reached its `resets_at`.
  - It judges only the paying account's limits (D38: never another account's money limits). A `usd` limit on an act whose `entry_id` carries neither a price nor a reported cost is refused `LIMIT_UNIT_UNAVAILABLE` with `unjudged`, because the act's cost cannot be measured (R21); never counted as zero.
  - `EXPLORE_NOT_ENABLED` when the owner's `explore` setting is `no` (A5, B6). With `yes` and no `explore` limit held, `explore` is judged against `overall` alone; `ask` without that day's approval is R6's answer. The owner's `explore` value is read through `ai-accounts.accountUsesOf` (its R17), no viewer; it reads `no` when the account is not held, cannot be read, or holds a value other than `no`, `ask` or `yes` (fail closed).
  - It writes nothing and never throws. A counter that cannot be read answers the refusal (fail closed) with `unjudged: true` and no `scope`, `unit` or `period`.
  - `walkable` is the ordered-list exception (R20): `true` only on an own or provider limit; `false` on `EXPLORE_NOT_ENABLED`, on an `unjudged` refusal and on any use its owner switched off.
  - Test: an own limit reached answers `source: "own"`, `walkable: true`; a recorded provider limit with a future `resets_at` answers `source: "provider"` and that time; after `resets_at` (pinned clock) it answers `null`; an unreadable counter answers `unjudged` with `walkable: false`. Control: under every limit it answers `null`.

Neither writes. Test: a `none` plan's month answers tokens and calls and `not_measurable: true`, no cost field equal to 0; a priced plan answers both cost sums; a non-owner is refused as R2 refuses. Control: an owner of a reported plan sees `cost_reported_sum`.
- **R5** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; AMEND; B7; BR23; amends R5) `limitsReached({viewer, at})` answers, for each account the viewer owns, each limit first reached in its current period: `{owner, scope, unit, period, period_start, reached_at, source, resets_at?}`, with a stable key per owner, limit and period (for a provider limit, per owner, kind and `resets_at`). **Use over time** (T48-29; N1026; DEC-215 (1); K3138, K3198)

**Provider-side limits, money and estimates, alerts, reconciliation, re-testing and health counts (T53; K5457, K5459, K5480, K5481, K5487)**

- **R18** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; BR23) `recordProviderLimit({owner, account_id, entry_id, kind, scope, resets_at, at})` records a provider limit that `agent-model` returned as a `limit` outcome (`kind`: `spend`, `quota`, `rate_window`, `plan_window`; `scope`: `account` or `entry`; `resets_at` null where the provider gave none), in the caller's transaction, and is idempotent per (account, entry, kind, `resets_at`). It is a **limit reached, not an error**: it never counts as a failure for health. A recorded limit stands until `resets_at`; with `resets_at` null it stands until `PROVIDER_REPROBE_MS` (BOB's) passes, after which `useCheck` lets one call through and a repeat limit re-records it. A malformed entry is refused `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID`. Never throws. Test: a spent-cap `limit` outcome recorded with a reset time makes `useCheck` refuse (`source: "provider"`) until that time and not after; the same call recorded twice stores one row; a null reset time allows one probe after the interval. Control: a plain rate-limit `error` outcome is not recorded here.

- **R21** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; BR22; AIR26) **Money appears only where the plan reports it or the catalogue prices it.** A figure in money is `cost_reported` (the provider's or the gateway log's statement) or `cost_estimated` (the entry's catalogue price at its date); wherever neither exists the answer is `not_measurable` (word key `ai.use.notmeasurable`), with tokens and calls; money is never 0 for an unmeasured use, and a sum of money says how many calls were `unpriced_calls`. Test: a `none` plan's act is `not_measurable`, tokens shown; a priced act shows `cost_estimated` apart from a reported one; a euro-unit cost is not added into a `usd` sum. - **R22** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; BR24; AIR26) `estimateSwitch({run, from_account, to_entry, at, viewer})` answers the added cost of moving the run to another entry, offered **before** the hop is shown (`ai-accounts`' hop notice): the cold-cache cost of resending the run's prefix, `{low, high, unit}` from the run's latest stated input tokens times the target entry's input price (low) and cache-write price (high), plus `payer_change` as `ai-accounts` R50 states it (`{from_owner, to_owner}` or null); null with `not_known` where the target is unpriced or the run states no input tokens, never zero. To the paying accounts' owners and the member whose run it is. Writes nothing; never throws. Test: a run with stated input tokens on a fake target with prices answers the range; an unpriced target answers `not_known`; a same-owner hop says `payer_change: null`. Control: a non-owner, non-member is refused as R12 refuses.

- **R23** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; BR24) `aiThresholdSet({owner, scope, unit, period, kind, level, by})` sets an owner's alert level: `kind` `percent` (of the matching limit, from `THRESHOLD_PERCENT_MIN` to `THRESHOLD_PERCENT_MAX`) or `amount` (a positive number in the unit); several per owner, scope, unit and period; `level: null` removes one. `unit` and `scope` follow R2's rules (including `usd` only where the plan's money allows). - **R24** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; BR24) `thresholdsReached({viewer, at})` answers, for each account the viewer owns, each threshold first crossed in its current period: `{owner, scope, unit, period, period_start, level, crossed_at, key}`, a stable key per owner, threshold and period, so `notice-producers`, which calls this read, raises one alert (word key `ai.alert.threshold`) and no more; it writes nothing and never throws.
