## Public

### Purpose

The library of wizard scripts: the authored step lists a wizard walks a member through on the real screens (DEC-120). Two libraries: the Civicsmith library, shipped with the release, its content approved by Bob, read-only to every group; and each group's own, governed like filing templates (versioned, never edited; approved by a project owner, widened by an administrator; retired, never deleted) (DEC-121 (1)). It holds the checks every script passes, serves the scripts that start on a screen to every member's session with no AI credential and no key, and keeps unattributed daily use tallies. A machine proposes and never approves. A script never says or submits anything for a member: what a step places in a field is a labelled draft, the member's words only when the member keeps or edits it (K1364 B3). It runs no wizard: the runner, the docked guide and the screens are the interface's (the UX stream's).

### Provides

Terms. A **script** is R1's record; a **version** R1's version. A version's **state** is one of `draft`, `submitted`, `approved`, `updated` (an approved version a later approved one follows) or `withdrawn`. A script is **retired** when withdrawn from use whole (R9). A version is **offered** when it is the latest `approved` version of a script that is not retired and not `broken` (R13), and the viewer may see it (R20); an `updated` version stays readable and is not offered (K1363 B9). A **step** is R2's. A **screen** is an id of the registered screen registry; an **act** an op the registry lists for a screen. A version id is written `<script>@<version>`. A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, code, check, translation, ...}`, naming the step where R12 says so. `author`, `by`, `proposer` and `viewer` are the control plane's stamps.

**Registration at start: `wizardRegister({screens, ops, machineRefused, machineDrafts, library})`** (in-process; `plane` R19)

**The script and its versions**
- **R1** A script is `{id, origin, scope, name, start, required, versions}`. `id` is an opaque `WIZ-` id, never a counter. `origin` is `civicsmith` (the Civicsmith library) or `group` (`imported` reserved, never written in this version; DEC-121 (8), "designed now, built later"). `scope` is `{project}`, the project it was drafted in, or `group` once R7 widens it. `name` is one line of 1–200 characters (`WIZARD_NAME_REFUSED`). `start` is the screen its first step names. `required` is true only for origin `civicsmith`. A version is `{script, version, steps, sha, state, author, contributors, derived_from, approved, ended}`; `version` counts from 1 within its script; `sha` is the SHA-256 (lowercase hex) of the steps in canonical form; once a version leaves `draft`, its steps and `sha` never change. (DEC-121 (1)) *(not yet met: T31)*
- **R2** A step is `{screen, act, what, why, draft?}`: `act` is null or an op the registry lists for `screen`; `what` and `why` are 1–300 characters each; `draft`, when present, is one of `{text}` (the script's own words, at most 4,000 characters), `{template}` (a filing-template version `filing-templates` R25 offers) or `{machine: <op>}` (one of the registered machine-draft ops), and is always answered labelled with its kind. A step says what the member does and why; it never acts. A draft is placed for the member, never submitted: it becomes the member's words only by the member's own act of keeping or editing it, and no step submits, signs or files (the member alone presses the act's button, and the act runs its own checks, reason and receipt). (DEC-120 (2); K1364 B3) *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizardDraft({project, name, recorded | from, author, viewer})** (`op=wizarddraft`) → `{ok, script, version, sha, state: "draft"}`
- **R3** Creates a script and its first draft, or a new draft version of the script `from` names. `recorded` is a list of `{screen, act}` and nothing else: a step carrying any other key is refused `WIZARD_RECORDING_CARRIES_VALUES`, so a recording never holds a typed value (DEC-121 (2): screens and acts, never values); each recorded step is kept as the version's recorded pairs, its `what` and `why` empty until revised (R4). `from` is a proposal (R5) or an approved version the author may see (a derivative). With neither, a blank start, the author needs a live editor grant (R8). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_WIZARD` (a machine or unstamped author); `NO_SUCH_WIZARD` (a `from` absent or not visible, one answer); `WIZARD_SCOPE_REFUSED` (the author is not a joined participant of the project, `membership` R54); `WIZARD_EDITOR_NOT_GRANTED`; `WIZARD_RECORDING_CARRIES_VALUES`; `WIZARD_NAME_REFUSED`. *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizardRevise({version, steps, adopt?, author, viewer})** (`op=wizardrevise`)
- **R4** Replaces a draft's steps by a new revision, keeping every earlier revision with its author and time. Without a live editor grant (R8), the new list's `(screen, act)` pairs must be a sub-multiset of the version's recorded or adopted pairs: the author may reword, delete or reorder, never add; otherwise it is refused `WIZARD_EDITOR_NOT_GRANTED`. `adopt: <proposal>` takes a proposal's steps (R5) as the revision. The author may run their own draft (R11) before approval. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_WIZARD`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; `WIZARD_SCOPE_REFUSED` (anyone but the version's author); `NOT_A_DRAFT`; `WIZARD_EDITOR_NOT_GRANTED`; R2's shape refusals (`WIZARD_STEP_REFUSED`, naming the step). (DEC-121 (2)) *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizardPropose({project | script, steps, why, proposer, viewer})** (`op=wizardpropose`)
- **R5** Any credential may propose steps, for a project or for a named script. A proposal is stored apart under an opaque `WZP-` id, labelled `proposalLabel(proposer, "wizard")` (`record-grammar` R42), with `why` of 1–1,000 characters. It becomes a draft only when a member names it as `from` (R3) or adopts it (R4), and its run then joins `contributors` (every member who revised the version and every adopted proposal's run, dated). Refusals in order: `WIZARD_NO_PROPOSER`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; R2's shape refusals; `WIZARD_WHY_REFUSED`. (DEC-121 (2)–(3), as `filing-templates` R6) *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizardSubmit({version, author, viewer})** (`op=wizardsubmit`)
- **R6** Moves a draft to `submitted` and fixes its steps and `sha`. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_WIZARD`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; `WIZARD_SCOPE_REFUSED`; `NOT_A_DRAFT`; R12's refusals against the registration, each naming its step. *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizardApprove({version, widen?, by, viewer})** (`op=wizardapprove`)
- **R7** Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE_WIZARD`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; `NOT_SUBMITTED` (or, with `widen`, `WIZARD_NOT_APPROVED`); `NOT_AN_APPROVER` (an owner of the script's project, `membership` R65, approves; `widen: true` makes an approved project script group-wide and needs an administrator, `membership` R64); `APPROVER_IS_AUTHOR` (the approver is the version's author and no other member contributed, `filing-templates` R10's rule); R12's refusals, run again. No review step precedes approval (DEC-121 names approval only; K1363 B9). On success the version is `approved` with the approver and the instant; the script's previous approved version becomes `updated`, naming its successor. Widening records the administrator and instant and changes no version. (DEC-121 (1)) *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizardEditorGrant({member, by})** (`op=wizardeditorgrant`), **wizardEditorRevoke({grant, by})** (`op=wizardeditorrevoke`)
- **R8** An administrator grants a member the advanced editor (a blank start, R3; adding steps, R4) and revokes it. Anyone else is refused `NOT_AN_ADMIN` (`membership` R84's one answer). A grant is recorded with the administrator and instant under an opaque `WEG-` id; a revocation is appended, never a deletion; a second revocation answers `existed: true` with the first. A grant is live while it is not revoked. (DEC-121 (2)) *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizardRetire({script, version?, reason, by, viewer})** (`op=wizardretire`)
- **R9** As `filing-templates` R11: without `version`, retires the whole script, by an approver of its scope (R7's right): none of its versions is offered again, and each stays readable with the retirement's reason; with `version`, withdraws a draft or submitted version, by its author (`withdrawn`). `reason` is 1–500 characters (`WIZARD_REASON_REFUSED`). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE_WIZARD`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; `WIZARD_NOT_THE_GROUPS` (a `civicsmith` script answers this to every write, R3–R9 included: groups cannot edit or retire required flows, DEC-121 (5)); `NOT_AN_APPROVER` (or, for a withdrawal, `WIZARD_SCOPE_REFUSED` for anyone but the author, and `NOT_A_DRAFT` for a version past submission); `WIZARD_REASON_REFUSED`; `WIZARD_ALREADY_ENDED` (answered with the first ending). *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizards({state?, viewer})** (`op=wizards`), **wizardRead({script, version?, viewer})** (`op=wizardread`)
- **R10** `wizards` lists, to the owners of a script's project and to administrators, the scripts the viewer may see, newest first, at most 200, with `truncated`: by default every offered version, each with its state, author, contributors, approver and dates, `broken` (R13) and its use (R15); `state` lists instead drafts, submitted, withdrawn, retired or `broken` versions, or open proposals (`proposed`). `wizardRead` answers one version (the latest approved by default) with its steps and its whole attribution. Writes nothing. *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizardsAt({screen, viewer})** (`op=wizardsat`)
- **R11** Answers the offered scripts whose `start` is `screen`, each `{id, version, name, steps, approver, finished}` (`finished` R15's finish count), plus the caller's own drafts marked `draft: true`, so an author may run their own draft privately. An unknown screen answers `[]`. It needs no AI credential and no key, and is reached by every member's session (DEC-120 (1): wizards run for every group with no AI and no key). Writes nothing. (DEC-121 (6)) *(not yet met: T31)*

**checkScript(steps, {screens, ops, machineRefused, machineDrafts, offered?})** (pure; `op=wizardcheck`)
- **R12** Answers `{refusals, warnings}`, each naming its step. Refusals: `WIZARD_NO_STEPS`; `WIZARD_SCREEN_UNKNOWN`; `WIZARD_ACT_UNKNOWN` (an act that is not an op the registry lists for its screen); `WIZARD_STEP_NO_WHY`; `WIZARD_DRAFT_REFUSED` (a draft whose source is not one of R2's three, a `{template}` not offered, or a `{machine}` op not registered); `WIZARD_STEP_CONCLUDES` (a step carrying a `draft` whose act is in `machineRefused`, unless the draft is `{machine: <op>}` with `<op>` in `machineDrafts`, the labelled drafts the canon already allows on a member's act, such as DEC-101's "what changed": a draft from doctrine or a template is never placed on an act a machine is refused, and every draft stays a labelled draft that becomes the member's words only by the member's own act; K1364 B3, K1368). Warnings: `WIZARD_TRIVIAL` (one step) and `WIZARD_DUPLICATE` (the same `(screen, act)` list as an offered script). Whether a step's words tell a member what to conclude is not judged here: the assistant's critique (`skills` R32) and the approving member judge it (K1364 B3). `op=wizardcheck` serves it as a read to any credential, `ai` included, against the registration, so a wizard the assistant plans on the fly passes the same checks (DEC-120 (1)). Never throws on a list; never writes. (DEC-121 (4)) *(not yet met: T31)*

**Registration and breaks: brokenScripts({after?, limit?, viewer})**
- **R13** `wizardRegister` is called once per construction of the object (`plane` R19), before the first request, with the screen registry `[{id, acts}]`, the member op table, `machineRefused` (`affordances`' `MACHINE_REFUSALS` keys), `machineDrafts` (the op names of the labelled machine drafts, `case-authoring` R39's `whatchangedpropose` and `escalationreasondraft`) and the Civicsmith library; a second call is refused (`WIZARD_ALREADY_REGISTERED`), and with none made every screen is unknown. `registeredScreens()` answers the registry as registered (`[]` before registration), for `affordances` R37's no-target answer. At registration every offered `group` script is checked again (R12): one that fails is `broken`, withheld from R11 and never shown broken, recorded with its first refusal and the instant it was found; a `broken` script that passes again returns, recorded likewise. `brokenScripts` lists each break and each return, at most 500 per page in (instant, version) order with `cursor` and `truncated`, for `queue-producers` R32. Writes nothing but the break log. (DEC-121 (5): an optional flow that stops matching the screens is withdrawn until fixed, its owner told) *(not yet met: T31)*

**requiredFailures({screens, ops, machineRefused, machineDrafts, library})** (pure)
- **R14** Answers each `required` script of the Civicsmith library that R12 refuses, with its first refusal; `[]` when every one passes. For the plane's release suite (`plane` R19): a failing required flow blocks the release. (DEC-121 (5)) *(not yet met: T31)*

**wizardProgress({script, version, event, step?})** (`op=wizardprogress`), **wizardUse({script, viewer})** (`op=wizarduse`)
- **R15** `event` is `start`, `step` (with `step`, the step reached) or `finish`. Each call adds one to an unattributed tally per (version, event, step) and per day. No member id, viewer, case, project or instant finer than the day is kept, and nothing else of the call. There is no abandon event: stopping is a non-event, and where members stop is read only as the drop between step counts. `wizardUse` answers the tallies, by version and day, to the script's project owners and its version's author. (DEC-121 (6); DEC-120: "a non-event"; K1364 B4) *(not yet met: T31)*

**tallyRefusal(op, code)** (in-process), **wizardCandidates({viewer})** (`op=wizardcandidates`)
- **R16** `tallyRefusal` adds one to an unattributed tally per (op, code) and per day, and keeps nothing else. `wizardCandidates` answers, to project owners and administrators, the screens and steps where offered scripts' step counts drop most and the (op, code) pairs most refused, never a member, case, target or project. (DEC-121 (6): "suggests candidate flows from where members abandon or are refused"; K1364 B4) *(not yet met: T31)*

**submittedFor({after?, limit?, viewer})**
- **R17** Lists every (version, owner) pair where the version is `submitted` and the owner is one of its project's owners who may approve it (R7; a `group` script's, the administrators), with the script's name and the author, at most 500 per page with `cursor` and `truncated`, for `queue-producers` R33. Writes nothing. *(not yet met: T31)*

### Invariants

- **R18** Nothing is deleted. Scripts, versions, revisions, proposals, editor grants and revocations, approvals, widenings, withdrawals, retirements, the break log and the tallies are append-only, in this module's own tables, declared to purge (K23). Every name in attribution (author, contributor, approver, ender, grantor) is held by value beside the member id. *(not yet met: T31)*
- **R19** A machine writes only a proposal (R5) and nothing else; R15's and R16's tallies are written for a member's session or by the door and carry no identity. A machine never drafts, revises, submits, approves, widens, grants, retires or withdraws (DEC-121 (1); Design Requirement 12; DEC-24). No step, and nothing here, submits, signs or files for a member (K1364 B3). *(not yet met: T31)*
- **R20** Visibility: a script of a project is seen by whoever may see the project (`viewerPredicate`); a `group` script and a Civicsmith script by every member; every read and act answers a script the viewer may not see as absent (`NO_SUCH_WIZARD`), and no count names one. Each refusal carries its row, held in this module's own `checks.mjs`, a new check family. No place, law or venue is in this module's behaviour or outward text. *(not yet met: T31)*
