## Public

### Purpose

The library of wizard scripts: the authored step lists a wizard walks a member through on the real screens (DEC-120). Two libraries: the Civicsmith library, shipped with the release, its content approved by Bob, read-only to every group; and each group's own, governed like filing templates (versioned, never edited; approved by a project owner, widened by an administrator; retired, never deleted) (DEC-121 (1)). It holds the checks every script passes, serves the scripts that start on a screen to every member's session with no AI credential and no key, and keeps unattributed daily use tallies. A machine proposes and never approves. A script never says or submits anything for a member: what a step places in a field is a labelled draft, the member's words only when the member keeps or edits it (K1364 B3). It runs no wizard: the runner, the docked guide and the screens are the interface's (the UX stream's).

### Provides

Terms. A **script** is R1's record; a **version** R1's version. A version's **state** is one of `draft`, `submitted`, `approved`, `updated` (an approved version a later approved one follows) or `withdrawn`. A script is **retired** when withdrawn from use whole (R9). A version is **offered** when it is the latest `approved` version of a script that is not retired and not `broken` (R13), and the viewer may see it (R20); an `updated` version stays readable and is not offered (K1363 B9). A **step** is R2's. A **screen** is an id of the registered screen registry; an **act** an op the registry lists for a screen. A version id is written `<script>@<version>`. A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, code, check, translation, ...}`, naming the step where R12 says so. `author`, `by`, `proposer` and `viewer` are the control plane's stamps.

**Registration at start: `wizardRegister({screens, ops, machineRefused, machineDrafts, irreversible, library})`** (in-process; `store-assembly` R17)

**The script and its versions**
- **R1** A script is `{id, origin, scope, name, start, required, versions}`. `id` is an opaque `WIZ-` id, never a counter. `origin` is `civicsmith` (the Civicsmith library) or `group` (`imported` reserved, never written in this version; DEC-121 (8), "designed now, built later"). `scope` is `{project}`, the project it was drafted in, or `group` once R7 widens it. `name` is one line of 1–200 characters (`WIZARD_NAME_REFUSED`). `start` is the screen its first step names. `required` is true only for origin `civicsmith`. A version is `{script, version, steps, sha, state, author, contributors, derived_from, approved, ended}`; `version` counts from 1 within its script; `sha` is the SHA-256 (lowercase hex) of the steps in canonical form; once a version leaves `draft`, its steps and `sha` never change. (DEC-121 (1)) A script also carries `based_on`: `null`, or the version id `<script>@<version>` of the approved version it was copied from (R3's `copy`), set once at the copy and never changed, so a copy always shows what it is based on (DEC-158 (4); K1818).
- **R2** A step is `{screen, act, what, why, draft?, via?}`: `act` is null or an op the registry lists for `screen`; `what` and `why` are 1–300 characters each; `draft`, when present, is one of `{text}` (the script's own words, at most 4,000 characters), `{template}` (a filing-template version `filing-templates` R25 offers) or `{machine: <op>}` (one of the registered machine-draft ops), and is always answered labelled with its kind. A step says what the member does and why; it never acts. A draft is placed for the member, never submitted: it becomes the member's words only by the member's own act of keeping or editing it, and no step submits, signs or files (the member alone presses the act's button, and the act runs its own checks, reason and receipt). (DEC-120 (2); K1364 B3) A step may also carry `via`: the `id` of another script, a side trip (DEC-139 (4)). A step with `via` sends the member through that script's steps, as R11 offers it to them, and, when the side trip ends, returns them to the step they left (DEC-139 (4): "returns from a side trip to the step left"); the side trip is never a step of this script, so R15 counts its use as that script's. Following it is the runner's (the interface's); this module holds and checks `via` (R12) and answers it (R11, R21).

**wizardDraft({project, name, recorded | from | copy, author, viewer})** (`op=wizarddraft`) → `{ok, script, version, sha, state: "draft"}`
- **R3** Creates a script and its first draft, or a new draft version of the script `from` names. `recorded` is a list of `{screen, act}` and nothing else: a step carrying any other key is refused `WIZARD_RECORDING_CARRIES_VALUES`, so a recording never holds a typed value (DEC-121 (2): screens and acts, never values); each recorded step is kept as the version's recorded pairs, its `what` and `why` empty until revised (R4). `from` is a proposal (R5) or an approved version the author may see (a derivative). With neither, a blank start, the author needs a live editor grant (R8). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_WIZARD` (a machine or unstamped author); `NO_SUCH_WIZARD` (a `from` absent or not visible, one answer); `WIZARD_SCOPE_REFUSED` (the author is not a joined participant of the project, `membership` R54); `WIZARD_EDITOR_NOT_GRANTED`; `WIZARD_RECORDING_CARRIES_VALUES`; `WIZARD_NAME_REFUSED`. A third start, `copy: <version>` (DEC-158 (1)–(3); K1818), names any approved version the author may see, the Civicsmith library's or the group's (R20), and creates a **new** script with `origin: group`, `scope: {project}`, `required: false` and `based_on` that version (R1); its first draft's steps are that version's steps, and their `(screen, act)` pairs count as the draft's recorded pairs (R4). The copy is never a version of its base, and the base is unchanged and still offered. It is a draft until approved like any wizard the group writes: the same editor grant (R4, R8), checks (R6, R12) and approval (R7). A copy of a `required` Civicsmith script never replaces it: the Civicsmith script stays the required one, still offered and still checked by R14. The rules for `from` are unchanged. A `copy` that is absent, not approved or not visible is refused `NO_SUCH_WIZARD` (one answer), in `from`'s place in the order.

**wizardRevise({version, steps, adopt?, author, viewer})** (`op=wizardrevise`)
- **R4** Replaces a draft's steps by a new revision, keeping every earlier revision with its author and time. Without a live editor grant (R8), the new list's `(screen, act)` pairs must be a sub-multiset of the version's recorded or adopted pairs: the author may reword, delete or reorder, never add; otherwise it is refused `WIZARD_EDITOR_NOT_GRANTED`. `adopt: <proposal>` takes a proposal's steps (R5) as the revision. The author may run their own draft (R11) before approval. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_WIZARD`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; `WIZARD_SCOPE_REFUSED` (anyone but the version's author); `NOT_A_DRAFT`; `WIZARD_EDITOR_NOT_GRANTED`; R2's shape refusals (`WIZARD_STEP_REFUSED`, naming the step). (DEC-121 (2)) For a copy (R3), `adopt` may also be `{base: <version>}`: the steps of a newer approved version of the script `based_on` names, whose `(screen, act)` pairs then join the version's adopted pairs, so an author without the advanced editor may bring the base's change across; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD` answers when the version named is not a newer approved version of that script (DEC-158 (4)).

**wizardPropose({project | script, steps, why, proposer, viewer})** (`op=wizardpropose`)
- **R5** Any credential may propose steps, for a project or for a named script. A proposal is stored apart under an opaque `WZP-` id, labelled `proposalLabel(proposer, "wizard")` (`record-grammar` R42), with `why` of 1–1,000 characters. It becomes a draft only when a member names it as `from` (R3) or adopts it (R4), and its run then joins `contributors` (every member who revised the version and every adopted proposal's run, dated). Refusals in order: `WIZARD_NO_PROPOSER`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; R2's shape refusals; `WIZARD_WHY_REFUSED`. (DEC-121 (2)–(3), as `filing-templates` R6)

**wizardSubmit({version, author, viewer})** (`op=wizardsubmit`)
- **R6** Moves a draft to `submitted` and fixes its steps and `sha`. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_WIZARD`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; `WIZARD_SCOPE_REFUSED`; `NOT_A_DRAFT`; R12's refusals against the registration, each naming its step.

**wizardApprove({version, widen?, by, viewer})** (`op=wizardapprove`)
- **R7** Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE_WIZARD`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; `NOT_SUBMITTED` (or, with `widen`, `WIZARD_NOT_APPROVED`); `NOT_AN_APPROVER` (an owner of the script's project, `membership` R65, approves; `widen: true` makes an approved project script group-wide and needs an administrator, `membership` R64); `APPROVER_IS_AUTHOR` (the approver is the version's author and no other member contributed, `filing-templates` R10's rule); R12's refusals, run again. No review step precedes approval (DEC-121 names approval only; K1363 B9). On success the version is `approved` with the approver and the instant; the script's previous approved version becomes `updated`, naming its successor. Widening records the administrator and instant and changes no version. (DEC-121 (1))

**wizardEditorGrant({member, by})** (`op=wizardeditorgrant`), **wizardEditorRevoke({grant, by})** (`op=wizardeditorrevoke`)
- **R8** An administrator grants a member the advanced editor (a blank start, R3; adding steps, R4) and revokes it. Anyone else is refused `NOT_AN_ADMIN` (`membership` R84's one answer). A grant is recorded with the administrator and instant under an opaque `WEG-` id; a revocation is appended, never a deletion; a second revocation answers `existed: true` with the first. A grant is live while it is not revoked. (DEC-121 (2))

**wizardRetire({script, version?, reason, by, viewer})** (`op=wizardretire`)
- **R9** As `filing-templates` R11: without `version`, retires the whole script, by an approver of its scope (R7's right): none of its versions is offered again, and each stays readable with the retirement's reason; with `version`, withdraws a draft or submitted version, by its author (`withdrawn`). `reason` is 1–500 characters (`WIZARD_REASON_REFUSED`). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE_WIZARD`; `NO_SUCH_WIZARD`; `WIZARD_NOT_THE_GROUPS` (a `civicsmith` script answers this to every write, R3–R9 included: groups cannot edit or retire required flows, DEC-121 (5)); `NOT_AN_APPROVER` (or, for a withdrawal, `WIZARD_SCOPE_REFUSED` for anyone but the author, and `NOT_A_DRAFT` for a version past submission); `WIZARD_REASON_REFUSED`; `WIZARD_ALREADY_ENDED` (answered with the first ending).

**wizards({state?, viewer})** (`op=wizards`), **wizardRead({script, version?, viewer})** (`op=wizardread`)
- **R10** `wizards` lists, to the owners of a script's project and to administrators, the scripts the viewer may see, newest first, at most 200, with `truncated`: by default every offered version, each with its state, author, contributors, approver and dates, `broken` (R13) and its use (R15); `state` lists instead drafts, submitted, withdrawn, retired or `broken` versions, or open proposals (`proposed`). `wizardRead` answers one version (the latest approved by default) with its steps and its whole attribution. Both answer each script's `based_on` (R1) with its base's name, or `null` (DEC-158 (4): the copy "shows what it is based on"). Writes nothing.

**wizardsAt({screen, viewer})** (`op=wizardsat`)
- **R11** Answers the offered scripts whose `start` is `screen`, each `{id, version, name, steps, approver, finished}` (`finished` R15's finish count), plus the caller's own drafts marked `draft: true`, so an author may run their own draft privately. An unknown screen answers `[]`. A Civicsmith library script that fails R12 against the registration (a step naming an act its screen does not list, or an op no spec declares) is not offered until it passes, as R13 withholds a group's (K1883). It needs no AI credential and no key, and is reached by every member's session (DEC-120 (1): wizards run for every group with no AI and no key). Writes nothing. (DEC-121 (6))

**checkScript(steps, {screens, ops, machineRefused, machineDrafts, offered?})** (pure; `op=wizardcheck`)
- **R12** Answers `{refusals, warnings}`, each naming its step. Refusals: `WIZARD_NO_STEPS`; `WIZARD_SCREEN_UNKNOWN`; `WIZARD_ACT_UNKNOWN` (an act that is not an op the registry lists for its screen); `WIZARD_STEP_NO_WHY`; `WIZARD_DRAFT_REFUSED` (a draft whose source is not one of R2's three, a `{template}` not offered, or a `{machine}` op not registered); `WIZARD_VIA_REFUSED` (a `via` naming the script itself, a script not in `offered`, or a script whose own steps lead back to this one through `via`, so a side trip always returns; DEC-139 (4)); `WIZARD_STEP_CONCLUDES` (a step carrying a `draft` whose act is in `machineRefused`, unless the draft is `{machine: <op>}` with `<op>` in `machineDrafts`, the labelled drafts the canon already allows on a member's act, such as DEC-101's "what changed": a draft from doctrine or a template is never placed on an act a machine is refused, and every draft stays a labelled draft that becomes the member's words only by the member's own act; K1364 B3, K1368). The exception for a registered machine draft does not reach `writinghelp` and `groupdescriptiondraft` (R13): a step carrying `{machine: writinghelp}` or `{machine: groupdescriptiondraft}` on an act R24 refuses is refused `WIZARD_STEP_CONCLUDES` (DEC-153 (4); K1818). Warnings: `WIZARD_TRIVIAL` (one step) and `WIZARD_DUPLICATE` (the same `(screen, act)` list as an offered script). Whether a step's words tell a member what to conclude is not judged here: the assistant's critique (`skills` R32) and the approving member judge it (K1364 B3). `op=wizardcheck` serves it as a read to any credential, `ai` included, against the registration, so a wizard the assistant plans on the fly passes the same checks (DEC-120 (1)). Never throws on a list; never writes. (DEC-121 (4))
