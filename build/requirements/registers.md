# registers — requirements

**Status** · New module, BOB's (K4403 (1); layer 5, after `workbooks`); every requirement not yet met (T53). Written from N1259-b (R1–R46) and N1259-d (R61–R72, the guardrails) in `build/plan/beta/capabilities/`; draft ids are kept (R47–R60 are other modules' and unused here); door names of the ops are BOB's.

## Public

### Purpose

Holds the public registers a team has imported, whole or in part, by fixed steps with no AI: lists the known and the team's own registers, plans an import (prefilled filter, delta, time estimate), runs it in the background as resumable slices, keeps and searches the rows and the captures they came from, cites a row as a labelled extract, compares two imports, keeps a project's stated interest as a filter for change-watching; holds no fact, score or judgment about anyone.

### Provides

**Terms.** *Held rows*: kept cells of rows in imports the viewer may see. *Scope*: `project` (sight the project's) or `team` (a pre-read; the team's). *Interest*: a filter a member attaches to a project and a register. *Slice*: one bounded unit of an import's work. Refusal codes are drafts.

**The listing and the doors** (T53; N1259; K4403 (2), DEC-411)

- **R1** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); DEC-411) **`registerList({viewer})` lists the registers known, with name, source, URL, bytes and rows, each figure with its basis.** It answers `{ok, known: [...], own: [...], proposed: [...]}`. A known register is `{key, name, source, url, bytes: {value, basis}, rows: {value, basis}, source_date?, measured_at, status, use_limit?, held}`: `name` the definition's `label`, `source` its `publisher`, `url` its `url`, the figures `jurisdictions` R129's (basis `stated`, `counted`, `estimated` or `not_stated`, never a bare number); `status` one of `importable`, `beta` (R3), `not_measured` (R4), `gated` (R5); `held` the viewer-visible imports of it (R2). `own` are the team's own definitions (R7), `proposed` its proposals (R6). Sorted by name; reads the profile and this module's tables; fetches and writes nothing. Test: the test profile's registers appear with name, source, URL and figures with their basis; `not_stated` shows a null value, never 0; a register the team holds an import of shows it. Control: a profile with no registers answers empty lists, `ok` true.

- **R2** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2), (7)) **Each held register shows what is held and what watching looks for.** `held` is, for each import the viewer may see: `{import, scope, project?, state, source_date, imported_at, rows_in, rows_out, filter, filter_fingerprint, watched, next_check?}`; `watched` is `auto` (a project's interest on this register is `autoWatchable`, `register-readers` R7, and a watch is running), `by_act` (the interest holds a name or organization, or the import is a team pre-read: re-checked only by a member's act, `registerRefresh`) or `none`; `filter` is shown only when the viewer may see the import (R19). Test: an import with a filer-id interest shows `auto` and its next check; one with a name shows `by_act`; a team pre-read with no interest shows `none`; a hidden project's import is absent for a non-participant. Control: an import with no interest shows `none`.

- **R3** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (6); the beta's BETA-2 lifts this) **Company officers' bulk import is listed and refused in this release, with the reason, except through R46.** `PRODUCT_PHASE` is exported and is `alpha` in this release. A register whose definition has `phase: beta` is listed with `status: beta` and the reason key `register.beta.officers`; `registerFilter`, `registerDelta`, `importStart` and the pre-read on it refuse `REGISTER_NOT_IN_ALPHA` naming the register and the reason key, fetch nothing and write nothing; R46's two doors (K5498) are the only exception and are not refused by phase. Nothing in code names company officers: lifting it is data (the officers definition gains pieces, `phase` re-set) and `PRODUCT_PHASE`, by the beta's BETA-2 (UNVERIFIED: not found in this checkout). Test: the test profile's `phase: beta` register is listed `beta` and every act on it is refused with the key, with zero requests; setting `PRODUCT_PHASE` to `beta` in a test lets it be planned. Control: an `alpha` register is unaffected; R46's lookup is not refused.

- **R4** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) **A register not yet measured is listed but not importable.** A listing entry with `status: not_measured` (jurisdictions R133) refuses `importStart` and the pre-read `REGISTER_NOT_MEASURED` (no pieces known), fetching nothing. A team brings one in by its own definition (R7). Test: refusal by name, zero requests, the listing shows the entry. Control: after `registerDefine` the team's own definition is importable.

- **R5** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K1449, K1492 (2), (3)) **A gated or fee-bearing register is never fetched by the product.** For `gate` `account` or `fee`, planning, import and refresh answer `member_act_required` and fetch nothing, writing nothing but that answer; the way in is a member's own capture or upload (or R46's file import), not this module. Test: the test profile's `gate: fee` register refuses every act with zero requests and no row, import or log written beyond the answer. Control: `gate: none` proceeds.

- **R6** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); DEC-411 `registerpropose`) **`registerPropose({name, url, publisher, note?, withdraw?, author, viewer})`: a team proposes a register not on the list; nothing is brought in.** Refusals in order, each writing nothing: a machine or absent author `MACHINE_CANNOT_PROPOSE`; `name`, `url` or `publisher` blank or over 300 characters `PROPOSAL_INVALID` naming the field; a `url` that is not a public https address (a login page, private address, non-https scheme, credentials) `PROPOSAL_NOT_PUBLIC`. The URL is checked as text and is not fetched. A proposal with the same normalised URL already held by the team answers `existed: true` with it. It is held as `{id, name, url, publisher, note, by, at, state: proposed}`, listed under `proposed` (R1) to the team, and `withdraw: true` by its author or an administrator takes it off the list (the row stays, `state: withdrawn`). `proposalsForUpstream({viewer})` (administrator) answers the held proposals as data for the administrator to send on by their own act; this module sends nothing out. Test: a proposal is held and listed, a second identical one `existed`; an `http:` address and one with a password are `PROPOSAL_NOT_PUBLIC`; the fake network sees no request; a machine author is refused; withdrawal keeps the row. Control: another instance lists none.

- **R7** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4405 (4); K1492; `jurisdictions` R128) **`registerDefine({proposal | key, definition, author, viewer})`: a team's own register, public sites only, its terms its own.** Completes a proposal into a definition the same `validateRegisterDefinition` accepts: pieces, delimiter, header, columns with roles, `row_key`, and `terms_basis` as the member's own statement. Columns are kept only when the member gives them a role and `keep: true` (the default for every column is drop); a `home_address` or `phone` role asked kept is refused `REGISTER_KEEPS_CONTACT`; a definition naming a host the profile's systems mark `gated`, or a `gate` of `people_search`, is refused (`member_act_required`, `REGISTER_PEOPLE_SEARCH`). The definition is recorded as the team's own research (`own: true`, `by`, `at`, the terms statement verbatim, "stated by the team, not checked"), serves only that team (team sight), is listed under `own`, and every revision is kept. Only a member may define (`MACHINE_CANNOT_DEFINE`); an administrator is not required. Test: `registers` R71; an accepted definition is importable by the team (R11) and absent from another team's list and the shipped profile. Control: a revision keeps the earlier version readable.

**Planning an import: the filter and the delta**

- **R8** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); `register-readers` R6, R8) **`registerFilter({register, project?, viewer})`: the filter, prefilled from the project and said so.** With a `project` the viewer may see, answers `{ok, filter, prefilled: true, basis, left_out, interest?}`: `filter` from `register-readers.prefillFrom` over the project's named subjects and identifiers (only those the viewer may see, read through `entities.subjectsOfProject`, `entities` R74), `basis` each criterion's source fact, `left_out` the criteria the register cannot apply, `interest` the project's saved interest on this register if any (R30). With no project, or one with no usable facts, `{}` (the whole register). Validated by `validateFilter`, not saved: a proposal the member edits. Nothing is inferred from a project's text; a person or organization enters only where the project already names it as a subject. Test: a project with a subject organization, a filer id and a period prefills `organizations`, `filer_ids` and `date` with their basis; its address fact and notes add nothing; an unseen project is `NO_SUCH_PROJECT`; no project gives `{}`. Control: an edit validates or is refused by `validateFilter`'s code.

- **R9** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); K4405 (6)) **`registerDelta({register, filter, scope, project?, budget_ms, viewer})`: what the import would add, before anything is brought in, each figure saying counted or estimated.** Answers `{ok, new_rows, already_held, bytes_in, bytes_stored, seconds, shelf, source_date, probe, timed_out}`. `already_held` counts the rows matching the filter in the imports the viewer may see (from the index). `new_rows` is `{value, basis}`: `counted` only when `acquisition.probeRegister` (`acquisition` R128) counted the rows for this same filter and the definition says the source is `append_only`, the value being source rows less `already_held`, floored at 0; otherwise `estimated` from `register-readers.estimate` and the last import's selectivity (rows kept per row read), or `not_stated`. `bytes_in` what the import would read (`stated` from the probe or the sum of pieces, else `not_stated`); `bytes_stored` estimated from rows and the last import's bytes per row (else the definition's sample); `seconds` `{value, low, high, basis: "estimated"}` from `bytes_in` over the median throughput of the last three imports (`took_ms` per byte), else `THROUGHPUT_DEFAULT` (M-R7), never `counted`; `shelf` `{free_bytes, would_use_pct, warn}` from the storage port (R22). It makes the governed probe requests only (no body), writes nothing, records nothing, and stops at `budget_ms` (default 1,200) with `timed_out: true` and what it has, never a guess. Refusals as `importStart` (R3-R5, `FILTER_*`). Test: a fake source with a count query and `append_only` gives `new_rows` counted and equal to count less held; with a name in the filter (unsupported by the count) it gives estimated; a source with no `Content-Length` gives `bytes_in` not_stated; a slow probe ends at the budget with `timed_out`; seconds equal the arithmetic and carry `estimated`; nothing is written (no table's row count changes). Control: a register whose last three imports are held uses their throughput.

- **R10** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4405 (6); DEC-411) **An estimate is recorded against its outcome.** An import records `took_ms`, `bytes_in` and `rows_in` when it ends; R9 uses them. Test: after three fixture imports of different speeds the next estimate equals the median rate's arithmetic; a failed import contributes none. Control: with none held the constant is used and `basis` says so.

**The import**

- **R11** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); K4405 (3); K1449, K1492) **`importStart({register, filter, scope, project?, author, viewer})` (`op=registerfileimport`: a new op; `registerimport` is `events` R38's other act, a machine's, `op-declarations/index.mjs:238`; scope `team` is the pre-read, `op=registerpreread`): a member's act that queues a background import and fetches nothing itself.** Refusals in order, each writing nothing: a machine or absent author `MACHINE_CANNOT_IMPORT_REGISTER`; `NO_SUCH_REGISTER`; `REGISTER_NOT_IN_ALPHA` (R3); `REGISTER_NOT_MEASURED` (R4); `member_act_required` (R5); `FILTER_*` (`register-readers` R6); scope `team` by anyone but an active administrator `NOT_AN_ADMIN` (K4405 (3)); scope `project` without a visible project the author takes part in `NO_SUCH_PROJECT`; `IMPORT_QUEUE_FULL` when `MAX_ACTIVE_IMPORTS` (a `runtime-limits` row, 2) are already queued or running in this instance. An import of the same register, filter fingerprint and scope that is already queued or running answers `existed: true` with it. Otherwise it writes the import `{id, register, filter, filter_fingerprint, scope, project, state: queued, author, queued_at}` (a `team` import, the pre-read, is a resource: every member's search includes its rows and every project's delta counts them as held), calls the `onImportQueued(module, fn)` listener (one per module, `membership.listenerRefusal`, as `following` R19; `scheduler` registers its `arm`, R9, R23), so an idle instance arms its alarm, and answers within the act's time, never waiting for a byte. Test: each refusal by name; a machine credential is refused; two identical starts leave one; a third concurrent import is `IMPORT_QUEUE_FULL`; the fake network sees no request during the act; an idle instance has its alarm armed afterwards. Control: a member's project import lands.

- **R12** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) **The import runs as resumable slices on the scheduler's alarm and ends with the same rows as an uninterrupted run.** `importDue(now)` is true while any import is queued, running or retrying and the host is not paused; `importWake(now)` the earliest instant one is due; `importTick(now, budget, host)` claims the oldest due import under the host's epoch (a retry never runs a slice twice; `host` is a parameter as `following`'s is, `monitoring` R21, R30, not imported), runs at most one slice (bounded by `budget_ms` and bytes, `register-readers` and `acquisition.streamRegister`, its R125–R127), records progress, and answers `{import, state, progress, done}`. States: `queued`, `fetching`, `reading` (reducing and indexing the arrived rows), `indexed` (done), `failed` (with `reason` and `retries`), `paused` (with `reason`: `member`, `shelf`, `host_paused`), `stopped`. A restart between slices re-derives from the import's table and cursor; a slice that fails is retried up to 3 times with growing delay, then `failed` with its reason, and a new `importStart` makes a new import. Test: `registers` R67 (G9): an import killed between each pair of slices of a 1M-row fixture ends as an uninterrupted run does (row-table digest, counts, segment digests); a paused `host` passed to `importTick` runs no slice (`paused: true`); `failed` carries the reason and retry count. Control: with nothing queued `importDue` is false and `importWake` null.

- **R13** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) **`importStatus({import, viewer})` and `importSet({import, action, author, viewer})`: progress to the member, and pause, resume or stop.** `importsEnded({after, limit})` lists, for the queue, imports that finished, stopped or failed (not refreshes); `importStatus` answers the import's state, its pieces (each `pending|reading|done|failed`), `progress` `{bytes_in, rows_in, rows_out, segments_stored, percent?}` (`percent` only when bytes are `stated`), `started_at`, `finished_at`, `took_ms`, `source_date`, `reduction` (R15), `reason` and `retries`; for an import the viewer may not see it answers `NO_SUCH_REGISTER_IMPORT`, the same as an absent one. `importSet` with `pause`, `resume` or `stop` is the starter's or an active administrator's act (`NOT_YOUR_IMPORT` otherwise; a machine author `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_IMPORT`); `stop` ends the import, keeps what was brought in and says so (`stopped`, with counts); `resume` on `stopped` is refused `IMPORT_STOPPED` (start a new import). Test: a running import shows pieces and progress; after pause the next tick runs nothing; resume continues from the cursor; stop keeps the segments and rows searchable and reads `stopped`; a hidden import is `NO_SUCH_REGISTER_IMPORT`. Control: stopping twice answers `existed: true`.

- **R14** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) **Every import is a capture with its source and date.** Each segment of each piece is filed through `acquisition` and `promotion` (`acquisition` R125, R133) with the import as authority; the import row binds each piece to its segments and captures, and states per piece the source address, the retrieved-at instant, the source's own stated date (`Last-Modified`, the dataset's `updatedAt` or the file's own date, else `not_stated`) and the original's fingerprint (`covers` whole file or named member). The import's `imported_at` is the oldest piece's retrieval; its `source_date` the oldest piece's stated date. Test: a 2-piece import shows each piece's address, retrieved-at, stated date and fingerprint; `imported_at` is the older retrieval; a piece with no stated date shows `not_stated`, never the retrieval time. Control: a second import of the same bytes shows `existed` captures and its own date.

- **R15** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (5); K1485; DEC-408) **The reduced copy on arrival is stated, never silent.** The import's `reduction` is `{kept_columns, dropped_columns: [{name, why}], withheld_cells, rows_in, rows_out, rows_undetermined, original_fingerprints, reason: "reduced under K1485"}` (names and counts only, no value) and the listing, status and every extract state "phone and address columns are left out as it arrives" by key (`register.reduced`). Test: a sentinel-bearing fixture import shows the dropped column names; no sentinel value appears in the import row, the status, the listing or any answer. Control: a register with no contact columns shows an empty `dropped_columns` for contact roles.

- **R16** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) **Importing is mechanical: no AI call is made.** `registers` and `register-readers` list no `ai-*`, `agent-*` or `skills` module in `Uses`; a complete cycle (plan, delta, import, search, cite, compare, interest, refresh, watch change) makes zero calls through the AI boundary and leaves the `ai-use` ledger and AI counters unchanged. Test: `registers` R68 (G10); both modules' `uses` hold no AI module. Control: the spy counts a call from a deliberately AI-using fixture module.

- **R17** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) **A retry or a second run never duplicates a row or a capture.** Rows are written by `(register, row_key, content_hash, sight scope)`; segments are content-addressed; a slice repeated after a failure writes nothing the first did. Test: a slice run twice leaves one index row per key and one object per segment digest. Control: a changed row writes one more version.

- **R18** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K1492 (1), (4)) **An import is polite and attributed.** Every request of a slice is `acquisition`'s (governor pacing, the agent naming the instance and purpose `register`, the definition's hosts only); a `HOST_COOLING_OFF` pauses the import (`host_cooling`) and retries after `retry_in_ms`, not counted as a failure. Test: a cooling governor pauses and resumes after the wait; every agent names the purpose. Control: an uncooled run is unchanged.

- **R19** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K1489; K4403 (2), (7)) **An import is seen as its scope is: a hidden project's import, its filter and its rows stay inside it.** A `project` import and its segments take the sight of the project (`membership`'s fence); a `team` import the team's. No list, count, delta, search, compare, status, extract, queue item or error names, counts or lets one infer an import, filter or interest the viewer may not see: `already_held` counts only visible rows; a row first stored in a hidden project's import is stored again (its own version) by a team import, not shared. Test: `registers` R70 (G13): a hidden project's import with sentinel names leaves no trace in a non-participant's reads, deltas included (counts equal to a record without it); a participant's show it. Control: a team import is visible to every member.

- **R20** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); K4354) **An import is never in the way of the member's screens.** A slice's transaction batches are bounded so that no single write holds the object's one worker longer than `SLICE_TX_MS` (draft, 100 ms, set with PLATFORM), and a slice yields between batches; no read op waits for a slice. Test: during a 1M-row fixture import `registerList` and a small register's `searchRows` answer within budget and no transaction exceeds the bound (instrumented). **Storage**

- **R21** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2) "storage designed for large datasets"; K1727's pattern) **A row version is stored once; a re-import writes only what is new or changed; every version seen is kept (storage S2 behind the port, K5530 F4, until M-R3).** For a sight scope, the cells of a row version live once, in the reduced segment of the import that first saw that version, and the index holds the row's terms (`register-readers` R5), `row_key`, content hash and a pointer (segment, row number, byte range); a later import of the same register and scope writes an index row and a segment row only for a key not held or whose content hash differs, and records `unchanged` for the rest; an older version stays addressable by its import. Test: a second identical import writes zero index rows and zero segments beyond the descriptor; one changed row writes one; the old version is still returned by `rowOf` for the first import. Control: another scope stores its own copy (R19).

- **R22** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2)) **The row index is a rebuildable, bounded table behind one storage port.** The index is declared to `record-core` with `derive: derived-rebuildable`, `from` (the table of segment descriptors, record-core R77) and a `rebuild(scope)` that recomputes it from the stored segments; `rebuildAndCompare` is `{same: true}` after any import sequence. The module reaches the index and the segments' rows only through one port (`rowStore`: put a delta, find by terms and range, get a row, list by key range, count, free bytes, rebuild); a contract test suite runs unchanged against every implementation of the port (an in-memory one and the team object's SQLite one). Tables are declared with classes: index `purge: clear`, `expunge: tombstone`, `export: never`, `sight: source`; import and interest rows `export: yes` (descriptors, R41); proposals and own definitions `sight: group`. Test: after a random import sequence `rebuildAndCompare` is `{same: true}`; the contract suite passes on both; every table is declared. Control: a corrupted index row gives `{same: false, first}`.

- **R23** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); K4354) **Storage is bounded in memory and in time, and refuses before it overruns.** A slice holds at most `SLICE_MEMORY_MB` (set from M-R7; a draft 32 MB) beyond the reader's two windows. Before a slice writes, the module asks the port for free bytes; when the import's estimated stored bytes would pass the object's warning mark (the Settings screen's 70%) it pauses the import with reason `shelf` and the figures, and an administrator's resume is refused while it would pass 90% (`SHELF_NEARLY_FULL`); nothing is partly written. The row index answers a point lookup and a range query in time that does not grow with the number of imports held (an index, not a scan), shown by M-R3 at the sizes (e) §3 lists. Test: a port reporting 5% free pauses a large import with `shelf`, nothing written past the pause; a 1M-row index answers `rowOf` and a one-criterion page within 1,500 ms on the test copy (M-R3). Control: with room it runs.

**Search, rows, citing and comparing**

- **R24** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (2); K1484, K1468, K1492 (6); T52-DEC406 §1 I4) **`searchRows({scope, name?, organization?, amount?, date?, filer_id?, form?, limit, after, budget_ms, viewer})` (`op=registersearch`): rows by person, organization, amount and date, from the held rows only.** `scope` is `{register}` (every visible import of it), `{import}` or all registers; criteria are `validateFilter`'s (a name matches on tokens, `register-readers` R5); `limit` is 1 to 200 (default 50, `SEARCH_BAD_LIMIT` otherwise); `after` the opaque `next` of an earlier page (`BAD_CURSOR`); `budget_ms` default 1,500, at most 10,000 (`SEARCH_BAD_BUDGET` above it). It never fetches, never calls `entities` or resolves a name, creates no entity or link, and answers rows as written. Each row `{import, register, row_key, src_row, cells, imported_at, source_date, version, cited, use_limit?}`. Order: date descending (undetermined last), then `row_key`. `matched` is the exact count to 10,000 and `capped` beyond. Every answer states the imports searched, each with `imported_at` and `source_date`. Refusals: `NO_SUCH_REGISTER`, `FILTER_*`, `SEARCH_BAD_LIMIT`, `SEARCH_BAD_BUDGET`, `BAD_CURSOR`. Test: `budget_ms` 10,001 is `SEARCH_BAD_BUDGET` and 10,000 is served, each writing nothing; a fixture of 5 imports finds a name by tokens in either order, an amount range, a date range and a filer id, each alone and together; paging 130 rows by 50 equals the unpaged 130; a hidden project's rows are absent; `registers` R62 (G2): no request, no entity or link row. Control: a row whose date is undetermined is listed last, not coerced.

- **R25** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; T52-DEC406 §1 I4; `retrieval`'s absence levels) **A search that finds nothing says where it looked, and a search stopped by its budget never says nothing was found.** With no match the answer carries `absence: {level: "import", imports: [{import, imported_at, source_date}]}` and the design's sentence "not in this import (source date …)", never "does not exist"; with no import held for the scope it says `no_import_held`. A search that reaches `budget_ms` returns the rows found, `stopped: {reason: "time"}` and `more_may_exist: true`, and carries no `absence`. Test: a name not in the fixture answers absence at import level with the dates; a fixture slowed past the budget answers rows and `stopped`, no `absence`; an empty scope answers `no_import_held`. Control: a complete search with matches carries no `absence`.

- **R26** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K1492 (6)) **Searches are not logged.** No query text, criterion, count or result, and no member id against one, is written anywhere: not to a table, the observation log, a tally, an error, a log line or a response header. Use counts, if kept, are unattributed. Test: `registers` R65 (G5 (6)). Control: the scan finds the sentinel in the search's own response.

- **R27** *(not yet met: T53)* (T53; N1259; K4403 (3); K1484; 11 CFR 104.15) **Rows of a register with a data-use limit carry the limit and cannot be listed out.** Every answer of `searchRows`, `rowOf`, `citeRow` and `compare` that carries a row of a register whose definition has a `use_limit` carries `use_limit: {key, citation, text_key}`; a search of such a register needs at least one criterion (`NO_CRITERIA`), pages to at most 1,000 rows per search (`RESULT_CAP`), and no op of this module returns rows as a file or an unbounded list.
