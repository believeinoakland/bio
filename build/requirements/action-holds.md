## Public

### Purpose

(T45; Actions D10; v0.2 rule 12) The group's litigation holds and the pressure it receives: a member marks pressure on a received entry or records pressure that arrived outside any action; any member who can see an action, an entry, a case or a project places a hold there with a reason, and releases it; the hold stops a purge of what it covers, a scheduled publication and a doorbell clearance; a member records that she sent the plain notice telling members what to keep outside Civicsmith. A member does every act; nothing here records whether any member received, read or complied with a notice (rule 10).

### Provides

Terms. An **action**, its document, entries and positions are `actions`' (its Terms). In the moved text (R1–R10) `actions`' ids read as this module's: R48 → R1, R52 → R2, R54 → R3, R55 → R4, R56 → R5, R57 → R6, R58 → R7, R59 → R8, R60 → R9, R69 → R10; an `actions` id not in that list (R15, R22, R25, R34) is still `actions`'. A **place** is R2's: an action, an entry of one, or a project.

**The moved requirements** (T45; K2781 (1))
- **R1** (T45; moved from `actions` R48) (K597 (1): prepared for opposition) A `received` correspondence entry may be marked `pressure: {kind: legal | retaliation | discrediting | other, note}` (note at most 500 characters, R22's text rule): a threat, retaliation, discrediting or legal harassment directed at the group or its supporters (Operational Principle 8, Design Requirement 13). It is stated as a key of `actionCorrespond` (R15) when the entry is recorded, or later by `actionPressure({target, ord, pressure, author, viewer})` (`op=actionpressure`), which appends the mark to a table of its own and never rewrites the entry (R34). Only a member marks it (`MACHINE_CANNOT_MARK_PRESSURE`); a mark on a `sent` or `no_response` entry is refused `PRESSURE_NOT_RECEIVED`, one on an entry already marked `PRESSURE_MARKED`. The read (R25) lists an action's pressure entries apart; `actionsFor` takes `pressure: true` to list only actions holding one. It is evidence like any capture and may be cited by an inquiry. A `legal` mark may carry a litigation hold (R52). (T45; K2781 (1); reconciliation R19) After the move `actionPressure` and its table are this module's; a `pressure` key stated at `actions`' `actionCorrespond` stays on the entry and is read here through `actions.actionRead`, both answered alike. The pressure entries an action holds, and its holds, are answered by this module's reads (R12's `pressureFor`, R2's `holdsOf`) in place of `actions` R25's list; `actionsFor`'s `pressure: true` is `pressureFor`'s.
- **R2** (T45; moved from `actions` R52) (K899 (7), DEC-61, DEC-113; N-A19) A received entry marked pressure of kind `legal` (R48) may carry a litigation hold. A member places it through `actionHold({target, ord, hold, reason, projects?, author, viewer})` (`op=actionhold`): `hold` is `in_place` (the group is preserving what the matter may reach), with `reason` (1 to 500 characters, R22's text rule). The statement records its **projects**: the action's own project (`record-core` R34's `project` of the action; none when it belongs to no project), filled in by this module and never taken from the caller, and each distinct project id in `projects` (at most 50). Each statement, with its projects, is appended to this module's own tables (`action_holds`: the action, the entry, `hold`, `reason`, `by`, `at`, a sequence; and its projects), keyed to the action (record-core R21) and never rewritten; the latest statement for an entry is its hold. A hold `in_place` **covers** the projects of every `in_place` statement on that entry since its last `released` statement (R56): a further `in_place` statement adds its projects, and a project leaves a hold only when the hold is released. A project is **held** while any hold `in_place`, on any action, covers it. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_HOLD` (C-117.20; an empty or machine author); `NO_TARGET`; `HOLD_RELEASE_IS_ITS_OWN_ACT` (C-117.23; `hold` is `released`: R56 is that act); `HOLD_REFUSED` (C-117.21; `hold` not `in_place`, or the reason not as above); `HOLD_PROJECTS_REFUSED` (C-117.24; `projects` present and not a list of project ids, or over 50 distinct); `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` (absent and invisible alike); `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `HOLD_NO_LEGAL_MARK` (C-117.22; no `legal` pressure mark at that entry); then, for the first named project the author may not name, `membership`'s `existenceAct` and then `noSuchProject` (its R77, R78). (T41; D54; K2484) A project a viewer **may name** here is one she sees at `FULL`, or, as an administrator neither invited nor joined, a hidden project she sees at `EXISTENCE` (`membership` R44, R60): naming it reaches its id and hold state, never its contents, so a custodian can place a hold on a hidden project (Membership §7.3). Otherwise it answers `{ok, target, ord, hold, reason, by, at, projects}`, `projects` being the projects the hold now covers that the author may name. An action that belongs to no project and names none lands a hold covering no project (`projects: []`). Any member who can see the action may place a hold. The read (R25) shows each `legal` mark with its statements, oldest first, each with those of its projects the viewer may name, and its current `hold` (null while none is stated). While a hold is `in_place`, no material of a project it covers, and not the action that carries it, is purged from the real record (R60, `store-door` R4); R58 is the read a device will ask before deleting an assistant transcript (the device's own rule waits for a device-storage module, K1251). (DEC-113; K1252) (T45; N858 D10; Actions D10; v0.2 rule 12 "Any member who can see an action or case may place a litigation hold with a reason") This replaces "A received entry marked pressure of kind `legal` (R48) may carry a litigation hold" and `HOLD_NO_LEGAL_MARK` (C-117.22 retired, its number never reused): a hold is placed at a **place**: an action (`{target}`), an entry of one (`{target, ord}`), or a project (`{project}`), with `from?` naming what prompted it: a pressure mark (R1), a pressure record (R12, `{pressure}`), a docket `legal` mark (`{docket_entry}`, read through `docket.docketPressureOf`, its R27; the place is that entry's case's project), or a counsel matter (the project itself, `project-roster.projectScope` answering `counsel_matter`). `actionHold` takes `{target?, ord?, project?, from?, hold, reason, projects?}`; not exactly one place is `HOLD_PLACE_REFUSED`, asked after `NO_TARGET`. A project place the author may not name is refused as a named project is; a case the member can see (`publication`'s case read) places a hold on its project. The latest statement at a place is its hold; R3–R10 read places where they read entries. `holdsOf({target | project, viewer})` answers a place's statements, oldest first, as the moved text's read did. Control: a hold on an action with no pressure mark lands; one naming two places is refused.
- **R3** (T45; moved from `actions` R54) (K899 (7)) `holdsDue({after?, limit?, viewer})` lists every `legal` pressure mark on an action the viewer may see on which no hold is stated: the action, the entry's position, the mark's note, who marked it and when, and the action's project. Results are at most 500 per page, in (action id, position) order, with `cursor` and `truncated` as `action-clocks` R5's. The action's state is not asked: a legal matter outlives the action. It is the one read `queue-producers` uses for its litigation-hold reminder (its R19). Writes nothing. (T45; reconciliation R14; S1 §D 10) `holdsDue` also lists each docket `legal` mark (`docket.docketPressureMarks`, its R27, kind `legal`) and each `legal` pressure record (R12) on which no hold is placed, each with where it arrived (`{action, ord}`, `{docket_entry, case}` or `{pressure}`) and its project; it is the one read `action-producers` uses for the hold to-do (its R5). A docket read that fails answers a refusal, never a short list. Control: a docket `legal` mark with a hold placed from it is not listed.
- **R4** (T45; moved from `actions` R55) (T42; N826; K2607) At start this module registers once with `doorbell` (`registerReader("litigation-hold", …)`, its R3) the reader doorbell's R3 asks before any act of doorbell's clears a discarded knock: it answers whether any litigation hold (R52) is in place in the group, that is, whether any entry of any action the instance holds has `in_place` as its latest statement, whatever the viewer. It answers `true` or `false`, writes nothing and never throws; a reader that cannot read answers `true`, so nothing is cleared (doorbell R3). (DEC-108; K1019; K1023) (T45; reconciliation R19) In the copy window this module exports the reader and registers nothing; it registers at start once `actions`' job (T45-27) has deleted `actions`' own registration, so one registration stands at any time (where the plane composes it, plan rule 4 (6)).
- **R5** (T45; moved from `actions` R56) (DEC-113; K1134 (3)) `actionHoldRelease({target, ord, reason, author, viewer})` (`op=actionholdrelease`) states `released` on an entry: the group is no longer preserving, or never needed to. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_HOLD` (C-117.20); `NO_TARGET`; `HOLD_REFUSED` (C-117.21; the reason as R52's); `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` (absent and invisible alike); `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `HOLD_NO_LEGAL_MARK` (C-117.22); `HOLD_ALREADY_RELEASED` (C-117.25; the entry's latest statement is already `released`). An entry with no hold stated may be released. Otherwise it appends the statement to R52's table, recording with it `restarted`: the projects R57 would have answered as `restarts` at that instant, whatever the author may see. It answers `{ok, target, ord, hold: "released", reason, by, at, restarted}`, `restarted` being those of them the author may name (R52). Any member who can see the action may release its hold. The administrators and the hold's placers are told once (`queue-producers` R29). (T45; Actions D10) It takes a place as R2 does; `HOLD_NO_LEGAL_MARK` is retired from it.
- **R6** (T45; moved from `actions` R57) (DEC-113: the release form states what will be deleted) `holdReleasePreview({target, ord, viewer})` (`op=actionholdpreview`) answers what releasing that entry's hold would restart, before any member releases it. Refusals in order: `NO_TARGET`; `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` (absent and invisible alike); `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `HOLD_NO_LEGAL_MARK` (C-117.22). It answers `{hold, restarts, out_of_view}`: `hold` the entry's current hold (null while none is stated); `restarts` the projects the entry's hold covers that no other `in_place` hold covers, each one the viewer may name (R52); `out_of_view` `true` exactly when such a project exists that the viewer may not name, with no id or count. An entry whose hold is not `in_place` restarts nothing (`restarts: []`, `out_of_view: false`). Writes nothing. (T45; Actions D10) It takes a place as R2 does; `HOLD_NO_LEGAL_MARK` is retired from it.
- **R7** (T45; moved from `actions` R58) (DEC-113: the held-project strip, and the read a device asks) `projectHolds({projects, viewer})` (`op=projectholds`): `projects` is a list of 1 to 50 distinct project ids, else `HOLD_PROJECTS_REFUSED` (C-117.24). For each project it answers one of:
  - `held: true`, with `since` and `recorded_by`: the `at` and `by` of the earliest `in_place` statement, among holds still `in_place`, whose projects include it;
  - `held: false`, when no hold covers it;
  - `held: null`, when the project is absent or the viewer may not name it (R52; `membership` R44), alike (DEC-36). An administrator at a hidden project's `EXISTENCE` is answered `held` with `since` and `recorded_by`, which is all this read names (D54).

  It names no action, entry or reason, and no member but `recorded_by`. It answers `held: false` only after reading every hold: a read it cannot complete is a refusal, never `false`. Writes nothing.
- **R8** (T45; moved from `actions` R59) (DEC-113: "the administrators and whoever placed the hold are told once") `holdsReleased({after?, limit?, viewer})` lists every `released` statement that ended an `in_place` hold, on an action the viewer may see: the action, the entry's position, the statement's sequence, who released it, when, the reason, `placers` (each distinct member who stated an `in_place` statement in the hold it ended) and those of `restarted` (R56) the viewer may name (R52). At most 500 per page, in (action id, position, sequence) order, with `cursor` and `truncated` as R54's. It is the one read `queue-producers` uses for its release notice (its R29). Writes nothing.
- **R9** (T45; moved from `actions` R60) (DEC-113; K1252, K1253) `purgeHeld({bundleId?})` answers, whatever the viewer, whether a purge of the record would reach held material. With `bundleId` absent or empty (the whole-store purge, `record-core` R22, R72) it answers `true` exactly when any hold in the instance is `in_place`: the same answer as R55's reader. With a `bundleId` (a single-bundle purge) it answers `true` exactly when any hold is `in_place` and at least one of these holds:
  - the bundle is a held project;
  - the bundle belongs to a held project (`record-core` R34's `project` of it names one);
  - the bundle is an action carrying an `in_place` hold (R52);
  - the bundle's project cannot be determined: `record-core`'s `bundleInfo` answers `null` (no such bundle is held, though rows keyed to its id may be) or cannot be read.

  It answers `false` otherwise, and `false` whenever no hold is `in_place`. It answers `true` or `false`, writes nothing and never throws; when it cannot read the holds, it answers `true`. Used by `store-door` R4. (T41; D54) It reads every hold and every bundle's project whatever any viewer may see, never through a viewer's sight (the founder's viewer no longer sees a hidden project, `membership` R43), so a hidden project no administrator was added to is held as any other.
- **R10** (T45; moved from `actions` R69) (N662, DEC-147 (3); K1790, K1830; `ratification` R45) At start this module registers once with `ratification.registerHoldReader` the reader its R41's `holds` asks: `holdsOn({project})` answers, read as the plane (whatever the viewer), R58's answer for that one project: `{held: true, since, recorded_by}` or `{held: false}`, naming no action, entry or reason; it answers `held: false` only after reading every hold, and `null` when it cannot complete the read, so `ratification` answers `SCHEDULE_UNCHECKABLE` (its R40) or its scheduled publisher stops (its R42). It writes nothing and never throws. (T45; reconciliation R19) As R4: exported, registered only after `actions`' own registration is deleted.

**holdNoticeSent, pressureRecord, pressureFor** (T45; N858 D10; Actions D10; v0.2 rules 10, 12; S1 §C 16)
- **R11** (T45; N858 D10; v0.2 rule 12 "a plain notice telling members what to keep outside Civicsmith is prepared and sent by a member's own hand") `holdNoticeSent({place, at, medium, artifactSha? | account?, author, viewer})` (`op=holdnoticesent`): a member records that she sent the hold notice for the hold at `place` (R2), held as the bytes sent or her account, never both and never neither (`actions` R34's rule). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SEND`; the place's R2 refusals; `HOLD_NOT_IN_PLACE` (the place's latest statement is not `in_place`); `BAD_DATE` (`civil-time.isCalendarDate`); `CAPTURE_AND_TESTIMONY`; `NEITHER_CAPTURE_NOR_TESTIMONY`. It records no recipient, read, acknowledgement or compliance of any member, and no read here answers one (rule 10). The notice's text is prepared by `filings` (its R47). This module exports `holdNotInPlace(place)`, the one minter of `HOLD_NOT_IN_PLACE` (C-149), which `filings` R47 calls (K231, K2848). Control: a hold in place takes the record; a released one is refused.
- **R12** (T45; S1 §C 16; v0.2 rule 12 "pressure … is recorded as evidence wherever it arrives") `pressureRecord({project, pressure, artifactSha? | account?, at, author, viewer})` (`op=pressurerecord`): a member records pressure that arrived outside any action (a letter, a post, a call), with R1's `pressure` shape, held as a capture or her account, in a project she sees at `FULL`, creating no action to the sender. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_MARK_PRESSURE`; R1's shape refusals; `NO_SUCH_PROJECT` (`membership.noSuchProject`); `BAD_DATE`; `CAPTURE_AND_TESTIMONY` / `NEITHER_CAPTURE_NOR_TESTIMONY`. A `legal` record may carry a hold (R2's place `{project}`, `from: {pressure}`). `pressureFor({project?, action?, after?, limit?, viewer})` lists marks and records together, each with where it arrived, at most 500 per page with `cursor` and `truncated`. It is evidence and may be cited by an inquiry. Control: a record lands with no action created.
