## Public

### Purpose

Brings material into the record with its provenance: fetches a document from its public address (directly, through a Google Drive export, from a web archive, or rendered as a visitor saw it), hashes and stores it as it arrives, records the fetch as a receipt, profiles what it is, captures a page's supporting files, and returns the provenance document a caller promotes. It also keeps what capture learns about sources and sites (reachability, site assets, links). The doorbell, by which anyone may hand the group material, is `doorbell`'s (T42, K2607). It writes no bundle: no intake path writes live state.

### Provides

Terms. The **document address** is what the record identifies the document by; the **retrieval locator** is what was actually fetched (they differ for an archive replay and a Drive export). A **public locator** is `https:` on a named public host (`isPublicHttpsLocator`: no bare IP, no `localhost`, no credentials, a dotted host). The **caller class** and whether the caller is a member session are the control plane's stamps. Every refusal names a `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check` and `translation`.

**acquire(body, {cls, member}) → answer** The one act that fetches and files. `body`: `locator`, optional `via` (`"archive.org"`), `address` (archive arm), `render`, `subresources`, `continue`, `authority`, `file`, `matchedSweep`. Success answers `{ok: true, existed, existed_undetermined?, document, parts?, subresources?, snapshot?, files?, subresources_skipped?, note}`.

**Links: recordLinks({sourceCapture, sourceBundle, capturedAt, links}), linksTo({address_norm}), resolveLinks({sourceCapture}), recordLinkVerdict(…)**
- **R27** `recordLinks` replaces a capture's outbound links, one row per (link, normalised citation), each with its raw and normalised address, fragment, partition and origin. `linksTo` answers every capture linking to an address and the elements cited. `recordLinkVerdict` appends a dated verdict for (source capture, address), never overwriting one, and answers the current verdict, the history and whether it changed. `resolveLinks` gives each `deferred` link, against the target's direct receipts only, a verdict: `contemporaneous` when one capture of the target was seen more than once on both sides of the source's retrieval; `superseded` when every capture of the target postdates it; otherwise `undetermined`, with the basis (bracketing captures differ, one capture at the source's own instant, all captures predate it, or a self-reference) and the target's capture and home. `offsite` when the record holds no capture of the target; `anchor`, `intra` and `refused` pass through. Undetermined is the resting state, stated as such. Every row passes the caller's viewer (membership R43, through the register's bundle) before it is listed or counted; a capture filed in no bundle is visible (D-701, K123). `linksTo` answers at most `limit` rows in (source, citation) order per answer, with `truncated` and `next` when more remain; `count` and `elements` are of the rows listed. The verdict history is the newest `limit` verdicts, oldest first, with `total` and `truncated`; `changed` is whether `total` exceeds one. Through the op, `resolveLinks` answers at most `limit` of the source's links per answer in link order, with `truncated` and `next`, the tally and verdict counts being of the links listed; an in-process caller that passes no `limit` is answered every link, and the answer says `limit: null`. *(bounded by N90, K175: `limit` defaults to 200, clamped 1…1000; each answer publishes `limit` and `truncated`, a paged read also `next`, the cursor passed back as `after`)*

That read (`navChanges`, `op=navchanges`) answers the newest `limit` observations (default 200, at most 500), with `limit` and `truncated` and no `next`, since a comparison needs a contiguous sequence (K236).

**The render allowance: renderAdmit({allowanceMs, reserveMs, cap, at}) → `{state: "admitted", slot, …}` | `{state: "waiting", …}` | `{state: "deferred", …}`; renderSpend({ms, releaseMs, slot, at}) → day's account**
- **R39** Admission, in order: with `cap` set, slots past their expiry are reclaimed, and with `cap` or more running the answer is `waiting` with nothing reserved or counted; a reservation that is not positive is `deferred` (an unreserved admission is refused); when the day's spent plus reserved plus this reservation exceeds the allowance it is `deferred`, and the day's deferral count rises; otherwise the reservation is added to the day, a slot is taken that expires after the reservation, and the answer is `admitted` with its slot. Days are UTC dates.
- **R40** `renderSpend` frees the slot, adds the reported elapsed time (rounded up) to the day, and releases at most the reservation still held (never below zero); with no elapsed time reported it adds nothing and leaves the reservation charged.
- Errors: never throws.

- **R86** A signed-in member brings into the record a file she holds, which no one fetched. Refusals, in order, each writing nothing: the member-session fence: a `by` that is not a signed-in member's stamp (absent, not a string, blank, or a machine identity) is refused `MEMBER_SESSION_REQUIRED` (403), so a machine credential is refused by it; a `statement` (her words on where the file came from, required) that is absent, not a string, blank (white space only) or over 2,000 characters, `UPLOAD_NO_STATEMENT`, a row in this module's table (R37), the next free number of C-118, awaiting promotion's stamp; a `name`, when given, that is not a string or is over 300 characters, the required-argument refusal naming it; zero bytes, `EMPTY`, and more than the capture size limit (`acquisition` R114), `TOO_LARGE` (413) with its size and the limit (R111) and the stream cancelled, each answered as `acquisition` R10 answers it. It is graded received, never fetched, exactly as a pulled knock is (K509 (3); `provenance` R51): it earns no fetched letter; its grade is the member's authored letter under the ceiling, stated as authored, never as measured; and its existence at the upload's instant is proven by the receipt's timestamp (chain of custody from her upload's receipt). Bytes the record already holds answer `existed: true` with the held capture's digest and no new document, the receipt being written as a second sighting (`provenance` R15). A later fetch of the same bytes from a public address (`acquire`, any arm) writes its own receipt for the same digest beside the upload's: the upload's receipt and statement stay, nothing is regraded, and the capture's grade is the strongest of its receipts' answers (`provenance` R59's rule; K1852 (3)), so the fetched letter then stands and the capture reads as fetched (`provenance` R62). It takes `within`, a synchronous function (`store-door` R7): it is called with the document inside the upload's own transaction, after the receipt and the actor are written. An answer `{ok: false, …}` rolls the whole upload back (no receipt, no actor) and is the upload's answer; a throw, or an answer that is not synchronous, rolls it back likewise and answers `UPLOAD_WITHIN_FAILED` (500, a programming fault, no row), its `detail` one fixed sentence, never the thrown message.
