## Public

### Purpose

From a case's draft to its public page, and what comes in from outside: preparing the case, sharing a review copy, the publication ceremony, the published case page, another group's case imported, the case's docket, and the doorbell's page for someone with no account. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

**`SCREENS`** (the screen contract, `interface-shell` R21; each sentence read by key)

**case-editor** (Projects › <project> › Case; the registry's 5 acts)
- **R1** Reads the project's case drafts (`casedrafts`), the draft's document (`casedocument`), its check (`casecheck`) and the "what changed" drafts (`whatchangeddrafts`). Draws the readiness ladder (Draft, Internally checked, Externally compliant, Distributed, each "not yet evaluated" until checked), the scope, what it leaves out with its acknowledgements, the people it names each with its reason (or that the reason is missing), and what changed since the last edition. Acts: `casedraft` "Prepare the draft"; `whatchangedpropose` "Draft what changed" (only while the assistant is reachable, drawn labelled machine work, `ai.label.untested` where its answer carries `untested`, kept only by the member's own words); `statementack` "Acknowledge what it leaves out", with the acknowledger's own reason (`case-inputs` R1, `STATEMENT_ACK_NO_REASON`); `attribute` "Name with the reason" (its consequence statement in the dialog, `op-grades` R4); `writinghelp` under the scope and "what changed" fields only (`interface-shell` R20; never under a reason). (T51 fix round 2; K4320) The statement box is labelled `review.statement.title`; a never-prepared edition is no failed read.

**review-copy** (Projects › <project> › Review copy; the registry's 3 acts; DEC-100)
- **R2** Reads the draft's review grants and their comments (`reviewcomments`). Each named reader is a row with what they may read and until when, offering `reviewrevoke` "Stop sharing". `reviewgrant` "Share with a named reader" (a name, a role, and an end date) carries `mark.outward.means` and answers the grant's secret once: the screen shows it once as the reader's link `<origin>/app/review#grant=<secret>`, with "Civicsmith sends no email" (K3137; journey 21), for the member to hand over, and never keeps it (no storage, address or later request). `reviewcomment` "Reply" answers a reader's comment. (T51 fix round 2; K4320) A session that may not share is drawn no share boxes.

**ceremony** (Projects › <project> › Publish; the registry's 7 acts; DEC-147, DEC-180, DEC-183; N683, N757, N788)
- **R3** The ceremony draws its steps in order (What, Strength, Leaves out, People, Photos, Disclosures, Bias, Preview, Ties, Sign), the current one marked, and carries the required "Publication ceremony" wizard (`screens-wizards` R7). Reads `publishpreflight` (each finding against the bar, every refusal that holds before signing, by its words), `publishtensions` (what will be disclosed with the case: unresolved contradictions, the timeline frozen at signing), `photomarks` for each photo the case carries, and `publishschedule` for an edition waiting to publish. Acts: `publishpreflight` "Check again"; `publishtensions` "See everything that will be disclosed".
- **R4** The Photos step (N757, N788; DEC-180, DEC-183): each photo the case carries (relied on or supporting) is shown with its marks; the member draws rectangles on the photo and marks each a person not part of a finding, a number plate, or a city staff member at work (a staff area with a reason), and sends `obscuremark` with those areas, or "nothing to obscure" (`areas: []`); a mark is withdrawn by `obscuremarkwithdraw` with a reason, never erased. The step says what the public copy carries (areas obscured and labelled, camera details removed) and that every photo must be marked or "nothing to obscure" before signing; a photo whose format cannot be covered stops the signing, naming it, by its refusal's words.
- **R5** amended (K1490, K3041; `case-authoring` R76; DEC-208 (2)): the Ties step's "Confirm, and prepare Edition <n> for signing" sends `publish` with `tieAttested: true`; Sign is offered only after it answers. Test: `publish` carries `tieAttested`; control: no Sign before it.

**published** (the public frame; the registry's 2 acts; DEC-112, DEC-147 (5))
- **R6** Drawn in the public frame with no session (`interface-shell` R30) at `/app/published/<case>`: reads `publishedcase` (the edition's findings, each with its two strengths and whether it meets the project's bar, its timeline, the people it names, its evidence, and when it was signed and when published) and `publishededitions`; says whose work the case is and how to read a case. Acts: `verify` "Check the signature"; `publishedcase` "Read the case". Nothing on the page needs an account. (T51 fix round 2; K4320) A finding's claim and falsifier are drawn from the signed `case_words`, else `body.conclusion` / `body.falsifies`, else the authored words, a leading heading being no words. (K5148; WALK-FINAL-10B C5, C6) The published page draws a finding's unrated axis ("not graded yet") and "no bar declared" when `bar.declared` is false, the newer edition's What-changed beside an older edition (`successor`), the lens's `closing`, the case id, manifest hash and basis-version name in the header, and `verification.detail`; the imported screens name a case by `scope` and a finding by `title`, never by id.

**imported** (Projects › Imported › Another group's case; the registry's 4 acts; DEC-96, DEC-112)

Acts: `caseimport` "Import a case"; `importaccept` "Accept what recreated, with a reason"; `importflag` "Flag an issue"; `importwatch` "Watch the publisher's docket".

**docket** (Projects › <project> › Docket; the registry's 2 acts; DEC-116, DEC-100)
- **R8** Reads `docket` (the case's entries: editions, corrections, withdrawals, court orders, each drafted or posted) and `docketprepare` for a draft entry. (T51 fix round 6, G20; K4611) The docket's document picker lists every held document, reading each next page from `next`, and says how many it lists where it cannot.
