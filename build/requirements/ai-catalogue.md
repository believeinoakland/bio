## Public

### Purpose

What a team's copy knows about the AIs it may be offered, and how it keeps that knowledge current without a software release. It holds the signed, closed list of entries (a model at a host), the plans through which each is reached, the evidence behind every statement, the seven check answers, the Gate 1 verdict per part, the characterization, the guidance for connecting, the terms that bear on use, the lifecycle, and the published results library (every AI Civicsmith or a team has tested, on the list or not, with the combined figures of its active results, K5482); it verifies and applies each signed update Civicsmith publishes, keeps the last good copy, and can roll back. It answers who may be offered which AI for which part and, when not, why. It chooses no account, holds no credential, calls no AI, makes no network call, writes no sentence and names no provider in code: every provider fact is data.

### Provides

Terms. An **entry** is a catalogue row for one model at one host (`DESIGN-BRIEF.md` §2); a **plan** is a way of reaching a host (an API account, a subscription, an aggregator, a gateway, the built-in AI, a custom endpoint). A **part** is one of the assistant's parts Gate 1 judges; the list of parts is data in the payload (R14). A **check** is one of `P1`…`P7`; **checks in force** is the list of check ids the caller computed for a piece of work (`ai-accounts`' to compute). A **payload** is one signed catalogue document, an **advisory** one signed lifecycle change, both numbered from one `seq` the issuer counts. `at` is a handed instant, else the store's clock (R50). Refusals and reasons are UPPER_SNAKE codes; no read returns an English sentence.

**Evidence** (AIR11, AIR19, BR31)
- **R1** (T53; AIR11, AIR19, BR31) Every claim in an entry or plan is a pair `{value, ev}`, `ev` either `{grade, source, date}` (`grade` exactly `REPRODUCED`, `REPORTED` or `STATED`; `source` a non-empty string; `date` a `YYYY-MM-DD` that exists) with `page` and `quote` where the claim is a check answer, a restriction or a binding term (those two must carry a `quote`), or `{open: true}`. The claims are: each capability, effort set and effort class, limit and price figure, zero-retention flag, standard task cost, each check answer, each gate verdict, each characterization property, a plan's scope, price basis, runtimes, money, token storage, means allowed, free-tier flag, each restriction and each binding term, and the guidance record. A claim lacking `ev` is refused `CATALOGUE_EVIDENCE_MISSING` naming its path. Test: an entry whose `P1` has no `date` is refused naming `entries[0].checks.P1`. Control: the same entry with `{open: true}` for `P1` is accepted.
- **R2** (T53; K3540; AIR11, AIR19) OPEN restricts nothing. An OPEN claim is returned as `{open: true}`, never defaulted to a value (not `0`, not `false`, not an empty list), and no read removes or lowers an entry or plan for an OPEN claim unless a check in force names it (R30). Test: an entry with OPEN price, scope, runtime and context is offered when `checksInForce` is `[]`. Control: the same entry with `P3` OPEN and `["P3"]` in force is not offered.
- **R3** (T53; AIR11, AIR19, BR31, CH4) A gate verdict and a measured characterization value are graded `REPRODUCED` (a Civicsmith run, naming `run_ref`) or `REPORTED` (a verified team bundle, or a person's reading, CH4); `STATED` is refused for them. A value taken from a provider's text (price, limit, term, restriction, scope) is `STATED` unless Civicsmith reproduced it. Test: a verdict graded `STATED` is refused `CATALOGUE_EVIDENCE_MISSING`. Control: `REPORTED` is accepted and returned with its grade.

**Entries** (AIR1, AIR8, AIR14, AIR17, AIR18, BR3, BR9, BR12)
- **R4** (T53; AIR1, AIR14, AIR24, BR3, BR9) An entry is `{entry_id, display_name, provider, host, model, version, version_kind, plan_ids, adapter_type, profile, developer, capabilities, effort_set, effort_classes, limits, prices, zero_retention, standard_task_costs, pin, served_ids, checks, shown, gate, characterization, lifecycle, confirmed_at, shutdown_date?}`. `entry_id` is lowercase `[a-z0-9._:-]`, at most `ENTRY_ID_MAX` characters, unique, and the only model name above the adapters; `display_name` is the model's name as people read it (DEC-466 U1; at most `DISPLAY_NAME_MAX` characters, no account or owner in it), the one name every machine label shows (`ai-runs` R104), a missing one refused `CATALOGUE_ENTRY_INVALID` naming `display_name`; `model` is the string an adapter sends; `version_kind` is `dated` or `fixed`, `floating` refused `CATALOGUE_ENTRY_INVALID` naming `version_kind` (AIR24: exact versions); `adapter_type` and `profile` `{id, min_version}` name what a copy needs to serve it (R38); `developer` is `{name, jurisdiction}` (R12); `served_ids` lists the model-version identifiers a response may state (`agent-model` R45), non-empty for every entry; `effort_classes` is `{low, medium}`, each a member of `effort_set.values` (absent for a model with no dial); `zero_retention` is `true`, `false` or OPEN (the host offers zero-retention routing); `standard_task_costs` is `{unit, by_part: {part: {low, high}}, price_date}` or OPEN (the measured all-in cost of a standard task per part, CH7). A field outside this list is refused naming it. Test: a well-formed fixture entry for a made-up provider is accepted. Control: `version_kind: "floating"` and an unknown field are each refused.
- **R5** (T53; AIR8, BR12, AD14) `provider` and `host` are ids from the payload's `providers`; every `plan_ids` entry resolves to a plan whose `provider` equals the entry's `host`; an unresolved or mismatched plan is refused `CATALOGUE_ENTRY_INVALID` naming `plan_ids`. A host serving a model is the recipient of material (BR12, AD14 disclosure per provider and host): the pair `(provider, host)` is the entry's `recipient`, returned beside it. Test: two entries of one model at two hosts answer two recipients. Control: a plan of another host is refused.
- **R6** (T53; AIR1, BR9) An entry whose host is of kind `aggregator` or `gateway` carries `pin` `{only: [provider ids], allow_fallbacks: false, deny_collection: true, require_parameters: bool}` (zero-retention routing is asked where the entry's `zero_retention` is true) and a non-empty `served_ids` (R4); without them it is refused `CATALOGUE_ENTRY_INVALID` naming `pin`. `allow_fallbacks: true` is refused. The pin is data; the adapter sends it. An aggregator's whole list is never held: no payload field can name a host's models except as entries. Test: an aggregator entry with `allow_fallbacks: true` refused. Control: the same entry at a `direct` host needs no pin.
- **R7** (T53; AIR8, BR3) `capabilities` has exactly these keys, each `{value, ev}`: `tools`, `parallel_tools`, `schema_output`, `schema_with_tools`, `image_input`, `streaming` (`none`, `text` or `tool_calls`), `session` (`none`, `provider_state` or `resend_only`), `caching` (`none`, `automatic` or `explicit`), `batch`: the names and value sets are `agent-model` R30's. `effort_set` is `{values: [lowercase words of at most `EFFORT_WORD_MAX` letters], default}`; a model with no dial has `values: []`, no `default` and no `effort_classes`, and a `default` outside `values` is refused. `limits` has `context_stated`, `context_effective`, `max_output`, `image_max_bytes`, each a number or OPEN. Test: `effort_set {values: [], default: null}` accepted. Control: `default: "high"` with `values: ["low"]` refused.
- **R8** (T53; AIR18, BR22) `prices` is `{unit: "usd_per_million_tokens", input, output, cache_read, cache_write, tiers?, date, ev}`; each figure a non-negative number, or `null` with OPEN where the provider states none: an unstated price is `null`, never `0` (BR22). A price carries its date. A plan whose `money` is `none` gives the entry `prices` OPEN (R16). Test: `cache_write: null` returned as `null`. Control: a price of `-1` refused.
- **R9** (T53; AIR14, SR8, CH1–CH10) `characterization` holds exactly the ten properties `CH1`…`CH10`, each `{value, method, samples, conditions, ev}` or OPEN; `CH10` is `{ref: "checks"}` (not a copy of the answers); `CH6` sets `limits.context_effective` and `CH7` `standard_task_costs` (R4). An entry whose `lifecycle` is `listed` or `watch` with no `characterization` record is refused `CATALOGUE_ENTRY_INVALID` (AIR17 (c)). A property not measured is OPEN, never inferred. `CH4` (the reasonableness reading) is graded `REPORTED`, names `reviewer` and `date`, and is read by no offering rule (SR3). Test: changing only `CH4` changes no `entriesFor` answer. Control: dropping the record from a listed entry refuses the payload.
- **R10** (T53; AIR35 as varied by AD34; README 7) `developer.jurisdiction` is a code or OPEN carried as data; no code in this module names a place. Test: a scan of the module's source finds no country code or place name from a list the test holds. Control: planting one in the source fails the scan.

**The seven checks** (AIR42, AIR43, AD32–AD35)
- **R11** (T53; AIR42, AIR43, AD32–AD35) An entry carries `checks.P1`…`checks.P7`, each `{answer: "met"|"not_met"|"OPEN", ev}`: (P1) no training on content; (P2) no human reading by default; (P3) a stated retention period, zero-retention used where offered; (P4) where it runs and under which country's law; (P5) no hidden onward passing (host pinned); (P6) works with the team's keep-away limits and the recipient notice; (P7) not developed by a Chinese organisation, judged by developer. `met` and `not_met` need `quote`, `page` and `date`; `OPEN` needs none. A missing check is refused `CATALOGUE_ENTRY_INVALID`. Test: an entry with six checks refused. Control: seven accepted.
- **R12** (T53; AD31 varied by AD34) `P7` follows the developer, not the host, and is policy data: the payload's `policy.origin` is `{check: "P7", barred: [jurisdiction codes]}`, revisable without code. An entry whose `developer.jurisdiction` is in `barred` cannot carry `P7` `met` (refused `CATALOGUE_ENTRY_INVALID` naming `checks.P7`); a developer with `jurisdiction` OPEN cannot carry `met` without a `quote`. Such an entry is **allowed** in the catalogue after Gate 1 and characterization like any other; it is simply not offered where `P7` is in force (R30). Test: one model of a barred developer at two hosts: both entries carry `P7` `not_met`, both are absent from `entriesFor({checksInForce: ["P7"]})` and present from `entriesFor({checksInForce: []})`. Control: an unbarred developer's entry is unaffected by `["P7"]`.
- **R13** (T53; AIR42, AD34) `shown` holds `terms_permit_use` and `breach_notification`, each answered as a check is, **shown and never required**: either id in `checksInForce` is refused `CHECK_UNKNOWN`. Test: `entriesFor({part, checksInForce: ["terms_permit_use"]})` answers `CHECK_UNKNOWN`. Control: the two values are still returned by `entryOf`.

**Gate 1 verdicts** (AIR13, SR6, SR10, SR25)
- **R14** (T53; AIR13, SR6, SR10, SR25) The payload's `parts` is the list of part ids: `ask`, `draft`, `read`, `transcribe`, `run`, `account` (`screens-ai`, the assistant screens' draft §1). An entry's `gate` maps a part to `{verdict, limits, partial_coverage, suiteVersions: {bank, scorer, thresholds, image}, tier, at, run_ref, ev}`; `verdict` is `passes`, `passes_with_limits` (non-empty `limits`, each `{kind, value}` with `kind` from `ai-gate` R36's closed list: `context_depth`, `no_image_input`, `no_streaming`, `no_parallel_tools`, `output_cap`), `not_suitable` or `not_yet_rated` (SR7: measures shown, no verdict until Bob's table); `partial_coverage` is true where SR13 applies. A part name outside `parts` is refused `CATALOGUE_ENTRY_INVALID`. A part with no record reads as `not_yet_rated`. Test: `passes_with_limits` with empty limits refused. Control: with limits it is accepted and returned.
- **R15** (T53; AIR5, AIR42, SR10) An entry is offered for a part only on `passes` or `passes_with_limits`; the verdict, its limits and its grade are returned in the row so the part is refused by name otherwise (AIR5, SR10). Gate 1 never depends on the check answers (AIR42). Test: an entry `not_suitable` for `draft` is offered for `ask` when it passes `ask`. Control: `not_yet_rated` is not offered.

**Plans** (AIR2, AIR3, AIR20, AIR29, AIR30, AIR31, AIR32, AIR34, AIR37–AIR41, BR13, BR14)
- **R16** (T53; AIR2, AIR29, AIR34, BR13, BR14) A plan is `{plan_id, provider, kind, scope, credential_kind, means_allowed, connection_ways, runtimes, token_storage, token_bounds?, price_basis, money, restrictions, binding_terms, guidance_id, free_tier_trains, warning_id?, lifecycle, confirmed_at}`. `kind` is `api`, `subscription`, `aggregator`, `gateway`, `builtin` or `custom`; `scope` is `member`, `project`, `team` or `unconfirmed`; `credential_kind` is `api_key`, `signin_token` or `none`; `connection_ways` a non-empty subset of `pasted_key`, `provider_signin`, `built_in` (AIR9); `price_basis` `{basis, amount?, currency?, period?, includes, limits, date}`; `money` is `reported` (the plan states cost), `priced` (the catalogue's per-token prices apply), `none` (flat or unmetered) or OPEN, and `ai-use` allows `usd` limits only for `reported` or `priced`; `token_bounds` `{min_length, max_length}` bounds a `signin_token` plan's token (`ai-accounts` R19); `free_tier_trains` a boolean or OPEN; each `restriction` is `{kind, quote, page, date}` or OPEN. Test: a fixture plan of each kind accepted. Control: `kind: "pooled"` refused `CATALOGUE_PLAN_INVALID`.
- **R17** (T53; AIR32, AIR45) `means_allowed` is a non-empty subset of `gateway`, `sealed`, `reference`, `issued`, `none`: `gateway` only where `credential_kind` is `api_key`, `issued` only where it is `signin_token`, and `none` only where it is `none` (the built-in AI holds no credential) and then alone. Anything else is refused `CATALOGUE_PLAN_INVALID` naming `means_allowed`. It is read from `plansOf`. Test: a `signin_token` plan allowing `gateway` refused; a `builtin` plan allowing `none` accepted. Control: an `api_key` plan allowing `gateway` and `sealed` accepted, and one allowing `none` refused.
- **R18** (T53; AIR3, AIR41) A plan's row answers `levels`, the account levels its scope serves (`group` is the team's identifier in code): `member` → `[member]`, `project` → `[member, project]`, `team` → `[member, project, group]`, `unconfirmed` → all three with `unconfirmed: true` (it restricts nothing, K3540). Enforcement is `ai-accounts`'. Test: scope `member` answers `[member]`. Control: scope `unconfirmed` answers all three with the flag.
- **R19** (T53; AIR37, AIR41, BR14) `runtimes` is a list drawn from `shared_worker`, `member_runtime`, `member_device` (the deployments the plan allows) or OPEN; `token_storage` is `sealed`, `reference_only`, `member_device` or OPEN. The row answers `constraints: {runtimes, token_storage, enforce}` with `enforce: "level_rule_only"` for a **personal subscription** (kind `subscription`, scope `member`): shown to the member, not enforced; and `enforce: "deployment"` for every other plan, enforced by the boundary only where the constraint is stated (OPEN enforces nothing). Test: a `subscription` plan of scope `member` with `runtimes: ["member_device"]` answers `level_rule_only`. Control: the same plan at scope `team` answers `deployment`.
- **R20** (T53; AIR30, AD20) A plan is offered only if its `guidance_id` resolves to a guidance record `{guidance_id, plan_id, steps: [{kind, text, url?}], ev}`, `kind` one of `link`, `choose`, `copy`, `approve`, `confirm`, with at least one step, each `text` a map from language tag to words (R51), and `ev` graded. A plan with no resolvable guidance is not offered (reason `PLAN_NO_GUIDANCE`); a payload may carry it, since guidance can arrive in a later payload. Test: a plan without guidance is absent from every entry's offered plans and `whyNotOffered` names `PLAN_NO_GUIDANCE`. Control: adding the record offers it.
- **R21** (T53; AIR31, AD21) A plan with `free_tier_trains: true` carries a `warning` record `{warning_id, text, ev}` and the row answers `warning_version`, the SHA-256 of the canonical text (`record-grammar` R12), so changed text cannot keep its version. A plan with `free_tier_trains` OPEN restricts nothing (R2) and is shown unconfirmed. Test: changing one character of the warning text changes `warning_version`. Control: `free_tier_trains: true` with no warning refused.
- **R22** (T53; AIR38–AIR40; K5493: "A, but presented as any website enables users to view their terms of service when signing up for an account. They can choose to read them, or not.") `binding_terms` is a list of `{doc, version, section, quote, effective_from, effective_to?, kind, ev}`, `kind` one of `political_use`, `high_risk_review`, `indemnity`, `no_sla`, `deprecation_notice`, `other`. They are optional reading behind a link beside the connect act, never a required step, and restrict nothing: no offering rule reads them, and no act waits on their being read. Test: an entry whose plan has a `political_use` term is still offered. Control: a term lacking `quote` is refused.
- **R23** (T53; AIR39) Terms with a future `effective_from` are never hidden. A plan row answers `terms_pending`, each `{doc, version, effective_from, days_until}` for every term whose `effective_from` is after `at`, soonest first; and `needs_reconfirmation: true` once a term's `effective_from` has passed with the plan's `confirmed_at` before it (a flag for Civicsmith's review of its own quotes, never a step for a team). `terms_pending` feeds the notice before a change takes effect (`ai-accounts` R76; K5493). Test: a term effective N days after `at` answers `days_until: N`; after its date with an old `confirmed_at` the flag is true. Control: a re-confirmed plan answers false, and neither state removes an offer.

**Lifecycle and advisories** (AIR24)
- **R24** (T53; AIR24) An entry or plan is `listed`, `watch`, `lapsed` or `retired`. Moves: `listed`→`watch`, `watch`→`listed`, `listed`→`lapsed`, `watch`→`lapsed`, `lapsed`→`retired`; no other. A state is changed only by a verified payload or advisory (R26, R35), never by any call. Test: `retired`→`listed` by advisory is refused `ADVISORY_BAD_MOVE`. Control: `watch`→`listed` (a clear) applies.
- **R25** (T53; AIR24, K3540) `listed` and `watch` are offered (`watch` flagged). `lapsed` and `retired` are not offered for any new run, and `entryOf` still returns them, so results already produced keep a resolvable label (AIR24); no state change alters anything outside this module. **Omission is not a state change**: an entry or plan that a newer payload leaves out keeps the state and record of the highest-`seq` record that names it (R27), is flagged `omitted_since` (the `seq` of the first payload without it) in `entryOf` and its row, and is withdrawn only by a signed advisory (R26) or by a newer payload that names it with state `lapsed` or `retired` through a legal move (R24); it is never read as retired by absence, so nothing changes silently (AIR24). Test: an entry omitted from a newer payload keeps its state, answers `omitted_since`, and a `retire` advisory (after `lapse`) moves it. Control: a payload that names it `retired` directly from `listed` is refused `ADVISORY_BAD_MOVE`.
- **R26** (T53; AIR24) An advisory is `{seq, target: {entry_id}|{plan_id}, kind, reason, effective_at, issued_at}` signed in its own namespace (R33); `kind` is `watch`, `lapse`, `retire` or `clear`; `reason` is one of `ADVISORY_REASONS`, **the one closed list** (`ai-issuer` R7 reads it from here and keeps no copy): `deprecation` (a provider's deprecation or shutdown date), `canary_drift`, `served_mismatch`, `team_divergence`, `health` (a health report), `terms_change`, `other`; a reason outside it is refused `ADVISORY_REASON_UNKNOWN`; `effective_at` may be future. Before `effective_at` it is **upcoming** (listed in `catalogueState`), after it, applied. Test: a `lapse` with `reason` `deprecation` and a future `effective_at` is upcoming now and applied at its date. Controls: `reason: "bogus"` is `ADVISORY_REASON_UNKNOWN`; an advisory for an unknown target is held and applies if the target arrives, and refused `ADVISORY_BAD_MOVE` if the move is not allowed.
- **R27** (T53; AIR24) The effective state of a target is that of the **highest-`seq`** record naming it, payload or advisory. Rolling back a payload (R41) never lowers a state set by a higher-`seq` advisory. Test: roll back to a payload that listed an entry a later advisory lapsed; it stays `lapsed`. Control: a payload of higher `seq` that lists it again returns it to `listed` only through a legal move (R24).

**Reads** (all: never throw, write nothing, frozen deterministic answers, ordered by `provider` then `entry_id`)
- **R28** (T53; AIR19, AIR24) `entryOf(entry_id)` answers the entry whole with its effective state, `recipient`, `needs_newer_release` (R38), `omitted_since` (R25) and `review_overdue` (`confirmed_at` plus the policy's `review_period_days` before `at`; shown, restricting nothing), or `null` for an id not in the catalogue. Test: a held id answers its entry; `null` for a raw model string of an aggregator not listed. Control: a retired id still answers.
- **R29** (T53; AIR2, AIR38–AIR41) `plansOf({provider?, entry_id?, plan_id?})` answers plans listed under each provider (AD18), each with `kind`, `scope`, `levels` (R18), `constraints` (R19), `terms_pending` (R23), `terms_version` (the SHA-256 of the canonical `restrictions` and `binding_terms`, as R21 does), `warning_version` (R21), `means_allowed`, `money`, `token_bounds`, `free_tier_trains`, `guidance_id`, effective state, and `offered` with `reasons` (`PLAN_NO_GUIDANCE`, `PLAN_LAPSED`, `PLAN_RETIRED`, `PLAN_NO_MEANS`). With `entry_id` only the plans that entry names; with `plan_id` only that plan. Test: two providers answer two provider lists. Control: an unknown provider answers an empty list.
- **R30** (T53; AIR5, AIR42, SR10) `entriesFor({part, checksInForce, at?})` is **the closed list**. An entry is returned only if all hold: a verified catalogue is held; its effective state is `listed` or `watch`; it does not need a newer release (R38); its `gate[part].verdict` is `passes` or `passes_with_limits`; every id in `checksInForce` is `met` on it (`not_met` and OPEN both fail); and at least one of its plans is offered (R29). Nothing else removes an entry (R2, R22, R28). Each row: `entry_id`, `recipient`, `state`, `part {verdict, limits, grade}`, `plans` (the offered `plan_id`s), `checks` (the answers for the checks in force). A `part` outside the payload's `parts` is refused `PART_UNKNOWN`; a check id outside `P1`…`P7` is refused `CHECK_UNKNOWN`; no verified catalogue held answers `CATALOGUE_UNAVAILABLE` and offers nothing. Test: fixture of eight entries, one failing each condition, returns exactly the passing one. Control: the failing conditions removed one by one return each entry.
- **R31** (T53; AIR5, AIR42) `whyNotOffered({entry_id, part, checksInForce})` answers `{offered, reasons}` using R30's predicate, `reasons` listing **every** failing condition in a fixed order, each `{code, …}`: `ENTRY_UNKNOWN`, `ENTRY_LAPSED`, `ENTRY_RETIRED`, `NEEDS_NEWER_RELEASE`, `PART_NOT_PASSED` (with `verdict`), `PART_NOT_RATED`, `CHECK_NOT_MET` or `CHECK_OPEN` (each with `check`), `NO_PLAN_OFFERED` (with the plan reasons), `CATALOGUE_UNAVAILABLE`. Test: for every fixture entry and part, `offered` is true exactly when `entriesFor` returns it. Control: an entry failing two conditions answers both codes.
- **R32** (T53; AIR24, AD25; K5483) `catalogueState({at?})` answers `{state, seq, schema_version, issued_at, source, last_check, last_good_seq, age_days, rolled_back, held_back_seq, advisories_seq, upcoming, entries: {total, needing_release}}`. `state` is the first that holds: `unavailable` (nothing verified held), `needs_newer_release` (the channel holds a signed payload of a schema this copy cannot read, R37), `stale` (no successful check for more than the policy's `stale_after_days`), `current`. `source` is `seed` or `pulled`. Test: each state reached by its cause. Control: a seed-only copy that checked today is `current` with `source: "seed"`.

**The signed channel** (AIR33, AD25, SR84)
- **R33** (T53; AIR33, AD25, SR84) The channel's signatures are `signatures`' (`signatures` R49–R56): namespaces `NS_AI_CATALOGUE` and `NS_AI_ADVISORY` (its R49), distinct from every other, verified by `verifyAiCatalogue` and `verifyAiAdvisory` (its R53) against `CATALOGUE_KEYS` (its R50), a list of its own that is not `RELEASE_KEYS`. A payload is signed by a statement binding `seq`, `schema_version`, `issued_at` and the payload's SHA-256 and byte count (its R51); an advisory by a statement binding every one of its fields (its R52). Test: a payload and an advisory each signed with a throwaway key in the list verify. Control: the same bytes signed in `NS_RELEASE`, or by a key outside the list, are each refused.
- **R34** (T53; AIR33, AD25) `pullRequest(kind, path?)` answers the request a caller makes and this module does not: `GET` of `CATALOGUE_ADDRESS` (the `index`) or of a relative `path` beneath it (`^[a-z0-9._/-]+$`, no `..`), headers only `accept` and the fixed user agent `CATALOGUE_USER_AGENT`; no cookie, `Authorization`, team name, slug, member, setting or installed version in address or headers. The caller reads at most `INDEX_MAX_BYTES` of the index and `CATALOGUE_MAX_BYTES` of a payload, follows no redirect. Test: the request for both kinds carries exactly those headers. Control: a `path` of `../x` or `https://other/x` is refused `CATALOGUE_PATH_INVALID`.
- **R35** (T53; AIR33, AD25, SR84) `receive({kind, status, body, at, trigger})` (`trigger` `schedule` or `manual`) is the only way a pulled byte becomes state. The index `{index_version, catalogue: {seq, schema_version, issued_at, sha256, bytes, path, armored}, advisories: [...]}` is checked; each advisory is verified and applied by R26 whether or not the payload is newer; a payload is fetched only when the index's `seq` is higher than the held. A payload is accepted only when, in this order: its SHA-256 and bytes equal the index's (else `DIGEST_MISMATCH`); the statement verifies (R33; `signatures`' reasons `MALFORMED`, `NAMESPACE`, `UNKNOWN_KEY`, `BAD_SIGNATURE` pass through); `seq` is higher than the highest held (else `NOT_NEWER`, so a replayed old payload changes nothing); its `schema_version` is readable (R37); and `validatePayload` (R36) holds. Acceptance is atomic. Each call appends one check `{at, trigger, outcome}`: `found`, `none`, or the refusal code, `unreachable` for a non-200. Test: a payload with one character changed stores nothing and records `BAD_SIGNATURE`. Control: an older correctly signed payload records `NOT_NEWER`; the same payload twice records `none` the second time.
- **R36** (T53; AIR33, AIR36) `validatePayload(payload, {supported})` is pure and shared (the issuer calls it before publishing): `{ok: true}` or `{ok: false, code: "CATALOGUE_PAYLOAD_INVALID", path}` naming the first offending path, applying R1–R23, R43–R44 and R53–R55 to the whole document; one invalid entry refuses the whole payload (no partial catalogue). Test: a payload with one entry lacking `P7` is refused naming it and the held catalogue is unchanged. Control: removing that entry makes it valid.
- **R37** (T53; AIR33, AD25) `SCHEMA_VERSIONS_READ` is the list of `schema_version`s this release reads. A signed payload of an unread version is **held, verified, unapplied**; `catalogueState` answers `needs_newer_release`, and the last-good payload keeps serving. Test: a schema above every version in `SCHEMA_VERSIONS_READ` held: state `needs_newer_release`, offers unchanged. Control: a release that reads it applies it at its next `receive` or restart.
- **R38** (T53; AIR33) Agent-model registers what the copy can serve: `registerAdapterTypes([{type, profiles: {id: version}}])`, called at start by the module that owns the adapters (this module imports none). An entry whose `adapter_type` is not registered, or whose `profile.min_version` exceeds the registered one, answers `needs_newer_release: true`, is not offered (`NEEDS_NEWER_RELEASE`), and stays visible to `entryOf`; with nothing registered every entry needs one. Test: an entry of an unregistered type is returned by `entryOf` with the flag and is absent from `entriesFor`. Control: registering the type offers it.
- **R39** (T53; AIR33, AD25) A release carries a **seed** payload, verified by R33 and R36 as a pulled one is and held as the first version (`source: "seed"`). A seed older than a held pulled payload is not applied (`NOT_NEWER`); a copy that has never pulled offers from the seed. Test: a copy with no network offers the seed's entries. Control: a seed with a bad signature is refused and the copy is `unavailable`. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R40** (T53; AIR33, AD25; K5483) The last good payload serves until a better verified one arrives: an unreachable address, a refused payload or an unreadable schema changes no state. After `stale_after_days` without a successful check `catalogueState` answers `stale`; offers are unchanged. Test: `unreachable` checks for longer than `stale_after_days` leave `entriesFor` as it was and `state: "stale"`. Control: one `found` returns `current`.
- **R41** (T53; AIR24, AD25) `rollback({to_seq, by, reason, at})` makes a held, verified earlier payload current (`CATALOGUE_ROLLBACK_UNKNOWN` for a `seq` not held), records `{from_seq, to_seq, by, reason, at}`, and sets `held_back_seq` to the `seq` rolled back from: payloads at or below it are still held but not applied; a higher one applies at the next `receive`. `unrollback({by, at})` clears the hold. Who may roll back is the caller's (Suggestions). Advisories are untouched (R27). Test: roll back from 7 to 5; a pull of 7 stays held; 8 applies. Control: after `unrollback` 7 applies.
- **R42** (T53; AD25) The module keeps the last `HELD_VERSIONS` verified payloads, the current one and the one rolled back from among them, and every advisory; older payloads are dropped only when a newer is applied. Test: `HELD_VERSIONS` plus two payloads leave `HELD_VERSIONS`. Control: the current is never dropped.

A figure not measured is OPEN. Test: a Civicsmith row and a team row for one entry, and a team row for a tested AI, are each returned with their grades. Control: a row whose `subject_id` is neither an entry nor a tested AI is refused.
- **R44** (T53; AD15) The row schema is **closed**: transcripts, request or response bodies, a team's id, address or member, and any key outside R43 are refused `CATALOGUE_PAYLOAD_INVALID` naming `library_private`. `team_name` is accepted only with a `consent_ref`; without it the row says `tested_by: "team"` and no name. - **R45** (T53; AIR36, AD12; K5482) `libraryRows({subject_id?, part?, at?})` answers the **active** rows (R43): those whose `tested_at` is less than `RESULT_ACTIVE_MONTHS` calendar months (Bob: six, K5482) before `at`, each with `age_days`, `on_list` (the entry's effective state, or `not_listed` for a tested AI) and `confirmed` (grade `REPRODUCED` or `repeated_by_civicsmith`; otherwise the screens say "reported by teams, not confirmed"). ### Suggestions

- **Files.** `ai-catalogue/src/{schema,entry,plan,checks,lifecycle,reads,channel,library}.mjs`. The payload is one JSON document; the validators are table-driven so adding a field is one row.
- **Fetching is a caller's job** (README 2): `host-governor` is layer 3 and `scheduler` layer 10, both after this module, so a layer-11 consumer registers `catalogue-check` (once a day at a minute drawn and kept per store, as `instance-setup` R88 does), makes the request R34 states through `host-governor` and hands the answer to `receive`. A manual refresh is an administrator's op over the same call.
- **Who may roll back or refresh** is the op's (`NOT_AN_ADMIN`, `membership` R84), with `by` the control plane's stamp; this module does not use `membership`.
- **The gate that reads it.** `ai-runs` R75's extended record is Civicsmith's published verdict for a listed entry (this module); a copy's own test-instance exception and its own test investigations stay as `run-rules` R29 has them, per entry.
- **Tests.** Build every fixture from throwaway keys and a made-up provider; one negative control per R; a table-driven check that each of R30's conditions fails independently; the property test of R31 against R30.
- **Parameters** (proposed <value>; set at START, K5457): `ENTRY_ID_MAX` proposed 96; `DISPLAY_NAME_MAX` proposed 60; `EFFORT_WORD_MAX` proposed 16; `INDEX_MAX_BYTES` proposed 64 KiB; `CATALOGUE_MAX_BYTES` proposed 4 MiB; `HELD_VERSIONS` proposed 5; `stale_after_days` 14 (Bob: two weeks, K5483); `RESULT_ACTIVE_MONTHS` 6 (Bob's, K5482, not proposed); `review_period_days` proposed 90; `CATALOGUE_CHECK_PERIOD` proposed daily; `CATALOGUE_USER_AGENT` proposed `Civicsmith-catalogue/1`.
- **Until Bob's thresholds exist** (AD13, SR7) every verdict is `not_yet_rated`, so no entry is offered for use; the catalogue works only after the threshold table has rated at least one entry.
