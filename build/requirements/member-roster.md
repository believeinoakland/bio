## Public

### Purpose

Who the members are and what each may do: the founder, administrators and their votes, invitations and enrolment, the two doors, handles, capabilities, expertise, the hosting-access record, the group's settings and description, the Home mark, the viewer's spelling, the one list of module order, and the refusals and listener slots every later module registers through. It reads no bundle and no project.

### Provides

Terms. *Administrators* are the founder (the root of trust's session, id `admin`, exported as `ROOT_ADMIN` for a caller that must name her (N1067), once the instance is claimed; `membership` keeps `Membership.ROOT_ADMIN` as an alias) and every member with role `admin` and status `active`. An act's `by` is the actor the control plane stamps, never the caller's claim. *Sight* of a project is `membership`'s (`membership` R44) and is not asked here. Every refusal names its reason; no service throws.
