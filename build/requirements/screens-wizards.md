## Public

### Purpose

The wizards a member walks on the real screens: the group's wizard library (`wizards`), and the runner, the wizard guide docked beside any screen, which says each step's what and why, rings the control the step names and never presses it. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

**`SCREENS`, `SCREEN_WORDS`, `GUIDE`** (the screen contract, `interface-shell` R21; words, its R5; the dock's guide, its R31)

Terms. A **script**, a **version**, a **step**, **offered** and a **side trip** (`via`) are `wizard-scripts`' (its Terms, R1, R2, R11). **The runner** is this module's `GUIDE`, registered with the shell for the dock (`interface-shell` R31). A **wizard mark** is the mark a screen shows for each offered script that starts there.

**wizards** (Settings › Wizards; the registry's 6 acts; DEC-121, DEC-158)
- **R1** Reads `wizards` (the scripts the member may see, each with its state, author, contributors, approver and dates, `broken`, its use, and `based_on` with its base's name), `wizardread` for one script's version with its steps and attribution, and `baseupdates` (each copy whose base has a newer approved version, with both step lists). Each script is a row (`interface-shell` R19): its name (a Civicsmith script's by its key `wizard.<id>.name`, a group's as recorded), whose it is (Civicsmith's or this group's), what a copy is based on, who approved it, and how many finished it; a copy whose base changed says so, showing what changed. Acts, each with its owner's arguments and drawn per `interface-shell` R12:
  - `wizards` "See the whole library": the list again with a state chosen (drafts, submitted, withdrawn, retired, broken, or open proposals);
  - `wizarddraft`: "Record a new wizard" (R3's recording) and "Copy a wizard to change it" (`copy`, any approved version the member may see, the new script showing what it is based on);
  - `wizardrevise`: reword, reorder or delete a draft's steps (adding one only with the advanced editor, `wizard-scripts` R8), adopt a proposal, or bring a base's change across (`adopt: {base}`);
  - `wizardsubmit`, `wizardapprove` (a project owner; "make it group-wide", `widen`, an administrator), `wizardretire` (a script, by an approver, or a draft or submitted version, by its author; with a reason).
  A Civicsmith script's row offers no write act but the copy (`wizard-scripts` R9: `WIZARD_NOT_THE_GROUPS`). Every refusal is drawn by `interface-shell` R13, a check's refusal naming its step.

**The runner** (the dock's wizard guide; DEC-120 (2), DEC-139 (4), DEC-140)
- **R2** A wizard starts from a screen's wizard mark (`wizardsat` for that screen: each offered script with its name and finished count, and the member's own drafts, run privately), from Home's front doors (`screens-daily` R9, through `cs.wizard`), or from the library (R1). While it runs, the dock holds the guide (`interface-shell` R31): the script's name, "step <i> of <n>", the step's what and why (a Civicsmith script's by its keys `wizard.<id>.step<n>.what` and `.why`, a group's as recorded), Back and Next, and Stop with "Stopping is fine: nothing is lost, and nobody is told". On each step the runner opens the step's screen (`cs.go`) and, where the step names an act, rings that act's control once (`cs.ring`); "Show me where" (Alt+Shift+W) rings it again, as often as the member asks. A step whose act the screen does not draw for this member (absent by `interface-shell` R12, or the screen in another state) says so and offers Next. The runner never presses a control, never sends an act, and never fills a field but as R4 says.
- **R3** "Record a new wizard" puts the guide in recording: as the member works on the real screens, each act she presses is added as `{screen, act}` (`interface-shell` R31 tells the guide the op and screen only), never a value she typed; she may remove a recorded pair before saving, and saving sends `wizarddraft` with `recorded` and the name she gives. Her draft's `what` and `why` are then written by `wizardrevise` on `wizards`.
- **R4** A step carrying a `draft` places it in the field of the step's act, by `cs.fill`, labelled with its kind ("Draft · from the wizard", "Draft · from your group's template", or, for `{machine: <op>}`, "Draft · the assistant's, asked by <handle>"), and never submits it: it becomes the member's words only by her own act. A `{machine: <op>}` draft is asked of its op only while the assistant is reachable (`interface-shell` R20) and at the member's press of "Draft it", carrying `ai.label.untested` where the answer carries `untested`; with the assistant not reachable the step offers no machine draft. A step whose act is one the assistant may not help with is never given a machine draft (`wizard-scripts` R24, R12).
- **R5** A step with `via` starts that script as a side trip, the guide saying "Side trip: <name>. When it finishes or you stop it, you come back to this step."; when it ends, by its last step or by Stop, the guide returns to the step left (DEC-139 (4)).
- **R6** The runner reports progress only through `wizardprogress` (`start`, each `step` reached, `finish`), which carries nothing naming the member, the case, the project or the instant beyond the day (`wizard-scripts` R15); stopping sends nothing. A running wizard's place is kept in the page's memory only, never in the browser's storage, an address or a request, so leaving the page ends it.
- **R7** (N551, its share; DEC-148) Home offers the required "Welcome a new member" first, and "Your first question" after it, to a member who has finished neither on this device (the finish kept on the device, never sent), as wizard marks at the top of Home; the ceremony screen (`screens-publish`) carries the required "Publication ceremony" as its wizard mark. A required wizard is offered, never forced. The welcome offered on Home after `join` continues at its first step on a working screen (step 3): its two `join` steps are counted done by the enrolment (one `wizardprogress` `start` and the `step` reached at the join's end, sent once the member's session exists); "Not now" keeps the offer (K3137; journey 28).

- **R10** (T48; N847; DEC-227 (1); `wizard-scripts` R33, R37; amends R2) While the guide runs a script that any of `startfrom`'s doors names (its `doors[].scripts`), every step carries one quiet line, `wizard.guide.elsewhere`, its `{link}` the reference `wizard.guide.elsewhere_link` opening `elsewhere` (its card `screen.elsewhere.name` and `.purpose`, `interface-shell` R16). The guide reads `startfrom` once when a run starts; a read that fails draws no line and stops nothing. Opening the screen sends nothing and keeps the run where it was (R6). Test: "Get a record" carries the line on every step; control: "Welcome a new member", which no door names, carries none.
