## Public

### Purpose

The deadlines on the group's actions, read across actions: which clock entries are pending and which have passed, the entries a profile's deadline would give (counted in calendar days, or business days on the profile's holiday calendar, saying whether a member has confirmed that calendar), and the reminders a member asks for on them. An action's clock is written in its own document (`actions`); this module reads it, proposes entries apart, and holds the members' reminders, which fire as asked and never otherwise. A member states every entry and every reminder; a machine proposes an entry and nothing else. A reminder is this module's record of a member's request, never part of the action's document.

### Provides

Terms. An **action**, its document and its **clock entry** (`{text, description, date, basis, status}`, status `pending`, `met`, `overdue`, `waived`) are `actions`' (its Terms). An entry's **position** is its index in the document's `clock[]`. A **day** is a `YYYY-MM-DD` local calendar day in the zone `actions` R12 reads for the action (the office's or venue's jurisdiction; `civil-time.localDay`), never the UTC day (K1444 (iii)); an entry is past on a day after its date (a deadline is met by anything on its day, `actions` R12). A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, ...}`; a catalogue-backed one also carries `code`, `check` and `translation`.

**pendingClocks({before, after?, limit?, viewer})** (`monitoring`'s read)
- **R1** `pendingClocks({before, after?, limit?, viewer})` refuses a `before` that is not a date `PENDING_CLOCKS_BAD_BEFORE` (its own row; K368), and otherwise lists every `pending` clock entry dated before `before` across visible actions: action, entry position, date, basis, text, and whether it is past at `before`; at most 500 per page, `truncated` stated. This is the read `monitoring` watches. (N237, N311) A page runs in (action id, entry position) order after `after`, a previous page's `cursor` or an action id (read as after all that action's entries), reads at most 500 actions, and may end inside an action; `cursor` is the last entry answered (`<action>#<position>`) when `truncated`, else null, and when the 500-action bound ends a page past an action with no qualifying entry, `cursor` is that action's last position, so a page always moves on; so every entry is reached by paging from the start through each `cursor` to null (ACTIONS #3 J1, K401).

**clockPropose({target, rule, proposer, viewer})**
- **R2** `clockPropose({target, rule, proposer, viewer})` offers a clock entry computed from a profile deadline (`jurisdictions` R26) that applies to the action's kind: the start from the event the rule names in the ledger, `received` being the counterparty's receipt of the group's request, counted from the group's `sent` entry (C-1; `jurisdictions` R26), never from a `received` entry; the date by `civil-time.evaluateRule` (its R9–R13: the count forward or backward, calendar or business days on the profile's holiday calendar, `jurisdictions` R33, the roll past closed days, the weekend from the profile's `weekend` fact and never from code (C-2), the extension computed, hours and "close of business" in the office's hours (C-4)), its `trace` answered with it; undetermined with why when the calendar does not cover the period, when a start the ledger does not hold is named, or when the rule is not held with a primary source (`UNMEASURED` is not a basis, K1445; `jurisdictions` R44); and `basis` the rule's citation and its profile basis. An uncertain due date (two candidates) is offered with both, the group's own deadline taking the earliest (K1444 (i)). It is stored apart, labelled as `actions` R19's proposals are (machine work, a member's, or unstated), and never written into `clock[]`; a member states a clock entry by a revision of the action. Refusals in order: `NO_AUTHOR` (no stamped proposer); `NO_TARGET`; an absent `rule`; `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` (absent and invisible alike); `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `NO_SUCH_RULE` (no active profile states a deadline of that rule for the action's kind, naming both; T51, K4227, a field added: it also answers `deadlines: [{rule, label?, citation?}]`, the deadlines the active profiles state that apply to the action's kind, what this call would accept as `rule`, each with its `label` and `citation` where the profile gives them, `[]` when none applies; the code and the rest of the answer are unchanged; control: a valid rule's answer carries no `deadlines`). An absent `rule` is either its own refusal `NO_RULE` at that place, its row held and a test driving it, or has no code of its own and is answered `NO_SUCH_RULE` at that code's place; either meets this requirement (N246). (T45; N882; Actions D15; v0.2 rule 6; S1 §C 9, §C 19; reconciliation R7) A rule whose `starts` is `received` counts from the receipt `action-records` records (its R4, was `actions` R80, read through `actions.actionRead`; T47, N961; `kind: "receipt"`) when one is held, else from the group's first `sent` entry as now, the `trace` saying which ("receipt not recorded: counted from sending"); a rule whose `starts` is `served` counts from the recorded service on that party (`action-records` R4, `kind: "service"`), and reads undetermined with why when none is held. The deadlines read are `local-facts.governingView`'s (its R12), so a group's confirmed rule governs; with none it equals `combine`'s. A proposal carries `owed_by` from the deadline's `owed_by` (`jurisdictions` R75), `undetermined` where the profile does not say. Controls: the same rule with no receipt counts from `sent`; `served` with no service reads undetermined. (T51 fix round 2, C68; K4313) `NO_SUCH_RULE`'s answer lists `deadlines` in the office's own level first (the level the addressee states, else the view's counterparty), those the profile ties to another level last, the rest between.

**overdueClocks({after?, limit?, viewer})** (`queue-producers`' read)
- **R3** (monitoring R34's "its action's members are told"; for `queue-producers` R15) `overdueClocks({after?, limit?, viewer})` lists every clock entry of a visible action that is not `resolved` or `abandoned` whose status is `overdue`, or `pending` with a date before the local day of the instance clock (Terms; `actions` R12): action, entry position, date, basis, text, the action's project (the project of the first determination among the action's `rests_on` legs, read through `conformance.determinationRead`; null when it rests on none) and the member who created it (the author of its first manifest entry); at most 500 per page in (action id, entry position) order with `cursor` and `truncated` as R1's. Writes nothing. (N609, K1675) Each item carries `zone`, the zone whose local day it was judged on (Terms; `actions` R12's zone for that action at the call, K1649), so a reader dates and ages the item in the same zone this module counted in (`queue-producers` R15). (T45; N882; Actions D15; v0.2 rule 6) Each item also carries `owed_by` (R18), `due_at` and its zone where the entry's rule states a time of day (`civil-time` R9–R13; for `monitoring` R34), `steps` (R16) and, where R17 holds, `noticed_by`. An item is a question about a date, never a finding: no word of it says the body failed or violated anything. While the action states `reply_owed: false` (`actions` R82), no entry `owed_by: body` is listed. Control: the same entry with `reply_owed` absent is listed.

**Reminders a member asks for: `reminderSet`, `remindersFor`, `remindersDue`, `reminderAnswer`** (DEC-94 (1)–(4); K613 (1), K614, K615, K624 (3); `BIO_Action_v0_1.md` §4 rule 5)
- **R4** `reminderSet({target, entry, on, from?, author, viewer})` (`op=reminderset`): the member `author` asks to be reminded of the dated clock entry at position `entry` of the action `target` on the day `on` (`YYYY-MM-DD`); with `from`, changes their own reminder on that entry from the day `from` to `on`, or, with `on: null`, removes it. A reminder is a row in this module's own table (the action, the entry, the day, the member who set it and when), the member's own request: it is never a field or a revision of the action's document, which this op leaves unchanged. A member sets, changes or removes a reminder at any time, and only their own; one is set when a dated option is chosen (`action-plans` R29, whose start sets it through this service with the choosing member as `author`), from defaults the member sees and can change at that moment (nothing preselected unseen, DEC-77), or later. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_REMINDER` (`author` a machine or unstamped); `NO_SUCH_ACTION` (`actions.noSuchAction`, its R43: absent and invisible alike); `REMINDER_REFUSED`, its detail naming the arm: an `entry` naming no clock entry with a `YYYY-MM-DD` date, an `on` that is not a date (or `null` without `from`), a `from` naming no reminder of the author's on that entry, a day the author already holds on that entry, or a 51st reminder on the action. Otherwise it answers `{ok, target, entry, on, from: from | null}`. `remindersFor({action, viewer})` answers an action's reminders, each with its entry, its day, who set it and its state (`waiting`, `due` or `answered`); an absent or invisible action answers `actions.noSuchAction`. (N427, K711) That refusal is minted at one site: `reminderRefused(arm, detail, extra?)`, a module-level function exported as `actions`' `noSuchAction` is, answers `{ok: false, reason: "REMINDER_REFUSED", code, check, translation, detail, arm}` with its row, `extra` adding a caller's fields, so a later module judging a reminder request's shape before the action exists (`action-plans` R29) answers through it; it writes nothing and never throws.
- **R5** `remindersDue({nowMs, after?, limit?, viewer})` lists every reminder whose day has come at `nowMs` (its `on` at or before the local day of `nowMs`, Terms) and that is not answered, on an entry still `pending` of a visible action not `resolved` or `abandoned`: action, entry position, the entry's date, basis and text, the reminder's day, the member who set it and the action's project (as R3); at most 500 per page in (action id, entry position, day) order, with `cursor` and `truncated` as R1's. It is the one read `queue-producers` uses for its reminder (its R18). Writes nothing. (N609, K1675) Each item carries `zone`, the zone whose local day its reminder was judged due on (Terms, as R3's), so `queue-producers` R18 reads the same zone.
- **R6** `reminderAnswer({target, entry, on?, author, viewer})` (`op=reminderanswer`): the member who set a due reminder answers it, with `on` another reminder on that later day, or without it no further reminder (DEC-10, recorded in `NOTIFICATIONS.md` at D-125); a further reminder is one the member accepts, perhaps at the system's suggestion (DEC-94 (1)). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_REMINDER`; `NO_SUCH_ACTION` (`actions.noSuchAction`); `NO_SUCH_REMINDER` (no due, unanswered reminder on that entry set by the author); `REMINDER_REFUSED` (`on` not a calendar date, `civil-time.isCalendarDate`, or not after the local day of the instance clock). Otherwise it records the reminder answered in this module's table and, with `on`, adds the new one there as R4 would, the action's document unchanged, and answers `{ok, target, entry, answered, next: on | null}`.

**The calendar's confirmation** (K921; `local-facts`)
- **R10** (amends R2) A `business` count states the status (`local-facts.factStatus`, its R2) of every holiday year it reads, counting on the value that governs on this instance: computed, with "counted on an unconfirmed calendar (<source>, <date>)", when any is `unconfirmed`; computed, naming the correcting member and date, when one is `corrected`; `undetermined` with why when one is `disputed` or `absent`; computed as today when all are `confirmed`. A count for an action addressed to, or filed at, an office reads the holiday entries that apply to that office (`jurisdictions` R43) and those for all offices. A `calendar` count reads no holiday and states none.
- **R11** `calendarFactsRead({viewer})` lists, once each, the `local-facts` paths (its R6) a live deadline reads: for every visible action not `resolved` or `abandoned` whose kind has a profile deadline counted in business days (`jurisdictions` R26), the holiday years of its profile from the UTC year of the instance clock to the year of its latest pending clock entry (at least the next year), for the offices it is addressed to or filed at, and those offices' `hours`; each path with the actions that read it, each as `{action, project, created_by}`, its project and creator as R3 states them (K1000). Writes nothing; at most 500 actions read, `truncated` stated. (N689; K1847) It also lists, for every such action whose kind has a profile deadline naming `closures` or `observed.closures` (`jurisdictions` R26, R47), whether that deadline counts on the list or only rolls on it, the path of each entry of that named closure list for the same years and offices: `local-facts.factPath` with the entry's `list` (its R6), the path R12 reads it at, never the office calendar's. A path both a rule and its `observed` name is listed once, with the actions that read it. So every count R10 states unconfirmed because of a closure list names a path a member can confirm.

**factReader(localFacts, viewer)** (K998, N474; for `filings` R30)
- **R12** `factReader(localFacts, viewer)` answers a `factOf` in `computeDeadline`'s shape: given a holiday entry, it reads that entry's `local-facts` path (`factPath`, its R6) through `localFacts.factStatus` (its R2) for `viewer`, and answers as R10's count reads it (the status and why, the value that governs here, whether it is a correction and its `says`, the latest act's member and day, a lapsed confirmation's day). An entry naming no local fact, a read that throws or is refused, or one `local-facts` cannot answer is `absent` with why. It answers null when `localFacts` has no `factStatus`, and the count then states its calendar `not_read`. It is the reader R10's own count uses; it writes nothing and never throws. (K998; D6; K1038) (N562; K1563 (10), K1650) An entry of a named closure list (`jurisdictions` R47) is read at its own path, the one `factPath` names with the entry's `list` (`local-facts` R6), never the office calendar's path for the same year and offices, and answered by its own status as any holiday year is; so R10's count states a court's judicial holidays confirmed, corrected, disputed or unconfirmed apart from the office calendar's.

**Adopting a computed deadline; the calendar file; the group's own lateness** (T33-74)
- **R13** (K1440) `clockAdopt({target, proposal, why?, author, viewer})` (`op=clockadopt`) adopts a standing proposal of R2 (or one the author amends before adopting) as a clock entry of the action in one member act: it writes the entry, with its basis (R7) and the trace R2 answered, into the action's `clock[]` through the action's revision (`actions`' write, its R1–R3, R7), and records the proposal adopted with who and when. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_ADOPT_CLOCK` (a machine or unstamped author); `NO_SUCH_ACTION` (`actions.noSuchAction`); `NO_SUCH_CLOCK_PROPOSAL`; a proposal whose date is undetermined `CLOCK_PROPOSAL_UNDETERMINED` (a member states such an entry by their own revision, with its basis); then the revision's own refusals. From then the entry is tracked and told once like any other, whatever its basis kind (K1440, K1431).
- **R14** (K1451) `clocksIcs({actions, viewer})` (`op=clocksics`; a read) answers one iCalendar file (RFC 5545) for download, holding one `VEVENT` per pending dated clock entry of each named action the viewer may see: an all-day event on the entry's local day in the action's zone (`TZID` with its `VTIMEZONE`), its summary the entry's text, its description the basis (R7) and the action's id, and a stable `UID` per (action, entry position). It is a one-off file the member saves: no address is published to subscribe to, nothing is pushed and no outside channel is used (DEC-94 (3)). An action absent or invisible answers `actions.noSuchAction`; an entry with no date is left out and counted. It writes nothing.
- **R15** (D234; K1471) `lateness({from, to, project?, viewer})` (a read) answers an index over the group's own clocks only: for the clock entries of the actions the viewer may see whose basis kind is `commitment`, `dependency` or `window` (the group's own deadlines) and whose date falls in `from`–`to`, the count met on time, the count met late with the days late each (`civil-time.span`), the count still pending past their date, and the denominator, each a computed fact (`calc-grammar`'s exact arithmetic), with what it could not count named. It is never a finding about government: no entry of kind `rule` addressed to a body's duty is counted, no counterparty is named in the index, and nothing is written or raised as an item. (T45; N882; Actions D15; v0.2 rule 6) "The group's own deadlines" are the entries `owed_by: group` (R18), whatever their basis kind, with `dependency` and `window` entries as before; a `commitment` owed by a body, and a `stated` entry not marked `owed_by: group`, are never counted. This replaces "whose basis kind is `commitment`, `dependency` or `window`".
