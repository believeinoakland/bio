**Status** · New in T53 (K5564, plan "Before R1" (9); K617, K624): a new module, layer 11 after `screens-inquiry`, split from `screens-inquiry` by copy: it takes the Document page (the registry screen `document`; `bio-plane/src/screens-inquiry/document.mjs` and its states) with its requirement lines and its tests. R1–R47 are `screens-inquiry`'s `document` requirements moved here, renumbered in the order of their old ids (monotone), meaning unchanged, each carrying "(was screens-inquiry R<old>; T53, K5564)"; `screens-inquiry` marks each of its own "moved to screens-document R<k>" and never reuses the id. A bare `R<n>` in a moved line that names no module is a `screens-inquiry` id: the map gives its place here when the line moved. The unmet T53 lines are marked.

## Public

### Purpose

The Document page: one captured document with its passages (the registry screen `document`, Projects › <project> › Document), its copy, its readings and pages, its acts (cite, vouch, watch, retire, attest, thread, connect, contradict), the record of the copy and its places among the team's work, and, since T53, how it was read and interpreted and where it came from. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.
