# people — requirements

**Status** · In force, reviewed (K1505; T33-36; K1592); meaning: the canon ladders and Bob's rulings (K1443, K1452, K1453, K1455, K1465, K1471, K1473, K1483–K1493, K1504). Changed T36, T47, T48, T51.

**Size (P6).** Expected 1,800–2,300 lines (constructs-2 §4.1), under 4,000. M-P5 (person read at 50,000 lines) and M-P6 (false merges) run inside the job.

## Public

### Purpose

The people the record is about, held fully (K1452, K1455): identity claims linking person records, never merging them, and the derived cluster; dated person facts; reads gathering a person's positions, career, credentials, interests, statements and acts from their owners; staffing as of a date; members' declared ties; the protected source-to-person link; the machine's interest checks, in the hypothesis layer, shown only after a measured false-alarm rate. Every fact is cited, dated and graded; the module never concludes about a person, never says two people know each other, stores no judgment on a person (R29). Posts, memberships, education, credentials, interests and ties are `lines`; statements and acts, `events` participants; money given and received, money facts; `holderAt` is `lines'`.

### Provides

Terms. A **person** is an `entities` entity of kind `person`. A **viewer** is the control plane's stamp, read through `membership`; absent, it fails closed. A **validity** is `civil-time`'s `{valid: {from, to, precision, zone}, basis}`, read only through `civil-time.validAt`; a null bound means "not stated". A **citation** is a content extent `content` judges (`checkContentExtent`). A **grade** is the A–D family (`record-grammar`), A strongest. Every refusal is `{ok: false, reason, detail}`, plus `code`, `check` and `translation` when it has a catalogue row. Every write is stamped by the control plane (`by`); a caller's own author field is never read. A machine is named as one (`class:<cls>`, DEC-52).

**Identity claims: claimIdentity({a, b, kind, basis, evidence, project?, note, by}), withdrawIdentityClaim({claimId, reason, by}), identityOf({entityId, viewer})** (`op=identityclaim`, `op=identitywithdraw`, `op=identity`)
- **R1** `claimIdentity` records one claim `IDC-` (`record-grammar`'s opaque form) between two distinct registered entities `a` and `b` of one kind, a person or an organisation (`institution`, `body` or `movement`; I-32, DEC-384), of kind `same_as`, `not_same_as` or `unsure`. Refusals, in order: `NO_ENDS`; `SELF_CLAIM`; `NO_SUCH_ENTITY` (`entities.noSuchEntity`) naming the end; `NOT_A_PERSON` naming the end (any other kind, e.g. office, fund, source); `KIND_MISMATCH` naming both kinds when the ends' kinds differ (e.g. person and organisation, institution and body); `UNKNOWN_CLAIM_KIND` naming the three; `UNKNOWN_BASIS`; `NO_EVIDENCE` (basis not `testimony`, no evidence); `NO_NOTE`; R2's `IDENTITY_GRADE_UNEARNED`. Answers `{ok, claim_id, kind, grade, why, at}`.
- **R2** The grade is earned from the basis (K1488), never stated by the caller: `identifier` earns A only when one scheme identifier (`entities`'), valid at both records' cited dates, is held on both `a` and `b`; `corroborated_name` earns B only when the evidence names a cited fact (a `lines` row, e.g. same post, licence or filer number) valid at both documents' dates, through `civil-time.validAt`; `name` earns C; `testimony` D. A failed condition is refused `IDENTITY_GRADE_UNEARNED`, naming it. `why` says, in Civicsmith's words, "claimed the same person, grade B, because …", never "is the same person".
- **R3** A machine (`class:<cls>`) may record only an `identifier` claim from source-native data with identifiers at both ends (K1443 as extended by BOB, K1470); any other machine claim is refused `MACHINE_CLAIM_REFUSED`.
- **R4** A claim is never edited or deleted. `withdrawIdentityClaim` refuses `NO_REASON` and `NO_SUCH_CLAIM`, answers a repeat `already: true`, and marks the claim withdrawn with who, when and why; a withdrawn claim links nothing. Neither act merges, re-points or changes any `entities` row, resolution or alias.
- **R5** `identityOf` answers the person's identity cluster for the viewer: the persons joined to it by `same_as` claims the viewer may see, transitively within one component, each link with its claim, grade and why. State `linked`, or `undetermined` when a `not_same_as` claim joins two members of the component (naming it); `unsure` claims are listed and join nothing. An unregistered id answers `found: false`. At most 500 members and 1,000 claims, `truncated` by reading one past.
- **R6** The cluster is a derived cache (`record-core`'s convention): rebuilt in the transaction of every claim and withdrawal, equal to its rebuild, read fail-closed: a cache differing from its rebuild answers `undetermined` with the reason, never a stale cluster.

**samePersonCandidates({entityId, limit, viewer})** (`op=samepersoncandidates`)
- **R7** Answers other entities of the entity's own kind (persons for a person, organisations of the same kind for an organisation; I-32) sharing a name's fold (an alias, `entities`) or a scheme identifier with it, each explained field by field (names, identifiers, posts held (`lines`), life facts (R9)) as `agrees`, `differs` or `absent` with the rows compared. No probability, score or rank is computed or answered; ordered by entity id. `limit` 1–200 (default 50), with `truncated`. An entity of a kind no claim joins is refused `NOT_A_PERSON`. Writes nothing; always says candidates are not claims (D167).
- **R8** On the synthetic same-name fixture (Suggestions), of offered candidates R7 marks agreeing on every compared field, at most 0.5% are different people (M-P6; K1504). The job's test asserts it; the read is not published to `op-declarations` until it passes.

**Person facts: recordPersonFact({person, kind, value, valid, citation, by}), withdrawPersonFact({factId, reason, by}), expunge({id, ground, demandKind?, order?, reason, by})** (`op=personfact`, `op=personfactwithdraw`, `op=personexpunge`)
- **R9** `recordPersonFact` records one `PFA-` (opaque form): `kind` closed (`name`, `birth`, `death`, `locality`, `address`, `contact`); the value as the cited document states it; a validity; a citation; optional `question`. Refusals, in order: `NO_SUCH_ENTITY`, `NOT_A_PERSON`, `UNKNOWN_FACT_KIND`, `NO_VALUE`, `NO_CITATION` (or `content`'s extent refusal), `BAD_VALIDITY` (`civil-time`'s, naming the bound), `QUESTION_NOT_HELD` (no inquiry the record holds and the actor may see; absent and unseen alike). A citation may be an extent a find answered (`retrieval`'s `findIn`: a match's `capture_sha` and `extent`, a person or office the group follows among its kinds), taken as any other: being found changes no refusal, grade or sight (R31) (T35; N698, DEC-164 (4), (5)). `question` is an inquiry's bundle id (a held bundle with `object_type` `inquiry`, `record-core` R37), kept beside the fact, unchanged by any later act, answered with it by every read answering the fact (`personAt`, R14, among them) to a viewer who may see that bundle (`membership.viewerPredicate`), withheld as absent from any other; without one, `question: null`. (T51 fix round 2, C7; K4303; screens-investigate R53) **Organisations.** For kind `institution`, `body` or `movement`: `name`, `address`, `contact` as for a person; `birth`, `death`, `locality` refused `NOT_A_PERSON` naming the entity; any other kind refused as for a person.
- **R10** An `address` or `contact` fact is recorded only by a member's act: a machine write of either is refused `CONTACT_NOT_IMPORTED` (K1485 row 9). Such facts are held in a table with export class `never` (R33), answered only to a viewer who may see the citing capture, and carried by every read as `publishable: false`.
- **R11** A wrong fact is corrected forward: `withdrawPersonFact` refuses `NO_REASON` and `NO_SUCH_FACT`, keeps the fact, marks it withdrawn with who, when and why (DEC-19).
- **R12** `expunge` removes the value of a `PFA-`, `IDC-`, `MTI-` or source link (R21), leaving a tombstone `{id, ground, at, by}`, only on a K1493 ground: `unlawful`, `confidential`, `court_order` (naming the recorded order) or `lawful_demand` with a `demandKind` the active profiles list (`jurisdictions`). Refusals, in order: `membership`'s `notAnAdmin` (only an administrator expunges); `EXPUNGE_GROUND_REFUSED` (another ground, or `court_order` naming no order), naming the grounds and the correction act (R11); `DEMAND_KIND_UNLISTED`; `NO_REASON`; `PERSON_ITEM_NOT_HELD` (this module's code) for an id answering no person fact, claim or tie, or a `{source, person}` naming no link. The removal reaches this instance and every later export; no read answers the removed value. The person read (`person`) answers `removed: [{ground, at, by}]`, one per tombstone of that person's facts, never the value, to every viewer who may read the person (K5148; WALK-FINAL-05 R149, OVERLAY-6).

**Reads: personAt({entityId, at, viewer}), careerOf({entityId, viewer}), credentialsOf({entityId, at?, viewer}), interestsOf({entityId, at?, viewer}), statementsOf({entityId, from?, to?, viewer})** (`op=person`, `op=career`, `op=personcredentials`, `op=personinterests`, `op=personstatements`)
- **R13** Each read answers over the identity cluster as the viewer sees it (R5), stating the cluster and its state, each item naming the member it is held on. An `undetermined` cluster answers each item with its own member and joins nothing across the `not_same_as`.
- **R14** `personAt` refuses `NO_ENTITY` (`entities.noEntity`) and `NO_DATE`; it answers the names and life facts (R9) valid at `at`, the posts held at `at` (`lines`' `holds`, with capacity), the duties binding the person at `at` (`duties.dutiesOf`, as obligor, K1453), each item with validity, grade and citation; an item whose validity does not settle `at` is listed `undetermined` with the reason, never as current.
- **R15** `careerOf` answers every `holds` line of the cluster, inside and outside government, in validity order, each with capacity, title as written (the line's `title`, `lines`' Terms; `null`, "not stated", when none, never composed from office or capacity), bounds, grade and citation. `credentialsOf` answers the `educated_at` and `credentialed_by` lines, each `credentialed_by` with the issuer's scheme identifier as `entities` holds it (its R43), under a scheme the active profiles name for the issuer's kind (`jurisdictions` R52, R61); with no such scheme or none held, `null` with why, never a guess. `interestsOf` answers the `owns_interest_in` lines (a Form 700 interest is one, entered by a member from its cited schedule extent) and the money facts where the person is payee of kind `income` or `gift` or payer of kind `contribution` (`money`).
- **R16** `statementsOf` answers, for each cluster member, its statements (`events.statementsOf`) and acts (events `events.eventsFor` answers with it as `decider`, `signatory`, `implementer` or `author`, K1465), merged in `events`' order (`events.sequence`), three-valued: two events whose order the record does not settle are answered as such, never placed.
- **R17** Every read bounds each list at 500 items in its stated order, `truncated` per list by reading one past, and never answers a truncated list's count as whole.

**staffingAt({organisation, at, viewer}), registerRosterSource(module, source)** (`op=staffing`)
- **R18** `staffingAt` answers, for an organisation and its parts at `at` (`lines.structureAt`), the persons holding a `holds` line to it valid at `at`, and beside them each registered roster source's answer for that organisation and date, the level stated: "held as a table, read by <source>" or "held as a table, not read". Roster rows are never copied into lines.
- **R19** `registerRosterSource(module, source)` takes one source per module, refused through `membership`'s `listenerRefusal` (`LISTENER_MALFORMED`, `LISTENER_DECLARED`); sources are asked in the modules' total order. A source that throws is named in the answer as failed; the read still answers.

**Members' ties: declareTie({entity, kind, note, attribution, by}), withdrawTie({tieId, reason, by}), tiesOf({member, viewer}), tiesConcerning({entities, member, viewer})** (`op=membertie`, `op=membertiewithdraw`, `op=memberties` (`tiesOf`); `tiesConcerning` in-process, no op)
- **R20** A member declares only their own tie (`MTI-`), to a registered entity, of kind `employer`, `relative`, `business` or `other`, with a note and the attribution level they choose for its disclosure (K1490). `by` is the member (the control plane's stamp); no field names another member; a machine is refused. A tie is seen only by its member and administrators: any other viewer's read answers exactly as for no tie. `tiesConcerning` answers, for `case-disclosures`, the member's ties to any of the given entities, each with its attribution level.

**The protected source link: linkSourceToPerson({source, person, evidence, sight, by}), sourceLinksOf({source | person, viewer})** (`op=sourcepersonlink`, `op=sourcepersonlinks`)
- **R21** It records that a source (`sources`) is a registered person, with evidence and a non-empty sight list of members (`NO_SIGHT_LIST`). Only the listed members read it; every other viewer, every other read of this module and every export answer exactly as if no link were held. `personAt` and every other person read never state that someone is a source (DEC-78 item 5; K1484 row 18). The read by source takes `source_id` (`source` is a door stamp, deleted from every query); a read naming neither `source_id` nor `person` is refused, never answered empty (K5148; WALK-FINAL-04B R40).

**sourceLinkSight(person)** (internal; T34, N600)
- **R34** A synchronous read for other modules' code, never an op nor answered to a viewer: not in `peopleOps` (R27), takes no viewer, and no module answers its result, or anything revealing a link is held, to a viewer R21 does not admit. It answers `null` when no source link to that person is held, else the members every held link to that person admits (the intersection of their sight lists), so `consequences` R16 tells "no link" (`null`) from "a link not admitting this viewer" (a list without them). Writes nothing, never throws; an unregistered or malformed id answers `null`.

**Interest checks: defineCheck({name, condition, denominator, project?, by}), switchCheck({check, project, on, by}), evaluateChecks({budgetMs}), checkResults({project?, check?, viewer}), recordCheckGate({check, version, goldSet, falseAlarmRate, by}), onCheckResult(module, fn), onChecksChanged(module, fn)** (`op=interestcheckdefine`, `op=interestcheckswitch`, `op=interestchecks`, `op=interestcheckgate`)
- **R22** A check (`CHK-`) is data-defined: its condition names line kinds, event roles or money-fact kinds at each end and a hop bound of at most two through `related_to` or `associate_of`; its denominator names the set it counts against. The shipped checks (the machine's own, K1491) include a revolving-door check (a `holds` in government followed, within the condition's span, by a `holds` at an organisation the earlier office oversees or contracts with). A member may add a check; one naming a hypothesis id (`record-grammar.isHypothesisId`) is refused `HYPOTHESIS_NOT_A_FACT` (K1467). A member of a project may switch any check off in it. A change is a new version; earlier versions are kept.
- **R23** `evaluateChecks` evaluates every switched-on check over the held record within its given time budget, writing each match, with its cited derivation (every row it rests on) and denominator, to the checks' own result table, never to a person's or entity's row. Answers `{evaluated, remaining}`, `remaining` true when the budget ran out; resumes where it stopped. A computation, never a model run.
- **R24** `checkResults` answers a check version's results only once its gate is recorded and its rate is at most 20% (K1504, M-C8), as `money-checks` gates its detectors: `recordCheckGate` (an administrator; `NO_GOLD_SET`; a rate outside 0–1 refused) records one version's gold set and measured false-alarm rate. Until then it answers `{gated: true, reason}` and no result. Each answered result is labelled the machine's, `layer: "hypothesis"`, "Noticed", with derivation and denominator. Wherever it reaches a member (`op=interestchecks`; what R25's listeners are told for `notice-producers`) it carries `mark: "Hint · machine work"` (DEC-131's words, exactly) beside `label: "Noticed"`, and its member-facing `detail` begins with that mark and calls it a "hint", never a "signal": "Hint · machine work. A check noticed this hint, a pattern in held facts, worth a look. It is not a finding and says nothing about anyone." `label`, `layer`, `by` and every key stay as they are (T35; N694, K1863).
- **R25** A result whose derivation rests on any row the viewer may not see is withheld whole and not counted (K1489). `onCheckResult` registrations (R19's rule) are told of each new gated-open result, once, for `notice-producers`.
- **R35** (T34, N605) `onChecksChanged(module, fn)`: one registration per module, refused through `membership`'s `listenerRefusal`. After a check is defined or versioned, or switched on or off in a project (R22), `fn({check, project})` is called once, after the act's transaction, with the check's id and the project (null when none), so `scheduler` re-arms its wake for R23's consumer (`scheduler` R9). A throwing `fn` never undoes the act; the notice writes nothing (`following` R19's pattern, K1666).
