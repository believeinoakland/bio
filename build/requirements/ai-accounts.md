## Public

### Purpose

Holds the AI accounts the assistant's use is paid by, sealed at rest, and chooses the one an act uses: a member's own (an API key, or her Claude sign-in's token), a project's, and the group's API key; each account's switch for every kind of use; the material limits that keep the group's or a project's material away from AI; the notices and the disclosure owed before an account pays; and each account's change history. Claude is today's only provider. The subscription token is each member's own, by Bob's choice (K1547, K1755; `build/terms/anthropic.md`, Bob's choices), not by a rule of Anthropic's. A project may hold one AI account: an Anthropic API key, or, while it has one member, that member's own sign-in (D34; K2353; R10); and the group one Anthropic API key (K1755, R5–R8). Who the members are is `membership`'s; what a person signs in with, and the grant an ask reads under, are `credentials`'. (T53; K5457) From T53 an account is an owner's plan at a provider, reached by one of five means (`gateway`, `sealed`, `reference`, `issued`, `none`) with an ordered allow-list of entries of `ai-catalogue`'s signed list; the older kinds above (a reference, a sign-in, the group's key, a project's account) are accounts of that form (R40, R59), the plan's scope, the checks and Gate 1 decide what is offered (R42, R52–R55), and a list of an owner's accounts, with the hop along it, decides which serves (R41, R50).
