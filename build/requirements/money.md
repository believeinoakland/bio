# money — requirements

**Status** · In force, a new product module, reviewed (K1505; T33-33; banner cleared K1580); its meaning the canon ladders' and Bob's rulings (K1443, K1457, K1463, K1464, K1468, K1470, K1471, K1486, K1489). Amount checks, detectors and `progressions` R32 are `money-checks`'; restrictions, thresholds, transfer authority and `pay` duties are `duties`' (K1504). R39, R40, R41 marked not yet met (T53), every other requirement met (MONEY #2, K1974).

**Size (P6).** About 2,600–3,100 lines with L2–L3, less `money-checks`' part; under 4,000.

## Public

### Purpose

Money as stated amounts. Each **money fact** is one source's reading of one amount, with its kind, phase or stage, basis of accounting, accounting period, parties, funds and two grades; never an "amount" without those axes, a world truth the machine asserts, or a total. Money refuses sums across axes by name, follows flows through member-declared money trails, reconciles two readings by naming the dimension that differs, sets committed against paid, and walks an amount's chain of authority. The occurrence an amount quantifies is an event; a promise to pay is a duty citing the fact; arithmetic is a calculation. The group's own money is followed like anyone's and never marked as the group's (K1463).

### Provides

Terms.
- A **money fact** `MNY-` is `{fact_id, amount, as_read, currency, sign, precision, kind, phase, stage?, adjusts?, basis, period, from, to, codes, balance_class?, buys?, concerns, source, grade: {reading, parties}, by, at}`.
- `amount` is an exact decimal (`calc-grammar`), never a float; `as_read` the figure exactly as printed; `sign` explicit; `precision` `exact`, `rounded`, `approximate` or `range` (with low and high).
- **Kind** (closed): revenue, expenditure, transfer, allocation, payment, contribution, gift, income, behested, settlement, debt, balance, fee charged, other.
- **Phase** (closed): proposed, adopted, adjusted, actual. **Stage** (closed, `actual` only): encumbered, incurred, paid; assessed, collected. An **adjustment** is a signed fact naming the fact it adjusts (`adjusts`).
- **Basis** (closed): budgetary, cash, modified accrual, accrual, undetermined.
- `period` is the accounting period only, a date-time range with precision and zone; a fiscal key (such as a fiscal year) is mapped by `civil-time` from the profile's `fiscal_year` for the body. When an occurrence happened is its event's, never this field.
- A **party** (`from`, `to`) is `{entity?, fund?, account?, as_written}`; payer and payee live here, never on an event. `codes` are `{scheme, code}` pairs in the profile's classification schemes.
- `balance_class` (kind `balance` only) is a class of the family the fund's type names (governmental funds: GASB 54's classes; proprietary and fiduciary: net position components), the families and a fund's type (`setFundType`) held by money. `buys?` is `{quantity?, unit?, what?}`, `quantity` an exact decimal; another shape is `UNKNOWN_FIELD`.
- `concerns` names events, entities of kind contract, fund, program or proceeding, or a line; never a duty (the duty holds the link).
- `source` is exactly one: a content extent (page region, cell, range, table, or a canonical table's row as a derived view of captured bytes) or another money fact; never a `CALC-`.
- `grade.reading` is the source's grade, never raised by a machine reading; `grade.parties` each party's entity resolution grade; answered apart.
- A **money set** `MSR-` is `{set_id, purpose: trail | attribution, label, concerns?, inclusions, exclusions}`; `concerns?` is one id or none: an id a fact's `concerns` may name or, for a trail, a project's id (R28; K4369).
- The **viewer**, the refusal shape and `by` are as in `entities` (the control plane's stamps; DEC-52).

**recordFact({…fields, by})** — one append site for every write
- **R1** Refusals, in order: `NO_AMOUNT`; `BAD_AMOUNT` (not an exact decimal by `calc-grammar`'s figure parser); `NO_AS_READ`; `NO_CURRENCY`; `NO_SIGN` (not `+` or `-`); `UNKNOWN_PRECISION` (a `range` without low and high is `BAD_RANGE`); `UNKNOWN_MONEY_KIND`, `UNKNOWN_PHASE`, `UNKNOWN_BASIS` (each naming its closed list); `NO_STAGE` (phase `actual` without a stage), `STAGE_NOT_ACTUAL` (a stage on another phase), `UNKNOWN_STAGE`; `NO_PERIOD`, `MONEY_BAD_PERIOD` (R29: any other period fault, a fiscal key the profile cannot map among them); `UNKNOWN_CODE_SCHEME`; `BALANCE_CLASS_NOT_IN_FAMILY`; each party's `NO_SUCH_ENTITY` and `NOT_A_FUND` (a `fund` not registered as a fund); `concerns`' `CONCERNS_UNKNOWN` (not a list of ids of the kinds the Terms name), `NO_SUCH_EVENT`, `NO_SUCH_ENTITY`, `CONCERNS_KIND` (an entity of another kind), `NO_SUCH_LINE` (a line the writer cannot see); `CONCERNS_DUTY`; then R2's (`SOURCE_EXTENT_UNREADABLE`, an extent `content`'s grammar cannot read, among its `SOURCE_NOT_HELD` checks); then `NO_METHOD` (no method of R3's list, or a machine's that is no machine reading), `TYPED_NOT_MEMBER` (a typed transcription not by a member); then R4's. Otherwise it allocates `MNY-<year>-<tail>` (`record-core`'s opaque allocator) and writes the fact.
- **R2** Source refusals, in order: `NO_SOURCE`; `TWO_SOURCES`; `SOURCE_IS_CALCULATION` (a `CALC-` id; K1468, R-3 H-7); `SOURCE_NOT_HELD` (an extent of no held capture the viewer can see, or an absent money fact); `ADJUSTS_NOT_HELD` (`adjusts` names no fact) (T35; N698, DEC-164 (4), (5)). An extent a find answered (`retrieval.findIn`: a match's `capture_sha` and `extent`, a money figure among its kinds) is the one source like any extent of a held capture: same refusals, same grade (R3). A find's match is never itself a source; a table's amount column is declared and counted through a calculation, never read in here row by row (K1468). Optional `question`, an inquiry's bundle id (`object_type` `inquiry`, `record-core` R37): after the source refusals, `QUESTION_NOT_HELD` when it names no question the record holds that the actor may see (absent and unseen alike). It is kept beside the fact, unchanged by any later act (adjustment, withdrawal), and answered by `readFact` (R8) and `moneyOf` (R9) to a viewer who may see that bundle (`membership.viewerPredicate`), as absent to any other; none recorded answers `question: null`.
- **R3** A figure read by a machine (OCR, a reader, a table binding) carries that method; its `grade.reading` is never above its source's. A typed transcription is the member's act and says so.
- **R4** The machine records a fact only from a table binding a member adopted, each party identified by a scheme identifier (K1443 as BOB extended it); a machine-stamped write lacking either is refused `MACHINE_NEEDS_IDENTIFIERS`. Rows become facts only at a member's request (K1468), through the caller (`calculations`' ingest writer); this module never sweeps a table.
- **R5** Facts projected from a capture's reading ride `promotion.registerStep`, through R1's checks.
- **R6** No field, flag or read marks a fact as the group's own, and none is derived (K1463); such a field is refused `UNKNOWN_FIELD`, as is every field outside the row's shape.

**withdrawFact({factId, reason, by}), readFact({factId, viewer}), moneyOf({entity, period?, kinds?, phases?, limit, viewer}), checkConstant({role, value}), factBySource({table, row, binding, viewer?})**
- **R7** `withdrawFact` refuses `NO_REASON`, `NO_SUCH_FACT`; a repeat answers `already: true`. A withdrawn fact remains, shown withdrawn with who, when and why; R9–R16 never count it. Nothing is deleted; a fact's adjustments and the facts citing it as source are answered beside it. (K5148; WALK-FINAL-07 R62) Money publishes `checkConstant({role, value})` (role `period`, `kind`, `phase`, `stage`, `basis` or `currency`; else `UNKNOWN_ROLE`): `{ok: true, role}` or money's own refusal for that value with `role` beside it (`MONEY_BAD_PERIOD` for any period fault, a missing one included, and the others), writing nothing; and `factBySource({table, row, binding, viewer?})`: the live fact written from that source row (`found: true, fact_id`) or `found: false` (also for a fact `viewer` may not see), `NO_SOURCE` without table, row or binding, by an indexed read.
- **R8** `readFact` answers `NO_FACT` for an empty id, `found: false` for an absent one, else every field, both grades, its source with citation, its adjustments and its withdrawal.
- **R9** `moneyOf` answers the facts in which the entity is a party (as entity or fund) or which concern it (`entity` any id a fact's `concerns` may name, `EVT-` and `LIN-` among them, K1563), filtered by period (`civil-time` overlap, undetermined ones listed apart), kinds and phases, ordered by period then id, bounded 1–500 (default 100) with `truncated`. Facts, never a total.

**summable({factIds})** — the summation rule
- **R10** Answers `{ok: true, interfund}` when every fact shares one kind, one phase (and, for actuals, one stage), one basis, one currency and one period; else the refusal naming the first differing dimension and two facts differing on it, with `calc-grammar`'s codes (its R13): `SUM_MIXED_KIND`, `SUM_MIXED_STAGE` (phase or stage), `SUM_MIXED_BASIS`, `SUM_MIXED_CURRENCY`, `SUM_MIXED_PERIOD`. `interfund` lists each transfer between two funds, flagged so a city-wide sum can net it. No total is computed or stored: a total is a `CALC-`.

**reconcile({a, b})** (stage 2b)
- **R11** Compares two facts (refused as `summable`: `NO_FACTS`, `NO_SUCH_FACT`, `FACT_WITHDRAWN`): `{a, b, consistent, amounts, differs}`. `amounts` is `equal`, within the coarser precision ("about $2 million" and "$2,097,431"), or `differ`; `differs` holds `{dimension, a, b}` for each of basis, period, kind, phase or stage, currency, parties and amount that differs, amounts differing only when they do not agree within the coarser precision; `consistent` is true exactly when `differs` is empty. Never "contradiction" or a verdict.

**createSet({purpose, label, concerns?, by}), include({setId, factId, reason, by}), exclude({setId, factId, reason, by}), proposeInclusion({setId, factId, method, by}), readSet({setId, viewer})** (stage 2b)
- **R12** `createSet` refuses `UNKNOWN_PURPOSE` (`trail` or `attribution`), `NO_LABEL`, and for `attribution` `NO_CONTRACT` (it concerns one contract entity); it allocates `MSR-<year>-NNNN`. `include` and `exclude` are a member's act only (`MEMBER_ACT_ONLY`), each refuses `NO_REASON` and an absent set or fact and is recorded with who, when and why; the latest act on a fact governs, every earlier one kept. (K5148; WALK-FINAL-07 OVERLAY 2/21) `readSet` answers a set whose `concerns` names a bundle the viewer may not see exactly as an absent set (`{ok, found: false, set_id}`), as R28 fences `moneySets`.
- **R13** `proposeInclusion` holds a machine's proposal apart, labelled the machine's with its method; it includes nothing until a member's `include` adopts it. `readSet` answers inclusions, exclusions and open proposals, each with reason and author.

**committedAgainstPaid({contract, at?, viewer})** (stage 2b)
- **R14** Refuses `NOT_A_CONTRACT`. Answers, under R10's rule, the committed facts (`actual`/`encumbered` concerning the contract's award event, and change orders: signed `actual`/`encumbered` facts concerning a change-order event that `amends` the award, per `events`) and the paid facts (`actual`/`paid` concerning the contract, and those in its attribution set, each labelled "attributed by a member, reason …"), each side's sum computed through `calc-grammar`, not stored, and the difference as a figure. A sum R10 refuses is answered as that refusal. Never "overpaid" or "unauthorised".

**authorityChain({factId, viewer})** (stage 2b)
- **R15** Answers, for the event the fact concerns, the chain of `authorises` relations held in `events` toward it (appropriation, award, resolution), each hop with citation and grades, within `connection-grammar`'s bounds (`truncated` and `undetermined` on exhaustion). With no authorising event held it says so, never "unauthorised".

**neighbours({node, kinds, at, page})** (the connection owner)
- **R16** The module registers once through `connection-grammar.registerOwner`, owner `money`. `neighbours` presents each fact as one edge from payer to payee (entity or fund) with its kind, fund, period, stage, evidence and both grades, as of `at`, from the indexes `(party, period)`, `(fund, period)` and `(concerns)`, paged by `connection-grammar`'s bounds; it passes `connection-grammar.ownerConformance`. Flow walks are `explore`'s presets.

**onFactChanged(module, fn)** (the change notice; TAD §12's `calculation_input_changed`, raised by `calculations`)
- **R23** A later module registers once; a malformed registration, or a second by the same module, is refused through `membership.listenerRefusal` (`LISTENER_MALFORMED`, `LISTENER_DECLARED`); listeners run in `MODULE_ORDER` (`events.onWhenChanged`'s pattern). Inside the transaction of every write that adds a fact, withdraws one (R7), adds an adjustment naming one, or changes a money set's inclusions (R12), each listener runs once per fact touched with `{factId, change: added | withdrawn | adjusted | set_changed, setId?}`. A listener that throws fails the write; nothing is written.

**kinds(), phases(), stages(), bases(), precisions()**
- **R17** Answer the closed lists, frozen, for `affordances` to publish with the members' words (K1486).

**The ops map; the read contract**
- **R18** The module publishes `moneyOps(money, url, body)`, one route arm per act and read above, on `entities` R40's pattern; every write goes through R1's one append site, stamped by the control plane.
- **R19** The tables `money_facts` (`fact_id`, `amount`, `currency`, `sign`, `kind`, `phase`, `stage`, `basis`, `period_from`, `period_to`, `from_entity`, `from_fund`, `to_entity`, `to_fund`, `source_capture_sha`: the capture holding the source extent, or the one a source table was read from, else null), `money_withdrawals` (`fact_id`) and `money_concerns` (`fact_id`, `concerns`: one row per id concerned) are a stated read contract (K1563) on `record-core` R37's terms (`money-checks`, `calculations`, `query-language`); every write stays this module's.

**recordedBy({captureSha, extent?, limit?, viewer})** (T36; N715)
- **R24** Answers in `events` R49's shape over the money facts whose `source` is an extent of the capture (R2): `kind: "money_fact"`, `field: "source"`, `by` the fact's, `withdrawn` per R7. A fact sourced from another fact cites no extent and is no item. Sight is R21's. (N715; DEC-164 (4); K1941, K2063)

**The money trail** (T36; N728; U108, U110–U114)
- **R25** (amends R13; N728; U108, U110–U114; K1988, K1430) `readSet` of a `trail` set answers beside each included fact (and each open proposal's fact) a trail row; it writes nothing:
  - `from`, `to`: the party as the source states it with its grade (`grade.parties`), or `{stated: false, says: "not stated in this source"}` when the fact holds none; never filled from another fact, an event's participants or an entity's role.
  - `moved`, from the events the fact `concerns` (`events.readEvent`). Phase `actual`: `{state: "dated", when, event, attestation}`, the concerned event's `when` at its own precision and zone with the attestation dating it (of several concerned events, the one of kind `payment` or `transfer` when exactly one is); else `{state: "undetermined", why}` (no event, its `when` null or undetermined, or several not singling one out, named); never placed by the fact's `period`. Other phases: `{state: "did_not_move", phase, when}`, `when` the concerned event's (such as an adoption) where one dates it, else null.
  - `compared`, for phase `proposed`, `adopted` or `adjusted`: each included `actual` fact sharing a `concerns` id, with `reconcile`'s answer (R11), never summed or merged; `budget_only: true` when none.
  - its adjustments (R7) beside it, never netted.
  - `gaps`: each of `from`, `to`, `moved` and `basis` answered not stated or undetermined, so a view can offer acts that add evidence.
  - A fact or event recorded later changes what the trail answers beside a fact, never that fact's fields or figure.

**A government's own fiscal year (T53; A2, DEC-458)**

- **R39** (T53; N1257; DEC-458; K5517; amends money R29, R34) **A fiscal key is mapped by the government's own year, from the place's records or a stated year.** A period `{fiscal, body}` is resolved over the governing view (`jurisdictions` R134, `civil-time` R31): the place's records first (a government a Civicsmith layer covers keeps the records' year), else the team's stated year, an entry of the one team layer (jurisdictions R115, origin team; local-facts R31), which maps a key exactly as a record's does. The fact keeps the date range, not the year's origin. R29's and R34's refusals stand, plus: a government with no year in the records and none stated is refused `MONEY_BAD_PERIOD`, the sentence naming the government and saying no fiscal year is held for it and none has been stated by a member (words by key, the design's; until the row exists the job REPORTs it, as R34). No fiscal refusal, detail, key or var ever holds `*` or another stand-in for a government. One government's stated year never maps another's key (refused, naming the other); a period with no body is never resolved from a stated year (R34's refusal).

- **R40** (T53; N1257; DEC-458; amends money R34) **The fiscal-year view is offered only for a government whose year is known, and says why where it is not** (DEC-458; never printing '*'). Money answers the governments whose year is known, each with its year (`start`, `named_by`, `label`) and origin (the place's records, or stated by a member, with member, date and `how`); a records `*` entry is answered once as every government, never as a government named `*`.
