## Public

### Purpose

Members' confirmation of the profile's local calendar and offices on this instance. A profile's holidays, office hours and time zone (`jurisdictions` R33, R41–R44) are researched facts; this module records each member's confirmation, correction or dispute of one, answers each fact's status and the value that governs here, lapses a confirmation at its horizon, and lists the facts a live deadline reads that are due. A member confirms; a machine never does.

### Provides

Terms. A **fact** is one of: a `holidays` entry's year (keyed by its year, its `offices` and, for an entry of a named closure list, its `list`; `jurisdictions` R33, R43, R47), an office's `hours` (a counterparty's or a venue's, R42) or the `time_zone` (R41), in one held profile; facts are per jurisdiction profile, never per template. Its **path** is `factPath(...)`'s string (R6). An **act** is `confirm`, `correct` or `dispute` (`LOCAL_FACT_ACTS`). A **status** is `confirmed`, `unconfirmed`, `corrected`, `disputed` or `absent` (`LOCAL_FACT_STATUSES`). A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, code, check, translation, ...}`. `by` and `viewer` are the control plane's stamps.

**factConfirm({path, act, how, value?, source?, by, viewer})** (`op=factconfirm`) → `{ok, path, act, at}`
- **R1** Records a member's `confirm`, `correct` or `dispute` of the fact at `path`. `how` is 1–500 characters saying how they checked (the official page at an address, a call to the office, a visit) (`FACT_HOW_REFUSED`). `correct` needs `value`, valid as the profile's own field (`jurisdictions.validate` of that field), and `source`, 1–500 characters (`FACT_VALUE_REFUSED`). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_CONFIRM` (a machine or unstamped `by`); `NO_SUCH_FACT` (a path R6 does not name in an active profile); `FACT_ACT_REFUSED` (an act outside `LOCAL_FACT_ACTS`); `FACT_HOW_REFUSED`; `FACT_VALUE_REFUSED`.

**factStatus({path?, viewer})** (`op=factstatus`)
- **R2** For one `path`, answers its status with the latest act's member, date and `how`, the profile's value with its `status` and `basis` (`jurisdictions` R44), and the value that governs on this instance: the latest correction's, marked "corrected locally by <member>, <date>", else the profile's (Q6). `confirmed` while a confirmation is the latest act and within its horizon (R3); a lapsed confirmation reads `unconfirmed`, naming it; `disputed` while a dispute is the latest act; `absent` when the active profiles hold no such fact or withhold it as a conflict (`jurisdictions` R15). Without `path`, it lists every fact of the active profiles with its status, corrections and disputes first, so a member can report them for a profile fix; the instance transmits nothing. `NO_SUCH_FACT` for a path R6 does not name.
- **R3** Horizons, per profile fact: a holiday year's confirmation lasts until that year ends, and a year not confirmed by 1 November of the year before is due; an office's hours, and the time zone, lapse 183 days after confirmation (Q7). A correction governs until a later act, and is itself confirmed by a later `confirm`. Every day here is a local day of the fact's profile's time zone (`civil-time.localDay` of the instant, `jurisdictions` R41), never the UTC day: a year ends, 1 November falls and the 183rd day lapses at that zone's local midnight; a profile with no time zone answers the fact's horizon undetermined, saying so, never a UTC fallback (§7 item 3; K1444 (iii)). (T45; N858) An office's own time zone lapses as the profile's does (183 days); a day for an office fact is that office's local day when the profile holds its own zone.

**factsDue({paths?, viewer})** (`op=factsdue`; `queue-producers` R21's read)
- **R4** Lists, once each, every fact that is `unconfirmed`, lapsed, due (R3) or `disputed`: with `paths`, only those of them (the facts a live deadline reads, as `action-clocks.calendarFactsRead` answers them, its R11, which the caller passes; this module, first in layer 9, reads no action, P4); without, every such fact of the active profiles. Each with its status and why it is due. A path R6 does not name is left out and listed in `unknown`. Writes nothing.

**The paths and the vocabularies**
- **R6** `factPath({profile, fact, year?, offices?, list?, office?})` answers the one path of a fact: `fact` one of `holidays` (with `year`, the entry's `offices`, absent for all offices, and the entry's closure `list` (`jurisdictions` R47), absent for the office calendar), `hours` (with `office`: a counterparty's `role` and `body`, or a venue's `kind`) and `time_zone`; the same fact always gives the same path, and `parseFactPath(path)` answers the parts or null. A named list's entry has a path of its own that names the list (`list=<name>`), never the path of the office calendar's entry for the same year and offices, and it is a fact as any holiday year is (R1–R4, R3's horizons): a court's judicial holidays are confirmed, corrected and disputed by year apart from the office calendar's. Both pure; neither throws. (T33-28; A ORG bridge, ladders §5.4) `governingPath({profile, fact, office, entity?, at, viewer})` answers the path whose fact governs an office: the office's own when the profile holds one; else, when `entity` (the office's registry entity) is given, the fact of the nearest entity it is `part_of` at `at` (`lines.structureAt`, followed upward one line at a time, at most `connection-grammar`'s default depth) that the profile holds, naming each line followed; else the profile's `offices` grouping as before (fallback), saying so. A `part_of` line undetermined at `at` stops the walk there and answers the fallback with why. It writes nothing and never throws. (T45; N858; Actions D8; v0.2 rules 6, 11) `factPath` takes `fact: "time_zone"` with `office` (a counterparty's `role` and `body`, or a venue's `kind`) for an office's own zone (`jurisdictions` R74), a path apart from the profile's zone. `governingPath` for `time_zone` answers the office's own path when the profile holds one, else the profile's, saying so.
- **R7** `LOCAL_FACT_ACTS`, `LOCAL_FACT_STATUSES` and the horizons of R3 are exported, frozen, for `action-clocks`, `filings` and `affordances` (its R30).
- **R21** (T51; D-B1; K5264, K5282) The team's own time zone is a fact the team states whether or not a place profile is installed: path `@team/time_zone` (`TEAM_ZONE_PATH`, exported; no profile id can spell it). `factConfirm` accepts a `correct` of it whose `value` is an IANA name valid as a profile's `time_zone` (`jurisdictions` R41; else `FACT_VALUE_REFUSED`), with `source` and `how`, with no profile held; `confirm` and `dispute` need a zone that governs (else `NO_SUCH_FACT`, which R1 also answers for this path). It governs from its latest correction, else the zone the active profiles agree on; with none, `factStatus` of the path answers `absent`. Horizon: 183 days (R3). It is read by its path and is not among the facts `factStatus` and `factsDue` list without a path. Every module that needs the team's zone reads this path's governing value, or the equal value R22 names.
- **R22** (T51 fix round 10; K5292; walk E N2, AP2; amends R21) Each correction of `@team/time_zone` also sets `record-core`'s setting `team_time_zone` to its value, in the same transaction; a copy holding a correction and no such setting has it set from its latest correction at this module's first read or write after this change. R21's governing value is unchanged; a module that reads settings alone, or comes earlier in the order than this one, takes the team's zone as `jurisdictions.governingZone(getSetting("jurisdiction_profiles"), getSetting("team_time_zone"))` (its R98), which answers the same value.

**Member-drafted jurisdiction rules** (T45; N858; Actions D8; DESIGN §10; v0.2 rules 10, 11: `jurisdictions` is pure and holds no store, its R17, so a group's own rule lives here)
- **R10** (T45; N858; Actions D8; v0.2 rule 11) `ruleDraft({section, entry, source, reason, by, viewer})` (`op=ruledraft`): a member drafts a jurisdiction rule from its primary source. `section` is `deadlines` (`jurisdictions` R26) or `legal_facts` (`jurisdictions` R79); `entry` is valid as that section's entry (`jurisdictions.validate` over a profile holding it alone, its `basis` and `status` supplied here); `source` a held capture extent of the primary source (`{captureSha, extent}`). Refusals in order, each writing nothing: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DRAFT_RULE` (a machine or unstamped `by`); `RULE_SECTION_UNKNOWN`; `RULE_ENTRY_INVALID` with `validate`'s errors; `RULE_NO_SOURCE` (`content`'s extent refusals, an unseen capture answered as absent); `RULE_IN_PROFILE` when the active profiles' view already holds an entry under the same key (a deadline's `rule` and `applies_to`; a legal fact's `key`), naming it (the route is R1's dispute and R13's offer); `RULE_NO_REASON`. Answers `{ok, rule, state: "drafted"}`.
- **R11** (T45; N858; Actions D8; DESIGN §10; v0.2 rules 10, 11) `ruleConfirm({rule, statement?, by, viewer})` (`op=ruleconfirm`) by a member other than its drafter. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_CONFIRM`; `NO_SUCH_GROUP_RULE`; `RULE_SELF_CONFIRM`; `RULE_NOT_DRAFTED` (confirmed or withdrawn); when the rule's tier is 2, 3 or undetermined (the tier of the `action_kinds` kind its `applies_to` names in the active view; a `claim` rule, or a kind with no tier, read as undetermined), `RULE_NEEDS_QUALIFICATION` without `statement` (1–500 characters, the confirming member's professional qualification in her words). For a Tier 1 rule no statement is asked and one given is not recorded. The statement is recorded on that confirmation only, as an attestation of that one act: it is answered only with the rule (R12's read of that rule), never by a read keyed by member, never counted, never in any view or offer (R13), and no other module reads it. The rule applies in the group from the confirmation.
- **R12** (T45; N858; v0.2 rule 11) `groupRules({section?, viewer})` answers the group's rules with their state, source, drafter, confirmer, dates and (confirmed with one) the statement, to a viewer naming a member. `governingView()` answers `jurisdictions.combine`'s view of the active profiles with each confirmed, unwithdrawn group rule added to its section, tagged `profile: "group"` and a `basis` naming the rule's id and confirmation date (no statement, no handle). With no group rule it equals `combine`'s view. Writes nothing; never throws. `action-clocks`, `filings`, `actions` and `duties` (its R5) read this view for those sections in place of `combine`, each in its own job.
- **R13** (T45; N858; v0.2 rule 11) `ruleWithdraw({rule, reason, by, viewer})` by any member (`NO_SUCH_GROUP_RULE`; `RULE_NO_REASON`; a repeat answers `already: true`), kept, never deleted, the rule leaving `governingView` from then on. `ruleOffer({rule, by, viewer})` answers, for a confirmed rule, its entry, primary source and `confirmed: {at, with_statement: true|false}` as canonical bytes with their digest, for a member to carry to the shared profile's maintainers; it never carries the statement's words or a handle, and the instance transmits nothing (R2's report rule). An offer of a rule not confirmed (drafted or withdrawn) is refused `RULE_NOT_CONFIRMED`.
