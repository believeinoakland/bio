# basis-version-grammar — requirements

**Status** · In force (BOB's, K4007): written for T51-148 from the split map; moved ids by copy, K624. Reviewed by a second reader against the code; a split by copy is BOB's (P17, K3946). R1–R7 are `basis-versions` R1–R5, R43 and R54 renumbered; R8 and R9 are copies of its R35 and R36; R10 is its R56. Each carries "(moved from `basis-versions` R<n>)". An id of `basis-versions` is cited `basis-versions` R<n>; a bare R<n> is this file's.

## Public

### Purpose

The grammar of an inquiry's basis versions: the findings for the version block, the four-state machine, the composition a version freezes to and the normaliser a writer and a comparison both read, the refusal rows and vocabularies these raise, the grammar's registration in record-core's C-2.8 slot, and the name code. A library: pure, no storage, no instance, no ops.

### Provides

Terms. A **version** is a `basis_versions[]` row with its `basis_version_legs[]` and `basis_version_grounds[]` rows, joined by the version's name (the shape `basis-versions` states in its Terms). Its **composition** is the canonical text of every field that defines it. A **finding** is `{check, severity, message}`, with `repairable` and `repairs` when repairs are named and `code` when one is. Every function here but `registerBasisVersionGrammar` is pure and writes nothing; none throws on the inputs it states, and `registerBasisVersionGrammar` writes only into the `record` it is handed and throws in the one case R6 states. Every module-level name below is exported by `index.mjs`.

**Findings: `basisVersionFindings(fm, findings)`** pushes findings onto `findings` (an array); it returns nothing. `fm` is a document's frontmatter; a missing or non-object `fm` adds nothing. Every finding it pushes itself has `severity: "error"`, `check` read from its row and `code` its row's key (R8); the findings of the arms it calls (`inquiry-grammar`'s, `connections`') are those arms' own, and `checkLegExtentGrammar` is handed the C-number of `VERSION_LEG_NOT_CITABLE`.
- **R1** (moved from `basis-versions` R1) A version carries a description (C-25.1) and a name of 1–64 permitted characters unique within its inquiry (C-25.2), a known state (C-25.12), a boolean `hidden` (C-25.13), a `kind` in `SUGGEST_KINDS` when present (C-27.15), and, once moved, `state_by` a named member and `state_at` an ISO instant (C-25.19). As built: a description is at least 12 characters after trimming; "once moved" means in a state that needs a reason (R4), where the reason is at least 8 characters and `state_by` is a named member, never a machine identity. `hidden` and `kind` absent (or null; or an empty `kind`) are legal. The vocabularies are exported: `VERSION_STATES` (`suggested`, `considering`, `accepted`, `rejected`), `SUGGEST_KINDS` (the five kinds of INVESTIGATIVE-SESSION §9, keyed by kind) and `VERSION_NAME_RE` (R7).
- **R2** (moved from `basis-versions` R2) `derived_from` names a version of the same inquiry (C-25.7) and the derivations form a tree (C-25.8). Every leg and ground row names a version that exists (C-25.15). As built: an absent, null, empty or `"null"` `derived_from` is a root; a `basis_versions` value that is not an array, a row that is not an object, and legs or grounds present with no `basis_versions` block are each C-25.15.
- **R3** (moved from `basis-versions` R3) Each version's legs obey `inquiry`'s leg grammar (its R4, R5): a lead or theme refused by name first; a canonical information or inquiry target that is not this inquiry (C-25.14); vocabularies (C-25.10). Its grounds obey DEC-32 per version: labelled all or none (C-25.5), each declared once with a named asserter and date (C-25.6), no empty ground (C-25.15); `relationship` states AND or OR (C-25.3) and agrees with the partition (C-25.4); a regrouped partition inherited from `derived_from` carries an attributed regroup (C-25.9). A version's leg on an imported finding reference passes C-25.14 when `inquiry-grammar` R11 judges its form, and is refused with `accepted-work` R3's finding (C-21.4, C-21.5) when it is new or changed against the version it derives from. (DEC-112 (6); DEC-96 item 1; N522) A version holds every kind of leg its question may hold: a leg on a standard or a calculation is judged by the same arms of `inquiry`'s leg grammar as the question's own leg, never refused for its kind, so a version can rest on all that its question rests on (K3873; N1215) *(not yet met for a standard leg: T51; R10)*. (T51 fix round 2, C1; K4308) The totality counts only the legs that support; a leg whose role is `cuts_against` needs no ground (inquiry-grammar R68) and never makes a version refused. (Fix round 10, cause 30b; K5077; DEC-32 clause 4, as `inquiry-grammar` R68) "Labelled all or none" (C-25.5) applies to grounds only as R68 states them: a supporting leg that names no ground, beside legs that do, is a necessary leg of the version, the unlabelled supporting legs together its one necessary part, and is no finding; a version with no ground at all (`grounds` empty, every supporting leg necessary) is clean. Test: a version of one labelled ground and one unlabelled supporting leg raises no C-25.5 finding, and a member's own reading concluded on such a question through `basis-versions`' `conclude({reading})` is `ok`; control: a leg naming an undeclared ground is still refused (C-25.6), and an empty declared ground still C-25.15.
  - As built, for the findings above: a leg on a calculation, a duty occurrence or a derived connection is judged by `inquiry-grammar`'s arms for it (its R14, R15, R17) in place of the id, grade and extent arms; an imported leg is judged by its R11 arm in the same place; a leg on a standard is refused `VERSION_LEG_NOT_CITABLE` until R10 is met. The refusal of the acceptance half (C-21.4, C-21.5) is the caller's, not this module's: this module judges the document alone. Every other leg: a target that is not a canonical id or not an information or inquiry id is `VERSION_LEG_NOT_CITABLE` (C-25.10); one naming the inquiry itself is `VERSION_LEG_SELF` (C-25.14); `grade`, `grade_axis`, `grade_source` outside their vocabularies (`record-grammar`'s) are C-25.10, and an extent is judged by `inquiry-grammar`'s `checkLegExtentGrammar` under C-25.10. Every leg of every kind, imported and derived included, has its `role` asked against `record-grammar`'s `BASIS_ROLES` (C-25.10). A leg's ground label outside the label form, or naming a ground no row declares, is C-25.6; a ground no leg belongs to is C-25.15; a leg with no ground is `VERSION_PARTITION_INCOMPLETE` (C-25.5), whether or not any ground is named (the partition is total); a version with no legs is legal.
  - `relationship` is `and` or `or` (`VERSION_RELATIONSHIPS`), case-folded; absent or other is C-25.3; it disagrees (C-25.4) when the legs carry more than one distinct ground and it says `and`, or one ground and it says `or`.
  - A ground's `asserted_by` is a named member (`sufficiencyClaimState` `claimed`), or `SUFFICIENCY_UNCLAIMED` on a version declaring exactly one distinct ground; the no-claim value on a version declaring several is C-25.6; a machine identity, a blank or a non-string is C-25.6 in every case. The sufficiency vocabulary is exported: `SUFFICIENCY_UNCLAIMED` (`none:independent-sufficiency`), `SUFFICIENCY_CLAIM_STATES` (`claimed`, `unclaimed`, `unstated`, `machine_stamped`, each the member-facing sentence by key), `sufficiencyClaimState(assertedBy)` (trimmed: blank `unstated`; the no-claim value, case-folded, `unclaimed`; a machine identity `machine_stamped`; else `claimed`) and `isSufficiencyUnclaimed(assertedBy)` (true exactly for `unclaimed`). `isImportedRef(target)` is true for a string that is an imported finding reference (`inquiry-grammar` R11's form, after trimming); `isDerivedKindRef(target)` is true for a calculation reference, a duty occurrence reference or a derived connection's id.
- **R4** (moved from `basis-versions` R4) The machine: `suggested` → `considering`, `accepted`, `rejected`; `considering` → `suggested`, `accepted`, `rejected`; `accepted` → `considering`, `rejected`; `rejected` → `suggested`, `considering`, `accepted`. `considering` and `rejected` need a reason. Exported: `VERSION_MACHINE` (`{legal: VERSION_STATES, edges: {<state>: [<states it may move to>]}}`), `VERSION_REASON_REQUIRED` (`["considering", "rejected"]`) and `versionNeedsReason(to)`, true exactly for those two states, false for anything else.

**Composition: `versionsIn(fm)`, `versionAsWritten(submission)`, `sameComposition(held, offered)`, `compositionDiff(before, after)`, `fmSafe(text)`**
- **R5** (moved from `basis-versions` R5; its `basisVersionsOf(fm)` is `versionsIn(fm)`) `versionsIn` answers each version, in document order, with its legs, grounds and composition: every field that defines it, in a fixed order and escaping, which is the text a freeze compares byte for byte. A row that is not an object, or has no name, is skipped. Grounds are sorted by label and legs are not (reordering legs is a change to the composition); what happened to a version (state, hidden, who moved it, its affirmation and regroup, author, at, run) is outside the composition. `kind` and a leg's referent are emitted only when present, so a composition frozen before they existed stays byte-identical. A leg carrying `extent_capture` adds a `leg_capture\t<ord>\t<capture>` line after the `leg_referent` lines (D-595: the pin is frozen with the version); `leg_edition` lines (a leg on another group's finding names its edition) then `leg_derivation` lines (a derived connection's five fields) follow, each only when a leg carries one. Each leg is answered with `src_ord`, the row's index in the document's `basis_version_legs[]`, which is not part of the composition. `versionAsWritten(submission) → {version, grounds, legs}` is what a submitted version becomes in the document, the one normaliser a writer and a comparison both read: every value frontmatter-safe (`fmSafe`: quotes and backslashes to apostrophes, line breaks folded, trimmed; idempotent); `claim`, `derived_from`, `author`, `level`, `observed_at`, a ground's `statement` and each optional leg field null when blank; a ground with no label dropped; each ground asserted by the author, or by the explicit no-claim value (`SUFFICIENCY_UNCLAIMED`) when the author is a machine; each leg `extent_kind: document` and its `extent_capture` when given, or, for a leg on an imported finding reference, its `target_edition` and no extent. Precondition: `submission` is an object or absent, and `grounds` and `legs` are arrays when given; a `null` submission or a non-array `grounds` or `legs` throws. Pure; never throws otherwise.
  - Added at the copy, so the names exported are stated (behaviour is `basis-versions` R6's freeze compare, which stays there): `sameComposition(held, offered)` is true when the two compositions are the same text, or when `held` holds no `leg_capture` line and equals `offered` with its `leg_capture` lines left out (a composition stored before the pin existed is compared without it; once a `leg_capture` line is stored the pin is frozen). `compositionDiff(before, after)` answers the first field that differs, as a sentence by key `basis-versions.text.added` or `basis-versions.text.removed` when a line was added or removed, `<field> changed` when one changed, and `nothing changed` when none did. `fmSafe(text)` is the rule above, held once here.

**Registration**
- **R6** (moved from `basis-versions` R43) `BASIS_VERSION_GRAMMAR` is the grammar as record-core's seam takes it: `{ids: ["C-2.8"], arm(ctx, findings)}`, whose arm runs `basisVersionFindings` for an inquiry only (`ctx.fm.object_type`, read by `normalizeType`, an inquiry) and adds nothing for any other type. `registerBasisVersionGrammar(record, module = "basis-versions")` (**the `module` parameter is new at T51-148**: the code being copied takes `record` only and registers as `basis-versions`) registers it once at start through record-core's grammar seam (`registerGrammar`, record-core R67), claiming record-grammar R28's `C-2.8` slot (`checkInquiryExtension`) after `inquiry-grammar`'s registration (module order, record-core R67, K766), by registering `inquiry-grammar`'s first (its registration is idempotent), and runs at the sub-slot `inquiry-grammar` R6 provides: for an inquiry only, after its entry, division and subject-entity findings and before its grounds and leg findings, where the catalogue's `checkInquiryBasis` called it. The registrant is `module`, whose default `basis-versions` keeps record-core's `module`, `heldBy` and `grammars()` answers, the error text and the registration order what they were before the split (the map's ruling, §11.2). It answers record-core's answer; `null` and registers nothing when `record` is missing, has no `registerGrammar` function, or already holds this registration (once per record). Where record-core refuses the registration it throws an `Error` naming the module, the reason and, when record-core names one, the holder: a wiring defect, the one throw in this module. The findings' order is unchanged (P1). Each finding it adds is the one the catalogue's `checkInquiryBasis` added by calling it, the same in check, severity, message, repairs and code.
- **R7** (moved from `basis-versions` R54) A version name outside the name form has a code of its own. The finding for a name that is not 1 to 64 permitted characters (the form `VERSION_NAME_RE` states: `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9 ._-]{0,63}$`, case-insensitive, the name trimmed first; a missing, blank or non-string name is outside it) is `VERSION_NAME_MALFORMED`, row C-25.36 held in this module's table with its words read by key (`refusal.VERSION_NAME_MALFORMED`); `VERSION_NAME_NOT_UNIQUE` (C-25.2) is minted only for a name another version of the question already carries. Test: a name of 65 characters gives the first and not the second; a duplicate gives the second and not the first. Control: a valid unique name raises neither.

**The refusal rows**
- **R8** (copied from `basis-versions` R35, the grammar's share) Each refusal this module raises is held with its C-number and translation, with its test: `BASIS_VERSION_GRAMMAR_CHECKS` holds, by code, C-25.1 `VERSION_NO_DESCRIPTION`, C-25.2 `VERSION_NAME_NOT_UNIQUE`, C-25.36 `VERSION_NAME_MALFORMED`, C-25.3 `VERSION_NO_RELATIONSHIP`, C-25.4 `VERSION_RELATIONSHIP_DISAGREES`, C-25.5 `VERSION_PARTITION_INCOMPLETE`, C-25.6 `VERSION_GROUND_UNASSERTED`, C-25.7 `VERSION_DERIVED_FROM_UNKNOWN`, C-25.8 `VERSION_DERIVATION_CYCLE`, C-25.9 `VERSION_REGROUP_UNATTRIBUTED`, C-25.10 `VERSION_LEG_NOT_CITABLE`, C-25.12 `VERSION_STATE_UNKNOWN`, C-25.13 `VERSION_HIDDEN_NOT_BOOLEAN`, C-25.14 `VERSION_LEG_SELF`, C-25.15 `VERSION_ORPHAN_ROW`, C-25.19 `VERSION_DISPOSITION_UNATTRIBUTED`; `VERSION_KIND_CHECKS` holds C-27.15 `VERSION_KIND_UNKNOWN`. Each row is `{check, where, translation}`: `check` the C-number, `where` the site that raises it (this module's `grammar.mjs`, not `basis-versions`'), `translation` the member's sentence by its key. Each finding this module pushes itself has its `check` read from its row and its `code` the row's key. A test holds every such code to a row, and every row to a code a document can raise. The rows that only the store or a read can raise (C-25.11, .16–.18, .37–.39) are `basis-versions`' and are not here.
- **R9** (copied from `basis-versions` R36) No place is named in this module's behaviour or outward text: not in a finding, a translation, a vocabulary sentence or a `where`.

**A standard leg**
- **R10** (moved from `basis-versions` R56; the id is the next free one, the split map assigning none; it amends R3) **A standard leg is judged by `inquiry-grammar`'s `standardLegFindings`.** In a version, a leg whose target is a held standard (a `STD-` id, with an optional `target_portion`) is judged, at `basisVersionFindings`' target arm, by `inquiry-grammar.standardLegFindings(label, leg, findings)` (its R13, imported and never copied), called in the chain after the imported and derived-kind arms: a `target_portion` that is not a portion path, and a `connection` or `testimony` axis or a `hunch` source on it, are the findings that function names (`STANDARD_LEG_AXIS`), so R3's standard half is met; the grade and extent arms are skipped for that leg when the function answers true, as for a derived leg. A standard leg is never refused `VERSION_LEG_NOT_CITABLE` for its kind. A lead leg is still refused by name. Test (pure): a version whose leg is a standard with a capture grade raises no finding; one whose leg carries a `testimony` axis raises `STANDARD_LEG_AXIS` and no `VERSION_LEG_NOT_CITABLE`; a standard leg stating a part is refused by `inquiry-grammar`'s own code. Control: a version resting on an information leg is judged as before, and a lead leg is refused by name. The promotion half (a member's own reading concluded on a question with a passage, a standard and a calculation leg, `basis-versions` R51) is `basis-versions`'. Built in T51-148's second commit, after the copy, once `inquiry-grammar` exports `standardLegFindings` (CR-8); until then R3's standard half is not met.
- **R11** *(retired: vacuous since R3 was amended to DEC-32 clause 4, K5077; no finding raises C-25.5 any more, so none carries `legs`; K5090)*

- `record-grammar`: `BUNDLE_ID_RE`, `ISO_TS_RE`, `OBJECT_TYPES`, `normalizeType`, `isMachineIdentity`, `BASIS_ROLES`, `BASIS_GRADES`, `GRADE_AXES`, `GRADE_SOURCES`.

- `text-chain`: `canonicalExtent`.
- `record-core` is reached only as the `record` argument of `registerBasisVersionGrammar` (its `registerGrammar`, R67) and is not listed.

### Invariants

- **R1–R5, R10** are pure: they read their arguments only (no storage, clock or instance), never mutate an argument other than the `findings` array `basisVersionFindings` is handed, and never throw on the inputs they state (R5's precondition).
- R6: is the one function that throws (a refused registration), and registers at most once per record.
- R8: every refusal row has a C-number, a code and a translation read by key; a code is minted only at the site its row names.
- R9: no place is named.
- The moved code raises none of the rows that stay in `basis-versions` (C-25.11, .16, .17, .18, .37, .38, .39).
- Imports point only at modules before this one: it imports nothing of `basis-versions`, `inquiry`, `promotion`, `membership`, `retrieval`, `bias` or `accepted-work`.

### Suggestions

- Keep `GRAMMAR_WHERE` as the one string the grammar's rows share, naming `src/basis-version-grammar/grammar.mjs`.
- `index.mjs` re-exports `grammar.mjs` and `checks.mjs`; `basis-versions` re-exports the module whole, so no user re-points (K3268).
- Tests: every finding gets a negative control; the arms that need no promotion are tested here with a small helper of their own, and the arms that drive a promotion stay in `basis-versions`.
- A caller that needs the freeze compare calls `sameComposition`; one that names what changed calls `compositionDiff`. Both are `basis-versions`' to call, here only for their definition.
