## Public

### Purpose

The member's one feed (QUEUE): things that want this member, each typed by class (an OBLIGATION a named person must do, a FINDING the record noticed and nobody has judged, a CONDITION about our own machinery), each filed under every case it belongs to, with the acts that reach it; the personal half (mute and snooze) kept apart from the record acts (resolve, dispose); and the obligation inbox the feed's obligations come from.

### Provides

Terms. An **item** is `{id, class, kind, case, subject, summary, detail, basis, age, assignee, assignee_role, options, disposition}`, with `catalogue_id`, `prior_disposition`, `options_grain` or `recipients` where a producer carries them. A **home set** (`case`) is `{state, ungrouped, reasons, depth_bound, ancestors}`, each ancestor `{id, type, title, state, terminal, depth}`. The **depth bound** is 6. A **case** is an inquiry or a project. A **member** is the control plane's stamp of who asks (empty for a machine credential).

**The catalogue** (pure)
- **R1** `classOfKind(kind)` answers `OBLIGATION` for authority-undetermined, bias-debt, endorsement-owed, expertise-confirmation-owed, membership-request, project-owners-inactive; `FINDING` for missing_predecessor, overdue_successor, cardinality_exceeded (a stage declared to hold at most one document holds more; it decides nothing about which belongs; N107, K209), temporal-expectation-due, source-modified, source-removed, duplicate-document, link-verdict-changed, reused-asset-changed, assistant-surfaced-focus, grade-improvable, objective-gap, measure-decay, export-performed, audit-finding, register-unbacked, out-of-inquiry-lead, stance-changed-here-not-elsewhere, new-version-arrived-from-another-team, shared-inquiry-concluded-by-another-project, newer-capture-affects-reference (a newer capture of something a member's reference is pinned to was graded affected or undetermined; reevaluation R14; N172); `CONDITION` for the twelve condition kinds (R5); null for anything else, a non-string or empty string included. Every kind has one sentence.
- **R2** `catalogueIdOf(kind)` answers `N-1` for export-performed and null for every other kind.
- **R3** `itemClassOf(id)` answers `FINDING` or `CONDITION` for an id `FINDING::<rest>` or `CONDITION::<rest>` (trimmed) with a non-blank rest, else null.
- **R4** `suppressedBy(item, mutes)` (`mutes` a map of case id to a set of kinds) answers the first of the item's ancestors whose set holds the item's kind, else null; there is no wildcard, so a kind not named when the mute was made is not suppressed. `mutedAsItem(item, set)` is true only for a CONDITION or FINDING whose id is in the set. `serializeMutedKinds` answers the sorted, de-duplicated, comma-joined set; `parseMutedKinds` inverts it.
- **R5** The condition kinds are `observation-log`'s vocabulary (monitoring-recheck-due, archive-fallback-eligible, capture-session-ttl-expiring, source-unreachable-governed, capture-completed-unattended, partial-capture-outstanding, text-undetermined, client-rendered-shell, invitation-spent-or-expired, governor-holding-host, runtime-ceiling-reached, render-deferred), re-exported as `QUEUE_CONDITION_KINDS`. *(not yet met: N49 — defined here today)*

**queueFeed({member, viewer, now, limit}) → feed or refusal** (`op=queue`)
- **R6** `limit` is clamped to 1–500 (default 200). Items are ordered OBLIGATION, FINDING, CONDITION, then by id, and cut at the limit. The answer carries `member`, `items`, `limit`, `item_count`, `truncated`, `classes`, `classes_deferred` (empty), `ancestor_depth_bound` (6), `mute`, `disposed`, and the control plane's decorated options and `vocabularies` (R17).
- **R7** Homes. An item's ancestors are every inquiry and project reached upward from its subjects by basis legs and by `cites` edges; an edge withdrawn in the citing document (severed on every spelling) is not a step. The walk stops at depth 6. An ancestor the viewer may not see is not named and adds `out_of_view`; a stop at the bound with an unvisited ancestor beyond it adds `depth_bound`; either makes the set `undetermined`. `terminal` is whether the ancestor's state has no outgoing edge in its machine, null when the machine does not name the state. `ungrouped` is true exactly when the set is determined and empty (DEC-16, DEC-36).
- **R8** OBLIGATIONs: each open or forwarded task on a subject the viewer may see, assigned to the member or `unassigned` (every one for no member), with `assignee`, `assignee_role` and `age` from its creation; a task on a subject the viewer may not see is withheld and not counted. Each uncleared bias debt (from `bias`) inside the viewer's gate whose recipients include the member or name nobody, at most 200, keyed `OBLIGATION::bias-debt::<run>`.
- **R9** FINDINGs: one per open proposal of `progressions.proposalsFeed`, kind overdue_successor when overdue else missing_predecessor, keyed `FINDING::<progression>::<stage>`, its subject bundles those the viewer may see (at most 8 named), `age` undetermined (`derived_on_read`), `prior_disposition` as the feed gives it. Per (progression, stage) with an open `cardinality_exceeded` instance (progressions R31): when that stage has a proposal item, the finding joins it (the item keeps its kind, `basis.kinds` gains `cardinality_exceeded`, the detail names the count); otherwise it is an item of kind `cardinality_exceeded`, keyed the same `FINDING::<progression>::<stage>` so one decision governs one item (R32), its `prior_disposition` from the feed's `dispositions[]`, its grade the weakest instance's (`connections.weakerGrade`) (N107, K209); out-of-inquiry-lead per captured lead, homed under the inquiry the evidence bears on, never the run's; stance-changed-here-not-elsewhere; new-version-arrived-from-another-team; shared-inquiry-concluded-by-another-project; export-performed, one per export among the latest 200, to an administrator member or the `admin` machine credential and to nobody else; newer-capture-affects-reference, one per open notice of `reevaluation` (its R14, read through `reevaluation.notices({holder, state: "open", viewer})`) on a holder the viewer may see, keyed `FINDING::newer-capture-affects-reference::<notice>`, homed under the holder's ancestors (R7) (N172); objective-gap, one per gap `intent.gaps({project, viewer})` answers (intent R6) for each project the viewer sees in which the member participates (every visible project when there is no member), at most 50 projects in id order, the bound published as `objective_gap_projects_bound` beside `objective_gap_projects_truncated`, each homed under its project (N172); source-modified and source-removed, one per monitored document whose latest tick flagged `reeval_pending` (read through `monitoring.flagged`, its R48; its `source_status` says which), homed under that document's ancestors (N229). *(not yet met: N330)*
- **R10** CONDITIONs, derived on read and writing nothing: governor-holding-host per held host (its documents gathered at most 16, past which the home set states `subject_bound` and is undetermined); partial-capture-outstanding per live capture session; capture-completed-unattended per bundle a machine credential wrote and per completed capture request; render-deferred per render request held under a code or expired, its reason the code's own translation; archive-fallback-eligible per address `monitoring.archiveEligible` answers (its R47, what the next tick would find: K406 Q2); monitoring-recheck-due per monitored address `monitoring({viewer})` (monitoring R32) answers overdue by more than its interval or unscheduled (monitoring R16, R31) (N229). *(not yet met: N330)*
- **R11** The mint. Before anything is suppressed, every item's class must be one of the three, its kind catalogued, and its kind's class its own; otherwise the whole feed is refused `NO_CLASS` (C-31.1), `NO_SUCH_KIND` (C-31.2) or `KIND_MISCLASSED` (C-31.3), with the check, the translation and the item's id.
- **R12** Every item carries `options`: the acts `affordances.deriveActs` offers on its first 8 subject bundles for this caller (the member, else a machine viewer's class), once each; and `disposition`: an OBLIGATION `available: false`, `instead` `biasdebtresolve` for bias-debt and `taskresolve` otherwise; a CONDITION `available: false`, `instead: queuemute`; a FINDING with a progression stage `available: true`, `scope: instance`, `key` `<progression>::<stage>`, `definition_version`, `requires: [definitionVersion]`; any other FINDING `scope: project`, `key: null`, `finding` its id, `projects` its project homes, `requires: [project, finding]`, or, with no project home, `available: false`, `reason: no_project_scope` (objective-gap, source-modified and source-removed take this project-scoped disposition); a newer-capture-affects-reference FINDING `available: true`, `scope: notice`, `notice` its notice id, `acts: [versionadopt, versionkeep]` (reevaluation R15's `adoptVersion`, `keepVersion`), `requires: [notice]` (N172).
- **R13** A project-scoped decision (R27) removes its finding from the feed for that project only: when every project home has decided, the item leaves `items`; otherwise it stays with the deciding projects removed from its homes and from `disposition.projects`, each named in `case.disposed_by`.
- **R14** Mutes apply to every item whatever its class, item mutes first: a suppressed item leaves `items` and is listed in `mute.suppressed` with its id, class, kind, the case that suppressed it (or null) and `scope` (`item` or `case`). `mute` is `{personal: true, cases, case_kinds, items, suppressed, suppressed_count, detail}`. With no member nothing is suppressed.
- **R15** `disposed` is `{personal: false, findings, count, recorded, bound: 64, truncated}`: the instance-scope decisions `proposalsFeed` records (each `id`, `scope: instance`, `key`, the version view) then the project-scope decisions that removed an item (`scope: project`, `project`, `finding`, `key`), at most 64.
- **R39** The answer carries a bounded `resolved` block beside `disposed`: the obligations (tasks and settled bias debts) resolved within a recent window on subjects the viewer may see, each with who resolved it and when, so a member who did not resolve one sees *resolved by X on this date*, not a gap (DEC-16). *(not yet met: N326)*
- **R40** A snoozed case's `snoozed_until` is published in `mute`, and each of its items is marked `snoozed` until that instant; the plane does not withhold them, so a surface can hold them back (DEC-10 (a)).
- **R16** A FINDING never reads as an obligation: its `basis` names its source and derivation (D-82), and it leaves the team's list only by a recorded disposition (D-79).
- **R17** `op=queue`'s answer decorates every option through `affordances.decorate` with the control plane's gate, so an option equals the act `op=affordances` publishes for that subject, and attaches the vocabularies `affordances.vocabulariesFor(kinds)` publishes over `actions`' kinds (affordances R26, actions R42; N231); a store refusal is passed through with status 400.
- **R18** An out-of-inquiry-lead offers the inquiry-grain acts on the inquiry it bears on: take it up (cite into that inquiry) and set it aside (the project-scoped disposition, R27); no item offers an act its op would refuse `NO_PROJECT_SCOPE`.

**queueMute({member, case, kinds, item, unmute, viewer, at})** (`op=queuemute`)
- **R19** Refusals, in order: `NO_MEMBER`; with `item`, also naming a case or kinds, `BAD_KIND`; without `item`: `NO_CASE`, `NO_SUCH_CASE` (absent and invisible alike), `NOT_A_CASE` (not an inquiry or a project), `NO_KINDS` (with the mutable kinds); then for each kind or the item: a comma in a kind, `BAD_KIND`; an unclassifiable kind or item, `UNKNOWN_KIND` (with the mutable kinds); an OBLIGATION, `KIND_NOT_PERSONAL` (C-33.27) with the obligation sentence. An item id is classed by R3, else as OBLIGATION when a task has that id.
- **R20** The item form records or removes one (member, item) mute and answers `form: item`, `item_class`, `muted_items`, `added`, `removed`, `at`. The case form adds kinds to the set already muted (never replacing it) or removes the named ones, keeps the case's snooze, and answers `muted_kinds`, `added`, `removed`, `at`. Both answer `wrote`, naming only `queue_item_mutes` or `queue_state`: no task, disposition or bundle is written.

**queueSnooze({member, case, until, clear, viewer, at})** (`op=queuesnooze`)
- **R21** `NO_MEMBER`, then R19's case refusals; unless `clear`: `NO_UNTIL`, `BAD_UNTIL` (not an instant), `UNTIL_IN_PAST`. Records or clears the member's `snoozed_until` for the case, keeping its mutes; no item, task or disposition changes.
- **R22** A consumer registered with `scheduler` is due when a snooze has expired, wakes at the earliest future one, and writes nothing.

**The obligation inbox: taskDrain, taskList, taskForward, taskResolve** (`op=taskdrain`, `op=tasks`, `op=taskforward`, `op=taskresolve`)
- **R23** `taskDrain({limit, actor, now})` takes up to `limit` (1–500, default 50) queued capture events in order. An event whose capture is filed in no bundle is kept with its attempts counted (`waiting`). One with a live task (open or forwarded) of its kind on that bundle adds a `folded` entry to the task's history. Otherwise a task is created, routed to an active owner of the bundle when it is a project, else to an active owner of the first project (by id) with a live `cites` edge to it, else to the earliest active administrator (the first member of `membership`'s `activeAdmins` after the founder, its R86; `group-admin`), else `unassigned`; an ungrammatical task (C-19.1) is dropped and reported under `refused`; an exhausted id space keeps the event, its `waiting` entry carrying `record-core.mintExhausted("TASK")`'s `code`, `check` and `detail` (its R62; N322). Answers `drained`, `created` (with each route's basis), `folded`, `waiting`, `refused`, `remaining`, `limit`. A consumer registered with `scheduler` runs it while events wait. *(not yet met: N329)*
- **R24** `taskList({assignee, status, refersTo, limit, viewer})`: tasks on subjects the viewer may see, newest first, filtered as asked, at most `limit` (1–1,000, default 200), `truncated` measured one past the cap, and per-status counts over the visible set plus the queued-event count.
- **R25** `taskForward({id, to, actor, now})`: `NO_ACTOR`; `MACHINE_CANNOT_FORWARD` (C-32.10); `NO_SUCH_TASK`; `ALREADY_RESOLVED`; `NOT_YOURS` (C-76.1) unless the actor is the assignee, the task is `unassigned`, or the actor is an administrator; `NO_SUCH_MEMBER` (an active member); `ALREADY_THEIRS`. Reassigns with role `member`, status `forwarded`, and appends `forwarded` with the actor to the history. `taskResolve({id, actor, now})`: `NO_ACTOR`; `MACHINE_CANNOT_RESOLVE` (C-32.11); `NO_SUCH_TASK`; an already resolved task answers `already: true` and writes nothing; `NOT_YOURS` as above; then status `resolved`, `resolved_at`, and `resolved` with the actor appended. With `items`, both run per item through `record-core.perItem` (its R50–R52).
- **R26** A muting of an OBLIGATION item by its published id (`OBLIGATION::bias-debt::<run>`) is refused `KIND_NOT_PERSONAL`, not `UNKNOWN_KIND`.

**proposeDispose — the dispatch and the project arm** (`op=proposedispose`)
- **R27** With `items`, each item is decided on its own through `record-core.perItem` (its R50–R52) with `PER_ITEM_ACTS`' identity groups, the decider, viewer and identity forced onto every item. A call naming `project` or `finding` takes the project arm; any other goes to `progressions.disposeProposal` after R28's bridge. Project arm refusals, in order: `NO_FINDING`, `NO_PROJECT_SCOPE`, `NOT_A_DISPOSITION`, `NO_REASON`, `BAD_REASON`, `NO_DECIDER` (these four as progressions R21 words them), `NO_SUCH_PROJECT` (absent, invisible or not a project; membership R44's existence answer where it applies), `PROJECT_ACT_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (membership R55, joined). Success keeps one decision per (project, finding), replaced on re-decision, with kind, state, reason, decider and instant, and answers `scope: project`, `key` `<project>::<finding>`; no bundle is written.
- **R28** The bridge: a `key` whose class (by R3, else by its first segment through R1) is CONDITION or OBLIGATION is refused `CLASS_NOT_DISPOSED` (C-33.44) with `class`, `kind` and `instead` (`queuemute` or `taskresolve`); a FINDING key is refused `NO_PROJECT_SCOPE` with `finding`, `kind` and `requires: [project, finding]`. Nothing is written.
- **R29** Both `NO_PROJECT_SCOPE` refusals carry a catalogued check id and a translation (DEC-49).
