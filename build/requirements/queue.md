## Public

### Purpose

The member's one feed (QUEUE): things that want this member, each typed by class (an OBLIGATION a named person must do, a FINDING the record noticed and nobody has judged, a CONDITION about our own machinery), each filed under every case it belongs to, with the acts that reach it; the personal half (mute and snooze) kept apart from the record acts (resolve, dispose); over the obligations `tasks` holds and the items `queue-producers` derive.

### Provides

Terms. An **item** is `{id, class, kind, case, subject, summary, detail, basis, age, assignee, assignee_role, options, disposition}`, with `catalogue_id`, `prior_disposition`, `options_grain`, `recipients` or `due` (`queue-producers` R25) where a producer carries them. A **home set** (`case`) is `{state, ungrouped, reasons, depth_bound, ancestors}`, each ancestor `{id, type, title, state, terminal, depth}`. The **depth bound** is 6. A **case** is an inquiry or a project. A **member** is the control plane's stamp of who asks (empty for a machine credential).
