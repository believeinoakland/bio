## Public

### Purpose

The acquisition act: fetches a document from its public address (directly, through a Google Drive export, from a web archive, or rendered as a visitor saw it), hashes and stores it as it arrives, records the fetch as a receipt, profiles what it is, captures a page's supporting files, requests co-attestation, and returns the provenance document a caller promotes. It writes no bundle. What capture keeps about sources and sites (reachability, site assets, links, sessions, the platform's ceiling, the render allowance, the event queue) is `capture`'s, reached through the capture store its caller hands in.

### Provides

Terms. The **document address** is what the record identifies the document by; the **retrieval locator** is what was actually fetched (they differ for an archive replay and a Drive export). A **public locator** is `https:` on a named public host (`isPublicHttpsLocator`: no bare IP, no `localhost`, no credentials, a dotted host). The **caller class** and whether the caller is a member session are the control plane's stamps. Every refusal names a `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check` and `translation`. The **store handed in** is the capture store the caller passes as the first argument (`capture`'s instance: its R8 outcomes and reachability, R15 enqueue, R22 sessions, R23 ceiling, R24–R25 site assets, R27 links, R39–R40 render allowance, R55 measurement and the validators R22 reads; this module is earlier in the order than `capture` and imports none of it). (T50; N1094, N1117) The store handed in also answers capture R46's `liveCaptureSessions` and capture R94's `recordCapturedFor`, which R46 and R47 call.

**acquire(store, body, {cls, member, sessMember, storeName, captureRequest}) → answer** The one act that fetches and files (`capture`'s `acquire(body, opts)` hands itself in; `op=acquire`). `body`: `locator`, optional `via` (`"archive.org"`), `address` (archive arm), `render`, `subresources`, `continue`, `authority`, `file`, `matchedSweep`. Success answers `{ok: true, existed, existed_undetermined?, document, parts?, subresources?, snapshot?, files?, subresources_skipped?, note}`. **archiveLookup(store, {address})** (R3), **profileOf({ev, sha, ct, total, multipart, headers, locator, view, retrieved})** and **profileView(core)** (R17), **governedFetch(store, target, purpose, delegated, opts)** and **governedCall(store, purpose)** (R9), **userAgent(env, purpose, delegated)** (R9, R24), **substanceDigests**, **profilesAsText**, **ODF_DIGEST_MAX** (R17), **acquireGradeNote**, **ACQUIRE_GRADE_NOTE** (R18).
