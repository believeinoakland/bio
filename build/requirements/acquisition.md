## Public

### Purpose

The acquisition act: fetches a document from its public address (directly, through a Google Drive export, from a web archive, or rendered as a visitor saw it), hashes and stores it as it arrives, records the fetch as a receipt, profiles what it is, captures a page's supporting files, requests co-attestation, and returns the provenance document a caller promotes. It writes no bundle. What capture keeps about sources and sites (reachability, site assets, links, sessions, the platform's ceiling, the render allowance, the event queue) is `capture`'s, reached through the capture store its caller hands in.

### Provides

Terms. The **document address** is what the record identifies the document by; the **retrieval locator** is what was actually fetched (they differ for an archive replay and a Drive export). A **public locator** is `https:` on a named public host (`isPublicHttpsLocator`: no bare IP, no `localhost`, no credentials, a dotted host). The **caller class** and whether the caller is a member session are the control plane's stamps. Every refusal names a `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check` and `translation`. The **store handed in** is the capture store the caller passes as the first argument (`capture`'s instance: its R8 outcomes and reachability, R15 enqueue, R22 sessions, R23 ceiling, R24–R25 site assets, R27 links, R39–R40 render allowance, R55 measurement and the validators R22 reads; this module is earlier in the order than `capture` and imports none of it).

**acquire(store, body, {cls, member, sessMember, storeName, captureRequest}) → answer** The one act that fetches and files (`capture`'s `acquire(body, opts)` hands itself in; `op=acquire`). `body`: `locator`, optional `via` (`"archive.org"`), `address` (archive arm), `render`, `subresources`, `continue`, `authority`, `file`, `matchedSweep`. Success answers `{ok: true, existed, existed_undetermined?, document, parts?, subresources?, snapshot?, files?, subresources_skipped?, note}`. **archiveLookup(store, {address})** (R3), **profileOf({ev, sha, ct, total, multipart, headers, locator, view, retrieved})** and **profileView(core)** (R17), **governedFetch(store, target, purpose, delegated, opts)** and **governedCall(store, purpose)** (R9), **userAgent(env, purpose, delegated)** (R9, R24), **substanceDigests**, **profilesAsText**, **ODF_DIGEST_MAX** (R17), **acquireGradeNote**, **ACQUIRE_GRADE_NOTE** (R18).

*Who may acquire, and from where*
- **R1** (was `capture` R1) A `daemon`-class caller is refused `NOT_PERMITTED` except on the archive arm and on the capture-request arm (K58); the archive arm is refused `NOT_PERMITTED` to any class but admin, probe and daemon.
- **R2** (was `capture` R2) The fetched address must be a public locator, else `BAD_LOCATOR`. Nothing a caller sends can supply a hop, a retrieval locator for the archive arm, an export address, an export format or a producer: every such fact is derived by this call at the moment it fetches.
- **R3** (was `capture` R3) **The archive arm** (`via: "archive.org"`). The body names the document's own public `address` (else `BAD_ADDRESS`). The arm runs only when the address is fallback-eligible (`capture` R8, through the store handed in), else `NOT_ELIGIBLE` with the reachability; it queries the CDX index through the host governor (`web.archive.org` held to 24 requests a minute), chooses a row by `capture-sources.selectCapture`, and fetches its replay. `ARCHIVE_UNREACHABLE`, `ARCHIVE_REFUSED`, `HOST_COOLING_OFF`, and a selection refusal with every row considered, are answered by name. The document address is the CDX `original`; the chain gains the archive's hop built from the CDX record this call fetched. `archiveLookup({address})` decides and reports the same without capturing.
- **R4** (was `capture` R4) **The Drive arm** (a Google Drive address, per `capture-sources.readDriveAddress`). A body carrying any hop fact (`export_address`, `export_format`, `producer`, …) is refused `DRIVE_HOP_FACT_SUPPLIED` (C-48.1) whatever its address. A folder, a file of undetermined kind and an unrecognised shape are refused `DRIVE_FOLDER_NOT_A_DOCUMENT`, `DRIVE_KIND_UNDETERMINED`, `DRIVE_SHAPE_UNRECOGNISED` (C-48.2–C-48.4). A harvestable address is fetched at its composed OpenDocument export; a non-2xx export is `DRIVE_EXPORT_UNREACHABLE` (C-48.6), an HTML content type is `DRIVE_EXPORT_IS_THE_SHELL` (C-48.5, body never read), and bytes detected as HTML are `DRIVE_EXPORT_BYTES_ARE_THE_SHELL` (C-48.7). The application shell is never filed in the document's place. The document address is the Drive link as given; the chain gains Google's hop (`capture-sources.driveHop`, `bound: false`).
- **R5** (was `capture` R5) **The render arm** (`render` present and not `false`). Any value but `true` is `RENDER_FLAG_MALFORMED` (C-83.1); with the archive arm, a Drive export or `continue` it is `RENDER_ARM_CONFLICT` (C-83.2); with no renderer bound, `RENDER_NO_RENDERER` (C-83.3, 501). Before any fetch it asks the host governor for the page's host (`RENDER_HOST_COOLING_OFF`, C-83.5) and the render allowance (`capture` R39, through the store handed in): over the concurrency cap it is `RENDER_AT_CAPACITY` (C-83.8, `waiting`, nothing reserved); with the day's allowance committed or unreadable, `RENDER_DEFERRED` (C-83.4). Served bytes that are not a single-part HTML page are `RENDER_NOT_A_PAGE` (C-83.6) and the reservation is released at no charge; a failed or oversized render is `RENDER_FAILED` (C-83.7) and nothing is filed. Every other path releases the reservation with the render's elapsed time.
- **R6** (was `capture` R6) A filed render is a pair: the rendered document is the primary (`capture.method` `rendered`, its own digest), the served shell is held beside it under its own digest as `snapshots/<name>.shell.html`, and the document carries `pair`, `render` (`capture-sources.renderBlock`, each subresource body the renderer handed over hashed by this instance and kept) and `shell`. Content authority is `capture-sources.renderedAuthority`'s.
- **R7** (was `capture` R41) A render asks for the locale `capture-sources.renderLocaleFor` answers for R17's view; timezone stays UTC.
- **R8** (was `capture` R42) `acquire` does not read the document: its answer carries no `reading` or `text_units`. Reading from the stored capture is `extraction`'s (K49).

*The fetch*
- **R9** (was `capture` R7) Every outbound fetch goes through the host governor: admitted (after any wait it names) or refused `HOST_COOLING_OFF` (429, with `retry_in_ms`); each response's status and `Retry-After` is reported to it. The user agent names CivicOS, the instance and the purpose (`acquire`), or the member-browser agent a request delegated.
- **R10** (was `capture` R9) The body is hashed as it arrives and stored content-addressed in parts of 8 MiB, each part under its own SHA-256 with its checksum. Refusals: a thrown fetch is `FETCH_FAILED` (502); a non-2xx status is `SOURCE_REFUSED` with the status; no body is `NO_BODY`; more than 256 MiB is `TOO_LARGE` (413) and the stream is cancelled; zero bytes is `EMPTY`. A single part whose digest differs from the running hash is `HASH_DISAGREEMENT` (500).
- **R11** (was `capture` R10) `existed`: for one part, whether that object was held before this call wrote it; for several, `true` exactly when `provenance.registerHolds` finds the whole digest registered under an existing bundle, otherwise `null` with `existed_undetermined` saying what was and was not asked and how many parts were already held. Several parts never answer `false`.
- **R12** (was `capture` R11) A continuation (`continue: <session>`) resumes that session's outstanding supporting files against the session's own primary; the primary is not fetched again.

*What is recorded*
- **R13** (was `capture` R12) Every filed capture writes one acquisition receipt (`provenance.recordReceipt`) under the document address (the CDX `original`, the Drive link as given, or for a direct fetch the address it resolved to after redirects), `via` (`archive.org` for the archive arm, else `direct`) and the retrieval locator, whatever its content type and whether or not its supporting files were captured. A failed receipt write does not fail the capture.
- **R14** (was `capture` R13) `document.transport` records the requested and resolved addresses, whether it redirected, the status, every response header, and `peer_address: null` with the reason it is unavailable.
- **R15** (was `capture` R14) Content authority: a non-empty `authority` in the body is `authority_state: "determined"` with a dated basis naming the asserting member or caller; none is `"undetermined"` with a dated basis, never refused. An undetermined capture enqueues one `authority-undetermined` event (`capture` R15's `taskEnqueue`, through the store handed in; one per capture digest; subject the fetched locator, or the Drive link for a Drive export); a failed enqueue does not fail the capture.
- **R16** (was `capture` R16) The document carries `provenance_chain`, first hop this instance (`who`: instance name, CivicOS and version; asserts "these bytes were served for <locator> at <retrieved>", or for a render what the render produced and, when its wait timed out, that they are not asserted to be the whole page; `bound: false`; `via`), then the archive or Drive hop; `capture` (`method`, `grade` R18, `actor_class` — `member` for a member session, `session` for a probe, `daemon` otherwise — `actor` (the member stamp of a member session, else null), `sha256`, `encoding: "binary"`, `bytes`, `content_type`, and `transport` unless rendered; `authority: "Internet Archive"` on the archive arm); `parts` (file, digest, size) when multi-part; `origin` (`named_request`, or `sweep` with `matched_sweep` and the deeming actor); `attestation_attempts: []`; and `file` `snapshots/<name>`, `locator`, `retrieved` (this instance's clock, to the second). On `capture` R65's arm only, the document also carries `source` and `knocker_note` (`capture` R65); a fetched capture's source stays its document address.
- **R17** (was `capture` R17) `document.profile` is `docprofile`'s over what was fetched: `identify` over the headers, document address, content type and (single-part, textual, within bound) the stored bytes read back; `doctypeFor` with the combined view of the instance's active jurisdiction profiles (`jurisdictions.combine` of record-core's `jurisdiction_profiles`); `profileRecord`, the content type key, label, version, confidence, signals and contract; the normalisation rules and boundary; `format` from `format-registry.detectFormat` over the bytes (read back whole up to `ODF_DIGEST_MAX` when not already read, so an office file's format and container digest are judged from its bytes; K659), else the declared type with the absence stated; and `digests`.
- **R18** (was `capture` R18) `capture.grade` is `provenance.captureGrade`'s rule: `EARNED_CAPTURE_CEILING` for a direct fetch, `ARCHIVE_CAPTURE_GRADE` for the archive arm, each read from its one definition.

*Supporting files (`subresources: true`)*
- **R19** (was `capture` R19) Only a single-part HTML primary of at most 8 MiB is walked; otherwise `subresources_skipped` names `TOO_LARGE_TO_PARSE`, `NOT_HTML`, `PRIMARY_UNREADABLE`, `NO_SUCH_SESSION` or `SESSION_UNREADABLE`, and the capture is unaffected. The walk is `subresources.captureSubresources` with: `isPublicHttpsLocator` as the fence; the stored object as `put`/`readBack`; the platform ceiling from `captureLimit` (`capture` R23) unless a probe is due; the host's site assets as `siteLookup` (`capture` R24); and a `fetchOne` that returns `HOST_COOLING_OFF` for a host the governor is cooling, waits a 50–250 ms jittered stagger (an instance setting), reports every outcome to the governor, and answers a non-2xx as `SOURCE_REFUSED`. Afterwards it records the observed ceiling (`recordCaptureLimit`), the links (`recordLinks`), the site observations (`recordSiteAssets`), and the compute measurement (`capture` R23, R27, R25, R55, each through the store handed in); parks a session (`capture` R22) when anything is outstanding and drops it when complete. The answer carries `subresources`, `snapshot` (manifest and companion digests, counts, `complete`, `outstanding`, `platform`, `reuse`, `part_fetch_spread`, `compute`, and a `continuation` naming the session) and `files`. A failed bookkeeping write never fails the capture.

*Co-attestation*
- **R20** (was `capture` R20) An acquisition requests, wherever the source permits, a trusted timestamp over the capture digest and a co-archive of the locator at capture time, recording each attempt and its outcome in `attestation_attempts`. Both are requested at every capture, the co-archive included (Bob, K60), through `provenance.attest` (K72).
- Errors: every refusal above is an answer, never a throw; a store or bookkeeping write that fails is stated or ignored as said, never turned into a failed capture.

*The capture-request arm* (for `capture-requests`, through `capture`'s in-process arm)
- **R21** (was `capture` R60) (N140; capture-requests R38; K287) `captureRequest.origin` (`{matched_sweep, deeming_actor}`, from the drain's row, never a body) files the capture with `origin: {kind: "sweep", matched_sweep, deeming_actor}`; the body's `matchedSweep` is ignored on this arm. Without it the arm's origin is `named_request`.
- **R22** (was `capture` R61) (N140; capture-requests R39; K287) With `captureRequest.heldSha` (64 hex): the fetch is conditional with the validators (`ETag` → `If-None-Match`, `Last-Modified` → `If-Modified-Since`) the store handed in recorded on the held capture's own successful fetch at the same document address (recorded on every filed direct capture: address, capture sha, etag, last-modified, time). A `304` files no capture and writes no receipt (the source asserts the bytes, it does not serve them); it records a `success` outcome (`capture` R8) and answers `{ok: true, existed: true, unchanged: true, capture: {sha256: heldSha}, basis}` with no `document`. With no validators recorded the fetch is unconditional, and bytes hashing to `heldSha` answer with `existed` and `held: true`. A `heldSha` that is not 64 hex is ignored.
- **R23** (was `capture` R62) (N140; capture-requests R41; capture-sources R56; K287) With `captureRequest.credential` (capture-sources R56's entry): `kind: "user-agent"` sends the secret as the `user-agent` in place of R9's; `kind: "login"` as HTTP Basic (`authorization: Basic base64(secret)`, the secret `user:password`); `kind: "other"` verbatim as the `authorization` header. The credential goes only to its own host: redirects are followed by hand, and a hop to another host is fetched without it. The document records `capture.credentialed: {credential, kind, supplied_by, scope, project}` and `capture.reproducible_by_public: false`, never the secret, in the answer, the receipt or any error; a thrown fetch's message is not carried when a credential rode it.

*The sweep's scope* (`link-sweep` R1, R5, R6; `monitoring` R53, R57, R58 before N506's split; K1036)
- **R31** An acquire with a sweep origin carries `scope`, a list of in-scope prefixes (`link-sweep` R1). A redirect whose target is not in scope is not followed. The acquire answers `{ok: false, code: "SWEEP_REDIRECT_OUT_OF_SCOPE", target}`, fetches nothing at the target and files nothing. A sweep-origin acquire without `scope` is refused `SWEEP_SCOPE_MISSING`. A sweep origin is one the in-process caller declares `{kind: "sweep", matched_sweep: "<bundle>#<id>", deeming_actor}` (link-sweep R5's shape), and `scope` rides that in-process arm only, never a body; capture-requests R38's drain origin (no `kind`) is not one. No caller other than `link-sweep` and `capture-requests`' sweep arm sets a sweep origin: a `matchedSweep` in `op=acquire`'s body is ignored and the capture files `origin: {kind: "named_request"}`, as R21 does (K1126). (Intake Doctrine §4, "Constraints as security controls")

*The archive lookup through Memento* (N492; K1032)
- **R32** (N492, K1032) The archive arm (R3) and `archiveLookup` find a memento through `capture-sources` R37's Memento services, so any compliant archive can serve the lookup and the CDX index is one source, not the interface:
  - the archive is a descriptor (`WAYBACK_MEMENTO` for the Internet Archive); its endpoints for the document address are `mementoEndpoints(archive, address)`;
  - the lookup asks the TimeGate with `Accept-Datetime` (`acceptDatetime`) and reads the answer with `readMementoAnswer`, or reads the TimeMap (`parseTimeMap`) and takes its candidates with `timeMapCandidates`, each through the host governor as R3's query is;
  - the chosen memento's raw bytes are fetched (the descriptor's `raw`, else the memento URI as given), its row is `mementoRow(answer, {sha256, bytes})` over the bytes received, and the choice is `selectCapture`'s, with R29–R31's rules as written;
  - the chain's archive hop is `mementoHop(chosen, mementoUri, {archive, answer})`, and the document address is the answer's `original` where R3 names the CDX `original`;
  - each Memento refusal (`MEMENTO_LINK_MALFORMED`, `MEMENTO_NO_ORIGINAL`, `MEMENTO_NOT_NEGOTIATED`, `MEMENTO_NO_DATETIME`, `MEMENTO_BAD_DATETIME`, `NO_USABLE_CAPTURE`) is answered by name, as R3's refusals are, and nothing is filed.

**civicosUserAgent(version, instance, purpose), CIVICOS_CONTACT_URL** (moved from the check catalogue, K649 (1))
