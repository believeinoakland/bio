## Public

### Purpose

A project's stage, what each stage has earned and still needs, and its work products' readiness, derived at the read from the questions the project draws on and what it has published (State Rules §4.3; K356). It writes nothing and owns no table.

### Provides

#### A project's stage: projectStage({project, viewer}) (`op=projectstage`; writes nothing)

- **R1** (was `publication` R44) `projectStage({project, viewer})` answers `{project, stage, closed_reason, questions: {read, with_legs, concluded, truncated}, published_editions, work_products, work_products_limit, work_products_truncated, readiness, basis, stages}`, derived afresh at every read. `NO_ID` for an empty `project`; `NO_SUCH_PROJECT` for a project that is absent, not a project, or at `membership` R44's `NONE` for the viewer, the three byte-identical and also the answer to an unrecognised viewer (R8); at `EXISTENCE` the viewer receives `membership` R44's `PROJECT_SEEN_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` with the id and name only; only a viewer at `FULL` receives the answer. It never throws: a part it cannot read is stated undetermined (R7).
- **R2** (was `publication` R45) `stage` is one of State Rules §4.3's four, decided by the first rule that holds: (1) **`closed`**: the project's own document records `current_state: closed` with a `closed_reason` of `resolved`, `superseded` or `abandoned`, carried in `closed_reason`; this is the owner's recorded act, shown as recorded, and a reopening is the owner's act too, after which the stage is derived again (K362); (2) **`matured`**: the project's stance on at least one question it holds is `concluded` (`basis-versions` R22's `conclusionOf`, the project's own adoption, never a no-project conclusion), or the project owns at least one ratified case edition; (3) **`investigating`**: at least one question it holds has at least one leg in its basis; (4) **`forming`**: otherwise. A question the project **holds** is one `basis-versions` R41 answers for it, whatever the question's shared state (dismissed, deferred and divided included; a divided parent's children only once the project cites them), and a project-scoped set-aside (`queue` R27, a later module) does not remove it. `basis` is `{rule, question, case, edition}`, naming the rule that decided and the question or case edition that met it: for rule 2, the first held question read that the project has `concluded` (its stance as `basis-versions` R41 answers it), else the first ratified case edition it owns by case id, at its first ratified edition. The document's recorded `current_state` enters only through rule 1: `forming`, `investigating` or `matured` written there is never read. `questions` counts the held questions read, those with a leg and those the project has `concluded`. Held questions are read in pages of 500, at most 2,000 per call, stopping once rule 2 is met; reaching the cap with rules 1–2 unmet answers `stage: "undetermined"` with `at_least` (`investigating` or `forming`, what was established) and `questions.truncated: true`, never filled in (R7). A read of the held questions that fails still asks rule 2's second half: a project owning a ratified case edition is `matured`, with that edition as `basis`; only with none is it `undetermined` with `at_least`. A document recording `current_state: closed` without a recognised `closed_reason` does not meet rule 1, and R4's `closed` entry says so.
- **R3** (was `publication` R46) Each case the project owns (its `cases.project_id`, or, for a case with no `cases` row, its stored unsigned document (`publication` R21) naming the project as `case_project`; in case id order, at most 200 (`work_products_limit`), with `work_products_truncated`) is one **work product** (DEC-72: a case is a production of a project), answered as `{case, readiness, editions, latest_edition, rungs}`. `rungs` states each rung of State Rules §4.3's ladder by its own condition (K364): `draft`, an unsigned case document of the case is stored (`publication` R21); `internally_checked` and `externally_compliant`, `{met: false, why: "no evaluation is recorded"}` while no Compliance or Argument Evaluation is recorded for it, never inferred from a signature or a gate; `distributed`, at least one ratified edition (a ratified case edition is the distribution record), with `editions` the count and `latest_edition` the highest. A work product's `readiness` is the highest rung whose own condition is met, or `none` when none is (a signed edition not yet ratified, with no unsigned document left), so a published case is `distributed` and never `draft`; a ratified edition is one whose `published_cases.ratified_at` is set (as `publication` R37, R41, R43). The project's `readiness` is `absent` when it has no work product, else the highest any work product has reached (`none` when all read `none`); `published_editions` counts its ratified case editions. No answer here composes a strength (R6).
- **R4** (was `publication` R49) `stages` lists State Rules §4.3's four in order (`forming`, `investigating`, `matured`, `closed`), each as `{stage, reached, earned, since, needs, why}`. It is produced in the same evaluation of R2's rules that decides `stage`, never by a second reading of the record.
  - **The three computed stages.** `reached` is true for the decided stage and each stage before it, and false for each stage after it. `earned` names what met that stage's own rule where the read found it (`{question}`, or `{case, edition}` as in `basis`), else null. `since` is, for a reached stage, the instant of the evidence that earns it now (the earliest current leg, the conclusion, or the ratified edition), and null for a skipped stage (reached, `earned: null`) and for a stage not reached; after a withdrawal it moves to the evidence that earns the stage then (K448).
  - **What a stage not reached needs.** `needs` is null for a reached stage. For a computed stage not reached, it is `{any_of: [{condition, have}]}`, and `why` is one fixed sentence per case.
    - `investigating`: `held_question_with_leg`, `have` = `questions.with_legs`. The `why` says whether the project holds no question yet, or holds some and none has a leg in its basis.
    - `matured`: `concluded_held_question` (`have` = `questions.concluded`) and `ratified_case_edition` (`have` = `published_editions`).
  - **No promise.** Any one listed condition, met with nothing else changed, makes R2 decide that stage or a later one. `needs` never lists an input R2 does not count: a conclusion made without the project, a severed citation, a signed edition not ratified, the document's own `current_state`.
  - **`closed`.** `reached` is true only by rule 1, and `earned` is `{closed_reason}`. Its `since` is the instant the document recorded the close, as the store holds it, else null (K452). When false, `needs` is null and `why` says a close is the owner's recorded act with its reason (resolved, superseded or abandoned), not a stage the record grows into. When the document records `current_state: closed` without a recognised reason, `recorded` holds the reason as written (null if absent, cut to 40 characters) and `why` says the close is not read because its reason is not one of the three.
  - **When `stage` is `closed`.** Rules 2–3 are still evaluated, bounded as R2 states, and the three computed stages are stated as above. Every `needs` is null, with `why` saying the project is closed and a reopening is the owner's act. So the answer shows how far the work had come, and a close never reads as the last bar of a finished stack (K448).
  - **When `stage` is `undetermined`.** Stages up to and including `at_least` are reached. Each stage above it has `reached: null` and `needs: null`, and its `why` is the answer's `detail`. With no `at_least` (the project's document was not read), all four have `reached: null`.
  - Every `why` is a fixed sentence that states counts only. It never names a member, a question's text or a place (R9).

- **R5** (was `publication` R47) A project's stage and its work products' readiness are computed at the read from the record, never stored or set: no table or column holds them, no op writes them, and `projectStage` (R1) is their only answer.
