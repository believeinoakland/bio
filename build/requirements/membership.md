## Public

### Purpose

Owns who the members are and what each may do: the founder, administrators, invitations and enrolment, capabilities, declared expertise; and projects as working groups at their setup: inviting, joining, leaving and removing participants, the visibility setting, the participation record, what a caller may see of a project, and the fence that says who may act in one. The roster's reads, ownership votes and rescue, the directory and requests to join are `project-roster`'s. It holds no integrity of the record (Membership v2 §2), and no credential: sessions and passwords, signer keys and AI credentials are `credentials`'.

### Provides

Terms. *Administrators* are the founder (the root of trust's session, id `admin`, once the instance is claimed) and every member with role `admin` and status `active`. An act's `by` is the actor the control plane stamps, never the caller's claim. *Sight* of a project is `FULL`, `EXISTENCE` or `NONE` (R44). Every service that names a project answers a caller at `EXISTENCE` with `PROJECT_SEEN_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (R44) and a caller at `NONE` exactly as an id that names nothing, before any other check; a `viewer` never sent (an internal caller) is not asked. Every refusal names its reason; no service throws.

- **R14** An administrator while two or more exist is created `proposed`; `by`'s own endorsement is recorded if `by` is an administrator; the answer is `CONSENSUS_REQUIRED` and no invitation exists until R6 completes.
- **R15** `inviteLook({invite})` returns `{cover, role, capabilities, expertise}` for a live invitation and never the member id. A spent invitation and one that never existed answer the identical `NO_SUCH_INVITATION`.

- **R17** `memberList({administer})` lists every member with handle, role, status, `status_by`, `invited_by` (each `not recorded` when never stamped), capabilities and expertise as R24 answers it. `cover` is present only when the control plane stamped `administer: true`; otherwise the key is absent.
- **R18** Each row of an administrator's roster lists the projects that member participates in.
- **R19** Whether a member's cover-and-handle pairing is published is a per-member setting either the member or an administrator may change.
- **R20** `memberSet({memberId, status, by})`: `NOT_AN_ADMIN` as R12; `BAD_STATUS` unless `active` or `revoked`; `NO_SUCH_MEMBER`; `ADMIN_REQUIRES_VOTE` for revoking an administrator. - **R97** (DEC-133 (2)) Every invitation (R6, R13, R101, R104) expires `expiresInDays` whole days after it is made: 7 when not given, else a whole number from 1 to 30 the administrator chose (for R13 at `memberAdd`, for the doors at R99 and R102), anything else refused `BAD_EXPIRY`. - **R98** (DEC-133 (3)) `inviteWithdraw({memberId, by})`: `NOT_AN_ADMIN` (R84); `NO_SUCH_MEMBER`; `NO_UNUSED_INVITATION` unless the member is `invited` and the invitation unspent (live or expired). **The fence: who may act in a project**
- **R54** `isProjectOwner(projectId, memberId)` is true exactly for a participant with the owner flag; `isJoinedParticipant` for a participant `joined` or `leaving`.
- **R55** `projectAuthority(projectId, identity, need, act)` returns null or a refusal: `need: "owner"` refuses `PROJECT_ACT_NOT_THE_OWNER` to anyone but an owner; `need: "joined"` refuses `PROJECT_ACT_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` to anyone not joined or leaving.
