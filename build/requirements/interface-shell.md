## Public

### Purpose

The member interface's page and frame. It holds the member's session (R32), loads the member's words, draws the working frame and the path, routes among the screens the screen modules register, and gives every screen one way to read, to act, to explain and to refuse. Pure: it reads no table and writes nothing; every read and act is an op the plane serves, called from the browser.

### Provides

Terms. A **screen** is a registry screen (`docs/development/ux-substrate/screens/registry.json`, carried by `wizard-scripts` as `SCREEN_REGISTRY`, its R13). An **act** is a control that calls one op; a **read** is a call of a non-mutating op. **Words** are the interface's keyed words (R5). A **screen module** is one of `screens-settings`, `screens-intake`, `screens-daily`, `screens-inquiry`. The **phone width** is a viewport of 760 px or less (`civicsmith.css`' breakpoint). These terms are read by every screen module.

**The page: `interfacePage(screens)`**
- **R1** (fix round 3, C15, P2.4; K4353, K4356) `interfacePage(screens)` answers one HTML document: `<html lang>` the member's language once known (`en` before), one `<link rel="stylesheet" href="/app/assets/civicsmith.css">`, icons referenced from `/app/assets/icons.svg` (no inline copy of the sprite), and one `<script src>` loading the shell's script, composed from the shell's own functions and each registered screen's and held at an address that carries the release version, served unchanged for that release (`control-plane` R75); the module exports that script's source and its hash, which the page policy names in place of a nonce, and the page carries no inline script and no nonce slot. The page holds no member-facing text outside its words (R5) and no secret.
- **R2** (fix round 3, C15, P2.4; K4353, K4356; amends R1) The page names no resource on another origin in any `src`, `href`, `@import`, `url()` or fetch, and loads no typeface but `interface-assets`' (DEC-122 (3)). It has no inline event-handler attribute, no `javascript:` address and no script made from a string (`answer-envelope` R6). A test serves the page through the door and finds one script element, loading the shell's versioned script, and no inline script.

**The session** (held by the shell, R32)
- **R3** On load the page reads the session record `SESSION_STORE_KEY` names (R32). With none, or one whose expiry has passed, it goes to `/` (the interface's `front`, R32) and draws nothing else. With one, it sends `op=whoami` (`control-plane` R18); an answer without a member session (`session` false or `member` empty) is treated as none. A `401` answer to any request clears the record and goes to `/`. The session token travels only in each request's `Authorization: Bearer` header: never in an address, a query, a body, a link drawn or the browser's history (`admission` R20). A test drives every screen with a sentinel token and finds it in each request's header and nowhere else.
- **R4** The masthead's member initial opens a menu with "Sign out": it sends `op=signout`, then clears the session record and goes to `/`, whatever the answer.

**Refusals**
- **R13** A refusal (`ok: false` at the top level or in `result`, or an HTTP error) is drawn beside the act or read it answers, never in place of the screen: the word `refusal.<code>` (or a screen's own key for that code) when one is held; else the answer's `translation` (`answer-envelope` R2); else `state.failed` with the code. A form keeps what the member typed; nothing on the screen changes but the refusal. A test drives every act of every screen with a refusal of each kind and finds the words drawn and the form kept. (T51 fix round 2, C11; K4317) A refusal drawn beside an act is drawn again by every redraw, until the act is pressed again, something else is drawn in its slot, or the screen is left.

**Explanations** (DEC-159 to DEC-163, DEC-175, DEC-177)

- **R15** No explaining element sits inside another (DEC-177 (1)): a control or a mark with no label of its own absorbs those inside it, said after its own; a title naming a particular thing explains that thing first.
