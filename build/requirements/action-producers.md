## Public

### Purpose

The feed's Action-layer to-dos: each derives, on read and writing nothing, the items an action's clocks, reminders, holds, checkpoints, escalations, templates, local facts, watches and plan reviews earn for a viewer, routed to the member who takes the thing up or set it, never assigned to anybody; it names each item's subjects, home subjects and doors for `queue-producers` to answer in `feedItems` and for `queue` to home, offer, mint and publish.

### Provides

Terms. An **item**, a **home set**, the **depth bound** and a **case** are `queue-producers`' (its Terms). In the moved text (R1–R8), an id that names another requirement of this family is `queue-producers`' as numbered there: its moved ids read as this module's (R15 → R1, R16 → R2, R17 → R3, R18 → R4, R19 → R5, R20 → R6, R21 → R7, R29 → R8), and its R11, R12, R13, R24, R25, R28 and R36 as this module's copies R14–R20; R8 and R14 there stay `queue-producers`'. **Take up** is `plan-work`'s act (`itemTakeUp`, its R11): a member routes an item's to-dos to herself; nobody assigns anybody (v0.2 rule 18). **R1's rule** for recipients is R1's T45 sentence: the member who last took the thing up, then the member who created or set it, each only while an active member who may see it; else the project's active owners; else the administrators.
