## Public

### Purpose

Civicsmith's one outward-facing service for the AI catalogue and the AI tests. It publishes the signed catalogue and signed advisories that every team's copy pulls; serves a single-use challenge to a test container; receives the sealed results bundle a container makes, checks its envelope, and keeps it for Civicsmith's scorer; keeps what Civicsmith's scorer concludes as results, for every AI tested, listed or not, answers a sending team its own result by a token only that team holds, and sums the content-free health counts teams choose to send; and records Bob's decisions, without which nothing enters the catalogue. It **signs nothing, opens no sealed payload, stores no team's name unless the team agreed, and learns nothing about the copies that pull from it.**

### Provides

Terms. The **catalogue key** list is `signatures`' `CATALOGUE_KEYS` (R50 there; used by C-R33). The **operator** is a Civicsmith session or person holding the operator credential (R19); **Bob's act** is a decision he gave, recorded by a ruling reference. A **document** is a payload, an advisory or the seal key. `at` is the store's clock (R22).

**Publishing** (AIR33, AD25, AD11)
- **R1** (T53; AIR33, AD25, AD11) Three static, read-only addresses under one fixed host: `GET /ai/index.json`; `GET /ai/catalogue/<seq>.json` and `GET /ai/advisories/<seq>.json`, each immutable once published (the same bytes ever); `GET /ai/seal-key.json` (R8). Bytes are served exactly as stored, with `cache-control` public (immutable for numbered documents, `INDEX_MAX_AGE` for the index) and no cookie. Any other method or path is `404`/`405`, the dynamic routes aside (R9, R12, R25, R26). Test: a stored payload is served byte-identical and its SHA-256 equals the index's. Control: `POST /ai/index.json` is refused. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R2** (T53; AD25; the update check's rule, `instance-setup` R88: nothing identifying) A request to R1 leaves no record of its own: no address, user agent, count per requester, or log row in the store; no `set-cookie`; the answer does not vary by caller. Test: after many requests with different headers the store holds no new row and every answer is byte-equal. Control: a challenge or bundle request (R9, R12) does write its own rows. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R3** (T53; AIR33, AD25, AD11) `publishCatalogue({bytes, statement, armored, by})` (an operator act) stores a payload for serving only when, in order: `armored` verifies by `signatures`' `verifyAiCatalogue` (R53 there; namespace `NS_AI_CATALOGUE`, keys `CATALOGUE_KEYS`; its reasons pass through); the statement's SHA-256 and byte count equal those of `bytes` (`DIGEST_MISMATCH`); its `seq` is higher than every published `seq` (`NOT_NEWER`) and the same counter as advisories (R4); `ai-catalogue.validatePayload` holds (`CATALOGUE_PAYLOAD_INVALID` naming the path); and **every entry in it has a `list` decision (R6) for that `entry_id` and `version`** (`ENTRY_NOT_DECIDED` naming it). Acceptance is atomic and rebuilds the index (R5). Test: a payload signed with a throwaway key in the list is stored and served. Controls: one changed byte `BAD_SIGNATURE`; an entry with no decision `ENTRY_NOT_DECIDED`; an old `seq` `NOT_NEWER`. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R4** (T53; AIR24, AIR33, AD11) `publishAdvisory({advisory, armored, by})` stores an advisory only when it verifies by `verifyAiAdvisory` (`signatures` R53; namespace `NS_AI_ADVISORY`), its `seq` is higher than every published `seq`, its move is legal for the target's last published state (C-R24, else `ADVISORY_BAD_MOVE`), and its decision is as R7 states. Test: a `lapse` for a listed entry is stored. Control: a `retire` of a listed entry is `ADVISORY_BAD_MOVE`. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R5** (T53; AIR33, AD25) The index `{index_version, catalogue: {seq, schema_version, issued_at, sha256, bytes, path, armored}, advisories: [...]}` names the newest published payload and every advisory whose `seq` is higher than that payload's, each with its own signature; it is rebuilt only by R3 and R4 and holds no field the signed statements do not (it is not itself signed: a withheld or stale index delays a copy but cannot change it, since a copy checks each document). Paths are relative and match `^[a-z0-9._/-]+$`. Test: after a payload (seq 7) and two advisories (8, 9) the index names 7 and lists 8 and 9. Control: after payload 10 the advisories 8 and 9 are no longer listed. *(not yet met: T53)*

**Bob's decisions** (AD9, AD11, AD13)
- **R6** (T53; AD9, AD11, AD13) `decide({entry_id, version, decision, ruling_ref, by})` (operator act; append-only) records `{entry_id, version, decision, ruling_ref, by, at}`; `decision` is `list`, `hold`, `lapse`, `retire` or `clear`; `ruling_ref` matches `^K[0-9]+$` and is required; a decision is never edited, only superseded by a later one. R3 reads the latest decision per `(entry_id, version)`. Test: `list` then `hold` leaves the entry undecided for R3. Control: a decision with no `ruling_ref` is refused `DECISION_NO_RULING`. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R7** (T53; AIR24, AD9, AD11, AD13; K5481: the standing rule, AQ7 B) An advisory that **narrows** (`watch`, `lapse`, `retire`) is accepted with a `ruling_ref` of the standing rule that lets it and a `reason` from `ai-catalogue`'s `ADVISORY_REASONS` (C-R26): the one closed list, `deprecation`, `canary_drift`, `served_mismatch`, `team_divergence`, `health`, `terms_change`, `other`, read from there and never copied here; a reason outside it is refused `ADVISORY_REASON_UNKNOWN`. The standing rule covers every reason but `other`; a narrowing advisory with `other`, one that **widens** (`clear`, or a payload listing a lapsed entry again), is accepted only with a `list` or `clear` decision (R6) naming that act's own ruling. Every narrowing advisory is also appended to a list the operator reads for reporting to Bob (`advisoriesSince({seq})`). Test: a `watch` with the standing ref and `canary_drift` is stored. Controls: a `clear` without its own decision is `ENTRY_NOT_DECIDED`; `other` with only the standing ref is `ENTRY_NOT_DECIDED`; `reason: "bogus"` is `ADVISORY_REASON_UNKNOWN`. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R8** (T53; SR62) `GET /ai/seal-key.json` answers `{key_id, public_key, not_before, not_after, seq, armored}`: the public key containers seal to, signed in `NS_AI_CATALOGUE` over `signatures`' seal-key statement (R54 there) so a container can check it with `verifyAiSealKey` against `CATALOGUE_KEYS` and a compromised address cannot swap it. `publishSealKey` is an operator act with that verification; a retired `key_id` keeps its document. The matching private key is **not** held here (R15). Test: a container-side verification accepts the document. Control: the document signed by a key outside the list is not published. *(not yet met: T53)*

**Challenges** (AIR15, SR80, AD8)
- **R9** (T53; AIR15, SR80, AD8) `POST /ai/challenge` `{provider, plan, entry_id?, declared_model?, image_digest, bank_version, run_public_key}` answers `{challenge_id, nonce, bank_version, selection_seed, issued_at, expires_at}`: a nonce of at least `NONCE_BITS` random bits, single use, and a seed from which the container draws its items from the signed bank it holds, so **no answer, item text or expected result is ever sent or held here**. It records which nonce went to which declared provider, plan and digest. It accepts no team name; `declared_team` is not a field. The request is refused `ISSUER_DIGEST_UNKNOWN` unless `image_digest` is in the registry (R11), `ISSUER_RATE_LIMITED` past the caps (R10), `ISSUER_BAD_REQUEST` naming the field otherwise. Test: a request with a released digest answers a nonce, recorded with its provider and plan. Controls: an unreleased digest is refused; two requests never answer the same nonce. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R10** (T53; AIR15, SR80, AD8) A nonce expires `NONCE_LIFE` after issue (a full run takes time); the number outstanding (`NONCE_OUTSTANDING_MAX`) and the number issued per day (`NONCE_DAILY_MAX`) are capped and past them the issuer answers `ISSUER_RATE_LIMITED`, issuing none. Expired and used nonces are kept as rows (R12 reads them). Test: the cap-plus-one request is refused and mints nothing. Control: after expiries free the cap a request succeeds. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R11** (T53; AIR15, AD8) `registerDigest({image_digest, release, ruling_ref, by})` and `withdrawDigest` (operator acts, append-only) maintain the registry of container image digests Civicsmith released; a digest is `sha256:` plus 64 lowercase hex. Test: a registered digest passes R9; a withdrawn one fails. Control: a malformed digest is refused. *(not yet met: T53)*

**Bundles** (AIR16, SR60–SR64, AD8)
- **R12** (T53; AIR16, SR60–SR64, AD8) `POST /ai/bundle` takes one bundle: a plaintext manifest and a sealed payload (the container's format, SR60–SR62). It checks the **envelope** only, in order, each failure answering as R13 states and the bundle kept as R13 and R14 state: size within `BUNDLE_MAX_BYTES` (`BUNDLE_TOO_LARGE`, refused); manifest schema and version readable (`rejected`, `BUNDLE_UNREADABLE`); the nonce was issued by this issuer (`NONCE_UNKNOWN`), is unexpired (`NONCE_EXPIRED`) and is **marked used in the same atomic step** (`NONCE_REUSED`); the image digest equals the one the nonce was issued for and is registered (`IMAGE_DIGEST_MISMATCH`); the sealed payload's SHA-256 equals the manifest's; the signature by the run key, whose public half the nonce was issued against, verifies by `signatures`' `verifyRunBundle` (R56 there; namespace `NS_AI_RUN`) over manifest and payload hash (`BAD_SIGNATURE`, or `UNKNOWN_KEY` for another key); and the manifest lists no field outside the manifest schema. Test: a well-formed bundle from a throwaway run key answers `verified_envelope`. Controls, one each: a reused nonce, an unissued nonce, a changed manifest character, an unknown digest. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R13** (T53; AIR16, SR64; K5482) The reply is `{status, reason?, receipt_id, result_token?}`, `status` one of `verified_envelope`, `unverified`, `rejected`; never "failed" (SR64). A kept bundle's reply carries `result_token`, at least `NONCE_BITS` random bits, given once; the issuer stores only its SHA-256 (R25). `unverified` bundles are kept for an operator to read but produce no result (R16). `rejected` bundles are not stored. Test: each failure of R12 answers `unverified` or `rejected` as stated and never `verified_envelope`. Control: only a bundle passing every check answers `verified_envelope`. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R14** (T53; AD15) What is stored of a bundle is its sealed payload (opaque), its manifest **redacted to the manifest schema**, the receipt, the envelope verdict and its time. `declared_team` and any other field naming a team, member or address are dropped before storing **unless** the manifest also carries `agree_named: true`, which is inside the signed manifest so the container's run key vouches for it; the request's address, headers and user agent are never stored. Test: a manifest with `declared_team` and no `agree_named` is stored without it. Control: with `agree_named: true` the stored manifest keeps the name and the result row may name the team (R17). *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R15** (T53; AIR16, SR62, AD15) The issuer holds **no private key that opens a payload, and no key that signs the catalogue**. It holds only public keys (R3, R8) and one operator credential (R19). Opening, secret scanning and scoring happen in Civicsmith's scorer (`ai-gate`) where the seal private key is kept; the issuer hands it sealed bundles (R16). A bundle later found to carry a secret is destroyed (R18) and its sender cannot be told by the issuer (it keeps no address): the receipt id is the way a team asks. Test: no export, binding or table of the Worker holds a key marked private. Control: a planted private key in a fixture environment is refused at start. *(not yet met: T53)*

**Results** (AIR36, AD12, AD15, SR81–SR83)
- **R16** (T53; AIR36, AD12, SR81–SR83) `bundlesToScore({after?, limit?})` (operator act) lists `verified_envelope` bundles (receipt, manifest, sealed payload bytes) for the scorer; `recordResult({receipt_id | own_run, subject_id, parts, figures, speed, task_cost, suiteVersions, scorer_version, tested_at, by})` stores the scorer's conclusion with grade `REPORTED` for a receipt and `REPRODUCED` only for `own_run` (a Civicsmith run on its own account, with `run_ref`). `subject_id` is the entry's id, or for an AI not in the catalogue the tested AI's id (`ai-catalogue` R54) that the operator gives from the declared and served provider, host, model and version, one id per AI (K5482: listed or not). The scorer records each `verified_envelope` bundle within `SCORE_WITHIN` of receipt. A result for a bundle not `verified_envelope` is refused `RESULT_REFUSED`, and so is a second result for a receipt unless its `scorer_version` or `suiteVersions` differ (a re-score, which supersedes the earlier one; append-only, latest wins). Test: a scored bundle stores a `REPORTED` row; a canary run stores `REPRODUCED`; a re-score under a new scorer supersedes. Controls: a result for an `unverified` bundle is refused; `REPRODUCED` for a receipt is refused; the same result twice is refused. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R17** (T53; AIR36, AD12, AD15, SR83; K5482) `libraryRowsFor({subject_id, at})` and `combinedFor({subject_id, at})` (operator acts, for building the signed payload) answer, for any subject, listed or not, the rows of its **active** results (`tested_at` less than `RESULT_ACTIVE_MONTHS` before `at`, Bob's six months) in `ai-catalogue`'s library schema (C-R43, C-R44), and their combined records by `ai-gate.combineResults` (C-R55); a superseded result is never returned. Rows carry `tested_by` `civicsmith` or `team`, the grade, `repeated_by_civicsmith`, the date; `team_name` and `consent_ref` only for a bundle whose manifest carried `agree_named` (R14); no transcript, exchange, address or team id exists in the issuer to return. A team's agreement to be named applies to rows built after it is withdrawn (`withdrawName({receipt_id})`, by the operator on the team's request); rows already in a signed payload change at the next payload. Test: a result from an unnamed bundle yields a row saying only `team`. Controls: a named one carries its name; after `withdrawName` the next call omits it. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R18** (T53; AD15, SR63) `destroyBundle({receipt_id, reason, by})` (operator act) deletes the sealed payload and the redacted manifest, keeps a tombstone `{receipt_id, reason, at}`, and removes any unpublished result from it. A scored bundle is kept only to re-score, never shown (K5482), and is destroyed the same way, by the store's daily sweep, once neither holds: its result is active (R17), or its subject is listed (last published state `listed` or `watch`) or left that state less than `LISTED_TAIL_MONTHS` (Bob's one year) ago. Test: a bundle of an unlisted AI tested seven months ago is destroyed by the sweep and its tombstone remains; one of an AI lapsed eleven months ago is kept. Control: the results of other bundles are untouched, and a bundle of a listed AI tested two years ago is kept. *(not yet met: T53)*

**Operator door and availability**
- **R19** (T53; AD11, AD25) Every act named above (`publish*`, `decide`, `registerDigest`, `withdrawDigest`, `bundlesToScore`, `recordResult`, `libraryRowsFor`, `combinedFor`, `healthFor`, `withdrawName`, `destroyBundle`, `advisoriesSince`) is behind one operator credential, refused `ISSUER_NOT_AUTHORISED` with nothing done and no hint which part failed; each act appends `{act, by, at, ruling_ref?, outcome}` to an audit that no act edits. The credential is never printed, logged or returned. Test: an act without it is refused and appends only its refusal. Control: with it the act is audited with `by`. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R20** (T53; AD25) The three public routes (R1) depend on stored documents only, never on the dynamic store: with the store unavailable the index, payloads, advisories and seal key are still served, and `challenge`, `bundle`, `result` and `health` answer `ISSUER_UNAVAILABLE` (a copy's last good catalogue and a team's retry are the fallbacks). Test: with the store stubbed to fail, `GET /ai/index.json` answers 200 and `POST /ai/challenge` answers `ISSUER_UNAVAILABLE`. Control: with it healthy both succeed. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R25** (T53; AD15, SR83; K5482: "a team sees its own sealed test result at once") `GET /ai/result/<result_token>` answers the sending team its own result, nobody else's: `{status: "pending"}` until the scorer records it (R16), then `{status: "scored", subject_id, parts, figures, speed, task_cost, suiteVersions, grade: "REPORTED", repeated_by_civicsmith, tested_at}` (the words say "reported by your team, not yet confirmed" until Civicsmith repeats it), or `{status: "destroyed", reason}` from the tombstone. It is found by the token's SHA-256 only; an unknown token answers `404`, the same for every caller; the request writes no row (as R2). Until it is scored the team's copy shows the container's preliminary figures (`ai-gate` R32). With the store unavailable it answers `ISSUER_UNAVAILABLE` (R20). Test: a token answers `pending`, then its scored result after R16. Control: another bundle's token never answers this result, and the store holds no row of the request. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R26** (T53; AD38, AIR24; K5487: "teams may opt in to send counts of errors and refusals, never their content") `POST /ai/health` takes one report (`ai-use` R33's shape): at most `HEALTH_MAX_BYTES`; `report_version` readable; `day` a past UTC day within `HEALTH_DAYS_MAX`; counts non-negative integers with `refused`, `silent` and the errors together at most `calls`; an `entry_id` not of a published entry is dropped. It **adds the counts into one aggregate row per (entry, day)**: `reports`, `calls`, `refused`, `silent`, and each error class. Nothing of the request is kept: no per-report row, address, header, user agent or time beyond the day (as R2). A malformed report answers `ISSUER_BAD_REQUEST` naming the field and adds nothing; past `HEALTH_DAILY_MAX` reports in a day it answers `ISSUER_RATE_LIMITED`. `healthFor({entry_id, from, to})` (operator act) answers the aggregate for `ai-gate`'s signal (R53 there). Test: two reports for one entry and day sum into one row with `reports: 2`, and the store holds no other row of either. Control: a report whose `refused` exceeds its `calls` adds nothing. *(not yet met: T53)*

### Suggestions

- **Shape.** One Worker; static documents from the Worker's asset or object store, the dynamic routes (R9, R12) on one SQLite-backed Durable Object so a nonce's single use is atomic; the operator door a separate path. The repository's `ai-issuer/` folder, deployed to the account `CLAUDE.md` names, never another.
- **The operator's tools** live outside the Worker: a script on an operator's machine builds a payload from the reviewed data files, signs it with the catalogue key (the way the release is signed), and calls R3; Bob's decision is a K ruling in the repository that `decide` cites.
- **Parameters** (proposed <value>; set at START, K5457): `INDEX_MAX_AGE` proposed a few minutes; `NONCE_BITS` proposed 128; `NONCE_LIFE` proposed 72 hours; `NONCE_OUTSTANDING_MAX` and `NONCE_DAILY_MAX` proposed at START from the first runs; `BUNDLE_MAX_BYTES` proposed at START against the host's request limit; `SCORE_WITHIN` proposed one day; `HEALTH_MAX_BYTES` proposed 64 KiB; `HEALTH_DAYS_MAX` as `ai-use`'s; `HEALTH_DAILY_MAX` proposed at START; `RESULT_ACTIVE_MONTHS` 6 and `LISTED_TAIL_MONTHS` 12 (Bob's, K5482, not proposed; the first read from `ai-catalogue`'s policy so one value serves both).
- **Tests.** Throwaway keys for catalogue, run and operator; one negative control per R; the container's bundle built by `test-support`'s fixture, not a live container.
