## Public

### Purpose

Where a member starts and works each day: the group's home, their queue, (T48) where else to go for a matter of her own, finding what the group holds and finding inside it, and asking. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

**`SCREENS`** (the screen contract, `interface-shell` R21; each sentence read by key, R34)

**home** (Home; the registry's acts `promote`, `startfrom`)
- **R1** Reads `queue` (the first items waiting on the member, each by its kind mark, under "Your queue (<n>)") and `projectdirectory` (each project the member may see, its question count and its stage ladder Forming, Investigating, Matured, Closed). Heading: the group's name and its description, from the public `groupidentity` read. Act: `promote` "Start a project" (a new project, as `store-request` R11 and `promotion` take it: its title, the member its owner), opening `project` on success. `startfrom` is not drawn in T46 (no runner can yet start what it lists).

**queue** (Queue; the registry's 11 acts; DEC-110, DEC-131)
- **R2** Reads `queue` with the member's chosen sort (time added, time due, case, kind; `queue` R49) and grouping by case, drawing each item's kind by `mark.kind.<k>`, its summary and detail (the `queue.<kind>.*` words where keyed, else the item's own `summary` and `detail`), due dates with `mark.due`, and hints with `mark.hint` opening in place (what was matched, how, the false-alarm rate, what it is not). The three-kinds note is shown once per member on the device.
- **R3** An item's acts are the item's own decorated `options` (`queue` R17), drawn when the op is one of the screen's registered acts (`taskresolve`, `taskforward`, `proposedispose` with a reason, `queuesnooze` with a date, `queuemute`, `adoptversion`, `keepversion`, `reminderanswer`, `promote`, `projectkeyset`, `exploreapprove`); any other option is drawn as "Open", a link to its subject's screen. An item resolved or muted leaves the list on the answer. (T51 K4265) `release` and `filingapprove` are registered acts of Home and the queue; a letter's item draws `filingapprove` ("Read and approve it"): `filingsfor {action}`, then `filingapprove {filing}`.

**finder** (Find; the registry's 6 acts)
- **R4** `search` with the member's words, each result with its grade and where it matched (`mark.origin.search`); `select` holds picked results together (the hold strip shows how many documents are held together and until when, from `selection`); `selectionrelease`; `findin` ("Find in these", opening `find-in` over the selection); `frontier` draws `mark.gap.nobody` where nobody looked; `calculationcreate` "Count" over the held set.

**find-in** (Find in this; the registry's 9 acts; DEC-164; N698)
- **R5** The scope is set by where it was opened (a document, the held set, a project) and shown under "Look in". The member ticks what to find and an optional term and question; `findin` answers results by kind, each with its words as written, its passage and `mark.origin.search`; a kind with none says "Nothing here" (`state.empty`). Each result offers only the recording act of its kind (`recordpersonfact`, `recorddatedfact`, `standarddeclare`, `recordfact`, `tabledeclare`, `cite`), the chosen question kept beside it; a result already recorded shows who recorded it. `standingquestionset` keeps finding over the scope; `search` is "See all in Find". Finding records nothing (K1468).

**answers** (Find › Ask; the registry's acts `ruleanswer`, `search`, `standingquestionset`, `standingquestionend`; "Ask" re-pointed to `ask`)
- **R6** While the assistant is reachable (`interface-shell` R20): "Ask" sends the question through `op=ask` (`agent-worker` R54's answer: summary, sentences with their support, looks, its `label` machine work), drawn with each holding as a link to its passage and the looks as where it looked; "Search instead" is `search`. While not reachable: the screen is "Find and count" (`search`, and `ruleanswer` "Show how this was counted"). In both, `standingquestionset` and `standingquestionend`, with "New matches will arrive in your queue as a list". `AI_*` and `ASSISTANT_OFF` refusals by their words (`ai.refused.*`). (K5131) While not reachable, the search the screen sends is one `retrieval` admits: never an `OR` joining a `passage:` route to a fields route (`retrieval` R88's `ROUTES_CROSSED`); and "Show how this was counted" (`ruleanswer`) is drawn only while `answers`' rule services are on (`answers` R7's switch, read by its state read); while off it is not drawn.
