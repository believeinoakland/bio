## Public

### Purpose

The record store's assembly and the world its tests ask. It builds every module of the record store on one Durable Object's storage, in the modules' order; migrates them before any request; composes their registrations and ops maps into the one route map; passes every store request through `store-door`'s frame; and publishes `storeWorld()`, the real store with a pinned clock, a real claim, and a copy for each test. It imports no platform module, so it runs in plain node; `plane` hosts it as the Durable Object class and the Worker's entry.

### Provides

Terms. The **assembled store** is what `assembleStore` answers (R1). The **owner map** is the arms of the ops whose owners export a service and no ops map (R86). A **world** is what `storeWorld()` answers (R90); `store-request` adds the door, the sign-ins and the recipes over it. The store's request from a page's is made by `store-request`'s `innerRequest` (K5052 (1)).
