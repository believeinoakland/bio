## Public

### Purpose

The page a group first meets at the root of its Civicsmith: claim, sign-in, enrolment, the healthy panel, the group's settings sections (Places, offices, members and keys, the assistant) and the record browser kept working (K102). Pure: it reads no table, writes nothing and registers nothing; the page drives the plane's public and member ops from the browser, and `instance-setup` composes it (R47's block in R14's slot) and serves it through `control-plane`'s `/` route.

### Provides

**The page and its group line: `pageOf(read)`, `groupLine(read)`, `PAGE_HTML`, `HOSTING_SLOT`, `GROUP_LINE_UNREAD`**
- **R1** (was `instance-setup` R20) The page carries one group line, from one read `{answered, result}`: *recorded* (the display name and `·` when given, the slug, the words "your group's Civicsmith" (in place of "group instance", DEC-149; T34), and the domain with its date only when `domain_verified_at` is a dated string); *none* (the read answered `group: null`); *unread* (anything else, never "none"). Every value is HTML-escaped. The template carries the unread line, so a page served without a read says it did not read.
- **R2** (was `instance-setup` R21) The page removes the URL fragment on load. `#boot=` pre-fills the one-time password; `#invite=` asks `op=invitelook` and shows enrolment, or states that the link is not live and hides the form. `#join=` (a join link, `membership` R102–R104; DEC-133 (6)) asks the name the person chooses for the group to know them by, sends it with the link in the body of `op=joinlinkinvite`, and on success shows enrolment for the invitation answered, as `#invite=` does; on a refusal it states that the link is not live (`NO_SUCH_JOIN_LINK`) or that the group's daily limit is reached (`JOIN_LINK_DAILY_CAP`) and hides the form. The link never leaves the fragment except in that body.
- **R3** (was `instance-setup` R22) It drives only the public ops `bootstrap`, `claim`, `login`, `invitelook`, `joinlinkinvite` (R2; T34) and `enroll` before sign-in; a claim needs a password of at least 12 characters typed twice alike. Enrolment, reached from an invitation (`#invite=`) or the join link (`#join=`, R2), likewise asks the new password twice: `op=enroll` is sent only when the two are alike, and while they differ the page says so and sends nothing (U77).
- **R4** (was `instance-setup` R23) After sign-in it offers only the acts `op=whoami` reports the session holds, and the members and keys section only to a session that administers.
- **R5** (was `instance-setup` R24) Its intake form offers the catalogue's own first states, headings and risk tiers; for an action, a named counterparty in the shape `actions` R9 requires (`{state: named, role, body, level?}`: the counterparty's role and the body it belongs to, never a bare name) or "not determined yet" with a basis, and a risk tier, with nothing preselected (an untouched tier is written `undetermined`).
- **R6** (was `instance-setup` R25) A bundle's history is listed in write order when every entry carries a distinct integer `seq`, else by snap key, and the page says which order it shows.
- **R7** (was `instance-setup` R32) The risk tiers R5's form offers, and the tier it writes, are `action-grammar`'s `RISK_TIERS` and `riskTierState` (its R1), never a copy; the page states `action_kind: other`, a product kind (actions R10), and offers no other kind.
- **R8** (was `instance-setup` R44; D-605, N397, K605) The members and keys section's key form sends `op=signeradd` the pasted public-key line split as membership R25 takes it: when the trimmed text's first whitespace-separated token is `ssh-ed25519` and a second follows, `keyB64` is that second token and `comment` is the tokens after it joined by single spaces, absent when there are none; any other text is sent trimmed, as pasted, as `keyB64`, and the plane says what it makes of it. `memberId` is the named member, trimmed and lower-cased.
- **R9** (was `instance-setup` R45; D-62, N397, K605) The intake form's bundle writer writes an information bundle's `content_hash: sha256:<hex>`, the captured document's own digest, whenever a document was captured, and none for typed intake, so a document bundle meets C-2.7's `content_hash` at `verified`.
- **R10** (was `instance-setup` R46; D-110, D-97, N397, K605) The intake form states each capture refusal `acquire` makes (`BAD_LOCATOR`, `SOURCE_REFUSED`, `FETCH_FAILED`, `EMPTY`, `TOO_LARGE`) in its own sentence and any other reason as "could not be captured: <code>"; it explains no refusal the plane no longer makes (no `NO_AUTHORITY` sentence).
- **R11** (was `instance-setup` R48; DEC-88 (2); capture R32; K1037) The inbox section's resolve, to `pulled` or `discarded`, asks the member for their reason before it posts `op=inboxresolve`, and sends it as `reason` with the knock and the status; when the plane refuses (`RESOLVE_NO_REASON` among its refusals), the page shows the refusal's own words and the knock is left as it was. Nothing else of the section changes (the record browser is a placeholder kept working, K102).
- **R12** (was `instance-setup` R49; DEC-122 (3)) The page (R1's page with its enrolment and record-browser sections) names no resource on another origin in any `src`, `href`, `@import`, `url()` or script fetch, and uses only typefaces installed on the device or bundled with the plane. A link the reader follows is not a load.
- **R13** (was `instance-setup` R15) The page shows the active profiles by name, and offers an administrator the choice among every held non-test profile, none preselected (`op=profilesset`, `instance-setup` R14); before a change is sent, it warns that local facts will read differently from then on, and choosing none is allowed and stated.

**The claim section** (DEC-109, DEC-134, DEC-136 (1); K1755)
- **R14** (was `instance-setup` R47, its page share; DEC-109; H17) The claim section carries, before the founder chooses a password and in place of the reassurance-only card, exactly one slot (`HOSTING_SLOT`) where `instance-setup` places R47's block when it composes the page. Nothing here asks for or records an acknowledgement.
- **R15** (was `instance-setup` R56; DEC-134 (3); `membership` R11) Once the claim succeeds, the page asks, in the same section, who holds the hosting account (one or more holders and an optional note) and records the answer through `op=hostingaccessset` (`membership` R11), stating `membership`'s refusal when it refuses (`NO_HOLDERS`). The founder may leave it unanswered; the members and keys section (R4) shows the current record (`hostingAccess`, or that none is recorded) and offers the same act to an administrator at any time. No administrator's addition asks it (`membership` R11).
- **R16** (was `instance-setup` R57; DEC-134 (2), (6)) In the same section, once the claim succeeds, the page recommends adding a second administrator, saying why (so the group is never stuck when one person is away, and no one person holds everything), and states that a group with one administrator depends on that person and on the hosting account. Both are shown only there, right after the claim succeeds: no other section, visit or later page shows either again. Nothing is asked or recorded, and nothing is gated: a group may run with one administrator (`membership` R13). The words are the design stream's (DEC-134, step 5); until they are given, the page says this meaning plainly.
- **R17** (was `instance-setup` R58; DEC-136 (1); `membership` R107) In the same section, once the claim succeeds, the page offers the choice whether members are told what a court can reach: the short explanation (some work is unlikely ever to draw a court order; journalists, lawyers and auditors may have protections others lack, such as shield laws or privilege; a group doing work that could draw one should make sure its members understand the risk), then "tell our members" or "don't", nothing preselected, sent as `op=courtnoticeset`. Leaving it unchosen records nothing (`membership` R107: unchosen reads as not telling). The members and keys section shows the current choice (`op=courtnotice`) and offers the same act to an administrator at any time.
- **R18** (was `instance-setup` R59; K1755) In the claim's section, as R15–R17, once the claim succeeds, the page offers the choice how the group's members reach the assistant, nothing preselected, whatever the installer recorded (`instance-setup` R53): **the group's API key** (one Anthropic API key, held by the group and serving every member who has no account of their own), **members' own accounts** (each member who wants it connects their own Claude subscription or API key), **both**, or **no AI** (the product without the assistant). Choosing any of the first three sets `instance-setup` R53's switch on (`op=assistantset`); choosing the group's key or both then asks for the key and sends it in the request's body only, through `op=groupkeyset` and `op=groupkeyswitch` with `on` true (`credentials` R33), stating `credentials`' refusal when it refuses and never showing the key again; choosing members' own accounts sets no key; choosing no AI sets R53's switch off. Each act is the claiming administrator's own and is appended as R53's and `credentials` R33's are. Leaving it unchosen records nothing and leaves R53 as it stands. The members and keys section shows the current state (`assistantState`, `op=groupkeystate`, never the key) and offers the same acts to an administrator at any time. The words are the design stream's; until they are given, the page says this meaning plainly.

**Places and offices** (DEC-150; N665)
- **R19** (was `instance-setup` R60, its page half; DEC-150 (1)) Under Places, the page (R13) offers an administrator "Name a place not yet listed" beside the held profiles, sends it as `op=placewanted` (`instance-setup` R60), and shows the named place, saying that administrators will be told when an update brings it.
- **R20** (was `instance-setup` R61; DEC-150 (2)) When no active profile names an office (`instance-setup` R50 seeds none), the page's offices section says that nothing is filled in because Civicsmith does not hold the group's place yet, names the place `instance-setup` R60 holds when one is named, and offers an administrator adding the group's offices itself through `op=entitycreate` (`entities`; kind `office`), each the administrator's own act, never machine-attributed and with no profile as its basis. Every office so added is shown marked as added by the group, wherever the section lists offices. The words are the design stream's; until they are given, the page says this meaning plainly.

**The words members see** (DEC-149; N664)
- **R21** (was `instance-setup` R63, its page share; DEC-149, Bob's "S4: B") Every member- or founder-facing string of this page (R1–R24 and their statements) calls the group's own Civicsmith "your group's Civicsmith", or is reworded so it needs no name, and never "copy", "instance", "plane" or "server" for it; a string answered to a reader with no credential says "this group's Civicsmith" (K1821 (2)). "Copy" keeps only its other meanings (a copy of a document, a public archive's own copy, copying a key or a line). Where "Civicsmith" alone could mean the software or the group's own, the sentence is reworded. Codes, op names, element ids, binding names and comments are not member-facing and stay. DEC-149's rows for this page (T34-87; `build/plan/draft-T34-dec149.md`, `bio-plane/src/setup.mjs` as numbered there) are :87, :115, :204, :208, :210, :219, :226, :240, :246, :256, :260, :286, :289, :304, :322, :329, :343, :376, :381, :455, :467, :476, :502, :594, :616, :828, :890 ("No keys are registered on this copy yet"), :1425, :1523, :1530, :1542, :1553, :1555, :1585, :1590 (35 rows: the setup page, the claim page, the healthy panel, the crumbs and the assistant switch's sentences), each applied where it lands in this module and named by a test.

**A member's language** (DEC-127 (1); N668)
- **R22** (was `instance-setup` R64, its page sentence) The enrolment section (R2, R3) offers the choice of language for the screens before `enroll`, starting from the device's setting, and sends `op=memberlanguageset` (`instance-setup` R64) once `enroll` has signed the new member in; the members-and-keys section and the account screen offer it at any time.

**The assistant on the page** (DEC-152; N667; K1755)
- **R23** (was `instance-setup` R65, its page half; DEC-152; K1837) On "Who your group is", while the assistant is on, the page offers the administrator the act of asking the assistant to help write the group's focus and purpose: it asks the design stream's few questions and sends the answers as `op=groupdescriptiondraft` (`instance-setup` R65); a draft answered is shown in the fields with its label ("Draft · the assistant's, asked by <handle>"), saved only when the administrator keeps it through `op=groupdescriptionset` (`membership` R109). Any refusal, `ASSISTANT_DRAFT_UNAVAILABLE` included (T34: the model turn is N686's), is stated in its own words and leaves the fields as they were. Without the assistant, the act is not offered and the page is unchanged.
- **R24** (was `instance-setup` R53, its page share) Every signed-in member is shown whether the assistant is on for your group's Civicsmith (`assistantState`), and when it was last set and by whom; only a session that administers is offered the switch (`op=assistantset`), which says what it will do before it is pressed. A read that did not answer says so and offers nothing.
