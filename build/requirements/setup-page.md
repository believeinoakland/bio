## Public

### Purpose

The page a group first meets at the root of its Civicsmith: claim, sign-in, enrolment, the healthy panel, the group's settings sections (Places, offices, members and keys, the assistant) and the record browser kept working (K102). Pure: it reads no table, writes nothing and registers nothing; the page drives the plane's public and member ops from the browser, and `instance-setup` composes it (R47's block in R14's slot) and serves it through `control-plane`'s `/` route.

### Provides

**The page and its group line: `pageOf(read)`, `groupLine(read)`, `PAGE_HTML`, `HOSTING_SLOT`, `GROUP_LINE_UNREAD`**
- **R1** (was `instance-setup` R20) The page carries one group line, from one read `{answered, result}`: *recorded* (the display name and `·` when given, the slug, the words "your group's Civicsmith" (in place of "group instance", DEC-149; T34), and the domain with its date only when `domain_verified_at` is a dated string); *none* (the read answered `group: null`); *unread* (anything else, never "none"). Every value is HTML-escaped. The template carries the unread line, so a page served without a read says it did not read.
- **R2** (was `instance-setup` R21) The page removes the URL fragment on load. `#boot=` pre-fills the one-time password; `#invite=` asks `op=invitelook` and shows enrolment, or states that the link is not live and hides the form. `#join=` (a join link, `membership` R102–R104; DEC-133 (6)) asks the name the person chooses for the group to know them by, sends it with the link in the body of `op=joinlinkinvite`, and on success shows enrolment for the invitation answered, as `#invite=` does; on a refusal it states that the link is not live (`NO_SUCH_JOIN_LINK`) or that the group's daily limit is reached (`JOIN_LINK_DAILY_CAP`) and hides the form. The link never leaves the fragment except in that body.
- **R3** (was `instance-setup` R22) It drives only the public ops `bootstrap`, `claim`, `login`, `invitelook`, `joinlinkinvite` (R2; T34) and `enroll` before sign-in; a claim needs a password of at least 12 characters typed twice alike. Enrolment, reached from an invitation (`#invite=`) or the join link (`#join=`, R2), likewise asks the new password twice: `op=enroll` is sent only when the two are alike, and while they differ the page says so and sends nothing (U77).
- **R4** (was `instance-setup` R23) After sign-in it offers only the acts `op=whoami` reports the session holds, and the members and keys section only to a session that administers.
- **R5** (was `instance-setup` R24) Its intake form offers the catalogue's own first states, headings and risk tiers; for an action, a named counterparty in the shape `actions` R9 requires (`{state: named, role, body, level?}`: the counterparty's role and the body it belongs to, never a bare name) or "not determined yet" with a basis, and a risk tier, with nothing preselected (an untouched tier is written `undetermined`).
- **R6** (was `instance-setup` R25) A bundle's history is listed in write order when every entry carries a distinct integer `seq`, else by snap key, and the page says which order it shows.
- **R7** (was `instance-setup` R32) The risk tiers R5's form offers, and the tier it writes, are `action-grammar`'s `RISK_TIERS` and `riskTierState` (its R1), never a copy; the page states `action_kind: other`, a product kind (actions R10), and offers no other kind.
- **R8** (was `instance-setup` R44; D-605, N397, K605) The members and keys section's key form sends `op=signeradd` the pasted public-key line split as membership R25 takes it: when the trimmed text's first whitespace-separated token is `ssh-ed25519` and a second follows, `keyB64` is that second token and `comment` is the tokens after it joined by single spaces, absent when there are none; any other text is sent trimmed, as pasted, as `keyB64`, and the plane says what it makes of it. `memberId` is the named member, trimmed and lower-cased.
- **R9** (was `instance-setup` R45; D-62, N397, K605) The intake form's bundle writer writes an information bundle's `content_hash: sha256:<hex>`, the captured document's own digest, whenever a document was captured, and none for typed intake, so a document bundle meets C-2.7's `content_hash` at `verified`.
- **R10** (was `instance-setup` R46; D-110, D-97, N397, K605) The intake form states each capture refusal `acquire` makes (`BAD_LOCATOR`, `SOURCE_REFUSED`, `FETCH_FAILED`, `EMPTY`, `TOO_LARGE`) in its own sentence and any other reason as "could not be captured: <code>"; it explains no refusal the plane no longer makes (no `NO_AUTHORITY` sentence).
- **R11** (was `instance-setup` R48; DEC-88 (2); capture R32; K1037) The inbox section's resolve, to `pulled` or `discarded`, asks the member for their reason before it posts `op=inboxresolve`, and sends it as `reason` with the knock and the status; when the plane refuses (`RESOLVE_NO_REASON` among its refusals), the page shows the refusal's own words and the knock is left as it was. Nothing else of the section changes (the record browser is a placeholder kept working, K102).
- **R12** (was `instance-setup` R49; DEC-122 (3)) The page (R1's page with its enrolment and record-browser sections) names no resource on another origin in any `src`, `href`, `@import`, `url()` or script fetch, and uses only typefaces installed on the device or bundled with the plane. **The session, the operator token, the page policy and recovery codes** (T35; F1, F10, F17, K1874, K1881; K1888)
- **R26** (F1; K1874) The session token leaves every address the page makes: every request the page sends under a session (`op=stats` on load, the record reads, the acts it posts) carries the token only in its `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header (`admission` R20), never in its address, its query or an `href` or `src`, and never in a body. A captured file's download (the record browser's \"download\") is a fetch with that header whose bytes the page hands to the browser as a file (a `Blob` behind a short-lived object address, released once the download starts); no link the page writes, copies or leaves in the browser's history carries a session token. A `401` answer signs the page out as today. A test drives every request the page makes with a sentinel token and finds it in no address and in each request's header.
- **R27** (F10; K1874 (Q6): DEC-2 is not reopened) The page carries a guide, reached from R14's block (its link) and from the members and keys section, to replacing the one-time password (`ADMIN_TOKEN`, the root of trust's credential) in the hosting account, in plain steps: sign in to the hosting account (Cloudflare); make a new long random value (a password manager's generator; at least 32 characters); open your group's Civicsmith's Worker, its settings' secrets, and replace `ADMIN_TOKEN` with the new value; then at once claim your group's Civicsmith again with the new value and choose the founder's password, because a replaced one-time password lets whoever holds the new value claim it (`credentials` R1); keep the new value where the hosting account's sign-in is kept, never in a message or a shared document. It says when to do it (the value may have been seen; someone who held the hosting account leaves; after any use of it but the claim) and what does not change (the record, the members, their passwords and keys). It is part of the page (R12: no other origin is loaded; a link to the hosting provider's own sign-in is a link, not a load), holds no secret and reads nothing. The words are the design stream's; until they are given, the page says this meaning plainly.
- **R28** (F17; `answer-envelope` R6 as T35 amends it) Every `<script>` element of the page carries the nonce of the response that serves it: the template holds one slot, `NONCE_SLOT`, in each script element's `nonce` attribute, filled by the door per response, so the page runs under a policy that allows no inline script without that nonce. The page has no inline event-handler attribute (`onclick=` and the like), no `javascript:` address and no script made from a string, so nothing of it needs `'unsafe-inline'`. A test serves the page through the door and finds every script element carrying the nonce the response's policy names, and no inline handler.
- **R29** (K1888; `credentials` R46) Once the claim succeeds and the founder is signed in (R3), in the claim's section before R15–R18, the page issues the founder's recovery codes (`op=recoverycodesissue`, `credentials` R46) and shows the ten codes once, saying what they are for (each sets a new password if the founder's is lost, `op=recover`, `credentials` R47; each works once), that they will never be shown again, and to keep them apart from the password and the hosting account's sign-in. A refusal is stated in `credentials`' words and the section goes on. The members and keys section offers every administrator `op=recoverycodesissue` for their own role at any time, saying that issuing again spends the earlier codes, and shows `op=recoverycodesstate`'s `{held, remaining, issuedAt}`, never a code. The words are the design stream's; until they are given, the page says this meaning plainly.
