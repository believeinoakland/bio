## Public

### Purpose

The calculation (`CALC-`): numbers a finding can rest on, computed only by `calc-grammar`'s closed recipe grammar over declared canonical tables, money facts, cited figures and counts over the record, with denominators, grade facts and a method, stored by key and recomputed at acceptance, at publication and in the checker, never on a read. It holds the tables, the money ingest writer (money facts from an adopted table binding, only at a member's request), the recorded random draw, fact-based rankings and the machine's patterns (held in the hypothesis layer, shown only after a measured false-alarm rate). A total is a `CALC-` and is never re-entered as a money fact; `compare` yields a labelled computed fact, never "breach".

### Provides

Terms. A **recipe** is `calc-grammar`'s (`bio-calc/1`), evaluated only by it. A **result key** is `sha(recipe, inputs, method_version)`. An **input** is one of: a declared table (by its canonical sha256), money facts (`MNY-`), a cited figure (a content extent `calc-grammar`'s figure parser reads), another `CALC-`, or a frozen record set (R21). A **viewer** is the control plane's stamp; it fails closed when absent. Every refusal is `{ok: false, reason, detail}`; one with a catalogue row carries `code`, `check` and `translation`. Every write is stamped by the control plane.

**Tables: declareTable({source, schema, header, roles?, vintage?, by}), readTable({sha, viewer}), tablesAt({key, at, viewer})** (`op=tabledeclare`, `op=table`)
- **R1** `declareTable` takes a captured source (a content extent of a CSV, a workbook range or a portal export, `content`) and a Table Schema with a declared header, and holds the table as canonical RFC 4180 UTF-8 CSV plus its schema, keyed by the canonical bytes' sha256, its bytes in the evidence store, streamed. A value that does not parse as its column's declared type is held `undetermined`, never coerced. Refusals: `NO_SOURCE`, the extent's refusal, `NO_HEADER`, `BAD_SCHEMA` (naming the field), `TABLE_TOO_LARGE` (over 20 MiB or 1,000,000 cells, naming the bound; K1576's 500,000 lifted by N571: a table is evaluated as `calc-grammar`'s streamed table, its R22, within a Worker's heap).
- **R2** A column may carry a role from closed lists: money roles (`amount`, `payer`, `payee`, `fund`, `account`, `period`, `kind`, `phase`, `stage`, `basis`, `currency`), person and entity keys (each naming the id space or captured crosswalk it resolves through), and `roster` roles (person key, organisation, post, period). A declaration is the member's; a machine may not declare one (K1468).
- **R3** A table may carry a vintage, a validity (`civil-time`) under a key the member names; `tablesAt` answers the vintage valid at `at` through `civil-time.validAt`, or `undetermined` with the reason, never the latest by default.

**Calculations: create({question, terms, period, inputs, recipe, kind, threshold?, methodNote?, evidences?, project, by}), evaluate({recipe, inputs, viewer}), accept({calcId, by}), recompute({calcId}), read({calcId, viewer})** (`op=calculationcreate`, `op=calculationevaluate`, `op=calculationaccept`, `op=calculation`)
- **R4** `create` records a `CALC-` with its question, terms, period, inputs, recipe, kind, method version, method note and project, evaluates the recipe through `calc-grammar` (`checkRecipe`, `evaluate`) and stores the results under the result key (`calc-grammar.resultKey`). The `CALC-` id is minted in `record-grammar`'s `ID_TABLE` form for `CALC`, opaque (its R46, N570): nothing in it counts the calculations minted; ids minted earlier in the sequential form are still read. A table result `calc-grammar` answers streamed (`{fields, rows}`, its R22) is stored and answered as the same table the row-object answer holds, so what is stored does not depend on how an input was bound (K1734). Refusals, in order: `NO_QUESTION`, `NO_PERIOD`, `NO_INPUTS`, `NO_SUCH_INPUT` (naming it), `HYPOTHESIS_NOT_A_FACT` (an input or threshold naming a hypothesis id, `record-grammar.isHypothesisId`; K1467), then `calc-grammar`'s refusal of the recipe, by name.
- **R5** Every result of a `share` or `ratio` carries its denominator; every `compare` result is labelled a computed fact and never "breach" or "violation" (D275); values that could not be counted (an `undetermined` cell, an input out of view at evaluation) are counted apart and stated, never as zero.
- **R6** A threshold is a value or a held standard cited at its version (`standards`); a standard not in force for the calculation's period (`standards.inForceAt`) is refused `THRESHOLD_NOT_IN_FORCE`, or stated `undetermined` with the reason.
- **R7** `evaluate` answers what `create` would store and writes nothing.
- **R8** Results are recomputed at `accept`, at publication and in the checker (`recompute`), never on a read: `read` answers the stored results with `computed_at` and the method version. `accept` recomputes and is refused `CALC_RECOMPUTE_DIFFERS`, naming the differing result, when the recompute differs from what is stored; a refused `accept`, by this or any other refusal, writes nothing, its recompute status and recompute record included (N619); `recompute` answers `{agrees, results}` and writes only the recompute status.
- **R9** `read` answers the calculation with its grade facts (K1447 (ii)): per input its capture grade capped by its derivation; the calculation's capture axis is the weakest; recipe arithmetic is not a weakening step; an unbound input is testimony (D); the method is disclosed, not graded. `strength` reads them. Each input `read` answers states `sha`, the lowercase hex SHA-256 of that input's canonical bytes as the calculation was computed over them: a table's canonical CSV (its sha256, R1), and equally a figure, money facts, another calculation, a frozen set or a draw, so a case file names every input's bytes and `publication` can commit them (its R22; N596, K1639). (T35; N676, K1799) An input whose value a third-party engine computed (a table declared from a workbook range any of whose cells is a formula, its value the file's cached result as `content` reads it; a `workbooks` result) carries `engine`, the engine's name and version as recorded, or "the file's spreadsheet program" when none is recorded, and `engine_measured`, `true` only when that engine's agreement is recorded as measured; while it is `false` the input's grade is `undetermined` with why, the derivation step "third-party engine" (K1447 (ii); `strength` R36). Every other input carries `engine: null`. `gradeFactsOf` (R30) answers the same two fields on each input.
- **R10** A calculation any of whose inputs the viewer may not see is withheld whole: every read answers it exactly as an absent one (DEC-36, DEC-85).
- **R11** `onInputChanged(module, fn)` (one registration per module, through `membership`'s `listenerRefusal`): when an input of a held calculation changes (a money fact it names is corrected or withdrawn, as `money` reports; a table vintage it names is superseded), the calculation's recompute status becomes `stale` and each listener is told once with `{calcId, input, cause: "calculation_input_changed"}`. Nothing is recomputed by itself.

**The synchronous reads** (N576; K1601: for the checks that cannot wait on `read`, which is asynchronous)
- **R30** `gradeFactsOf({calcId, viewer})` answers synchronously R9's grade facts, `{found: true, accepted, capture: {grade, why}, inputs, method}`: `accepted` whether the calculation's acceptance is recorded (R8); `capture` the calculation's capture axis, its weakest input's (R9); `inputs` each input's name, kind, reference and grade with why, as R9 states them; `method` the recipe and method version, disclosed and not graded. A calculation not held, one R10 withholds from the viewer (an input the viewer may not see), and any call without a viewer each answer `{found: false}`, identically. It equals what `read` answers for the same calculation and viewer, recomputes nothing (R8), writes nothing and never throws.
- **R31** `calcStatusOf({calcId, viewer})` answers synchronously `{held, visible, accepted}`: `held` whether a `CALC-` of that id is held; `visible` whether R10 admits the viewer to it (false without a viewer); `accepted` whether its acceptance is recorded, false when not visible. It is a read for other modules' synchronous checks (`inquiry`, `strength`, `hypotheses`, `consequences`, `promotion`'s registered steps), not an op of R24: a caller answers a viewer a calculation that is not `visible` exactly as one not `held` (R10, DEC-36). Writes nothing; never throws.

**Money over calculations** (C:B-1, K1463, K1468)
- **R12** A total over money facts is refused by name across kind, phase or stage, basis, currency or period (`money.summable`, whose codes are `calc-grammar`'s); a total across funds that includes interfund transfers is answered with the interfund flag `money.summable` names.
- **R13** No act of this module records a money fact whose source is a `CALC-`, and no result is ever written as a money fact.
- **R14** `adoptBinding({table, roles, by})` records a member's adoption of a table's money roles (R2). `ingestMoney({binding, rows, reason?, by})` writes money facts through `money.recordFact`, machine-attributed, only at a member's request, only from an adopted binding, and only for rows whose payer and payee each resolve through an identifier (an id space or captured crosswalk): each fact cites its canonical row as its source. Rows that cannot be written are listed `not_written`, each with its reason. `rows` selects rows; all rows are taken only with `rows: "all"` and a stated `reason`.

**Joins by person** (C:B-2, K1452)
- **R15** A `join` from a table to registered entities, persons included, runs only through an id space (`id-spaces`) or a captured crosswalk table declared as one; a join on a name alone is refused `JOIN_NOT_BY_IDENTIFIER`. Rows may be grouped by person.

**Fact-based analysis: buys, unit cost, budget against actuals, rankings** (K1471; ladders §8.5)
- **R16** A unit cost is a `ratio` of a money fact's amount over its `buys` quantity, with the unit stated; budget against actuals compares an `adopted` total with an `actual` total per fiscal period (`civil-time`'s periods from the profile), each total within one phase and basis (R12), the bases stated beside the result.
- **R17** A ranking orders by one stated, measured quantity and names its quantity, scope and period, cites its inputs, records its method and states what it could not count. A recipe composing several measures into one score, or a measure across mixed kinds of link, is refused `SCORE_NOT_A_FACT` (K1471, K1473).

**Recorded draws: draw({set, n, seed?, by})** (`op=calculationdraw`; K1448)
- **R18** A draw is over a frozen set (a table's sha or a frozen record set), records the seed, the set's sha and the drawn members, and reproduces exactly from them (`calc-grammar.draw`). A population estimate is answered only over a recorded draw, with its exact interval (`calc-grammar.interval`); an estimate without one is refused `ESTIMATE_WITHOUT_DRAW`. (N735; U119) A draw may carry `question` (text of 1 to 2,000 characters), the words each drawn item is judged by when members visit it (R38). A draw differing from another only in its question is its own draw and reproduces the same sample. A question that is not such text is refused `BAD_QUESTION`, writing nothing. *(not yet met: T36)*

**Registrations it fills**
- **R19** At start it registers with `duties.registerOccurrenceEvidence`; asked `{duty, occurrence}`, an accepted calculation that names, in `evidences`, the duty occurrence it measures is answered to `duties` as that occurrence's evidence, with its results and grade facts; a stale or withheld one is answered as such.
- **R20** At start it registers with `people.registerRosterSource`: for an organisation and a date it answers the rows of tables with `roster` roles valid at that date (R3), by person key, with the table's sha, and reads no row into a line.

**Counts over the record** (C3)
- **R21** `freezeSet({query, by})` freezes the ids a saved query answers for the asking member (`retrieval`) as a record set with its sha, so a count over the record ("41 of 58 contracts at Grade B") is a recipe over a frozen set with its denominator, reproducible from the set.

**The machine's patterns** (K1471, K1473, K1491; Rules 7; Choices 17)
- **R22** Shipped, data-defined patterns: sequence anomalies (events out of their declared flow's order, `progressions`, `events.sequence`), lateness (days late per occurrence, `duties`, and per meeting's posting, `events`), and patterns about an office and across proceedings, each a recipe with its denominator and cited derivation. `runPatterns({budgetMs})` evaluates them within the budget into their own result table, never onto a person or entity row, and answers `{evaluated, remaining}`.
- **R23** A pattern's results are answered only once its gate is recorded at a false-alarm rate of at most 20% on its gold set (K1504, M-C8), by an administrator's `recordPatternGate({pattern, goldSet, falseAlarmRate, by})`; until then the read answers `{gated: true, reason}`. Each answered result is labelled the machine's, `layer: "hypothesis"`, "Noticed", and is switchable off per project; `onPatternResult(module, fn)` tells `notice-producers` once per new result. A result resting on a row the viewer may not see is withheld whole and not counted.

**The patterns of application** (T35-38; N645; Capability Ladders §6B.5, §6C.5; K1713 (2), K1723, K1471, K1483)
- **R32** (§6B.5's "what is held") `freezeUses({provision?, decider?, subject?, kinds?, from?, to?, project?, by})` (`op=usesfreeze`) freezes the held uses of powers a member may see (`events.usesOf`, its R46: acts of discretion, waivers, assessments) as a table input, declared as R1 declares a table but from the record, not a capture: canonical CSV with a stated schema, keyed by its sha256, one row per act, columns `event_id`, `kind`, `provision_standard`, `provision_portion`, `decider`, `decider_kind`, `subject`, `subject_kind`, `subject_sector`, `when_start`, `when_end`, `when_precision`, `reason_stated` (`1`, or `0` for "no reason stated"), `reason_fold` (the stated reason's words, `extraction`'s term fold, empty when none), `outcome`, and `relationships` (the kinds of direct tie held between the decider and the subject valid at the act's `when`: a post held, employment or former employment, a contract, a donation, a member-declared relation, each through its owner's `neighbours` at one hop, `connection-grammar`; empty when none is held, read "none held", never "none"). An act whose `when` is undetermined is a row with its band. The frozen set records its filter, the asking member and the instant, and is an input like any table (R4, R9: each row's grade its event's governing attestation's, the table's the weakest); a member freezes, the machine never (`MEMBER_ACT_ONLY`); `NO_USES` when the filter answers none. Refusals `NO_SUCH_PROJECT` as R21.
- **R33** (§6B.5's "what is measured") The module ships, as data, the recipes of application, each a `calc-grammar` recipe template a member instantiates through `create` (R4) over a frozen uses table (R32), listed by `applicationRecipes()`: `outcome_rate_by` (the share of each outcome, grouped by one named column: decider, subject, subject's kind or sector, a period of `when`, `reason_stated`, a relationship kind); `reasons_missing` (the share of acts with no reason stated); `reasons_repeated` (the number of distinct stated reasons and the share of acts whose folded reason is given word for word in another act, each group listed with its count: boilerplate shown as a measured quantity, never named so by the machine); `waiver_share` (waivers as a share of all uses under the provision or group); and R34–R36's. Every result states its denominator (the rows counted), its population (the frozen set's filter and sha) and its derivation (the recipe), with rows that could not be counted (an undetermined `when` for a period grouping, an `other` outcome where the recipe names outcomes) counted apart and stated (R5). A grouping by a person (decider or subject) names people only as K1483 allows; a result is a calculation the member made, never a "Noticed" item, never gated by R23, and none says why a pattern exists: the machine never names an unstated reason (K1713).
- **R34** (§6B.5's consistency, before and after) `consistency`: over the rows grouped by the like-case keys the member names (columns of R32; at least one, `NO_LIKE_CASE_KEYS`), for each group of two or more acts the share whose outcome is the group's most frequent, with the group's size; groups of one are counted apart. `before_after`: the outcome shares before and after a named change, `change` a held standard's version (its period's `from`, `standards`) or a date, a row whose `when` falls in a band across the change counted apart; the change's own date and what it rests on are stated. Both are R33's recipes, answered as R33 says.
- **R35** (ST6; K1723) `target_met`: over a measure (a table or calculation the member names) and a target held on a standard (`standards` R42: metric, threshold, period, definition), for each period of the target, the measured value beside the threshold and, through `standards.bindsAt` for the body measured on the period's last day: where the target binds the body, `met`, `not met` or `undetermined`; where it is a benchmark, the comparison only (`at or above`, `below`, `faster than`, `slower than`, as the comparator and unit read), labelled "Benchmark · not binding on <body>" (DEC-145 (2)), never `met` or `not met`; where `bindsAt` is `undetermined`, the comparison with that why. The target is read at its version in force for the period (R6's `THRESHOLD_NOT_IN_FORCE`); a measure whose definition differs from the target's own definition says so beside the result (the member states it; the module never judges two definitions equal).
- **R36** (PO11, ST9; K1504 (1)) `policy_against_practice`: a provision at its version in force for the period (`standards.inForceAt`, its confirmed force, `standards` R35) held beside a measured practice (a `CALC-` over events or a table the member names), answering the divergence (the measured rate of acts that depart from the provision's words as the member defines departure in the recipe) with its denominator; the provision and the practice are answered side by side, the practice never as the rule and never stored as a standard. A provision whose standard is not a measure (`standards.isMeasure` false: cited or absent) is refused `PROVISION_NOT_A_MEASURE`, naming its held state.
- **R37** (K1723; DEC-145 (2)) No result, label or sentence of this module says "nonconforming", "violated", "breach" or "not met" against a standard `standards.bindsAt` does not answer `binds` for the body measured; against a benchmark it says "below", "above", "slower than" or "faster than", with the benchmark's label. R27's list of words never used gains "nonconforming" and "violated" for every result. No pattern of application is a shipped machine pattern (R22) in T35, and none reaches the queue as "Noticed": the machine-raised disparities wait on a gold set of discretion acts (K1491, K1504).

**The ops map**
- **R24** The module publishes `calculationsOps(calculations, url, body)`, route arms for the ops above (C:A-16).

**Spot-checks: visits to drawn items** (N735; U119; K2015, K1430)
- **R38** `recordVisit({draw, item, testimony, finding, exhibits?, by})` (`op=spotcheckvisit`) records a member's visit to one drawn item as testimony tied to that item. `testimony` is the capture of the visitor's own firsthand observation (`provenance`'s `testify`, its R28: a register entry that is `authored`, its `author` the visitor, provenance R48). `finding` is one of `yes`, `no` or `could_not_tell`, against the draw's `question`. `exhibits` is an optional list of further held captures the visitor may see (a photo), cited with the visit. Refusals, in order, each writing nothing: `MEMBER_ACT_ONLY` (no stamp, or a machine), `NO_SUCH_DRAW` (a draw that is absent, or that the visitor may not see, answered alike), `NO_QUESTION` (the draw carries no question), `NOT_DRAWN` (`item` is not in the draw's sample), `NOT_TESTIMONY` (`testimony` is not an authored capture the visitor may see), `NOT_YOUR_TESTIMONY` (its author is not `by`), `BAD_FINDING`, `NO_SUCH_EXHIBIT` (naming it). A visit is append-only. It is never changed or removed, and a member who visits again records a new visit. Each visit is kept with its visitor as `provenance` records the testimony's author, the testimony's `observed_at`, and the instant it was recorded. A visit's grade is the testimony's (D, R9), never stronger. *(not yet met: T36)*
- **R39** `spotCheck({draw, viewer})` (`op=spotcheck`) answers a spot-check's data. It gives the draw: its set and the set's size, `n`, the seed and method, and `reproduced` from R18's reproduction. It gives the question. It gives each drawn item with its visits in recorded order and its standing. The standing is `yes` or `no` when the item's visits finding `yes` or `no` all agree, a `could_not_tell` visit beside them not counting against them. It is `disagree` when its visits find both: both are kept, shown in tension and never picked (U115). It is `could_not_tell` when every visit finds that, and `not_visited` when the item has no visit. It gives the counts `yes`, `no`, `judged` (`yes` plus `no`), `could_not_tell`, `disagree` and `not_visited`, which sum to `n`. Every item that could not be judged is counted apart by its reason, never as `no` or as zero (R5). It also names the `estimate` calculations held over the draw (R40), each with its recompute status (`stale` included). It computes no estimate itself (R8). A draw the viewer may not see, or one any of whose visits' testimony or exhibits the viewer may not see, answers `{found: false}` exactly as an absent draw (R10, DEC-36). It writes nothing and never throws. *(not yet met: T36)*
- **R40** (R18's estimate over visits) An `estimate` (`create`, kind `estimate`, R4) over a draw that carries a question takes that draw's visits as they stand at evaluation. They are carried in the draw input's canonical bytes (its `sha`, R9) as one row per drawn item with its standing (R39). Its results, each `calc-grammar`'s (R25), are as follows. `share_judged` is `yes` over `judged`, with its denominator (R5). `interval` is `calc-grammar.interval` with `frame_size` the set's size, `sample_size` `judged` and `successes` `yes`, at confidence 0.95 unless the terms state another, and it answers `low` and `high` as counts of the set and as shares of it (R18, calc-grammar R17). `estimate_count` is `share_judged` times the set's size, an exact decimal, never rounded. `counted_apart` gives `could_not_tell`, `disagree` and `not_visited` with their counts. `assumption` is the stated sentence that the interval treats the judged items as a simple random draw from the set, that is, that the drawn items not judged are like those judged, and that it says nothing else of them. With no item judged, the estimate is refused `NOTHING_JUDGED`, writing nothing. The calculation's capture axis is testimony's (R9: the visits are its weakest input). A visit recorded after evaluation makes the calculation `stale` (R11: `input` the draw, `cause: "calculation_input_changed"`) and never changes its stored results (R8). An estimate over a draw with no question is answered as R18 states today. No result or label says "breach", "violation", "nonconforming" or "not met" (R27, R37). *(not yet met: T36)*

### Suggestions

- **Factory.** `calculationsOf(ctx)`. Evaluation over a 20 MiB table streams from the evidence store; CPU and peak memory measured at the job.
