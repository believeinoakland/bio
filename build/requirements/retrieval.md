## Public

### Purpose

Retrieval runs the query language over the record and says what it could not see. It keeps each bundle's metadata projection and text index current with its promotion; answers searches at bundle grain (a page, a count, every id, facet counts, the wider reading of an empty conjunction) and at meaning grain, with the four-level statement of which absence is true and, for passages, the content-axis tally; publishes the vocabulary; checks the index against the corpus; holds a member's selections and says whether a selected set moved; answers one capture's content-axis state; and reads the frontier: per level, what was looked for, what came of it, and which subjects nobody has looked at. It builds no SQL of its own for a search (`query-language` compiles every statement) and runs none that lacks the viewer's gate. It mints nothing: a hit is an address.

### Provides

Terms. A **viewer** and an **owner** are the control plane's stamps. The **projection** is the columns of a bundle's row that `query-language`'s fields read; the **text index** is `bundles_fts`. **Hidden** means a bundle the viewer may not see (`membership` R43); a hidden thing answers exactly as an absent one, and no count includes it. Every bound is published as applied, after clamping.

**The projection and the text index** (a projection registered with `promotion`, K31)
- **R1** After a promotion commits, the bundle's projection equals `projectionOf` over its `bundle.md`, and its text-index row equals `query-language.textOf` over its files, in the same transaction. A revision replaces the bundle's own index row: the row key is allocated once per bundle and never reassigned while the bundle exists.
- **R2** `projectionOf(bundleMd, nowMs)` is pure: `schema_id`, `produced_mode`, `capability_tier`, `source_locator`, `source_authority`, `source_retrieved`, `source_status`, `content_hash`, the three monitoring columns, `annotations_open`, the three re-evaluation columns (a record or a legacy boolean), `fm_json`, and, only for a bundle whose type normalises to `action`, the six action columns, `action_clock_overdue` judged at `nowMs`. Frontmatter that does not parse gives every column null, never a guess.
- **R53** `registerActionFacts(module, fn)`: the six action columns of R2 are what the registered `fn(bundleMd, nowMs)` answers (`actions.actionFacts`, actions R12: `kind`, `risk_tier`, `counterparty_state`, `resolution`, `clock_next`, `clock_overdue`), registered once at start by `actions` (K31's pattern; a second registration is refused `FACTS_DECLARED`). This module holds no copy of the clock rule (`actionClockNext`, `actionOverdue`); with nothing registered the six columns are null (K75 (2), K92 (5)).
- **R55** `registerLegGrades(module, fn)`: R12's earned resolution is what the registered `fn(legs)` answers, `{grade, why}` for each capture-axis leg with a letter and a non-inquiry target, called once per page; registered once at start, `inquiry`'s resolver (its R52) registered under `inquiry`'s name by `plane` (plane R10; K861), a second registration refused `RESOLVER_DECLARED`, a malformed one `RESOLVER_MALFORMED`. With nothing registered every leg passes unchanged.
- **R56** `registerProjectionDecoration(module, fn)`: R5's single-bundle answer gains the keys the registered `fn(row, {viewer, nowMs})` answers (an object or a promise of one), one registration per module (`actions`, `inquiry`, `ai-runs`; K75 (2)), applied in the modules' total order; a module's second registration is refused `DECORATION_DECLARED`, a malformed one `DECORATION_MALFORMED`.
- **R62** (N136, N137; K75 (2), K649 (6)) `registerField(module, field, {table, key, col})`: a later module that holds the column of one of `query-language`'s fields in a table of its own registers it once at start (`inquiry`: `legs`; `strength`: `capture` and `connection`, its R23's cache). Every compile this module runs names each registered relation to `query-language` (its R26), so the field reads it. A field with none registered is read where it stands (`bundles`). A registered `table` holds at most one row per `key`, which its registering module keeps (`query-language` R26 reads one value per bundle); so no answer changes by a registration: the rows, counts, orders and `cached` notes are those the same column gave on `bundles`.
- **R57** `registerHiddenRunTail(module, fn)`: R39's rows left out for a viewer are those the registered `fn(viewer)` excludes, answered as a WHERE tail `{sql, args}` over `observation_log`; registered once by `ai-runs` (K80), a second registration refused `TAIL_DECLARED`, a malformed one `TAIL_MALFORMED`. With nothing registered, or a registration that throws or answers no tail, a run's rows are left out for every viewer but a machine credential (fail closed).
- **R58** `migrate()` creates this module's projection table (R61), moves any columns held on `bundles` into it once, idempotently, creates its text index and selection tables and backfills them (K4, R3), idempotently; `retrievalRoutes(retrieval, url, body)` answers the ops of R1–R54, and R63–R65's (R66), for the control plane's router.
- **R61** (N106, K75 (3), K144) The projection columns of R2 and the text-index key `fts_id` are held in a table of this module's own, `bundle_projection`, keyed by `bundle_id` and declared to `record-core`'s purge by that key. They are not held on `record-core`'s `bundles`. This module names that relation to `query-language` (its R25) and is its only writer (R1, R3).
- **R3** `reproject({limit})` re-derives the projection and index of rows lacking either, at most `limit` (500 by default, 1–5,000) per call, and answers `{reprojected, reindexed, limit, remaining}`; the instance runs it once at start with 500.

- **R5** With `bundleId`: the row, or null when absent or hidden. Without: `{bundles, limit, cursor, total}` in id order, `limit` 200 by default and at most 5,000, `cursor` the last id when the page is full, `total` counted through the gate; `jsonPath` with `jsonEquals` filters on the frontmatter.

**search({q, viewer, sort, dir, limit, offset, mode, facets, facetMode, widen, snippetChars})** (`op=search`)

The answer carries `query {q, terms, match, sort, warnings, mode}`, `gate {scope, applied}` where `applied` is the number of statements run, `total`, `limit`, `offset`; a page's `hits`, each with the bundle's provenance columns and a snippet and no score; or `ids`, every id in the page's order, with `truncated` when the cap (`IDS_MAX`) was reached.

- **R8** `cached` publishes `query-language.cachedNotes` for the routes this answer actually ran (no facet route on a count, no sort route on a count).
- **R9** When a conjunction of more than one atom finds nothing, and `widen` is not false, the OR reading is counted, and offered as `widen {interpretation: "OR", total, q, detail}` only when it finds something; otherwise `widen` is null.

**meaningRows({q, rows, viewer, limit, offset, ids})** (`op=meaningrows`)
- **R10** No `rows` is `MEANING_ROWS_NO_ARM` (C-23.1); a `rows` that is not an arm is `MEANING_ROWS_UNKNOWN_ARM` (C-23.2), its detail naming every arm with its grain. Each refusal carries its `check` and `translation`.
- **R11** The answer: `arm`, `table`, `grain`, `identity`, `query {q, warnings, meaningArms}`, `gate`, `cached` (filter route only), `rows`, `count`, `limit`, `offset`, `total` (the gated count of matching rows, never larger than paging reaches), and the four-level statement (R13).
- **R12** A leg row carries `grade` resolved against what the record can earn for its target on the capture axis, `grade_authored` verbatim, and `grade_why` (null when the authored letter stands); a connection-axis leg, a null grade and an inquiry target pass unchanged. One earned-registry read per page.
- **R13** The four-level statement: `level`; `scope {documents, documents_with_rows, documents_without_rows}` over the query's other arms; `levels` naming all four: `internet` undetermined (this read does not reach the observation log) with its reason, `document` counted, `content` and `meaning` counted at the arm's own level and undetermined otherwise, each saying which read answers it; and `says`, one sentence distinguishing no document in scope, no row of this kind in any document, and rows the filters excluded.
- **R14** For `rows=passage` the statement also carries the content-axis tally over the captures of the documents in scope (other arms): at most 500 counted, `captures_counted`, `captures_truncated` observed by reading one more, `captures_bound`, and one count per state of `observation-log`'s content-axis vocabulary plus `undetermined`; a state the vocabulary does not know is counted undetermined, never dropped; the vocabulary travels with the answer. `says` leads with coverage: how many captures in scope were never read at passage grain, and whether an absence covers the documents or only the part read. The captures not read are split by `observation-log`'s missing-row cause into `never_looked` and `undetermined` (the log cannot rule out an earlier reading or a purge), counted apart, and `says` states that nobody has read a number of them only from the `never_looked` count (K102).
- **R15** A text term found only inside a captured document's text is found by `passage:` and not by `text:`; searching mints no content row.
- **R54** Passage search honours `NEAR` (`query-language` R24, K105): a `passage:` term written with `NEAR` finds a unit only where its terms lie within the stated distance inside that one unit, never across two units; `rows=passage`, its snippets and R14's tally read the same match, and the syntax sentences (R16) state the operator.

**searchFields()** (`op=searchfields`)

**searchIndexCheck({after, limit, viewer})** (`op=searchindexcheck`)
- **R17** Over up to `limit` (200 by default, 1–1,000) visible bundles after `after`, each finding is `NO_FTS_ID`, `NO_INDEX_ROW` or `DIVERGED` (its columns and both lengths), the stored row compared with `textOf` over the stored files. `orphans` lists index rows no bundle claims, at most 100 with `orphans_truncated`. `counts {bundles, indexed, keyed}` are taken through the gate (`indexed` excludes rows a hidden bundle claims and keeps every orphan). `cursor` is set when the page is full; `ok` only with no finding and no orphan.

**Selections** (`op=select`, `op=selection`, `op=selectionlist`, `op=selectionrelease`)
- **R18** `selectionCreate({q, viewer, owner, sort, dir, ids, kind})`: `NO_OWNER`; `BAD_KIND` unless `query` or `enumerated` (ids given means enumerated); `EMPTY` for an enumeration of no id; `TOO_LARGE` over 10,000 ids with `limit` and `got`, never turned into a query. Members are resolved through the compiler under the viewer, so a hidden id never enters. A query selection stores its criterion and a digest of the ordered ids, no items; an enumeration stores each item with its sha. An owner holds at most 32: the oldest is collected, the new one never refused. It answers `{handle, kind, n, q, expires, ttlSeconds, gate}`; a selection lives 300 s from its last use.
- **R19** `selectionResolve({handle, viewer, owner, weight})`: an unknown, released or expired handle is `NO_SUCH_SELECTION` (C-33.20), one answer; another owner's is `NOT_YOURS`. Use extends the life. An enumeration is re-resolved under the current viewer: an item now hidden or purged leaves it (`drift.hidden`, `drift.purged`), a revised one is reported with its class (`mechanical` with its operation, `authored`, or `unknown`), nothing is ever added. A query selection is re-run and its digest compared: `added`, `removed`, `digestChanged` and the sentence that which rows moved is not recoverable. `moved` is per-row movement.
- **R20** With `weight: "refuse"`, an answer that changed (a moved row, or a query whose digest changed at a constant count) is `SET_MOVED` (C-33.32) with no members; with `report` the members are given with the drift.
- **R21** `selectionList({owner, viewer})`: `NO_OWNER`; the owner's selections newest first, the caps, and the instance's selection bytes, excluding rows that name a bundle the viewer cannot see. `selectionRelease({handle, owner})`: `NO_OWNER`, `NOT_YOURS`; releases one, or all the owner's, and answers how many.
- **R22** `sweepSelections()` removes every expired selection and answers how many; it runs before every selection act.
- **R51** `sweepWake(now)` (for `scheduler`'s `selection-sweep`) answers null when no selection is held, else `now` + the selection lifetime (300 s) + 30 s. Pure over the table; writes nothing; never throws.
- **R52** `onSelectionCreated(module, fn)`: a later module registers once at start; a malformed registration, or a second by the same module, is refused through `membership`'s `listenerRefusal` (its R81: `LISTENER_MALFORMED`, `LISTENER_DECLARED`), and the listeners run in the modules' total order (`membership`'s `MODULE_ORDER`, R83). After each successful `selectionCreate` (R18) this module calls every registered listener once, in the modules' total order (R83), with `{handle, expires}`; a listener that throws or rejects never changes the selection or its answer. (`scheduler` registers its `arm`, K72 (9).)
- **R59** (N142) `answerChanged(drift, moved) → boolean`, a module-level function: true exactly when `moved` is true, or when `drift.digestChanged` is true (a query whose answer changed at a constant count, REC-55). It is the one rule R20's `SET_MOVED` and `citation`'s set-moved note read. It is pure and never throws.

- **R24** Otherwise: `extraction`, the latest content-level observation with the `extract` authority; `index`, the latest with the `derive` authority (null when none); `indexed`, `determined` and `why` by `observation-log`'s content-axis rule over them; `missing_cause` by its missing-row rule when no extraction row exists; `bundle_id`; the vocabularies. The extraction axis and the index axis are never merged.

**frontier({level, limit, viewer, identity})** (`op=frontier`, K78 (5): *what have we looked for at this level, and what came of it*, the candidate list for fetching, extracting and deriving)

Terms. A subject's **entry** is its latest row at the level (`observation-log` R9) with `last_verified` and `unreachable_since` (its R10). A **supply** is one bounded fetch of rows made before the gate and the cut; it is **full** when it returned as many rows as it asked for.

- **R35** `level` is `document` (the default), `content`, `meaning` or `internet`. Any other level answers `found: false`, `built: false`, empty `looked`, `never_looked` and `tally`, `truncated: false`, and a `note` saying this reader reads no such level, which is not an empty frontier. `limit` is 200 by default, clamped to 1–2,000, and published on every answer, the empty ones included. Never throws.
- **R36** Every built level answers `found: true`, `built: true`, `level`, `limit`, `truncated`, `looked`, `never_looked`, `never_looked_count`, `tally` and `note`. `looked` is the entries the viewer may see (R38), newest first, at most `limit`, each with its row's state, authority, actor class, referent, detail and `at`, `governed` as a boolean, `last_verified` and `unreachable_since`. `NEVER_LOOKED` is never a state in `tally`: a subject with no row is listed apart.
- **R37** `truncated` is true when a list the answer cuts at `limit` held more than `limit` visible rows, or when any supply behind a list was full; otherwise false. A supply reads at most `(limit + 1) × 2` rows (`× 3` for the meaning level's entries); the internet level's lists are fetched already gated, at `limit + 1`. On a full supply every viewer reads `true`, so the bit says nothing about what was withheld, and no answer states how much was withheld.
- **R38** The gate, row-whole. At the document level an entry, and a never-looked address (judged as a row whose referent is the capture linking to it), appear only when `observation-log.rowVisible` (R13) admits them. At the content level a capture appears only when a bundle holds it and the viewer may see that bundle (`membership` R43). At the meaning level a capture is gated so, a reference through the capture its authority names (with none it is withheld), an entity is not gated (K102), and a row whose authority is a run appears only when the viewer may read that run. A withheld row is absent from every list. After a per-bundle purge a member therefore sees no row whose capture was purged (K102, `observation-log`'s question 1).
- **R39** `tally` counts rows per state over the whole level, never the page, and names no subject, bundle or address. At the document, content and meaning levels R38 does not narrow it (the REC-110 ruling), but it leaves out every row whose authority is a run in a project the viewer cannot see (D-486). At the internet level it counts only the looks the viewer may read (R47).

Document level
- **R40** Entries are at subject kind `address`. Each carries `result_purged`: true when its referent is a capture the record no longer holds, false when it holds it, null for any other referent. `never_looked` is each normalised address in the `deferred` link partition that has no document-level row, with `from_document`, the capture linking to it, in address order.
- **R41** A missing row at this level is read through §5.1's order, as at the others: an address the record holds a capture of (provenance's `captured_locators`) with no row is in `missing_unexplained` with the cause `pre_log`, never in `never_looked`, and every `never_looked` and `missing_unexplained` row carries `not_ruled_out` and `evidence_one_sided` (`observation-log` R11). The evidence at this level is one-sided (`observation-log`'s `DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE_IS_ONE_SIDED`, K306), so a `never_looked` address names all three causes, and a `pre_log` address (its capture held) names `pre_log` alone, the artifact showing a look happened (K331).

Content level
- **R42** Entries are at subject kind `capture`; a capture's entry is its latest `extract` row. Each carries `indexed`, `indexed_determined` and `indexed_why` (`observation-log` R12, over the entry and the capture's latest `derive` row); `tier3_candidate` when the state is neither `PRESENT` nor `LOOKED_ABSENT`; `calibration_drifted` and `calibration_id` when a transcription of the capture rests on a calibration a worse one superseded (`extraction` R38); and `recandidate` when either holds. `recandidates` and `recandidate_count` are exactly the entries of `looked` with `recandidate`; `calibration_drift_truncated` says the drift read reached its bound.
- **R43** The captures the record holds with no content-level row (one supply, gated by R38) are split by `observation-log` R11's cause: `never_looked` holds only the cause `never_looked`, and every other is in `missing_unexplained` with `missing_cause` and its `why`. Each list is cut at `limit`, with its count, and every row carries `not_ruled_out` and `evidence_one_sided`. The answer carries `missing_causes`, `evidence_one_sided` (two-sided at this level), the content-axis `vocabulary` and `undetermined_value`.
- **R44** The `note` says nothing the answer contradicts.

Meaning level
- **R45** Entries span three subject kinds, kept apart: `capture` (did anything read this document for entities), `reference` (did anything try to match this name) and `entity` (did anything derive connections over this subject), with any other kind R38 admits. Each carries `ran_and_found_nothing` (its state is `LOOKED_ABSENT`); `by_subject_kind` gives `{looked, ran_and_found_nothing}` per kind, counted over `looked` itself.
- **R46** The missing subjects, one supply per kind: a capture held with no meaning-level capture row; a reference a reading carries with no row, entered at the earliest registration of a capture carrying it; an entity with no row, entered at its creation. The cause is §5.1's, in order: `pre_log` when the kind's artifact exists (a reading, a resolution, a connection); otherwise `observation-log` R11 against the level's earliest row; a kind with no artifact rule reads `purged`, never `never_looked`. They are split, cut, counted and annotated as in R43, with this level's `why` sentences, `missing_causes` and `evidence_one_sided` (one-sided at a reference and an entity).

Internet level
- **R47** The member asked is the caller's positional identity (`membership`); with none, the answer is empty with `empty.cause` `no_member`. Otherwise it reads only the looks at level `internet` under a `lead` authority that the viewer may read (`observation-log` R19), gated before the latest-per-subject grouping: `looked`, `last_verified`, `unreachable_since`, `never_looked`, `tally`, `truncated` and the empty cause depend on those looks and the visible leads alone, so a lead outside the viewer's reach changes no byte of the answer. Each entry carries `lead`, `coverage` and `found_nothing`, and a referent the viewer cannot read is given as null, as `leadRead` gives it (`observation-log` R18).
- **R48** `never_looked` is each visible lead with no internet-level look, oldest first, with `missing_cause: "never_looked"` and R41's two fields. `empty` is null when `looked` has an entry, else `never_followed` when a visible lead stands, else `no_leads_visible`, which reads the same whether or not leads exist beyond the viewer's reach. Every answer carries `reads`, `not_read` (a run's open-internet searches, and an acquire at an unheld address, each with why), `tally_scope: "visible_to_viewer"`, `evidence_one_sided`, `empty_causes` and an empty `missing_unexplained`.
- **R49** The internet level carries `vocabulary {states, outcomes}`, member-facing words for every state (`observation-log` R21).

**counts(hid)** (N171, K209; for `queue`)
- **R60** (N171, K209; for `queue`) `counts(hid)` answers `{indexed, selections, selectionItems}`: text-index rows, held selections and their items. `hid` (`{sql, args}`, as `run-productions`' `counts`) leaves out the index rows a hidden bundle claims (R17's `indexed` rule) and the selection items naming a hidden bundle.
