- PNG, interlaced or not, any depth: IHDR, PLTE, tRNS, IDAT (its chunking kept), IEND, CRCs checked; the orientation as a minimal eXIf where the original's was. An unknown critical chunk is refused `NOT_A_STRIPPABLE_FORMAT`; `acTL` naming more than one frame `ANIMATED_IMAGE` (one naming one frame is stripped to its IDAT image).
- GIF: header, screen descriptor and global table, the image's graphic control extension, the image; more than one image `ANIMATED_IMAGE`.
- WebP: VP8X (ICC, EXIF, XMP flags cleared; EXIF set when the orientation is kept), ALPH, VP8/VP8L, a minimal EXIF chunk; animation `ANIMATED_IMAGE`.
- JP2: signature, ftyp (must list `jp2 `), jp2h (ihdr, bpcc, pclr, cmap, cdef and one enumerated colr; an ICC colr becomes enumerated sRGB, or greyscale for one or two components without a palette), the first jp2c; xml, uuid, res, rreq and every other box left behind; fragment tables (`ftbl`) refused `NOT_A_STRIPPABLE_FORMAT`. JP2 has no standard orientation; none is read from an EXIF `uuid`.
- J2K codestream (alone or in jp2c): every COM removed, in the main header and in tile-part headers, each SOT's Psot and the TLM's lengths adjusted; the packet data copied whole; anything after EOC left behind.
`changed` is computed as "the answer's bytes differ from the input's"; every difference the walk makes is a removal (or the fresh R2 structures replacing a removed one), and an unchanged answer is a copy of the input.
