## action-grammar (own requirements)

**Status.** In force: `actions`' second split for size (K617, K653), meaning unchanged; met by ACTION-GRAMMAR #1 (K835). R1-R25 met; R26-R29 marked not yet met at T46-T48 pending promotion stamps; R30 (T51); **R31 (T53) not yet met.**

**Purpose.** The grammar of an action document as the record checks it: the vocabularies, C-2.10's arms (basis legs, correspondence ledger, quotes, lifecycle, counterparty, records law, governing laws, tier history), C-6.1's `responds_to` arm, C-11.1's clock discipline, the readers `actions` answers its read with, and the rows its acts mint; pure, reads no record. Whether a write lands is `actions`' (its R1-R11, R33). It also holds the T45 field grammars (R15, R20-R25) and the phrasing lists with the pure finder (R16-R19); pointing out at approval and recording the resolution are `filings`'.

**Terms.** Terms are `actions`' (an **action**, its document's keys, the lifecycle); a **finding** is `record-grammar`'s (its R11): `{check, severity, message, repairs?, code?}`.
