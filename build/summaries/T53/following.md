**Status.** In force; a seam beside `monitoring` with no copy (K617); following a whole policy portal or a standard's new editions is not here. R37-R46 not yet met (T53).

**Purpose.** The group's following of what happens at a body and in a register, on the scheduler's one alarm: a body a member follows is read from its Legistar records so `events` can write what it did; a document watched per meeting is captured before each meeting by the body's notice period; a public register is re-read or re-rendered on the tick, and one behind a member's account only at that member's act; a member may follow a register query naming a person; a portal's dataset is snapshotted with its changes keyed field by field. Like `monitoring`, it watches and captures; what a change means is never its to say.

**Terms.** A follow is a member's standing act naming what is followed and for which period. A tick is one scheduled read of one subject. A subject is `{kind, id}`, `kind` one of `body`, `meeting`, `register`, `person-query`, `portal`, `policy` (R20), and (T53) `register-query`, `register-interest`. The host is `monitoring.sweepHost()`'s services (its R65). Every refusal names `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries `check`, `code`, `translation`. A watch is a follow, a watched address (`monitoring` R15), a duty (`duties` R2) or a standing question (`answers` R15, R28); a link records that a plan set it running (R23).
