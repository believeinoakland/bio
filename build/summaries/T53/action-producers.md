## action-producers (own requirements)

### Public

Purpose: the feed's Action-layer to-dos. Each derives on read, writing nothing, the items an action's clocks, reminders, holds, checkpoints, escalations, templates, local facts, watches and plan reviews earn for a viewer, routed to the member who takes the thing up or set it, never assigned to anybody; it names each item's subjects, home subjects and doors for `queue-producers` to answer in `feedItems` and for `queue` to home, offer, mint and publish. (R13)

**Take up** is `plan-work`'s act `itemTakeUp` (its R11): a member routes an item's to-dos to herself; nobody assigns anybody. **R1's rule for recipients**: the member who last took the thing up, then the member who created or set it, each only while an active member who may see it; else the project's active owners; else the administrators. (R1, R13)

## Uses: membership

- Purpose: who the members are and what each may do, and projects at their setup (participation, visibility, the fence). (R43)
- R43 `viewerPredicate(viewer)`: the one rule of what a viewer may see. A machine credential sees every bundle; the founder (member `admin`) sees every bundle except a hidden project in which she is neither invited nor joined; a `member:<id>` viewer sees every non-project bundle, and a project only as a participant (any state) or as an active administrator (an administrator sees a hidden project only as a participant); a bundle belonging to a project is seen exactly when its project is; any other viewer sees nothing. Also returns the viewer's member id (null for a machine). The founder's viewer is `admin` or `member:admin`.
- R80 `inSight(bundleId, viewer)`: true exactly when the bundle is held and R43 admits the viewer; false for an absent/empty id, unrecognised viewer, or a project R43 does not admit. Writes nothing, never throws.
- R74 `participation(projectId, memberId) → {state, owner}` or null: state `invited`, `joined`, `leaving` or another roster state, and whether she is an owner; null when she has none or the project is not held. Never throws.

- R154 `memberFacts`, `activeAdmins` and `isAdministrator` (R68, R86, R64 retired here) are delegates of `member-roster` R24, R29, R23 with the same name and shape; read `member-roster` for their signatures (not in this set).

## Uses: civil-time

- Purpose: the one engine for civil time; pure, "now" is the caller's; a refusal is `{refused: CODE, why}`, an undetermined answer `{undetermined: true, why, candidates?}`. (R1)
- R1 `localDay(instant, zone) → YYYY-MM-DD`: the calendar day in `zone` on which `instant` falls, across daylight-saving changes; unknown zone refused `ZONE_INVALID`.
- R6 `isCalendarDate(s)`: true only for a `YYYY-MM-DD` naming a real proleptic-Gregorian day.
- R7 `dayRange(fromDay, toDay, zone) → {start, end}`: inclusive local-day range, `start` the first instant of `fromDay`, `end` the first instant after `toDay`; `fromDay` after `toDay` refused `RANGE_INVERTED`; an invalid day `DATE_INVALID`.
- R24 `span(a, b, {unit, view?, office?}) → {min, max, trace}` or undetermined: the count of `unit` (`days`, `business days`, `hours`, `months`, `years`) on local days; `min` and `max` differ when a precision leaves it open.

- R17 `noticed_by: {duty, occurrence}` appears when an adopted duty of the addressee office has an occurrence triggered by the action's own sending whose due date (or a candidate) is the entry's date; R1 of this module draws no overdue item for it (but see R22 here).
- R16 A step and a clock entry stand for each other when the step is tied to the action and its `byWhen.date` is the entry's date; R3's and R5's items carry `steps: [ids]`. Read R16 whole.
- R18 A clock entry is `owed_by: group`, `body` or `undetermined`, never defaulted; a missed `group` date is told once by this module.
- R5 `remindersDue({nowMs, after?, limit?, viewer})`: every unanswered reminder whose day has come, on an entry still `pending` of a visible action not `resolved` or `abandoned`: action, position, the entry's date, basis, text, the reminder's day, the member who set it, the action's project; each item carries `zone`; at most 500 per page in (action id, position, day) order with `cursor` and `truncated`. Writes nothing.
- R11 `calendarFactsRead({viewer})`: once each, the `local-facts` paths a live deadline reads (holiday years and office hours, plus closure-list entries), each with the actions that read it as `{action, project, created_by}`; at most 500 actions read, `truncated` stated. Writes nothing.

## Uses: escalation

- Purpose: the stages a group works through to bring a government act back into conformance; the next stage is proposed when its trigger is met and a member advances it; it decides nothing about significance. (R16)
- R16 `escalationsDue`: every open escalation with at least one proposed edge not advanced or declined since its trigger was met, with the edge, the trigger's instant and age, the escalation's project and the member who opened it, oldest first, at most 500 (`truncated` stated). An escalation `stopped_pursuing` is never listed.

## Uses: action-plans

- Purpose: a project's working material for deciding what to do about matters: options, scenarios with checkpoints a member judges, links to started Actions; never published. (R17)
- R17 `checkpointsDue({nowMs, limit?})`: for open plans, checkpoints whose day has come and that no member has judged, oldest first, at most 500, with the plan, its project, scenario, phase, the member who set the scenario's current version, and days since due. A paused plan's checkpoints and a set-aside scenario's are not due.

## Uses: following
