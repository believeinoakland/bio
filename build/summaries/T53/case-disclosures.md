## case-disclosures (own requirements)

Status: in force, split from `case-authoring` (K617; K1333), meaning unchanged; the renderers live only here. Amended T34–T46 (R6, R7, R22, R29, R30, R31). R35–R46 new at T53, not yet met. Size (P6): about 1,210 lines.

Purpose: what a case discloses about what it rests on, judged and written before anything is signed (Publication §3 rule 16, §5C; DEC-76, DEC-81, DEC-96, DEC-112, DEC-119): unresolved conflicts; each document's grade, co-attestation and self-attestation acknowledgement; what may be said of a source; every material a chain reaches, held whole and attested; the method; another group's accepted work and flags; grading facts and passages; hunch debt. Judged against the owner's lists and the record at the act; spells each disclosure's lines; `case-authoring`'s `publishCase` asks it in order (its R55). No table, no op.

Provides: terms are `case-authoring`'s and `publication`'s (`prepared`, `memberRoles`, `viewer` the stamp). Every refusal names `reason`, with `check`, `code`, `translation` where a row exists. A malformed owner's list is `BAD_COMPLETENESS` naming the field (not a list; entry not an object; words not a string, over 2,000 characters, or holding a double quote, backslash or line break). Judgments answer `{refusals, …}` in order; `publishCase` answers the first, the pre-flight lists all.
