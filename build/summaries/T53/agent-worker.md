Purpose: the fleet member performing an AI run for the plane under the run's `ai` credential; a deterministic table decides every step, the model only what to search and what reports mean; writes nothing itself (every change is the plane's); states the serving account without a secret; never attests, concludes, accepts or publishes.

Terms: a refusal is `{ok:false, reason, code, detail, worker:"agent-worker", …extra}`, `reason` equal to `code`. The plane is `env.PLANE`. A plane answer is silent when the binding throws or the body is not JSON; refused when status is not 200, the envelope's `ok` is not `true`, or the innermost `result` stating an `ok` says `false`; else the result. A plane refusal passes with its code, C-number and body unchanged.
