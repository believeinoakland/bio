## ai-use (own requirements)

Status: in force; new module (layer 6, before `ai-runs`), BOB's from T40's N812 (K2373, K2376, K2394); R1–R4 were `ai-runs` R48–R51 (B10); changed T41–T50. Size (P6) under 4,000.

Purpose: what each AI account has spent and may spend: counts each use against the paying account and the acting member, holds and judges each owner's limits, answers owners their use and members theirs. No credential; does not choose the payer (`ai-accounts`').

Terms. **Owner**: `group` (administrators act), `project:<id>` (any owner) or `member:<id>` (she alone) (`ai-accounts` R10, R11, R14). **Use**: an `ai-accounts.USE_KINDS` entry, its R14's nine (`ask`, `draft`, `run`, `standing`, `explore`, `enquire`, `read`, `transcribe`, `account`). **Limit**: `{owner, scope, unit, period, amount, inclusive}`. **Period**: current local `day` or `month` of the group's calendar (`civil-time`); a month resets on the 1st. No limit set: nothing capped here (a provider's own limit answers as `ai-runs` states). `viewer`, `by`: control plane's stamps. Every refusal names `code` and carries its row's `check` and `translation` (R8).

Counting (`countUsage`)
- R1 *(not yet met: T53)* · `countUsage({owner, member, use, mode, entry_id, label, usage, calls, at})`, called in the caller's transaction. `owner` `group`, `project:<id>` or `member:<id>`; `entry_id` and `label` (`agent-model` R26's: provider, host, model version, effort, account level, owner) replace the model string and carry the effort (R15). `usage` is `agent-model` R5's record. Adds to counter `ai_usage`, per owner, member, local day (`civil-time.localDay`) and `use`: token sums (reasoning included), `calls` (a `null` counted as one), `cost_reported_sum` and `cost_estimated_sum` per unit, kept apart, never added, and `unpriced_calls` (calls stating no cost and having no estimate). An `unstated` figure is never counted as 0: a day's sum with any unstated figure keeps `unstated` listing it. `countAskUsage({member, mode, entry_id, label, usage, calls, at})` counts an ask or draft alike, `mode` `ask` or `draft`, owner as `accountFor` answered; other `mode` → `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID`. Sums what `usage` carries, reads no price table (`agent-model` R13 estimates; K2376). Malformed entry → `AI_RUN_CONSUME_INVALID`, nothing counted. Counter holds no content, question, address or secret (`admin-only`). Pre-T40 rows "not recorded", toward no limit; older rows keep dollars as `cost_estimated_sum` in `usd`, `entry_id` null. T: one call stating a cost, one priced without → `cost_reported_sum` and `cost_estimated_sum` separately; neither → `unpriced_calls`, no sum; `cache_read` unstated stays unstated. C: negative token count refused, nothing counted.

Limits (`aiLimitSet`)
- R2 *(not yet met: T53)* · `aiLimitSet({owner, scope, unit, period, amount, inclusive?, by})`. `scope`: `overall`, a `USE_KINDS` entry, or `per_member` for a team or project account. `unit`: `usd`, `tokens`, `calls`. `period`: `day`, `month`. `amount`: positive (`usd` to the cent; others whole), or `null` to remove. `inclusive` (default `true`) only for a use scope. Else `AI_LIMIT_INVALID` naming the field. **`usd` only on an account whose plan's money is `reported` or `priced`; on `none` or OPEN → `LIMIT_UNIT_UNAVAILABLE`.** Follows the plan, not the account kind (a sign-in plan reporting money allows `usd`; a flat-fee API-shaped plan not); the old "`usd` on a member's sign-in is refused" retires. `tokens`, `calls` always allowed. Setting is the owner's own act (`NOT_YOUR_CEILING`; `ai-accounts`' project owner refusals; `NOT_AN_ADMIN`). At most one limit per owner, scope, unit, period; each set appended with who and when. Idempotent. T: `usd` on money `none` → `LIMIT_UNIT_UNAVAILABLE`; on `reported` and `priced` set; OPEN refused; `tokens` set on all. C: plan changed to `reported` accepts `usd`.

Judging (`useCheck`)
- R3 *(not yet met: T53)* · `useCheck({owner, member, use, at, entry_id?})` → `null` or `AI_LIMIT_REACHED` with `{whose, scope, unit, period, source, resets_at?, walkable}` and its plain-words row, naming no cost. `source`: `own` (a limit this account's owner set, judged **before** the call from the counter) or `provider` (a provider limit recorded by R18, not yet reset, judged from the record **after** the provider said it). Refuses when, for the current period: the owner's `use` limit, its `overall` limit (over non-exclusive uses, unless `use`'s limit is exclusive) or its `per_member` limit (over `member`'s use of that account) reached its amount; or a provider limit covering the account (or entry) has not reached `resets_at`. Judges only the paying account's limits (D38). A `usd` limit on an act whose `entry_id` has neither price nor reported cost → `LIMIT_UNIT_UNAVAILABLE` with `unjudged` (R21); never counted as zero. `EXPLORE_NOT_ENABLED` when the owner's `explore` is `no` (A5, B6); `yes` with no `explore` limit → judged on `overall` alone; `ask` without that day's approval is R6's answer. `explore` read via `ai-accounts.accountUsesOf` (its R17), no viewer; reads `no` when not held, unreadable, or not `no`/`ask`/`yes` (fail closed). Writes nothing, never throws. Unreadable counter → refusal (fail closed) with `unjudged: true`, no `scope`, `unit`, `period`. `walkable` (R20): `true` only on an own or provider limit; `false` on `EXPLORE_NOT_ENABLED`, `unjudged`, and any use its owner switched off. T: own limit → `source: "own"`, `walkable: true`; recorded provider limit with future `resets_at` → `source: "provider"` and that time; after it (pinned clock) `null`; unreadable → `unjudged`, `walkable: false`. C: under every limit `null`.

Reads (`aiUsage`, `aiUsageMine`, `limitsReached`)

Neither writes. T: `none` plan → tokens, calls, `not_measurable: true`, no cost field 0; priced → both sums; non-owner refused as R2. C: owner of a reported plan sees `cost_reported_sum`.
- R5 *(not yet met: T53)* · `limitsReached({viewer, at})` answers, per account the viewer owns, each limit first reached in its current period: `{owner, scope, unit, period, period_start, reached_at, source, resets_at?}`, a stable key per owner, limit and period (provider limit: per owner, kind and `resets_at`).
