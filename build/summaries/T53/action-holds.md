## action-holds (own requirements)

Status: new at T45 (K2781 (1)): `actions`' third split; R1-R10 moved from `actions`, R11-R13 new; R14-R22 added T46-T51. Not yet met (T51).

Purpose: the group's litigation holds and the pressure it receives. A member marks pressure on a received entry or records pressure that arrived outside any action; any member who can see an action, entry, case or project places a hold there with a reason, and releases it; the hold stops a purge of what it covers, a scheduled publication and a doorbell clearance; a member records that she sent the plain notice telling members what to keep outside Civicsmith. A member does every act; nothing here records whether any member received, read or complied with a notice (rule 10).

Terms: an **action**, its document, entries and positions are `actions`'. In the moved text (R1-R10) `actions`' ids read as this module's: R48 → R1, R52 → R2, R54 → R3, R55 → R4, R56 → R5, R57 → R6, R58 → R7, R59 → R8, R60 → R9, R69 → R10; an `actions` id not in that list (R15, R22, R25, R34) is still `actions`'. A **place** is R2's: an action, an entry of one, or a project.

- R1 · A `received` correspondence entry may be marked `pressure: {kind: legal | retaliation | discrediting | other, note}` (note at most 500 characters, R22's text rule): a threat, retaliation, discrediting or legal harassment directed at the group or its supporters. Stated as a key of `actionCorrespond` (R15) when the entry is recorded, or later by `actionPressure({target, ord, pressure, author, viewer})` (`op=actionpressure`), which appends the mark to a table of its own and never rewrites the entry (R34). Only a member marks it (`MACHINE_CANNOT_MARK_PRESSURE`); a mark on a `sent` or `no_response` entry is refused `PRESSURE_NOT_RECEIVED`, one on an entry already marked `PRESSURE_MARKED`. The read (R25) lists an action's pressure entries apart; `actionsFor` takes `pressure: true`. It is evidence and may be cited by an inquiry. A `pressure` key stated at `actions`' `actionCorrespond` stays on the entry and is read through `actions.actionRead`, both answered alike; an action's pressure entries and holds are answered by `pressureFor` (R12) and `holdsOf` (R2) in place of `actions` R25's list.
- R2 · A hold is placed through `actionHold({target?, ord?, project?, from?, hold, reason, projects?, author, viewer})` (`op=actionhold`) at a **place**: an action (`{target}`), an entry of one (`{target, ord}`), or a project (`{project}`); not exactly one place is `HOLD_PLACE_REFUSED`, asked after `NO_TARGET`. `from?` names what prompted it: a pressure mark (R1), a pressure record (R12, `{pressure}`), a docket `legal` mark (`{docket_entry}`, read through `docket.docketPressureOf`, its R27; the place is that entry's case's project), or a counsel matter (the project itself, `project-roster.projectScope` answering `counsel_matter`). `hold` is `in_place` (the group is preserving what the matter may reach), with `reason` (1 to 500 characters, R22's text rule). The statement records its **projects**: the action's own project (`record-core` R34; none if it belongs to none), filled in by this module and never taken from the caller, and each distinct project id in `projects` (at most 50). Each statement, with its projects, is appended to this module's tables (`action_holds`: the action, the entry, `hold`, `reason`, `by`, `at`, a sequence; and its projects), keyed to the action (record-core R21) and never rewritten; the latest statement at a place is its hold. A hold `in_place` **covers** the projects of every `in_place` statement on that entry since its last `released` statement (R56): a further `in_place` statement adds its projects, and a project leaves a hold only when the hold is released. A project is **held** while any hold `in_place`, on any action, covers it. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_HOLD` (C-117.20; empty or machine author); `NO_TARGET`; `HOLD_PLACE_REFUSED`; `HOLD_RELEASE_IS_ITS_OWN_ACT` (C-117.23; `hold` is `released`: R56 is that act); `HOLD_REFUSED` (C-117.21; `hold` not `in_place`, or the reason not as above); `HOLD_PROJECTS_REFUSED` (C-117.24; `projects` present and not a list of project ids, or over 50 distinct); `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` (absent and invisible alike); `NOT_AN_ACTION`; then, for the first named project the author may not name (a project place included), `membership`'s `existenceAct` and then `noSuchProject` (its R77, R78). `HOLD_NO_LEGAL_MARK` (C-117.22) is retired, its number never reused: a hold on an action with no pressure mark lands. A project a viewer **may name** is one she sees at `FULL`, or, as an administrator neither invited nor joined, a hidden project she sees at `EXISTENCE` (`membership` R44, R60): naming it reaches its id and hold state, never its contents, so a custodian can place a hold on a hidden project. It answers `{ok, target, ord, hold, reason, by, at, projects}`, `projects` those the hold now covers that the author may name (`[]` for an action in no project naming none). A case the member can see (`publication`'s case read) places a hold on its project. Any member who can see the action may place a hold. `holdsOf({target | project, viewer})` answers a place's statements, oldest first, each with those of its projects the viewer may name, and the current `hold` (null while none). While a hold is `in_place`, no material of a project it covers, and not the action that carries it, is purged from the real record (R60, `store-door` R4). R3-R10 read places where they read entries. Control: a hold naming two places is refused.

**declarePurge(module, tables, {exempt?}) → void**
- R21 · A module that owns tables declares them here once, at start, through `declareTable(module, entries)` (S0-3), one entry per table, each naming its classes: `purge` (`clear` or `exempt`), `expunge` (`tombstone`, removable under R79, or `none`), `export` (`yes`, `admin-only` or `never`), `sight` (`group`, `bundle`: the sight of the bundle its keys name, `source`: of the capture or source a row cites, or `owner`), `derive` (`stored` or `derived-rebuildable`, R77) and `version_chain` (`true` when a change appends a version and never overwrites). An entry missing a class, or with a value outside these, is refused `TABLE_CLASS_MISSING` or `TABLE_CLASS_UNKNOWN`, naming the table and the class, and the call registers nothing. `declarePurge(module, tables, {exempt?})` is kept as `declareTable`'s default form (plan T33, Rules (6)): each table `purge` `exempt` when listed in `exempt`, else `clear`; `expunge` `none`; `export` `admin-only`; `sight` `bundle` when the table is keyed to a bundle, else `group`; `derive` `stored`; `version_chain` `false`; a module with a T33 job declares its tables explicitly. `purge` clears every declared table whose `purge` is `clear`; this module declares its own tables (`bundles`, `files`, `history`, `manifest`, `leases`, `bookkeeping_tally`). `declaredTables()` answers every declaration with its classes, in order. A table declared twice, or by two modules, is refused with `TABLE_DECLARED`.
- R46 · A declared table is a name, keyed to a bundle by its `bundle_id` column when it has one, or `{name, keys: [columns], whole: "<WHERE clause>"}`: keyed to a bundle by the named columns (any of them matching), and cleared by the whole-store form only where the clause holds. Under `declareTable` (R21) the same fields sit beside the classes: `{name, keys?, whole?, clears?, purge, expunge, export, sight, derive, version_chain}`.

**transact(fn) → fn's result**
- R32 · Runs `fn` as one transaction over the whole store: when `fn` throws or returns a refusal (`ok:false`), every row written inside it, in any module's tables, is rolled back, and no id allocated inside it is spent. This holds for a nested call too: it joins the outer transaction, and a nested call that throws or refuses rolls back its own writes and ids (a savepoint), while the outer call decides the rest. Rethrows what `fn` throws, after the rollback.

**bundleInfo(bundleId) → `{id, type, title, project}` or null; listBundles({project, after, limit}) → `{ids, cursor}`**
- R34 · `bundleInfo` answers a held bundle's type, title and project from this module's tables, or `null` when it is not held. `project` is the project id the last `commit` of the bundle named (R44), `null` for a bundle that belongs to no project.
- R35 · `listBundles` lists held bundle ids in id order after `after`, for `project` when given, at most `limit`; `cursor` is the last id listed. Never throws.

**stampInstant(precision, when?) → string; instantOrder(a, b) → number** (module-level functions, not methods)
- R47 · `stampInstant` spells the instant `when` (milliseconds since the epoch, default now) in UTC as ISO 8601: `"second"` gives `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`, `"millisecond"` gives `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.sssZ`; any other precision throws, naming it.
- R48 · `instantOrder` compares two instants in either spelling as instants, never as strings (`...:00Z` before `...:00.123Z`): negative, zero or positive, NaN when either side is not a non-empty readable instant. Never throws.
