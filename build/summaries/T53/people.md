## people (own requirements)

Status: in force, reviewed (K1505; T33-36; K1592); changed T36, T47, T48, T51. Size (P6): 1,800–2,300 lines expected, under 4,000; M-P5 (person read at 50,000 lines) and M-P6 run inside the job. No id carries a not-yet-met mark; none is retired.

Purpose: the people the record is about, held fully (K1452, K1455): identity claims linking person records, never merging them, and the derived cluster; dated person facts; reads gathering a person's positions, career, credentials, interests, statements and acts from their owners; staffing as of a date; members' declared ties; the protected source-to-person link; the machine's interest checks, in the hypothesis layer, shown only after a measured false-alarm rate. Every fact cited, dated, graded; the module never concludes about a person, never says two people know each other, stores no judgment on a person.

Terms: person = `entities` entity of kind `person`. Viewer = the control plane's stamp, read through `membership`; absent, fails closed. Validity = `civil-time`'s `{valid: {from, to, precision, zone}, basis}`, read only through `civil-time.validAt`; null bound = "not stated". Citation = a content extent `content` judges. Grade A–D (`record-grammar`). Refusal `{ok: false, reason, detail}` plus `code`, `check`, `translation` with a catalogue row. Every write stamped by the control plane (`by`); a caller's author field never read. A machine is named as one (`class:<cls>`, DEC-52).

Identity claims (`op=identityclaim`, `op=identitywithdraw`, `op=identity`):
- R1 · `claimIdentity({a, b, kind, basis, evidence, project?, note, by})` records one `IDC-` (opaque) between two distinct registered entities of one kind, person or organisation (`institution`, `body`, `movement`), kind `same_as`, `not_same_as`, `unsure`. Refusals in order: `NO_ENDS`; `SELF_CLAIM`; `NO_SUCH_ENTITY` (`entities.noSuchEntity`) naming the end; `NOT_A_PERSON` naming the end; `KIND_MISMATCH` naming both kinds; `UNKNOWN_CLAIM_KIND` naming the three; `UNKNOWN_BASIS`; `NO_EVIDENCE` (basis not `testimony`); `NO_NOTE`; `IDENTITY_GRADE_UNEARNED`. Answers `{ok, claim_id, kind, grade, why, at}`.
- R2 · The grade is earned from the basis (K1488), never stated by the caller: `identifier` earns A only when one scheme identifier (`entities`'), valid at both records' cited dates, is held on both `a` and `b`; `corroborated_name` earns B only when the evidence names a cited fact (a `lines` row, e.g. same post, licence or filer number) valid at both documents' dates, through `civil-time.validAt`; `name` earns C; `testimony` D. A failed condition is refused `IDENTITY_GRADE_UNEARNED`, naming it. `why` says, in Civicsmith's words, "claimed the same person, grade B, because …", never "is the same person".
- R3 · A machine may record only an `identifier` claim from source-native data with identifiers at both ends (K1470); else `MACHINE_CLAIM_REFUSED`.
- R4 · Never edited or deleted. `withdrawIdentityClaim({claimId, reason, by})` refuses `NO_REASON`, `NO_SUCH_CLAIM`, repeat `already: true`; marks withdrawn with who, when, why; a withdrawn claim links nothing; no act changes any `entities` row, resolution or alias.
- R5 · `identityOf({entityId, viewer})`: persons joined by `same_as` claims the viewer may see, transitively in one component, each link with claim, grade, why; state `linked`, or `undetermined` when a `not_same_as` joins two members (naming it); `unsure` listed, joins nothing; unregistered `found: false`; at most 500 members, 1,000 claims, `truncated` by one past.
- R6 · The cluster is a derived cache rebuilt in each claim's and withdrawal's transaction, read fail-closed: differing from its rebuild answers `undetermined` with the reason.
- R7 · `samePersonCandidates({entityId, limit, viewer})` (`op=samepersoncandidates`): entities of the same kind sharing a name's fold or a scheme identifier, each field (names, identifiers, posts, life facts) `agrees`/`differs`/`absent` with rows compared; no probability, score or rank; by entity id; `limit` 1–200 (default 50), `truncated`; other kinds `NOT_A_PERSON`; writes nothing; says candidates are not claims (D167).
- R8 · On the synthetic same-name fixture at most 0.5% of all-agreeing candidates are different people (M-P6; K1504); the job's test asserts it; not published to `op-declarations` until it passes.

Person facts (`op=personfact`, `op=personfactwithdraw`, `op=personexpunge`):
- R9 · `recordPersonFact({person, kind, value, valid, citation, by})` records one `PFA-`: `kind` closed (`name`, `birth`, `death`, `locality`, `address`, `contact`), value as cited, a validity, a citation, optional `question`. Refusals in order: `NO_SUCH_ENTITY`, `NOT_A_PERSON`, `UNKNOWN_FACT_KIND`, `NO_VALUE`, `NO_CITATION` (or `content`'s), `BAD_VALIDITY` (naming the bound), `QUESTION_NOT_HELD`. A found extent (`retrieval`'s `findIn`) is a citation like any other (R31). `question` is an inquiry bundle id (`record-core` R37), kept beside the fact, answered with it by every read to a viewer who may see that bundle, withheld as absent otherwise; none: `question: null`. Organisations: `name`, `address`, `contact` as for a person; `birth`, `death`, `locality` refused `NOT_A_PERSON`.
- R10 · `address`/`contact` only by a member's act (machine: `CONTACT_NOT_IMPORTED`); held in a `never`-export table (R33), answered only to a viewer who may see the citing capture, `publishable: false`.
- R11 · `withdrawPersonFact({factId, reason, by})` refuses `NO_REASON`, `NO_SUCH_FACT`; keeps the fact, marked withdrawn with who, when, why (DEC-19).
- R12 · `expunge({id, ground, demandKind?, order?, reason, by})` removes the value of a `PFA-`, `IDC-`, `MTI-` or source link (R21), leaving a tombstone `{id, ground, at, by}`, only on ground `unlawful`, `confidential`, `court_order` (naming the order) or `lawful_demand` with a `demandKind` the active profiles list. Refusals in order: `membership`'s `notAnAdmin`; `EXPUNGE_GROUND_REFUSED` naming the grounds and R11's act; `DEMAND_KIND_UNLISTED`; `NO_REASON`; `PERSON_ITEM_NOT_HELD` (id answering no fact, claim or tie, or a `{source, person}` naming no link). No read or later export answers the value; `person` answers `removed: [{ground, at, by}]` per tombstone, never the value (K5148).

## Used services

record-grammar.md Purpose: the shared grammar below every module reading or writing a document: id and type vocabulary, restricted front-matter parser, canonical JSON, actor identity, grade vocabulary, public-locator test, the one SHA-256; no store, network or clock.

jurisdictions.md Purpose: holds the jurisdiction profiles, every local fact the product uses as data with its measurement; lists, returns, validates and combines them; no other module names a place.

civil-time.md Purpose: the one pure engine for civil time (local days, precision and zone, EDTF bands, three-valued comparison, law-counted time rules, due dates, recurrences, fiscal periods, `validAt`); "now" is the caller's; local facts from the given jurisdiction view. Service `validAt({valid: {from, to, precision, zone}, basis}, date)` → `in` | `out` | `undetermined{why}`:
- civil-time R22 · `out` when `date` lies wholly before `from` or after `to`; `in` when wholly within both stated bounds; else `undetermined`. A null bound is "not stated", never "always": after a stated `from` with null `to` is `undetermined`, why "no end is stated".
- civil-time R23 · A bound may be `{event, edge, at?}`; without `at` undetermined ("the bounding event is not resolved"); value and event both: `BOUND_BOTH`; zone defaults to the view's `time_zone`.

connection-grammar.md Purpose: the one shape every relationship is presented in, the registry of owners of each kind and members' words for them, and the bounds and rules of walking; every owner registers its kinds and `neighbours` here; holds no relationship, walks nothing. Services `registerOwner({owner, kinds, neighbours})` (`kinds` `{kind, word, class}`), `ownerConformance({owner, neighbours, kinds, fixture, declares?})` → `{ok, failures, inapplicable}` (fixture: two viewers, one who may see a fenced item); R6 and R10 (`hubBoundOf`) per K5811 F2:
- connection-grammar R2 · Registers once; `class` `evidentiary`, `derived`, `declared`, `hunch`; `word` non-empty. Refused: `OWNER_DUPLICATE`, `KIND_TAKEN`, `CLASS_UNKNOWN`, `WORD_MISSING`, `NEIGHBOURS_MISSING`.
- connection-grammar R3 · A `word` containing "knows", "network", "conflict", "suspicious" or "most connected" is refused `WORD_FORBIDDEN`.
- connection-grammar R6 · `items` are connections in R1's shape, of the owner's own kinds within `kinds`, with `node` at one end; each is evaluated at `at` by `civil-time.validAt`, and one `out` at `at` is not returned, one `undetermined` is returned marked so, its `why` exactly as `civil-time` answers it (`{key, vars}`, civil-time R30; K3958), never re-worded or turned into a string here; the owner-conformance battery judges such a `why` as a key the word table holds (or a `localize` id) with a `vars` object. At most `BOUNDS.fanout` items per page; `next` continues; a node whose set of one kind exceeds that kind's bound (`BOUNDS.hub_by_kind`, R10) is answered `hub: {set_size, why}` with no items, never a partial set shown as whole. The read writes nothing and records no one's reading (D68).
- connection-grammar R9 · The battery checks R1's shape, the owner's kinds, R6's `at` rule, R7's sight (missing viewer refused), R8's hunch scope, paging stable and complete, the fan-out and hub rules, `derivedId`, labels, determinism; `declares: {undated?, group_wide?}` moves those checks to `inapplicable`, never `failures`.
- connection-grammar R10 · `BOUNDS` is frozen: `depth_default` 8, `depth_max` 10, `fanout` 1,000 per hop, `nodes` 5,000 visited, `hub` 1,000 members, `hub_by_kind`, and `time_budget_ms`, a default every walk may lower (K1470). `hub_by_kind` is the hub bound per kind, frozen: `event_voted` 4,000 members; every other kind `hub`'s 1,000 (a protective limit, K1881). `depthOf(requested)` answers 8 for none, 1 to 10 as requested, refuses `DEPTH_OVER_MAX` above 10. `hubBoundOf(kind)` answers that kind's hub bound (T36-40).

record-core.md Purpose: owns the record's storage (id allocation, leases, append-only history and manifest of promotions, purge); every storing module writes through it. Services `allocId(prefix, year)`, `transact(fn)`:
- record-core R1 · Mints from `ID_TABLE`: sequential `<prefix>-<year>-N`, four-digit padded, never cut, no ceiling, never reused even after purge; an opaque prefix gets R76's id. Never throws.
- record-core R2 · Identical step alone or inside a caller's transaction.
- record-core R32 · `transact` runs `fn` as one transaction over the whole store: a throw or refusal (`ok:false`) rolls back every row written, in any module, and spends no id; a nested call joins as a savepoint; rethrows after rollback.

membership.md Purpose: owns who the members are and what each may do (founder, administrators, invitations, capabilities, expertise) and projects at setup (participants, visibility, what a caller may see, the fence); no record integrity, no credential. Service `notAnAdmin(by, act, extra?)` → refusal (a module-level function); its block holds:

content.md Purpose: the unit the record points at, a part of a captured document: extent grammar, content address and row, attested transcriptions; never moves a reference.
