## network-notices (own requirements)

Status: in force, folded by BOB #94 at T23's opening (K1113; DEC-111, K1019, K1031, K1100); last changed T27 (N520: R21); every requirement met until T53, which marks R23, R30 and adds R32, each *(not yet met: T53)*. None retired. Size about 1,300–1,900 lines.

Purpose (as written; R23 amends it and its test greps it for "directory"): This module lets a project's owner tell the network that the group is working on something, and it keeps that notice honest. A notice is prepared from a real project and signed in the browser by one of the project's owners, as a published case is signed. It is published at the group's own public address. The copy computes what the owner cannot honestly assert alone, and signs those facts with its own instance key: the activity level, re-signed monthly, the cases the project has published, the seals opened, and the closing or lapse of the notice. The module seals each week of a project's work under an independent timestamp, and opens the seals that relate to a case when the case is published. It serves the group's public signing keys without names. It sends nothing anywhere. The network pages read what it publishes.

Terms: a **notice** is one "working on" statement about one project, with an opaque **notice id** never the project's id. A **revision** is one owner-signed version, format `civicsmith-working-on/1` (`civicos-working-on/1` before T31; DEC-124). An **attestation** is one statement about a notice signed with the copy's instance key (R13), format `civicsmith-working-on-attestation/1` (`civicos-…` before T31). A **week** is a UTC ISO week, Monday 00:00Z to the next. A **member act** is R8's; a **counted week** has at least one member act on the project. The **activity window** is the 13 complete weeks before an attestation's `as_of`. A **seal** is the digest committing to one week's member acts on one project (R15). The **project reference** is the notice id, carried by the notice and every later case of the project (R19).

`prepareNotice({project, notice?, wording, body?, matter?, since, collaborate, handoff?, final?, viewer, by}) → {ok, revision, statement, digest, warning, caution, expires}` (`op=noticeprepare`; member session; writes nothing).
- R1 · Refusals in order, each writing nothing with a catalogue row (DEC-49): no `project`, the required-argument refusal; absent or invisible to `by`, `membership`'s `noSuchProject` (same either way), but a project `by` sees only at `existence`, either form (`membership.sight` R44; D54, K2529), `membership.existenceAct`'s refusal (its R77, C-70.1); a machine or AI credential or operator token `MACHINE_CANNOT_POST_NOTICE`; `by` not an owner (`membership.isProjectOwner` R54) `NOTICE_NOT_THE_OWNER`; a project at stage `closed` (`project-stage` R2) `NOTICE_PROJECT_CLOSED`, unless `final` is `stopped` on an open notice (R11); no group slug (`promotion`'s `producingGroup`) `NOTICE_NO_GROUP_SLUG`; no instance key bound (`attestation.instanceKeyBound` R6) `NOTICE_NO_INSTANCE_KEY` (the check signs nothing, never setting `first_used`); `wording` empty, multi-line or over 280 characters, `body` or `matter` multi-line or over 120, `handoff` multi-line or over 280, `NOTICE_WORDING_MALFORMED`; `since` not a date `NOTICE_SINCE_MALFORMED`; before the project's creation (UTC date of its first history entry) `NOTICE_SINCE_BEFORE_PROJECT` naming it; after today (UTC) `NOTICE_SINCE_IN_FUTURE`; a first revision while the project has an open notice `NOTICE_ALREADY_OPEN` naming it (a change is a new revision, R6); a `notice` named that is not the project's latest unstopped notice, or a stop naming none, `NOTICE_NOT_OPEN` (C-127.14); `since` earlier than the notice's current `since` `NOTICE_SINCE_EARLIER` (C-127.15); a non-stop revision whose `wording`, `body`, `matter`, `since`, `collaborate` all equal the current ones `NOTICE_UNCHANGED` (C-127.16).
- R2 · Otherwise answers, changing nothing: `revision` as canonical JSON with exactly R3's fields; `digest` its SHA-256; `statement` = `signatures.noticeStatement(noticeId, revision number, digest)`; `warning` the outward-act warning (meaning: the public, including anyone examined, will see it, and stopping later will not unsay it; words the UX stream's, K1031 (5)); `caution` `two_open_without_published_work` when the group has published no case edition (`publication` R40) and already has two or more open notices, else null, never refusing (K1031 (4)); `expires` 60 minutes later. Same inputs on the same UTC day give identical bytes.
- R3 · A revision carries only: `format` (`civicsmith-working-on/1`; older revisions keep `civicos-working-on/1`, both accepted forever, a notice may mix them, `previous` the prior revision's digest over its stored bytes), `group` (slug), `notice` (minted at the first revision); `revision` (1, 2, …), `previous` (or null); `wording`, `body`, `matter` as the owner gave them (absent omitted; never filled from the project); `since`, `posted` (dates); `collaborate` (true only when chosen) and with it `doorbell`, the doorbell's path relative to the public address (`capture` R32); `status` `open` or `stopped` (R11), `handoff` (with `stopped` only); `others_welcome`, a fixed sentence. No member's name, handle or id; level, cases, seals, `closed`, `lapsed` appear only in attestations (R12). Test: an old-label revision verifies, a new one carries the new label, a mixed notice is served whole and in order (R20).

`postNotice({digest, signature, acknowledged, by}) → {ok, notice, revision, published_at, attestation}` (`op=noticepost`; member session; mutating).
- R4 · Refusals in order, writing nothing: R1's caller refusals again; `acknowledged` not exactly `true` `NOTICE_WARNING_NOT_ACKNOWLEDGED`; no prepared answer from `by` with this `digest`, or past `expires`, `NOTICE_STALE`; a signature `signatures.verifySshsig` rejects (namespace `NS_NOTICE`, over exactly `statement`, against `credentials.attestingKeys()` R11 restricted to `by`'s keys) `NOTICE_SIGNATURE_REFUSED` with the reason; a first revision whose notice id is already spent `NOTICE_STALE`. The post records the notice id through `record-core.recordOpaqueId` (its R75) inside its own transaction.
- R5 · Otherwise the revision is stored with its armored signature and first-published instant; in the same transaction an attestation (R12) is issued over it; both served by R20 from then.
- R6 · A change to an open notice (new wording, body or matter, `collaborate` changed, or a later `since` within R1's bounds) is a new revision through R1–R5, naming the prior in `previous`; the prior stays served; `group` and `notice` never change.

The activity level.
- R7 · Five words by counted weeks in the window: `Very active` (10–13), `Active` (7–9), `Some work` (4–6), `Quiet` (1–3), `Dormant` (0); cut-offs are this module's constants.
- R8 · A member act is a write to a project bundle (`record-core.listBundles({project})` R35) whose history entry has a member author (outside `record-grammar`'s non-member set, writer neither `mechanical` nor an AI run). A member's adoption of a machine draft counts, the draft does not; only whether a week has an act matters, never how many.
- R9 · `activity` is `{level, weeks_counted, window: 13, as_of, method}` (`method` R10's version).
- R10 · `activityMethod()`, no credential, answers the method as fixed text with its version (window, cut-offs, R8, what never counts); a change is a new version; old attestations keep naming theirs. Label `civicsmith-working-on-activity/1`; `civicos-working-on-activity/1` is listed as the same method; a rename is not a change.

Stopping, closing and lapse.
- R11 · An owner stops a notice with a final revision through R1–R5, `final: stopped`, carrying `status: stopped` and optional `handoff` (one line ≤280 characters, may name another group or an open lead). A stopped notice takes no further revision.
- R12 · An attestation carries only: `format` (`civicsmith-working-on-attestation/1`, old label accepted forever), `group`, `notice`, `as_of`, `kind`; `revision` (latest revision's digest); `status` `open`, `stopped`, `closed` or `lapsed`; `activity` at `as_of`; `cases` (public reference, case id and edition, of every case edition the project published, from `publication`'s `cases.project_id` and `published_cases` under R40); `seals` (each counted week's `{week, seal, timestamp_sha, untimestamped}`); `openings` (references so far, R17). `kind`, each issued once: `posted` (each revision), `monthly` (first UTC day of each month while open), `published` (a case edition published, R17), `closed` (project reaches `closed` while open; signed at once; an owner may still add a stop with handoff, K1100), `lapsed` (`Dormant` at two consecutive `monthly` attestations with no revision between; also goes into the group's record). A stopped, closed or lapsed notice takes no further `monthly`; it stays served, never deleted.
- R13 · An attestation is signed with the instance key (`attestation` R5) over `attestation.instanceStatement(<its R12 format>, sha256(attestation))`; pre-T31 ones stay signed over the old label and verify (tested). With no key bound when a `monthly` falls due it is not issued; the miss is stated in R22 and becomes a condition for the owners (`queue-producers` R27); the notice shows its last attested level with its date.

Seals: proof of activity.
- R14 · After each week ends, for every project not `closed` (notice or not), seals the week's member acts: each a leaf of bundle id, bundle digest after the act, operation and instant (never author), with its own random 256-bit salt; the week seal is the SHA-256 Merkle root over them, padded with salted dummy leaves to a fixed size; no act, no seal. Hash tags `civicos-seal-leaf/1`, `civicos-seal-node/1`, `civicos-week-leaf/1`, `civicos-seal-dummy/1` and method `civicos-working-on-seal/1` never change. `sealWake(now)` answers null when nothing is to seal (no timer held, `scheduler` R15), else the next due instant.
- R15 · One RFC 3161 timestamp per instance per week (`signatures.timestampRequest`, through `host-governor`, trying `TSA_ENDPOINTS` in order) over the root of all that week's project seals, token kept; when every authority fails the seals are kept, marked `untimestamped`, and still count.
- R16 · Salts and leaves are never served, exported or answered until opened (R17); a seal reveals nothing.
- R17 · `openSeals({case, edition})`, called by `ratification`'s ceremony once an edition is committed (`ratification` R37), no choice offered: for each sealed week of the project, publishes the leaves for acts on bundles the edition publishes (`publication.caseCitedParts` R41 and the members), each with salt, Merkle path to the project seal and week root, and the token; nothing else revealed; idempotent per (case, edition, week); format `civicsmith-working-on-opening/1` (old label accepted forever, `verifyOpening` included), `method` `civicos-working-on-seal/1`. An edition committed but not yet published whole (its R53) answers `NO_PUBLISHED_EDITION` and the request is kept; the `working-on-attest` tick opens it once published whole; no committed document keeps nothing. A `published` attestation follows. `attestWake(now)` null when no notice is open and no opening kept, else the next due instant.
- R18 · `verifyOpening(opening)` is pure: whether the leaves hash to the sealed root and the token is bound to it (`signatures.parseTimestampResponse`), checkable without this instance.

The project reference.
- R19 · `noticeReferenceOf(project)` answers the notice id of the project's open or most recent notice, or null; `case-authoring` writes it into the case document (`case-grammar` R10, `case-authoring` R41).

## Used services

record-grammar (`build/requirements/record-grammar.md`): the record's shared grammar (ids and types, front-matter parser, canonical JSON, actor identity, grades, public-locator test, SHA-256); no store, network or clock.

signatures (`build/requirements/signatures.md`): builds and verifies SSHSIG statements and RFC 3161 timestamp requests and responses; no record, stored private key or clock; every network call is the caller's.

record-core (`build/requirements/record-core.md`): owns the record's storage: id allocation, leases, append-only history and manifest, purge.
- record-core R6 · `mintOpaqueId(prefix, year, tail, taken) → id or null` returns `<prefix>-<year>-DDDD<tail>`, DDDD uniform over 0000–9999 (CSPRNG, rejection sampling), checked against `taken(id)` and every id ever handed out; a collision draws again.
- record-core R7 · Each id is recorded before return in the caller's transaction, so a rollback takes it back.
- record-core R8 · A recorded id is never drawn again, for any caller, purged or not; the ledger is exempt from both purges.
- record-core R9 · `null` only after 64 colliding draws in a row; never throws.
- record-core R75 · `recordOpaqueId(id) → {ok: true, id}` or refusal (N503): records `id`, an opaque id its caller chose, in the opaque-id ledger (`minted_ids`) inside the caller's transaction, as R7 records a drawn one: when that transaction rolls back, `id` is not recorded. Once recorded, R6 never draws it and R8 holds for it. Refusals, each recording nothing: an `id` that is not a non-empty string `OPAQUE_ID_MALFORMED`; an `id` already in the ledger (drawn, recorded or seeded, R40) `OPAQUE_ID_SPENT`, naming it; a call outside any `transact` (R32) `OPAQUE_ID_NO_TRANSACTION`. Never throws.
- record-core R63 · `registerCounts(module, keys, counts)`: once, at start, a non-empty key list and a synchronous `counts(hid)` (`hid` `{sql, args}` or null, passed on unread); answers every key in order, null (never zero) when unreadable. A held key or second registration `COUNTS_DECLARED` naming the holder; malformed `COUNTS_MALFORMED` (C-102.13, .14); a refused one registers nothing; never throws.
- record-core R21 · `declarePurge(module, tables, {exempt?})` is `declareTable`'s default form (once, at start): each table `purge` `exempt` if listed else `clear`, `expunge` `none`, `export` `admin-only`, `sight` `bundle` when keyed to a bundle else `group`, `derive` `stored`, `version_chain` `false`. Explicit classes: `purge`, `expunge` (`tombstone`/`none`), `export` (`yes`/`admin-only`/`never`), `sight` (`group`/`bundle`/`source`/`owner`), `derive`, `version_chain`; missing or unknown `TABLE_CLASS_MISSING`/`TABLE_CLASS_UNKNOWN`; declared twice or by two modules `TABLE_DECLARED`. `purge` clears every `clear` table; `declaredTables()` lists all.
- record-core R46 · A table is keyed by its `bundle_id` column, or `{name, keys, whole}` (any key column matching; whole-store clear only where the clause holds).
- record-core R32 · `transact(fn)`: one transaction over the whole store; a throw or `ok:false` rolls back every row in any module's tables and spends no id; a nested call is a savepoint the outer call decides over; rethrows after rollback.
- record-core R47 · `stampInstant(precision, when?)` (module-level): UTC ISO 8601, `"second"` or `"millisecond"`; another precision throws, naming it.
- record-core R48 · `instantOrder(a, b)` compares instants as instants, never strings; NaN for an unreadable side; never throws.

membership (`build/requirements/membership.md`): who the members are and what each may do, and projects at setup (participants, visibility, sight, the fence); no credential.
- membership R77 · `existenceAct(projectId, viewer) → refusal or null` (N76): answers `PROJECT_SEEN_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (C-70.1: the project's id and name only, with its check id and translation) when `viewer`'s sight of `projectId` is existence only (R43), else `null`; `null` when no `viewer` is given. When the viewer is an administrator (the founder included) at a hidden project's `EXISTENCE` (R44), and for that case only, C-70.1 also carries `owners`, the owners' current handles in R65's order (D54); for every other caller no `owners` key. Every act of this module and of `promotion` that names a project asks it before its own no-such-project answer, so C-70.1 is minted only here. Never throws.
- membership R78 · `noSuchProject(projectId, extra?) → refusal` (module-level): the one answer when no project answers to `projectId` or the caller's sight is not `FULL` (after R77), answered as absent: `{ok: false, reason: "NO_SUCH_PROJECT", code: "NO_SUCH_PROJECT", check, translation, project, detail}`, `project` as asked (null when none), `detail` one fixed sentence for every caller; `extra` adds fields, never replacing these. Every module answers this condition through it (one row, membership's). Writes nothing; never throws.
