Size (P6): about 2,150 lines; past about 2,500 the cost relay, follows and later-found move to a module directly after it (BOB's, at the job's report).

Purpose: a step is work done by a member or the system toward an answer to a question, or to give context, develop a question, or understand how to respond to findings (D32, D35). The module holds each step: its place (and so who sees it), doer, state and per-question outcome, products, waits and dates, money cost, the duplicate search, each member's following of a question (D36), and the later arrival of data a dead end looked for (H39). A step is never evidence and writes no leg (D33).

Terms: a step is `STP-` (`record-grammar` R51). Its place is exactly one of `{questions: [inquiry ids]}`, `{project: id}` or `{group: true}`, fixed at creation. A reference is a question's link to a question-placed step. Doer is a member's handle, or `system` with `enabled_by` (`ai-use` R6's label). Taken in is the project the creating act named (`project`, the caller's own joined project), or none. Touched: started, or holding a product, a look, a learned line or a cost. A viewer is the control plane's stamp; an absent viewer fails closed. Every refusal names its reason and nothing is written.

`record-core` (`build/requirements/record-core.md`): owns the record's storage (id allocation, leases, append-only history and manifest of every promotion, purge); holds no member, capability, fence or promotion write path; every storing module writes through and reads from it.

`content` (`build/requirements/content.md`): content is the unit the record points at, a reference to part of a captured document up to the whole: the extent grammar, the content address `hash(capture, extent, chain)`, the content row and its transcription-axis claim, a member's typed transcription a second member attests; says whether a newer capture carries a cited passage; never moves a reference.

`entities` (`build/requirements/entities.md`): the entity axis and subject registry: things a case is about, with first-class aliases and member-declared constitutive relations; resolves a reading's references to entities with the §8.1 grade (A, B, C by recogniser; D only by testimony); answers which documents concern or name an entity; judges shared identifiers (§8.3); derives no connection.

`connections` (`build/requirements/connections.md`): the record's connections: derived connections (two captured documents resolving to one entity, graded by the weaker end), edges between bundles (`references[]` projected: who cites what, backlinks, dangling, `links_to`), and themes (a lens for finding, never a claim's basis); grade states how a connection was established, never credibility.

`observation-log` (`build/requirements/observation-log.md`): records looking (each look, at which of four levels, under what authority, what it found, including nothing or undetermined), append-only and apart from the record; holds the one append site, the shared vocabulary, writers driven by events, rules for reading a missing row, the viewer fence, and the member's lead (somewhere to look, never evidence).

`leg-earning` (`build/requirements/leg-earning.md`): what the record can earn for a leg of an inquiry's basis and which questions rest on a target: the earned registry, the earned basis as a viewer may read it, projected and resting legs, the one cycle walk, and the projects drawing on a question; writes no basis, grades no conclusion.

`setup-words` (`build/requirements/setup-words.md`): the interface's word list as frozen data (key, English, note, meaning, protected mark) as the design stream's `words.json` gave them at a named commit; pure data; `instance-setup` reads it.
