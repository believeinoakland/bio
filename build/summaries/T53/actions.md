Status: in force (K102); R112-R116 T53, not yet met. Moved ids never reused; R53, R84, R89-R91, R96 unused.

Purpose. The Action object (State Rules v1.5 §4.4): what the group sends or does outside the system, to whom, why, under which laws, at what legal exposure, by when, and what came back: kind, risk tier, counterparty, clock (each deadline with its basis), basis legs, lifecycle, correspondence ledger; governing laws and tier as members' acts (proposals, receipts, scopes, promises are `action-records`'); fee quotes and the records-request lifecycle as evidence; the action's own outcome (DEC-14). Clock reads across actions, proposed entries, reminders: `action-clocks`. A member decides every move; a machine prepares and never advances, testifies, sets a tier or states a law.

Terms. Action = bundle whose type normalises to `action`. Document: `action_kind`, `risk_tier` (1, 2, 3, `undetermined`; absent = `undetermined`), `counterparty`, `clock[]` (`{text, description, date, basis, status}`; `pending`, `met`, `overdue`, `waived`), `action_basis[]` (`{target, kind, note?, date?, extent_capture?}`; `rests_on` | `advances`), `correspondence[]`, `governing_laws[]` + `_by`/`_at`, `risk_tier_history[]`, `resolution`, `law` (`records_request` only), `consequence` (own outcome, not the breach consequence). Lifecycle: `planned -> active | abandoned`; `active -> awaiting_response | resolved | abandoned`; `awaiting_response -> active | resolved | abandoned`; `resolved`, `abandoned` final. Refusal `{ok: false, reason, ...}`; catalogue-backed adds `code`, `check`, `translation`.
