## escalation (own requirements)

Status: approved by Bob 2026-09-26 (K102); R11-R12 K14; R14's `CONSEQUENCES_UNDETERMINED` K172; DEC-89 K1019.

Purpose: the escalation protocol (Design Requirement 7 as amended): stages bringing a government act back into conformance, from a live noncompliant determination to its end, each with entry condition, defined acts, trigger for the next. The next stage is proposed when its trigger is met, from the record at reading; a member advances it. Ends only when compliance is restored and consequences addressed (OP 6). Decides nothing about significance, no position on policy (OP 1).

Terms: stages `1 documentation`, `2 notification`, `3 clock`, `4 response_evaluation`, `5 legal_tools`, `6 sustained_attention`, `7 political_accountability`. Stage table: 1→2; 2→3; 3→4; 4→5, 4→7; 5→6, 5→7; 6→4; 7→4. State `open`, `suspended`, `ended` (and `stopped_pursuing`, R31). Breach action: document states `breach: true` and rests on the escalation's determination (`actions` R8). Refusal `{ok: false, reason, ...}`.

- R1 · `escalationOpen({determination, reason, author, viewer}) → {ok: true, id, stage: 1, proposed}`. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_OPEN` (empty/machine author); `ESCALATION_NO_REASON` (R24; absent, blank, over 2,000 chars); `NO_SUCH_DETERMINATION` (absent/invisible, one answer; `conformance.noSuchDetermination`); `DETERMINATION_SUPERSEDED` (`conformance.determinationSuperseded`); `DETERMINATION_VOID` (`conformance.determinationVoided`, its R33; K2850); `NOT_NONCOMPLIANT` (`conformance` R4); `ESCALATION_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (author not joined in the determination's project; C-116.6, K275); `ALREADY_OPEN` (open/suspended one named). Else opens at stage 1 recording standards pursued, reason, who, when. Any joined member may open. (DEC-89 (1); K1019)
- R2 · `escalationRead({id, nowMs?, viewer}) → {id, state, stage, history, standards, actions, triggers, proposed, exit}`. At `nowMs` (caller's, else instance clock) derives per edge out of the current stage whether its trigger (R4-R10) is met, naming meeting ids or what is missing; `proposed` lists met ones with the instant first met (latest date of the meeting ids: a `sent` entry's `at`; for a clock the day after its date; never read time; K171) and age. `NO_SUCH_ESCALATION` for absent/invisible. Also `context` (R32). Server instant (R37).
- R3 · `exit` answers R14's two conditions separately, each `met`, `not_met` or `undetermined` with ids and why, never composed into a score.
- R4 · Stage 1: entered by R1; acts: published findings and the determination. Trigger to 2: determination live and the act's actor is an office. Actor resolved to its `office` entity (`conformance` R25 `entity_id`, via `entities`), read by stage 2 and stage 7 targets (R12); no entity held still triggers, read states unresolved.
- R5 · Stage 2: act is a breach action to the actor's office, attached here (R9). Trigger to 3: its ledger holds a `sent` entry (`actions` R16).
- R6 · Stage 3: act is a clock entry with basis on the notification action, stated by a member (`action-clocks` R2 may propose; K617). Trigger to 4: after `sent`, a `received` or `no_response` entry, or the clock's earliest pending entry is past (`actions` R12 at `nowMs`). No clock entry never triggers by time; read says so. `reply_owed: false` (`actions` R82) never triggers by time or lack of reply; read says "no reply owed".
- R7 · Stage 4: act is a member's evaluation (R10). Triggers to 5 and 7: latest evaluation `denied`, `partial` or `none`; `complied` proposes no stage, points at R14. Partial compliance recorded, does not stop the clock or end the escalation (FA Layer 3 Function 5). Trigger names the evaluation `<id>/evaluation#<n>`, `n` its place (1, 2, ...) among this escalation's evaluations, counting no other entry (N462; DEC-36, K913).
- R8 · Stage 5: acts are breach actions attached here with filings/counsel packets (`filings.filingsFor`); availability by tier from the profile's kinds for the actor's office via `filings.availableActions` (its R21), never the published case (K171). Trigger to 6: an attached action's ledger holds `sent`. To 7: as R7, from the evaluation in force, R7's id (N462).
- R9 · `escalationAttach({id, action, purpose?, standards?, reason, author, viewer})` to the current stage, which must be 2, 5 or 7. Refusals: `MACHINE_CANNOT_ATTACH`; `ESCALATION_NO_REASON` (R24); `NO_SUCH_ESCALATION`; `NO_SUCH_ACTION`; `ACTION_PREMISE_OVERRIDDEN` (R23; after `NO_SUCH_ACTION`, before next); `NOT_A_BREACH_ACTION` (no `breach: true`, or no `rests_on` leg naming this determination); `STAGE_TAKES_NO_ACTION` (stages 1, 3, 4, 6); `ALREADY_ATTACHED`. One escalation, one stage per action; reason recorded. At stage 7 records R12's `purpose` and `standards`, checked here; `actions`' document unchanged (K171).
- R10 · `escalationEvaluate({id, response?, reading, reason, author, viewer})`: reading `complied`, `partial`, `denied` or `none`, naming the response (`{action, ord}` of a `received` entry; none for `none`), reason 1-2,000 chars. Refusals: `MACHINE_CANNOT_EVALUATE`; `NO_SUCH_ESCALATION`; `NOT_IN_EVALUATION` (stage not 4); `READING_UNKNOWN`; `NO_SUCH_RESPONSE` (not a `received` entry of an attached action); `RESPONSE_FOR_NONE`; `ESCALATION_NO_REASON` (R24). - R12 · Stage 7, entered from 4 or 5 (K14).
