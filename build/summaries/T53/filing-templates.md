## filing-templates (own requirements)
- R15, R33, R35 · shortened in this summary below their source (R15: when `@records-request` stays unresolved; R33: the kinds never asked; R35: the `TEMPLATE_NOT_REVIEWED` reason by key): read each whole in build/requirements/filing-templates.md before changing it

**Status** (§Status): in force, approved by Bob 2026-10-01 (K921; K924 governs; K922 (1) moved `FILING_BLANKS`, `FILING_TEXT_MAX` here). Last changed T48 (R33). R15, R35, R36, R37, R38 are *(not yet met: T53)*.

**Purpose** (R1): the group's governed library of filing templates: wording with named blanks that a group may file in its own name (Tier 1 and 2 kinds) or send to counsel as the basis of a briefing (any tier). Versioned, never edited; each version carries notes, whole attribution, reviews (by members, or professionals through a revocable grant) and approval by a member who is not its sole author. Every approved version (current and updated) is offered, the latest by default; a retired template offers none. A machine proposes wording and nothing else. The profile's templates are read as approved versions, read-only.

**Terms** (R1, R2, R14, R21, R27): **state**: `draft`, `in_review`, `approved`, `updated` (an approved version a later approved one follows, naming it; K924), `withdrawn` (`TEMPLATE_STATES`, R21). **retired**: withdrawn whole (R11). **use**: `file` or `brief` (`TEMPLATE_USES`). **origin**: `group` or `profile` (`imported` reserved, never written). **blank**: `{{name}}`, `name` a key of `FILING_BLANKS` (R19). **offered**: `approved` or `updated` and template not retired (R14); nothing else. **tier** of a kind: the profile's (`jurisdictions` R25) in the combined view, the strictest across the profiles named (for `general`, the view), undetermined when none states one. Version id `<template>@<version>`. Refusal `{ok: false, reason, code, check, translation, ...}`. `author`, `by`, `proposer`, `viewer`, `secretSha` are the control plane's stamps; `secretSha` arrives in the body, the rest in the query (R27).

- R1 · Template `{id, kind, use, profiles, name, scope, origin, versions}`. `id` opaque `TPL-`. `kind` matches `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*$` (`TEMPLATE_KIND_REFUSED`, C-115.33); with a `profiles` list, an `action_kinds` kind of every profile named (`TEMPLATE_KIND_UNKNOWN`, naming the profile). `use` `file`/`brief` (`TEMPLATE_USE_REFUSED`). `profiles`: non-empty list of held profile ids (`TEMPLATE_PROFILE_UNKNOWN`) or `general` (offered for its kind under every profile). A `file` template on a kind of tier 3 in any profile named (for `general`, the active view) is `TEMPLATE_TIER3_FILE` (C-115.36); a `brief` may serve any tier. `name` one line 1-200 characters (`TEMPLATE_NAME_REFUSED`, C-115.32), not held by another non-retired template of the scope (`TEMPLATE_NAME_TAKEN`, C-115.37). `scope` `{project}` or `group` once R10 widens it.
- R2 · Version `{template, version, text, sha, state, notes, author, contributors, derived_from, reviews, approved, ended}`; `version` counts from 1. `text` non-empty UTF-8, at most `FILING_TEXT_MAX` bytes (`TEMPLATE_TEXT_REFUSED`, C-115.35), every blank a key of `FILING_BLANKS` (`TEMPLATE_BLANK_UNKNOWN`, naming the first unknown). `sha` SHA-256 lowercase hex of `text`. Past `draft`, text and `sha` never change. `derived_from`: null or `{filing, sha}`, `{version}`, `{proposal}`.
- R3 · **templateDraft({template?, project?, kind?, use?, profiles?, name?, text, from?, notes?, author, viewer})** (`op=templatedraft`) → `{ok, template, version, sha, state: "draft"}`. Creates a template and first draft (R1 judges), or a new draft of `template` keeping kind, use, profiles, name, scope. `from`: a version the author may see, a proposal (R6), or only through `filings`' service (`filings` R32) `{filing, sha}`.
