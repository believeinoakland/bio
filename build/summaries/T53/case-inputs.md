Purpose: what other acts record about a case before its next edition is prepared, which `case-authoring`'s `op=publish` reads into the document (Publication §3, §6A; Action v0.2 §3, rules 8, 17): the statement's acknowledgements (members and review recipients say they read the statement before signing) and who wrote it; drafts of a new edition's statement of what changed; the one source of a case's open requests for comment at signing (`action-replies` fills it); outcomes a member carries into the next edition; and the case's standard details (R20–R28), beside the case and never in its signed document. `case-authoring` asks and writes the answers into the document; this module writes no case document but an unsigned one's acknowledgement list (R2), and calls nothing of `case-authoring`'s.

Provides, terms: terms are `case-authoring`'s and `publication`'s. A statement is a case document's completeness statement. A case's details are R21's four fields (area of government, point of contact, related work, relationship disclosure; no classification, K4185); a field's standing value is its latest entry; "not stated" is no entry or a clear. `viewer`, `author`, `proposedBy` and `by` are the control plane's stamps (R13). Every refusal names `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check`, `code` and `translation` (R12).
