Purpose: the plane's half of the assistant: what an ask may read, what an answer must look like, and the checks every answer passes before a member sees it, so every rule comes from the plane and every quote, figure and absence is bound to what was read. It also answers the plane's rule services (law, time, organisations, people, figures) and keeps a member's standing questions, which re-run their saved search on the schedule and call the model only when something new is found. The model only understands the member's words, writes queries, quotes and translates; it never answers from its own knowledge.

Terms: an **ask** is one member's question, answered under a **grant**: `credentials`' short-lived, read-only `ai` grant minted at that member's act, with that member as viewer, writing no run row (K1450). The **read log** is the set of reads the plane served under one grant, each with its op, its arguments and what it answered. A **level** and the **absence terms** are `observation-log`'s (four levels; five terms). A **rule item** is one answer of R12. Every refusal names `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check`, `code` and `translation` (DEC-49).

## Used services

record-grammar (`build/requirements/record-grammar.md`): the record's shared grammar: id and type vocabulary, restricted front-matter parser, canonical JSON, actor identity, grade vocabulary, public-locator test, the one SHA-256; no store, network or clock.

jurisdictions (`build/requirements/jurisdictions.md`): the jurisdiction profiles, every local fact as data with its measurement; lists, returns, validates, combines them; no other module names a place.

civil-time (`build/requirements/civil-time.md`): the one pure civil-time engine: local days, date-times with precision and zone, EDTF bands, three-valued comparison, time rules on a jurisdiction's calendar, due dates with basis kind, recurrences, fiscal periods, `validAt`; "now" is the caller's.

calc-grammar (`build/requirements/calc-grammar.md`): the pure calculation engine: figures read exactly as printed as exact decimals with unit, currency, precision; recipes in the closed grammar `bio-calc/1`, refusing forbidden totals by name; recorded random draws.

record-core (`build/requirements/record-core.md`): the record's storage: id allocation, leases, append-only history and manifest of every promotion, purge; no member, fence or promotion write path.

membership (`build/requirements/membership.md`): who the members are and what each may do (founder, administrators, invitations, capabilities, expertise) and projects at their setup, their visibility and fence; no record integrity and no credential.

credentials (`build/requirements/credentials.md`): the credentials a person or the instance acts by (passwords, sessions, recovery, signer keys, AI work credentials, the short-lived grant an ask reads under, the group's outside-service keys, refused-request tally); membership decides who; `ai-accounts` decides which AI account.
