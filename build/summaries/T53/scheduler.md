## scheduler (own requirements)
- R1-R35 · most Test sentences are cut to file names in this summary: read a requirement whole in build/requirements/scheduler.md before writing or changing its test

**Status.** In force, approved by Bob 2026-09-26 (K102). R34, R35 (T53) not yet met; R1, R28-R33 (T51) not yet met; others met.

**Purpose.** The plane's periodic work runs on one reconciling Durable Object alarm, never on a cron (SCHEDULER.md). Holds the consumer registry, runs the due consumers when the alarm fires, keeps the alarm at the earliest wake any wants and deletes it when none does, so an idle instance holds no timer.

**Terms.** A **consumer** is `{name, key, due(now), wake(now), tick(now)}`: `wake` the instant it next wants the alarm or null; `due` the instant it is due or null; `tick` does the work and answers what it did. The **grace** is 250 ms.

**onAlarm(now) → answer**; `alarm()` is `onAlarm(Date.now())`
- R1 · Runs the `tick` of every consumer whose `due(now)` is not null and at most `now` + grace, in registry order from the consumer R30 names, awaiting each for at most what is left of the firing's budget (R30, R31); then reconciles authoritatively over the whole registry: alarm set to the smallest non-null `wake(now)`, or deleted when none.
- R2 · Answer `{swept, drained, created, folded, refused, waiting, remaining, rearmed, nextAt, probes}` (drain's counts, zero when it did not tick; `rearmed` whether an alarm is set; `nextAt` its instant or null; `probes` the test probes that ticked), plus each other consumer that ticked under its key: `monitor`, `connderive`, `overduescan`, `queuerenotify`, `monitorcadence`, `airunreap`, `capturerequests`, `airunwake`, `calibration`, `groupdomain`, `biasdebt`, `deadlinerecheck`, `gatheringsweep`, `workingonseal`, `workingonattest`, `follow`, `dutytransitions`, `interestchecks`, `moneydetectors`, `standingquestions`, `datedwaits`, `scheduledpublish`, `filescan`, `filerender`, `filedeeper`, `fileforward`, `filereputation`, `doccopy`, a registered consumer's own; T53 adds `registerfileimport` (R34), `discardclear`, `mailclear` (R35). A consumer that did not tick is absent. A real consumer never appears in `probes`.
- R3 · A consumer whose `due`, `wake` or `tick` throws is answered under its key as `{error}` with the message; every other consumer still ticks and the reconcile still runs.

**arm(now) → instant | null** (the producers' door)
- R4 · Reconciles without firing: alarm set when none is set or the earliest wake is sooner, never pushed later; deleted when no consumer wants one; answers the alarm as it stands; runs no tick, writes no work. An alarm past by more than a minute, with no firing running, is spent: `arm` sets it afresh to the later of the earliest wake and now. A live alarm is never pushed later.

**Heartbeat, watchdog, budget, in-flight, ledger clock, re-entry** (T51 fix round 9)
- R28 · `heal(now)` is the request paths' door: with no alarm or a spent one it arms as `arm` (R4); otherwise asks no consumer anything; at most one look in five seconds per instance, none inside a firing. Every firing records storage value `sched_beat` `{last_run, running_since, watchdog_seen, watchdog_ran}`: `running_since` at start, cleared when it ends however it ends, `last_run` when it completes. `health(now)` answers `{last_run, running_since, watchdog_seen, watchdog_ran, alarm_at, oldest_overdue, stale}`: `oldest_overdue` the earliest wake already come (or null); `stale` true when work is owed and either the oldest wake already come is at least 300 s old and no firing has completed in 300 s, or one firing has run longer than 300 s (`running_since` at least 300 s old); a firing completes at its stored end instant `ended` (`last_run` when absent); one in progress does not reset the clock. False for: work owed under 300 s, a row in back-off, a firing every 10 s, a row owed 400 s with a firing 20 s ago, nothing owed. A process signal only; re-arms nothing. Every firing also records `{scheduled_for, started, ended, next_set_for, gap_ms}` and prints it as `{"scheduler_alarm": {...}}`; no pace change. Amended (G159; K4980): `heal` also arms when a consumer's wake already come is earlier than the alarm set. Tests: `r7-pace.test.mjs`, `r8-stale.test.mjs` over `alarmDouble`.
- R29 · `watch(at)` is the watchdog's one call, by the plane Worker's cron once a cycle (K4566, K4570, K4574). Records `watchdog_seen` (the cron's slot, never backwards) and `watchdog_ran` (instant the call ran, from its own clock; G94) and re-arms outright, to the later of the earliest wake and now, only when: no alarm is set and no firing in progress (`running_since` within a cycle); or the alarm is spent; or work is owed, no firing in progress, `last_run` older than two cycles and the alarm still ahead. Runs no tick, reads no consumer's rows or any document; idempotent: a late or repeated call with a live alarm and fresh `last_run` sets nothing and asks nothing. Answers `{alarm_at, last_run, running_since, watchdog_ran, rearmed, why}` (`why` null, `no_alarm`, `spent`, `stale`).
- R30 · A firing has a budget and rotates. Its consumers share about 10 s (`FIRING_BUDGET_MS`, an injected constant; tests inject 50 ms). When the budget is spent before every due consumer has ticked, the firing starts no further consumer, reconciles (R1) and ends; the next firing begins at the consumer after the last one that ran, so no consumer is always last and a slow one ahead of `pending-work` (registered through R8, after R5's list) never holds its tick for ever. A consumer past its turn at a firing's end is not run twice in it. Every consumer a firing runs is recorded as `{"scheduler_consumer": {name, started, ended, outcome}}`, written before and after the await past an injected threshold (R-LOG); the heartbeat (R28) carries the last firing's slowest consumer and holds `rotate_from` (null when the last firing reached every consumer), `inflight` (`{name: started}`) and `slowest` (`{name, ms}`). While a firing runs the alarm is never read in the past: an `arm` made inside a firing weighs no wake and sets nothing past; it leaves the alarm as it stands or, when the platform answers none, sets the instant the firing can no longer outlast (start, budget and grace). The reconcile at the firing's end weighs every wake, such an arm's work included, and sets the next alarm outright; a firing with no arm leaves `getAlarm()` none. Tests: `r8-budget.test.mjs` (budget 50 ms; `scanlike` gated before `reading`, not started twice), `r9-visible.test.mjs`, `plane/r8-headofline.test.mjs` (R80).
- R31 · A call that outlasts the budget is neither abandoned nor started twice. A consumer whose call is still running when the budget is spent stays in an in-flight set: a later firing does not ask its `due` or start it again while in flight, and takes its result when it settles; a rejection after the firing is that consumer's `{error}` (R3) at the next firing. The bound on a service-binding call (scanner's, renderer's) is `file-safety`'s; the budget is the firing's. Test: gated past the budget, not started at the second firing.
- R32 · The ledger's clock is the row's own. The scheduler states to each consumer it runs the instant that consumer's own work began (third argument of `tick(now, rank, started)`) and records it (R30's line), so a consumer stamping how long its row took (`store-assembly` R71's `last_ms`) reads its own start, never the alarm's; `last_mem` is NULL when unmeasured, as workerd measures none.
- R33 · A firing is idempotent on re-entry. An alarm that completed and runs again (platform retry) or was cut by a deploy and restarted from the top ticks each due consumer again; the heartbeat, the held set (R27) and the in-flight set (R31) are re-derived from storage at the start of every run, not trusted from memory, and a `running_since` left by a cut run is cleared at the next start.
