## docket (own requirements)

Status: in force, a new product module (N520, DEC-116, DEC-100); readings and place BOB's (K1134 (4), K1256, K1257); codes its own (N533; K1331, K1291); last changed T47 (R12 amended, R29 new). T53 adds R32, R33, each *(not yet met: T53)*. None retired. Size about 1,600–2,400 lines.

Purpose: after a case is published, the world responds without changing the case. The module keeps each case's docket: dated entries beside the case, each naming the edition it concerns, on three shelves. Any member files to the group's record; only the case's manager places an entry in public, signed with their registered key. It holds the withdrawal of an edition, grants of standing, and the required core the manager is asked to list, and answers the public shelves and a feed per case for anybody to pull. It sends nothing anywhere, and no entry is ever evidence (DEC-116; DEC-100; Publication §5D).

Terms: the **case** and its **editions** are `publication`'s; an edition an entry names is a ratified edition number of that case. The **manager** of a case is an owner of its owning project (`membership.isProjectOwner`, its R54). The **named subject** of an edition is each subject entity of its member findings (`inquiry.subjectEntityOf`, its R43). A **holder** is one to whom a grant of standing (R10) was made; a grant is **live** from its entry's `date` until a `standing-withdrawn` entry ends it. **Shelves**: `listed` ("what the group stands behind as accurately listed"), `reactions` ("Reactions elsewhere"), `record` ("In our record only"); their labels and every word a member or reader sees (outward-act warning, invitation, stamp) are the UX design stream's, never decided here. A **record entry** is a member's filing on `record`; a **public entry** a manager's signed entry on `listed` or `reactions`.

Filing: `docketFile({case, edition, kind, from, capture, contests?, proposed, reason, author, viewer})` (`op=docketfile`; member session; mutating).
- R1 · Any joined member of the case's project files a record entry. Refusals, in order, each writing nothing and carrying a catalogue row (DEC-49): (K5148) the summary filed with an entry is kept for every kind that has one, and `docketPrepare` uses it when the prepare names none (R4). A machine or AI credential, or an operator token, is `MACHINE_CANNOT_FILE_DOCKET`; a case that is absent, has no ratified edition, or whose project `viewer` does not see in full (`membership.sight`, its R44) is `NO_SUCH_CASE`, the same answer for each; `author` not a joined participant of the project (`membership.projectAuthority`'s "joined", its R55) is `PROJECT_ACT_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT`, membership's row and words, relayed; `DOCKET_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (C-129.2) is no longer answered; then the form checks, never merit: `edition` not a ratified edition of the case is `DOCKET_NO_EDITION`; `from` absent is `DOCKET_NOT_ATTRIBUTED`; `from: {kind: "subject", entity}` naming no named subject of that edition is `DOCKET_NOT_THE_SUBJECT`; `from: {kind: "holder", grant}` naming no grant live at this instant (R10) is `DOCKET_NO_STANDING`; `from: {kind: "other", name}` with `name` absent, blank or over 200 characters is `DOCKET_NOT_ATTRIBUTED`; `capture` not a capture `provenance`'s register holds (its R48) with a locator in `captured_locators` is `DOCKET_NO_CAPTURE`; `kind` not one of `response` (from the subject or a holder), `statement` (the subject's public statement elsewhere, a press release included), `reaction` (anyone else) or `outcome` is `DOCKET_KIND_UNKNOWN`; `proposed` not one of `record`, `public`, `both`, or `reason` absent, blank or over 2,000 characters, is `DOCKET_NO_REASON`. Otherwise it records the entry with its instant, `author`, `from`, `capture`, `proposed`, `reason`, `contests` (true only when the member says the response contests the edition) and `found_by`: `watch` when the capture was filed with a sweep origin (`acquisition` R21's `origin.kind: "sweep"`, the origin `capture` R82 reads), else `paste`. A contesting entry is reported to `reevaluation` (R13). A record entry of kind `outcome` also states `outcome: {kind, on}` (`kind` is `cure`, a later act of the office that the member links as the cure, or `harm_addressed`, a member's reasoned statement that a harm was addressed; `on` the date the outcome occurred, a calendar date no later than the filing's day) and `summary` (the member's words, one paragraph of 1 to 600 characters, as R4 bounds a `reactions` summary). Refusals, after R1's form checks, each writing nothing: `outcome` absent, its `kind` not one of the two, or `on` not such a date, `DOCKET_OUTCOME_UNREADABLE` (a new row of C-129, numbered at its stamp); a missing or malformed `summary`, `DOCKET_NO_SUMMARY`, as R4. An entry of any other kind stating `outcome` is refused `DOCKET_OUTCOME_UNREADABLE`. An `outcome` entry is filed by a member through this op only, at once; no other module files it. It is never core (R9), and is placed on `listed` only by the manager's signed act (R4, R5). A reply from an office that is not a named subject files as a `response` once the manager grants it standing (R10); no new `from` kind. Controls: an `outcome` without `outcome`, with an unknown kind, or with `on` after today, is refused; a `response` stating `outcome` is refused; a well-formed outcome files.
- R2 · `docketPressure({entry, pressure: {kind, note}, author, viewer})` (`op=docketpressure`; mutating) appends a threat mark to a record entry, never rewriting it: `kind` `legal`, `retaliation`, `discrediting` or `other`; `note` ≤500 characters. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_MARK_DOCKET_PRESSURE`; absent or not seen in full `NO_SUCH_DOCKET_ENTRY` (one answer); not joined `PROJECT_ACT_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (relayed); already marked `DOCKET_PRESSURE_MARKED`; bad kind or note `DOCKET_PRESSURE_REFUSED` (own rows, R22; `action-grammar`'s C-117.14, .15, .17 never answered here).
- R3 · `docketOf({case, viewer})` (`op=docket`; read) answers a full-sight viewer every entry on all three shelves, oldest first: each record entry with `proposed`, `reason`, `found_by`, `contests`, pressure mark and state (`placed` with the public entry, `declined` with reason, `receipted`, `taken-back`, `pending`); each public entry as R14; the core still due (R9); for each contesting entry its prompts (the re-evaluation cause, R13; the offer of a plan checkpoint, `action-plans`' act). Others get `NO_SUCH_CASE`. Writes nothing.

Placing in public: `docketPrepare({case, kind, shelf, edition, entry?, summary?, reason?, holder?, takesBack?, viewer, by}) → {entry, statement, digest, warning, expires}` (`op=docketprepare`; writes nothing) and `docketPost({digest, signature, acknowledged, by}) → {ok, case, seq, published_at}` (`op=docketpost`; mutating).
- R4 · Only the manager prepares. Refusals, in order, each writing nothing: a machine or AI credential, or an operator token, `MACHINE_CANNOT_PLACE_DOCKET`; `NO_SUCH_CASE` as R1; `by` not the manager `DOCKET_NOT_THE_MANAGER`; no group slug recorded (`promotion`'s `producingGroup`, its R40) `DOCKET_NO_GROUP_SLUG`; `entry` naming no record entry of the case `NO_SUCH_DOCKET_ENTRY`; one already `placed`, `declined` or `receipted` `DOCKET_ENTRY_SETTLED`; R1's form checks on the entry it would sign, then the kind's own refusals (R7, R8, R10–R12); `shelf` is `listed` or `reactions`: a `reaction` goes only to `reactions`, every other kind only to `listed`; any other pairing `DOCKET_WRONG_SHELF`; `shelf` may be omitted, the kind's own then used; a `reactions` entry requires `summary`, one paragraph ≤600 characters (`DOCKET_NO_SUMMARY`), and a capture whose co-archive succeeded (`attestation` R3, R7) (`DOCKET_NO_ARCHIVE_COPY`). Otherwise answers the entry that would be published (R6) as canonical JSON, its SHA-256 `digest`, `statement` = `signatures.docketStatement(case, seq, digest)`, the outward-act warning (meaning: the public will see it; a later entry can take it back but never unsay it) and `expires` 60 minutes later; same inputs on the same UTC day give identical bytes.
- R5 · `docketPost` refuses in order, writing nothing: R4's caller refusals again; `acknowledged` not exactly `true` `DOCKET_WARNING_NOT_ACKNOWLEDGED`; no prepared answer from `by` with this `digest`, past `expires`, or its `seq` taken since, `DOCKET_STALE`; a signature `signatures.verifySshsig` rejects (namespace `NS_DOCKET`, over exactly `statement`, against `credentials.attestingKeys()` restricted to `by`'s keys) `DOCKET_SIGNATURE_REFUSED` with the reason. Otherwise stores the entry with armored signature and first-published instant, served by R14, R15 from then; a placed record entry reads `placed`; a withdrawal is reported to `reevaluation` (R13). `docketSigners()` answers each key (`keyB64`) that signed a public entry with its first instant; viewer-free, names no member (for `network-notices` R21).
- R6 · A public entry carries only: `format` (`civicsmith-docket-entry/1`; pre-T31 `civicos-docket-entry/1`, both accepted forever, a chain may mix them), `group` (slug), `case`, `seq` (1, 2, … per case), `previous` (prior entry's digest over its stored bytes, or null); `shelf`; `kind` (`response`, `statement`, `reaction`, `outcome`, `edition`, `disclosure`, `withdrawal`, `standing-granted`, `standing-withdrawn`, `receipt`, `take-back`, `court-order`); `edition` (number, or `all` for a withdrawal); `date`, and `received` when placing a record entry; `from` (the subject's canonical name per `entities.readEntity`, a holder's name as granted, or the source's name as filed); `capture` `{sha256, origin, archived}`; by kind `summary`, `what_changed`, `reason`, `holder`, `answers`, `takes_back`, or `order` (R25). No member's name, handle or id. An `outcome` entry also carries `outcome` `{kind, on}` and `summary` (additive; a pre-T45 one reads `outcome: null`).

What the group lists, and never trims.
- R7 · A `response` or `statement` on `listed` is listed whole (R14 serves its capture's bytes as captured; nothing alters, trims or redacts them). The manager may instead decline a submission containing redactions: `docketDecline({entry, reason, by})` (`op=docketdecline`; mutating) records it with a reason (`DOCKET_NO_REASON`), publishing nothing; refusals R4's first three, then `NO_SUCH_DOCKET_ENTRY`, `DOCKET_ENTRY_SETTLED`. A submission without standing (`reaction`, or `from` `other`) is listed only by the group's choice and never core.
- R8 · A `receipt` entry lists a subject's or holder's reply that names a private person: `received`, `from`, `reason` ("names a private person", with the manager's words), never its text or capture bytes. `docketInvitation({entry, viewer})` (`op=docketinvitation`; read) answers the prefilled request to resend without the name, sent by a member's own means. A resent reply is filed (R1) naming the receipt (`answers`) and placed whole (R7).

The required core: `coreDue({case?, viewer})` (a read).
- R9 · Answers, for each case the viewer manages (one case when `case` is given), each core item still due, with its `since`: (a) each record entry `from` the named subject, or from a holder whose grant was live at its filing, of kind `response`, or of kind `statement` from the subject, not yet `placed`, `declined` (R7) or `receipted` (R8); (b) each ratified edition above 1 with no `edition` entry naming it; that entry quotes the edition's "What changed" statement (`case-grammar`'s `whatChangedOf`, its R8); (c) each candidate `case-tensions.caseTensions` (its R4) answers on a member of the latest ratified edition whose role is `load_bearing`, with no `disclosure` entry naming it. A withdrawal is core by being itself a `listed` entry (R12). `outcome` entries are never core, nor is anything about the group's own contacts with the subject. Writes nothing; answers no case the viewer does not manage.

Standing.
- R10 · A `standing-granted` entry, placed by the manager with a `reason` and the `holder`'s name, makes a grant: the holder's submissions filed while it is live carry exactly the named subject's rights (core, R9 (a); listed whole or declined only for redactions, R7). A `standing-withdrawn` entry, with a reason and naming the grant's entry, ends it for submissions filed after its `date` only; those filed before keep the grant's rights. A grant already ended is refused `DOCKET_NO_STANDING`. A grant is never deleted.

Taking back, and withdrawal.
- R11 · A `take-back` entry names an earlier public entry (`takes_back`, its `seq`) with a reason; both stay answered, the earlier marked taken back. A record entry is taken back by a later record entry with a reason and reads `taken-back`. Nothing is deleted. Taking back a `withdrawal` or `court-order` is `DOCKET_WITHDRAWAL_FINAL`; a `take-back`, `DOCKET_TAKE_BACK_FINAL`.
- R12 · A `withdrawal` entry, placed on `listed` by the manager, names one ratified edition or `all` (every edition ratified at its `date`; an edition ratified later is not withdrawn), with a published `reason` (`DOCKET_NO_REASON` as R1). An edition already withdrawn, or `all` when every ratified edition is, is refused `DOCKET_ALREADY_WITHDRAWN`. A withdrawal is never lifted (R11); standing behind the case again is a new edition. `withdrawalOf({case, edition})` answers the withdrawal entry naming that edition, `{seq, entry, date, reason, digest}` (`entry` the public entry's id, which `public-read` R20's link to it reads), or null; viewer-free, it writes nothing. The edition itself is untouched and answers forever (`publication` R24).
- R13 · At start fills `reevaluation`'s docket registration once (its R30) with `withdrawals({after, limit})` (case, owning project, editions named, each member finding at its pin `{bundle_id, sha, edition}` read through `publication`, signing instant, `seq`, entry id) and `contested({after, limit})` (each `contests: true` record entry: case, edition, member findings at pins, filing instant, entry). After a withdrawal's post or a contesting filing commits, calls `reevaluation.docketActed` once with kind, case and entry; a throw there never undoes the act.

A court order.
- R25 · A court order to the group (remove or redact a published case, or seal or unseal material) is complied with openly: a manager's `court-order` entry on `listed` through R4, R5, with `order: {effect, editions, parts?}` (`effect` `remove`, `redact`, `seal` or `unseal`; `editions` the ratified editions or `all`; `parts` for `redact`, `seal`, `unseal`); `capture` the captured order; `reason` the manager's words. Refusals beside R4's: bad `effect` or missing `parts` `DOCKET_ORDER_UNREADABLE`; `unseal` of a part no `seal` entry names `DOCKET_NOTHING_SEALED`. In the post's transaction the entry is named to `publication.stampEdition` (its R62), each named edition carrying the stamp linked to it. Never taken back; a later order is a new entry. Names no member, public whole; a sealed part is never carried by it. At start registers with publication (R62) `courtOrderOf(case, entry)` → `{seq, effect, editions, parts}` or null.

Dates as "what we did".

## Used services

record-grammar (`build/requirements/record-grammar.md`): the record's shared grammar: id and type vocabulary, restricted front-matter parser, canonical JSON, actor identity, grades, public-locator test, the one SHA-256; no store, network or clock.

signatures (`build/requirements/signatures.md`): builds and verifies SSHSIG statements and RFC 3161 timestamps; no record, stored private key, clock or network call.

record-core (`build/requirements/record-core.md`): owns the record's storage: id allocation, leases, append-only history and manifest, purge.
- record-core R6 · `mintOpaqueId(prefix, year, tail, taken) → id or null` returns `<prefix>-<year>-DDDD<tail>`, DDDD uniform over 0000–9999 (CSPRNG, rejection sampling), checked against `taken(id)` and every id ever handed out; a collision draws again.
- record-core R7 · Each id is recorded before return in the caller's transaction, so a rollback takes it back.
- record-core R8 · A recorded id is never drawn again, for any caller, purged or not; the ledger is exempt from both purges.
- record-core R9 · `null` only after 64 colliding draws in a row; never throws.
- record-core R63 · `registerCounts(module, keys, counts)`: once, at start, a non-empty key list and a synchronous `counts(hid)` (`hid` `{sql, args}` or null, passed on unread); answers every key in order, null (never zero) when unreadable. A held key or second registration `COUNTS_DECLARED` naming the holder; malformed `COUNTS_MALFORMED` (C-102.13, .14); a refused one registers nothing; never throws.
- record-core R13 · `readFile(bundleId, path)` → `{text, sha256}` inline or `{blobSha, bytes, sha256}` (never the bytes) from its own tables.
- record-core R14 · `null` when the bundle or path is not held; never throws.
