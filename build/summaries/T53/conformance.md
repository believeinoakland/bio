## conformance (own requirements)
- R1 onward · history and some K/N tags are dropped and wording trimmed in this summary: read a requirement whole in build/requirements/conformance.md before changing it or its test

**Purpose.** A member's determination that a named act is compliant, noncompliant or unclear against named standards, per standard; a machine prepares the comparison, never determines; unclear sends each question back to an inquiry; no ranking of significance. Findings: R2, R31, R32; never adopts another group's determination (R35); void state R33; cure link R36, R37.

**Terms.** An **act** is an event held in `events`: `{event, actor: {role, body, entity_id}, evidence}`. The **actor** is the office; who decided, authored, signed or carried it out are the event's participants (`decider`, `author`, `signatory`, `implementer`), never the actor. An `ACT-` id aliases an event (`events.aliasAct`, `eventForAct`; R26); "the same act" is equality of the event, never of description, actor or date. **Outcome**: `compliant`, `noncompliant`, `unclear`. **Row**: `{standard, requires, did, reading, content}`, `reading` `aligns`, `diverges` or `open`.

**determine({project, act, findings, standards, rows, questions?, supersedes?, reason?, author, viewer}) → `{ok: true, id, ...}` or refusal**
- R1 · Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_DETERMINE` (empty or machine author); `NO_SUCH_PROJECT` (absent or not visible, one answer); `DETERMINATION_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (author not `joined`); R25's act refusals; `ACT_INCOMPLETE` (no actor role or body, or no evidence), naming the part; `NO_FINDINGS`; `FINDING_NOT_PUBLISHED` (R2), naming it; `NO_STANDARDS`; `NO_SUCH_STANDARD` (`standards.noSuchStandard`), naming it; `STANDARD_NOT_IN_FORCE` (R3); `ROWS_INCOMPLETE` (a named standard with no row, or a row whose `requires`, `did` or `reading` is empty); `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`; `UNCLEAR_NO_QUESTION` (R6); `SIGNIFICANCE_IS_A_MEMBERS_JUDGMENT` (R8); R22's refusals in order.
- R2 · Each finding is of one of three kinds, recorded per finding: **published** (a finding-version in a published case edition of `project`, `publication.publishedEditionsOf` (R37); pins edition and version); **checked and held** (R31); **accepted** (R32). None: `FINDING_NOT_PUBLISHED` (code unchanged, detail naming the three kinds; translation owed to UX). Published only by another project and not accepted: still refused.
- R3 · Each standard is read through `standards.inForceAt` (`inForce` an alias) at the act event's `when` (each end of a band or period): `not_in_force` refused; `undetermined`, including an act whose `when` is undetermined or placed nowhere, accepted and stated beside that standard with its reason.
- R4 · Outcome is per standard, from the member, never composed. All rows `aligns` with `noncompliant`, or any `diverges` with `compliant`, is accepted with the disagreement stated beside it, never corrected.
- R5 · The three outcomes carry the same obligations (R1-R4) and the same reads.
- R6 · `unclear` names at least one question `{question, inquiry}`: an existing inquiry the author may see, or a new inquiry opened in the same act (state `open`, titled from the question, in `project`). Together or not at all: two `promotion.promote` calls inside one outer `record-core.transact`, a refused second rolling back the first.
- R7 · Never edited. `supersedes` names an earlier determination of the same act (its act id) in the same project; the superseder carries its act id, with a `reason`: absent, null or blank `CONFORMANCE_NO_REASON`; over 500 characters or not text `CONFORMANCE_BAD_REASON` (R23); both reads name the link. Superseded at most once: a second is `DETERMINATION_SUPERSEDED` (R20), naming the first. C-113.18 `ALREADY_SUPERSEDED` retired, number not reused.
- R8 · No input or answer carries a significance, severity, priority, urgency, rank or score; a determination carrying such a key is refused.
- R18 · `determine` takes `proposal?`, the comparison id (R12) it drew on (absent, invisible or of another project: `NO_SUCH_COMPARISON`, C-113.20). At most 50 findings, 50 standards, 200 rows, 20 questions, 50 evidence content ids (a comparison the same); over any, `DETERMINATION_TOO_LARGE` naming part and cap.

**determinationRead({id, viewer}); determinationsFor({project?, act?, standard?, finding?, outcome?, live?, void?, after?, limit?, viewer}) → `{items, cursor, truncated}`**
- R9 · `determinationRead` answers R1's fields, per-standard outcomes, each finding's pinned edition and strength pair frozen at publication beside its live pair (`strength.inquiryStrength`), per axis, never composed (DEC-44), author, time, supersession links, R10's flag, R22's cause, `outcomes_differ: true` when per-standard outcomes differ, the act as R25, each finding's `basis` (R2, R31, R32, R34), `void` (R33), `cured_by` and `cures` (R37). Absent or invisible: R19; unseen things withheld whole (R24).
- R10 · `live` until superseded. `basis_changed`, naming each cause, when a finding is reopened, superseded or published in a later edition, a named standard is superseded, or a text or evidence content id has a newer capture not carrying the passage (`content.passageNotice`). A notice only; a member supersedes.
- R11 · `determinationsFor`: at most 200 per page (lower `limit` honoured), id order, `truncated` by reading one past; `live: true` leaves out superseded; `act` filters by act id. ## Uses: record-core
Purpose: storage: ids, leases, append-only promotion history, purge.
**allocId(prefix, year) → `{id}`**
- R1 · From `record-grammar`'s `ID_TABLE`: a sequential prefix (or one the table lacks) returns `<prefix>-<year>-N`, next unused number in scope, zero-padded to four digits, never cut, no ceiling, never returned twice, purge or no purge; an opaque prefix returns R76's id.
- R2 · Same step alone or inside a caller's transaction. Never throws.

**transact(fn) → fn's result**
- R32 · One transaction over the whole store: `fn` throwing or returning `ok:false` rolls back every row in any module's tables and spends no id. A nested call joins the outer; if it throws or refuses it rolls back its own writes and ids (savepoint). Rethrows after rollback.

**stampInstant(precision, when?) → string; instantOrder(a, b) → number**
- R47 · Spells `when` (ms, default now) in UTC ISO 8601: `"second"` gives `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`, `"millisecond"` gives `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.sssZ`; any other precision throws, naming it.
- R48 · `instantOrder` compares instants in either spelling as instants, not strings: negative, zero, positive; NaN when either is not a non-empty readable instant. Never throws.
