## money (own requirements)

Status: in force (K1505, K1580); amount checks, detectors are `money-checks`'; restrictions, thresholds, transfer authority, `pay` duties are `duties`' (K1504). R39, R40, R41 not yet met (T53), every other met. Size (P6) under 4,000 lines.

Purpose: money as stated amounts. Each money fact is one source's reading of one amount, with kind, phase or stage, basis, accounting period, parties, funds and two grades; never an amount without those axes, a machine-asserted truth, or a total. Money refuses sums across axes by name, follows flows through member-declared trails, reconciles two readings by naming the differing dimension, sets committed against paid, walks an amount's chain of authority. The occurrence is an event, a promise to pay a duty, arithmetic a calculation. The group's own money is never marked as the group's (K1463).

Terms. Fact `MNY-` `{fact_id, amount, as_read, currency, sign, precision, kind, phase, stage?, adjusts?, basis, period, from, to, codes, balance_class?, buys?, concerns, source, grade: {reading, parties}, by, at}`; `amount` exact decimal, never float; `as_read` as printed; `sign` explicit; `precision` `exact`, `rounded`, `approximate`, `range` (low, high). Kind (closed, 14): revenue, expenditure, transfer, allocation, payment, contribution, gift, income, behested, settlement, debt, balance, fee charged, other. Phase: proposed, adopted, adjusted, actual. Stage (`actual` only): encumbered, incurred, paid; assessed, collected. Adjustment: a signed fact naming `adjusts`. Basis: budgetary, cash, modified accrual, accrual, undetermined. `period` the accounting period only (range, precision, zone); a fiscal key mapped by `civil-time` from the profile's `fiscal_year` for the body. Party `{entity?, fund?, account?, as_written}`; `codes` `{scheme, code}`. `balance_class` (kind `balance`) a class of the fund type's family (GASB 54; net position), families and fund type (`setFundType`) held by money. `buys?` `{quantity?, unit?, what?}`, `quantity` exact decimal, else `UNKNOWN_FIELD`. `concerns`: events, entities of kind contract, fund, program or proceeding, or a line; never a duty. `source` exactly one: a content extent or another fact; never `CALC-`. `grade.reading` never raised by a machine; `grade.parties` per party; apart. Set `MSR-` `{set_id, purpose: trail | attribution, label, concerns?, inclusions, exclusions}`; `concerns?` one id or none: an id a fact's `concerns` may name or, for a trail, a project's id (R28; K4369). Viewer, refusal shape, `by` as `entities`.
