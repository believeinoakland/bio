Purpose: publishing a signed case edition at a set time (DEC-147). The case ceremony (`ratification`, `op=publishat`) signs and, in place of `publication` R22's commit, sets the edition to wait here. Its signature is held beside the document, never on it, so until published its document answers as an unsigned preparation and nothing of it is public (`publication` R29). At its time the one publisher `ratification` registers runs every signing check again and commits through `publication` R22 only when nothing has changed; otherwise the edition stops, once, and publishing it needs a new signing. An owner of the case's project may move or cancel the time until it comes. This module holds the waiting editions and their table, the publisher's and listeners' registrations, and answers the schedule's read (for the case and the queue), the wake (for `scheduler`) and the waiting-edition read (for `case-authoring`). Every case-document table and every write to one stays `publication`'s (its R24); this module reads them only under `publication` R40, and `publication` reads whether a document waits through R8.

Terms are `publication`'s: a case, an edition, the case document. A **waiting edition** is a signed case edition this module holds to be published at a set time. Every refusal names `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check`, `code` and `translation`.

- R1 · (was `publication` R66; DEC-147 (1), (2)) `scheduleEdition({case, edition, docSha, signature, signer, deliveredBy, at, checked, by})` is called by the case ceremony (`ratification`, for `op=publishat`) in place of `publication` R22's commit, after every refusal that commit would make has passed, inside the caller's transaction. `at` is `{date, time}`: a `YYYY-MM-DD` and a 24-hour `HH:MM`, a local time in the team's governing zone, read as `retrieval` R69 reads it: the value that governs on this instance for the `time_zone` path as `local-facts` answers it (its R2, a local correction first), else the active profiles' `time_zone` (`jurisdictions` R41, read through `jurisdictions.combine` over record-core's `jurisdiction_profiles` setting) (T50; N1203, K3635 doubt 5). It is resolved to its instant through `civil-time` (`bounds` of the minute-precision date-time, its `earliest`). `checked` is what the ceremony checked at signing, as R2's publisher will compare it. Refusals, in order, each writing nothing: `PUBLISH_AT_MALFORMED` (a date failing `civil-time.isCalendarDate`, or a time not `HH:MM` from `00:00` to `23:59`); `PUBLISH_AT_NO_ZONE` (no zone held; the time is never read as UTC); `PUBLISH_AT_PAST` (an instant not after now; publishing now is `publication` R22's commit); `NO_CASE_DOCUMENT` (no unsigned document of the case edition at `docSha`); `CASE_EDITION_ALREADY_RATIFIED`; `PUBLISH_AT_ALREADY_SET` (the case edition already waits, answered with it). Otherwise it records one waiting edition `{case, edition, doc_sha, signature, signer, delivered_by, signed_at, at: {date, time, zone}, publish_at, set_by, state: "waiting", checked}` and answers `{ok: true, case, edition, state: "waiting", at, publish_at}`; the same call again answers `existed: true`. The signature is held beside the document, never on it: until R2 publishes it, `publication` R1, R2, R12 and R29 answer the document as unsigned, so nothing of the edition is public.
- R2 · (was `publication` R67; DEC-147 (3), (5)) `publishWake()` answers the earliest `publish_at` of a waiting edition, or null. `publishDue(now)` takes each waiting edition whose `publish_at` is at or before `now`, in `publish_at` order, handing each, with its `checked`, to the one publisher registered at start: `registerScheduledPublisher({publishScheduled(entry, now)})`, `ratification`'s (K31's pattern, as `publication` R23's provider). The publisher may answer asynchronously; `publishDue` awaits each answer before taking the next (K1832) and hands it the entry R1 recorded, its held `signature` included. The publisher runs again every check signing ran and commits through `publication` R22 only when each passes and nothing `checked` records has changed since signing: a source the case rests on (`publication` R51's consent, R59's accepted work), a publishing member's confirmation of no undeclared tie, a hold. It answers `{published: true, published_at}` or `{stopped: [{code, translation}]}`; each stop entry is recorded with its `check` and `cause` exactly as the publisher answered them (T39; N805), so an edition stopped by C-122.6 or C-122.7 keeps that code and translation. The edition then becomes `published` (with `published_at`) or `stopped` (with its reasons), once: never tried again; a stopped edition has committed nothing (`publication` R22's refusals write nothing); publishing it needs a new signing. With no publisher registered, or one that throws or gives neither answer, the edition is `stopped` with `SCHEDULED_CHECK_UNAVAILABLE` (R9) and never published unchecked. An edition taken after its time (a late alarm) is checked when taken and keeps both `publish_at` and `published_at`. *(T51, G54; K4711)* A published answer may carry `after: {containers: [{case, edition, built, code?}]}`, one entry per case file the publisher assembled (`ratification` R42). The row settles `published` whenever the document is signed, whatever the list says.

## Used services

civil-time (`build/requirements/civil-time.md`): the one engine for civil time: local days, date-times with precision and zone, EDTF bands, three-valued comparison, time rules counted the law's way, due dates, recurrences, fiscal periods, `validAt`. Pure: no store, network or clock; "now" is the caller's; every local fact comes from the jurisdiction view it is given. Values: a date-time is `{value, precision, zone}`, `precision` `day` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), `minute` (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`), `second` or `edtf`; `zone` an IANA name; an instant matches `record-grammar`'s `ISO_TS_RE`; a refusal is `{refused: CODE, why}`; services throw `TypeError` only for a wrong JavaScript type or absent required argument, else never.
- civil-time R2 · `bounds(dt) → {earliest, latest}`: the half-open span `[earliest, latest)` a date-time covers in its zone: a day its local day (23 or 25 hours across a daylight-saving change), a minute its minute, a second its second, an EDTF value its widest reading (R3). A malformed `value`, or one not of its `precision`'s form, is refused `DATE_INVALID`.
- civil-time R6 · `isCalendarDate(s) → boolean`: `true` only for a `YYYY-MM-DD` naming a real day of the proleptic Gregorian calendar (`2024-02-29` yes, `2026-02-31` and `2026-13-01` no).

local-facts (`build/requirements/local-facts.md`): members' confirmation of the profile's local calendar and offices on this instance. A profile's holidays, office hours and time zone (`jurisdictions` R33, R41–R44) are researched facts; this module records each member's confirmation, correction or dispute, answers each fact's status and the value that governs here, lapses a confirmation at its horizon, and lists the facts a live deadline reads that are due. A member confirms; a machine never does.

jurisdictions (`build/requirements/jurisdictions.md`): holds the jurisdiction profiles, every local fact the product uses, as data, each with the measurement it rests on; lists, returns, validates and combines them. No other module names a place. `combine(list) → {ok: true, view, conflicts}` or `{ok: false, errors}`: `list` is the active profiles in order (held `id`s or profile objects); which are active is an instance setting held by the record; this module does not store it.
- jurisdictions R12 · An `id` not held gives `UNKNOWN_PROFILE`; an object failing `validate` gives `INVALID_PROFILE` with its errors; a profile given twice is combined once; never throws; two different profiles with one `id` give `INVALID_PROFILE`; a non-array list `NOT_A_LIST`; an unknown field in an entry `UNKNOWN_SECTION`.
- jurisdictions R13 · `view` has the profile shape, plus `profiles` (ids combined, in order), `covers` (the union), `test` (true when any combined profile is a test profile); each fact carries `profile` beside its basis; `id` the combined ids joined by `+`, and a `name`.
- jurisdictions R14 · List facts unioned in order; an entry equal to an earlier one in all but basis and `profile` is kept once carrying both (`bases: [{profile, basis}]`, first giver's `basis` and `profile`); order kept; a space's distinct labels joined with "; ".
