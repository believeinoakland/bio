## basis-versions (own requirements)

Status: in force (K102), later folds reviewed. Changed T41 (R48), T42 (R49), T47 (R50), T48 (R28 names `version.author`), T50 (R51–R54), T51 (R55 `compareVersions`; R9's reading-screen fields). Purpose: an inquiry's basis holds several versions, each a complete, named, described alternative account of the support for its claim, frozen once written (INVESTIGATIVE-SESSION §6). This module holds the versions and their legs, their six-act state machine, the version a project stands on (CURRENT, §7), a project's conclusion as the adoption of that version's claim (§7.1), and narrowing a leg to a part of its document (Content Framework §14.4). Every act is a member's; a machine proposes a version and holds no act that moves one.

Terms: a version is a `basis_versions[]` row `{name, description, claim?, relationship, state, derived_from, hidden, kind?, run?, author, at, state_by?, state_at?, state_reason?, affirmed_parts?, regroup_by?, regroup_at?, regroup_note?}` with `basis_version_legs[]` (legs as `inquiry` defines them) and `basis_version_grounds[]`. Its composition is the canonical text of every field that defines it. States `suggested`, `considering`, `accepted`, `rejected`. A project's CURRENT is its `current_versions[]` row for the inquiry; its conclusion record its `conclusions[]` rows for it, the latest its stance.

## Used services

record-grammar (`build/requirements/record-grammar.md`): the shared grammar below every module reading or writing a document: ids and types, front-matter parser, canonical JSON, actor identity, grades, public-locator test, one SHA-256; no store, network or clock.

text-chain (`build/requirements/text-chain.md`): the transcription chain: what each derivation step (incl. a member's typing) may claim, a chain only weakens, member attestation and its extent, the extent geometry; nothing throws.

record-core (`build/requirements/record-core.md`): owns storage: ids, leases, append-only history and manifest, purge; every storing module writes through it.
- record-core R21 · `declarePurge(module, tables, {exempt?})` is `declareTable(module, entries)`'s default form: each table `purge` `exempt` if listed else `clear`, `expunge` `none`, `export` `admin-only`, `sight` `bundle` when keyed to a bundle else `group`, `derive` `stored`, `version_chain` `false` (classes: `purge` clear/exempt, `expunge` tombstone/none, `export` yes/admin-only/never, `sight` group/bundle/source/owner, `derive` stored/derived-rebuildable, `version_chain`); missing or unknown class `TABLE_CLASS_MISSING`/`TABLE_CLASS_UNKNOWN`; twice or by two modules `TABLE_DECLARED`; `purge` clears every `clear` table; `declaredTables()` lists them.
- record-core R46 · A declared table is a name keyed by its `bundle_id` column, or `{name, keys: [columns], whole: "<WHERE clause>"}` (keyed by any named column; whole-store purge only where the clause holds); under `declareTable` these fields sit beside the classes.

membership (`build/requirements/membership.md`): members and what each may do, and projects as working groups (participants, visibility, sight, the fence for acting); no record integrity, no credential.
