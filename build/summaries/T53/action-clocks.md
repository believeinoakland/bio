## action-clocks (own requirements)
- R1-R26 · their Test and Control sentences are left out of this summary: read a requirement whole in build/requirements/action-clocks.md before writing or changing its test

**Status**: R27-R29 *(not yet met: T53)*; R21-R23 marked not yet met (T47); split from `actions` (K617, K624).

**Purpose.** The deadlines on the group's actions, read across actions: which clock entries are pending and which have passed, the entries a profile's deadline would give (calendar days, or business days on the profile's holiday calendar, saying whether a member has confirmed that calendar), and the reminders a member asks for on them, which fire as asked and never otherwise. An action's clock is written in its own document (`actions`); this module reads it and proposes entries apart. A member states every entry and every reminder; a machine proposes an entry and nothing else.

**Terms.** An action, its document and clock entry (`{text, description, date, basis, status}`; `pending`, `met`, `overdue`, `waived`) are `actions`'. An entry's **position** is its index in `clock[]`. A **day** is a `YYYY-MM-DD` local day in the zone `actions.actionZone` reads (R21), never UTC; an entry is past on a day after its date. A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, ...}`; a catalogue-backed one also carries `code`, `check`, `translation`.

- R1 · `pendingClocks({before, after?, limit?, viewer})` refuses a `before` that is not a date `PENDING_CLOCKS_BAD_BEFORE`; else lists every `pending` entry dated before `before` across visible actions: action, entry position, date, basis, text, whether past at `before`; at most 500 per page, `truncated` stated. A page runs in (action id, entry position) order after `after` (a previous `cursor` or an action id, read as after all that action's entries), reads at most 500 actions, may end inside an action; `cursor` is the last entry answered (`<action>#<position>`) when `truncated`, else null; when the 500-action bound ends a page past an action with no qualifying entry, `cursor` is that action's last position; every entry is reached by paging through each `cursor` to null.
- R2 · `clockPropose({target, rule, proposer, viewer})` offers a clock entry computed from a profile deadline (`jurisdictions` R26) applying to the action's kind: start from the event the rule names in the ledger, `received` being the counterparty's receipt of the group's request, counted from the group's `sent` entry, never from a `received` entry; date by `civil-time.evaluateRule` (R9-R13: forward or backward, calendar or business days on the profile's holiday calendar `jurisdictions` R33, roll past closed days, weekend from the profile's `weekend` fact never from code, extension computed, hours and "close of business" in the office's hours), its `trace` answered with it; undetermined with why when the calendar does not cover the period, a start the ledger does not hold is named, or the rule is not held with a primary source (`UNMEASURED` is not a basis, K1445; `jurisdictions` R44); `basis` the rule's citation and profile basis. An uncertain due date (two candidates) is offered with both, the group's own taking the earliest (K1444 (i)). Stored apart, labelled as `actions` R19's (machine work, a member's, or unstated), never written into `clock[]`. Refusals in order: `NO_AUTHOR` (no stamped proposer); `NO_TARGET`; an absent `rule`; `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` (absent and invisible alike); `NOT_AN_ACTION`; `NO_SUCH_RULE` (no active profile states a deadline of that rule for the action's kind, naming both; also answers `deadlines: [{rule, label?, citation?}]`, what this call would accept as `rule`, `[]` when none applies, the office's own level first, those tied to another level last, the rest between; a valid rule's answer carries no `deadlines`). An absent `rule` is its own refusal `NO_RULE` at that place (row held, test driving it) or answered `NO_SUCH_RULE`. A `starts` of `received` counts from the receipt `action-records` records (its R4, via `actions.actionRead`; `kind: "receipt"`) when held, else from the first `sent` entry, the `trace` saying which ("receipt not recorded: counted from sending"); `served` counts from the recorded service (`kind: "service"`), undetermined with why when none is held. Deadlines read are `local-facts.governingView`'s (its R12); with none it equals `combine`'s. A proposal carries `owed_by` from the deadline's (`jurisdictions` R75), `undetermined` where not said.
- R3 · `overdueClocks({after?, limit?, viewer})` (for `queue-producers` R15, monitoring R34) lists every entry of a visible action not `resolved` or `abandoned` whose status is `overdue`, or `pending` with a date before the local day of the instance clock: action, entry position, date, basis, text, the action's project (that of the first determination among its `rests_on` legs, via `conformance.determinationRead`; null when none) and the member who created it (author of its first manifest entry); at most 500 per page in (action id, entry position) order, `cursor` and `truncated` as R1's; writes nothing. Each item carries `zone` (R21), `owed_by` (R18), `due_at` and its zone where the entry states a time of day (`civil-time` R9-R13), `steps` (R16), `noticed_by` where R17 holds, `venue` (R23). An item is a question about a date, never a finding: no word of it says the body failed or violated anything. While the action states `reply_owed: false` (`actions` R82), no entry `owed_by: body` is listed.
- R4 · `reminderSet({target, entry, on, from?, author, viewer})` (`op=reminderset`): member `author` asks to be reminded of the dated entry at position `entry` of action `target` on day `on`; with `from`, changes their own reminder on that entry from `from` to `on`, or with `on: null` removes it. A reminder is a row in this module's table (action, entry, day, who set it, when), never a field or revision of the action's document, which this op leaves unchanged. A member sets, changes or removes a reminder at any time and only their own; one is set when a dated option is chosen (`action-plans` R29 sets it through this service, the choosing member as `author`), from defaults the member sees and can change, or later. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_REMINDER` (`author` machine or unstamped); `NO_SUCH_ACTION` (`actions.noSuchAction`); `REMINDER_REFUSED`, detail naming the arm: an `entry` naming no clock entry with a `YYYY-MM-DD` date, an `on` not a date (or `null` without `from`), a `from` naming no reminder of the author's on that entry, a day the author already holds on that entry, or a 51st reminder on the action. Else `{ok, target, entry, on, from: from | null}`. `remindersFor({action, viewer})` answers an action's reminders (entry, day, who set it, state `waiting`, `due`, `answered`); an absent or invisible action answers `actions.noSuchAction`. `reminderRefused(arm, detail, extra?)`, a module-level function exported as `noSuchAction` is, answers `{ok: false, reason: "REMINDER_REFUSED", code, check, translation, detail, arm}`, `extra` adding a caller's fields, so `action-plans` R29 answers through it; writes nothing, never throws.
