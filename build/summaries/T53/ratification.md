## Used services

record-grammar (`build/requirements/record-grammar.md`): the record's shared grammar (ids, types, front-matter parser, canonical JSON, actor identity, grades, locator test, SHA-256); no store, network or clock.

signatures (`build/requirements/signatures.md`): builds and verifies SSHSIG statements (release, notice, manifest, bundle ratification, case ratification) and RFC 3161 timestamps; no record, stored key, clock or network call of its own.

record-core (`build/requirements/record-core.md`): the record's storage: ids, leases, append-only history and manifest, purge; every storing module writes through it.
- record-core R60 · **textAtSha(bundleId, sha) → string or null**: the inline text of the bundle's `bundle.md` whose SHA-256 is `sha`, live or from any historical snapshot, from its own tables alone; `null` when an argument is absent, no such text is held, or the file is held only as a blob. Never throws.
- record-core R32 · **transact(fn)**: one transaction over the whole store; a throw or a refusal (`ok:false`) rolls back every row and spends no allocated id; a nested call joins as a savepoint. Rethrows what `fn` throws, after the rollback.
- record-core R47 · **stampInstant(precision, when?)**: UTC ISO 8601, `"second"` `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`, `"millisecond"` `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.sssZ`; other precision throws naming it.
- record-core R48 · `instantOrder(a, b)` compares instants as instants, never strings; NaN for an unreadable side; never throws.

membership (`build/requirements/membership.md`): who the members are and what each may do; projects at setup, sight and the fence; no record integrity, no credential.
- membership R77 · **existenceAct(projectId, viewer) → refusal or null**: `PROJECT_SEEN_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (C-70.1, id and name only) when the viewer's sight is existence only; `owners` added only for an administrator at a hidden project's `EXISTENCE`; `null` without a viewer; asked before any no-such-project answer; never throws.

promotion (`build/requirements/promotion.md`): the one write path for a bundle (compare-and-swap, append-only history, whole or nothing); runs the check catalogue at ratification (the gate).
