Purpose: a case's account (D56, D61): the written account of what the case's evidence shows, and the reviewers' comments a publisher chooses to carry with it. The system drafts the account from cited evidence in one framing or several (time order, by question, by rule), and may flag sentences of a member's account the cited evidence does not support; each draft is labelled as the system's, machine work when a machine proposed it, never the case's account. The member writes the account, from a draft or from nothing, published as hers; when she takes a draft up, how is recorded, and taking it up as proposed is refused. The module holds the drafts and acceptances, the account's shape, and the judgment of every sentence of the account and of the case's four statements against what each cites (through `case-disclosures` R30); the one reader of review copies' comments (`review` registers it) and the comments the publisher chose. `case-authoring`'s `publishCase` and `publishPreflight` ask it in the order case-authoring R69 states and write what it answers into the case document; this module writes no case document.

Provides, terms: terms are `case-authoring`'s and `publication`'s. An account is a list of sentences `[{text, cites, bias_statement?}]`; a cite is `{kind, ref, ord}`, `kind` one of `case-grammar`'s `ACCOUNT_CITE_KINDS` (a finding, a leg of one with its `ord`, a passage by its `content_id`, a material by its `materials:` ref). A draft is a row stored from an AI run: `kind` `case_account` (an account's words in one framing) or `account_check` (flags on a member's account). `viewer` and `proposedBy` are the control plane's stamps. Every refusal names `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check`, `code` and `translation`.

Services: `accountPropose({case, kind?, framing, text | flags, run, proposedBy, viewer})` (`op=accountpropose`), `accountDrafts({case, viewer})` (`op=accountdrafts`); `accountDraftJudged(accountDraft, caseId, sentences)`, `acceptanceRecorded({caseId, edition, drafted, by, at})`; `accountShaped(account, statementCites, excludedCount)`, `accountJudged({…})`, `citedFor(rows, prepared, reached, viewer)`; `registerReviewComments(fn)`, `reviewCommentsChosen(reviewComments, caseId, edition, viewer, draft)`, `reviewCommentBodyLines(given)`; `advocacyCheck({project, sentences, author, viewer})` (`op=advocacycheck`); `caseAccountOf(host, deps)`.

## Used services

record-grammar (`record-grammar.md`): the record's shared grammar below every module reading or writing a document: id and type vocabulary, restricted front-matter parser, canonical JSON, actor identity, grade vocabulary, public-locator test, the one SHA-256; no store, network or clock.

record-core (`record-core.md`): owns the record's storage: id allocation, leases, the append-only history and manifest of every promotion, and purge; the tables and services no later module owns. No member, capability or fence (`membership`'s) and no promotion write path (`promotion`'s); every module that stores anything writes through and reads from it.

- record-core R1 · `allocId(prefix, year) → {id}`: mints from `record-grammar`'s `ID_TABLE` (its R46). For a sequential prefix (and one the table does not hold) returns `<prefix>-<year>-N`, N the next unused number in the `<prefix>-<year>` scope, zero-padded to four digits and never cut, no ceiling (the 10,000th is `…-10000`), stepping that scope's counter so the number is never returned again, purge or no purge. For an opaque prefix it returns R76's id. Tests allocate the 10,000th id of every sequential prefix; `entities`' identifier checks accept it (B0.12).
- record-core R2 · `allocId` takes the identical step whether called alone or inside a caller's transaction, so the two never diverge. Errors: never throws.
- record-core R62 · `mintExhausted(prefix, extra?) → refusal` (module-level; N322, N250, K275, K392): the one answer to no free opaque id (R6's `mintOpaqueId` null, R9; or R76's 64 hits): `{ok: false, reason: "MINT_EXHAUSTED", code: "MINT_EXHAUSTED", check, translation, prefix, detail}`; `prefix` one of R3's gated set, or `SRC`, or a prefix R76 mints (`CALC`, `STP`, `GUD`; T42, K2617) and `ACD` (record-grammar R55), its sentence naming a case account draft; `detail` one fixed sentence per prefix naming the id it could not draw and saying nothing was written, the same for every caller; `check`, `translation` its row's. `extra` adds fields, never replaces. Every act answering this condition answers through it (`promotion` R19, `case-authoring` R7, `review` R27, `tasks` R1, `sources`); one row (C-59.6), `where` naming this function; review's C-87.12 gives way. Writes nothing, never throws. (T48; N1018, K3112) Also `WCD` and `OUTC` (`case-inputs` R6, R9; record-grammar R46), each its own sentence (a draft of a new edition's statement of what changed; a carried outcome). Test: each answers its own `detail`; control: `CASE`'s unchanged.
- record-core R13 · `readFile(bundleId, path) → {text, sha256} | {blobSha, bytes, sha256} | null`: the file's live content when inline, or its blob reference (never bytes) when a blob, from this module's tables alone.
- record-core R14 · `readFile` returns `null` when the bundle or path is not held. Errors: never throws.
- record-core R21 · `declarePurge(module, tables, {exempt?}) → void`: a module declares its tables once at start through `declareTable(module, entries)` (S0-3), each entry naming classes `purge` (`clear`/`exempt`), `expunge` (`tombstone`, removable under R79, or `none`), `export` (`yes`/`admin-only`/`never`; read by `corpus-export`), `sight` (`group`/`bundle`/`source`/`owner`), `derive` (`stored`/`derived-rebuildable`, R77), `version_chain` (`true` when a change appends a version). Missing or unknown class: `TABLE_CLASS_MISSING`/`TABLE_CLASS_UNKNOWN` naming table and class, nothing registered. `declarePurge` is `declareTable`'s default form: `purge` `exempt` when listed in `exempt` else `clear`; `expunge` `none`; `export` `admin-only`; `sight` `bundle` when keyed to a bundle else `group`; `derive` `stored`; `version_chain` `false`. `purge` clears every table whose `purge` is `clear`; this module's own tables are declared by it. `declaredTables()` answers every declaration in order. A table declared twice or by two modules: `TABLE_DECLARED`.
- record-core R46 · A declared table is a name, keyed to a bundle by its `bundle_id` column when it has one, or `{name, keys: [columns], whole: "<WHERE clause>"}` (keyed by the named columns, any matching; whole-store purge only where the clause holds). (K61) Under `declareTable` the same fields sit beside the classes, `{name, keys?, whole?, clears?, purge, expunge, export, sight, derive, version_chain}`.
